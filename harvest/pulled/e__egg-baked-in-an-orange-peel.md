The peel is the whole point. Citrus rind is wet and dense, so it heats slowly and evenly instead of burning through the way a paper or thin metal vessel would. That gives the egg a long, forgiving window in the coals, and it means no pan to scrub afterward.

This is not a backpacking recipe and there is no honest way to make it one. Eggs and whole oranges need a cooler, and the whole trick depends on carrying the fruit intact. Cook over coals, not flame, and keep the foil on unless you want ash in the egg. Expect a faint citrus edge on the finished egg. Some people like it, some do not.
