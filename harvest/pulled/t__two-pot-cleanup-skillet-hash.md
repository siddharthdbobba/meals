An aluminum pot heats fast and unevenly, which is why this recipe rehydrates before it browns. Boiling first gets the potatoes soft with the water doing the work; the browning at the end is short and needs constant attention or you get a scorched ring and raw shreds above it. A titanium pot makes that ring worse. If titanium is what you carry, keep the flame low and stir through the whole browning step.

The jerky rehydrates in the same water and gives back most of the salt it was cured with, so go light on added salt until the end. Cheese and oil are the calorie load. Dehydrated hash browns are close to weightless dry and triple in volume, so a cup of them is a real dinner, not a side.
