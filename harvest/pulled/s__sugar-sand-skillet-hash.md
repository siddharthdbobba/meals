The whole trick here is rehydrating before browning. Dehydrated potato shreds will not crisp in a dry pan; they scorch on the outside and stay hard in the middle. Soaking first, then draining hard, gets you a surface dry enough to actually take color.

Oil is not optional. It is the browning agent and about a third of the calories, and dried potato absorbs a surprising amount of it. Do not stir constantly. Contact time with the pan is the only thing standing between hash and wet potato shreds.
