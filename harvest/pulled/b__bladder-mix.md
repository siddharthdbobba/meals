A hydration bladder holds more water than you will drink plain, and sweat takes salt out faster than water alone puts anything back. This is the cheapest fix: sugar for a little energy, sodium and potassium for what you lost, flavor so you keep sipping. The sugar is not just calories. At roughly this concentration it helps the water and sodium move out of your gut instead of sitting there.

Mix it in the bladder, not in a bottle you pour in later. Sugar in a hose is the tradeoff, so rinse the tube at home and hang the bag open to dry, or run the sweet version in a separate bottle and keep the bladder for plain water. Everything here is dry, weighs about two ounces, and keeps indefinitely in a snack bag.
