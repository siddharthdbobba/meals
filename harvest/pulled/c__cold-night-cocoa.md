Cold nights are a calorie problem more than anything else. A body that has run out of fuel stops making heat, and no amount of insulation fixes that. This is a warm liquid with real fat and sugar in it, taken right before you get horizontal, so digestion is doing some of the work of keeping you warm.

Whole milk powder and butter powder are the load-bearing ingredients. Nonfat milk powder makes a thin, sad cup that is mostly hot water. Keep the water short of a boil: milk solids stuck to the bottom of a titanium pot are a real chore to scrub in the dark at ten degrees.
