Bean flakes and instant rice hydrate at different speeds, which is why the rice goes in first and gets the hot water to itself. Dump both at once and you get soft beans wrapped around hard rice grains.

The oil and cheese powder are the calorie load. Beans and rice alone are bulky and cheap but thin on fat, and after a long day thin is the wrong thing to eat. Chips go in at the end for salt and crunch that the pot cannot otherwise produce.
