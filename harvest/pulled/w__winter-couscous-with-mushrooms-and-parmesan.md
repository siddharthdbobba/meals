Couscous is already cooked and dried, so it only needs to sit in hot water. That means one short boil instead of a long simmer, which is the whole argument for it in cold weather: less fuel, less time standing over a stove with your hands out.

The mushrooms and bouillon do the work a stock would do at home. Oil and cheese are the calories. Add them off the heat so the fat coats the grain instead of scorching to the pot.
