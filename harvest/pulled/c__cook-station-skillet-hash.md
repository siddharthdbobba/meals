The whole recipe hinges on getting the water out. Dried potatoes need a soak to become edible, but any liquid left in the pan turns the hash into paste. Rehydrate first, drain hard, then brown. Two separate steps, not one.

Summer sausage does the work bacon would do at home without needing a cooler. It renders enough fat to help the crust along, and the oil covers the rest. A wide pan on a stable two-burner station matters more than heat here: crowded potatoes steam each other and never crisp.
