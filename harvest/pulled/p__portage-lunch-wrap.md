This is lunch for a day when stopping means pulling onto a gravel bar, not making camp. No stove comes out, which matters on moving water where you want the boat repacked and back in the current in ten minutes.

The bean flakes are the load-bearing part. They rehydrate in cold water, they thicken the filling so it does not run out of the tortilla and into the bilge, and they turn a 2.5-ounce fish packet into something that holds you until dinner. Oil is where most of the calories live. Chips go on at the last second for texture, since everything else in the bag is soft by design.
