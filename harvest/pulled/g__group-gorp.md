Gorp works because nobody has to plan it. Each person owns one ingredient, which means no single shopping trip and no leader doing math. The mix comes out different every time and it is always roughly right, because the categories take care of themselves: something salty, something sweet, something fatty, something with bulk.

The ratios matter more than the specific items. Nuts and seeds are the calories, dried fruit is the sugar, pretzels and cereal are volume so a handful is not overwhelming. If the group brings four kinds of candy and no nuts, it is a bag of candy. Assign items instead of asking for volunteers, and cap the chocolate at one cup per gallon so it does not glue the rest together in a warm pack.
