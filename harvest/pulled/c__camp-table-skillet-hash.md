The crust is the whole point, and you only get it if the potatoes are drained. Rehydrated hash browns hold more water than they look like they do, and wet starch steams instead of frying. Drain, then leave the pan undisturbed. Stirring is what turns this into mush.

Shelf-stable sausage does two jobs: protein, and rendered fat that seasons the pan before the potatoes go in. Dried onion and pepper flakes rehydrate in the same water as the potatoes, so there is no second pot and nothing fresh to keep cold.
