Home dehydrating is worth the oven time here because pasta and beans are mostly water anyway. Drying them removes the weight you were never going to eat and leaves something that comes back in ten minutes off the flame instead of twenty minutes of simmering fuel.

The rinse step on the beef matters more than the drying. Fat left in the crumbles will turn on you in a week, and a rancid dinner four days from a trailhead is a real problem. Lean meat, hot rinse, airtight bag. The oil goes in at camp, where it cannot spoil in your pack.
