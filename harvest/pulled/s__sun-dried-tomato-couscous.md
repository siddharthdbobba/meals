Couscous is the only grain that cooks entirely on residual heat, which matters when fuel is the thing you are actually rationing. One boil, five minutes under a lid, done. The tomatoes and chickpeas go in while the water is still hot because they take longer than the grain does; add them at the same time and you get soft couscous around hard beads.

The olive oil is not a garnish. Couscous and chickpeas are lean, and without two tablespoons of fat this lands closer to a side dish than a dinner. Stir it in at the end so it coats the grain instead of pooling at the bottom of the pot.
