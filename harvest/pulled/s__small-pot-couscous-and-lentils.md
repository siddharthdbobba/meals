The source here was a review of a small solo pot, not a recipe, so this is built around what that pot can actually do: one boil, one lid, no simmering. Couscous and lentil flakes both rehydrate off residual heat, which means the stove runs for about three minutes total and the fuel canister lasts the trip.

The sun-dried tomatoes are the reason this does not taste like wet sand. They give up acid and salt as they soften and do the job that fresh vegetables would at home. Insulating the pot for those eight minutes matters more than it sounds — below freezing, an uninsulated small pot drops out of the useful range before the lentils are done.
