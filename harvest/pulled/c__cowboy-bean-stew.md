This is car-camping food, not pack food. Nine cans and three potatoes weigh close to eight pounds, so it stays where a vehicle goes. What it buys you is eight dinners out of one pot with no measuring and almost no technique.

The canned liquid is doing the work. Draining the cans would leave you short on broth and force you to carry more water, so everything goes in wet and the half-can rinse gets the last of it. Condensed tomato soup is the thickener — it holds the whole thing together as the potato starch releases. Stir it often; a dutch oven over coals scorches from the bottom and you will taste it for the rest of the trip.
