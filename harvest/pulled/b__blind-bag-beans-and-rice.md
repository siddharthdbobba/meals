Beans and rice want twenty minutes and a lot of fuel. Instant versions of both want one boil and a lid. The rice goes in first because it needs the heat; the bean flakes go in last because they thicken on contact and will glue the rice together if you rush them.

The chips and the oil are the calories. Bean flakes are close to fat-free, so without them this lands around 500 calories and eats like a side dish. Crush the chips in the bag before opening it, or you will be fishing shards out of the pot.
