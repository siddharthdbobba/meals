Couscous is pre-steamed semolina, so hot water is doing all the work. That means one boil and a lid instead of a simmer, which is most of the fuel savings on a short trip. Take the pot off the flame before the grain goes in or the bottom scorches and you own that mess for the rest of the week.

The fat matters more than the grain. Couscous alone is a thin dinner, and the salami, parmesan, and oil are what turn it into a real one. Dry-packed sun-dried tomatoes rehydrate in the same five minutes and add the acid the dish otherwise lacks.
