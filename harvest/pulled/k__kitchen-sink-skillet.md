Every trip ends with a food bin holding half-portions of four things and a full portion of nothing. This is the pot that clears it. The structure is fixed — starch, protein, vegetables, fat, salt — and the specifics are whatever survived the week.

Order matters more than the ingredient list. Dried vegetables go in while the water is still boiling, because they rehydrate on heat and time, not on sitting. The starch goes in after, and the meat and oil go in off the heat so nothing scorches on the bottom of a thin pot. Salt at the end, once you can taste how much water the grain actually took.
