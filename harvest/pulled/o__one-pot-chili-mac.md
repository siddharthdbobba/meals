Pasta water is usually a waste product. Here it becomes the sauce. The bean flakes and tomato powder go in after the boil and thicken whatever liquid is left, so you never drain anything and you never carry a second pot.

TVP does the protein work without a cooler, and it rehydrates on the same timer as the macaroni, which is the only reason this stays a single pot. The oil goes in last because fat added early coats the pasta and slows how it takes up water.
