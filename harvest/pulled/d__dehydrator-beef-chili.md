Lean meat is the whole point. Fat does not dry, it goes rancid, so the beef gets cooked, rinsed hot, and dried at 145 F until it rattles. That leaves a protein brick with almost no calories of its own, which is why the oil goes back in at camp instead of at home.

The bean flakes do two jobs: protein and thickening. Added off the heat, they turn a thin broth into something that stays on a spoon. Add them while it is still boiling and you get glue stuck to the bottom of the pot.
