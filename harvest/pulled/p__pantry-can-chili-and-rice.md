This is a food-bank shelf turned into dinner. Canned beans, canned tomatoes, canned corn, and instant rice are the four things that are always there, and they happen to make a complete meal without a cooler or a second pot.

The trick is not draining the beans or tomatoes. That liquid is already salted and starchy, so it counts toward the water the rice needs and it thickens as the rice swells. Blooming the spices in oil first is the other half; dumped in dry at the end, chili powder tastes like dust. Instant rice matters here too, since regular rice would want twenty minutes and a lot more fuel.
