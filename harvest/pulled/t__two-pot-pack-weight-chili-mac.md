The bean flakes do two jobs. They rehydrate instantly in the pasta water, so nothing gets drained and no calories go on the ground, and they turn the leftover starch into a sauce thick enough to cling. Draining pasta on a trail is a waste of fuel and water; measuring the water right at the start avoids the problem.

Tomato powder replaces canned sauce, which is mostly water and glass. Cheese is the usual finish here and it is the first thing to skip when it is warm out, so the oil carries the fat instead. Meat comes from a foil packet because it needs no cooking and no cold storage.
