Hut breakfasts run on eggs, potatoes, and bacon, all of it hauled uphill on somebody's back. This is the same plate with the refrigeration removed. Dehydrated hash browns and egg powder weigh a fraction of the real thing and cook in the same pot, one after the other.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Potatoes need a long soak in boiling water; eggs need low heat and constant motion. Do them together and you get scrambled eggs studded with hard potato shreds. The cheddar powder and bacon go in last, off the flame, because both scorch and neither needs cooking.
