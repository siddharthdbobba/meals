Home-cooked sauce dries better than anything you can buy in a packet. Water leaves, flavor stays, and a serving that weighed most of a pound wet ends up around two ounces. The lentils are the reason it holds together as a dinner rather than a hot drink: they carry the protein and they come back from drying without turning to mush.

The pasta and the sauce flakes go in at the same time on purpose. Flakes need longer in water than they look like they should, and the pasta's boil time covers it. Dry the sauce thin and check for soft spots before you bag it. A single damp patch will spoil the whole portion and you will not find out until you open it on day four.
