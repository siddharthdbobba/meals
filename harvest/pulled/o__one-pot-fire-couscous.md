Couscous is the only grain that cooks itself off the heat, which is the whole point when your fuel is a fire you have to feed. Boil once, kill the heat, wait five minutes. A 1 L pot over coals is enough hardware for the entire meal, and nothing sits over flame long enough to weld to the bottom.

The dried mushrooms and onion go in before the grain because they need the full boil to rehydrate, while the couscous only needs the residual heat. Oil and cheese go in last, after the pot is off the fire, so the fat coats the grain instead of frying out of it. Both are carrying most of the calories here.
