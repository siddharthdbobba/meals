This started as a home breakfast with fresh berries and milk. Neither travels, so both got replaced: dried fruit rehydrates in the pot while the oats soften, and whole milk powder does the job of cream without a cooler. Dried elderberries need real heat, which is why this is a boil-and-sit recipe rather than a cold soak.

The fat is the part people cut, and it is the part that matters. Oats and fruit alone come to a thin 400 calories. Walnuts and a spoon of oil roughly double it and slow the crash two miles down the trail. Sugar is there to balance the berries, which dry tart.
