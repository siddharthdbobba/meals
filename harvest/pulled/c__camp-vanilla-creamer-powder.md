The original is milk, cream, maple syrup, and vanilla shaken cold and kept in the fridge. None of that travels. The fix is to carry every ingredient in its dry form: milk powder for the milk, cream powder for the heavy cream, granulated maple sugar for the syrup, and vanilla bean powder instead of extract. Extract is mostly alcohol and water, which is weight you do not need, and the ground bean carries more of the flavor anyway.

Cream powder is the part worth buying. Milk powder alone dissolves into thin, chalky coffee. The added fat is what makes it read as creamer instead of powdered milk. The pinch of salt is not in the source recipe, but it sharpens the maple and covers the flat note milk powder tends to have.
