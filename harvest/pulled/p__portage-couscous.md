Couscous does not cook, it rehydrates. That is the whole reason it belongs in a boat or a pack: one boil, no simmer, no fuel burned watching a pot. The hummus powder is doing double duty here, thickening the water into something that clings and adding the protein couscous does not have.

The sun-dried tomatoes want the full five minutes under the lid or they stay leathery. Lemon powder is not optional filler either. Without acid the whole pot reads as flat starch, and a half teaspoon weighs nothing.
