Couscous is the fastest carb you can carry. It does not simmer, it rehydrates, so the stove runs for one boil instead of ten minutes of babysitting. That matters more than it sounds when fuel is finite and the wind is up.

The sun-dried tomatoes go in with the water rather than after, because they need the full soak to stop being leathery. Oil and the foil packet go in last, off heat, so the fat stays on the grain instead of scorching. Skip the oil and this is a side dish, not dinner.
