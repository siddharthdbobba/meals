Couscous is the fastest hot dinner that still eats like a real meal. It never touches a simmer, so the fuel cost is one boil and nothing after that. That matters at the end of a long day when the canister is light and you are not interested in tending a pot.

The tomatoes go into the water rather than the dry mix on purpose. They need the full boil to soften; added later they stay leathery. Oil and cheese are the calories here, and they also give the grain enough fat to keep from drying into sand by the second half of the pot.
