Couscous is the fastest hot dinner on the trail. It is not really cooked, it is soaked, so the stove runs for two minutes instead of ten and a small canister lasts a week. Rehydrating off the heat also means nothing scorches on the pot bottom, which is most of the cleanup problem solved before it starts.

On its own couscous is starch and not much else. The hummus mix is the fix: powdered chickpea thickens the whole pot and brings the protein up to where dinner belongs. Sun-dried tomatoes need the hot water more than anything else here, so they go in first while the water is still near boiling. The oil goes in last, off the heat, because that is where a third of the calories live.
