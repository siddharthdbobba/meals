Integrated canister stoves boil fast and simmer badly. The burner sits close under a narrow pot, so anything left on the flame scorches in a ring at the bottom. This meal is written around that limitation: the stove's only job is to produce 350 ml of hot water, and the actual cooking happens off the burner in a bag.

Fine couscous rehydrates on residual heat alone, which is why it is the base rather than pasta or rice. Freeze-dried chickpeas come back in about the same five minutes and carry most of the protein. The oil goes in last, after the water is absorbed, so it coats the grain instead of floating on top. Skip the sun-dried tomato and you have a bowl of salty starch.
