The source of this one was a boat cook poaching fish on a rack over half an inch of water in a covered skillet. That trick does not survive a backpack, so the fish here comes out of a foil pouch and is already cooked. What carries over is the method: a small amount of water, a tight lid, and residual heat doing the rest. Couscous needs no boil at all once it is wet, and the salmon only needs to warm through.

Keeping the fish on top instead of stirring it in is the difference between salmon and salmon paste. It breaks apart on the last stir, when the couscous is already set. The oil goes in off the heat because it is fat, not a cooking medium, and it is where a third of the calories live.
