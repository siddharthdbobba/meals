Wild game that came out strong tasting is not ruined, it is just loud. Drying concentrates that, so the fix on trail is dilution and acid. Mushrooms and bouillon give the broth a savory floor to sit on, and the vinegar at the end cuts the gaminess in a way salt cannot.

The potato flakes do the structural work. They turn a thin, jerky-flecked broth into something with body, and they hold the oil in suspension instead of letting it pool on top. Chop the jerky small at home. Whole strips will still be tough after ten minutes of simmering, and simmering longer is fuel you do not have.
