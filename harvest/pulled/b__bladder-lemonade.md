Sweat is not just water. Replacing it with plain water dilutes what is left, which is why people drink four liters on a hot day and still cramp. The sugar is not there for calories. Glucose and sodium get pulled across the gut wall together, so a small amount of sugar makes the salt and the water absorb faster than either would alone.

The ratios matter more than the flavor. Roughly a half teaspoon of total salt per liter, and no more than about three tablespoons of sugar. Push the sugar higher and the drink turns hypertonic, which drags water into your gut instead of out of it. If you run this through a bladder, rinse the hose at home the same day. Sugar water sits in a bite valve and grows things.
