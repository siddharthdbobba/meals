Salt is the whole schedule here. It pulls water out of cucumber and radish within an hour, and a bag of salted slices becomes a bag of brine with limp things floating in it. Dressing without salt keeps the slices crisp for two or three days in a cold cooler, so season it in your hand at camp, not in your kitchen.

The sugar is not there for sweetness so much as to keep the vinegar from being the only thing you taste. Slice thin: raw radish is tough enough that a thick coin stays a raw radish, while a thin one takes on the dressing. Dried dill is a fine trade if fresh herbs will not survive the drive.
