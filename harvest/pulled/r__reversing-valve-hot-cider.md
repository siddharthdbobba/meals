Hot sugar water is the cheapest way to make a cold camp bearable, and the reason to premix it at home is that measuring spices by headlamp at 20 degrees does not happen. Two packets instead of one is deliberate. One packet in 350 ml tastes like apple-scented water.

The dried apple is not garnish. It rehydrates in the two minutes the drink is cooling to a drinkable temperature, and it turns a cup of liquid into something with a few bites in it. Ginger and salt are there because sweet alone gets cloying by the third night out.
