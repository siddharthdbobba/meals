Couscous is the fastest hot dinner that still eats like a meal. It is pre-steamed semolina, so it only needs to rehydrate, which means one boil and no simmer. That matters more than it sounds: simmering is where you burn fuel and where food welds itself to a thin pot.

The hummus mix is not there for flavor alone. Couscous on its own is thin protein and thinner body, and the chickpea powder fixes both while thickening the pot into something that stays on a spoon. Oil goes in at the end, off heat, because that is where most of the calories are and heating it just costs you volume.
