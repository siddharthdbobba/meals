Beans are the load-bearing ingredient. Instant refried flakes rehydrate in under two minutes and thicken whatever else is in the pot, so the rice does not sit in a puddle. The order matters: rice first, off the heat, then beans, or the flakes seize into a paste before the grains have softened.

Canoe camping tempts you into hauling a cooler and cooking like you are at home. This is the version that survives a portage. Everything is shelf-stable, the whole thing needs one boil, and the pot wipes clean with a chip and a splash of water because nothing ever touched a hot surface long enough to weld itself on.
