This is built around the mug, not the coffee. A double-walled 20 oz mug with a gasketed lid holds two cups, and the second one is still drinkable an hour later, which is the whole point on a morning where you are packing a boat instead of sitting still. A thin single-wall metal cup dumps its heat in ten minutes and leaves you with tepid coffee, which is worse than either hot or cold.

The mixing order matters more than the ingredients. Milk powder dropped into a full mug of boiling water seizes into lumps that never break up. Making a slurry with a small amount of water first gets you a smooth cup every time. The pinch of salt is not a garnish either: it blunts the bitterness of cheap instant coffee, which is the only kind worth carrying.
