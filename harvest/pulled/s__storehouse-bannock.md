AMC's high huts get bulk flour and sugar by helicopter because those are the things nobody wants to carry uphill. What the kitchens do with them is bread, baked fresh because it cannot arrive fresh. Bannock is the one-pan version of that idea: the same four staples, no oven, no rising time.

Baking powder does all the lift, so there is nothing to wait for and nothing to keep alive. Powdered milk replaces the fresh dairy a real kitchen would use and adds enough fat and protein to make it hold you. Low heat matters more than anything else here. A hot pan gives you a black crust over raw dough, every time.
