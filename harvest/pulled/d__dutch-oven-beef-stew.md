Half a cup of water per person sounds like nothing, and that is the point. The beef browns and sweats in a shallow puddle rather than boiling, which is where the flavor comes from. The vegetables release more liquid once they go in, so the pot ends up as stew even though you never poured in a quart of water.

Order matters. Beef gets the first 20 minutes alone because it needs the time; potatoes and carrots need half that and turn to mush if you give them more. This one stays a car-camping recipe — it needs a cooler for the raw beef and a real bed of coals. The canned-beef variation is the version that goes in a pack.
