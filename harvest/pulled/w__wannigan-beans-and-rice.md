This is barrel food. Every component sits on a shelf for months, none of it needs cold storage, and the whole thing runs on one pot and a single boil. Bean flakes are the reason it works: they thicken the pot without simmering, which matters when your heat source is a fire that is either roaring or dying.

Browning the sausage first is not a garnish step. It renders fat into the pot, and that fat plus the oil is where most of the calories come from after a day of paddling. Add the beans slowly. Dump them all at once and you get a dense brick you have to chip apart with a spoon.
