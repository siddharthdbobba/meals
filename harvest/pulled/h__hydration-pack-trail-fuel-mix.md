This is a no-cook snack built around the reality of a hydration pack: you drink constantly and eat nothing, then bonk at mile eight. A mix you can reach without stopping fixes that. The pretzels and the added salt are doing the real work, replacing the sodium you lose when you can sip without ever putting the bottle down.

Fat carries the calories. Peanuts, pumpkin seeds, and coconut push this past 600 per serving in four ounces, which is a better ratio than any bar. Chocolate is the failure point in heat, so cacao nibs are the summer swap.
