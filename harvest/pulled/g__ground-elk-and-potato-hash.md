Elk is lean to the point of being a problem. Almost all the fat comes off at the butcher, which is what makes it dry well and what makes it taste like nothing on its own. So dry it thin, cook it long enough to actually soften, and add the fat back yourself with oil.

Dehydrating ground meat is the whole trick. Brown it at home, rinse the grease off in hot water, spread it on trays, and dry it hard. Grease is what turns dried meat rancid in a pack, so rinsing is not optional. The potato flakes go in off the heat and thicken the gravy into something you can eat with a spoon instead of drink.
