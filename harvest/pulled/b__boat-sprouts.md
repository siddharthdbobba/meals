Long trips run out of fresh food before they run out of days. Sprouting is the one way around that: the beans weigh almost nothing dry, need no soil, no light, and no cold, and they gain water weight only as you eat them. Sailors have run this on multi-week passages using a pan and a towel. A jar works the same and spills less.

Drainage is the whole recipe. Beans that sit in water go sour and slimy within a day, so the rinse matters more than the soak. Use drinking water, not raw lake or salt water. The oil and acid at the end are doing what dressing does anywhere: sprouts alone are damp and bland, and fat is what turns them into lunch.
