A small solo pot holds less than you think, which changes how ramen behaves. Half a liter of water and a full seasoning packet is a salt bomb, so use half and save the rest. Breaking the block also matters: a whole disc floats above the waterline in a narrow pot and cooks unevenly.

Ramen alone is about 380 calories and almost no protein. The foil packet of fish or chicken is what makes it a dinner instead of a hot snack, and it needs no refrigeration before opening. The oil goes in last because heat drives off what makes sesame oil worth carrying.
