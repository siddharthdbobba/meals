Couscous is the only grain that cooks on residual heat, which means one boil and roughly two minutes of fuel for the whole dinner. The five-minute lid rest is doing the actual cooking, so resist opening the pot early.

Jerky rehydrates poorly in one sitting, which is why it goes in during the simmer and gets cut small at home. Even then it stays chewy. That is fine here — it is the protein and the salt, and the couscous around it stays soft. The oil goes in at the end because heat is not needed to melt it and stirring it into a hot pot early just makes the grain gummy.
