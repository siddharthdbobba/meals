Cutting the ears into thirds is the whole point. Full-length ears lie flat and take a wide pot with a lot of water to cover; stubs stand on end, so a 12-inch dutch oven holds four ears' worth in maybe a third of the water. Less water means less fuel and a faster boil.

The corn finishes off the heat. Once the pot is boiling hard enough to spit, there is enough stored heat in that much cast iron and water to cook sweet corn through in four or five minutes. Leave it on the coals and you get mush. This one needs a cooler and a real fire, so it is a car-camping or basecamp dish, not something to carry.
