The oven version depends on dry heat from all sides, which no campfire gives you. Foil solves it a different way. The packet traps the moisture already in the broccoli and steams it, and the direct contact with the coals browns whatever touches the bottom. You get both textures out of one packet.

Slice the lemon thin enough and you eat it, rind and all. Thick slices stay bitter and leathery, so use the sharpest knife in the kit. This one needs a cooler, so it belongs to car camping and basecamps, not a pack. Nothing here is shelf-stable and there is no way to make broccoli so.
