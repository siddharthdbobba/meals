This one lives or dies on the coals. Flames scorch the foil in one spot and leave the middle raw, so let the fire burn down and cook over red embers. The wet paper towel between two sheets of foil is doing the same job an oven does: it turns direct radiant heat into steam heat, so the vegetables cook through before the outside chars.

Dicing size matters more than seasoning. Potato and carrot take longer than chicken strips, so cut them small enough to finish in the same 25 minutes. This is cooler food, not pack food, which is why it lives in the car-camping column. Cleanup is the packet itself: pack the used foil out, it does not burn.
