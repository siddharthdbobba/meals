This is a slow-cooker dish moved onto coals. The original relies on a countertop appliance holding a low temperature for hours; a dutch oven with coals on the lid and almost none underneath does the same thing, and ninety minutes gets you most of the way there because canned beans are already cooked. All the long cook is doing is reducing the liquid and pulling the sausage fat and paprika through it.

Everything here is shelf-stable, so no cooler. Canned beans are heavy, which is why this is a car-camping recipe and not a backpacking one. The bean liquid matters: drain it and you lose the starch that thickens the pot, and you end up spooning beans out of water.
