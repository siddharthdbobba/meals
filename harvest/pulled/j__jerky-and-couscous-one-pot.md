Couscous is the whole reason this works on a small fuel budget. It needs steeping, not simmering, so the stove runs about two minutes and then sits cold while the pot does the rest. That two-minute boil is really for the jerky and the dried vegetables, which are the slow parts.

The jerky is the protein and most of the salt, so go light on the bouillon if yours is a salty brand. Oil goes in at the end, off the heat, because it coats the grains instead of frying into the bottom of the pot. Fluffing matters more than it sounds: unstirred couscous sets into a single block.
