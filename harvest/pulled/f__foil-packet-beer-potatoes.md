The liquid is the whole point. Potatoes on coals want to scorch, and a can of tomatoes plus half a beer keeps the packet steaming instead of burning. It is a pressure cooker made of foil. That is also why the seams matter more than anything else you do here.

Red potatoes hold their shape through forty minutes of hard heat where a russet turns to mush. Garlic goes in whole because whole cloves go sweet and soft in that time rather than burning the way minced garlic would. Skip the fresh herbs if you are packing light; dried rosemary loses less than dried basil does.
