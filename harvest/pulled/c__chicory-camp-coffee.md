Chicory root is not coffee, but it roasts dark and brews bitter in the same register, and it stretches an expensive instant coffee ration by a third. It also has no caffeine, so a heavier scoop of chicory makes an evening mug that will not keep you awake.

The milk powder is doing more than flavor. Fat carries the roasted notes that instant coffee loses in freeze-drying, and it gives the drink enough body that it reads as a cup rather than as brown water. Whisk the paste before adding full water or you will be chewing lumps.
