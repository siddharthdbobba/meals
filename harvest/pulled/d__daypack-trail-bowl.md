Couscous is the only grain that cooks on stored heat alone. Boil, dump, lid, wait — the fuel bill is one boil, which matters more on a day out with a small canister than on a trip where you are running the stove anyway.

The oil is not optional. Couscous and tuna together are lean enough to leave you hungry an hour later, and fat is the cheapest weight you can carry. Sun-dried tomatoes do the work a fresh vegetable would, and the acid at the end keeps the whole bowl from tasting flat.
