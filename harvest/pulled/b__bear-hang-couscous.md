The source page was about hanging food away from bears, not about cooking, so this is the dinner built to suit that constraint: everything for one meal lives in a single sealed bag that goes straight up the line. Couscous is the key. It is precooked semolina, so cold water alone will soften it given an hour, which means no stove, no pot, and no greasy cookware to store overnight.

Oil and tuna carry the calories and the protein, and both come in packets that seal shut again. Sun-dried tomato and bouillon replace the salt and depth you would normally get from simmering. Soak it in your pack while you walk and dinner is finished by the time you stop.
