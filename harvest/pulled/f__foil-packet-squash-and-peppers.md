Summer squash is mostly water, which is the whole problem and the whole solution. Sealed in foil it steams in its own liquid, so you never add any. Cut too thin and it collapses into mush. A quarter inch holds its shape through twenty minutes on coals.

The source version was a fry-up — egg wash, flour, a pan. None of that travels. The foil packet gets the same result with no pot, no oil to pour off, and nothing to scrub. Salt everything as you layer rather than at the end; it draws out the water that does the cooking.
