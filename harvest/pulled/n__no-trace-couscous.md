This exists because of the packing-out problem. Meals that need draining, rinsing, or scraping generate waste that smells, and waste that smells is a critter magnet in camp all night. Couscous solves it: it absorbs every drop of water you put in, so there is nothing to pour off and nothing left in the pot but what your spoon can reach.

The premix does the rest of the work. Tomato powder and bouillon build a sauce out of nothing but the soak water, and the TVP rehydrates in the same ten minutes the grain does. Olive oil goes in last, off the heat, because it is most of the calories here and you want it coating the food rather than cooking off. One bag in, one flat empty bag out.
