This is a long simmer, not a quick one, and the hour matters. Canned tomatoes taste raw and sharp straight from the can. Held at a low temperature under a lid, they break down and the chili powder stops sitting on top of everything. The sugar is not there for sweetness — it is there to blunt the acid so the beans read as savory.

The dutch oven does the work that a stove burner cannot. Coals on the lid heat from above, so the top does not skin over while the bottom scorches. Keep the temperature down after the boil and stay off the lid. Every lift dumps heat you then have to rebuild.
