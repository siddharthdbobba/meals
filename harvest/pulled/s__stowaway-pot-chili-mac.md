A 1.1-liter pot holds less than it looks like it does. Pasta foams, and foam is what climbs the wall and puts out your stove. So this uses 475 ml and a low simmer rather than a hard boil, and the starchy water stays in the pot instead of getting drained off. That starch is half the sauce.

The bean flakes do the thickening and carry most of the protein. They hydrate in seconds off heat, which is why they go in last, along with the cheese and tomato powders. Dump powders into boiling water and you get lumps with dry centers. Fat comes from the oil; without it this is a thin, gritty bowl.
