Couscous is the fastest starch you can carry. It does not cook so much as swell, which means one boil and a covered pot do the whole job with no simmer time and no fuel burned watching a pot.

The tomatoes go in with the water on purpose. They need the full five minutes to soften, and the soaking liquid picks up their salt and acid, which is most of what makes this taste like a meal instead of wet semolina. Oil and cheese go in last, off the heat, so the fat coats the grain rather than cooking into it.
