This is the dinner that gets forgotten in the worst way. Cook pasta on a trip without vegetables in the bag and you get a bowl of starch and salt, which is fine on night two and grim on night six. Half a cup of dehydrated vegetables weighs almost nothing and is the difference.

The cooking water becomes the sauce, so measure it rather than guessing. Tomato and milk powder thicken what is left, and the fat has to go in last, off the heat, or it splits and sits on top. Everything here is a boil-water operation, which is what keeps fuel use honest on a long trip.
