Cold weather eats calories faster than walking does, and the meal that gets eaten is the one that needs almost nothing from you. This is a boil, a wait, and a stir. Jerky and dried vegetables go in the water early because both are stubborn; rice goes in last because it is not.

The gravy mix is doing two jobs. It seasons, and its starch binds the loose water so you get something that holds on a spoon instead of a pot of wet rice. Add it off the heat or it will scorch on the bottom. The oil is not optional in the cold: it is a third of the calories and the only fat in the pot.
