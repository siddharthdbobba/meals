Dehydrated hash browns are the whole point. They weigh almost nothing, they keep forever in a hot vehicle, and once rehydrated they crisp in oil the same way fresh shredded potato does. The ten-minute soak is not optional, and neither is draining. Excess water is the difference between a hash and a pan of grey mush.

The chicken goes in last because it is already cooked and only needs to warm through. Hard cheese stands in for the sour cream this kind of skillet usually gets at home; it melts into the fat and does the same job without a cooler.
