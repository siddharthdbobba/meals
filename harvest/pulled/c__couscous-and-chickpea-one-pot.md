Couscous is the fastest starch in a pack. It does not need to simmer, so you spend fuel on one boil and nothing after. That matters more than it sounds on a long trip.

The hummus powder is the structural piece. On its own, couscous absorbs water and stays loose and dry. The chickpea flour thickens what is left into a sauce that grabs the oil, which is where most of the calories live. Skip the oil and this is a side dish, not dinner.
