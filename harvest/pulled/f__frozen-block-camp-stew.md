This is a winter sled meal, not a backpacking meal. It is cooked at home, frozen, and hauled in, which means sixteen ounces per serving of mostly water. On a sled that is fine. On your back it is not, so dehydrate it instead if you are carrying it.

The shape of the frozen block is the whole recipe. A block frozen in the pot itself has surface contact everywhere and thaws in twenty minutes; a random lump takes closer to an hour and burns a lot of fuel doing it. Also move your stove between meals. It melts a pit into the snow, and by the third night that pit holds enough meltwater to drown the flame.
