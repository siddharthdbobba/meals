This is a coal-bed recipe, not a stove recipe. A dutch oven holds enough heat that a full 8 cups keeps simmering even after two pounds of frozen fish goes in, which a lightweight pot would not. Keep the fire moderate and the boil low. A hard boil shreds white fish into the broth.

The order matters more than the timing. Fish first, because it needs the longest and it seasons the water. Noodles second, since they only need a few minutes and will turn to paste if they sit. Frozen vegetables last, because they are already cooked and only need warming. The ramen seasoning is the entire salt and flavor budget here, so use all four packets.
