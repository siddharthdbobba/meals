Couscous is the only grain that cooks on residual heat alone. You boil once, kill the flame, and the pot does the rest under its lid, which matters more than it sounds when a canister is down to its last few ounces. Everything else in the bag is chosen to rehydrate in that same five minutes: the chickpeas and tomatoes are already dried thin, so they come back in the time the grain takes.

The oil and cheese are the calories. Couscous is cheap, light, and mostly starch, so without fat added at the end this is a side dish rather than a dinner. Add them off the heat, not during the boil, or the cheese clumps and the oil just floats.
