Chili in a dutch oven is a heat management problem more than a cooking problem. You need two temperatures out of one fire: hot enough to brown meat, then low enough to hold a bare simmer for an hour. Get the coal bed built early and pull coals off rather than adding them, since taking heat away is faster than making more.

The hour is not optional. Chili powder tastes raw and dusty until it has had real time in liquid, and the jalapenos need it to go from sharp to round. The tomato juice is the only liquid here, which is why the lid stays on and the stirring stays regular. This one keeps its cooler slot for the beef, so cook it the first night.
