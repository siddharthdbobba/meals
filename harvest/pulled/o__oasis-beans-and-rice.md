Bean flakes are the whole reason this works on a stove that only gets one boil. They rehydrate in about two minutes with residual heat, while the rice needs a real soak, so the order matters more than the timing does. Rice first, off the flame, then beans as a thickener.

Dried beans and rice from a home kitchen would need twenty minutes of simmering and a lot of fuel. Instant versions of both cut that to a single pot of hot water. The oil is not optional padding: beans and rice are lean, and without added fat this lands near 500 calories, which is not a dinner after a long day.
