This is a house trick moved outdoors, and the changes are all about what survives a pack. Fresh orange and fresh cranberries are mostly water and mostly weight, so both go dried here. Dried peel throws more oil per gram than a fresh slice anyway, and the cranberries are there for color and a little tartness in the steam more than anything else.

The real fuel cost is the simmer, not the boil, so keep the flame at the lowest setting that holds and expect to add water twice. In bear country be honest about what you are doing: this is a scented pot in camp, which is the same as cooking. Run it at the kitchen area, well off from where you sleep, and pack the wet solids out with the rest of the trash.
