This is a burrito with the tortilla and the refrigerator removed. Bean flakes and instant rice both rehydrate off residual heat, so the stove runs for one boil and then stops. That matters more than it sounds: fuel is the thing you actually ration on a long trip.

The order is the whole recipe. Rice first with the lid on, beans second off the heat. Reverse it and the bean flakes seize into paste before the rice has softened, and no amount of stirring brings it back. The oil goes in last because fat coats the starch and stops it setting up into a brick.
