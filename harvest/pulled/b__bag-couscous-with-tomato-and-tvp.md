Couscous is the only grain that cooks on residual heat alone. It is already steamed and dried at the factory, so hot water finishes it in ten minutes without a flame. That is the whole reason this is a bag meal rather than a pot meal: nothing here needs to simmer, so nothing needs scrubbing afterward.

The TVP and the sun-dried tomatoes are the two things that make it a dinner instead of a side. TVP triples in volume and brings the protein up to something that matters after a long day; the tomatoes carry acid and salt that bouillon alone cannot. Cut the oil and you lose roughly 240 calories, which is most of the point of carrying it.
