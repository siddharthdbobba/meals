Slow cookers work because they hold beans at a low temperature for hours. On a trip you do not have the hours or the outlet, so the soak does the work instead. Beans that sat in water since breakfast finish in about an hour over a fire, which is the difference between dinner and going to bed hungry.

Everything fresh in the original has a dried equivalent that rehydrates in the broth: onion, celery, carrot, kale, parsley. The ham bone is the one real loss. Vacuum-packed ham or a hard summer sausage gets you the salt and the fat, and the smoked paprika covers the smoke the bone would have given up. Hold the salt until the beans are soft, since salting early keeps the skins tough.
