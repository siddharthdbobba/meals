This is built around the constraint, not around the dish. A 750 ml pot holds about 350 ml of water before a rolling boil starts climbing the walls, and couscous is one of the few starches that turns into dinner at that ratio. It needs no simmer, so the stove runs for five minutes and then stops.

Dried vegetables go in during the boil, not after. Couscous absorbs the water almost instantly once it hits the pot, and anything added later has nothing left to rehydrate in. The oil is not optional either. Couscous and tuna together are lean, and a tablespoon is most of what makes this a dinner instead of a side.
