The whole trick is that yeast does not care where it rises. Mixing dry at home
and hydrating at camp means you carry flour, not dough, and the overnight rise
happens while you are asleep anyway.

Do not skip the honey butter and call it a savings. Plain fried flatbread is
filler; with the glaze it is the reason people get out of the bag in the morning.
