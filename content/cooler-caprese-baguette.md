There is a specific pleasure in eating well on the first day, before the food gets
boring and everything starts tasting like the inside of a stuff sack. This is that
meal. It requires a cooler, it does not survive to day two, and it is worth planning
the first day around.

The pressing step is not optional. It is what turns a pile of ingredients into a
sandwich.
