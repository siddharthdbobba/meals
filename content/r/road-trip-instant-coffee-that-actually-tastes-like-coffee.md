Two things separate camp instant coffee from bad camp instant coffee, and neither is the brand. The first is water temperature. Water straight off a rolling boil scalds the coffee solids and pulls out a burnt, bitter edge, so give the pot a minute off the heat before it hits the mug.

The second is blooming. Dumping powder into a full mug of water leaves undissolved clumps and a gritty layer at the bottom; making a paste with a splash of water first dissolves everything and gives you an even cup. The salt is not for flavor exactly, it suppresses bitterness, which matters more with instant than with brewed. Full-fat milk powder replaces the creamer no one wants to carry a cooler for.
