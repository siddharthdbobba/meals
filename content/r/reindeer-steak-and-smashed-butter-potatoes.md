Two things make this work, and neither is the seasoning. First, the potatoes are already cooked when they reach camp, so the pan is only browning them. Second, the meat comes off the coals early. Reindeer and venison carry almost no fat, so the window between medium rare and shoe leather is short and the fire will not warn you.

The berries are the balance. Crushed raw with a spoonful of syrup, they stay sharp and cut the butter. Cooking them turns the whole thing into jam, which is the wrong direction. This one needs a cooler and real fire, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp, not in a pack.
