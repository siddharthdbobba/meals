The sheet pan version roasts potatoes, asparagus, and a fillet in stages at 425F. None of that survives a stove with one burner and one pot, so the parts get swapped: potato flakes for diced Yukon golds, freeze-dried asparagus for fresh, pouched salmon for raw fish, dried rosemary and garlic powder for the fresh infusion. What carries over is the shape of the flavor, which was always oil, rosemary, garlic, acid.

The mustard and balsamic go on at the end, off heat. They are the sharp edge against a pot of starch and fat, and boiling them just flattens them out. Rehydrate the asparagus before the flakes go in, otherwise it stays leathery in a bowl of paste.
