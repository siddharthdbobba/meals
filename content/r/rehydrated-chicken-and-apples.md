Freeze-dried chicken is shelf-stable protein. The apples soften into the broth as the pot sits, adding body, while parmesan adds fat and salt. You end up with a meal rather than a cup of hot water with bits in it.

Forty grams of protein keeps you going through a long day better than carbs alone. Freeze-dried ingredients work anywhere—just add hot water and wait fifteen minutes.
