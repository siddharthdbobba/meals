This started as an air fryer recipe, which is not a thing you carry into the woods. The method transfers cleanly to a grill grate over coals because the crust does not need hot oil to set. Crushed ramen is mostly fried wheat already, so it browns on dry heat and holds a crunch that plain breadcrumbs will not.

The marinade is the other half of the job. Wild turkey breast is very lean and turns to rope if you overcook it, so the acid in the dressing plus a few hours in the bag buys you some margin. Do all the cutting, marinating, and crushing at home. In camp you are coating and grilling, nothing else. This one needs a cooler, so it is a first-night dinner.
