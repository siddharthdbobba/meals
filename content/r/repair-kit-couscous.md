Couscous is the fastest hot dinner on a stove that runs on fuel you have to carry. It does not simmer. It sits off the heat in a covered pot and rehydrates on its own, which means one boil and no watching a flame.

The salami and oil are doing the calorie work. Couscous by itself is a starch with nothing behind it, and it goes down like paste. Fat and cured meat give it something to be. The sun-dried tomatoes need the boiling minute up front or they stay leathery in the finished pot.
