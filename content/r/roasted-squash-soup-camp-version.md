The original is a sheet-pan soup: roast squash, carrots, onion, tomato, and a whole head of garlic, then blend with five cups of broth. None of that survives a pack. The roasting still matters, though, so it happens at home. Roast the vegetables the same way, blend them, spread the puree on dehydrator trays, and dry it to a leather. Ground down, that leather is the entire soup minus water.

Garlic powder replaces the roasted head, and tomato powder replaces the fresh tomato, because both were mostly there for depth rather than texture. The oil goes in last and off the heat: it is a third of the calories and the only thing keeping this from being a hot drink instead of a dinner.
