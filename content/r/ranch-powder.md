The source recipe leans on yogurt, sour cream, mayonnaise, and buttermilk, none of which survive a warm pack. What travels is the part that was already dry: seven herbs and spices in a jar. Everything else gets rebuilt in camp from milk powder for body, oil for fat, and citric acid for the sour edge that dairy would have brought.

The ratio of water to powder is the whole game. Two tablespoons of water to two of milk powder gives a dressing that coats; more and it runs off the food and pools in the bottom of the bowl. Mix it before you cook dinner so the parsley and dill have time to soften.
