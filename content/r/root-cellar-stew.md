Root vegetables are what a cellar is full of in February, and they are also what dries best. Sweet potato, carrot, onion, and turnip lose most of their weight as water and take it back in a pot of hot water. A pound of garden harvest ends up around two ounces in your pack.

The lentils are doing the protein work, and they happen to soften on the same schedule as thin-sliced roots, so it stays one pot and one boil. Potato flakes go in off the heat at the end. Add them early and you get glue instead of stew.
