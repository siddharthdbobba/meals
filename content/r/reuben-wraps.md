Corned beef and sauerkraut both hold a lot of water, which is why this needs a home dehydrator and not a shopping trip. Once they are dry they weigh almost nothing and rehydrate together in the same pot, since the kraut brine flavors the beef as it softens. Ten minutes off the heat under a lid does the work that would otherwise cost you fuel.

The cheese is the only piece that has not been dried, and Babybel is the reason this works without a cooler. The wax shell holds up for several days in a pack. Add it after the boil so it melts into the mixture instead of scorching on the pot bottom, and keep the dressing cold-side until the end so it does not break in the heat.
