The home version of this leans on four fresh peppers and a blender. Neither travels. Dehydrated pepper strips carry the same sugar and color at a fraction of the weight, and instant potato flakes do the blender's job — they bind the liquid and give the soup body without any dairy.

Simmer time is not optional. Dried peppers stay leathery if you rush them, and the tomatoes need the full fifteen minutes to give up their flavor into the water. Toast the almonds at home; a camp pot busy with soup is not the place for it.
