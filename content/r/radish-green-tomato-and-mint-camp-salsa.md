This is day-one food. Nothing in it is shelf-stable past the first afternoon, so it belongs in a cooler-adjacent car camp or the top of a pack on a short trip. What it buys you is acid and bite, which is exactly what freeze-dried dinners and canned beans are missing.

Radish greens are the point. They carry the same pepper as the root and usually get thrown out. Cut them thin and the tough ones stop being a texture problem. The oil is not optional either: it holds the lime and salt onto the leaves instead of pooling at the bottom of the bag.
