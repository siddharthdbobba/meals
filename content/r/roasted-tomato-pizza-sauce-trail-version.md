The source recipe is a water bath canning project: ten pounds of roma tomatoes roasted, milled, jarred with bottled lemon juice for acidity. None of that travels. What survives the trip to a backpack is the flavor logic, so this rebuilds it from dried parts.

Tomato powder does the roasting work in advance, since drying concentrates sugars the same way an oven does. The vinegar goes in off the heat and replaces the canning lemon juice, which existed for safety rather than taste but sharpened the sauce anyway. Fat matters here: without the oil the sauce tastes thin and flat, and the herbs need something to dissolve into.
