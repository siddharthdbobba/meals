This is a hot dog wearing a Reuben's clothes. The parts that make a Reuben read as a Reuben are the sauerkraut and the pink dressing, and both of those survive a cooler far better than corned beef and rye do. The paprika does the color and most of the smell; the sour cream carries it.

Draining the kraut is the step people skip. Sauerkraut is mostly brine, and brine goes straight through a bun in about a minute. Squeeze it in a paper towel or press it against the jar lid until it stops dripping. If you are going without a cooler, sour cream powder rehydrated with a splash of water makes the same sauce and asks nothing of your ice.
