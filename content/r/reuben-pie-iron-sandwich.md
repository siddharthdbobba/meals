A pie iron is a small sealed press, which is why a Reuben works in one. The two cheese slices go on the outside of the filling, top and bottom, so they melt against the bread and glue the sandwich shut. Kraut in the middle would otherwise steam the bread into mush.

Drain the sauerkraut hard before it goes in. Wet kraut is the single reason these come out soggy. Deli sliced corned beef matters too: the canned kind breaks down under heat and turns the middle to paste. This one needs a cooler, so it is a car camping meal, not a pack meal.
