This is a plain dinner and it is meant to be. Two weeks of paddling means two weeks of the same bag, and the whole design is one boil, five minutes of fuel, and under two dollars a serving. The second seasoning packet is what keeps that from tasting like water: the extra half cup of TVP soaks up salt that would otherwise flavor broth you are only going to drink.

TVP is the reason this hits 24 grams of protein at four and a half ounces packed. It rehydrates in the same three minutes the noodles need, so nothing gets a separate step. Moringa powder stands in for vegetables at a tablespoon and no real weight, which is the trade if dried and freeze-dried greens do not agree with you. Calories are the weak point at around 500. Add the oil and you are closer to 640, which is what most people actually want after a long day.
