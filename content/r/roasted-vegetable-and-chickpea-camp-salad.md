The original is an oven recipe with a bowl of leaves under it. Neither travels. Foil packets over coals do the same job the oven did, and the greens get dropped because they wilt into slime in a cooler within a day. What is left is the part that actually holds up: vegetables cooked until their sugar browns, beans for substance, and an acidic dressing to keep it from tasting flat.

Salting and draining the eggplant at home matters more than it sounds. Unsalted eggplant releases water in the packet and everything else poaches. The chickpeas go in off the heat because they are already cooked and only need to warm through and soak up dressing. Feta is left out of the base recipe on purpose. It is a cooler item and it is the only thing standing between this and a vegan dinner.
