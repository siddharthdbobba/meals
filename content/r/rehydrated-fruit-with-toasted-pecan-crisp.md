Soft rehydrated fruit against crunchy toasted topping is the whole appeal. The butter does the real work at home, helping the oats and pecans brown instead of just crisping dry. By the time you reach camp, the topping is ready to go—nothing but boiling water and waiting.

You can eat this as breakfast or dessert depending on how your legs feel that day. The pecans give it protein, and the volume of fruit makes it feel substantial without the weight. Keep the fruit and topping in separate bags or they'll lose the crunch in minutes.
