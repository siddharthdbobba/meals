Red lentils are the only lentil worth carrying. Split and hulled, they cook in about ten minutes instead of forty, and they collapse into their own sauce so you never need a thickener. The whole dish is one pot and one boil.

The protein is stacked deliberately. Lentils bring roughly nine grams per half cup cooked, the TVP adds most of the rest, and the hemp seeds finish it with fat and a few more grams. Tofu was the obvious protein in the original but it is water and refrigeration, so it went. Add the hemp off the heat: it is there for fat and texture, and boiling it just makes it taste like dust.
