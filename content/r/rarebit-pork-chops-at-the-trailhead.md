Welsh rarebit is a cheese sauce stiff enough to sit on top of something instead of running off it. That is the whole trick here. The yolk and the cream bind the cheddar so it browns rather than pooling into the coals, and the mustard and Worcestershire cut fat that would otherwise flatten out after a long day.

This is cooler food, not pack food. Pork chops and fresh cheese do not go up a mountain. What makes it work at a campsite is that the topping is mixed cold in a mug and the whole cook is two stages over the same coals: hard heat to sear, low heat with a lid to melt. Keep the crust away from direct flame or the cheese scorches before it blisters.
