This is a snack you build once at home and eat out of for a week. The seeds carry the calories and almost all of the protein; the freeze-dried fruit and vegetables are there for crunch and sugar, since they weigh almost nothing and never go soft in a hot car.

Salt is the part people skip. Two cups of seeds without it reads as bland by the third handful, and on a long driving day or a hot hike the sodium is doing real work. The paprika is optional but keeps the mix from tasting like dessert.
