At home this is leftovers plus stock, blended. On trail there are no leftovers and no blender, so the roasting happens before the trip: toss squash, carrot, and onion in oil and spice, roast them, then dry them flat until they snap. Roasting first is the part worth keeping. It caramelizes the sugars, and that flavor survives the dehydrator, which plain dried vegetables do not.

The soaking time matters more than the boiling time. Dried roasted vegetables are dense and will stay gravelly if you rush them, so give them the full ten minutes off heat with the lid on. A spoon does the blender's job well enough, and the potato flakes cover for whatever the spoon misses. The oil goes in last because it is most of the calories here and it does not need to be cooked.
