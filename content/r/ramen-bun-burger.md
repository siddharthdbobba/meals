This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. The noodle buns need a press and a cold rest, the beef and mayo need a cooler, and none of that shrinks into a pack. Trying to trail-ify it would leave you with a different dish.

The egg is structural. It sets around the noodles as they fry and turns a loose tangle into a cake that survives being picked up. Press them under weight while they cool or they will fall apart in the pan. The vinegar cucumber is not garnish either, it is the only thing cutting a patty built on soy sauce and sesame oil.
