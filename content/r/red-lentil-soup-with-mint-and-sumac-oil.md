Red lentils are the reason this works on a stove that burns fuel by the minute. They have no skins to soften, so they fall apart in about twenty minutes and thicken the pot themselves. No blender, no straining, no second dish.

The original finishes with mint and sumac sizzled in butter. Butter is a problem in a pack, so olive oil does the job and the spices go in off the heat instead, where they won't scorch. Sumac carries the sour that a fresh lemon would, which is why it is worth the half ounce of jar space. Salting at the end matters: bouillon is built to be salty on its own.
