This started as a campfire skillet with a pound of raw hamburger and fresh vegetables, which is fine off a raft with a cooler and nowhere near workable out of a pack. Everything perishable got swapped: the meat is browned, drained, and dried at home, the vegetables come dried or freeze-dried, and the tomato is a powder instead of a can. Condiment packets do the work of a bottle of ketchup at a fraction of the weight.

The potato flakes go in last and are the reason this eats like hash rather than soup. Dried meat needs the full fifteen minutes of simmering to soften all the way through, so resist thickening early. Draining the fat before dehydrating matters more than it sounds: fat left in the meat goes rancid in a few weeks, and the oil you carry separately replaces those calories without the risk.
