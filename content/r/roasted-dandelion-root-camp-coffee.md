Chicory and dandelion root got mixed into coffee because coffee was expensive, and the habit outlived the shortage. On trail the arithmetic is weight, not money: roasted root brings a dark, faintly bitter body to a weaker pour than you would tolerate at home, so a smaller scoop of coffee still tastes like a full cup.

The original is brewed and served with fresh milk. Powdered whole milk does the same job here and needs no cooler. The important part is the order: steep and settle in the pot, then finish in the mug. Milk powder added to grounds turns into gritty lumps you will be chewing at the bottom.
