Red lentils are the only legume worth carrying for a one-pot dinner. They break down in under ten minutes without soaking, and as they break down they thicken the water into an actual stew rather than leaving you with beans in broth. Everything else in the bag is powder and dried vegetable, so the whole thing rehydrates in the same 300 ml.

The cured sausage is doing two jobs: fat, which lentils have none of, and salt, which you want after a long day. It goes in at the very end because it is already cooked and boiling it just leaches the fat out into the pot. Smoked paprika is what keeps this from tasting like plain lentil mush, so do not cut the amount.
