Ramps are a short-season plant and they are strong. Fat is what makes them usable in quantity: the cream cheese holds the sulfur compounds and spreads them across a whole cracker instead of hitting you all at once. The olive oil is not filler either, it thins the block enough to spread without a knife or a bowl.

The home version calls for a fridge, and this one does not. Softened cream cheese is fine at ambient temperature for a day, and shelf-stable cream cheese or the powdered kind carries it further. Bacon bits are already dry and salty, so they do not add moisture to something you want to keep for a couple of days. Harvest ramps by cutting one leaf and leaving the bulb; patches are slow to recover.
