The Mountain House chicken and mashed potatoes base provides protein and salt in a single packet. The rice noodles add bulk and calories without a separate pot. The freeze-dried broccoli contributes texture and color without requiring any work.

The method here is soaking, not cooking. Two five-minute waits let the noodles and broccoli rehydrate completely while you sit, which beats standing over a burner and makes cleanup trivial. The sweet-savory flavor comes from sugar balancing soy sauce; garlic and cayenne add depth without fussiness.
