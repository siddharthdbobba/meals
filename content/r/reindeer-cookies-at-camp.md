This is decoration work, not cooking. The only heat involved is melting chocolate, and that is where it goes wrong at camp: a stove under a thin pot will scorch chocolate in under a minute. Keep the flame at its lowest, pull the pot early, and let the residual heat finish the job. The spoonful of shortening thins the melt so it coats in one dip rather than sitting on the cookie in a lump.

Everything here is already shelf-stable, which is why it travels at all. The assembly-line order matters more than technique. Dip, place, prop the antlers, then noses and eyes last. Set chocolate holds the whole thing together, so anything that looks unstable while wet is probably fine in twenty minutes. This is a car-camping dessert; the cookies are too fragile to survive a pack.
