The original is a stovetop soup built on fresh vegetables and homemade stock, which is a non-starter three days from a trailhead. Bouillon replaces the stock, tomato powder replaces the can and the paste, and dried vegetables replace everything that used to live in a crisper drawer. The lentils are the one thing that did not need changing.

Red lentils are the reason this works on a stove that burns fuel by the minute. They cook in about twelve minutes and then fall apart, which thickens the pot without any flour, dairy, or extra step. The instant rice absorbs whatever water the lentils do not, so you get a stew rather than a bowl of broth with things in it. Simmering, not just steeping, matters here — lentils will not soften in a cozy.
