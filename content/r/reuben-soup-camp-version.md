The original is a stovetop cream soup with three cups of half and half in it, which is a non-starter anywhere without a fridge. Whole milk powder mixed into cold water does the same job and carries dry. The roux is what holds it together, so cook the flour in the fat before any liquid goes in, and keep the pot off a hard boil once the milk is added or it will break.

Drain the sauerkraut harder than you think you need to. The brine is aggressive enough to curdle the milk and to bury the beef, and the kraut still brings plenty of acid after a squeeze. The rye replaces the broiled bread-and-cheese lid from the original, which needs an oven you do not have.
