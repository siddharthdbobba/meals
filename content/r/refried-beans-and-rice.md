Freeze-drying preserves beans without the heat damage that makes commercial dehydrated versions taste burnt and bitter. The process removes water without scorching the beans, so these actually taste like refried beans instead of charred powder.

Instant rice and freeze-dried beans rehydrate at the same rate, which is why this works as a one-pot meal. Five minutes under cover with boiling water and it's done. The recipe is light on fat, so any oil or cheese you carry is worth its weight.
