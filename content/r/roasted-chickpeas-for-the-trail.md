This started as a kitchen snack and needed almost nothing to become a trail one. Chickpeas roast down to something dense and dry, which is exactly what survives in a pack: no moisture left to spoil, no crumbling like chips do, and around 9 grams of protein per handful.

The drying step is the whole recipe. Wet chickpeas steam themselves in the oven and stay leathery, and leathery chickpeas mold. Get them dry, roast them until they crack, and cool them all the way before the bag closes.
