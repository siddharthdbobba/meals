The original is two russets, a pot of salted water, twelve minutes of boiling, and a ricer. None of that travels. Instant flakes are already riced potato, dried, so the texture problem the ricer solves is solved before you leave home. What you actually have to replace is the butter and cream, and that is what powdered whole milk and butter powder are for.

Mix off the heat and add the dry in stages. Potato flakes hydrate fast and unevenly, and once a lump forms in a camp pot it stays a lump. The oil goes in last because fat coats the starch and slows absorption if it is in the water first.
