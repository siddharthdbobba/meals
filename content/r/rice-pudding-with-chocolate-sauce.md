The original is a baked rice pudding: raw rice simmered slowly in fresh milk, thickened with eggs, then finished in an oven. None of that survives a trail kitchen. Instant rice and whole milk powder do the same job in ten minutes on one burner, and the eggs go away completely — the starch coming off the rice is what thickens this, so you do not need them.

Make the sauce first, in the lid or a mug, while the pot is still cold. Chocolate mixed into boiling milk seizes into lumps, and once it does you are not getting it back. Keep stirring during that first minute on the flame too. Milk powder sticks and burns faster than anything else you will cook out there.
