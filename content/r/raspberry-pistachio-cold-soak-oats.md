The original is a fridge recipe built on fresh berries, milk, and Greek yogurt. None of that travels. Freeze-dried raspberries rehydrate into something close enough, whole milk powder carries the fat, and vanilla whey covers both the tang and the protein the yogurt was providing.

Crushing half the berries matters more than it sounds. Powdered fruit bleeds through the whole jar overnight and thickens the soak, while the whole berries stay intact enough to bite. Leave them all whole and you get plain oats with red lumps in them.
