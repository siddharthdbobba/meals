This is a home chili with the water taken out of it. Nothing about the cooking is unusual; the trail part happens on the dehydrator trays and depends on two things. Drain the beef hard, because fat does not dry and it is what turns a bag of chili rancid in a pack. Spread everything thin, because a thick layer dries on the outside and stays wet underneath, and that is the batch that spoils.

Beans and ground beef rehydrate at different speeds, which is why the cold soak comes before the boil. Ten minutes in cold water gets liquid into the beans before the heat sets anything, and the simmer afterward is short. Skipping the soak and going straight to a boil gives you hot sauce with gravel in it. Everything here is already gluten-free; check your chili powder if that matters, since some blends carry flour.
