Fish oil is a cooking fat, not waste. Heating it with garlic before any water goes in is what separates this from a tin dumped on noodles: the garlic gives up its flavor into the oil, and the oil then coats everything the water touches. Cold garlic in a cold pot brought up slowly extracts more than garlic thrown into hot oil, which just scorches.

The sardines go in last and stay whole. Boil them and they shred into the broth and turn woolly. One minute under the lid off heat is enough to warm them through. The tin is heavy for what it delivers, so treat this as an early-trip dinner and let the lighter meals ride further into the week.
