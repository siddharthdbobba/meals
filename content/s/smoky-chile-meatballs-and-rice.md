The spice mix does the work here, so mix it at home. Measuring cumin, coriander, paprika, cayenne, and garlic powder separately in camp is five containers and five chances to spill one. Skip commercial taco seasoning: it carries starch and sugar that turn the pan sauce gummy.

Raw beef means this is a cooler meal, day one or two. Freeze it flat and it chills the rest of the cooler on the drive in. If you want the same dinner without a cooler, use precooked shelf-stable meatballs, bloom the spices in the oil for a minute, then add the tomatoes and let the meatballs heat through in the sauce.
