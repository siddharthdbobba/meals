The original is a fridge recipe: fresh onion, a bag of baby spinach, half a lemon. None of that survives a weekend in a hot car. Dried onion, dried spinach, and bottled lemon juice do the same jobs and weigh a fraction as much, so the only heavy things in the pack are the cans.

Order matters more than technique here. The spices need thirty seconds in hot oil before liquid arrives or they stay dusty, and the spinach goes in at the very end because dried greens turn to sludge if they simmer. The lemon is not a garnish. Tomato and chickpea both go flat without acid, and it goes in off the heat so it does not cook away.
