The original is an Instant Pot appetizer built on a bag of frozen meatballs, which is two things a pack does not have: a fridge and 1200 watts. Freeze-dried or home-dried meatballs solve the first, and a simmer solves the second. The sauce was already trail-ready — soy, sugar, garlic, ginger, chile — so it converts to powders almost one for one.

The cornstarch slurry is the part worth keeping. Rehydrating meatballs takes more water than you want left in the pot, and the slurry turns that surplus into a glaze instead of a puddle you have to drink or dump. Mix it in cold water first; dropped in dry it seizes into lumps that never dissolve.
