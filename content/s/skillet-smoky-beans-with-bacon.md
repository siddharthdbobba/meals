This is a car-camping side, not a pack-in meal. Two cans of beans and a cooler item are heavy, and there is no way around that. What you get for the weight is a pot that feeds six off one burner and holds heat long enough that it is still warm when the grilled food finally comes off.

The cooking is mostly about heat control. Brown sugar and ketchup scorch fast, so the pan wants coals or a moderate flame, never a tall fire. Cast iron helps because it evens out hot spots, and the browned crust you scrape off the bottom is where most of the flavor ends up. Twenty minutes of slow simmer is what turns canned beans into something that tastes cooked rather than opened.
