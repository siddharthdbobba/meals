Goat cheese collapses the second it gets warm, which is the whole problem with cooking it. Freezing the balls solid at home buys you the minute or two it takes for the panko to brown before the inside turns to a puddle. Skip the freezer and you will be eating crumb-flecked cheese sauce out of the pan.

The original bakes these in an oven. A skillet over a low flame does the same job with less gear, but it needs your attention: the oil browns panko fast, and low heat is the difference between a crust and a scorch. This is a cooler recipe and a first-night recipe. Goat cheese does not keep past that.
