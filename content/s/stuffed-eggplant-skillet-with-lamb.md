The original bakes lamb filling inside hollowed eggplant shells for half an hour. There is no oven on a trail, and no whole eggplant worth carrying, so this rebuilds the same flavors as a skillet stew. Dried eggplant slices rehydrate into something soft and a little creamy, which is most of what the shells were doing anyway.

The soak before the simmer matters. Eggplant is slow to take on water, and if you go straight to a boil you get leathery pieces in a thin sauce. Tomato powder and oil supply the moisture and richness that the pan juices provided in the baked version; the pepper flakes stay firm enough to give the pot some texture.
