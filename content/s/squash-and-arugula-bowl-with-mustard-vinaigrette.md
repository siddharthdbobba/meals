Squash is one of the few vegetables worth carrying whole. It does not bruise, it does not need a cooler, and it takes a hot grate better than anything else in the produce aisle. Cut it at home so you are not doing knife work on a picnic table in the dark.

The dressing does double duty: half of it is a marinade that helps the cut faces brown, half of it goes on at the end where the acid still tastes like something. Dropping the squash on the arugula hot is the point. The greens collapse just enough to stop being a salad and start being dinner. The parmesan and pine nuts are what keep this from being watery, so do not leave them behind.
