The original is a fridge sauce built on fresh chili, garlic, and ginger. Dried versions of all three work here because the sauce boils for long enough to rehydrate them, and the vinegar and sugar do most of the flavor work anyway. Nothing in the finished sauce needs cold storage: the acid and the sugar concentration are the preservative, which is why the sugar quantity looks absurd and should not be cut.

Cornstarch is the only fussy part. Dumped in dry it seizes into lumps you will never stir out, so it goes in as a cold slurry, off a rolling boil, poured slowly. It also has to boil for a minute or two afterward or the starch never sets and you get sweet vinegar. Make it at the trailhead or on night one; it keeps a couple of weeks in a bottle out of the sun.
