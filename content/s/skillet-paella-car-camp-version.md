Paella is a rice technique wearing a seafood costume. The technique is what travels: short grain rice, hot seasoned liquid added once, and no stirring after that, so the grains cook in place and the bottom layer catches. A cast iron skillet on coals does this better than a stove burner because the iron spreads heat instead of concentrating it under one spot.

Everything perishable got swapped. Canned shellfish for fresh, since raw shrimp and live mussels are a cooler problem you do not need. Onion flakes and sun-dried tomatoes for the fresh versions, olive oil for the butter, freeze-dried peas for frozen. The canned seafood is already cooked, so it goes on at the end under foil and just warms through. Add it early and it turns to rubber.
