The street version leans on fresh cucumber, tomato, and onion, none of which travel. What actually carries the dish is the spice blend and the texture contrast, and both of those are dry goods. Freeze-dried corn rehydrates cold in about ten minutes and comes back with real sweetness, which is more than can be said for most dried vegetables.

Potato flakes replace the boiled diced potato. They do not look like cubes, but they thicken the water into something that clings to the corn instead of pooling at the bottom of the bag. The sev is the whole point of the last step: add it early and you get soggy noodles, add it last and you get the crunch the dish is built around.
