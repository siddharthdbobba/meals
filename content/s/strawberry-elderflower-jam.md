Jam is a ratio problem. Strawberries are low in pectin and high in water, so the only things holding this together are sugar, acid, and time over heat. The lemon supplies the acid that lets the little pectin there is actually set, and it keeps the color from going brown.

Elderflower is added at the end on purpose. The aromatics are volatile and boil away, so ten minutes in a cheesecloth bundle gets the muscat smell into the jam without the green, bitter taste that comes from cooking stems. Cook this in camp only where you have a real pot and a cooler; hot sugar and a wobbly stove is a burn waiting to happen.
