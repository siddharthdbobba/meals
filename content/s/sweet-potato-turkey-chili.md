The original is a Dutch oven dish with a fresh onion, a whole sweet potato, and two cans. None of that goes in a pack, so every wet ingredient here is its own dried version: tomato powder for the can, dried dice for the sweet potato, freeze-dried crumbles for the ground turkey. The spice ratios are unchanged, including the cinnamon, which is the part worth keeping. An eighth of a teaspoon reads as depth rather than dessert, and it makes cheap chili powder taste like it cost more.

The sweet potato sets the timing. It is the only ingredient that genuinely needs to cook, which is why it goes in alone first and why the dice has to be small. Everything else is just rehydrating. The oil goes in off the heat because this is otherwise a very lean dinner and fat is where the calories that carry you to the next morning actually live.
