This is a car-camping soup, not a pack soup. Fresh kale and raw sausage need a cooler, and eight cups of stock is weight nobody carries. What it buys you is a full dinner for eight out of one pot in about half an hour, which is the right trade on a Friday night when the tents are barely up.

The sun-dried tomatoes matter more than they look. Canned tomatoes bring acid and liquid but not much depth; the dried ones bring concentrated sugar and salt that stand up to the sausage fat. Beans do the thickening, so the broth ends up with body without any flour or dairy in it.
