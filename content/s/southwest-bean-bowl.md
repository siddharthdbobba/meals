The original is a fridge-clearing salad: lettuce, avocado, fresh tomato, bottled dressing. None of that carries. So the lettuce becomes crushed corn chips, which give you the same job — bulk and crunch — while adding fat instead of water weight. Avocado is gone entirely; olive oil in the dressing covers the same ground and does not turn brown in your pack.

Cold soaking works here because beans and corn are the only things doing real rehydration, and both are already cooked before drying. The ranch powder has to be mixed separately with water and oil first. Dumped in dry, it grabs onto the beans in lumps and you spend the meal chewing seasoning.
