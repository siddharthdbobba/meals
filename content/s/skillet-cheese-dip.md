Cheese does not want to become a sauce. Heat it alone and the fat separates out into a greasy puddle with rubber in the middle. The cornstarch tossed through the shreds is what prevents that: it holds the emulsion together while the cheese melts. Evaporated milk helps for the same reason, and it comes in a can, which is why this works in camp at all.

The two things that break it are heat and impatience. Keep the skillet low and keep the whisk moving. The grainy stage looks like failure and is not. Grate at home, because grating a cold block over a picnic table is slow and the shreds melt faster than chunks.
