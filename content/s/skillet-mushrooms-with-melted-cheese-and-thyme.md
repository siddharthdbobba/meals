The original is an oven dish: raw mushrooms under a broiler until the cheese bubbles. Camp has no broiler, so the work moves to a skillet, and the order changes. Mushrooms are mostly water. Broiling drives that water off upward; a covered pan traps it, and you end up with cheese floating on mushroom liquid. So the mushrooms get browned dry first, and the cheese only goes on at the end.

Fresh mozzarella balls are also out. They carry too much water for a cooler and release it all into the pan. Low-moisture block mozzarella melts the same way and holds up for days on ice. Thyme is the only seasoning that matters here, and dried works fine since it sits in hot oil long enough to open up.
