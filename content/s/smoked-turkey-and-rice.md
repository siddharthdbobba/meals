Smoked turkey breast is shelf-stable protein that arrives at camp already cooked and seasoned. The work here is just breaking it into pieces and mixing it into warm rice. The bouillon cube and measured water means the rice cooks in one step without simmering or stirring—this is pure efficiency for tired legs.

The turkey carries enough fat from smoking that you might skip the oil if you're light on weight, but it's there if your calories are running short. The black pepper and garlic echo the brining spices from the smoking process. If you made the turkey yourself, allspice brings back that exact flavor.
