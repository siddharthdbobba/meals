The original is a skillet dish built on fresh bell peppers blistered in oil and a can of crushed tomatoes. Neither travels. Dried peppers rehydrate into something close to roasted, and tomato powder does the work of the can at a tenth of the weight. Fresh Italian sausage becomes dry-cured salami, which needs no cooking and no cooler.

The capers and cayenne are what keep this from tasting like generic red sauce, so do not drop them for weight. Couscous goes in last and off the heat, because it only needs to sit in hot liquid, and boiling it turns the whole pot to paste.
