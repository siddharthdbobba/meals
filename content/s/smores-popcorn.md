This is a home-prep snack, not a camp cook. The point is getting s'mores flavor out of a bag on a fire-ban night or halfway up a climb, without a stick, a fire, or sticky hands. Everything happens on a kitchen stove days before the trip.

The marshmallow is glue, not filling. Melting it off-heat matters: butter and sugar over a live burner turn brown and grainy in under a minute, and grainy marshmallow will not coat popcorn. Chocolate goes in last and cold-sets, so the chips stay chips instead of a smear. Recognize that this snack has a temperature ceiling. Above the mid-80s it goes soft and welds into one lump, which is edible but not portionable.
