The flavor packet is the weak part of any ramen, so it goes in the trash and bouillon plus fajita seasoning takes over. The dried lime is what keeps it from tasting flat: acid at the end does the work salt would otherwise have to do, and powdered lime weighs nothing.

A teaspoon each of four dried vegetables sounds like a rounding error, but they swell to several times that volume and give the broth something to chew. The corn chips go on last, dry, and stay crisp for about a minute. That is the point of them.
