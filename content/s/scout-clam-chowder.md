Real chowder wants cream, fresh potatoes, and a fridge. This version gets there with powder. The milk powder rehydrates into the dairy body, the potato flakes thicken without any peeling or boiling, and the leek soup mix does the aromatic work that would otherwise mean an onion and a cutting board.

The clam juice is not optional. It is the only actual seafood stock in the pot, and draining the can leaves you with milky potato soup. Bacon bits and crackers go on at the table rather than in the pot, since both turn to mush the moment they sit in liquid. Serves four out of one pot, so it fits car camping or a basecamp night better than a solo stove.
