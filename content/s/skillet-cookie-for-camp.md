This is the standard skillet cookie with two changes for camp. Fresh eggs and cold butter do not travel, so the eggs are powdered and rehydrated, and the butter is canned or replaced with refined coconut oil, which stays solid enough at camp temperatures to cream into the sugar. Everything else is dry goods that keep indefinitely.

The hard part is heat control, not the dough. Coals give you even radiant heat; open flame burns the bottom before the middle sets. Cover the skillet so the top cooks too, rotate it, and start checking early. The 15-minute rest matters more than it sounds — cut it hot and you get a pan of crumbs instead of wedges.
