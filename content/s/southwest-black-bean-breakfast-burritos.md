This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Eggs and cheddar want to stay cold, so it belongs on a car-camping or basecamp morning where a cooler is thirty feet away. Beating the eggs at home into a bottle is the part that saves the most time and mess in camp.

The foil does two jobs. It holds the roll shut so the burrito survives being handled around a fire ring, and it lets you warm one or two at a time as people wake up instead of serving everyone at once. Keep the packets near coals rather than over flame; the tortilla scorches long before the center is warm.
