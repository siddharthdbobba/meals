The original version of this is a cooler recipe: fresh sausage, a fresh apple, butter. None of that travels well past day one, so all three got replaced. Hard summer sausage browns nearly as well as raw sausage and needs no refrigeration. Dried apple rehydrates in the pan and holds its shape better than fresh, which tends to collapse into juice. Ghee stands in for butter and keeps for weeks.

Cooking the sausage and apple first is the point of the one-pan order. The fat and browned bits stay in the pan and end up in the polenta. Add the polenta slowly and keep stirring, because it thickens fast and will catch on the bottom the moment you walk away. If it stiffens past the point of eating, a splash of hot water loosens it.
