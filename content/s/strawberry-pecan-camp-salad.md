This started as a home salad, so most of the work was making it survive a day in a pack. Fresh strawberries bruise and weep, so freeze-dried slices carry the same flavor at a fraction of the weight and no mess. Garlic powder replaces the minced clove because nobody is chopping garlic at a trailhead. The greens have to be sturdy ones; delicate mixes are already wilted by the time you stop for lunch.

The dressing is the part that actually matters. Mustard emulsifies the vinegar and oil so it stays mixed in the bottle instead of pooling, and the sugar takes the edge off the balsamic. Goat cheese is the only cooler item here, and it is the one thing standing between this and a fully shelf-stable lunch. Leave it out and the salad still works.
