This is the standard s'more taken apart and rebuilt as a shared pan. Roasting marshmallows one at a time on a stick is slow and half of them end up on the ground. Melting the chocolate and marshmallow together in a skillet means one heat cycle for the whole group, and the dipping keeps everyone out of the fire.

The cast iron matters. It holds coal heat evenly and gently, so the chocolate melts instead of seizing while the marshmallow tops brown. Set the pan on coals, not flames, or the bottom layer burns before the marshmallows do anything. The dippers are all shelf-stable and need no prep, so the only real work is timing the fire.
