Fresh strawberries are a bad trail food. They bruise in a day, weigh mostly water, and go to mush at the bottom of a pack. Freeze-dried slices keep the flavor and drop the weight, which is the whole reason this works out of a bag instead of a cooler.

Strawberries alone are about fifty calories a cup, so they are flavor, not fuel. The coconut chips and pumpkin seeds carry the fat and protein, and the salt keeps the fruit from tasting flat after a few hours of sweating. Crush the fruit at home on purpose: broken pieces distribute through the mix instead of floating on top and getting picked out first.
