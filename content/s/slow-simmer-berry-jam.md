This started as a slow cooker recipe, which is the wrong appliance for a campsite. The principle survives: low heat, no added water, a long uncovered reduction. All the liquid comes out of the fruit, so the flavor concentrates instead of getting diluted and boiled off again.

Sugar at roughly half the fruit's weight is what makes it keep without a fridge for a few days, and the lemon juice supplies the acid berries are short on, which is what lets it set. Cheap out on either and you get warm syrup. Watch the bottom of the pot near the end; jam scorches in the last five minutes, not the first thirty.
