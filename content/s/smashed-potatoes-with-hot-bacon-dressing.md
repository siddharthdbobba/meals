Camp cooking usually fails at potatoes because people try to do the whole thing over flame. Boiling first is the fix. The simmer cooks the inside where the fire cannot reach, and the smash gives you flat faces that go crisp in a few minutes instead of twenty.

The dressing exists because bacon grease is a free ingredient you already made. Vinegar and mustard cut it, honey rounds it off, and pouring it on warm means it soaks in rather than sitting on top. Everything but the potatoes and bacon is shelf-stable, so the cooler only has one job.
