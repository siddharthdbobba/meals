The original cooks in jars in a slow cooker overnight, which assumes an outlet and fresh apples. Neither travels. Dried apple rings do the same job here — they rehydrate into soft fruit and pull sweetness back out of the water instead of leaving it behind. Maple sugar replaces maple syrup because granulated sugar mixes into a dry bag and does not leak.

The rest of the change is heat. Rolled oats do not need a long simmer; they need hot water and a lid. Five minutes off the flame gets you the same texture for a fraction of the fuel, and the pecans stay crisp because nothing simmered them soft.
