This is a real yeast bread, so it needs time rather than skill. The potato flakes are why it works in camp: they hold water in the crumb and keep the rolls soft for a day even after a rough bake over uneven coals. Bread flour matters too, since the extra gluten tolerates the sloppy kneading you will do on a picnic table.

Fresh milk and a fresh egg do not travel, so the dough is built from milk powder and egg powder with plain warm water. Honey goes into the dough as well as the topping, which feeds the yeast and gives you enough sugar for browning under a lid where you cannot see anything. Keep more coals on the lid than under the pan, or the bottoms burn before the tops color.
