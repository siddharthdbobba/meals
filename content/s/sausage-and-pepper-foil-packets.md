Foil packets solve the group dinner problem: everyone gets their own, nothing needs plating, and the cookware count is zero. Slicing the sausage on a diagonal is not decoration. It opens more surface to the heat so the links cook through in the same time the peppers soften, instead of leaving you with raw centers.

Seal the packets tight. The vegetables release a lot of water, and that trapped steam is what actually cooks the inside while the bottom browns against the coals. A packet that leaks dries out and burns. Fresh sausage and peppers need a cooler, so this is a first-or-second-night meal; the cured sausage variation is the version that keeps.
