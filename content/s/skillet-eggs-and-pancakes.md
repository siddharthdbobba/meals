Freeze-dried eggs reconstitute quickly and don't need a cooler. Shelf-stable bacon gives you real smoke and meat fat you can't get from the trail diet. Cook sequentially in one pan: eggs, then pancakes, then bacon. You have time on a car-camping morning.

Bacon is the key. It brings fat and salt to what would otherwise be carbs and water. The syrup is dead weight anywhere else, but weight means nothing when parked. One pan, one fire, and you've got breakfast that tastes like home.
