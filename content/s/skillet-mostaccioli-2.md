The oven version bakes to melt cheese and set the top. There is no oven, so the pot does both jobs: the residual heat under a lid melts mozzarella in about five minutes, and the sauce thickens on its own once the pasta stops releasing water. Drain hard before the sauce goes in or you get soup.

Fresh onion and garlic are replaced with dried minced onion and garlic powder, which rehydrate in the sausage fat and sauce. Low-moisture mozzarella and canned parmesan survive a day or two out of a cooler; if the trip runs longer, move this meal to the first night.
