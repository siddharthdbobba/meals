The oven version of this is a casserole held together by eggs and baked in a cast iron pan. Neither of those travels, so the eggs are gone and the structure comes from the potato itself. Dehydrated mashed sweet potato rehydrates into roughly the same texture the casserole had, and it does it in three minutes off the heat instead of thirty in an oven.

Butter and whole milk become ghee or butter powder and milk powder, which is where most of the calories still live. The pecans are the whole point of the original dish and they survive the trip fine, as long as they stay in a separate bag. Sugar in the mix, heat and salt on the nuts.
