Couscous is really just wheat that has already been cooked and dried, so it needs steeping rather than simmering. That means one boil, then the stove is yours again for coffee while breakfast finishes under a lid. The dried apples go in with the hot water, not after, so they get the full five minutes to soften.

The walnuts and butter are what make it a meal instead of sweet porridge. Fat and a little protein hold you past the first hour of walking, and the nuts stay crunchy only if they never sit in the pot. Keep them in their own bag and add them at the bowl.
