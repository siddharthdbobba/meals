The original is a crockpot dish: dry beans, eight hours, a potato masher at the end to thicken it. None of that travels. Bean flakes get you to the same place because they are already cooked and mashed, so the thickening the masher was doing happens the moment they hit hot water.

The vegetables need a head start. Freeze-dried mirepoix rehydrates slower than the flakes do, and if you dump everything at once you get soup with hard confetti in it. Smoked paprika and bacon crumbles cover for the hours of low simmering you are not doing. Lime and cilantro go in last, off the heat, or the acid flattens out.
