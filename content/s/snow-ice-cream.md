This is the one recipe where the bulk of the ingredients is already on the ground. The base is nothing but sweetened milk, and the snow does the job that a churn and a freezer do at home: it aerates and freezes at the same moment you stir it in.

The original called for fresh milk, which does not survive a trip. Powdered whole milk reconstituted with cold water gets you the same fat and body without a cooler. Two things matter: use dry powder snow, because crusted or wet snow melts into slush on contact, and make the base sweeter than seems right. The snow dilutes everything, and a base that tastes correct in the pot tastes like nothing once it is folded in.
