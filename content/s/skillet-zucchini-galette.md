This started as an oven recipe and an oven is the one thing a campsite does not have. A cast iron skillet with coals under it and coals on the lid gets close enough. The heat is less even, so the quarter-turn rotation matters more than any other instruction here.

Roasting the zucchini first is not optional. Raw squash releases a lot of water, and that water has nowhere to go but into the crust. Fifteen minutes in the dry pan drives most of it off. The cheese layer underneath the filling is doing the same job from the other side, sealing the dough against whatever moisture is left.
