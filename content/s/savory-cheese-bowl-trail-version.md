The original is a fridge dish: wet curd, raw vegetables, no cooking. The curd is the problem on trail, so this uses freeze-dried cottage cheese, which rehydrates cold and keeps most of the protein. That protein is the whole point — close to 30 grams for under three ounces carried.

The vegetables get a head start because dried pepper and tomato need real soaking time while the cheese powder needs almost none. Add them together and you get chalky cheese around leathery pepper. Use salt-free seasoning; the cheese powder is already salty enough on its own.
