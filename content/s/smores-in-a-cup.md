The original is a fridge dessert: cold milk, a cold set, and a kitchen torch. Powdered milk replaces the liquid, which is the whole trick. Instant pudding sets on the starch and the milk solids, not on temperature, so once the powder is fully hydrated it firms up in a warm tent as well as it does in a refrigerator.

Shake time matters more than anything else here. Under-shaken powder gives you chalky pockets that never dissolve. The layers are not decoration either: graham crumbs on the bottom stay dry until the last few bites, which keeps some crunch in a dessert that is otherwise all soft.
