This one keeps its butter and its live starter, so it stays a car-camping and basecamp recipe. Sourdough discard is half flour and half water by weight, which is why the flour here is dry-measured and no additional water goes in. Nothing to reconstitute at camp.

The baking powder does the rising. Unfed discard has no reliable leavening left in it, and there is no overnight proof out here anyway, so it contributes tang and moisture only. Cold butter is the other half of the structure: the cubes melt during the bake, leave gaps, and those gaps are what makes the crumb flaky. Warm butter smears into the flour and you get a scone. Drop biscuits instead of cut rounds because a floured board is one more thing to carry and wash. Coal placement matters more than time. Most of the heat has to come from the lid or the bottoms scorch before the tops set.
