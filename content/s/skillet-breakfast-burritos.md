This is a one-skillet breakfast, and the order of operations is the whole recipe. Potatoes take three times longer than eggs, so they go in first and alone with the sausage, which renders fat the potatoes then brown in. Eggs go in last, off a lower heat, or they turn to rubber while you wait on the spuds.

Cooking for four in one pan is the failure point. An overloaded skillet steams instead of browns and the potatoes come out pale and mealy. Cook in two rounds if your pan is small. Dicing the potatoes at home is the other real time saver: knife work by headlamp is slow, and you are already boiling coffee.
