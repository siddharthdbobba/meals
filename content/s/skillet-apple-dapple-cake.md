This started as a home oven cake with fresh Granny Smiths. Fresh apples are mostly water and mostly weight, so they became dried apples rehydrated in the pot. That trade is close to even: the apples give back their water, and the batter is wet enough from the oil that nothing goes chalky. Powdered egg replaces the three eggs, and ghee replaces butter in the sauce if there is no cooler.

The oil is the reason this travels. An oil-based batter does not need creaming, does not care about temperature, and stays soft for two days in a pot, which a butter cake will not. Coal placement matters more than time here: too much heat underneath burns the bottom before the middle sets, so keep most of the coals on the lid.
