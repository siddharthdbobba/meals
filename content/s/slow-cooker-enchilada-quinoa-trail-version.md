The original is a slow cooker dish: eight servings, five cans, ninety minutes of unattended heat. None of that survives a pack. What does survive is the ratio — quinoa as the starch, black beans as the protein, and enough cumin and chili powder that the whole pot tastes like enchilada sauce without carrying any.

Dried tomato and freeze-dried corn replace the cans and rehydrate in the same water the quinoa cooks in, so nothing is wasted. Quinoa needs a real simmer, not a soak, which is why this is fifteen minutes of fuel rather than five. The cheese and oil go in off the heat: they carry the calories and they seize if you boil them.
