Shredded sweet potato behaves like hash browns without needing a parboil, which is why this works on a fire. The shreds hit the pan with enough surface area to crisp in fifteen minutes, and the pieces that don't crisp turn soft and sweet. You get both textures out of one skillet.

The eggs are steamed, not fried. Sitting them in wells and covering the pan traps enough heat to set the whites from above without turning the bottoms to rubber. Eggs are the only thing here that doesn't keep, so this is a first-morning meal out of the cooler. The potato itself needs no refrigeration at all.
