Baked eggs need an oven, which nobody carries. The fix is a covered skillet over low heat: the trapped steam sets the whites from above while the pan works from below, and the result lands close enough. Coals give better control than a roaring fire, and a canister stove on its lowest setting is easier still.

Everything wet in the original became a powder. Dried mushrooms and home-dried spinach rehydrate in the same water you would have drained away, whole milk powder stands in for the cream, and ghee replaces butter because it keeps at ambient temperature for weeks. Nutmeg sounds odd in a mushroom pan and is doing real work — it rounds off the tinny edge that reconstituted milk brings.
