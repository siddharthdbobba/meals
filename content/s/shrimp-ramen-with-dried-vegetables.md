This is a grocery-store dinner, not a freeze-dried one. Every part of it comes off a normal shelf: instant noodles from the center aisle, shrimp from the canned fish section, dried vegetables from wherever your store hides them. Nothing needs a cooler.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Dried vegetables rehydrate slowly and noodles cook fast, so the vegetables go in first and get their own boiling time. Shrimp goes in last, off the heat, because it is already cooked and more heat only makes it tough. The onion flakes and sesame oil are doing the work the seasoning packet cannot, which is making it taste like it was cooked rather than reconstituted.
