Venison shoulder is all connective tissue and almost no fat, which is why smoking it start to finish turns it into jerky. Two stages fix that. The smoke goes on first, while the surface is still dry enough to take it, and the braise comes after, so the collagen gets the wet heat it needs to break down.

The beer and the adobo do the rest. Onions and peppers give off enough liquid on their own that you never need to add water, and the shoulder ends up sitting in its own seasoned braise. This one needs a cooler and six hours of fire, so it is a truck meal, not a pack meal. Cook it the first night of a hunt camp and eat the leftovers cold in tortillas the rest of the week.
