Sauerkraut is one of the few vegetables that travels without a cooler. It ships in a sealed pouch, it keeps in its own brine, and it survives whatever your pack does to it. That makes this the rare trail dinner with actual acid in it, which matters more than it sounds after four days of salt and starch.

The kraut goes in cold and boils with the bouillon so its liquid becomes the cooking water rather than something you drain off. Potato flakes then absorb all of it, which is why the ratio stays near 400 ml and there is nothing left in the pot at the end. Add the mustard off the heat; boiling flattens it. The sausage is the fat and most of the protein, so do not treat it as a garnish.
