Restaurant food is priced for the labor, not the ingredients. This is the same dinner stripped back to what it actually costs: rice, beans, and spices, all bought dry and measured at home.

Bean flakes are the part worth knowing about. They rehydrate in minutes without a simmer, so one boil does the whole pot, and they thicken the rice into something that holds a spoon instead of running. The oil is not optional. Beans and rice are cheap calories but light ones, and two tablespoons buy back 240 of them for almost no weight.
