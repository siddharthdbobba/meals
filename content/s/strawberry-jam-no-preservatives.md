There is nothing in this but fruit, sugar, and time. Sugar at roughly three quarters the weight of the berries is what makes it keep — it ties up the water so nothing grows in the jar. That is the whole preservation system, which is why cutting the sugar hard also cuts the shelf life.

Strawberries are low in pectin, so the set comes from boiling water off rather than from a packet. That takes patience and a wide pot. The cold-plate test is the only honest way to know when you are done; the clock is a guess. Once opened, a jar lives in the cooler.
