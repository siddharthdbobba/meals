The original is a home-kitchen sandwich built on a runny sunny-side-up yolk, fresh avocado, and a fridge. None of that travels. Whole egg powder scrambles instead of frying, which is the point: a set scramble stays inside the bread and does not run down your wrist. Powdered guacamole rehydrates into a paste that clings, so it behaves like the mashed avocado the original wanted rather than sliding out the side.

Mayo packets are the shelf-stable trick here. They carry fat with no cooler, and cut with sriracha they do the work that both a condiment and a sauce would. The dried onion, cilantro, and lime powder are small weight for the acid and bite that keep this from tasting like plain egg on bread. Mix the sauce first so the egg goes straight from pot to bagel while it is still hot.
