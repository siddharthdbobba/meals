This is a car-camping dessert, not a trail one. It needs a cooler for the butter, yolks, and dough, and it needs a real bed of coals. Nothing here substitutes cleanly into a stove pot, so it stays what it is.

The custard is the part that fails. Corn syrup and sugar carry it, but four yolks set it, and yolks curdle the second they hit hot syrup. Cool the syrup first and add them slowly. Coal placement does the rest: twice as many on the lid as underneath, or the crust burns before the middle sets.
