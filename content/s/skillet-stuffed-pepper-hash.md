The source version hollows out bell peppers and bakes them in coals for forty minutes. That is a long time to babysit a fire for a dish whose real content is sausage, rice, and tomato. Chopping the peppers into the pan gets the same flavors in a quarter of the time, and it removes the part of the recipe most likely to burn on one side while staying raw on the other.

Cooking the rice at home is the other change worth making. Rice takes twenty minutes and a lot of water, and it soaks up tomato juice better once it is already cooked. Bagged cold rice goes straight into the skillet and thickens the whole thing on contact. The cheese goes on off the heat so it melts instead of seizing.
