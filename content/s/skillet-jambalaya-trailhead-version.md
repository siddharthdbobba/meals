The original is an oven dish: browned chicken thighs, raw long-grain rice, canned tomato sauce, an hour at 350. None of that travels. The chicken becomes cured andouille, which needs no cold and no browning. The tomato sauce becomes tomato powder. The trinity becomes dried flakes. Raw rice becomes instant rice, because forty minutes of simmering rice is forty minutes of fuel you did not carry.

The order matters more than the timing. Vegetable flakes need real boiling water to soften, so they go first and alone. Rice goes in last and off the heat, where it steams instead of scorching to the bottom of the pot. The oil at the end replaces the rendered sausage fat you would have gotten from a skillet.
