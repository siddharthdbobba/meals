This started as an oven tray bake. Foil over coals does the same job: the packet traps steam, so the cabbage and shallots collapse while the potatoes cook through without drying out. The apple mostly dissolves into the cabbage and takes the edge off it.

The sausage goes in late on purpose. It is already cooked, so thirty minutes of heat only renders the fat out and leaves you with dry casings. Ten minutes is enough to warm it and let the fat run into the vegetables, which is where the flavor ends up. Cut everything at home; a cutting board is the one piece of gear you will regret carrying.
