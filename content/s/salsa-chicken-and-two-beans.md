The original is a slow cooker dish, which is three things a stove pot is not: eight hours, two cans of liquid, and a fridge. The swap is powders and freeze-dried protein. Both salsas survive dehydration well because their flavor is chili, cumin, and acid rather than fresh tomato, and the red and the green really do taste different in the pot.

Order matters more than technique here. Garbanzos are the slowest thing in the bag and go in the boil. Bean flakes are the fastest and go in dead last, because they will turn the whole pot to paste if they sit. The oil is not optional — nothing else in this recipe carries any fat, and without it dinner is protein and starch with nothing holding it together.
