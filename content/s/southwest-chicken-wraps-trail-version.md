The original is a hot-weather kitchen recipe: rotisserie chicken, sour cream, fresh avocado, iceberg lettuce. Every one of those is a refrigerator problem, so all of them got replaced. Foil-pouch chicken carries the same protein at room temperature. Sour cream and guacamole come back from powder with a little cold water. Lettuce is the one thing with no honest substitute, so freeze-dried cabbage takes its place for texture.

The bean flakes are the reason this works without a stove. They rehydrate in cold water in about ten minutes and set up thick enough to hold a wrap together, which keeps the salsa from soaking through the tortilla on the way to your mouth. Drain the chicken pouch before it goes in, or the whole thing runs.
