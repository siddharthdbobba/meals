The original is a sheet pan in a 425-degree oven, which is not something you have. Everything here moves to one pot: the sweet potato gets softened by a soak in boiled water instead of dry roasting, then fried in the same pot to get some browning back. That soak is the whole trick. Dried sweet potato dropped straight into hot oil stays gravel.

Fresh eggs and refrigerated sausage are the two things that do not travel, so both are swapped. Egg powder rehydrated in a mug behaves like beaten eggs if you mix it cold and let it hydrate for a minute. Dry-cured sausage carries the fat and salt that the fresh links were providing. Pull the pot early — residual heat finishes the egg, and overcooked powdered egg turns rubbery fast.
