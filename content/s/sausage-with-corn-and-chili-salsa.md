This is a campfire dinner with almost no cooking in it. The sausages are already cooked, so the fire is only there for color and heat; the salsa is assembled cold and gets better sitting in its own oil while the coals come down. That split is the whole point — nothing has to finish at the same time.

The cilantro oil does more work than a chopped herb would. Blending or finely chopping the cilantro into oil spreads it through every kernel of corn instead of leaving green flecks on top, and the oil carries the chili heat too. Canned corn replaces the fresh cobs, which is the only real substitution here. It is wetter and less sweet, so drain it well and lean on the lime. This one needs a cooler, so it is a car-camping or basecamp meal, not something to carry.
