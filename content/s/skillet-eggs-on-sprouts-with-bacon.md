This is a hot-pan-over-cold-greens dish, which is why the order matters. The sprouts never touch the skillet. They get wilted by the eggs landing on them and dressed by the fat and yolk running down, so they keep some crunch instead of collapsing into a grey mat.

Sunflower shoots hold up better than anything from a salad bag because their stems are thick and full of water. Rendered bacon fat is doing double duty here: it fries the eggs and it becomes the dressing, so there is no oil or vinaigrette to carry. Keep the yolks loose. A hard yolk leaves you eating dry greens.
