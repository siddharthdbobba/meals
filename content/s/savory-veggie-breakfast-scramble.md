Camp breakfast defaults to sugar: oats, bars, sweetened granola. This is the other direction, and it comes from an ordinary home breakfast saute — a pan of onion, pepper, mushroom, and greens with an egg on top. Everything in that pan dehydrates well, which is the only reason it works on trail.

Order matters. The vegetables and potatoes need real soak time, and the egg needs almost none. Rehydrate first, drive off the water, then add the egg last and cook it fast. Reverse that and you get rubber suspended in vegetable broth. The oil is not optional either — powdered egg is lean and sticks to a dry pot.
