Two things decide whether this works, and neither is the ingredient list. The first is the fire: a settled coal bed holds even heat, while open flame torches thin fish skin in under a minute. The second is patience. Fish welds itself to hot iron and then lets go once a crust forms, so the pan tells you when to flip. Prodding it early is how you end up serving scrambled trout.

Juniper is the whole seasoning plan. Crushed berries release resin into the butter, and a green sprig laid in the pan smolders and smokes the fish from underneath. Mushrooms go in late because they cook fast in fat and would otherwise turn to leather while the fish finishes. This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal: fresh fish and butter both need ice, so it belongs at a car site or a boat landing where the catch is close to the pan.
