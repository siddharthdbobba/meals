Most freeze-dried pasta dinners are salt with tomato flavoring. This one is a real sauce cooked at home, dried flat, and reassembled in the backcountry. The dehydrator step is doing the heavy lifting: a pound of leather and dry noodles feeds two people who walked all day.

The soak matters more than the cook. Start the sauce rehydrating in the morning and by dinner it only needs heat, not time. Cook the noodles separately and drain them before the sauce goes in, or you end up with soup. Make the leather within a week of leaving, since there is fat in the beef and fat is what goes off first.
