This one stays a real bake because the whole point is the two crusts. It needs a dutch oven and a cooler, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp, not in a pack.

The order inside the pot matters. Cheese goes down first, against the bottom dough, so it seals the crust from the meat juice. Cooking the vegetables until they stop weeping is the other half of that job; raw mushrooms and peppers put out enough liquid to turn the base to paste. Sauce sits on top instead of inside, which is how the Chicago version works, and it keeps the top crust from drying out under the coals.
