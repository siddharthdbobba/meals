This is a simmer, not a boil-and-wait, so it belongs on a fire or a stove you can hold at low heat for half an hour. Sweet potato is the reason: it needs real time to go soft, and the size of your cubes sets the clock. Small cubes cook faster and there is no other trick to it.

Draining the beans at home is worth doing. Canned liquid is dead weight, it makes the pot watery, and rinsing in camp costs water you may not want to spend. The chili heat is doing structural work here too, in cold weather a bland pot of beans reads as filler and a hot one reads as dinner.
