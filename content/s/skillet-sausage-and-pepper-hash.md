Hash is usually a fridge recipe: raw potatoes, fresh peppers, a pound of breakfast sausage. All three have shelf-stable versions that behave nearly the same once water goes back into them, so the whole thing packs dry and rehydrates in the pan it cooks in.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Rehydrate first with the heat off, then fry. If you dump water onto hot oil and sausage you get a steamed pile that never browns. The dried vegetables also give up a little liquid as they cook, which is why the pan needs stretches of undisturbed time to drive it off.
