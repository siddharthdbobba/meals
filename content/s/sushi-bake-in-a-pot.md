The original is a casserole: rice pressed into a pan, crab and cream cheese on top, ten minutes in a hot oven. None of that survives a pack. Crab sticks and cream cheese need refrigeration, and there is no oven. Pouched fish carries the protein instead, and the last minute over a low flame does what the bake did, which is warm the topping and marry it to the rice.

The vinegar, sugar, and salt are not optional seasoning. They are what separates sushi rice from plain rice, and they weigh almost nothing. Nori stays on the side and stays dry; the whole point is scooping hot rice onto a crisp sheet, and a sheet packed against wet rice goes limp in seconds.
