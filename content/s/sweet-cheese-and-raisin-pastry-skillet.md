This is a Romanian cheese pie made without a refrigerator. The original leans on fresh sweet cow's cheese and milk, which is a non-starter past day one. Powdered whole milk plus a dry cheese powder, hydrated to a paste, gets close enough on texture and holds the sugar and fruit in place.

The store-bought sheets are the reason this is worth carrying. You are not making dough on a picnic table. Oil between layers is what keeps them separate as they bake, so do not skip it, and keep the heat low and the pan turning. Filo goes from pale to burnt in about a minute over direct flame.
