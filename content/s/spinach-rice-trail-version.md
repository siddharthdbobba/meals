The original is a stovetop side: raw long grain rice, a can of broth, a box of frozen spinach, and half an hour of simmering while you stir so it does not scorch. None of that survives a canister stove. Frozen spinach and canned broth are water you would be carrying, and thirty minutes of simmering is most of a fuel canister.

So the water leaves at home. Instant rice cooks on residual heat in a covered pot, bouillon replaces the broth, and dried spinach flakes rehydrate in the same water. Butter powder carries the fat the stick of butter used to. The onion goes in early because dried minced onion stays crunchy if you add it at the end.
