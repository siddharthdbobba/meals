The camp version of a quesadilla fails in two places: watery filling and burnt tortillas. Both come from the same mistake, which is putting wet food on hot bread. Cooking the filling all the way dry first solves it. Freeze-dried chicken and dried vegetables help here too, because you control exactly how much water goes in and it all cooks back out.

Sour cream and fresh peppers do not travel without a cooler, so they are gone. Salsa in a pouch keeps at ambient temperature and does the same job of cutting the fat. Cheese is the structural ingredient, not a garnish. Cut it thin so it melts before the tortilla is done.
