Raw sweet corn is the whole point here. Picked young it is soft and milky, closer to a vegetable than a grain, and it needs no heat at all. Field corn is the opposite: dense with starch, unpleasant raw, and rough on your gut. Buy sweet corn in season and use it fast, because the sugars turn to starch within a day or two of picking and the flavor goes flat and grassy.

This is a day-one lunch and nothing else. Cut kernels have no shelf life without a cooler, so eat it the first day out or leave it home. The beans and oil are what turn a side dish into a meal — corn alone is about 86 calories per 100 g and thin on protein, so the beans carry the load and the oil carries the calories. Everything happens in the bag, which means no pot and nothing to wash.
