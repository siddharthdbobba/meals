The TVP is the point. Plain oatmeal is fast sugar and quits on you an hour down the trail; two tablespoons of soy protein pushes this to about 20 grams and changes how long it lasts. It has no flavor of its own, so the brown sugar and raisins still run the show.

Breaking up the oats at home matters more than it sounds. Whole rolled oats need a real simmer to soften; chopped ones hydrate off boiling water alone, which is what makes this a pour-and-wait breakfast instead of a pot to scrub. The flax and wheat germ thicken it as it sits, so give it the full five minutes before deciding it needs more water.
