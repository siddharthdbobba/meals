This is a real bread recipe, so it belongs at a car-camping site where you have a cooler, a table, and time to let dough rise. The cheese goes into the dough rather than on top, which is what keeps the crumb tender once it cools off in cold air. Boxed UHT milk replaces refrigerated milk with no change to the result.

Raw sausage in a rolled dough is the part that can go wrong. Bread crumbs mixed into the meat soak up rendering fat so it does not pool and leave a greasy layer under the spiral. Coal placement matters more than time: heavy on the lid, light underneath, or the bottoms scorch before the middle cooks through.
