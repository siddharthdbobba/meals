The original is a blender soup: boil sweet potato in stock, puree it, fold in ground walnuts. There is no blender on a trail, so the grinding moves home. Dehydrated sweet potato ground coarse rehydrates into something close to a puree on its own, and the walnuts go in already fine.

Walnuts and oil are why this is a dinner instead of a hot drink. Sweet potato is mostly carbohydrate and water weight you left behind; the fat is what gets the calories back up. The sage matters more than it sounds like it should, since it is the only thing cutting the sweetness.
