The original is a home recipe with an oven, ground meat, and a fridge. None of that travels. Freeze-dried shredded chicken rehydrated to just-damp does the same structural job as raw ground chicken: it gives the panko something to grab. Get the water ratio right and the mixture binds. Too much water and you have a paste that will not hold a ball.

The sauce is worth the second step. Cornstarch whisked into cold soy sauce before heat is the whole trick — dump it into a hot pot and you get lumps you cannot fix. Honey and vinegar balance the salt, and the sauce reduces fast enough that you are not burning fuel waiting on it. Everything happens in one pot, in order, with a wipe in between.
