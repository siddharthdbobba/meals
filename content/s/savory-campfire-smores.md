This is a charcuterie board with one step added: the cheese gets hot. Melting is the whole point, so treat it like a marshmallow that punishes impatience. Flame chars the outside before the middle softens and the round slides off into the ashes. Coals give you even heat and enough time to watch for the slump.

Everything else is cold and does no work over the fire, which is why the assembly is laid out first. The pickles and jam are there for acid and sugar against three salty ingredients. Drop either one and it reads as a mouthful of fat and salt. Cheese and cured meat need a cooler, so this stays a car-camping snack.
