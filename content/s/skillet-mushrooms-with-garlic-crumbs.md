The original is a skillet dish built on fresh wild mushrooms, which do not travel. Dried mushrooms do, and they concentrate the flavor into something a quarter of the weight. The trade is time: ten minutes of soaking, then a longer fry to drive off the water they just absorbed. That browning is the whole point, so do not rush it.

The crumbs are texture, not filler. They go in last, in fat, on their own patch of pot, because panko added to wet mushrooms just turns to paste. Butter is swapped for ghee since it keeps without a cooler. Save the soaking liquid — it is the best broth you will get in camp for free.
