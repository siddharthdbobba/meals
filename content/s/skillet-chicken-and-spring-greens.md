This started as a home skillet dinner: chicken, spring vegetables, a starch, all cooked in one pan. The trail version keeps the structure and drops everything that needs a refrigerator or a knife. Fresh vegetables become freeze-dried peas and dried greens, and the starch becomes couscous, which asks for hot water and five minutes rather than a real simmer.

The two-stage cook is the whole point. Chicken and peas want boiling water and a few minutes to soften; couscous added at the same time turns to paste. Simmer the slow things first, then kill the heat and let the couscous take up what is left. Sun-dried tomato and lemon pepper do the work an acidic pan sauce would do at home, and the oil goes in last so it does not cook off.
