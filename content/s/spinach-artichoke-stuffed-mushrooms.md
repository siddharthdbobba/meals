steps:
  - "Twist the stems out of the mushrooms and wipe the caps with a damp bandana. Do not rinse them. They soak up water and steam instead of browning."
  - "Squeeze the thawed spinach out over the dirt until it stops dripping. Wet spinach makes wet filling, and wet filling runs out of the caps."
  - "In the pot or a bag, mash the cream cheese with the sour cream, mayo, garlic, salt, and pepper. Fold in the spinach, chopped artichoke, mozzarella, and parmesan."
  - "Pack a heaping spoonful into each cap. Set them on a doubled sheet of heavy foil with the edges turned up. Do not seal it flat or the tops will never brown."

This is a party appetizer that survives being dragged to a fire ring, but only if you deal with water. Mushrooms are mostly water, frozen spinach is mostly water, and canned artichokes sit in brine. Squeeze and drain all three and you get filling that stays in the cap. Skip it and you get a foil sheet of grey soup.

The air fryer and the oven both come down to dry heat from above, which a grate over coals or a Dutch oven with lid coals does fine. Mixing the filling at home is the real change. Cream cheese, mayo, and shredded cheese are all cooler food with a short leash, so make this the first or second night out and keep the bag buried in ice until the fire is ready.
