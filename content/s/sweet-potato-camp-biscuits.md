The original wants cold roasted sweet potato and cold milk, which is a fridge recipe. Dried sweet potato powder does the same two jobs without the cold chain: it brings the starch and moisture-holding that keeps the crumb soft, and it hydrates in the dough itself. Ghee replaces butter because it sits in a pack at 85 degrees and does not go rancid or weep.

The vinegar is not a flavor. It reacts with the baking powder for a second push of lift, which matters when your oven is a cast iron pot with uneven coals. Keep the fat in visible pieces and keep the dough on the dry side. Melted fat and wet dough both give you hockey pucks, and there is no second batch out here.
