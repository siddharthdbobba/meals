Stuffed peppers do not survive the trip to a campsite. The peppers are a fresh vegetable, the filling wants an oven, and the whole thing takes an hour. So the pepper stops being a container here and becomes an ingredient: freeze-dried, rehydrated in the first two minutes of boil, chopped small enough that it never had to hold together.

Everything else is a swap for something refrigerated. Tomato powder replaces jarred sauce and weighs almost nothing. The creamy cauliflower sauce becomes cauliflower powder plus powdered milk, which does the same job of softening the acid in the tomato without cream. Instant rice matters more than it sounds: regular rice needs twenty minutes of active fuel, and this needs none once the water is hot.
