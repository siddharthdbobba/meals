Simmering the meatballs directly in the marinara is the whole point at camp. There is no oven, no baking sheet, and no reason to dirty a second pan browning them first. The sauce cooks the meat through and picks up the fat that renders out, which is what would otherwise be left in a skillet you have to scrub.

The binder is where this changes from the home version. Fresh eggs and a fresh onion both travel badly, so powdered egg and dried minced onion do the same jobs without the cooler space. Grated parmesan from a canister is stable at any temperature. The ground beef is the one thing still on a clock, so this is a first-night dinner unless you carry the precooked meatballs instead.
