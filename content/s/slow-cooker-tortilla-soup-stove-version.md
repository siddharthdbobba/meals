The original runs six to eight hours in a slow cooker, which is a stove you leave at home. Nothing in it actually needs that time: the chicken is already cooked in the pouch, and the long hold was only ever there to soften beans and pull the tortillas apart. Bean flakes and crushed chips do both jobs in the time it takes water to boil.

Tomato powder is the substitution that matters. A can of diced tomatoes is mostly water and steel, and it hits the same note at a fraction of the weight. Add the lime off heat and stir the bean flakes in slowly, or you get a pot that is thin on top and paste at the bottom.
