Sweet potato does two things in a biscuit: it adds sugar that browns, and it adds moisture that keeps the crumb soft. Fresh mashed sweet potato is not a trail ingredient, so this uses the instant flakes and rehydrates them inside the dough with the water. Buttermilk powder replaces the fresh buttermilk and reacts with the leavening in the self-rising flour the same way.

The original folds chilled butter into a sheet of dough eight times for flaky layers. That does not work over a fire with warm shortening and no counter, so these are drop biscuits. Cutting the fat in coarsely and stopping the mixing early gets most of the lift back. Coals on the lid matter more than coals underneath, or the bottoms burn before the tops set.
