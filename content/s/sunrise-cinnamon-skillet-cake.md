The original is a buttermilk cake, which means a fridge and a carton you will not finish. Powdered buttermilk and powdered egg do the same job out of a bag, and they are what make this a camp recipe instead of a kitchen recipe hauled outdoors. The acid in the buttermilk powder is not decoration: it is what the baking soda reacts with, so do not swap in plain milk powder without adding an acid.

Heat control is the whole game. Coals on top and few underneath means the cake bakes rather than fries, and the rotating keeps the hot spot from parking in one place for half an hour. Mix it just until the flour disappears. Stir it longer and you get a tough, rubbery slab.
