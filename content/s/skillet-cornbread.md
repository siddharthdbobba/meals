Cornbread in a skillet sidesteps the oven problem entirely. The covered pan holds heat just like an oven would, and the flour-forward batter is thick enough to set without constant attention. Everything ships dry and mixes with boiled water at camp.

This recipe is good weight-to-calorie only at basecamp, where a cast iron already lives in the kitchen box and you're staying put long enough to justify the fuel. Each slice is dense carbs, useful for breakfast or a substantial side. Skip it on anything longer than a weekend.
