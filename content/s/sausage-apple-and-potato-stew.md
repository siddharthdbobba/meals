The gravy powder does two jobs. It seasons, and it thickens the cooking water into something that clings to the potatoes instead of running off them. Toss it with the raw vegetables before the water goes in, or it clumps.

Apple with sausage sounds off until it cooks down. The chunks half-collapse and sweeten the broth, which cuts the fat coming off the pork. Skins stay on everything here, apple included: less prep, less trash, and the potato pieces hold their shape better with the skin holding them together.
