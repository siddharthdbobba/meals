This started as a desk lunch, which means it assumed a refrigerator and a microwave. On a trip it works because kohlrabi is one of the few vegetables that will sit in a cooler for three days without going soft or weeping, and because smoked salmon comes sealed and needs no cooking at all.

Cutting the kohlrabi at home is the part that matters. Ribbons cook in five minutes over a stove; chunks take twenty and burn the outside first. The salmon goes in off the heat because it is already cooked, and reheating it hard turns it dry and chalky. The hummus and sticks on the side are there because a hot pan of vegetables is not enough food by itself, and they need no pot.
