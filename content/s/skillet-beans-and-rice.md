The home version simmers raw brown rice for 45 minutes and finishes with sour cream and shredded cheese. Neither survives a camp kitchen. Instant brown rice gets the same nutty texture off five minutes of residual heat, and the fresh onion becomes dried minced onion, which rehydrates in the pan fat before the beans go in.

Toasting the spices in oil first is the step worth keeping. Cumin dumped into wet beans tastes flat; cumin bloomed in hot oil for sixty seconds coats everything. The bean can does the rest of the work, since canned beans arrive already cooked and only need to be warmed.

Carry the can and you are carrying water. That is fine from a truck or a boat and miserable on your back, which is what the refried flake variation is for.
