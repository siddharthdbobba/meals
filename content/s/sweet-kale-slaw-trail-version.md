A raw kale salad is the last thing you would expect to survive a pack, and the fresh version does not. Dehydrated shredded vegetables do, and they come back close enough: cabbage and broccoli stem rehydrate with real snap, which is what this salad is actually built on. Kale mostly contributes color and body. Slice everything thin before drying or it never fully rehydrates.

The dressing is the other change. Greek yogurt is out, yogurt powder is in, reconstituted separately so it goes smooth instead of pebbly. Citric acid replaces lemon juice at a fraction of the weight and does the same job of cutting the sweetness. Give the finished salad a rest before eating; the vegetables keep drinking, and a bowl that looks watery at first will not be by the time you get to it.
