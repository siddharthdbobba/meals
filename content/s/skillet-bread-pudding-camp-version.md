Bread pudding is a good candidate for camp because the hard part is soaking, not cooking. Powdered whole eggs and whole milk powder replace eight fresh eggs, milk, and cream, which is the entire reason this works without a cooler. Reconstituted, they set into custard the same way. Ghee instead of butter removes the last thing that needs to stay cold.

The bread has to be dry. Fresh bread turns to paste once the custard hits it, so cube it and leave it out overnight at home. Twenty minutes of soaking before the lid goes on is not optional either: dry centers show up as chalky pockets after baking, and no amount of extra time on the coals fixes them.
