Dried shrimp carries the protein without requiring refrigeration. Toasting it in oil before adding the rice infuses the whole pot with its flavor. Instant rice absorbs all of it—the shrimp, the oil, the aromatics.

You're building a complete dinner: carbs from rice, protein from shrimp, bulk from vegetables, fat to make it stick. The soy and sesame oil finish it into something composed.
