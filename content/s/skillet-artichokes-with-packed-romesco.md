The original of this is a food processor recipe: whole raw artichokes, fresh tomatoes, a blender full of sauce. Neither of those travels. Fresh tomatoes turn to mush in a cooler and there is no processor in camp, so the tomatoes here are oil-packed sun-dried and the sauce is mashed by hand into a coarse paste instead of an emulsion. It sits looser than the blended version. It also holds better in a jar.

The artichokes need both dry heat and wet heat. The sear builds the flavor the sauce hangs on, and the covered steam is what actually cooks them through, since a whole artichoke needs half an hour no matter how hot the pan is. Trim them at home. Doing it on a picnic table with a pocket knife adds twenty minutes and a lot of waste.
