This started as a slow cooker recipe, which is really just a dutch oven with a plug on it. Coals do the same job: low, even, unattended heat for four hours. The trade is that you have to feed the fire every 45 minutes instead of walking away entirely.

The sauce goes in late on purpose. Ketchup scorches against a hot cast iron wall over a long cook, so the meat braises in broth and soy sauce first, then the ketchup joins the drippings at the end and the corn starch pulls it together. Brisket needs a cooler and a day one plan, so this is a car-camping dinner, not something you carry.
