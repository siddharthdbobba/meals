A pizookie is a normal cookie recipe that stops baking early on purpose, so the center stays raw enough to eat with a spoon. That underbake is what makes it work on a fire, where even heat is a fantasy. You are aiming for set edges and a soft middle, which is exactly what an inconsistent bed of coals gives you anyway.

The home version leans on cold butter and a fresh egg. Butter powder plus oil replaces both jobs — flavor from the powder, fat from the oil — and powdered egg handles the binding. Cream of tartar with baking soda supplies the lift without a fridge or a leavening packet that has gone flat. The coals on the lid matter: heat only from below gives you a scorched bottom and wet dough on top.
