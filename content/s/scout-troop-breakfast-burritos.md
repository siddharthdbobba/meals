This is a troop breakfast: one skillet, one pot, and a line of people holding plates. The pot is the whole trick. Each component cooks separately in the skillet, then gets parked in the pot near the fire, so nothing goes cold waiting on the eggs and you never need a second burner.

Steaming the sausage in a little water before browning is worth the extra minute over a fire, where heat is uneven and the outside chars long before the middle is done. The fat left behind is the cooking medium for the eggs, which is why they come out rich enough that the burrito holds together without much cheese. It needs a cooler for the eggs, sausage, and cheese, so treat it as a car camp or basecamp meal; the shelf-stable swap is in the variations.
