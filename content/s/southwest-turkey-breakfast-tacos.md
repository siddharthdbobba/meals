This one stays on the cooler-trip list rather than in a pack, because eggs and raw turkey both need cold. The trade is that you get real scrambled eggs and real meat, which no freeze-dried breakfast matches.

The order of operations is the whole recipe. Turkey and onion first because they take the longest and leave browned bits in the pan. Beans and spices next, in the rendered fat, so the seasoning coats everything instead of sitting on top. Eggs last and off to one side, since they overcook in seconds and turn rubbery if they share heat with meat that is still browning. Pico goes on at the plate, not in the skillet, or the tortillas turn to paste.
