The original is a skillet dish built on butter, fresh chard, cream, and raw fillets steamed over the greens. None of that travels. Dried onion and dried greens rehydrate in the same broth that becomes the sauce, whole milk powder replaces the cream and sour cream, and pouch salmon replaces the fillets so nothing has to cook through.

Honey and chipotle are the reason this reads as a dish rather than boiled vegetables. The sweetness and the smoke both survive drying and powdering, which is more than can be said for most fresh flavors. Add the milk powder off the heat: boiled, it separates and goes grainy. The lime at the end cuts the sweetness, and the pumpkin seeds give you something to chew.
