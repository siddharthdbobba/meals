This is the Italian way to cook any green. Dried spinach or foraged greens rehydrate in the same pot where they'll cook, making this a true one-pot dinner. Everything rests on the garlic oil—cook it gently for a minute until it's fragrant (powder) or golden (sliced). That's where all the flavor comes from.

Dried greens are light and shelf-stable; the principle works as well as it does with fresh. Sliced dehydrated garlic beats powder significantly but weighs the same. Fresh lemon is an upgrade if you're carrying it; otherwise red pepper and salt are sufficient. The greens are incidental. The oil carrying the garlic is everything.
