Salmon cakes are a good camp dinner because the expensive part is already cooked and sitting in a can. What normally makes them a kitchen recipe is the fresh egg, onion, parsley and mango, and every one of those has a dry version that rehydrates in the time it takes the stove to warm up. The egg powder matters most: it is the binder, and without something holding the salmon to the panko you get hash instead of cakes.

Work the oil hard. A dry pan makes the crust stick and tear, and the crust is the reason to fry these instead of eating salmon out of the can. The mango sauce reads like a garnish but it is doing real work against the cayenne and the salt, so mix it before the pan is hot and let it sit while the cakes cook.
