The oven version of this recipe relies on dry ambient heat to crisp the cubes. A camp stove gives you the opposite, so the method changes: cover the pan first and let the potatoes steam in their own moisture until they are cooked through, then uncover and let the pan run dry so the outsides brown. Trying to crisp raw potato in a skillet just gives you burnt edges around a hard center.

Russets are the right potato because they are starchy and dry, which is also why they travel well. No cooler needed. The oil is not optional — potato is nearly all carbohydrate, and four tablespoons is what turns a side dish into something that actually holds you until lunch. Coconut and avocado oil are both stable at pan heat and neither goes rancid in a week of warm weather.
