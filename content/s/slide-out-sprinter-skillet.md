This is a van-galley dinner scaled down to one pan and one boil. Everything in it sits on a shelf for months, so it works the same whether you have a fridge running or not. Two eaters, twenty minutes, no cutting board if you slice the sausage before you leave.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Browning the sausage first and blooming the spices in that fat gives the dish a base that no amount of bouillon will fake. The rice then finishes off-heat under the lid, which keeps it from scorching on a stove that only knows one setting.
