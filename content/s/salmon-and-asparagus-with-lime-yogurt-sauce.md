This started as a grocery-budget family dinner: fresh salmon, roasted asparagus, and a feta-yogurt sauce whipped in a blender. None of that survives a warm pack, so everything got swapped for its shelf-stable twin. Foil-pouch salmon carries the protein without a cooler. Asparagus dehydrates well if you cut it thin, and it rehydrates in the same water that cooks the instant rice, so there is one pot and no drained liquid to dump.

The sauce is the part worth doing right. Yogurt and cheese powders will seize into grit if you dump them into a boiling pot, so they get mixed separately with a few spoonfuls of hot broth until smooth, then poured over at the end. Lime powder and soy sauce are standing in for the marinade and the acid; between them they do most of the flavor work, since dried asparagus alone tastes like very little.
