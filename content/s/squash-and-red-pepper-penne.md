The original roasts squash and peppers for forty minutes and runs them through a blender. Neither of those exists on trail, so the puree happens at home in a dehydrator and comes along as powder. Rehydrated in pasta water it does the same job: a thick, slightly sweet sauce with no cream and no cheese sauce packet.

Not draining the pasta is the whole trick. The starch in that water is what gives the sauce body, and dumping it means dumping half your thickener. Add the squash powder in stages, since it grabs water faster than you expect and a lump of it stays a lump.
