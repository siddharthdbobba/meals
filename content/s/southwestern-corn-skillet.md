The original is a skillet side built on fresh corn, onion, and pepper, which is three refrigerated items and a saute pan you're not carrying. Freeze-dried corn is the piece that makes this work on trail: it rehydrates in minutes and keeps enough sweetness to play against the chile flake and salt. Dried onion and pepper never fully return to crisp, but they don't need to here.

The presoak matters more than the simmer. Dropping everything into boiling water cooks the outside of the corn before the middle takes on any water, and you end up chewing. Cold soak first, then heat. The oil goes in off the flame because it's the only fat in the pot and you want it carrying spice, not smoking.
