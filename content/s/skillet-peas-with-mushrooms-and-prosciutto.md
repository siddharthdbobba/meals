This started as a restaurant side, cooked with fresh shell peas and whole porcini. Neither travels. Dried mushrooms and freeze-dried peas do, and the swap costs less than you would think: the mushroom soaking water becomes the broth, so the flavor you would have lost goes back into the pan.

The order matters. Brown the mushrooms in dry fat before any liquid touches them, or they stay pale and rubbery. The peas go in last and barely cook, because freeze-dried peas turn to mush in about a minute of hard boiling. Prosciutto and ghee replace the butter and fresh ham because both hold at ambient temperature.
