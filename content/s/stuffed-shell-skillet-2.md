Stuffed shells are an oven dish with three separate components: boiled pasta, a cooked vegetable filling, and a sauce. None of that survives a camp kitchen. The fix is to stop stuffing anything and cook the filling, the sauce, and the pasta in the same pot, in that order, using the pasta water as the sauce base instead of pouring it out.

The ricotta is the part that had to go, since fresh cheese does not travel. Powdered milk plus dry parmesan plus a little oil gets close: enough fat and salt to coat the pasta, no cooler required. Breadcrumbs were binder in the original and they are thickener here, so add them last and only as needed. Rehydrating the mushrooms and spinach before the pasta goes in matters, because they take longer than the shells do and will stay leathery otherwise.
