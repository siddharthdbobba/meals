This is candy plus water and nothing else. Licorice boats are mostly sugar, syrup, and gelling starch, so heat and a little water take them apart into a sauce. Salmiak licorice also carries ammonium chloride, which is why the result reads as salty rather than only sweet.

The only real technique is patience over low heat. Sugar this concentrated burns fast on a canister stove's narrow flame, so stir and keep the pot moving. The sauce sets firm when cold, which is a feature on trail: make it at home, carry it as a solid, and melt it back with a splash of water. Nothing here needs a fridge.
