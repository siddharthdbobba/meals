This is a weeknight sheet pan dinner moved onto coals. The pucks matter more than the shape sounds like it should: a full loaf needs an hour of even oven heat, and a campfire does not do even heat for an hour. An inch thick cooks through in twenty minutes and forgives a hot spot.

The stuffing mix is doing three jobs at once. It binds like breadcrumbs, it seasons the meat so you carry no separate spice blend for the loaf, and it comes shelf-stable in a box. It is also wheat, so this is not gluten-free, and the beef and eggs mean it needs a cooler. Cook it the first night out.
