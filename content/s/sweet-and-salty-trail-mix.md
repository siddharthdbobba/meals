This is the sweet-and-salty ratio worked out: roughly half nuts, a quarter starch and chocolate, a quarter dried fruit. The nuts carry the calories and the fat, the pretzels give it salt and something that crunches, and the fruit replaces the sugar you burn off between meals. Skew it too far toward fruit and you have candy that costs the same weight as food.

The hard-shelled chocolate is a deliberate choice. Bare chips melt into the pretzels on a warm afternoon and set into one lump overnight. Coconut flakes are the cheapest fat here by weight, and toasting them at home keeps them from going soft in the bag.
