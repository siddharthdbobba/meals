Shepherd's pie is an oven dish: a braise under a potato crust, browned under heat from above. None of that survives a backpack, so this version keeps the two layers and drops the oven. The filling simmers down to a gravy in the pot. The potatoes go on top at the end and set from residual heat instead of browning.

The stout and the beef broth in the original are both there for depth, and bouillon plus tomato paste covers most of that ground at a fraction of the weight. Cook the filling down further than feels right. If it is still soupy when the potatoes land, the layers merge and you have soup with flakes in it.
