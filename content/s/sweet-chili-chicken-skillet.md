The original is fresh chicken breast in a cast iron skillet, which works fine at a car campsite with a cooler and less fine without one. Pouched chicken removes the cold chain and the food-safety math both. It is already cooked, so the skillet time is about browning the outside and reducing the sauce, not about getting the middle to temperature.

Cornstarch is the part worth getting right. Sweet chili sauce alone runs off the chicken and pools at the bottom of the pan. A tablespoon of starch mixed into the cold liquid before it goes anywhere near heat turns that runoff into a coating that stays put. Mix it dry into hot sauce and you get lumps you cannot stir out. Rice cooks off the heat while the skillet works, so this is one burner and one pot of hot water.
