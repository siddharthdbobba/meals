This is dairy in a cooler, so it is a car camping dish and nothing else. What makes it work over a fire instead of an oven is the cast iron: the skillet holds an even, moderate heat off coals, and the foil lid traps enough steam that the cheese melts before the bottom browns. Direct flame will split the cream cheese into grease and curds.

Cream cheese is the structure. It is what keeps the hot sauce from running off the chicken and pooling. Mix it at home while it is soft and you skip the worst part of the job at camp, where cold cream cheese and a plastic spork are a bad pairing. Canned chicken works fine here and saves you carrying anything raw.
