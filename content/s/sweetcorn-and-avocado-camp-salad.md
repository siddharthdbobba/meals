The original is a summer table salad built on fresh corn on the cob, which is a lot of weight and a boil you do not want at midday. Freeze-dried corn solves both. It rehydrates in cold water in about twenty minutes and comes back sweet rather than starchy, so nothing needs heat.

Cherry tomatoes go to mush in a pack, so sun-dried in oil stand in. They bring their own acid and their own fat, which means the dressing is doing less work. Avocado and mozzarella are the only cooler-dependent parts, and they are what pin this to the first day out. Everything else will sit in a pack for a week.
