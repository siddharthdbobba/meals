There is no fresh dairy here and no egg, which is why a home-baked cookie makes it onto a packing list at all. Butter in a baked cookie is stable at ambient temperature for a week, and the dough is dry enough that nothing inside stays wet long enough to spoil. Two to three days is comfortable in summer, a week in cold weather.

The powdered sugar is structural, not decoration. It carries no moisture, so it seals the surface, and the double coat gives you something to lose to the bottom of the container without ruining the cookie. In real heat the coat will go tacky and gray. Roll them at camp instead, or accept it.

Nothing about this recipe is adapted for cooking on the trail, because it is not cooked on the trail. Bake at home, carry the result, and treat the container like the fragile thing it is.
