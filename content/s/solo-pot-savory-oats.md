Oats are cheap, light, and rehydrate off residual heat, which is most of what you want from a solo dinner. The usual objection is that they taste like breakfast. Bouillon, onion, and fat fix that. Treat oats as a starch rather than a cereal and they behave like polenta.

The cheese and salami are both doing double duty as protein and as the fat that makes this filling. Both hold up for several days out of a fridge, which is why they show up in so many solo kitchen kits. Cut the meat cold and add it at the end so it warms through instead of turning to grease in the pot. One boil, one pot, one spoon.
