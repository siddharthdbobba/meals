The original is an oven trick: pour the batter into a sheet pan, bake it once, and stop standing at a griddle flipping rounds one at a time. A dutch oven over coals does the same job in camp, and it is the only way to feed six people pancakes before the first ones go cold.

The swaps are the usual no-fridge ones. Powdered milk and powdered egg carry the batter, and both go into the dry mix at home so camp is one bag plus water. Do not expect browning. Baked batter comes out pale and closer to a thick cake than a pancake, so the syrup is doing more work here than it would on a griddle. Keep the coals under the pot sparse or the bottom scorches before the middle sets.
