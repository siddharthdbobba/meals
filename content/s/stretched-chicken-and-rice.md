Meat is the expensive part of dinner and the heavy part of a pack, so this recipe treats three ounces of chicken as a seasoning rather than a portion. The beans and rice carry the protein load underneath it; the bean flakes also thicken the pot so the chicken shreds get suspended through the whole thing instead of sinking.

Shredding matters more than it sounds. Chunks of pouch chicken distribute badly and leave most spoonfuls meatless, which is exactly the feeling you paid to avoid. Instant rice and instant bean flakes both rehydrate off residual heat, so the whole meal costs one boil and no simmering fuel.
