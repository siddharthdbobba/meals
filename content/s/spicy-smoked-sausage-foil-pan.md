This is a coals recipe, not a stove recipe. The covered foil pan traps steam, which is what actually cooks the onion and pepper down; the sausage is already cooked and is just soaking up sauce and heat. Uncovered, the sugar in the sweet and sour sauce burns long before the vegetables give up.

The sauce ratio is the whole design. Sweet and sour brings sugar and body, hot sauce brings vinegar and heat, and together they reduce into something that clings instead of pooling in the bottom of the pan. Jalapeno seeds decide how hot it lands, so pull them if you are feeding a group you do not know. Sausage and vegetables need a cooler, which keeps this at the trailhead rather than in a pack.
