The original is a baked meatball with fresh greens and a raw egg, neither of which survives a pack. Freeze-dried cooked beef solves both problems at once: it rehydrates into crumbles that already have the texture of browned meat, so the pan work is about crust, not doneness. Powdered egg and flour do the binding they always did.

The rehydration water is the thing to watch. Too much and the mix will not hold a shape; too little and the balls crack apart in the pan. Drain hard after the soak, then add water back a spoonful at a time. Parmesan is not optional here — it is salt, fat, and glue in one ingredient.
