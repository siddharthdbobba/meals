Home chili gets its body from an hour of simmering. On a stove burning fuel you carried, that is not available, so the thickening moves to instant bean flakes, which set up in minutes and hold the spices in suspension. Dried mushrooms do the work canned beans and fresh peppers used to: they rehydrate into something with chew, and the soaking water becomes stock.

The cocoa and cinnamon look like mistakes and are not. Cocoa rounds the raw edge off chili powder, cinnamon fills the gap where a long simmer would have gone. Soy sauce powder replaces the liquid original for the same savory weight without a bottle. Honey is out for the vegan version; a teaspoon of brown sugar does the same job and packs dry.
