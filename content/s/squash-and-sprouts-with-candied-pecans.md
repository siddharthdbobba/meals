This started as a sheet-pan Thanksgiving side, which does not travel. The oven was doing two jobs: caramelizing the vegetables and candying the nuts. Only the second one survives a trip, so it moves to the kitchen at home, where the glaze can cool hard and stay that way in a bag.

The vegetables get dried instead of roasted. You lose the browning and there is no honest way around that. What you keep is the spice and fat, which is most of what made the original work. Coconut oil goes in after the heat is off so it coats rather than fries, and the pecans go on last so they are still crunchy when the spoon reaches them.
