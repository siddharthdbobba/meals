Canned biscuit dough is a shortcut that actually works over a fire. It is already leavened and already portioned, so pressing eight biscuits into one round gives you a crust with no rising, no kneading, and no flour dust in camp.

The two-stage bake is the part people skip. Setting the bottom first means the crust is a solid floor by the time wet sauce hits it, instead of soaking through into raw dough. The foil lid traps enough heat above the toppings to melt cheese, which a bare skillet over coals will never do.
