This is a boxed-rice dinner doing more work than it was designed for. The two seasoning packets handle all the salt and spice, the tomato juice replaces part of the water, and the sausage renders enough fat to carry the whole pot. Nothing here needs a second pan.

Coal placement is the only real technique. Full heat underneath to reach a boil, then four briquettes below and eight above so the rice steams instead of scorching. If you are cooler-free, the dried pepper and onion swap in the variations gets you the same dish with nothing that can spoil.
