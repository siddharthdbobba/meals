Venison carries a depth that a long simmer needs. The chipotle peppers in adobo (already canned, shelf-stable) do the heavy lifting—they add both heat and smoke without needing fire-roasted fresh peppers. Dried onion and garlic replace fresh versions; they reconstitute into broth and carry the same flavor at half the weight.

The beans thicken the broth and carry the protein. Leave this simmering at the edge of the fire and it improves through the night. Skip the bacon fat or the chipotle and you have a thin meat soup instead of a chili.
