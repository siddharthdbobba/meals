The order matters more than the ingredients. Garlic first and briefly, tomatoes long enough to break down, mushrooms last so they brown instead of stewing. Do it in that sequence in one pot and you get a real sauce out of five things; do it all at once and you get warm vegetable soup.

Blue cheese is the reason this works without a long simmer. It is salty, sharp, and fatty enough to stand in for hours of reduction, and it melts into the sauce on residual heat. Cook the pasta first and reuse the pot — carrying two pots for one dinner is not worth it, and a spoonful of the starchy water loosens the sauce back up when you combine them.
