The work here happens at home. Split peas take 45 minutes of simmering to break down, which no one is doing on a canister at 7 p.m. Cook them soft, dry them, and they rehydrate in the same fifteen minutes as everything else in the bag. Skip that step and you will chew gravel.

Dry milk and the hash brown shreds are what make it a soup instead of broth with peas in it. The potato starch thickens as it sits, so the cozy time is doing real work, not just keeping it warm. Bacon is shelf-stable off the shelf, no cooler needed, and it carries most of the fat.
