The source version starts with sliced bread, which is dead weight and dead volume in a pack. Bannock solves that. Flour, baking powder, salt, and water is the whole bread, it weighs a fraction of a loaf, and it does not crush.

The two constraints are heat and hydration. Add water slowly, because dough that gets too wet cannot be fixed with more flour in the field. Cook it slow, off direct flame, on the edge of the coals. Aged hard cheese holds several days out of a fridge; soft cheese does not, so do not substitute it.
