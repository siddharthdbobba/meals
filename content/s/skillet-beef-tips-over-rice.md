This is a cooler recipe, not a backpacking one. Two pounds of beef and a bunch of fresh vegetables only make sense when a car is involved, so it lands squarely in car camping or a basecamp where weight does not matter.

The home prep is what makes it work in camp. Beef goes into the marinade in a zip bag before you leave and rides frozen flat, which keeps the rest of the cooler cold. Vegetables get cut at home. Rice comes precooked in pouches so you are not tending a second pot of boiling water over coals. That leaves one hot skillet and about fifteen minutes of actual cooking. Sear the beef and pull it out before the vegetables go in, or the pan temperature drops and everything steams instead of browning.
