This is a lunch built around not cooking. On a long hunt or a big mileage day you stop for ten minutes, not forty, and anything that needs a pot does not get eaten. Everything here goes from bag to mouth.

The cheese and jerky carry the meal. Hard aged cheese sits out for days without trouble, unlike string cheese, which turns greasy and sour by afternoon two. The sugar is there to be quick, not to be nutrition. Six and a half ounces gets you about 700 calories, which is honest for a lunch but short for a day. Plan the other two meals around that gap rather than trying to close it here.
