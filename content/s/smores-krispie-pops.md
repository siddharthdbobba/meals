This is a dessert that gets built at the kitchen counter and carried, not cooked. Candy melts matter more than they sound: they contain no cocoa butter, so they set hard at room temperature and stay set in a warm car. Real chocolate blooms and softens, and you end up with six squares glued to each other.

The toppings only stick during the short window before the coating firms up, so dip and decorate one at a time rather than lining up all six and racing the chocolate. Everything here is shelf-stable, which is why it beats actual s'mores when there is no fire or no patience for one.
