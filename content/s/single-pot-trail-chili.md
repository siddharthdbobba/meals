Chili is a formula: beans and tomato, plus spice and heat. On the trail, powdered ingredients matter more than home freshness. Tomato powder delivers real tomato flavor in minimal weight, and dehydrated beans are beans—no different once they're rehydrated than beans that came from a can, except they don't weigh three times as much.

The textured vegetable protein adds protein and body without weight. It hydrates into the broth as the chili simmers, turning powder and beans into something warm and complex. The 20-minute simmer is when this happens. Skip it and you get a thick soup instead of chili.
