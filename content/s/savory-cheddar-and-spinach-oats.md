Sweet oatmeal gets old around day three. This is the same base grain doing the opposite job: salt, fat, garlic, and enough cheese to read as a real breakfast rather than a bowl of porridge with fruit in it.

The cheese goes in off the heat on purpose. Cheddar left over a live flame breaks and leaves an oily slick with rubbery strings in it. Residual pot heat is enough to melt it smoothly. Dried spinach replaces the fresh leaf from the original, which needed a cooler and wilted in the bag anyway; it rehydrates in the boil and adds bulk without adding weight.
