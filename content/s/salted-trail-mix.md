Paddling sweats out electrolytes faster than most activities, especially salt. Nuts cover the three things your body needs on the water: salt to replace what's gone, protein for muscles, and fat for steady energy.

Dried fruit adds carbs without weight, and the texture keeps you interested on a long day. Mix it at home and eat straight from the bag.
