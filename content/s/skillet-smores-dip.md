A cast iron pan is the point here. It holds heat long after it comes off the coals, so the dip stays molten while eight people work through it instead of seizing up after the second cracker. A thin pan gives you two good minutes and then a puck.

The order matters. Chocolate on the bottom melts slowly and evenly against the iron; marshmallows on top get direct radiant heat and brown. Put them in together and you get scorched sugar over chocolate that is still solid. Keep the pan over coals, not flame — the sugar catches fast and there is no recovering it.

This is a car camping recipe and stays one. There is no backpacking version worth carrying: the whole thing is fat and sugar in a heavy pan, and it does not keep once melted.
