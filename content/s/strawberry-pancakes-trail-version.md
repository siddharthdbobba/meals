The original is a kitchen recipe: fresh milk, fresh eggs, a box of U-pick strawberries. None of that rides in a pack. Powdered whole milk and powdered egg do the same structural jobs, and freeze-dried strawberries hold their shape in the batter instead of bleeding water into it the way fresh or frozen fruit does. That water matters. Fruit juice thins the batter and the pancakes go flat and gummy in the middle.

The rest is heat management. A canister stove concentrates flame in a narrow ring, so the center scorches while the edges stay raw. Keep the flame as low as it will hold, keep the pancakes small, and grease the pan before every batch. Over a campfire, cook on coals rather than flame for the same reason.
