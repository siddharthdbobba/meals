This started as an oven muffin recipe, which is useless without an oven. The batter itself is fine on the trail, so it goes into a pan instead of a tin. The buttermilk is the part worth keeping. Its acid reacts with the baking soda for lift, and the tang cuts the sugar. Powdered buttermilk does the same job dry and keeps for a year in a bag.

A covered pan on a very low flame is a crude oven. The trapped steam sets the top while the bottom browns, which is the only way to get the center cooked before the base burns. Quick oats matter here too: in twenty minutes over a stove, whole rolled oats never soften, and you get a cake full of hard flecks. Fat is not optional. Cut the butter and the cake goes dry and sticks to the pan.
