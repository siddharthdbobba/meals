The lime juice is the reason both meats can share a pan. Acid loosens the flank steak enough that it finishes in the same twenty minutes as chicken breast, so you are not juggling two cook times over a fire you only half control.

This one stays a cooler recipe. Raw meat and fresh peppers do not have a shelf-stable version worth eating, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp with a cold box, not in a pack. Do all the slicing and marinating at home: at the fire you are opening two bags and stirring. The sour cream and guacamole from the original are gone for the same reason the cooler is doing enough work already. Salsa in a jar covers the same ground.
