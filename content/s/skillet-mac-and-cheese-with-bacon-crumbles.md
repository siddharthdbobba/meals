The oven is the part that does not travel, so the crisp topping moves to the front of the recipe. Toasting the panko in a dry pot before anything wet goes in gets you the same texture without baking, and you scatter it on at the end instead of underneath a broiler.

Everything fresh got swapped. Milk and half and half become powdered whole milk mixed at camp, the roux is mostly cornstarch so it thickens without long whisking, and raw bacon becomes precooked crumbles that need no pan and no cooler. Cheddar in a wax block will ride a couple of days in a pack in cool weather; in heat, carry it in a cooler or leave it out and lean on the parmesan.
