This started as a way to use up Thanksgiving turkey, which is not a problem anyone has on trail. The trail version keeps the structure and swaps every perishable: freeze-dried turkey for leftovers, bean flakes for canned beans, bouillon for stock, dried pepper and onion for fresh. Nothing needs a cooler and nothing weighs what a can does.

Blooming the spices in oil before the water goes in is the step people skip, and it is the difference between soup and hot salty water. Smoked paprika in particular needs fat to open up. The bean flakes go in at the end because they thicken on contact and will scorch if you boil them. Lime last, off the heat, for the same reason it works at home.
