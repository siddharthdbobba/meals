Crackers turn to dust by day three and tortillas go green. Bread you make at camp has neither problem, because the flour keeps indefinitely and the bread only exists for twenty minutes. Baking powder does the lifting, so there is no yeast, no proofing, and no waiting.

The milk powder is not filler. It browns the crust and tightens the crumb, which otherwise comes out chalky. The real skill is heat control: coals raked off to the side, or a stove turned as low as it will go without cutting out. High heat gives you a black bottom and raw dough in the middle, every time.
