The ten-minute soak off the stove is the whole recipe. Instant hash browns and dried beans rehydrate at different speeds, and boiling them together long enough to soften the beans turns the potatoes to paste. Simmering for a minute and then letting residual heat finish the job in a covered pot gets both there without wrecking either, and it burns almost no fuel.

Salsa dries into a hard, sticky flake that wants to sit at the bottom of the pot in one lump. Stir it in aggressively after the heat is off. Water is measured tight here on purpose: at 320 ml there is nothing left to drain, which is the only way this stays a one-pot meal.
