This started as a skillet dinner that leans on fresh cheese and a long braise in the oven. Neither travels. Instant rice takes the place of the raw rice so the pan only has to hold a boil for five minutes, and shelf-stable grated parmesan replaces the block you would otherwise grate at home. Pouch chicken goes in last because it is already cooked and only needs warming.

The pepper is the whole point, and it belongs in the hot oil before the water. Blooming it that way pulls the heat out into the fat, where it coats every grain. Dumped in at the end it just sits on top and tastes dusty. Keep the pan off direct flame while the rice steams or the bottom scorches before the top is done.
