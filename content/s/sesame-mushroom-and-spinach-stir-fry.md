Dried mushrooms rehydrate completely and pull their weight in flavor. Malabar spinach won't travel, so this uses dehydrated spinach flakes instead, which soften quickly in hot water. The cooking oil helps everything reconstitute without turning to mush. A brief high-heat finish drives off excess water and builds back the texture you lose by going dehydrated.

Sesame oil and seeds carry the bulk of the calories and flavor—they're doing the work that transforms mushrooms and greens from side dish to dinner. This works alone as a bowl, or over instant rice or noodles. On the trail, it's as close to a fresh vegetable dinner as you'll get without carrying actual weight.
