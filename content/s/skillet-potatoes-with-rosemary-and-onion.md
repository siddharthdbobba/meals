This started as an oven recipe and an oven is not something you carry. A cast iron skillet over coals does the same job as long as you accept that the heat comes from one side instead of all around. That is what the lid and the stirring are for: the cover traps enough heat to cook the interiors while the pan face browns the outsides.

Cut the potatoes smaller than you would at home. Coals run hotter and less evenly than an oven, and a big quarter will scorch on the outside while the middle stays raw. Whole potatoes and onions keep for days without a cooler, so this is a fine day-three dinner, not just a first-night one.
