Inner loins are the two thin strips tucked under the spine, and they are the leanest, most tender muscle on the animal. That cuts both ways. There is almost no intramuscular fat, so they go from rare to dry in under a minute of extra heat. Butter is not a garnish here, it is the fat the meat does not have.

Cook them whole rather than in pieces. A single thick strip gives you more margin before the center overcooks, and it holds heat through the rest. Slicing early dumps the juice onto the plate. Five minutes off the heat is the whole difference between this and jerky.
