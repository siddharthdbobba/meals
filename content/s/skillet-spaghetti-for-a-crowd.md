This is a scout-troop dinner, and it earns that job by being hard to ruin. Two burners, two pots, and one ingredient you actually have to time. The sauce comes out of a can already finished, so the only real cooking is browning the meat and not walking away from the noodles.

Draining the fat matters more than it seems. A pound of ground beef sheds a few tablespoons of grease, and jarred sauce has nothing in it to absorb that, so it pools on top and coats the noodles. The beef is the only cooler-dependent part here. Cook it the first night out, or use the lentil swap below and carry the whole meal on a shelf.
