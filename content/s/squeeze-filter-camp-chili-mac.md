This one exists because the water is the constraint, not the food. A squeeze filter with a soft reservoir lets you draw from a cattle pond or a silty creek and put clean water straight into the pot, which means you can cook a pasta dish somewhere you would otherwise ration what you carried. Filter first, then boil. The boil handles what the filter misses.

The rest is built for one pot and one boil. Instant beans and tomato powder rehydrate off residual heat, so they go in after the flame is out and do not extend cook time. Cheese powder and oil go last for the same reason: heat makes the cheese seize, and the oil is where a third of the calories live.
