This is a heavy car-camp meal that lives or dies on grease management. Rendering the sausage first is not a shortcut for washing a second pan — it is where the cooking fat comes from. Smoked sausage gives up enough to fry four pounds of potatoes, and that fat carries the smoke and the spice into every slice. Pull the sausage out before the potatoes go in or it turns to jerky over the next twenty minutes.

Thin slices and heat control are the other half. Cast iron over a fire has no dial, so move the pan instead: closer to the coals to brown, farther off to let the insides soften. An adjustable grate makes this easy, a couple of flat rocks make it possible. There is nothing to rehydrate here and no water needed, which is why this stays a cooler meal rather than a pack meal.
