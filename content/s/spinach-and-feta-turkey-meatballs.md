Spanakopita without the phyllo, which is the part that does not survive a cooler anyway. The filling is the good part: spinach, feta, dill, lemon. Bound with egg and panko and turkey, it becomes something you can cook in one pan on a grate.

The lemon butter is split for a reason. Half goes inside the mix, where it keeps lean turkey from drying into gravel. Half goes over the top, where it pools in the pan and becomes the sauce you spoon back on. The spinach goes in raw and chopped; it wilts as you mix and again as it cooks, so precooking it in camp is wasted fuel. Large meatballs are deliberate — small ones overcook before you can check them by firelight.
