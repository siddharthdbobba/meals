The original is two baked russets, an hour in an oven, and a scoop of cold sour cream. None of that travels. Potato flakes get you the same starch in three minutes, and the salsa and sour cream go in as powders, which is where all the flavor of the original filling actually lived.

The veggies cook first because they need real simmer time and the potatoes need none. Reusing the pot without rinsing keeps the seasoning that stuck to the bottom, and the leftover veggie water thins the mash instead of going on the ground. Freeze-dried cheese melts into hot potato better than a hard cheese does, though a crumbled hunk of jack works if that is what you carry.
