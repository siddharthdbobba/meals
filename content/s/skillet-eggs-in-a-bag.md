The source version is fresh eggs cracked into a bowl at the campsite, which works from a cooler and nowhere else. Powdered whole egg is the swap that makes it carryable. It rehydrates into something close enough, and it drops the weight to roughly a third. Summer sausage and hard cheddar were already shelf-stable, so those stayed.

The mushrooms need their own soak first. Dropped in dry they pull water out of the eggs and leave you with a rubbery pan. Low heat matters more here than with fresh eggs: reconstituted egg has less fat holding it together and will stick to a thin camp pan the moment you stop stirring.
