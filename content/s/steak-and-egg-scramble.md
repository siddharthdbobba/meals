Freeze-dried ingredients are the key to a protein-heavy breakfast that weighs almost nothing and never spoils. The beef and eggs rehydrate in cold water—no boiling—and come back close to the fresh version. Cooking them together in one pot with a little oil takes about 15 minutes from sleep to food, making this faster than fire-building on cold mornings.

This scramble is high protein and genuinely satiating. The oil matters; it carries calories and keeps the eggs from tasting institutional. To cook for a group, scale the ingredients by thirds and use a larger skillet. The method stays the same.
