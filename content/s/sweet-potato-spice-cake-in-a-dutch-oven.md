The original is a layer cake with cream cheese frosting, which is two things a campsite cannot give you: an oven that holds temperature and a fridge. The fix is to bake it flat in a dutch oven and replace the frosting with a powdered-sugar glaze mixed in a mug. Sweet potato flakes do the same job as roasted sweet potato at a fraction of the weight, and they rehydrate in the batter water without a separate step.

Coal placement is the whole recipe. Roughly two-thirds of the heat belongs on the lid, because coals underneath will burn the bottom long before the middle sets. Rotating the oven and lid in opposite directions evens out the hot spot every fire has. The cake tastes like a spice cake, not like sweet potato; the potato is there for moisture, which matters more when you are baking over an uneven heat source.
