This one does not compress into a dry bag, so treat it as what it is: a car camp dinner for the night the cooler is still cold. Everything cooks in one skillet in sequence, and each stage feeds the next. The beef leaves fat and browned bits behind, the pancetta crisps in that fat, the pine nuts toast in what is left, and the balsamic and marmalade lift all of it off the pan as dressing. No separate dressing bowl, no second pot.

Rest the beef before you slice it or the juice ends up on the cutting board instead of in the salad. The brie should go on last and unheated, so residual warmth from the beef softens it rather than melting it into grease. Pine nuts go from pale to burnt in under a minute over a fire, which is the one step worth watching.
