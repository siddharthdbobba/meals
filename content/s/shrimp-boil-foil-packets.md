A real shrimp boil needs a stockpot, gallons of water, and a way to dump it all. None of that travels. Foil packets get you the same thing by steaming everything in butter and its own liquid instead, and the only cleanup is a ball of foil.

The pre-boiled potatoes and corn are the whole trick. Shrimp needs about five minutes and raw potatoes need forty, so cooking the vegetables at home puts both on the same clock. Indirect heat matters for the same reason: over flame the butter scorches and the shrimp turn to rubber before the packet is even hot through. Shrimp is cooler food, not a hot-day-three meal, so plan it for the first night.
