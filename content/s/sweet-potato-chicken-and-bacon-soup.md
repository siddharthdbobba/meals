The original is a home kitchen job: whole sweet potatoes boiled forty-five minutes, then mashed. That is a lot of fuel for something a dehydrator already did. Dried sweet potato flakes hit the same texture in a minute of simmering, and they carry the mash step with them.

Bacon grease is what made the source version taste like more than squash, so the olive oil replaces it and the cooked bacon bits carry the smoke. Add the chicken and bacon off the heat. Boiling pouch chicken turns it stringy, and the bacon loses its bite the longer it sits in liquid.
