The original is a baked casserole, which is not happening on a stove. What survives the translation is the structure: zucchini, meat sauce, and something white and soft between them. Dried zucchini is the whole reason this works on trail. It weighs almost nothing, and the water it gave up at home is water you would otherwise have to boil off.

The flour dredge in the oven version exists to soak up zucchini moisture. Here that job goes to potato flakes, added at the end, where they thicken the pot without lumping. Cottage cheese and egg are a fridge problem, so milk powder and parmesan carry the dairy instead. Both go in off the heat. Boiled milk powder curdles and turns grainy.
