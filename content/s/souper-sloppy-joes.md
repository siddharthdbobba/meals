This is a cooler meal for the first night out, when the ground beef is still cold and the fire is already going. The canned soup does two jobs at once: it is the sauce and the vegetables, so there is nothing to chop and no second pot.

Draining the fat matters more than it seems. Leave it in and the soup never thickens, and the filling runs straight out of the roll. Simmering after the soup goes in is what cooks the liquid down to something a bun can hold. It is not backpacking food, but it feeds four people out of one skillet in twenty minutes.
