The soak is the whole recipe. Salt water overnight softens the nuts and starts them germinating, which is fine at home and useless on trail, because a damp almond is a mold problem in a ziplock. Drying them back down to hard is what makes them carryable.

Low heat matters for texture as much as anything else. Dried slowly, the nut goes brittle all the way through and snaps. Roasted hot, the outside browns before the inside gives up its water, and a week later the bag tastes stale. Salt is the only seasoning that survives the soak, since everything else washes out with the drain.
