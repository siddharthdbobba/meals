Cast iron and canned tomatoes do most of the work here. The tomato juice is the only liquid in the pan, which means you carry no extra water and the sauce reduces into something that clings instead of pooling. Dried onion, dill, and lemon peel replace the fresh versions one for one in flavor and roughly nothing in weight.

The fish is pouched or canned rather than fresh, so the covered five minutes at the end is reheating, not cooking. Pull it early if you are unsure. Half the olives and feta go in at the end on purpose: cooked feta melts into the sauce and disappears, and the raw half is what you actually taste.
