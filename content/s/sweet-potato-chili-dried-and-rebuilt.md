The original is a slow cooker chili with no beans in it, which makes it unusually easy to move to the trail. Beans are the thing that never rehydrates on schedule. Sweet potatoes do, as long as you dice them small before drying and give them a real soak off the heat.

The canned tomatoes and tomato sauce collapse into three tablespoons of tomato powder, which is most of the weight savings. Cook the beef at home and drain it hard before drying: fat left in the meat is what turns a bag of dried beef rancid two weeks into a resupply box. The oil you stir in at the end puts those calories back where they can't spoil.
