The original wanted a colander and a kitchen. This version keeps only what matters: you cook the pasta in as little water as it will tolerate, pour off most of it, and let the last half cup do the work of a sauce. Starch from the pasta thickens it just enough to hang onto the oil.

Smoked salmon in a foil packet is the whole reason this travels. It is shelf-stable until opened and it needs no cooking, so it goes in after the flame is off and stays in flakes instead of turning to paste. Capers and hard grated parmesan are the salt; go easy before you taste.
