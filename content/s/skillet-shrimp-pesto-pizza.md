Camp pizza fails in one of two ways: raw dough under burnt cheese, or a crust welded to the pan. Coals instead of flame fix the first, and cornmeal under an oiled skillet fixes the second. Rapid-rise yeast is doing the other piece of work here, buying a single 45-minute proof instead of two.

The shrimp come from a pouch and the pesto from a jar, which is the whole reason this travels. Fresh mozzarella would flood the crust with water, so the low-moisture block is not a compromise, it is the better choice. Lemon juice and zest go on the shrimp off the heat, since heat flattens the zest and the shrimp are already cooked and only need warming.
