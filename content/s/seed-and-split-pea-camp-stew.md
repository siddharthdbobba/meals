This started as a list of cheap protein sources rather than a dish, so most of it had to be rebuilt for a pack. Eggs and cheddar are out. What survives the trip is the plant column: split peas, sunflower and pumpkin seeds, and greens dried down to nothing. Together they clear 30 g of protein for under a dollar and about five and a half ounces of carry weight.

The seeds go in off the heat on purpose. Boiled, they go soft and taste flat; stirred in at the end they hold some bite and their fat stays put in the pot instead of skinning over. Split peas are the fuel cost here — twenty minutes of simmering is real gas. Soak them at home and dehydrate them if you are counting canisters, or use the lentil swap below.
