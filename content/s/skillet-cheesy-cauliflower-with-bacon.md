The original is a home skillet dish leaning on fresh cauliflower, whole milk, and refrigerated bacon. All three got swapped. Freeze-dried cauliflower comes back close enough once it browns, milk powder plus cornstarch makes the same sauce out of a bag, and precooked bacon is shelf-stable until you cut the seal.

Drain the cauliflower before browning it, and save that water for the sauce. Skip the draining and you get a pan of soup with cheese floating in it. The cornstarch is what turns the milk into something that clings; without it the liquid just pools under the vegetables.
