This is a home oven recipe rebuilt for coals. Two things had to change. Frozen meatballs became canned or vacuum-packed ones, since nothing stays frozen past the first afternoon. Store-bought refrigerated dough became a dry crust mix, which weighs less, does not care about temperature, and rises fine in a bowl while the pasta boils.

The cornmeal is not a garnish. Rolling on cornmeal instead of flour keeps the bottom crust from gluing itself to the oven and gives it some grit to crisp against. The other thing worth respecting is the coal ratio: most of the heat goes on the lid, because a dutch oven bottom sitting on embers will burn a crust long before the top browns.
