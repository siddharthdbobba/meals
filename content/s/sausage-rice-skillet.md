Freeze-dried sausage doesn't get enough credit in trail cooking. It carries protein and fat without a cooler, and brings genuine salt and savory depth to a dish that would otherwise taste like hot cardboard. Instant rice alone is fuel. Rice with sausage, sour cream, butter, and cheese is dinner.

This is a one-pot meal designed to move: one boil, one stir, then you walk away for 15 minutes while the rice absorbs everything. The sour cream powder is the key—it prevents the rice from turning to mush and adds the tang that makes this taste like real food instead of trail subsistence.
