The source for this one was a homestead feed formula: split peas, barley, wheat, oats, millet, sunflower, kelp, corn. Those are all human food, and the ratio is a sound one for a hot breakfast — legume for protein, grains for starch, seed for fat. What it needed was a cook time short enough for a canister and quantities scaled to one person instead of a flock. Fish meal, mealworms, alfalfa, and oyster shell stayed behind.

Cracking the peas and barley at home is the whole trick. Whole pearl barley wants forty minutes; broken up it softens in fifteen, and the split peas fall apart into a thickener at the same rate. The oats go in off the heat because they only need to swell, and the sunflower seeds carry the fat that makes it hold you until lunch.
