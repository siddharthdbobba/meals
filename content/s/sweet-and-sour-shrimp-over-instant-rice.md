The original is a marinade recipe, and a marinade is dead weight on trail. What actually matters is the sauce: fruit juice, acid, soy, and cornstarch, cooked down until it clings. All four are available as powders, so the whole thing collapses into one premix bag and one boil.

Cornstarch is the piece that needs attention. It sets fast and only once, so mix it dry with the sugar and juice powders at home and pull the pot off the moment it coats the spoon. Reheating past that point breaks the sauce back to water. Soaking the shrimp first and reusing that water for the sauce saves both fuel and the flavor that leaches out.
