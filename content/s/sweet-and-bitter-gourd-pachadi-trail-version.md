Bitter gourd is the whole point and the whole problem. Sugar does not remove bitterness, it sits next to it, and the two together read as something more interesting than either alone. Jaggery is the sweetener that works because it brings its own molasses edge instead of clean sweetness.

The home version uses fresh gourd, fresh coconut, and a fresh tomato, none of which survive a pack. Dried gourd slices rehydrate close enough, dried coconut carries the fat, and tomato powder handles the acid. The jaggery goes in last on purpose: it burns fast and once it catches, the pot is ruined and the dinner tastes like ash.
