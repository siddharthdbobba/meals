The original uses butter in the sauce, which does not travel. Olive oil does the same job here: it carries the sriracha, sticks to the popcorn, and doubles as the popping fat. Nothing in the pot needs a cooler.

The three-kernel test and the off-heat pause matter more than they sound. Cold kernels dropped into hot oil pop in waves, so the first batch burns while the last is still sitting there. Letting them all warm together gets most of them popping at once. Crack the lid or the steam softens everything into chew.
