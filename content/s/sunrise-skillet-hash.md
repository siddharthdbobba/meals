The original is a campground skillet built on a dozen fresh eggs, a pound of raw pork, and a cooler full of chopped vegetables. None of that travels well past day one, so everything here is the dried version: carton hash browns instead of shredded raw potato, cured or precooked sausage instead of bulk ground meat, dried pepper and onion instead of a cutting board.

The soak matters more than the fry. Dry potato shreds thrown straight into hot oil brown on the outside and stay chalky in the middle, so they get hot water and ten quiet minutes first. Drain hard after that. Wet potatoes steam instead of crusting, and the crust is the whole point of cooking this in a pan rather than a pot.
