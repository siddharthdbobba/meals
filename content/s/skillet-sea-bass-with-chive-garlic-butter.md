The original is a stovetop sear finished in an oven, which is not a thing you have in camp. On a grate over coals the fix is heat management instead: sear over the hot center, then slide the pan to the edge and let residual heat carry the fish through. Cast iron holds enough heat to do that without a second burner.

The compound butter is the part worth doing at home. Mixing softened butter with herbs and garlic in a bowl at a picnic table is a mess, and the log form means you carry exactly two portions and dose them without a knife. It also melts into an instant pan sauce, which is the only sauce you get when the whole meal is one skillet.
