This is a base, not a meal. Two things make it work in camp: the hard sear on the first side builds flavor that no seasoning packet can fake, and the splash of water under a lid turns the pan into a steam oven so the inside finishes before the outside goes to leather.

The pan liquid is the payoff. Shred straight into it and the meat reabsorbs what it lost, which matters when you are reheating it over a stove the next day. Thighs are the safer choice over a campfire because their fat buys you a few minutes of slack on the timing. This needs a cooler and real cookware, so it belongs at a car camp or basecamp, not in a pack.
