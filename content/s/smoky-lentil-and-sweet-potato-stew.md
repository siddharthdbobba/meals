The home version of this is a 40-minute pot of stew built on fresh onion, celery, sweet potatoes, and a can of tomatoes. None of that travels. Dehydrated sweet potato dice rehydrate in the same time the lentils need, dried onion and celery carry the aromatics, and tomato powder does what the can did at a fraction of the weight. Chicken broth became bouillon so the recipe stays vegan.

Two things are worth not skipping. The vinegar goes in off the heat, at the end: it sharpens a pot that otherwise tastes flat and brown, and cooking it drives that off. The oil is not optional either. Lentils and sweet potato are almost fat-free, and without it this is a large volume of food that does not sit like dinner.
