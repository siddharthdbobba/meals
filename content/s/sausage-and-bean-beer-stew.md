This is a car-camping stew and it stays one. The weight is in two cans and a bottle, none of which belong in a pack, and the fat in a smoked sausage is what makes the beans taste like a meal instead of a side.

The beer goes in gradually for a reason. Bean liquid is starchy, and adding liquid a splash at a time lets that starch thicken as it goes. Dump the whole bottle at once and you get bean soup you then have to boil back down. Tomato paste goes in near the end because it scorches on a fire grate faster than you expect.
