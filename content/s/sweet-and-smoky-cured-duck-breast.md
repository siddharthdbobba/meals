This is bacon method applied to a bird. The salt and cure pull water out and fix the color, the syrup balances the salt, and the smoke does the rest. Everything that makes it work happens at home over several days, which is the point: what you carry is finished food that needs no stove, no water, and no pot.

Weigh the meat before you measure anything. Curing salt is dosed by weight of meat, and the 16 g salt / 1.6 g cure figures here are tied to 624 g of breast. Scale both up or down together. On the trail this is dense protein and fat in a small packet, but a cure and a smoke are not shelf stability, so it rides in a cooler or gets eaten in the first two days.
