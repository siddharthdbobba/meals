The original is deep-fried in four quarts of oil, which nobody is carrying up a trail. Pan-frying in three tablespoons gets close: the cornmeal still browns and crisps at the edges, it just does it on one face at a time. Flatten the rounds or the centers stay raw.

Buttermilk powder replaces three cups of refrigerated buttermilk and does the same job, which is giving the baking powder something acidic to react against. The eggs are gone; there is enough starch and liquid to hold a cake this small together without them. Keep the batter stiff. A loose one spreads into pancakes and steams instead of frying.
