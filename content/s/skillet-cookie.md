This is a home dessert dragged outdoors. The original mixes everything in the skillet and finishes it in an oven at 170 C for 20 minutes. Coals do the same job if you keep them low: a bed underneath and a ring on the lid gives you top and bottom heat without a hot spot burning the bottom out.

Two changes matter. Fresh eggs are the one ingredient that will not ride in a pack, so powdered whole egg goes in instead and rehydrates in a cup. The ice cream is gone for obvious reasons, and it was never doing much except being cold. Underbake the center a little. It keeps setting in the hot pan after you pull it, and a cookie you can scoop is the point.
