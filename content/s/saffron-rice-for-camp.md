Saffron rice is a home recipe that needs almost nothing to move outdoors. The original leans on a rice cooker, a carton of broth, and bottled lemon juice. In camp that becomes one pot, bouillon powder, and lemon powder, which weigh nothing and do not spoil. Butter turns into ghee for the same reason.

The saffron is the only expensive part, and it earns its keep only if you bloom it first. Crushed threads sitting in hot water release their color into the whole pot; whole threads dropped into cold water mostly stay yellow specks. The lemon and salt are there to keep the flavor from going flat, which is what happens to plain broth rice after a long day.
