The source for this was a gag recipe from a hunting forum, the kind where you stuff a bird with popcorn and wait for it to blow the oven door open. The three real ingredients underneath it, though, are sound: chicken, butter, bread stuffing. That is a roast dinner, and it packs down to a box and two pouches.

Nothing here is baked. Stuffing mix is already dried bread, so it rehydrates off the heat in about five minutes and does not need an oven at all. The butter matters more than it looks: without it the mix is dry and salty, and it is where most of the calories in the pot come from. Pouched chicken goes in last because it is already cooked and will shred to nothing if you stir it over a flame.
