The original wanted a pot of boiling potatoes, a frypan, fresh milk, and butter. That is two burners and a cooler. The swap is instant potato flakes with milk powder and butter powder, which get you the same mash off one pot and one boil.

The rest holds up as written. Tomato paste from a tube replaces the tin, dried vegetables replace frozen mixed veg, and a shelf-stable smoked sausage stands in for fresh links. The sausage renders enough fat to brown itself, and that fat is what carries the gravy. Cook the gravy first and the mash second, so the mash goes on the plate hot.
