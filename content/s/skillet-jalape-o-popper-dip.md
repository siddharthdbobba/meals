This is a dairy-heavy dip, so it only works on a trip with a real cooler. There is no trail version of it worth writing. Everything but the bacon is a cold ingredient, and cream cheese that has ridden warm for two days is not something to serve to a group.

The fat ratio is doing the work. Sour cream and mayo thin the cream cheese enough that it melts into a scoopable dip instead of splitting into grease and curd. Coals under a grate, not flame: direct fire scorches the bottom long before the middle is hot. The panko and butter give you a crust that holds up while people dig at it.
