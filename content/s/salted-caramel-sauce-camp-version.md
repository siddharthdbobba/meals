Caramel is one ingredient doing all the work: sugar heated until it browns and turns bitter enough to be interesting. The other three just stop it. Butter and milk drop the temperature and cut the sauce with fat, which is why they go in off the heat and why the pot foams when they hit.

The home version uses half and half, which is not a thing you carry. Whole milk powder reconstituted thick does the same job, and it has enough fat that the sauce still comes out smooth. Two real cautions: a canister stove runs hotter and less evenly than a home burner, so keep the flame low and watch the color instead of the clock, and molten sugar burns are serious. Long sleeves, and do not walk away from the pot.
