The seal is what makes this work, and the seal depends on two things: a tortilla warm enough to pleat without splitting, and a skillet hot enough to set the seam before the folds relax. Cook seam side down first, always. Everything else is stacking.

The tostada in the middle is structural, not decorative. It keeps the wet layer above from soaking into the beef layer below, which is why this holds together in a hand at a picnic table instead of collapsing. Camp changes here are minor: jarred nacho cheese instead of anything refrigerated, and the vegetables prepped at home so you are not cutting lettuce on a tailgate.
