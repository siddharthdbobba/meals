This started as an oven tray bake and moved onto a fire, which is a smaller change than it sounds. A sealed foil packet on coals is an oven with worse temperature control. The fix is cutting everything small and rotating the packet so no one side sits on the hottest spot for half an hour.

Thigh meat is the reason this works at camp. Breast goes dry the moment the coals run hot, and coals always run hot somewhere. The chickpeas add protein without needing to cook, and the whole garlic cloves turn to paste in their skins — squeeze them out at the end and they do the job a sauce would.
