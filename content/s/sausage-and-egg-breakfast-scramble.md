The original is an oven dish baked in tortilla bowls, which is not a thing you have at camp. Everything here does the same job in one pan: powdered egg instead of shells you would crack in your pack, vacuum-sealed sausage instead of refrigerated, dried onion and pepper instead of fresh. Soft tortillas replace the baked shells and pack better than anything rigid.

Rehydrating the vegetables separately is the step people skip. Dropped in dry, they pull water out of the egg and stay tough. The cheese and bacon go in after the heat is off so the cheese melts into the egg instead of frying onto the pan.
