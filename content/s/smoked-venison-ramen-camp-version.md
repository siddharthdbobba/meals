The original version of this starts with a smoked venison shank and five hours of simmering. Neither of those fits in a pack, so the flavor comes from three dry things instead: smoked jerky, kombu, and shiitake. Jerky gives you the smoke and meat, kombu gives glutamate, shiitake gives the dark savory bottom that would otherwise take bones and time. Fifteen minutes of simmer gets you most of the way there.

Miso goes in off the heat. It is a live ferment and boiling flattens it into salt. The sesame oil at the end is not garnish either — the broth is nearly fat-free without it, and fat is what makes a soup read as dinner rather than a hot drink.
