Everything rehydrates in place because each component—rice, chicken, vegetables, beans—is already partially cooked or dehydrated before it hits the pot. The southwestern flavor comes from chili powder and paprika together, which build heat and earthiness without the weight of heavy cumin. Dehydrated chicken carries the protein; the vegetables and beans add texture and volume without adding weight.

The critical variable is rehydration time. Twelve minutes is the minimum if your water stays hot; fifteen is more forgiving and accounts for cooler conditions or lower elevation. Instant rice is essential—long-grain rice needs more water and more time. Dice-sized chicken pieces rehydrate faster than chunks, so prep accordingly when you dehydrate at home.
