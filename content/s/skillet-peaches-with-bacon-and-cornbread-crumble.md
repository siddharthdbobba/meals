Fresh peaches are the whole point of the original and the one thing you cannot carry. Dried slices get you most of the way back: they hold their sugar, and rehydrating them in a small amount of water leaves you with fruit and syrup instead of fruit soup. Measure the water carefully. Too much and you are boiling it off for twenty minutes over someone's dinner fire.

The bacon is doing the same job it does in the skillet original, which is salt and fat against sweet fruit. Precooked crumbles are the trail version and they need no cold storage. Cornbread dried hard at home replaces a cobbler topping you have no way to bake out here; it softens in the syrup on contact, so add it last and eat soon after.
