Carbonara at a campsite normally means a big pot of boiling water, a colander, and a grey-water tank you did not want to fill. Ribboned courgette skips all of it. You get the same job done by the same sauce, in one skillet, with no water carried or dumped.

The sauce is an emulsion, not a scramble, and the only thing standing between the two is pan temperature. Take the skillet fully off the burner before the eggs go in. Camp stoves run hotter and less evenly than a home range, so give it longer off the heat than you think you need. The courgette also releases water as it cooks; drive that off first or the sauce will never tighten.
