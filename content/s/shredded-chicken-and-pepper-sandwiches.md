The original was a way to finish off a rotisserie chicken. On the trail there is no rotisserie and no leftovers, so the chicken comes from foil pouches, which are shelf-stable, already cooked, and shred with a fork. Dried peppers and onion stand in for fresh once you are past the first day out of the cooler.

The sauce does the real work. Bouillon plus tomato sauce reduced down to a glaze is what keeps a bun from going soggy and what makes pouch chicken taste like something other than pouch chicken. Cook it until the liquid clings to the meat. The slaw goes on dry and cold for crunch, so it can be the last bagged thing you have.
