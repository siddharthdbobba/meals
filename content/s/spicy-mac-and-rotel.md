Boxed mac is engineered to be foolproof, which is exactly what you want at the end of a driving day. The tomatoes and chiles are doing the work: acid and heat cut through cheese sauce that otherwise sits flat after the first few bites.

Two details matter. Buy the box with the liquid cheese pouch, because the powdered kind needs milk and butter you do not have. And drain the can properly, since the packing liquid is mostly water and it will thin the sauce into something you drink instead of eat.
