The original is a crock pot dump meal: raw chicken thighs, canned beans, canned corn, and a jar of salsa, cooked slow and shredded. None of that travels. Freeze-dried chicken carries the same protein at a fraction of the weight, dehydrated beans skip the can, and salsa powder replaces sixteen ounces of mostly water. The spice ratio stays as written because that is where the flavor actually lives.

Order matters more than technique here. Beans and freeze-dried chicken rehydrate slowly, so they get water before the rice does; put the rice in first and you end up with mush wrapped around hard beans. The sour cream and shredded cheddar are gone since neither survives a pack, and guacamole powder mixed cold does the job of both — fat and something bright against all that cumin.
