This is a proper bread bake, so it belongs at a car camp or basecamp where you have an hour to let dough rise and a fire you can burn down to coals. The dutch oven is doing what an oven does: trapping heat around the dough so it sets before the bottom scorches. Coals on the lid are not optional. Heat from below alone gives you raw tops and a black underside.

The rolls are cut from a rolled log, which is what gets fat and cheese into every layer instead of only on top. Rotating the pot and lid in opposite directions each five minutes is the whole trick to even browning over an uneven fire. The dipping sauce takes ten minutes on a second burner or the edge of the coals and is mostly salt and tomato paste concentrating while the bread bakes.
