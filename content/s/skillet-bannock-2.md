Bannock is bread made by people without ovens, which is why it works at camp. There is no yeast and no rising time. Baking powder does the lift the moment water hits it, so the clock starts when you mix and the pan should already be warm.

Low heat is the whole game. A hot pan gives you a burnt crust over raw dough, every time. Ghee instead of butter is the only real change for the trail: it does not need a cooler and it will not go rancid in a week of pack heat. The fat also matters for texture, so do not cut it down to save weight.
