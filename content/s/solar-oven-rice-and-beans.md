A solar oven is a slow cooker that runs on sightlines instead of fuel. It holds around 100 to 120 C, which is enough to cook rice and rehydrate beans but nowhere near a boil. That means time replaces heat: four hours instead of twenty minutes, and no fire ban, no canister, no smoke.

The ingredient list is built around that ceiling. Whole dried beans need real boiling and will stay gravel, so this uses instant bean flakes that only need hot water and patience. Tomato powder and dried vegetables go in dry with everything else because the long soak in a sealed pot rehydrates them on its own. The oil goes in at the end, where it still tastes like oil.
