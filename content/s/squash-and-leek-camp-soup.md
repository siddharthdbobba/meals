The original is squash, leek, and stock simmered soft and blended. There is no blender on a trail, so potato flakes do the smoothing instead. They pull the whole pot together into something with body without a second dish.

The home dehydrator step is the real work. Thin slices of squash dry to almost nothing and rehydrate in about ten minutes; thick chunks will still be crunchy when your fuel runs out. Oil and hemp hearts are there because the source soup is 34 calories a bowl, which is not dinner after a long day.
