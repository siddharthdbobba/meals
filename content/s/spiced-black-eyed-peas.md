Powders replace fresh aromatics—fresh ginger, garlic, and onions don't survive a backpack. They toast in oil and hit the same notes. Pre-cooked, dehydrated peas are essential because raw beans demand forty minutes at altitude, an impossible time budget for dinner. Dehydrated peas rehydrate in the time it takes to toast your spices.

Oil is what makes this a dinner. Three tablespoons carry enough fat to get you through a cold night. Lemon juice finishes it, cutting through the richness and waking the spices. Skip the oil or the lemon and you have warm beans, not a meal.
