  - "Cut a wide lid off the pumpkin around the stem. Scrape the seeds and strings out until the inside walls are smooth. Salt and pepper the cavity."

  - "Brown the sausage in the dutch oven over the coals, breaking it up as it goes. Pour off most of the fat."

This is a car-camping recipe because a four-pound pumpkin is not a backpacking item. It earns the weight by being the pot: the pumpkin steams itself from the inside, and the walls soften into something you scrape off and eat with the filling. Nothing is wasted and nothing else gets dirty.

The original goes in a home oven, which a dutch oven over coals imitates well enough if you keep coals on the lid as well as under it. Two things travel badly here, so handle them at home: cook the wild rice before you leave and freeze it, and keep the sausage cold until it hits the pot. Everything else in the list is dry, shelf-stable, or a root vegetable that does not care.
