This is a car-camping breakfast, not a backpacking one. Mascarpone, butter, and eggs all need a cooler, and there is no honest way around that. What did change is the milk: powdered whole milk reconstituted in the pot does everything fresh milk does here and removes one more thing that can spoil in a warm cooler.

The strawberries are dried and simmered with water rather than fresh and quartered. Dried fruit already has the sugar concentrated, so the syrup thickens faster over an uneven campfire. Two things matter at the pan: cut the pocket without cutting through, and dip fast. Thick brioche soaked long turns to custard in the middle and will not set before the outside burns.
