The original is a Romanian winter preserve: fresh hot peppers and roasted sweet peppers cooked down with sugar and vinegar, then sealed in jars. None of that travels. Jars are heavy, fresh peppers rot, and canning is not a camp activity. So the peppers come dried, the tomato and vinegar come as powder, and the whole thing rehydrates in one pot in under ten minutes.

Sugar and acid are doing the structural work, not just the flavoring. The sugar pulls the heat back from a raw burn into something you can eat a spoonful of, and the vinegar keeps it from going flat and sweet. The short simmer matters too: dried pepper needs a few minutes of real heat to give up its color and stop tasting like dust.
