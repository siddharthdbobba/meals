This is a cooler recipe, not a stove recipe, so the whole thing lives or dies on cold management. Smoked salmon and cream cheese both sit in the danger zone fast on a warm morning. Keep them sealed and buried in ice, put them out for one round, then put them back. That single habit is most of the food safety here.

The board format exists to solve a real problem: assembled bagels go soft. Cucumber, capers, and onion all carry water, and bread absorbs it within minutes. Keeping every component in its own pile means the bagel meets the wet stuff about ten seconds before someone eats it. It also means four people feed themselves without four pans.
