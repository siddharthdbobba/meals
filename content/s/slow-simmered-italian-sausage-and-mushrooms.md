Sausage links held below a boil for an hour stop being sausages and start being pot roast. The casing keeps them whole while the fat renders into the tomato, which is why loose ground sausage does not work here — it just falls apart into meat sauce. Browning first is not optional either. That crust is most of the flavor in the finished sauce.

Fresh mushrooms are heavy and go slimy in a cooler, so this uses dried ones and their soaking water in place of both the mushrooms and the stock. Pour the soaking liquid off carefully and leave the last spoonful behind; grit settles to the bottom. Parmesan from the shelf-stable can holds up in a hot car better than a wedge does, and it dissolves into the sauce anyway.
