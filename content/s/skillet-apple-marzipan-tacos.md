This is apple pie with the pastry problem solved. A flour tortilla folded in hot butter stiffens into a shell in about two minutes, which is the only structural work a camp dessert should ask of you. Roll it in cinnamon sugar while it is still greasy and the coating sticks on its own.

The marzipan is the part worth carrying. Grated into the pan at the end, it melts into the apples and turns a thin fruit filling into something with body and fat behind it, so four people get a real dessert out of two apples. It is almond paste, so leave it out if anyone at the table is allergic. Butter, apples, and marzipan all need a cooler, which makes this a car-camping or basecamp dish rather than a backpacking one.
