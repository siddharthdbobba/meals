This started as a home cookie recipe, which means an oven, a stick of butter, and a fresh egg. None of that travels. Powdered butter and powdered egg rehydrate into something close enough, and the whole batch bakes as one flat cookie in a covered pan instead of twelve on a sheet.

The cornstarch is worth carrying. It cuts the flour's gluten, so the middle stays soft even though a camp pan cooks unevenly and you will almost certainly overshoot. Low heat and a lid are the other half: the lid traps enough steam to cook the top before the bottom scorches. Pull it early. It keeps setting off the heat.
