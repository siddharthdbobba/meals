The original is an air fryer dish, which is not a thing you carry. Pouched salmon is already cooked and already salted, so the fryer step disappears and the recipe becomes an assembly job: hot rice, cold protein, a sauce built in the bag.

The mayo-sriracha-honey mix does most of the work. Fat is what makes plain rice read as dinner, and single-serve mayo packets are the only shelf-stable way to carry it without a bottle that leaks. Dried carrot rehydrates in the same water as the rice, so nothing needs its own pot. Check your soy sauce if the gluten-free tag matters to you; most brands are brewed with wheat, which is why tamari is listed first.
