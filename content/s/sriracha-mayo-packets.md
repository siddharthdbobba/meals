Mayonnaise is the one condiment that carries real calories, and single-serve packets solve the refrigeration problem the jar creates. The original calls for a bowl and 3 dl of homemade mayo. On trail you want two tablespoons, mixed in the bag it will live in, with no dish left over.

Chili pastes vary a lot in heat and salt, so add yours in small increments and taste as you go. Sugar and garlic powder are there to fix a paste that comes in too sharp. This is a sauce for other food: tuna packets, hard eggs on day one, a tortilla with anything in it, or the potatoes at the bottom of the pot.
