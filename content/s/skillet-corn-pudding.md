This starts as a slow cooker casserole, which is three and a half hours of electricity you do not have. On a stove the whole thing collapses into one pot: rehydrate the corn, build the batter in its own soaking water, then cook it low and covered until it sets.

Everything wet in the original is a powder here. Canned creamed corn becomes freeze-dried kernels plus the starch from the muffin mix. Sour cream, egg, and a stick of butter become sour cream powder, egg powder, and butter powder, which together account for most of the fat and all of the tang. Low heat is not optional. Cornbread batter on a thin pot bottom will burn before the middle firms up, so keep the flame small and keep the pot moving.
