The original is a microwave recipe, which is a problem three miles from an outlet. Steaming the mug in a covered pot gets to the same place: gentle, even heat that sets the batter without scorching the bottom. It takes ten minutes instead of one, and it costs a little fuel, so it is a camp dessert rather than a hurry.

The fresh egg becomes powdered egg, which rehydrates fine in a batter this wet. Coconut oil is the other trail-friendly swap already built into the recipe: it is solid at most camp temperatures and needs no cooler. Do not cut the fat or the syrup. Without them this is a dry protein puck, and the whole point is that it stays tender.
