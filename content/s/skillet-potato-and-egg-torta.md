This is a one-pan Spanish tortilla done the lazy way: no flipping, no plate trick, just a lid and low heat until the eggs set. The potatoes do most of the work and they need the full browning time before anything else joins them, because nothing later in the pan will cook them through.

The spices go in dry, straight into the hot oil, before the tomato. That minute matters. Cumin and paprika are fat-soluble, and dumping them into a wet pan gives you dusty potatoes instead of a coating. It needs a cooler for the eggs and potato, so it belongs to car camping and basecamp rather than a pack.
