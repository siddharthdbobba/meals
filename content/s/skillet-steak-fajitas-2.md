This is a car-camping dinner, not a pack meal. It needs a cooler and a heavy skillet, and both of those are fine when you are cooking twenty feet from the car.

The work that matters happens at home: slicing the steak thin against the grain and cutting the peppers to a uniform quarter inch. In camp you are managing one variable, which is heat. Cook the vegetables and the meat separately over real coals and let each one sit still long enough to char. Crowd them together in a lukewarm pan and everything releases water and turns grey. The lime and chipotle sit on the steak the whole time the fire is burning down, which is the marinade doing its work for free.
