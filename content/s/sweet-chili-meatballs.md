The original is a crockpot dish: frozen meatballs, a jar of chili sauce, a jar of grape jelly, four hours on high. None of that travels. Frozen meat is out, and two glass jars of mostly water is a pound of carried liquid for a dish that already has a stove under it.

So the sauce gets built from parts. Bottled chili sauce is tomato, vinegar, sugar, and spice, and all four of those exist in dry or tube form. The jelly stays because it is doing structural work — the pectin and sugar are what make the sauce cling to the meatballs instead of pooling at the bottom of the pot. Add it at the end and over low heat; boiled hard, it thins out and scorches on the pot floor.
