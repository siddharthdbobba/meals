Croutons on the trail are less about salad and more about turning bread that has gone hard into something that stores well. Drying the bread all the way through is what buys the shelf life. Anything left chewy in the middle holds moisture and will mold in a few days.

The oven version does this with residual heat after the bake. A stove has no residual heat, so the substitute is low flame and patience, plus a cooling spread before the bag is sealed. Oil is not optional. It carries the salt onto the surface and it is where most of the calories live.
