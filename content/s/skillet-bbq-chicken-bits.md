Frozen chicken is the one raw protein that makes sense in a cooler. It goes in solid, keeps everything around it cold for a day, and thaws right about when you want dinner. That is the whole logic of this recipe in winter camp, where the cooler is mostly a box that keeps food from freezing solid.

Cutting the tenders into bits is what makes the timing work. Small pieces cook through in five to seven minutes over uneven fire heat, so you are not guessing at the middle of a thick strip. Sauce goes on last, off the heat. Barbeque sauce is mostly sugar, and sugar on a hot pan turns black before the chicken is done.
