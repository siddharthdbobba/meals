The original is a fridge dessert: fresh ricotta, whipped cream, real strawberries. None of that survives a day in a pack. Whole milk powder and instant pudding mix do the same job — the pudding's starch and the milk fat set a cold slurry into something spoonable without a whisk, a bowl, or a chill. Ghee replaces butter because clarified fat does not go rancid or weep at 80 degrees.

Freeze-dried strawberries are the part worth paying for. Regular dried fruit stays leathery and bleeds sugar into the filling. Freeze-dried berries are dry sponges that rehydrate off the pudding itself, so they end up soft and tart while everything around them stays thick.
