This one stays close to a home kitchen recipe because it has to. Butter and fresh fish do not survive a long trip, so this is a lake-day dinner or a night-one meal out of the cooler, not something you carry for a week. Everything else is dry and premeasured at home.

The flour is not decoration. It dries the surface so the fish releases from the pan instead of shredding, and it thickens the butter sauce slightly when the two meet. Take the pan off the heat before the lemon juice goes in. Acid in a screaming hot pan splits the butter and you get grease with lemon in it.
