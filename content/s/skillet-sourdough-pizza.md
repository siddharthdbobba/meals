Sourdough is one of the few doughs that suits camp, because it does not need a packet of yeast or a warm kitchen. It needs time, and a day of hiking or paddling is exactly that. Mix it before you leave camp in the morning and it is ready when you get back.

The original bakes on a stone at oven temperature, which nothing in camp can do. Flipping a thin crust in a hot skillet gets you to the same place: the pan browns the base, and the lid traps enough heat to melt the cheese. Tomato paste replaces a jar of sauce because it is concentrated, resealable, and does not slosh. Keep the crust thin. A thick one chars underneath before the center cooks.
