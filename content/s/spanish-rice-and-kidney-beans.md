The original is a stovetop pilaf built on fresh onion, canned beans, canned tomatoes, and chicken broth. None of that travels. Dried onion and garlic powder replace the aromatics, sun-dried tomatoes replace the can and rehydrate in the cooking liquid anyway, and bouillon replaces the broth. Home-dehydrated cooked kidney beans weigh a fifth of what the cans do and come back in the same twenty minutes the rice needs.

The lemon and olives are what keep this from being another beige rice dinner, so add them off the heat. Cooked in, the acid goes flat and the olives turn to mush. The rice needs a real simmer with a lid, not a rolling boil, so this wants a stove you can throttle or a bed of coals rather than open flame.
