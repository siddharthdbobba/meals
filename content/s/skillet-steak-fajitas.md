This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Fresh steak and three bell peppers are heavy and perishable, so it belongs at a site you drove to. What makes it work in camp is that all the knife work happens on a kitchen counter days earlier. At the fire you are moving a spatula and nothing else.

The order matters more than the heat. Peppers and onion go in first because they need real time to soften and char; the steak needs about four minutes total and gets tough past that. Giving the meat a bare, undisturbed patch of hot pan is the difference between browned strips and grey ones swimming in vegetable liquid.
