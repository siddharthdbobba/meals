This is a cast iron biscuit adapted for a fire pit. The two changes that matter: powdered buttermilk instead of the carton, rehydrated with the same 3/4 cup of water, and a dutch oven standing in for the oven. Everything else about the original method survives the move.

The grated cold butter is the whole technique. Solid flecks of fat suspended in the flour turn to steam in the heat and push the layers apart, so the dough has to stay cold and barely handled. Heat the pan before the dough touches it — biscuits set their crust in the first minute, and a cold pan gives you a dense bottom instead.
