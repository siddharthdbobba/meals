This is a fridge recipe adapted to a cooler, and two swaps do the work. Whole milk powder plus cold water replaces the jug of milk, so nothing spoils if the ice runs low. Dry whipped topping mix replaces Cool Whip, which is a frozen product and a bad bet three days into a trip.

The alcohol is why chilling matters. Vodka interferes with the starch that sets instant pudding, so the mix stays looser than it would at home. Keep the ratio at roughly four parts liquid to one part vodka and give it real cooler time. Push the booze higher and you get chocolate soup in a cup.

Marshmallows are usually made with gelatin and graham crackers are wheat, so this is not vegetarian or gluten-free. Read your own boxes if that matters to anyone in camp.
