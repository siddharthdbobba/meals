Ready-to-eat rice packets were invented for kitchen convenience, but they work just as well at camp—lighter than most freeze-dried meals and actually tasting like rice instead of seasoned air. Dried steak matters more than it seems. Cold jerky is rubbery. Hot jerky crisping in a pot of oil is something else. That sizzle in the pan is your actual dinner.

At 600 calories, this will not carry you through a long day of walking. But for a high camp with tomorrow's mileage already behind you, or a weekend car-camping trip where weight is not the constraint, it is the kind of meal that reminds you why you bothered to cook at all.
