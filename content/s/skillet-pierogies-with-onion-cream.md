Caramelized onions are a thirty-minute job at home and nobody is doing that on a camp stove. Dried minced onion gets most of the way there in eight minutes, because the drying already did the work of driving off water. The sugar and the low flame do the rest. Rehydrated sour cream powder carries it, so nothing needs a fridge.

The pierogies are the reason this stays a car-camping and basecamp meal. Vacuum-packed shelf-stable ones exist and are worth hunting down; frozen ones out of a cooler work the first night. Low heat matters when browning them, since the dough scorches well before the filling warms up.
