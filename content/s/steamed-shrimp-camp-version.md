The original is an Instant Pot recipe, which is a way of saying it is steam and a timer. A camp pot with a lid does the same job on a stove. The only real variable is water volume: an inch is plenty, and more than that turns steaming into boiling, which leaches flavor out of the shrimp and into water you are about to pour on the ground.

Shrimp is the rare protein that punishes patience. The window between done and rubber is roughly ninety seconds, so check early and pull early. Carryover heat in a hot aluminum pot will finish them while you are getting the seasoning open. This one stays in cooler territory, not shelf-stable, so it is a day-one or day-two meal off a car or a boat.
