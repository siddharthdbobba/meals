This one is not a trail meal and there is no honest way to make it one. Fresh pasta and a cooler are the whole premise, so the work moves home: slow-cook the squirrel, mix the filling, roll and cut the ravioli, and freeze them flat before you leave. Camp gets one boil and one pan of sauce.

Freezing does double duty. It keeps the meat and cheese safe in the cooler for a couple of days, and frozen ravioli hold their shape in a rolling boil far better than fresh ones do. The smoked mozzarella cube in the center is the reason to bother — it melts into the squirrel and mushrooms and gives the filling the fat that lean wild meat does not have.
