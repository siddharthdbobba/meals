Jerky is the rare trail food you cannot make in camp, so all the work happens weeks ahead. The payoff is protein density: about 26 grams per ounce and a half, with no water weight and nothing that needs cooling. The marinade is doing two jobs. The salt and sugar pull moisture out of the meat, and the vinegar and Worcestershire soften the muscle fibers on a cut that is otherwise tough.

Fat is the reason batches fail. It does not dry, it goes rancid, and it makes the strips chewy in the wrong way, so trim harder than feels necessary and stay with eye of round or rump. Slicing thin and evenly matters as much as the smoker temperature does; a thick strip in the middle of the rack is the piece that spoils the bag. Dry to the bend test, cool it completely, and it travels.
