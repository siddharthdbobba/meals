This is a fat-and-salt delivery system dressed up as a sandwich filling. Everything in it is shelf-stable in the sealed package, which is the whole point: the canned meat, the jarred cheese, and the mayo packets all sit in a pack for days before you open them. Mashing them together at home means camp lunch is a jar and a spoon.

The pimentos matter more than they look. They are the only acid and moisture in a mix that is otherwise pure fat, and draining them is not optional. Wet pimentos turn the paste into something that runs off a cracker. The original called for bread and a cooler for leftovers; tortillas and a same-trip finish work better away from the car.
