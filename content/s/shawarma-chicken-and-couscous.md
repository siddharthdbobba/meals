The original is a slow cooker recipe, which is not a trail appliance. The spices are the part worth keeping: cumin, paprika, turmeric, garlic, cinnamon, clove, cayenne. They survive a spice bag indefinitely and taste like something other than salt, which is most of what a dinner bag needs.

Two swaps make it work in one pot. Freeze-dried chicken replaces raw breast, so the boil is about rehydrating rather than cooking meat safely. Citric acid replaces lemon juice, which is water and glass you would otherwise carry. Couscous goes in off the heat because it only needs to sit in hot water, and that saves fuel at the end of a long day.
