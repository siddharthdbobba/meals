This is a home recipe that survives the trip mostly intact, because the oven was never doing anything a lid cannot do. The original bakes the chops at 375F after a sear; over a fire you cover the pan and drop the heat instead. The trapped steam brings the center up gently, which is the whole point of the oven step. Fresh herbs became dried, which is the only real loss.

The sear is not optional. Everything the sauce tastes like is stuck to the pan after those five minutes, and the wine is there to lift it back off. Butter goes in off the heat so it thickens the sauce instead of splitting it. Thick chops matter too: thin ones go from raw to dry in the time it takes to find your fork.
