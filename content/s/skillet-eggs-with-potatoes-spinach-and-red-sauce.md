The original is an oven dish — potatoes roasted, then everything layered in a skillet and baked until the eggs set. A stove can do the same thing in one pot if you build it in the right order: hydrate and brown the potatoes first, sauce them second, and put the egg on top last where the lid does the work the oven used to.

Everything perishable got swapped. Frozen spinach becomes dried, a jar of marinara becomes tomato powder and water, and the eggs are powdered. Keep the sauce thick. Too much water and the egg never sets, it just floats.
