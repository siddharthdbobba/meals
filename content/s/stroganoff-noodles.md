Sour cream powder tastes flat by itself. Butter powder adds the fat stroganoff needs, beef bouillon adds salt and umami that carry the whole thing, and cornstarch stops the sauce from being watery. Without them you have rehydrated noodles and powder. With them you have dinner.

Freeze-dried beef and mushrooms both rehydrate in the ten minutes the noodles need to soften. Dicing the beef small matters: large chunks stay crunchy in the middle. Three ounces packs easily and satisfies.
