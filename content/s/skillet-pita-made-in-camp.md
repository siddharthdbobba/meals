Bread is heavy to carry and it arrives at camp destroyed. Making it there costs a rise you spend doing something else and about three minutes of cooking per round. The dry mix weighs less than the loaves it becomes, and nothing in it can spoil.

Two changes from a home version. The oven is gone, so a dry hot pan does the work; the puff comes from water flashing to steam inside a thin round, and a skillet delivers that fine as long as it is genuinely hot before the dough lands. Butter and honey are swapped for ghee and sugar, which sit in a pack at any temperature. Instant yeast means no proofing step and no separate cup of warm water to fuss with.
