This is a stroganoff that skips the browning and the roux. Canned soup already contains the thickener and the mushroom flavor, so the only real work is heating precooked meatballs through without burning the bottom of the pot. Stirring is the whole technique.

The sour cream is the one thing tying it to a cooler. Sour cream powder rehydrated with water behaves close enough and turns this into a pantry meal, though it wants a little extra salt. Cook it over coals rather than open flame; a hot spot under a Dutch oven will scorch a dairy gravy fast.
