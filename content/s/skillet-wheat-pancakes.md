Whole wheat behaves differently than white flour. It drinks more water and it browns faster, which is why this batter rests before it hits the pan and why the fire stays low. Rush either one and you get scorched outsides over raw middle.

The original called for fresh milk or buttermilk and whole eggs. Neither travels, so both come as powder and go into the dry mix at home. Powdered egg is not required, but without it the pancakes are fragile and want to fall apart on the flip.
