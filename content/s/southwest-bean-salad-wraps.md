This is a cooler lunch, not a pack lunch. Beans, tomato, and avocado are wet and perishable, so it works for car camping, a paddle day, or a short hike out from the trailhead — anywhere the container can stay cold until noon. Past that, the math stops working and you want something dried.

The two rules are draining and timing. Beans and corn straight from the can carry enough liquid to turn a tortilla to paste in twenty minutes, so drain them until nothing pools in the container. The avocado goes in at camp for the same reason: mashed early it releases water and browns, folded in late it does the job of a sauce without one.
