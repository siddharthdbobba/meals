This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Cream cheese and crab both want ice, so it lives at the truck or in a raft cooler and nowhere else. What makes it worth the space is that once the pan is on the grate it needs nothing: no stirring, no watching, no second burner. It cooks while you set up tents.

Indirect heat is the whole trick. Straight over flame the dairy breaks and the bottom scorches long before the middle warms through. Off to the side with the lid down, it heats evenly and picks up smoke from the coals, which is the one thing an oven version cannot do. The lemon and hot sauce are there to cut the fat; leave them out and it reads as a bowl of warm cheese.
