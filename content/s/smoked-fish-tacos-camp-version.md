This is a car-camp or basecamp dinner, not a pack-in one. Three pounds of fish and a cooler full of crema puts it firmly in the day-you-drove-in category, which is also the day you are most likely to have caught something.

The marinade does two jobs. The citrus and tequila loosen the flesh of a lean panfish so it does not dry out over slow heat, and the salt starts pulling moisture back in as brine. An hour is the ceiling — acid keeps working, and past that the fillets go opaque and chalky before they ever see the fire. The cabbage soaking in vinegar for the same hour is the other half: it cuts the crema and the barbecue sauce, both of which are sweet, and it stays crisp in a way lettuce will not after a day in a cooler.
