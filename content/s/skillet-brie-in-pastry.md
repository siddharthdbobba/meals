This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Brie and raw puff pastry both need a cold cooler, so there is no backpacking version worth writing. What the camp version does change is the egg wash: one egg for two tablespoons of wash is a bad trade in a cooler, so milk powder stirred into water goes on instead. It browns nearly as well and nothing gets left over.

The coals are the part people get wrong. A skillet directly over flame burns the pastry bottom long before the cheese inside melts. Low, even coals and a quarter turn every ten minutes get the whole wheel soft while the crust sets. The ten minute rest is not optional either. Cut it hot and the brie runs out of the pastry and off the board.
