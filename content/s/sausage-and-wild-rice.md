The original was a home-kitchen dish with fresh sausage from a fridge. Dry-cured summer sausage does the same job without a cooler, and it renders enough fat to season the whole pot. Brown it first, in the pot you will cook the rice in, so the fond dissolves into the water.

Instant wild rice matters. Real wild rice takes forty-five minutes and a lot of fuel. The quick blends are parcooked and finish in ten, which is what makes this a one-pot dinner instead of a fuel problem. Dried onion and pepper flakes rehydrate in the same water and cover for the fresh vegetables you are not carrying.
