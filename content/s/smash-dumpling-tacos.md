Wild hog grind is lean and coarse, which makes it bad at holding a meatball shape and good at this. Pressed thin against the tortilla, the meat gets full contact with the pan and cooks in three or four minutes with a real crust. The tortilla steams from the fat underneath, so it never dries out on a hot skillet.

This is cooler food, not pack food. The meat has to stay cold, so it is a car camp or basecamp dinner. Mix the seasoning into the pork at home and freeze it flat: it chills the cooler on day one and is ready to press on day two. Everything else on the list is shelf-stable except the slaw.
