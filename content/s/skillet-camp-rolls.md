This is home bread dragged outdoors. The original relies on fresh milk, a whole egg, and salted butter, none of which travel. Milk powder, egg powder, and ghee do the same jobs and sit in a pack for weeks. Ghee is the important swap: butter turns rancid in heat, clarified butter does not.

Instant yeast is what makes this a camp recipe instead of an afternoon. It rises once, not twice, so the whole thing fits in the gap between setting up and eating. The dough is deliberately too wet to knead. Wet dough handles the uneven heat of coals better than a stiff one, since the extra water buys time before the crust sets.
