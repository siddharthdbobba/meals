This is a car-camping braise adapted from a Pennsylvania hunting-lodge recipe. One pot, 12 servings, 50 minutes from cooler to plate. Brown the meat to develop flavor, add sauce, simmer covered.

The brown sugar and cranberry sauce balance the sauerkraut's vinegar and salt, while the chili sauce adds umami and body. Each serving is a reasonable bowl of meatballs and sauce. The canned goods make it heavy for backpacking, but ideal for car-camping where weight is less critical. Fresh ground beef works best if you keep it cold; half venison is excellent if you have game.
