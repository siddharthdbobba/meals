Two things decide whether these come out crisp or limp, and neither is heat. The first is moisture: bacon that has not been drained and eggs that go in still steaming will soften the tortilla from the inside before the outside ever browns. Let the scramble sit on a plate for a minute. The second is fire management. Coals that have burned down give steady radiant heat; flames give you a black tortilla and cold cheese.

Cheese on both sides of the filling is not excess. It melts into the tortilla and glues the wedge together, which is the whole point of eating breakfast standing up next to a packed car. Salsa goes on the plate, never inside the fold, for the same moisture reason.
