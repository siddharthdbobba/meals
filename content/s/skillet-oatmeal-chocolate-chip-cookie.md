This started as a butter-and-egg cookie dough for six dozen cookies, which is not something you do at a campsite. The batch is cut to one skillet, the egg becomes powder, the butter becomes ghee, and the molasses and white sugar collapse into brown sugar, which is the same thing in one bag. Everything but the fat rides as a single dry mix.

A campfire has no thermostat, so this is baked as one thick round rather than individual cookies. A slab is forgiving. The lid traps steam and cooks the top while the coals work the bottom, and rotating the pan is what stands in for even oven heat. Coals, not flames, or the bottom is black before the middle sets.
