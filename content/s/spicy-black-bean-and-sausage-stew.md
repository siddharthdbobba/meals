This is a car-camping pot, not a trail meal. Everything in it is canned, which is heavy but means nothing spoils and nothing needs a cooler. The whole thing works because you are not really cooking so much as holding it hot until the sausage fat, the bean starch, and the chile liquid stop being three separate things.

The long simmer is doing the work. Twenty minutes gets you warm canned beans. Forty-five gets a stew with body. The heat level comes entirely from which can of tomatoes and chiles you grabbed, so decide that in the grocery store, not in camp. Browning the sausage first is the one step worth not skipping: it renders fat into the pot that carries the smoke flavor everywhere else.
