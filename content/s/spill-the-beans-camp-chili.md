This is a long simmer over coals, which is why it belongs at a basecamp and not on a summit. Time is doing the work: an hour breaks the coarse grind down and lets the chili powder stop tasting raw. Chili powder in this volume is the backbone, not a garnish, so measure it by the quarter cup and do not flinch.

Two adaptations for camp. Fresh garlic became garlic powder, since a clove is one more thing to mince by headlamp and the powder disappears into a long simmer anyway. The beans go in at the end because canned beans held at a simmer for an hour turn to paste. Salt last, after the tomato sauce has reduced and told you how salty it already is.
