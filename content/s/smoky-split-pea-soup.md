Split peas are one of the few dried legumes that cook from hard to soup without soaking, which is why this works in camp at all. The cost is fuel. Budget close to an hour of low flame, and run it on liquid fuel or a big canister rather than a small one, or plan on eating late.

The smoked paprika is doing the job the ham hock used to. Without it this is just green and starchy. Fresh leeks and celery are not carryable, so both go in as flakes and get bloomed in oil first, which gets most of the way there. Salt at the end because bouillon is already salty, and the acid at the very end because a pot of peas with nothing sharp in it tastes flat no matter how long it simmered.
