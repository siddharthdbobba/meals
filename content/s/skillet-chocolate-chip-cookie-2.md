A skillet cookie is easier in camp than drop cookies for the obvious reason: you press it once and bake it once. No sheet pans, no batches, no scooping in the dark. It also survives uneven camp heat better, because a thick slab has enough mass to forgive a hot spot on one side of the pot.

The home-kitchen version leans on cold butter and fresh eggs. Neither travels, so this uses ghee or canned butter and powdered whole egg rehydrated in a little water. Melted fat makes a chewier, denser cookie than creamed butter does, which is fine here — that is the texture you want out of a dutch oven anyway. Coal count matters more than time: most of the heat goes on the lid so the top browns and the bottom does not burn.
