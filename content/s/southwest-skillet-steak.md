This is a fire-cooked steak dinner that survives contact with a campsite because the hard parts happen at home. Trimming, portioning, and dicing in your kitchen means the skillet work is two moves: sear the meat, char the vegetables. Everything lands in one pan and the pan gets wiped, not washed.

The order matters more than the seasoning. The steak goes in first so it sears in a clean, dry, very hot pan, and it rests while the vegetables cook in the fond it left behind. Lime goes on last and off the heat, because acid on a hot pan cooks off in seconds. Cast iron over coals runs hotter and less evenly than a range, so judge doneness by feel rather than clock.
