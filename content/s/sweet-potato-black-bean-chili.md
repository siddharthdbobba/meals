The original is a stovetop chili built on cans, which is the wrong shape for a pack. Cans are mostly water and the sweet potatoes take forty minutes of simmering. Dehydrating the potatoes at home fixes both problems at once: diced small and dried hard, they come back in eight minutes of boiling instead of a long simmer, and they weigh almost nothing.

Bean flakes and tomato powder go in off the heat because they thicken instantly and will scorch on a canister stove if they sit over flame. The oil is added last for the same reason and because it is where a third of the calories come from. Two tablespoons of chili powder per serving would be closer to the source ratio; one is enough unless you like the burn.
