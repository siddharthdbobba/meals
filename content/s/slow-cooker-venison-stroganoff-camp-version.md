  - "Get a bed of coals going. You want steady low heat for a couple of hours, not flames."
  - "Set the dutch oven over the coals with the oil. Salt and pepper the venison and brown it in two batches so it sears instead of steaming. Pull each batch to a plate."
  - "Add the mushrooms and onion to the same pot. Cook 5 minutes, until the mushrooms give up their water and it cooks off. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the garlic."

Venison stew meat is lean and full of connective tissue, which means it is tough until it isn't. There is no shortcut past the long low simmer. A dutch oven buried in coals does the same job a slow cooker does at home: steady heat, tight lid, no attention needed for two hours while you do something else.

The order at the end is the part that matters. Cornstarch needs a real simmer to thicken, dairy needs the pot off the heat. Do them in the wrong order and you get a grainy, broken sauce that no amount of stirring fixes. Cream cheese first because it melts slowly, sour cream last because it is the most fragile. This is a car-camping recipe by design, but the variations list makes it cooler-free if you want it.
