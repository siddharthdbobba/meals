Shrimp is a bad trail protein raw and a good one freeze-dried. It rehydrates fast, it stays firm instead of turning to mush, and it is nearly all protein by dry weight. The taco seasoning goes in the water with it rather than on it afterward, so the spice ends up inside the shrimp instead of coating the pot.

The slaw is where the home version had to change. Fresh cabbage, sour cream, and mayonnaise are all fridge food. Dried cabbage and carrot soaked in cold water get most of the crunch back, and yogurt powder cut with a small amount of water makes a dressing thick enough to cling. The ancho does the real work on heat — the seasoning blend alone is mild, and after a day of walking mild reads as nothing.
