This is a home bake carried into the field, not a camp recipe. It earns pack space because the shape is right: a flat, dense bar with no filling, no glaze, and nothing that separates when it gets warm or squashed. Cookies crumble and pastries go to grease. A slab of butter cake with apple in it does neither.

The apples are structural, not decoration. They cook down into the sponge and hold moisture there, so the bars stay chewable on day three instead of going dry and chalky. Cut them cold and wrap them individually, or they fuse into a block and you end up eating handfuls. Butter and eggs mean this is a shoulder-season snack, not a July one.
