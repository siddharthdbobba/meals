The original was fresh mackerel on toast, which does not travel. Tinned smoked mackerel does, and it is one of the few shelf-stable proteins that carries real fat with it. Sun-dried tomato stands in for fresh tomato and adds acid without the water weight or the mess.

Horseradish is doing more than heat here. Oily smoked fish gets heavy fast, and the sharp mayo cuts through it enough that you finish the tin. Mix the mayo first and taste it. Horseradish strength varies wildly by jar, and there is no fixing it once it is on the crackers.
