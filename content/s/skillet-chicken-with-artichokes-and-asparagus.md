The original is a roast chicken in a hot oven, which is not a thing you have. What survives the translation is the pan sauce: artichoke brine, oil, and mushroom liquid reduced down until it coats everything. That sauce is the whole point, so the pickling liquid is not optional.

Dried mushrooms and freeze-dried asparagus rehydrate in the same pan and give back a lot of the water they took, so the added water stays low. Foil-packet chicken goes in last and only needs warming; cook it longer and it turns to string. Bread is a day-one item, and it is also your dish sponge.
