Nuts are the densest calories you can carry, and this is a way to make a cup of them interesting enough to actually eat. The sugar in the sweet chili sauce is what does the work: heated in the pan it reduces onto the cashews and sets as a thin shell when they cool. That shell is also why the timing matters. Pull them a minute late and the sugar goes bitter.

The original calls for fresh lime zest. Zest dried at home is the trail version and keeps the citrus without carrying a lime. Zest goes in off the heat either way, since direct heat kills the bright part. Moisture is the enemy after that: cool the batch completely before it goes in a container, keep it out of the cooler, and it stays crisp for the trip.
