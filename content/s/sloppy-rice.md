Instant rice does the heavy lifting—it rehydrates in fifteen minutes and soaks up the seasoned beef without fussing. The sloppy joe packet provides all the seasoning and the fat that makes rice taste like food. Cheese adds calories without adding weight.

Regular shredded cheese works fine on cool trips as long as you never touch it with bare hands. On warm-weather trips, use freeze-dried cheese instead. There's also a freezer-bag cooking method if you want to skip carrying a pot.
