This is an appetizer, not backcountry food, and it makes no attempt to be. Both the dough and the dip are refrigerated, so it belongs at a car camp on a night when the cooler is still cold. Nothing here can be made lighter without becoming a different recipe.

The layout does the work. Dough on the outside ring gets direct contact with hot iron and browns; dip in the center stays off the metal and heats gently instead of splitting. Foil or a lid traps enough heat to cook the tops of the dough balls, which is the part a bare skillet over a fire never manages. Watch the bottom more than the clock.
