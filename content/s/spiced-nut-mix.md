The butter carries the spices evenly through the nuts and adds the calories that make this a meal snack. Smoked almonds are already cooked, so you are just warming everything to help the seasonings stick. Skip the butter and hot sauce if you want to cut weight, and toast everything briefly in a dry pot instead. The cumin and seeds carry enough flavor.

Buy pre-roasted blanched almonds and hazelnuts to avoid the roasting step entirely. Toss cold with the spices for a no-cook version that still tastes intentional.
