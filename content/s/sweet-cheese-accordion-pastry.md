This is car-camping food, not pack food. Phyllo, fresh cheese, and three eggs need a cooler and a dutch oven, and there is no honest way to shrink that down to a pot on a canister stove.

The accordion fold is the whole point. Folding each sheet back on itself instead of layering flat leaves gaps everywhere, and those gaps are what the egg and dairy mixture soaks into. Layered flat, the custard sits on top and the pastry underneath stays dry. The mineral water thins the mixture so it runs into the folds instead of pooling, and the pressing step after pouring is not optional. Bottom heat browns the base too fast in a dutch oven, so keep most of the coals on the lid.
