At 40 calories an ounce this is the least efficient thing in the library, and it
does not care, because the water it is boiled in is the only thing that gets
dirty. Nothing touches a pot.

Worth carrying on the day you know you will arrive late and cold, when the real
constraint is willingness to cook rather than pack weight.
