This is an oven tray bake moved onto a fire. The foil packet does what the roasting pan did: it holds the oil and the moisture the vegetables give off, so the salmon steams and roasts at the same time instead of drying out over open heat. Coals are the right heat source here, not flame. Flame chars the foil before the broccoli stems soften.

The two things that decide whether this works are uniform cuts and a real seal. Broccoli stalk is dense, so peel it and slice it thin or it stays raw next to the zucchini. Lemon goes on at the end, off the heat, because acid cooked for twenty minutes tastes flat. This one needs a cooler, so it is a first-night meal, not a resupply meal.
