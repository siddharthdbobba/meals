This is what a freeze-dried pantry is actually for. Home-canned staples — pasta, sausage crumbles, dried peppers and onion, cheese powder — were bought to be assembled, not eaten as a mystery entree, and they happen to make a good one-pot trail dinner with no substitutions needed.

The timing is the whole trick. Freeze-dried meat and vegetables rehydrate in roughly the time small pasta takes to cook, so everything goes in the same pot at the same moment and you boil once. Hold back a little water at the end; the cheese powder needs something to dissolve into, and pasta keeps drinking as it sits.
