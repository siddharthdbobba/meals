The source built this on Greek yogurt out of a cooler. That works for one morning and fails for the rest of the trip, so this version uses whole milk powder mixed thick with a little cold water. It is tangier than yogurt is not, but it carries the same job: fat and body against a potato that is otherwise all starch and sugar.

Foil is doing two things. It keeps the skin off the coals so the outside does not carbonize before the middle gives, and it becomes the bowl, which means no pot to scrub. Toppings stay out of the packet until the potato is open. Steam softens pecans in about a minute, and a soft pecan is just a piece of fat with no crunch to it.
