This one comes off a product page for a battery stick frother, not a kitchen, so treat the drink as the point and the gadget as the reason it works. A spoon cannot get air into powdered milk. A frother can, and that is the whole difference between camp coffee with milk in it and something that looks like it came off a machine.

Use whole milk powder. Nonfat dissolves fine but the foam collapses almost immediately, since there is no fat holding the bubble walls together. Water off the boil rather than at it, or the milk proteins seize and you get grit at the bottom of the cup. The pinch of salt is doing what it always does with instant coffee: covering the flat, papery edge.
