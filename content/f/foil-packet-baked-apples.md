Cooking an apple in foil over fire is straightforward because the apple shell contains everything. The fruit softens as it heats, and the filling stays in place instead of spilling into the coals. Butter melts into the apple, carrying the brown sugar and spices with it.

This recipe needs cold storage, so it suits car-camping with coolers rather than backpacking. The filling is flexible: use whatever granola and dried fruit you have available. Cooking time depends on fire intensity, so check the apple after 15 minutes with a fork. The foil packet is your cleanup—toss it and you're done.
