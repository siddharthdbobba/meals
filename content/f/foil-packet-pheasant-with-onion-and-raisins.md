steps:

Pheasant is lean to the point of being a problem. There is almost no fat in the breast, so the whole method here is about adding some and then trapping it. The bacon renders down over the top, the oil pools in the foil, and the onion underneath steams instead of searing. Everything the bird loses, it sits in.

Raisins stand in for the wild grapes these birds eat on their own. They swell in the packet and turn sweet against the thyme and the salt. Foil is the whole cooking vessel, so there is nothing to scrub afterward, which matters when you are cleaning game and dishes on the same water.
