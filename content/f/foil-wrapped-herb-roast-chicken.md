A whole chicken is heavy and perishable, which makes it a bad trail food and a very good first-night one. Freeze it solid at home and it acts as its own ice, thawing slowly inside the pack while you walk. Salt it a day early so the seasoning has time to move past the skin; a bird salted at the trailhead is only seasoned on the outside.

The foil is the whole cooking vessel. Two or three layers keep the ashes out and trap steam, so the bird effectively roasts and braises at once instead of scorching against the coals. Cook it on a settled bed, not on flames, and turn it once so neither side sits in the hottest spot the entire time. Nothing to wash afterward but a spoon.
