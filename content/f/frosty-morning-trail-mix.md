This is a cold-weather mix, and the ratio reflects that. Walnuts are close to 70% fat, which is where nearly all the calories live. The cherries are there for sugar that hits fast when your hands are too cold to work a stove, and the acid keeps a fatty mix from going flat after the third handful.

The espresso beans are a dose, not a garnish. A quarter cup spread over two servings lands somewhere near a weak cup of coffee each, which is enough to get you moving on a frozen morning without brewing anything. Dark chocolate is the binder in cold weather and the problem in warm weather, so this one belongs in shoulder season and winter.
