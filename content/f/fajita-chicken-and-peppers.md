The original is a slow cooker recipe: raw chicken and fresh peppers layered up and left alone for four hours, then drained because the vegetables give up a lot of water. On trail you run that backwards. Everything comes dried, and you add back a measured amount of water rather than pouring off whatever the peppers released.

That is why the volume matters. 400 ml is enough to rehydrate the chicken and still leave the peppers with some structure. Go heavier and you get soup that soaks through a tortilla before you can fold it. The oil goes in off the heat because dried peppers absorb water first and fat second, and fat added early just coats everything and slows the whole thing down.
