This started as an oven recipe at 400F for half an hour. A coal bed does the same job with worse temperature control, which is why the potatoes get halved. Cut faces roast and steam at once, and half a potato cooks in roughly the time you can hold a fire steady. A whole one buried in ashes takes an hour and comes out raw in the middle as often as not.

Oil is doing two things: it keeps the cut side from drying into leather, and it adds the fat that a sweet potato has none of. Coconut oil earns its spot on a trip because it stays solid in a jar until it hits the heat. Salt on both faces before wrapping, since once the foil is sealed you are not seasoning anything again.
