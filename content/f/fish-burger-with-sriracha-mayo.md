This is a car-camp dinner, not a pack dinner. The fish is the reason: white fillets need real cold, so this only works with a cooler and gets cooked the first night out.

The flour dredge is what keeps the fish in one piece. Pollock is lean and falls apart on a hot pan, and the thin coat of seasoned flour gives it a crust to hold onto while it browns. The squash ribbons are a five-minute pickle doing the job lettuce and tomato usually do, and unlike either of them, vinegar and sugar mean they get better sitting in a cup while dinner cooks. Mayo packets are worth seeking out; a jar opened at camp with no fridge is a problem you carry for the rest of the trip.

Dietary tags are left empty on purpose: fish, wheat buns, and egg-based mayo rule out every one of them.
