Air fryer recipes assume dry circulating heat and a machine to make it. Coals do the same job if you keep the beans in one layer and off direct flame. The foil tray is the whole trick: it holds enough heat to crisp the crumbs but keeps them from dropping into the fire.

The original used fresh eggs, which is not a camp ingredient. Powdered whole egg rehydrated thick enough to cling does the same work and rides in a pack for months. Two kinds of breadcrumb are worth carrying: panko for texture, fine crumbs to fill the gaps so the coating actually sticks. Green beans are the one thing here with a clock on them, so cook this the first night out.
