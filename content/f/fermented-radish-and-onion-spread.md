This is a home-prep condiment, not a camp recipe. All the work happens days before you leave: grate, salt, wait, squeeze, mix. What you carry is two ounces of finished spread per person that adds fat and a hard flavor edge to crackers, hard salami, or a foil packet of fish.

Two things decide whether it works. Squeeze the fermented radish until it stops dripping, because trapped water separates out and turns the jar into a slick mess in a warm pack. And use poultry fat or sunflower oil, not beef or pork fat, which set up hard and stay that way until you warm the jar. Even so, let it come to air temperature before you open it.
