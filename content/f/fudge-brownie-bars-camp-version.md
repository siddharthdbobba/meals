The original is an oven recipe with a cooked cream frosting, which is a nonstarter in camp. The frosting is gone here. The brownie itself does not need it, and a pan of ganache in a cooler is a mess waiting to happen. Real eggs are swapped for powdered egg so nothing has to stay cold on the drive in.

The high sugar-to-flour ratio is what makes these fudgy rather than cakey, and it also makes them forgiving over uneven coal heat: there is not enough flour to go tough if you overbake by five minutes. Coals on the lid do most of the work. Coals underneath will scorch the bottom if you get greedy, so keep the ratio lopsided and rotate often.
