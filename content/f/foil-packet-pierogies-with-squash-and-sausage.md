This started as a sheet pan dinner, which is already most of the way to camp food. The oven becomes coals and the sheet pan becomes foil, and the method does not otherwise change: everything is cut to a size that finishes in the same twenty minutes.

The frozen pierogies are the reason this needs a cooler. They also do the work of a starch without any water, since they steam themselves inside the packet. Sausage that is already cooked means the only real doneness question is the squash, so cut it small and check it there.
