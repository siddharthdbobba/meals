This is a bulk snack, not a meal, and the ratio is what makes it work. Popcorn and cereal give volume with almost no weight, so the bag looks like more food than it weighs. The dried fruit, nuts, and candy carry the actual calories. Roughly half sweet, half salty, which is why you keep reaching into it after the third hour instead of getting sick of it.

It came off a holiday gift-tag recipe, and the only change worth mentioning is portioning. A pretty jar of this crushes into dust in a pack. Bagging it at home in single servings keeps the pieces intact and stops you from eating four days of snacks on day one. The chocolate is the weak point in summer heat; everything else is stable for months.
