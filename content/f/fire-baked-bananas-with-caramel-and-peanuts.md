The original is an oven tray bake finished with vanilla ice cream. Ice cream is the one thing a campsite cannot produce, so it is gone. What replaces it is coconut milk powder mixed thick with cold water, which is cold, white, and fatty enough to do the same job of cutting the sugar. The cocktail berries become dried tart cherries, which travel and hit the same sour note.

Heat is doing real work on the banana, not just warming it. Sugar in the fruit breaks down and the flesh goes soft and jammy, which is why overripe bananas are the right ones. The foil packet keeps the steam in so the banana cooks rather than dries, and it means nothing to wash afterward.
