Papanasi are normally built on fresh farmer's cheese and eggs, which is a nonstarter without a fridge. Powdered whole milk and powdered egg do most of the same work: protein and fat that bind the dough and let it puff. The semolina matters more than it looks like it should, because a camp measurement is never exact and it soaks up whatever extra water you added by accident.

The frying is the part that goes wrong. A hot flame under a thin pot browns the crust in ninety seconds and leaves raw dough behind the hole. Keep it low, accept six minutes per doughnut, and pack out the used oil in the bottle it came in.
