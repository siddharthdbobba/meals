This is a car-camping dinner, not a pack dinner. Fish sticks are frozen, which is the point: they act as cooler ice on day one and become dinner on day two, and their breading holds up on a grate far better than raw fish does. Cook them in a skillet or a foil pan rather than directly on the bars, or half of them fall into the coals.

The sauce is doing the work that makes it read like a taco instead of a school lunch. Lime, cumin, and garlic powder in sour cream take thirty seconds and cover the fact that the protein came out of a box. Everything else on the list is optional and mostly texture. If you have no ice at all, this recipe does not survive the trip, so use the tuna version in the variations.
