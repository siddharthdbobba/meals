Roulades look fussy for camp, but foil does the hard part. Twisting the ends tight turns each packet into a small pressure cooker: the thigh holds its shape, the pepper steams inside it, and none of the juice runs into the fire. Bone the thighs at home if you can, in the kitchen, with a cutting board and good light.

Heat control matters more than timing. Coals at the edge of the fire cook these through in about half an hour without scorching the foil; direct flame will char the outside while the center is still raw. The seed and herb coating goes on after cooking, not before, because sesame burns. The chili sauce goes on last for the same reason, and because sugar in a hot packet turns to tar.
