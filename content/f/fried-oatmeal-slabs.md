This is a two-night recipe pretending to be a one-morning one. Oatmeal that sits overnight sets up because the starch retrogrades — it goes from porridge to something you can pick up and put in a pan. That only works with rolled oats cooked thick. Instant packets stay slack and will smear instead of slabbing.

The trail changes are the dairy. Fresh butter and milk don't travel, so ghee does the frying and powdered milk goes into the oats at the cooking stage, where it also stiffens the set. Fry it hard enough to get a crust and the inside stays soft, which is the whole point of the technique. Make double at dinner and this costs you almost nothing in the morning.
