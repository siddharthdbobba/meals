Everything in the packet cooks in the steam the vegetables give up, which is why the 2 tablespoons of water matter more than they look like they should. Without a sealed edge the moisture leaves, the potato scorches on the foil side, and the patty dries out. Crimp hard.

This is a car camping meal and stays one. Fresh vegetables and a frozen patty need a cooler, and the patty should be cooked through the same day it thaws. Coals do the work here, not flame — a fire still throwing flame will burn the outside layer of onion before the potato is anywhere near done.
