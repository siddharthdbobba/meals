This one stays a cooler recipe on purpose. Yogurt is not shelf-stable and the whole point is the frozen texture, so it belongs on car trips, in a basecamp cooler, or in a day pack for a few hours. All the work happens at home, which is the right trade for a snack you want to hand out without setting anything up.

The milk matters more than it looks. Straight yogurt freezes into something you have to chew like a rock; thinning it lets the bites soften fast in the mouth. Maple syrup lowers the freezing point a little for the same reason, and the coconut on top keeps them from welding into one block in the bag.
