Spam earns its place by being fat and salt in a shape that survives a hot van and a wet week. It cures itself and needs no cooler, and the rendered fat is what fries the rice, so the whole dinner runs on one pan and no added oil. That fat matters on a cold day; rice alone runs through you fast.

The mayo goes in off the heat on purpose. In a hot pan it separates into oil and water and the rice turns greasy, but stirred into the residual warmth it coats every grain. Single-serve packets keep at ambient temperature, which is the only reason mayo belongs on this list at all. If you need the recipe gluten-free, swap the soy packets for tamari, since standard soy sauce is brewed with wheat.
