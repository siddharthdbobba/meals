The original is wings in a slow cooker for three hours, then a broiler pass. Neither exists at camp, so this keeps the parts that survive: cooked chicken and barbecue sauce, heated until the sauce tightens onto the meat. Pouched chicken is already cooked, which means you are only warming and glazing, not worrying about a bone-in cut over uneven coals.

Drain the pouches properly. The packing liquid dilutes the sauce and you end up with a soupy packet that never reduces. Foil keeps it a one-packet job with nothing to scrub. The ranch powder is optional weight, but a cold dip against hot sweet sauce is the reason the pairing existed in the first place.
