There is no flour in this. The thickening comes from two hours of collagen breaking down in the beef and from the mushrooms giving up their water and then taking sauce back on. That takes time, which is the whole point of cooking it at a basecamp rather than on a stove at a tent site with a fuel canister you are rationing.

Searing first is not optional. Brown crust is where most of the flavor in the finished gravy comes from, and you cannot add it back later. The cream cheese at the end is the one fridge item; if that is a problem, full-fat milk powder whisked into a ladle of hot stew does the same job and weighs nothing.
