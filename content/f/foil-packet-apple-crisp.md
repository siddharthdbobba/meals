Foil packets are the right vehicle for a crisp in camp because the pan is disposable and the portioning happens up front. Each person gets a sealed packet, the apples steam in their own juice, and nothing needs scrubbing afterward. The crimp on the seam matters more than the heat does. A loose fold leaks sugar into the coals and leaves you with dry fruit.

The flour and baking powder in the topping are what keep it from turning into wet oatmeal. Butter binds the crumble into clumps that brown against the foil instead of dissolving. Everything except the butter and apples travels dry, so the whole dessert is one freezer bag plus whatever is already in the cooler.
