steps:
  - "Burn the fire down to coals, or set a grill grate over it. Flames scorch foil packets and cook nothing through."

  - "Give the potatoes 15 minutes, turning once. They start cooking before the fish because they need the head start."
  - "Build the second packet: vegetables on the bottom, salmon on top, remaining oil, garlic, and rosemary, lemon slices over the fish. Seal it loosely so steam has somewhere to go."

Two packets, not one, because potatoes and fish do not cook on the same clock. Start the potatoes on the coals alone, add the salmon packet later, and both land at the same time. Trying to run them together gives you either raw potato or salmon with the texture of a shoe.

The oil matters more than it looks. Foil conducts heat hard and fast, and without fat between the food and the metal the bottom layer sticks and burns. Vegetables underneath the fish do the same job, holding it off the foil and steaming in what drips down. Cleanup is folding the foil closed and packing it out.
