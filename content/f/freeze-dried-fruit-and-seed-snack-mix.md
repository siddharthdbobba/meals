Freeze-dried fruit is the whole point here. It carries the flavor of fresh berries at roughly a tenth of the weight, and it does not go soft, sticky, or moldy in a hot pack the way most dried fruit does. The catch is that it is almost pure sugar and almost no fat, so on its own it burns off fast.

The seeds fix that. Pumpkin and sunflower bring the fat and most of the protein, which is what turns this from candy into something that holds you between meals. The dried mango sits in the middle: chewier than the freeze-dried stuff, so your jaw has something to do on a long climb. Salt matters more than it looks like it does, especially on a hot day when you are sweating it out faster than you replace it.
