Fruit leather is the cheapest way to carry fruit that is not mostly water. A 600 gram bag of frozen fruit leaves the dehydrator weighing a few ounces, and none of that weight is packaging or pit.

The applesauce is structural. Pureed fruit alone dries into something brittle that shatters in your hip belt pocket; the pectin in applesauce gives it enough flex to roll and unroll. Cooking first breaks the cell walls down so the puree spreads flat instead of sitting in lumps that dry at different rates. Store it in the freezer at home if it will sit for months, but on trail it is stable for the length of any trip you are likely to walk.
