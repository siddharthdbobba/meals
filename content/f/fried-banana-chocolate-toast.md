This is a camp dessert that needs no water and no second pot. The heat is the whole point: warm hazelnut spread turns to sauce and the banana goes soft, which is a different thing entirely from the same sandwich eaten cold.

Low heat matters more than technique. Butter and sugar both scorch fast, and a hot pan gives you a black crust over a cold middle. If you are only cooking one, invert your pot over the pan as a lid and the top slice warms while the bottom browns. The banana is the one perishable here, so this is a first-night recipe unless you start with fruit that is still firm.
