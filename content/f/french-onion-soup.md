Freeze-dried onions are the trick. They carry the depth of caramelized onion without the weight of fresh vegetables. Added to hot water, they rehydrate fully and release that flavor into broth. Mozzarella and parmesan work together: mozzarella gives body, parmesan adds sharpness and salt. Together they turn one cup of water into a complete dinner.

This is for cold camps when you need heat and calories from almost nothing. Freeze-dried onion slices rehydrate better than minced—they stay distinct instead of turning to paste. The soup carries light, uses only boiling water, and produces no waste.
