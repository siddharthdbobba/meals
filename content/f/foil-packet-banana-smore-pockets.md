This is a s'more that stopped needing sticks. The foil traps steam, so the banana goes soft and the chocolate melts into it instead of sliding off a cracker into the fire. Almonds are the structural element — without them the whole packet collapses into sweet mush.

Build the packets right before they go on the coals. Banana sitting on graham cracker for an hour turns the base to paste. Coals, not flame: gentle heat all the way around is the difference between melted and burnt, and foil gives you no warning between the two.
