  - "At home: heat the oil in a skillet and cook the hash browns until the edges brown and the water is gone. Wet potatoes make a soggy burrito."

All the cooking happens in your kitchen. At camp you are reheating, which is the only job worth doing over an open fire with kids running around. That also means one pan at home and zero pans at the site.

Two things decide whether these hold up. Drive the water out of the hash browns in the skillet, and let the filling cool fully before rolling. A warm filling steams the tortilla in the foil and you unwrap paste. The taco seasoning goes into the eggs rather than sprinkled on at the end so the salt is distributed instead of sitting in one bite.
