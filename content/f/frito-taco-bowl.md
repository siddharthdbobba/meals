This is a resupply meal, not a recipe: a bag of corn chips, a spoon of taco seasoning, and cheese, built in the bag with no stove and no water. Corn chips are cheap, they survive a week of being crushed against a bear can, and they carry more calories per ounce than almost anything else on a gas station shelf. The seasoning is doing all the work of turning them from a snack into something that reads as a meal.

The cheese is the reason to bother. Fat and salt in one block, no cooking, and it clings to the seasoning powder instead of sitting at the bottom of the bag. Hard cheese keeps for days out of a fridge in cool weather; string cheese is fine for a day or two and easier to buy in town. Eat the candy bar after, not with. Nothing about the chips gets better with chocolate in it.
