The original wanted a tub of sour cream, a brick of cream cheese, and a frozen block of spinach, which is three refrigerated items for one bowl of dip. Powdered cream cheese and sour cream rehydrate into the same thick base and ride in a pack all week. Freeze-dried spinach replaces the frozen block and skips the wringing-out step entirely.

Cook it on coals, not flame. Foil conducts fast and the dairy solids on the bottom will scorch before the middle warms if you set it over open fire. Mozzarella is the one thing worth a cooler slot; grated parmesan is dry enough to sit in the food bag without complaint.
