Home-dehydrated vegetables are the whole point here. Store-dried vegetables are usually cut too thick and stay leathery no matter how long they sit; vegetables you dried yourself, sliced thin, rehydrate in the same fifteen minutes the pasta needs. That matching of rehydration times is what lets one bag of water do all the work.

Small pasta shapes matter for the same reason. Anything thicker than macaroni will not soften in a bag that is only holding heat, not making it. The oil goes in last because fat coats the starch and slows absorption if you add it early, and because a bag of hot oil is harder to stir than a bag of hot water.
