The paper towel between two sheets of foil is the part worth keeping. Coals run hot and uneven, and the bottom of a packet sitting directly on them scorches long before the egg sets. The damp layer turns that into steam heat, buying you the ten minutes the egg needs without a black crust underneath.

Everything here except the egg is already cooked or dried, so the packet is really just heating food through and setting one egg. That is why the timing is forgiving. Dehydrated hash browns and powdered egg take the cooler out of the equation, at the cost of a soak step and a slightly flatter result.
