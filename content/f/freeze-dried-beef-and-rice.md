Freeze-dried meat rehydrates at the same speed as instant rice—you get real protein in one boil and one sitting period. Cheese adds calories; oil carries the salt through the whole thing and keeps the cheese from clumping into hard beads.

Insulation does the work. The pot sitting covered for fifteen minutes lets everything absorb water and soften; the heat trapped in there means you don't need a second boil. Stir once halfway through to prevent rice sticking to the bottom, or skip it and wrap the pot in a down jacket, and the same thing happens on its own.
