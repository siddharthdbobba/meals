Couscous is already cooked and dried, so it only needs to rehydrate. That means no simmering, no pot to scrub, and about two minutes of fuel instead of ten. The ground freeze-dried fruit does double duty: it dissolves into the water and colors the whole thing, so you get fruit flavor in every bite rather than a few chewy pieces.

The oil matters more than it looks. Couscous alone is close to fat-free and will leave you hungry an hour up the trail. A tablespoon adds roughly 120 calories and keeps the grains from clumping into a paste. Carry it apart from the dry mix; premixed oil soaks into the couscous and turns off-tasting within a few weeks.
