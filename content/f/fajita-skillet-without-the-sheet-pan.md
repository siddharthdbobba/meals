The original is a sheet pan in a 350-degree oven, which is not a thing you have. The method translates anyway, because roasting peppers and onions is mostly about driving water out of them, and dried vegetables arrive at camp with that already done. Adding a measured amount of water back and simmering it off gets you to roughly the same place in one pot.

The seasoning ratio is the part worth keeping intact. Cumin leads, chili powder is a quarter of it, and the garlic and salt are small. Dump in more chili powder and it turns into generic taco filling. The oil goes in at the end rather than the start: added early it just floats on the simmer water, added late it coats the vegetables and carries the spice.

No tags for vegetarian or gluten-free here. There is chicken in the pot and wheat tortillas in the bag.
