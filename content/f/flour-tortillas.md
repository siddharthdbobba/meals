Flour tortillas are practical trail bread. The dough comes together in minutes, cooks in minutes, and carries almost no weight—just dry ingredients and oil. They're lighter than bread or flatbreads, and more versatile than either for wraps and sides.

The key is using oil instead of butter for shelf stability in warm conditions. The taste is identical. The five-minute rest is worth the time: it lets the flour hydrate and the gluten relax, so the tortillas won't tear when you roll them. Make them fresh at camp for lunch and dinner wraps, or cook a few before breakfast to have wraps ready when you want them.
