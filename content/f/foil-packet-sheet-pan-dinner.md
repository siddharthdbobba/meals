This is a sheet pan dinner moved onto a fire. The oven version works because everything on the tray finishes at once; over coals you get less control, so the knife does the work instead. Carrots and potatoes go small, peppers and onions go big, tomatoes go in whole and late. Cut them all the same size and you get raw carrot next to collapsed tomato.

Seasoning the sausage apart from the vegetables costs nothing and keeps two flavors on the plate instead of one. The lime and cilantro go on after the packet opens — heat flattens both. This one needs a cooler, so it is a car-camping or first-night meal, not something to carry on day four.
