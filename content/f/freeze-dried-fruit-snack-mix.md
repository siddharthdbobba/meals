This is what the freeze-dried fruit aisle is actually good for. Freeze-drying pulls the water out without cooking the fruit, so the berries stay sharp and sweet instead of turning leathery like conventionally dried fruit. They also weigh almost nothing, which is the whole point when the snack bag is the part of the food you eat most often.

The seeds are doing the calorie work. Fruit alone is fast sugar and not much else, and you feel the drop an hour later. Pumpkin and sunflower seeds bring fat and protein, and the dried vegetables and salt keep it from tasting like candy by mile eight. Seeds instead of nuts here on purpose, so the bag stays safe to hand around a group.
