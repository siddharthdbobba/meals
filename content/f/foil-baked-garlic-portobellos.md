steps:
  - "Wipe the caps clean with a dry bandana. Do not rinse them. A wet mushroom steams instead of browning and comes out slack."
  - "Snap out the stems and chop them. Mince the garlic."
  - "Mix the chopped stems, garlic, rice, sun-dried tomatoes, olives, herbs, soy sauce, and half the oil in a mug or bag."
  - "Brush the outside of each cap with the rest of the oil. Set each one gill side up on a sheet of foil and pile the filling in."
  - "Fold the foil into a sealed packet with a little air space above the filling so it does not crush the cap."

Mushroom caps are mostly water, and the whole method is about getting that water to work for you. Sealed in foil, the cap releases its liquid and then sits and cooks in it, which is why this needs no water carried in and no pot. The rice and the sun-dried tomatoes soak that liquid back up so nothing runs out when you open the packet.

The original is a fridge-clean-out dish, so the trail version fixes the fillings to things that keep: dry-packed tomatoes, pouch olives, dried herbs, and rice you already cooked. Cheese and egg are off the list because neither is worth the cooler space here. The mushrooms are the only item that needs to stay cold, and they only need to survive the drive.
