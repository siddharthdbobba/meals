The original uses frozen mango and a tub of whipped cream, neither of which survives a trailhead. Dried mango soaked in cold water gets close, and whipping cream powder shaken with a little cold water sets up thick enough to sit on top instead of running off. Use less water than the package says. Runny cream ruins the crumbs.

Frying the biscuit crumbs in butter is the step worth doing properly. Raw crumbs are just dry biscuit; browned in fat they go crisp and nutty and hold their texture under the wet mango for the few minutes it takes to eat. Keep the crumbs moving and take the pot off the heat early, because the residual heat in an aluminum pot will keep cooking them.
