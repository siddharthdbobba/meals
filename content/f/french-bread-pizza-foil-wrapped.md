Home versions of this go in a 400-degree oven. A fire has no thermostat, so the fix is foil and coals rather than flame. The packet traps steam long enough to melt the cheese, and the coal bed heats the bottom crust evenly instead of blackening it in one spot.

Oil and garlic powder on the cut face are doing structural work, not seasoning work. Bread wicks sauce fast, and a wet crust in a foil packet turns to paste. Low-moisture mozzarella matters for the same reason: fresh mozzarella releases water with nowhere to go. This one wants a cooler for the cheese and pepperoni, so it is a car-camping or basecamp dinner, not a multi-day carry.
