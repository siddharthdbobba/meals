Foil packets fail in one predictable way: the potatoes are still hard when everything else is done. Boiling them for ten minutes at home fixes that, and it costs nothing on the trip. Everything else in the packet — sausage already cooked, thin beans, diced onion — is fast, so a parboiled potato puts the whole thing on the same clock.

Indirect heat matters more than temperature. Straight over coals the foil scorches the bottom layer before the middle gets warm. Off to the side, the sealed packet turns into a small steam oven, and the sausage fat plus the oil carries the seasoning through the rest. This one needs a cooler, so it is a car-camping or basecamp dinner, not a backpacking one.
