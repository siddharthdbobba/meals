This started as a desk lunch built to be cheap: one can of tuna stretched across two servings with whatever vegetables were in the drawer, and yogurt in place of mayonnaise. The stretching part carries over well. Vegetable flakes weigh almost nothing and give the filling enough bulk that one small can feeds a real lunch.

The fridge parts are the ones that had to change. Yogurt powder rehydrates into something tangy and thick enough to bind, and dried celery, carrot, and cabbage come back close enough to raw after five minutes in cold water. Add the oil if you are walking all afternoon. Yogurt powder is protein, not fuel, and the tortillas alone will not cover the gap.
