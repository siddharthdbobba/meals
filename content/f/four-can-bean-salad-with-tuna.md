This is pantry cooking moved outdoors: everything comes from a shelf, nothing needs a cooler, and the only tool that matters is a can opener. It started as a power-outage meal, which is the same problem car camping has — food that keeps, and a meal you can assemble with no heat at all.

The salsa does double duty as dressing and seasoning, so there is no oil bottle or vinegar to carry. Draining matters more than it sounds like it should. Bean liquid is starchy and salty and it will pool at the bottom of the pot and dull everything above it. Four cans of beans and corn on their own are mostly carbohydrate; the tuna is what makes it hold you until dinner.
