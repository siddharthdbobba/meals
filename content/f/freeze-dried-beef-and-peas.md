Freeze-dried beef and peas need only boiling water and time. Twenty minutes under a lid rehydrates both fully. Boiling water matters—cold water works but takes much longer and produces tougher beef.

This is protein-focused. Thirty-two grams from the beef, six from the peas. Butter powder and Parmesan add fat that makes the broth actually stick to your ribs instead of passing straight through. For calories per ounce carried, it's hard to argue with on a thru-hike.
