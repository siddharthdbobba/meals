A frother is a luxury item that weighs about as much as a spare battery, and it earns the space by fixing the one thing wrong with camp coffee: milk powder does not dissolve. Stirred, it clumps and settles and leaves grit at the bottom. Whipped, it emulsifies, and the fat in whole milk powder holds air the way real milk does.

The paste step is the part people skip. Hydrating the powder in a small amount of water first breaks up the dry clumps while there is still enough friction to work against. Dump everything into a full mug at once and you get lumps no amount of whisking will save.
