Foil packets fail two ways: raw potatoes and a burned bottom. Rehydrating the potatoes first fixes the raw half, since dried slices soak up water in fifteen minutes and then only need heating through. Coals rather than flames fix the other half.

The sausage is the reason this works without a cooler. Vacuum-sealed smoked sausage sits in a bin for days, and it renders enough fat to carry the spices into the potatoes. The oil covers what the sausage does not.
