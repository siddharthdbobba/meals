The original is a way to use up leftover fried catfish, which is not a thing you have in camp. Foil-pouch fish gets you to the same place: already cooked, already flaked, no cooler. Powdered egg and cracker crumbs do the binding that eggs and crumbs always did, and the dried onion and bell pepper rehydrate in the time it takes to light the stove.

The water measurements matter more than the cracker measurement. Too wet and the cakes fall apart in the pan; too dry and they crumble when you flip them. Squeeze a test patty before you commit the whole batch. The sauce is mayo packets and pantry bottles, so it keeps fine in a pack, but mix it before you start frying because you will not want to stop mid-cook.
