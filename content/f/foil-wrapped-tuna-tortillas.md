This is a campfire sandwich that solves the problem of cold tuna salad on a cold night. The foil does the cooking: it traps steam from the mayo and tuna and heats the roll evenly instead of charring one side. Wrapped tight, it also means no pot and no plate.

Heat control is the whole recipe. Put these in flames and the tortilla blackens in a minute while the cheese sits there solid. Coals that have gone grey, a grate, and steady rotation gets you melted cheese and an intact wrap. If you have no cooler, the variation below moves the whole thing to shelf-stable parts — pouch tuna, mayo packets, hard cheese — and it works the same.
