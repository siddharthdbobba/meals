This is a day-one meal and it does not pretend otherwise. Fresh meat is the one thing worth carrying on ice, and it only stays safe for the first night out, so cook it before anything else in the cooler starts arguing with you. Everything else here is dry weight that would survive a month.

The couscous is doing more than filling space. It cooks off residual heat rather than sustained flame, which means the pot leaves the fire while the skewers are still on it, and the fat rendering off the meat has somewhere to land. Seasoning the meat at home matters too: salt needs hours to get past the surface, and you will not want to stand around a fire waiting for it.
