This is an oven appetizer moved onto coals. The foil packet does what the covered baking dish did: it traps steam so the cheese melts through before the crust burns. A grate over embers gives you the same steady low heat an oven does. Direct flame does not, and the bottom of the slab will char in a couple of minutes if you try.

Assembling at home is the real change. Layering nine things on a picnic table is a chore, and cold cuts sitting out while you do it is how a lunch goes wrong. Cheese on both the top and bottom of the meat is not redundant either, it seals the bread against the mustard and mayo so the rolls hold up in the cooler.
