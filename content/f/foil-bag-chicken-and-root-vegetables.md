The foil bag is a steam oven. The quarter cup of water and the bouillon cube turn into a small amount of hot broth that cooks the vegetables through while the fat browns the chicken against the foil. That is why the water matters and why you seal the bag properly instead of tenting it.

Dark meat is the whole reason this works on a fire you cannot dial in. Legs and thighs sit in that hour-long window without going stringy, and the potatoes need close to the full hour anyway. This one needs a cooler, so it belongs at a car campsite, not in a pack.
