This is the chili you make when camp went up late and everyone is standing around the picnic table. The entire cook is one pot and about half an hour, and most of that is unattended simmer time while you deal with firewood.

The short simmer is the reason for the ingredient list. Canned beans are already cooked, canned tomatoes are already broken down, and diced tomatoes with green chiles bring the heat without a second spice jar. The onion is the only thing that actually needs those twenty minutes, so dice it small and it will be soft by the time the rest is hot. This one needs a cooler for the beef; if you have no cooler, use the crumbles from the variations and the whole meal becomes shelf-stable.
