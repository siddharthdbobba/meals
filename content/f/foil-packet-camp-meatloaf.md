This is car camp cooking, not pack cooking. It needs a cooler, an egg, and a real fire, so it stays at the trailhead. What it buys you is a dinner with no pot to scrub: the foil is the pan, and it goes in the trash bag when you are done.

Croutons instead of breadcrumbs is the useful swap. They are already dry, already salted, already seasoned, and they crush fine in the bag they came in. The egg is structural, not optional. Without it the loaf falls apart when you slide it out of the foil. Keep the packets thin and flat, because a thick loaf over coals chars before the center is safe.
