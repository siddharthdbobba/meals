The source of this one is a home-freezer trick: precook pasta, rinse it, oil it, and stash it so a later dinner is fast. There is no freezer on trail, so the storage step becomes a dehydrator. The rest of the logic survives intact. Undercooking by two minutes and rinsing cold stops the starch before it turns to paste, and the oil keeps the pieces from welding together while they dry.

Dried precooked pasta rehydrates in about five minutes off the heat, against eight to ten minutes of active boiling for raw pasta. That is most of a canister's worth of fuel saved over a week, and no pot of starchy water to dump. Sauce powder and parmesan go in the same bag because they thicken whatever water the pasta does not absorb, which is what keeps this from being noodles in a puddle.
