The original is baked dough. Dough needs proofing, an oven, and 450F you will not get out of a fire ring, so the tortilla does the job instead: same fold, same handheld result, no rise time. Everything inside is already cured or aged, which means no cooler and no food-safety math.

The order of the filling matters more than the amounts. Cheese against both faces of the tortilla melts into a seal, so the sauce stays in the pocket rather than in the foil. Keep the sauce thin and the border wide, and use coals rather than flame. Foil conducts fast, and the outside will char well before the middle is warm if the fire is still burning high.
