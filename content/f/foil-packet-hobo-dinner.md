This is the classic scout foil dinner, and the only real technique in it is the wet paper towel between two layers of foil. Steam from the towel spreads the heat and buys you a buffer against the hot spots in a coal bed. Without it the side facing the embers scorches before the potato gives up.

The rest is geometry. Raw beef and raw root vegetables need roughly the same twenty minutes, so dice the potato and carrot small and keep the packet flat and thin. Breaking the beef into pieces rather than leaving a patty cooks it faster and makes it easier to eat off foil with one fork. This one wants a cooler, so it is a car-camping or basecamp meal, not something to carry.
