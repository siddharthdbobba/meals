The original of this is oven food: cream cheese in a pepper, bacon around the outside, a rack to catch the grease. None of that survives a trip without a fridge, so the filling gets rebuilt from powders. Milk powder and dry hard cheese give it the body cream cheese was doing, the cheese sauce mix supplies salt and the tang, and oil plus a small amount of water pulls it into a paste. Keep the water low. A loose filling runs out of the pepper and burns on the foil.

Bacon becomes crumbles pressed into the top instead of a wrap, which also removes the reason for the rack. An open-topped foil tray matters more than it sounds: a sealed packet traps steam and you end up with pale, wet peppers. Coals rather than flame, because jalapeño skin blackens long before the filling is hot through.
