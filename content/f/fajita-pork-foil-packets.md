This started as a sheet-pan dinner, which is the same idea as a foil packet: one surface, one heat source, nothing to wash. Foil just moves the oven outside. The doubled sheet matters, since a single layer tears on the grate and dumps everything into the coals.

Pork shoulder is the right cut here because it has enough fat to survive uneven camp heat without drying out. The pineapple is doing work beyond sweetness — its juice steams inside the packet and keeps the onions from scorching before the pork is done. Drain canned pineapple well or the packet floods and everything boils instead of roasting. This one needs a cooler, so it is a first- or second-night meal, not a backpacking dinner.
