Feta does not melt so much as slump. That is the point here: it softens and takes on the tomato liquid and oil without running off through the grate, so you get a spreadable block instead of a puddle in the coals. The sealed foil traps the steam coming off the tomatoes, which cooks the peppers through in the same twenty minutes.

Medium coals matter more than time. Over open flame the bottom layer scorches before the cheese warms up, which is why the tomatoes go down first — they take the heat and turn into the sauce. This is a hosting snack, not a trail meal: it needs a cooler and something to scoop with, so it belongs where you drove in.
