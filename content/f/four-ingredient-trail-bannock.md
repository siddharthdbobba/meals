Bread is one of the cheapest things to make from scratch and one of the few that survives being made without a kitchen. Flour, baking powder, salt, and fat is the whole system. Baking powder does the lifting, so there is no yeast to keep alive and no rise to wait through — you mix and you cook.

The home version of this leans on buttermilk and an oven. Neither travels, so powdered milk carries the dairy and a covered pan over low heat does the oven's job: the lid traps enough steam to cook the middle before the crust gives up. Keep the round thin and the flame low and it works every time.
