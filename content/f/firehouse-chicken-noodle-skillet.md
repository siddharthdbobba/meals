The original is an oven casserole held together by cans: two condensed soups, sour cream, milk, and shredded mozzarella. None of that travels. Powders do. Dry soup mix, milk powder, sour cream powder, and shaker parmesan rehydrate into the same gravy using the pasta water you were going to pour out anyway, which is why there is no draining step and no second pot.

Cooking the pasta and the dried onion and celery together saves fuel and gives the vegetables the full boil they need to come back. The corn flakes are the one thing worth carrying separately. Their whole job is texture against a soft, heavy bowl, and they lose it about ninety seconds after they hit the butter.
