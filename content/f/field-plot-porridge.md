Three cheap bulk grains, each covering a gap in the others. Oats thicken fast and go soft in minutes. Fine bulgur brings chew, so the pot does not turn into wallpaper paste. Split peas carry the protein and the savory weight that oats alone never have.

The fat matters more than it looks. Grains and legumes are almost all carbohydrate, and at 600 ml this is a lot of volume for the calories. The tablespoon of oil is a third of the dinner. Add it off the heat so it does not just cook off into the steam.
