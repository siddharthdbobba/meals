The original called for real potatoes, fresh milk, and a pan of bacon. All three are cooler problems. Instant flakes and powdered milk get you the same mash with no perishables, and shelf-stable bacon crumbles carry the smoke and salt without a skillet.

The damp paper towel inside its own foil pocket is the part worth keeping. Coals put heat into the bottom of a packet far faster than the top, and mash scorches quickly. The wet towel holds around boiling until it dries out, which buys you the twenty minutes you need to melt the cheese through.
