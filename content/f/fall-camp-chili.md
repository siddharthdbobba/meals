This is stovetop chili from a home kitchen, moved to a fire ring with as few changes as possible. Everything except the beef and the vegetables is shelf-stable, and the vegetables are the kind that survive a day in a bin without a cooler. Only the meat actually needs cold.

The chili beans carry their own sauce, so this does not need a spice kit to taste like chili. That is the reason the recipe holds up in camp: fewer small containers to lose. The long simmer is not optional — twenty minutes in it still tastes like a can, and by thirty the tomato and the beef have found each other. Coals over flame, and stir the bottom, or you will be scrubbing scorch by headlamp.
