steps:
  - "Build a bed of coals or set a grate over the fire. You want steady moderate heat, not flames."
  - "Tear two arm-length sheets of heavy foil and stack them. Cut the pepper into strips and pile it in the middle with the sun-dried tomatoes and olives."
  - "Squeeze the lemon half over the vegetables, add the oil, and season. Fold the foil into a sealed packet and set it on the grate for 10 minutes, turning once."
  - "Open the packet away from your face. Pat the cod dry, salt it, cut it into two pieces, and lay them on the vegetables. Reseal."

The original is a sheet pan in a 220 °C oven. A foil packet on coals does the same job — the sealed pouch traps steam so the peppers soften before the fish goes in, and staging it in two rounds keeps the cod from overcooking while the vegetables catch up.

Cherry tomatoes and fresh herbs are the parts that do not survive a food bin, so sun-dried tomatoes and dried parsley stand in. The tomatoes bring the same acid in a form that will not burst in your pack. Feta and cod are the only cooler items, which makes this a first-night dinner. Everything else keeps indefinitely.
