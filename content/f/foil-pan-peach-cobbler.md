This is a dump dessert with one real technique in it: the dry cake mix never gets mixed with liquid. It sits on top and pulls juice up from below, which is why the fruit needs to be swimming and why only one can's juice goes back in. Two cans of juice and the topping turns to batter soup.

The ten-minute rest off the fire is not optional. That is when the mix finishes absorbing and firms into a crust. Pull the foil early and you get a pan of hot peaches with powder on them. Heat should be coals, not flame, since a fire pushing up under a thin foil pan scorches the bottom long before the top sets. Cooler required for the butter, so this stays a car-camping dessert.
