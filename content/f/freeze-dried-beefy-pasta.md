Freeze-dried ingredients rehydrate instantly and completely when mixed with hot water. The pasta, vegetables, and meat all soften at the same rate, eliminating the timing games of traditional one-pot meals. The starches in the pasta swell and thicken the broth into something substantial enough to eat with a spoon.

Everything in this recipe is shelf-stable and light enough for extended backcountry trips. The entire meal weighs less than eight ounces and keeps for years without refrigeration or special care. You can buy the freeze-dried components ready-made or process your own with a home freeze-dryer.
