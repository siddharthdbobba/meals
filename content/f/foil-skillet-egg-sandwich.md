Every part of this is a swap for something that needs a refrigerator. Powdered egg is the one that matters: it rehydrates in five minutes, weighs almost nothing, and scrambles like the real thing as long as you let it hydrate fully before it hits heat. Ghee replaces butter because it keeps at ambient temperature for weeks. Hard cheese and sealed ham do the same job.

The foil tray is the reason this is one sandwich and no dishes. Shaping it over the outside of your pot gives it a rim, which is the difference between a tray and a sheet with egg running off it. Foil conducts fast and has no thermal mass, so cook over coals or a low flame and keep stirring. When you are done, the tray folds into a square and goes in the trash bag.
