The source was a week of cheap supermarket dinners for a family of four: ground beef, potatoes, tomatoes, cheese, buying one store's loss leaders. Most of that list is water and refrigeration, which is the wrong shape for a pack. What survives the conversion is the cost logic and the flavor base, so this is one skillet dinner built from the cheapest shelf-stable versions of the same four things.

Dehydrating the beef at home is the only real work, and it is what keeps the protein honest at 34 grams. Rinse the cooked crumbles with hot water before drying or the fat goes rancid in a week. The potato flakes go in last and off the heat: they absorb whatever liquid is left and turn a thin tomato broth into something that stays on a spoon. Oil and cheese carry the calories, since lean dried beef brings almost none.
