This is a lunch for warm days when soup is wrong and another tuna packet is worse. The oil is the preservative: feta and olives both arrive packed in brine, and covering them in oil keeps air off, so the crock sits on the table instead of in a cooler. That is why it works without a fridge for a couple of days.

The green onion is the weak link and the only thing here with a clock on it. Use it the first day or two, then leave it out and lean harder on the oregano and paprika. Everything else is shelf-stable until opened, so a second jar's worth can ride in the pack unopened.
