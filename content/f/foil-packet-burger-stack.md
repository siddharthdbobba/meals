The original stacked lettuce into the packet. Lettuce in a foil packet turns to grey slime in about four minutes, so it is gone here. The onion does the job lettuce was probably meant to do: it sits against the metal, gives up moisture, and keeps the meat from welding to the foil.

The real variable is potato thickness. Beef is done long before a thick slice of raw potato is, so cut them thin and put them next to the patty where the heat concentrates. This one needs a cooler, so it stays a car-camping meal. There is no way to carry raw ground beef up a trail.
