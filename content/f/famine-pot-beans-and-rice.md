This started as a year-of-food storage list: bulk beans, bulk rice, potato flakes, oil, sugar, salt. That list assumes an hour of simmering and a kitchen. On the trail the same four ingredients work if you buy the instant versions, which are the only real change here. Dry beans need forty minutes and a lot of fuel. Refried bean flakes need sixty seconds.

The potato flakes are structural, not filler. Instant rice and bean flakes both give up water as they sit, and the flakes take it back so dinner does not turn into a puddle at the bottom of the pot. Oil is where most of the calories live. A teaspoon of sugar sounds odd in a bean pot, but plain stored beans taste flat and it fixes that for almost no weight.
