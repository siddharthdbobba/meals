The original used raw chicken breast and thirty-five minutes in an oven. On a fire that is a long time to guess at, so this version uses cooked pouch chicken and dried peppers instead. Nothing in the packet needs to reach a safe temperature; it only needs to get hot enough to steam the rice, which is why it comes off the coals in twenty minutes.

Water is the part people get wrong. Instant rice wants roughly equal parts water by volume, and the salsa adds more, so measure rather than pour. Coals matter too. Open flame scorches the bottom of the foil before the middle ever steams, so rake a flat bed and cook on that.
