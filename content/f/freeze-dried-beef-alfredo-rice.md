Freeze-dried ingredients make a full dinner weigh almost nothing. The alfredo sauce mix and powdered sour cream dissolve into hot water without needing fresh dairy, and the rehydration happens passively while you sit and rest. All the protein comes from the beef, which is stable indefinitely and doesn't need refrigeration.

The oil carries the alfredo flavor and adds back the calories that dehydration removes. Without it you have a bland, lean survival meal. The fifteen-minute wait does the cooking for you—no flame, no stirring, no cleanup beyond sealing the bag.
