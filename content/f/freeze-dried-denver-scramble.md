steps:
  - "Put the freeze-dried peppers and onion in a small bag. Add 1/4 cup cool water, seal, and let sit for 10 minutes."
  - "In another bag, combine the freeze-dried eggs and 1/4 cup cool water. Seal and shake for 10 seconds. Let sit for 10 minutes."
  - "In a third bag, add the freeze-dried cheese and 1-2 tablespoons cool water. Shake every minute or two to soften it evenly."
  - "Heat the oil in a skillet or wide pot over medium heat. Add the rehydrated vegetables and sauté until they start to soften, about 2 minutes."
  - "Sprinkle in the bacon bits and heat for 1 minute."
  - "Pour in the rehydrated eggs and stir constantly, breaking up the curds as they set. This takes about 2 minutes."

Freeze-dried ingredients mean you can carry breakfast without ice. The rehydration step—ten minutes while you get settled—gives everything the right texture. Butter powder and oil carry most of the calories, not the eggs. Without them, you have egg-flavored protein; with them, you have a meal.

Everything is shelf-stable enough to eat today or split across two days. Keep the bacon bits sealed once opened or they'll absorb humidity and turn to paste.
