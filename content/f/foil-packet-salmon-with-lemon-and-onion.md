The onion, lemon, and garlic are not garnish. They lift the fish off the hot foil so it steams instead of frying, and the liquid they give up mixes with the butter into a sauce that ends up in the bottom of the packet. Skip the layer and you get a scorched underside.

One big fillet beats four small ones over a fire, where the heat is never even. A thick piece is forgiving, and you can split it after it rests. This one stays a car-camping recipe. Salmon and butter need a cooler, so cook it the first night and do not try to stretch it further.
