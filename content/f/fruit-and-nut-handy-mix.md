This is a fat mix wearing fruit as a disguise. Macadamias and cashews are most of the calories, and the almonds add chew so the whole thing isn't soft. The dried fruit is there for sugar you can absorb while walking, which nuts alone won't give you.

The salt matters more than the quarter-teaspoon suggests. Sweet dried fruit gets cloying by the second handful, and salt is what keeps you reaching back into the bag on a long afternoon. It also replaces some of what you're sweating out. If your nuts came salted, use a pinch and taste before adding more, because you can't take it back out.
