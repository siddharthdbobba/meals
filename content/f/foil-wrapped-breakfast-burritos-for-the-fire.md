This is assembly work done at the kitchen counter so that camp mornings are one step: put foil on coals, wait. Nothing here needs a pan, a plate, or a spoon at the site, and the wrapper is the dish.

Temperature discipline at home is the whole game. Filling that goes in warm sweats inside the foil and the tortilla goes gummy by day two. Cool everything to room temperature first, keep the beans as the layer against the tortilla, and they reheat clean. Coals rather than open flame — foil against flame scorches the tortilla before the center thaws.
