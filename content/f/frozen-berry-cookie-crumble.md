The original is a way to use up melting ice cream. Nobody has melting ice cream on a trip, so the cold part is gone and what stays is the texture: something thick and cold, fruit, and crunch. Instant pudding and milk powder do the thick-and-cold job with 120 ml of water and no stove.

Freeze-dried berries are load-bearing here, not decoration. They soak up free water as they rehydrate, which is what keeps the mix from sliding into soup. Add them last and eat it fast, because the cookie crumbs are on the same clock going the other way.
