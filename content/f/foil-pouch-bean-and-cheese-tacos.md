Foil pouch refried beans are the whole point here. Canned beans are heavy and leave you with a can; the pouch weighs about half as much, needs no water, and packs down to nothing when it is empty. They come out of the pouch at the right thickness, so this is a warming job, not a cooking job.

The cheese is doing the work that fat usually does in a trail dinner. Beans and tortillas are mostly starch and protein, and without a couple of ounces of hard cheese the meal reads as light an hour later. Grate it at home if you want it to melt evenly — thin slices work, but they sit in stripes.
