The original is a cast iron dish with two pounds of fresh mushrooms, a cup of mascarpone, and a half cup of red wine. None of that travels. Dried mushrooms lose the water weight and gain concentration, and the soaking liquid replaces the veggie broth, so nothing is wasted. Powdered cream cheese does what the mascarpone did without a cooler.

The flour matters. Without it the sauce is mushroom water with dairy floating in it. Cook it in the oil for a minute before any liquid goes in, or the whole pot tastes like paste. Draining the noodles first is the only fussy part, and it is worth it: noodles cooked in the sauce release enough starch to turn the whole thing into glue.
