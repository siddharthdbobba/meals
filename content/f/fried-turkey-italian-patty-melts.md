This is a home kitchen recipe that needs a cooler and a real fire, so it belongs at the truck rather than in a pack. Everything else about it travels fine: the flour and crumb mix is dry goods, the marinara comes in a shelf-stable jar, and hard cheese slices hold a couple of days on ice.

The breading is the whole point. Ground leg and thigh meat is lean and dries out fast over a fire, and a flour-egg-crumb crust seals the outside before the inside overcooks. Slice the cheese rather than shredding it. Shredded cheese slides off a domed patty and burns in the oil.
