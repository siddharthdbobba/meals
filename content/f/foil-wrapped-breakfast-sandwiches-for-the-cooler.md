This is a kitchen recipe that survives the trip because of the foil. Each sandwich is its own sealed packet, so it goes from cooler to coals without a pan, a plate, or a spatula, and the only cleanup is a ball of foil in the trash bag.

The underbaked eggs are the part worth getting right. Fully set eggs come out of the fire rubbery, since the reheat is a second cook. Pull the pan while the middle still looks slightly loose and it lands correctly at camp. Powdered milk stands in for fresh in the egg mix, which matters only if you are batching these somewhere without a fridge.
