This started as an oven appetizer, which is the wrong tool at a campsite. Foil does the same job: the sealed packet traps steam so the rolls warm through without drying, and opening it for the last ten minutes drives that moisture off so the granola actually crisps. Skip the second stage and you get a soggy lid on top of good cheese.

Brie is soft ripened and does not travel outside a cooler, so this is a car-camping or first-morning recipe and nothing else. Assembling at home is the real saving. Hollowing twelve rolls and slicing a brie log evenly is fussy work you do not want to do on a picnic table before coffee.
