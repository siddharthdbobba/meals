Eggplant is mostly water, which makes it a bad trail vegetable and a good dehydrating candidate. Dried until brittle it weighs almost nothing, and it comes back spongy rather than mushy as long as it rehydrates alongside something that needs the same simmer time. Red lentils do. They collapse in about twelve minutes and turn the pot into a stew instead of vegetables sitting in water.

The cinnamon is doing structural work, not decoration. A quarter teaspoon reads as depth against the tomato; a full teaspoon reads as dessert. Add the oil off the heat so it coats rather than fries, and salt at the end because lentils that boil in salted water take longer to soften.
