This one only works with fresh mushrooms, so it is a car camping or basecamp dish, not something you carry for four days. Big flat caps are the whole point. They sit on the coals like small steaks and cook without falling apart, and there is nowhere for a bug or a soft spot to hide, so pick clean ones.

The oil goes on after, never before. Oiled mushrooms dripping onto coals flare up and coat the caps in soot. Cooking them dry lets the wood smoke do the seasoning, and the raw garlic and herbs land on hot mushrooms at the end, where the heat pulls the flavor out without cooking it flat. Cook them longer than looks necessary. Wild mushrooms rushed through heat are a bad night.
