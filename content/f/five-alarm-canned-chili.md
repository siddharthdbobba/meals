This is a canned-goods dinner, which means it is heavy and it is meant for a car, a basecamp, or a canoe. Nothing here gets rehydrated. The whole recipe is a heating job, so the only real risk is scorching the bottom of the pot over a fire that is hotter than you think.

The canned chili is the base and the extra beans and corn are the stretch: two cans feed three people, four cans feed six. BBQ sauce is doing more than sweetening. It adds smoke and acid that canned chili is short on, and it thins the mix just enough to stir. Add the heat in stages and taste as you go, because you cannot take it back out.
