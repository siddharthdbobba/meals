An 8-ounce jar of applesauce is mostly water. Dry it and you are left with roughly half an ounce of fruit and a recyclable jar you never carried. That ratio is the whole argument: same sugar, same fiber, a tenth of the weight, and no core or peel to pack out at the end of the day.

Thickness is the only thing that goes wrong. Too thin and the sheet cracks into shards in your hip belt pocket; too thick and the middle stays wet, which is how a bag of leather goes moldy in week two. Aim for even, check the center rather than the edge, and give it another hour if you are unsure.
