Freeze-dried fruit is the lightest sweet thing you can carry, but it is almost pure sugar and it disappears fast. The seeds are what make this a snack instead of candy: fat and protein that slow the whole thing down, plus something to actually chew. The salt matters more than it looks like it should, because six hours of walking leaves you wanting salt more than sugar.

Seeds instead of nuts is a deliberate choice. Pumpkin and sunflower seeds hold up in a hot pack better than most nuts, cost less per ounce, and keep the bag safe for anyone with a nut allergy sharing snacks at a break. Nothing here needs water, a stove, or a bowl.
