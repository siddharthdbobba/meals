Couscous is already cooked when it leaves the factory, so it needs only water and time. Freeze-dried chicken rehydrates at the same rate, so everything finishes together in 15 minutes. Seal the bag well if storing longer than a couple of days; freeze-dried meat doesn't tolerate air exposure.

Freezer bag cooking trades pot cleanup for a sealed plastic bag and a cozy. Pour hot water, seal, wrap it in your fleece or a dedicated insulator, and walk around your camp while it steeps. Eat straight from the bag or transfer to a bowl.
