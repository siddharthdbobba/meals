Popcorn is the cheapest volume-per-ounce snack you can carry. Two tablespoons of kernels weigh almost nothing and come back as a couple of quarts of food, which is the whole reason it earns space in a pack.

The foil bag does two jobs: it holds the oil against the kernels and it traps steam so the corn pops instead of just toasting. The failure modes are both about geometry. Too little headroom and the popped corn crushes itself against the foil, so the rest never gets hot enough. Too still over the fire and the bottom kernels scorch before the top ones go. Keep it swinging, and pull it off early rather than late.
