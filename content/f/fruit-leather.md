This is one of the few trail snacks worth making from scratch, because the store version is mostly sugar and corn syrup and this is mostly fruit. The applesauce is not filler. Apples are high in pectin, and that pectin is what turns the puree into a flexible sheet instead of a tray of brittle flakes.

Thickness decides everything. Too thin and the edges go crisp and crack before the middle is dry; too thick and the center stays tacky and will mold in the bag. Spread it even and check the middle, not the rim. Fully dried leather is shelf-stable for weeks, which is what makes it a real resupply item rather than a day-one treat.
