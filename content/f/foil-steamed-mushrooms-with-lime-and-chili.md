  - "Pour just enough hot water over the dried mushrooms to cover them, about 150 ml. Let them sit 10 minutes, then lift them out. Keep the soaking liquid."

Matsutake and other strong wild mushrooms get worse when you brown them hard. Steam keeps their smell intact, which is the whole point of carrying them. Foil does what parchment does at home: traps the aromatics against the mushroom and holds the moisture in.

The field version leans on dried everything, since fresh cilantro, scallion, and chili turn to slime by day two. The soaking liquid is the concentrated part, so a couple of spoonfuls go back into the packet rather than down the drain. Oil matters more than it looks like it should. Without it the packet tastes thin and watery, and it is also most of the calories here.
