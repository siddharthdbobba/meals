The original is an oven ham for a crowd. On a trip you have neither the oven nor the crowd, so this is the same glaze on a shelf-stable ham steak, cooked in foil on coals. Canned and pouched ham is already cured and cooked, which is why the time is short and why nothing here is a food-safety gamble in the heat.

The sugar is doing the work. Honey and brown sugar melt, hit the cut surface of the meat, and thicken into a coating instead of running off. Mustard powder and cloves keep it from being flat sweetness. Cook it over coals rather than flame or the glaze turns black while the middle is still cold.
