Foil is the whole method here. A sealed packet turns the coals into a small oven, and the apple juice steams the chop instead of letting the fire dry it out. The butter and rendered fat pool in the bottom of the packet, so the meat sits in its own sauce the entire time.

This is cooler food, not pack food. The apple and onion are doing more than flavor work: they hold the chop off the hot foil and keep the underside from charring. Bone-in chops are worth the extra weight because they forgive an extra five minutes on the fire. Serve straight from the foil and the only thing to clean is your fork.
