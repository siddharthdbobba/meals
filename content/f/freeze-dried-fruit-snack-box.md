Freeze-dried fruit is the highest calorie-per-ounce sweet snack that does not melt, weep, or need a cooler. Water is removed under vacuum rather than heat, so the pieces stay full size and shatter instead of chewing like a dried apricot. That texture is the whole appeal, and it is also the fragility problem.

The corn and pumpkin seeds are here to keep it edible past hour two. Straight fruit is all sugar and acid, and most people quit on it by the second day. Salt and something savory reset the palate. The banana chips carry the only real fat in the bag, which is why the calorie number is not lower.
