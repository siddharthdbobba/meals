This is a car-camping dinner, not a trail one. Canned vegetables and a pound of raw meat weigh close to four pounds, and the meat needs a cooler until it hits the fire. What you get for that weight is a real dinner with no pot and no dishes.

The layering is the part that matters. Meat on the bottom sits closest to the coals and gets the most heat, which it needs, while the canned vegetables are already cooked and only have to warm through. Drain the cans hard or the packet steams into soup. The cheese goes on last so it melts down through everything instead of burning against the foil.
