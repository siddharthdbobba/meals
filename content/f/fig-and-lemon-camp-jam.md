Fig preserves are a long, slow home project. On the trail the constraint is fuel, not time, so this version starts from dried figs and a measured 300 ml of water instead of fresh fruit that would spoil in a pack. The soak rehydrates the figs enough that they break down under a lid rather than needing an hour of simmering.

Equal weights of fruit and sugar is what makes the jam keep without a fridge. Sugar that high binds the water, so the finished pot sits on a picnic table for days. The lemon is not just flavor: the acid helps the set, which is why a low-sugar version comes out loose.
