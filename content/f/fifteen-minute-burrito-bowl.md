Dehydrated pinto beans are the whole reason this works. Raw beans need an hour of hard boil and fuel you did not carry. Precooked dried beans come back in ten minutes off a single pour of hot water, which puts them on the same schedule as instant rice.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Beans and dried vegetables go first and get the longer soak, because they are the slowest things in the pot. Rice goes in second with the rest of the water. Oil at the end, since it coats the grains and stops the seasoning from settling into a paste at the bottom.
