This is a cooler-and-grate meal, not a pack meal. It needs two skillets, standing oil, and raw meat, so it belongs at a site you drove to.

The green tomato is the reason the burger works. Unripe tomatoes are firm and sour, so they survive the pan instead of collapsing, and the acid cuts through a pound of bacon and eight slices of cheese. Mixing the seasoned flour at home means one bag at camp instead of five spice jars in the dirt. Fry the tomatoes before the patties: the burgers hold heat under cheese, and cold fried tomatoes are limp.
