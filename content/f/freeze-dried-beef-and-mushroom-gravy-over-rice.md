The trick here is parallel timing. Vegetables start rehydrating while water comes to a boil for the rice and gravy. The whole process runs about 30 minutes from first water to plate, but you are not actively cooking for most of that—just waiting for hydration and steeping to work.

Freeze-dried beef and sun-dried tomatoes stay recognizable when rehydrated. The gravy is the structure. Without it you have rice and vegetables. With it you have a plate with body and depth. Mushroom gravy echoes the rehydrated mushrooms and builds the flavor profile.
