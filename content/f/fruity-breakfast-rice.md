Instant rice is already cooked and dried, so it only needs to reabsorb water. That means the stove runs for the boil and nothing more, and the fifteen-minute wait is done off the flame. Cheap on fuel, and the pot never scorches.

The butter powder and dry milk are what turn it from wet rice into something with body. Both go in dry with everything else, so there is nothing perishable and nothing to measure at camp. Stir a second time at the end. The starch and the milk solids need it to come together.
