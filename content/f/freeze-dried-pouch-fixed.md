Freeze-dried entrees are engineered around shelf life and rehydration speed, not fat or salt balance, so most land somewhere between thin and flat. The fixes are small and cheap. Oil adds the calories the pouch is missing and carries the flavors that are already in there. Hard cheese adds salt, fat, and glutamate at once, which is most of what "tastes like food" means.

The dried onion and garlic go in before the water on purpose. They need the full soak time to soften, and dropped in at the end they stay crunchy and raw-tasting. Keeping the whole thing in the pouch means no pot to scrub, which matters more on night four than it does on night one.
