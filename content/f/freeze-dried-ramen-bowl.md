Freeze-dried chicken and vegetables transform ramen from a snack into actual dinner. The chicken adds protein, and the vegetables give the broth body instead of just heat. Everything rehydrates at once: pour cool water over the freeze-dried bag, wait 10 minutes, then boil the pot. No separate cooking steps, no refrigeration needed.

Ramen packets are salty by design. If you don't drink all the broth, sodium stays reasonable. Most people keep just enough liquid to warm the noodles and eat the solids. It's shelf-stable, travels light, and cleans up in one pot.
