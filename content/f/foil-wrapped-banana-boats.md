The peel does the work here. It holds shape, keeps the fruit off the metal, and steams the banana from the inside while the fillings melt. Nothing to wash after, which is the actual argument for cooking dessert in fruit.

Firm bananas matter more than the chocolate does. A ripe one turns to liquid and leaks out of the slit before the marshmallows are anywhere near melted. Coals, not flame — foil over open fire burns through and the sugar scorches. This is car-camping food most of the time, but bananas will survive day one in a pack if you keep them on top.
