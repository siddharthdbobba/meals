The original grilled fresh fillets and blended a sauce in a food processor, neither of which happens away from a kitchen. Pouch salmon is already cooked, already flaked, and keeps at ambient temperature for years, so the fish stops being the hard part. The sauce is the whole recipe anyway.

Dried dill and chives need liquid and a few minutes to taste like anything, which is why the sauce gets mixed first and the sandwich gets built last. Sour cream powder plus a little water replaces the fresh stuff and thins the mayo enough to spread in cold weather. Capers go in last and stay whole: they are the only sharp thing in a sandwich that is otherwise fat and fish.
