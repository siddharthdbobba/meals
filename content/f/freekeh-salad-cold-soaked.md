Freekeh is green wheat, roasted, so it already tastes a little burnt in a good way. That smoke is the whole reason to carry it instead of couscous. The cost is time: cracked freekeh needs a couple of hours cold, which means you start it at the morning break and eat it at the pass.

The original is a fridge salad built on cucumber, tomato, and two lemons. None of that travels. Dried vegetables rehydrate in the same water as the grain, and lemon powder does the acid work without the weight or the leak risk. Oil goes in after the soak, not before, because fat coating dry grain slows the water down. Feta is the one perishable left, and a wedge of the hard brined kind holds a couple of days out of a cooler if it stays sealed.
