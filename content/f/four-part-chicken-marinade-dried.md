The source is a home marinade formula: fat, acid, salt, flavor, four parts that do four different jobs. Fat carries the flavor compounds, acid loosens the surface of the meat, salt opens a path for both, and the last part is whatever you feel like. That structure survives the trip out. The ingredients do not.

So everything wet became a powder or a packet. Lemon juice is citric acid, soy sauce is soy sauce powder, fresh garlic is garlic powder. Raw chicken is freeze-dried chicken, which is already cooked, so this is not really a marinade anymore — it is a sauce applied after rehydrating. The five minutes under the lid at the end is the only soak time you get. Take it.
