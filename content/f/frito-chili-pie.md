The chips are structure, not garnish. Corn fries up rigid enough to hold its shape under hot chili for a few minutes, which is what separates this from soup with crumbs in it. Eat it before the bottom layer gives up.

The cheese does the fat work. Chili packets are lean and salty by design, so an ounce of hard cheese stirred in at the end is most of what makes this a dinner instead of a side. Grate it at home; a cold block will not melt in there.

No dietary tags on this one: the cheese rules out vegan and dairy-free, and chili packet blends vary too much to call it anything else with confidence.
