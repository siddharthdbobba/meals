Freeze-dried meals are honest backpacking food. Everything rehydrates in boiling water while you're sitting around, and there's no game to play about portion sizes. The chicken carries protein, and the pasta carries calories efficiently. Parmesan is your fat and flavor—don't skip it.

The 15-minute sit is not negotiable. The pasta needs that time to absorb water and soften. In cold weather, wrap the pot in a cozy or sleeping bag to hold heat and speed things up. You'll waste stove fuel trying to rush it.
