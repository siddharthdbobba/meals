The original bakes potatoes in an oven and scoops the flesh out. Coals do the same job, and there is no reason to lose the skins to it, so this version cooks in foil and tears everything in whole. Skins hold the cubes together and carry most of what makes a potato taste like a potato.

Dressing hot potatoes is the whole point. Warm starch takes on oil and acid the way cold potato never will, which is why this needs no mayonnaise and no cooler. Toasting the cumin seeds first matters more than it sounds like it should — raw seeds stay hard and taste like nothing.
