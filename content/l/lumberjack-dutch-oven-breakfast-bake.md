This is a cooler-dependent breakfast, so it belongs at a car campsite or a basecamp and nowhere else. Eggs and cheddar have to stay cold until the moment they go in the pot. The payoff is that one vessel does the work of three pans, and six people get fed at the same time instead of in shifts.

Most of the failures here are moisture failures. Frozen hash browns dumped straight in release water that never cooks off under a lid, and you end up with a wet layer at the bottom. Thaw them and drain them. Cooking the sausage at home does the same job for grease and cuts the camp cook time down to one bake. Coals on the lid matter too — bottom heat alone sets the potatoes long before the eggs in the middle catch up.
