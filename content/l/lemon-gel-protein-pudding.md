The original is a refrigerator dessert. On the trail the fridge is the cooler or the night air, and fresh yogurt is not coming with you, so the yogurt is powdered and the protein comes from whey powder instead of a tub. Everything else survives the change intact.

The cooling step is not optional. Gelatin liquid still above body temperature will curdle milk protein on contact, and you get a grainy, separated set that no amount of stirring fixes. Boil early in the evening, cook dinner while the pot sits, then mix the powders in when the pot is cold to the back of your hand. Sugar-free gelatin sets firmer than sweetened, so the protein powder can go in without loosening it.
