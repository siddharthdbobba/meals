This started as a home stew built on fresh greens, fresh mushrooms, and raw ground lamb. Two of those three do not travel. The fix is dried mushrooms, which rehydrate into the broth and give up more flavor than fresh ones do, and freeze-dried spinach, which needs nothing but residual heat. The meatballs get formed and browned in a kitchen and carried cooked, which removes the messiest step from camp and makes the pot a matter of boiling and waiting.

The dried favas are the point. They dissolve as they cook and turn thin broth into something with body, which is why the simmer is long and why you should not stir them gently. Buy them split and peeled. Whole ones keep their skins, and picking bean skins out of your teeth by headlamp is a bad end to a good dinner.
