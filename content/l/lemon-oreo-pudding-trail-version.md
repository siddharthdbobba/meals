The original is a refrigerator pie: crust, Cool Whip, two hours of chill. None of that travels. What does travel is the chemistry underneath it. Instant pudding sets on cold liquid alone, no heat, but it needs the calcium and protein in milk to do it. That is why powdered milk stays in and plain water does not work by itself.

Cool Whip and the crust are gone. The crushed Oreos take over the crust's job, and doubling them makes up the calories the whipped topping was carrying. Skip the twenty-minute wait and you get lemon soup, which is edible but disappointing. Give it the time.
