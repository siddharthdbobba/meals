The original is an oven recipe. Ovens do not come backpacking, so these get browned in the pot instead, and the mini muffin tin that held their shape is replaced by packing them tight and leaving them alone until a crust sets.

Two things keep them from turning to mush. The lentils come off the heat at ten minutes, well short of done, so they hold some structure. The oats then absorb whatever water is left and bind the rest. Dried mushrooms do the same job as fresh at a fraction of the weight, and their soaking water is worth keeping if you want to thin a sauce.
