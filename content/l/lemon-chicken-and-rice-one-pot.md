The original is a baked casserole with a four-hour marinade, which is two things a stove cannot do. The fix is to stop treating the marinade as a marinade. Soy, lemon, ginger, and pepper all rehydrate instantly, so they go in with the boiling water and season the rice from the inside as it swells. The chicken shows up already cooked.

Citric acid is doing the work that lemon juice did. Dried peel smells like lemon but is not sour, and without the acid the whole pot tastes flat and salty. The oil goes in last, off heat, because it is most of the fat and stirring it into hot rice is the only way it stays distributed.
