This is a car-camping breakfast because it depends on a cooler and a real skillet. Both are worth carrying when you sleep next to the truck. The parboiling is the part that matters: raw potato cubes take twenty minutes over uneven coals and will still be hard in the middle. Boiled at home, they only need to brown.

Order of operations does the rest. Shrimp cook in two minutes and turn to rubber in three, so they come out first and go back in last. The sausage renders fat that the potatoes and onion cook in, which means less butter and better browning. Everything happens in one pan, so cleanup is a scrape and a wipe.
