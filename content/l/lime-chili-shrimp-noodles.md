This is a broth dinner, not a thick one, so the calories are lower than a pot of noodles usually implies. What it delivers is protein and salt: two foil packets of shrimp put roughly 24 grams a serving in the pot, and shrimp packets are shelf-stable in a way almost no other seafood is.

Two seasoning packets across three blocks is the whole trick. Ramen seasoning is calibrated for two cups of water each; at four cups total you would be drinking salt. The coconut flakes soften in the simmer and give the broth some body and fat, and the lime and jalapeno go in late so the acid and heat stay bright instead of cooking flat.
