This is built around a simple idea from the source: get the protein from lean meat, not from the fat riding along with it. Freeze-dried chicken breast is close to pure protein by weight, which is exactly what you want when every ounce is on your back. Two ounces dry is roughly 40 g of protein and almost no fat, so the calories you do carry can go into carbs that actually move you up a hill.

The beans and rice do the rest. Instant refried beans thicken on their own without a simmer, so the stove only runs long enough to rehydrate the chicken and cook the rice. Fat here is a dial, not a fixed ingredient — leave the oil out on a warm day and it is a lean 400-calorie dinner, add it on a cold one and it is a real meal. No dairy, no fridge, one pot.
