The original is a pressure cooker dump recipe built on frozen hash browns, canned soup, and a block of cream cheese. None of that travels. Potato flakes and powdered whole milk do the same two jobs — starch and fat — at a fraction of the weight, and freeze-dried mushrooms cover what the cream of mushroom can was there for.

Add the flakes slowly. All at once and you get mashed potatoes with cheese in them, which is fine but is not soup, and you cannot walk it back without more water and more fuel. The cheese goes in off the heat: direct flame breaks it and you end up with grease on top.
