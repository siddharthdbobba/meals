The original is an Instant Pot recipe built on fresh cauliflower, block cream cheese, and an immersion blender. None of that travels. Freeze-dried cauliflower rehydrates to roughly the same texture, powdered cheese and powdered cream cheese carry the dairy without a cooler, and a spoon pressed against the pot wall does what the blender did — coarser, but the soup still reads as soup.

Fat is what keeps this from being hot vegetable water. Cauliflower has almost no calories of its own, so the oil and the cheese powder are doing all the work. Add the powders off the boil and stir them in gradually; dumped in hot they clump and you will be chasing lumps with a titanium spoon.
