Instant oatmeal on its own is thin and gone in twenty minutes. The four additions fix that separately: milk powder gives it body and a little protein, dates bring dense sugar that dissolves into the mash, almonds add fat and something to chew, cranberries cut the sweetness.

The five minutes in a cozy matter more than they look. Instant oats hydrate fast but the dates and cranberries do not, and pulling the bag early leaves you with hard fruit in soft porridge. Off-the-boil water is deliberate too, since full-boil water against thin freezer bag plastic is how bags fail in your lap.
