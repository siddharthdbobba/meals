This started as a pressure cooker recipe, which is not a thing you carry. The move is to replace the two cans of tomatoes with tomato powder and sun-dried pieces — canned tomato is mostly water, and water is at the trailhead. Boiling the pasta directly in the tomato broth means the starch stays in the pot and thickens it, which is the same reason the original works.

The cheese is doing two jobs: salt and fat. Mozzarella does not travel, so it is gone; hard grated parmesan is shelf-stable for weeks and melts into the broth without turning it into strings. The oil goes in at the end because boiled oil just floats. Off the heat and stirred, it binds to the starch.
