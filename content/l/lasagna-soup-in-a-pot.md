Lasagna is a casserole problem: layers, a pan, an oven, and a fridge full of ricotta. The soup version drops all of that and keeps the parts that actually carry the flavor — tomato, garlic, basil, and melted cheese. Broken noodles cook loose in the broth in about the same time the vegetables need, so it stays one pot and one boil.

The home recipe leans on two cans of tomato and fresh peppers. Tomato powder replaces both cans at a fraction of the weight, and dried peppers and onion rehydrate in the first five minutes of simmering. Fresh basil and shredded mozzarella are the two things that do not travel, so use dried basil and a hard low-moisture cheese instead. The canned parmesan is doing salt and body work here, not garnish.
