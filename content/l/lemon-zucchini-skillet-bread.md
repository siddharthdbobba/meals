This started as an oven loaf and a heap of fresh shredded zucchini, which is a poor thing to carry. Dehydrated zucchini rehydrates to nearly the same texture and weighs almost nothing, and it lets you control the water instead of hoping a wet squash gets it right. Powdered milk and powdered eggs replace the two things that would otherwise need a cooler.

A Dutch oven over coals runs hotter on the bottom than a home oven does, so most of the heat belongs on the lid. Rotating both oven and lid is the only real technique here. Skip it and you get one burnt edge and one raw one.
