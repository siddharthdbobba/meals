A borosilicate teapot with a steel infuser basket is a nice thing to own at home and a bad thing to carry. The method transfers, though. A cook pot holds the same 1200 ml, and the leaf can float loose as long as you strain it on the way out.

Steep time and water temperature are the only variables that matter. Under-boiled water leaves green tea thin; boiling water on delicate leaf turns it bitter within a minute. Letting the pot rest a minute after the boil splits the difference without a thermometer.
