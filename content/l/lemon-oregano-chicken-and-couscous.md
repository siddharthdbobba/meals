The original is a slow cooker recipe: raw breasts poached for ninety minutes in broth, butter, and lemon. None of that survives a trail kitchen. Freeze-dried chicken does the same job at a fraction of the weight, and it comes pre-shredded, which is what the slow cooker was working toward anyway.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Couscous grabs water fast and will leave the chicken chalky if both go in at once, so the meat soaks first. Lemon goes in off the heat because acid dulls when it boils, and butter powder is the only fat here — leave it out and the bowl reads like salty broth on grain.
