Lasagna is a layering problem, and layering needs an oven you do not have. Take the same ingredients, boil them together, and you land in the same place with one pot and one burner. The noodles cook directly in the broth, which means the starch they shed thickens the soup instead of going down a strainer.

The cheese is the part that has to change on the trail. Ricotta is a fridge item and a poor traveler, so this drops it and leans on grated parmesan stirred through plus cubed low-moisture mozzarella melted on top. Dried onion and spinach save cooler space and rehydrate fine in a pot that is already wet. The meat still needs a cooler for day one or two — if you are past that, use the pepperoni swap.
