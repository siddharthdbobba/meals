The original is a bowl of Greek yogurt with lime gelatin powder stirred in, which means it needs a fridge and a cooler and a spoon that stays clean. Powdered yogurt fixes all three. It rehydrates to about the right thickness at roughly one part powder to three-quarters water, and gelatin powder is already dry, so the whole dip becomes two bags and a squeeze bottle of water.

Add the water slowly. Dumping it in at once gives you lumps that never break up, because the powder skins over before it wets through. The rest period matters as much as the mixing does — the dip that looks too thin at minute one is usually right at minute ten. Fresh fruit still needs a cooler, so this is a car-camping and day-hike snack, not a resupply-to-resupply one.
