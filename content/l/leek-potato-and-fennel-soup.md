The original is a blended soup: sweat the alliums, simmer potato and fennel in stock, puree it smooth. You cannot blend anything on trail, so the potato does that job as flakes. They dissolve into the broth and give you the plush texture the blender was there to produce.

The rest is substitution. Dried leek, fennel, and onion rehydrate in the same simmer that cooks the broth, and olive oil replaces the butter that was slathered on bread alongside. The acid at the end is not optional. Without it this reads as bland starch; with it, the onion and fennel flavors pull into focus.
