The original is a Dutch oven stew with fresh leeks, fresh wild mushrooms, and five cups of homemade stock. None of that travels. Dried mushrooms and leek flakes do the same job at a tenth of the weight, and they rehydrate in the same water that cooks the grain, so nothing gets poured off.

Buckwheat is the reason this holds up as dinner. It takes about 15 minutes of low simmer, roughly the same as the vegetables need, and it thickens the pot into something you eat with a spoon rather than drink. Give it the off-heat rest before you judge the texture. The oil goes in at the end because fat added early just coats the grain and slows the water down.
