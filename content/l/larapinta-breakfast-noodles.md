Vegetables are the part that fails on trail. Anything thick or hard stays crunchy no matter how long it sits, so the carrot gets grated and blanched before it goes in the dehydrator, and the broccoli gets broken down small. Surface area is the whole game here.

Freeze-dried tofu is doing the protein work — about 20 g without carrying anything wet. The vegetables and tofu go in before the noodles because they need the longer soak; adding everything at once gives you mushy noodles and hard carrot.
