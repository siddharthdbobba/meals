This is a resupply recipe, not a planned one. Every piece of it sits on a shelf in a small-town grocery or a decent gas station, which is why it shows up so often on long trails. Nothing here needs a cooler or a second pot.

Potato flakes rehydrate off the heat, so the stove work is one boil. The bacon and cheese are the calories: the potatoes alone are mostly starch and water weight, and the fat is what turns them into a meal that holds until morning. Cheese melts better if you kill the heat first and let the lid do the work, since direct flame on a pot of potatoes scorches the bottom fast.

The whole package is around a thousand calories. Listed here as two servings, but on a big mileage day it is one.
