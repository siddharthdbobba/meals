Lentils and pearl barley both finish in about forty minutes, which is the whole reason this works as one pot. Nothing gets added at staggered times and nothing turns to mush waiting on something else. Red and yellow lentils break down and would leave you with a thick sludge, so stick with brown or green.

The original is a home soup with fresh onion, carrot, spinach, and lime. Dried versions of all four rehydrate in the simmer and weigh almost nothing. The two things that matter at the end are the oil and the acid: fat carries the cumin and paprika, and the lime cuts through what is otherwise forty minutes of earthy starch. Add both off the heat, not during the boil, or you lose them.

Forty minutes is real fuel. On a short trip it is worth it. On a long one, carry the instant-rice version.
