Perogies are potato, cheese, onion, and dough. This pot is the same four things with the folding step deleted, which is the only part that needs a kitchen. Broken lasagna sheets give you wide, chewy pieces of dough that read as wrapper rather than as pasta, and the potato binds around them instead of sitting next to them.

The original called for butter and fresh milk. Oil or ghee and powdered milk do the same job without a cooler, and the milk powder goes into the cold water before it heats so it dissolves instead of clumping. A vacuum-sealed block of cheddar holds a few days unrefrigerated if it stays out of direct sun, which is what makes this work on a canoe trip. Two pots is faster, but one works if you drain the noodles into a mug and reuse the pot.
