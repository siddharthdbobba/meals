The source page here was a gas heater, not a recipe, so this is the drink that goes with sitting next to one. Cold-weather camp cocoa fails in two predictable ways: it comes out watery, or it comes out lumpy. The fix for the first is whole milk powder and a fat, which give the drink body that cocoa and sugar alone cannot. The fix for the second is making a paste before you add the full volume of water.

The instant coffee and the salt are doing quiet work. Neither is detectable on its own, but cocoa without them reads flat and one-note, especially when the mix has been sitting in a pack for a week. Mix everything dry at home so camp is one bag and one pour.
