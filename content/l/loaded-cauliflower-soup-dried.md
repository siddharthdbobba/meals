The original is a pressure cooker soup finished with cream cheese and an immersion blender. Neither travels. The trail version dries the cauliflower at home, rehydrates it in bouillon, and gets its body from a spoon instead of a blade — cauliflower that has been boiled soft mashes into a purée with almost no effort.

Cheese powder and milk powder replace the cheddar and cream cheese. They are the whole texture of the dish, so add them off the heat: dairy powder dropped into a rolling boil clumps, and clumps do not dissolve later. The oil is not optional either. Cauliflower is close to free of calories, and without added fat this is a hot drink, not a dinner.
