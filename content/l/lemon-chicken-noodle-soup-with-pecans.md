The pecans are the reason this is not just ramen with bouillon in it. Chopped fine, they release fat and starch into the broth and thicken it within a minute or two. Watch the liquid level drop after you stir them in. That is also why the noodles get a short cook and a covered rest instead of a long boil: the pecans are going to take up some of the water anyway.

Canned chicken carries wet, so this meal weighs about nine ounces packed. That is a lot for one dinner, and the freeze-dried swap in the variations cuts it hard if you are counting. The lemon powder is a small amount doing real work, cutting the salt and the fat so the whole thing does not sit heavy.
