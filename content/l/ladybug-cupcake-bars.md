This started as a from-scratch cupcake with fresh buttermilk, a stack of eggs, and a Swiss meringue buttercream built over a double boiler. None of that survives a weekend outdoors. Buttermilk powder and powdered egg do the same job dry, shortening replaces the butter because it does not care about temperature, and the meringue frosting becomes a plain powdered-sugar buttercream that will not break when the sun hits it.

Baking it flat in a dutch oven instead of in liners is the other change. Thirty-six cupcakes means six batches over coals; one pan of bars means one. The rotation matters more than the timer — a dutch oven cooks hottest where the coals sit, and a quarter turn every ten minutes is what keeps one edge from turning to carbon. Note the marzipan: almond paste, so this is not nut-free.
