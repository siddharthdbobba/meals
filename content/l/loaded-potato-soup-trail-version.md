The original is a pressure-cooker soup built on whole potatoes, chicken broth, sour cream, and grated cheese. None of that travels. Potato flakes are already cooked and dried potato, so they do the same job in five minutes instead of thirty, and the powdered dairy carries the tang and fat without a cooler.

The ratio is the whole game. Too many flakes and you have mashed potatoes; add them slowly and stop early, because they keep thickening for a minute after you quit stirring. Sour cream powder is what keeps it from tasting like flat cheese sauce, and the bacon goes on at the end so it stays chewy instead of dissolving.
