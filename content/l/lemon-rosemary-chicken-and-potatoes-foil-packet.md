A sheet pan dinner is really just a single hot surface and two things that finish at the same time. Foil over coals does the same job with less gear. The potatoes sit underneath and take on the fat and juice coming off the thighs, which is the whole point of the original and the part worth protecting.

This one stays a cooler recipe. Raw chicken has no shelf-stable substitute worth eating, so it belongs on a car-camping or short paddle trip, cooked the first night out. Seal the packets with a little slack inside: the steam trapped there cooks the potatoes through before the skin burns. If you want that skin browned, open the packet at the end and let the fire finish it.
