Fresh lemon and a jar of mayo are the two things this dish normally leans on, and neither belongs in a pack. Citric acid powder gives the same sharp edge at a fraction of an ounce, and single-serve mayo packets stay stable for months in the heat. Everything else was already shelf-stable.

Mixing inside the pouch is the point. There is no bowl to wash, no fish smell in your cook pot, and the empty pouch seals back up to carry out. Keep the crackers away from the wet part until the moment you eat, because they go soft in about a minute.
