The original is a stovetop pot of fresh peppers, celery, and two cans of beans. Cans and fresh produce are the two heaviest things you can put in a pack, so both are gone. Dried mirepoix and tomato powder carry the same flavor at a fraction of the weight, and instant refried bean flakes do the job of a can of black beans while also thickening the pot at the end.

Quinoa is the reason this works on trail. It cooks in twenty minutes with no soak, it holds its texture in a wet pot, and it brings enough protein that the beans are not doing all the work. Rinse it at home and dry it on a towel; the saponin coating on unrinsed quinoa is bitter and you cannot fix it once it is cooking. The oil goes in at the end, off the heat, because that is where the calories are and boiling it just wastes them.
