Lasagna soup is already a one-pot dish, so the trail version mostly involves removing the refrigerator. Canned tomatoes become tomato powder and a handful of sun-dried pieces: the powder does the color and acid, the pieces do the chew. Ricotta and fresh mozzarella are out entirely. A hunk of low-moisture mozzarella or provolone melts into the broth the same way and survives two or three days in a pack.

The cook time is the real cost here. Lasagna sheets are thick, and even broken small they want ten minutes at a boil, which is more fuel than most one-pot dinners. Stir often. Starch settles and scorches on a thin titanium pot faster than you would expect, and burnt tomato is not something you can stir back out.
