The original is an Instant Pot soup built on fresh sausage, two cans of tomatoes, six cups of broth, and a scoop of ricotta. None of that travels. Tomato powder replaces the cans and the paste at a fraction of the weight, bouillon replaces the broth, and a dry sausage chub does the job of ground Italian sausage without a cooler. The ricotta is gone — soft cheese and backcountry do not mix — so hard grated parmesan carries the dairy end alone.

Water is the number that matters. Canned tomatoes and six cups of broth are mostly water you are otherwise hauling; here you add it at camp. Keep the simmer low and stir, because pasta cooked in a thick tomato base scorches fast on a stove with one heat setting. Tomato powder needs a minute in hot water before the noodles go in or you will find dry pockets of it later.
