The original is a home soup: fry an onion, simmer real sweet potatoes and leeks in stock, then blend it smooth. Nothing there survives a pack, so all three vegetables come dried and the blender is replaced by sweet potato flakes, which fall apart into the water on their own. The texture lands close enough.

Order matters more than technique. Dried leek and onion are tougher than the potato and need a head start, or you end up with soft soup and chewy green rings in it. The oil goes in off the heat because the home version's whole fat budget was that first tablespoon in the pan, and out here it is also most of the calories.
