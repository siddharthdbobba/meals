Piccata is a pan sauce built on lemon, butter, and brine, and none of those need a slow cooker or a fridge to happen. Butter powder rehydrates into something close enough, citric acid does what two lemons did at a fraction of the weight, and the chicken arrives already cooked in a pouch. The flour dredge and the browning step are gone; they existed to thicken the sauce and crisp raw cutlets, and neither problem exists here.

Couscous replaces the wild rice because it needs no simmer at all, just hot water and a lid. That keeps this to one boil and one pot. The capers are the part people are tempted to leave home, and they are the part that makes it taste like piccata instead of chicken and grain, so bring them.
