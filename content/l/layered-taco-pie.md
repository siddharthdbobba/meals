This is a lasagna with the Italian filed off. Corn tortillas hold their shape under sauce far better than flour ones, which turn to paste, so the layers stay readable after twenty minutes of covered heat. Two tortillas per layer is not a shingle job — they overlap and act as one sheet.

Draining the beef matters more than anything else here. A pound and a half of 80/20 throws off a lot of fat, and with a lid on there is nowhere for it to go. Thinning the taco sauce with tomato puree does the same job: straight taco sauce is thin and sharp, and the puree gives it enough body to sit on a tortilla instead of running to the bottom. Everything except the tortillas needs a cooler, so this is a first-or-second-night meal out of the car.
