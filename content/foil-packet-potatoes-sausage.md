The foil packet solves the group cooking problem: nobody has to agree on anything.
Vegetarians build a packet without sausage, the picky eater leaves out peppers,
and it all cooks in the same fire at the same time.

Cut the potatoes smaller than feels necessary. Undercooked potato is the only way
this fails.
