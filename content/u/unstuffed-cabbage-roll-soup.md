This is a stuffed cabbage roll that gave up on rolling. The filling and the wrapper go in the same pot, and the rice cooks in the tomato broth instead of being parboiled and stuffed. That trades an hour of assembly for one pot, which is the whole reason it belongs at a campsite.

Two changes from a kitchen version. Canned broth is heavy and mostly water, so bouillon plus water from the jug does the same job at a fraction of the weight. The rice goes in before the cabbage because it needs twice as long; reverse that order and you get mush wrapped around crunchy grains. Coals over flame matters too, since rice on the bottom of a hot pot scorches fast.
