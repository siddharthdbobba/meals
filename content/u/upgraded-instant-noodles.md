Instant noodles are cheap, light, and cook in three minutes, but on their own they are refined wheat and salt. That is a blood sugar spike, not dinner. You will be hungry again in an hour, which is the wrong outcome after a long day.

The fix is to treat the block as a starch base and build on it. Fat from the oil roughly doubles the calories for a third of an ounce of weight. A foil protein packet takes it from around 10 g of protein to 30. Dried vegetables add bulk and fiber, and they cost nothing to rehydrate if you start them in the cold water. Ditch the flavor sachet, which is mostly salt, and season with miso or soy instead.
