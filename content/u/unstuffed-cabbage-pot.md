The original is a slow cooker dish: raw meat, a whole head of cabbage, and hours of low heat. None of that travels. The rewrite keeps the parts that matter — cabbage, tomato, rice, paprika — and replaces the fresh ones with dried, so it cooks in twenty minutes instead of six.

Order is the whole trick. Dried cabbage takes the longest to rehydrate, instant rice the least, so the vegetables go in first and the rice goes in last with the heat already off. Reverse it and you get mush wrapped around cabbage that still crunches. Tomato powder plus sun-dried tomatoes does the work of a jar of sauce at a fraction of the weight.
