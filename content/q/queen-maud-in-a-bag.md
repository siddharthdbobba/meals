The original is a Norwegian party dessert: egg yolks whipped with sugar, gelatin bloomed and melted in, whipped cream folded through, then layered with grated chocolate and left in the fridge for four hours. None of that survives a trailhead. Raw yolks, sheet gelatin, and heavy cream all need a refrigerator, and four hours of setting time needs one too.

Instant pudding mix does the same structural job with none of the logistics. Its starch thickens in cold water in about ten minutes, which is what replaces both the gelatin and the chill. Powdered coconut milk brings back the fat the cream was carrying, and it whisks into cold water without lumping the way milk powder does. The chocolate and raspberries go in last and stay in distinct layers, which is closer to how the original eats than stirring everything smooth would be. The port is a tablespoon per person and it is the only part that tastes like the source.
