This is a ratio, not a recipe. Two thirds of the weight is chocolate and peanut butter chips, which is where the calories live, and the rest is there for texture and to keep it from being straight candy. The chow mein noodles are the odd ingredient and the reason it works: they are fried, salty, and shatter instead of chewing, so the whole handful stops tasting like dessert halfway through.

Nothing here needs cooking or cold, which is the point on a river trip where the cooler is full of dinner. The only real enemy is heat. The candy shell buys you a lot of margin, but a bag left on a hot rock still fuses. Stow it in the dry bag, not on the deck.
