Most trail dinners get their protein from a foil packet of meat. This one gets it from the pot itself. Quinoa and red lentils each bring something the other lacks in the amino acid column, and together they land near 28 grams without anything refrigerated. Sunflower seeds add another few grams plus the fat that makes it eat like a meal.

The red lentils are load-bearing. They break down completely in about the same window the quinoa needs, which thickens the whole pot into something that holds a spoon instead of a bowl of loose grain in broth. Brown or green lentils will not do this in fifteen minutes, and you will burn the fuel finding that out. Keep the flame low and stir; quinoa scorches fast on a thin titanium bottom.
