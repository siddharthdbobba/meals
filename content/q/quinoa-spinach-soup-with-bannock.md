Quinoa is the reason this works. It carries real protein, it cooks faster than any other whole grain, and pre-cooking then dehydrating it at home cuts the camp simmer to under ten minutes of fuel. Dried spinach and peppers rehydrate in the same window, so the whole soup is one pot and one boil.

The calories come from the coconut oil and the whole milk powder, not the vegetables. Skim milk powder weighs the same and gives you less, so use whole. The bannock is deliberately oversized: it costs nothing extra to cook while the stove is already lit, and cold flatbread the next day beats another bar.
