Instant grits expand almost instantly, so you boil the water and chicken first, then kill the heat before adding the dry goods. This avoids the mess of stirring scorched grits. Freeze-dried tomato and cheese rehydrate in minutes from residual heat, while the chicken broth counts toward your liquid total.

The whole meal is five minutes of waiting and one minute of stirring. Boil water and chicken together, remove from heat, dump in the dry mix, cover and walk away. In cold weather or high altitude, wrap the pot in a cozy to keep it warm while it sits. If you can't find freeze-dried mozzarella, use Kraft Parmesan powder instead—it travels well and keeps longer anyway.
