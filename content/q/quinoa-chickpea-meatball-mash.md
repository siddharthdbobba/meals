The original is a freezer project: cook quinoa, grind walnuts and chickpeas, bind with eggs and flour, roll, bake, freeze. None of that survives a trail kitchen. Eggs, a food processor, and an oven are all out, so the meatball becomes the mash. Every ingredient that was doing structural work in the ball is doing flavor work here instead.

Chickpea flour replaces the can and thickens the pot at the same time. Quinoa flakes rehydrate in minutes where whole quinoa wants fifteen and a covered simmer. Tomato powder covers both the paste in the binder and the sauce that was supposed to come from a separate jar. The walnuts are the only real fat besides the oil, so keep them.
