Making instant pasta at home cuts weight by a third. A pound of raw noodles becomes 14 ounces after cooking and drying. On trail, you rehydrate in minutes with minimal water—no starchy overflow and no giant pot needed. The key is thorough rinsing before drying to prevent clumping and ensure even rehydration.

Don't overcook; one minute of boiling and residual heat do the work. Butter and cheese aren't optional—they're your calories. Without them you have a warm, salty snack rather than a trail dinner.
