Quinoa and lentils are the two legume-and-grain staples that cook fast enough to be worth carrying raw. No soaking, no pre-cooking, no dehydrator time. That leaves the vegetables as the only home prep, and frozen peas and carrots dry with zero effort because they were blanched before they were frozen.

The wrap is the fuel-saving part. Both ingredients finish on residual heat if you insulate the pot, so you pay for one boil instead of twenty minutes of simmer. Water volume is forgiving here: too much and you have soup, too little and you add more mid-cook. Neither ruins it.
