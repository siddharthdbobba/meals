Couscous is the reason this works at camp. It asks for equal parts boiling water
and five minutes under a lid, so the whole starch problem is solved while the
skewers are still cooking.

Space the pieces on the skewer. Packed tight, the bacon between them steams
instead of crisping and the middle cubes stay pale.
