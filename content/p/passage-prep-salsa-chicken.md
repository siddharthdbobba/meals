The source version braises seasoned chicken thighs. Thighs need a fridge, so this uses canned chicken instead and moves the work to the spices. Blooming smoked paprika and cumin in oil before anything else is what makes a jar of ordinary salsa taste like it has been on the heat for hours. Skip that step and you have warm salsa on chicken.

The uncovered finish matters more than the simmer. Canned chicken and jarred salsa both carry a lot of water, and if you stop while it is still loose you end up eating soup with a fork. Cook it down until the sauce coats the meat. Make a double batch on a night with fuel to spare; it is the same pot and the same twenty-five minutes, and cold leftovers go into tortillas without any cooking at all.
