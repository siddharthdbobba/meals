Store-bought freeze-dried dinners are light and expensive and taste like a compromise. Drying your own vegetables costs almost nothing and lets you decide how much salt goes in. Water is the only thing that lets bacteria and mold work, so vegetables dried until they are genuinely brittle keep for months in a sealed jar.

The method matters more than the ingredient list. Rehydrating the carrots in the pot water before it boils means they finish at the same time as the pasta, and cooking the pasta in the water you plan to eat keeps the starch — that starch plus the oil and cheese is what makes this a sauce rather than wet noodles. Tuna goes in off the heat because boiling it just makes it stringy.
