The original is a fridge-stocked lunchbox: celery, fresh apple, berries, yogurt dip. None of that walks. Celery goes limp and weighs almost nothing in calories, fresh fruit bruises, and yogurt is a cooler item. What survives the translation is the structure — a fat source, a starch to carry it, something sweet, and something salty — so the swaps are dried fruit for fresh, powdered milk for yogurt, and a wax-wrapped cheese round that tolerates a warm pack for a day.

Peanut butter is doing the caloric work at roughly 100 calories a tablespoon, and it is the reason this holds up as a lunch rather than a snack. Pre-building the cracker sandwiches at home matters more than it sounds: spreading peanut butter on a trail with one hand and a wind-blown wrapper in the other is how crackers become crumbs.
