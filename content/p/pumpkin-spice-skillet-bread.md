Quick breads are the easiest thing to bake over a fire because there is no rising to wait on. The soda and powder do their work the moment the wet and dry meet, so the clock starts when you stir. Get the coals ready first, then mix.

Heat placement is the whole game. Most of the coals go on the lid, not underneath, or the bottom scorches while the middle stays raw. The pumpkin puree carries a lot of water, which keeps the crumb from drying out over a long bake, and it doubles as most of the fat's counterweight. Honey and syrup brown faster than sugar does, so pull it as soon as the center sets.
