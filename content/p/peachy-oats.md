Butter powder and dry milk give this breakfast staying power. They deliver calories and fat without spoiling or needing refrigeration, and they thicken the oatmeal from watery porridge into something substantial. Without them, you'd have oats floating in sweetened peach juice.

This recipe was written for boondocking with canned peaches, but it travels to the trail just fine. Use dried peaches instead and add them to the boiling water first—they'll rehydrate and sweeten the whole pot. The pumpkin spice is just there to make breakfast feel intentional.
