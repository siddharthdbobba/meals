The original bakes this under a sheet of crescent dough, which needs an oven and refrigerated tube dough. Neither travels. The filling was always the good part, so it stays and the lid becomes melted cheese instead of pastry.

Dicing the peppers and onion large matters more than it sounds. Small pieces turn to water in a hot skillet and thin out the gravy; big pieces hold shape and keep some bite. The gravy powder is doing double duty as salt and thickener, so add the water first and let it reduce rather than dumping in extra.
