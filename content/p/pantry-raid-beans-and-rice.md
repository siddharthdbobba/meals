This started as a use-up-the-cupboard dinner at home, which makes it an easy one to move onto the trail. Nothing in it was fresh to begin with. The swaps are small: instant rice instead of dry long-grain so it cooks off residual heat, bean flakes instead of canned, tomato powder instead of a jar of sauce.

The bean flakes do the structural work. Rice alone drinks the water and leaves you with a thin bowl; the flakes bind it into something you can eat with a spoon and they carry the oil, which is where most of the calories live. Add them slowly. Dumping them in at once gives you a lump in the middle and soup around the edge.
