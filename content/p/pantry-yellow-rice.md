Yellow rice is a pantry dish by nature. Everything in it already lives on a shelf, which means the trail version needs almost no substitution: instant rice for regular, bouillon for stock, dried onion for fresh, TVP for the meat. Nothing here needs a fridge or a second pot.

The ten-minute lid-on soak is the whole method. Instant rice is already cooked and dried, so it only needs hot water and time, not a simmer — you spend one boil of fuel instead of fifteen minutes of it. The oil goes in at the end because fat coats the grains and stops them clumping into a brick, and because pouring it over heat wastes calories to the pot walls.
