This is a night-one meal and only a night-one meal. The cooking all happens in your kitchen, where you have a bowl, a pan, and time to roll a kilo of mince. What you carry is a frozen brick that thaws through the drive and needs nothing but a stove and twenty minutes.

Freezing the bag flat matters twice over. It keeps the cooler cold on the way in, and it thaws at an even rate instead of leaving a frozen core at dinner. Parmesan is the one dairy that earns its place here: a hard wedge holds for days unrefrigerated as long as it can breathe through paper. Couscous is the partner because it cooks off residual heat, so the second pot boils once and then you leave it alone.
