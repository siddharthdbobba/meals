The original is a slow cooker recipe with a pound of raw beef, canned pumpkin, and sour cream on top. None of that travels. Freeze-dried beef crumbles, pumpkin powder, and tomato powder do the same jobs at a fraction of the weight, and the pasta cooks in the same pot that rehydrates everything else.

Pumpkin powder is the thickener here, not just a flavor. Add it at the end and off heat, because it grabs water fast and will turn the pot to paste if it goes in early. Cinnamon in a chili sounds wrong and is not. A quarter teaspoon rounds off the chili powder without reading as sweet.
