This started as an oven recipe for a mini cake pan, which is a thing nobody carries. A 10-inch dutch oven bakes the same batter in one round, and the coal count matters more than the clock: most of the heat goes on the lid, or the bottom burns before the middle sets.

The substitutions are the usual camp ones. Eggs and milk become powder and water. Butter becomes ghee, which keeps at ambient temperature for weeks. Canned pumpkin is the one heavy item and it stays, because it is what keeps the crumb moist; the self-rising cornmeal already carries the leavening, so nothing else has to go in the pot.
