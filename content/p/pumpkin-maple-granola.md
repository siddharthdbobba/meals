Granola is one of the few backpacking foods where all the work happens at home and none of it happens at camp. The pumpkin puree is the reason the batch clumps: it is mostly water, and as that water leaves in the oven it glues the oats and syrup together into clusters. That same water is why the bake takes 25 to 40 minutes and why you stir it every 10. Underbake and you carry a bag of soft, damp cereal that will mold on day three.

The pepitas and almonds are doing the calorie work. Oats alone run thin for the weight, and this mix packs roughly a third of its calories as fat, which is what you want in something you eat by the handful. Cranberries go in after cooling because dried fruit in a 300 F oven turns into small hard rocks.
