This started as a cheap indoor dinner built in a toaster oven, which is not a thing that exists in camp. The fix is foil and a grate. Direct radiant heat from an oven melts cheese from above; over coals you only have heat from below, so the foil tent traps enough of it to finish the top while the bottom crisps.

Drain the mushrooms hard. Canned mushrooms carry a lot of liquid and pita has no structure to spare. Everything here except the cheese is shelf-stable, so if you are out longer than the cooler lasts, drop the mozzarella and lean on oil and salami instead.
