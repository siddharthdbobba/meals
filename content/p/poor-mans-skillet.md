The original is a skillet of leftovers: ground beef, diced potatoes, corn, whatever cheese was in the door of the fridge. None of that travels, so all four became their dried counterparts. The rehydrate-then-fry order matters. Soak everything first, then put it back on heat with oil, or you get a pot of wet starch instead of something with browned edges.

The oil is not optional. Freeze-dried beef carries almost no fat, and without it the potatoes stay pale and the whole thing tastes like water and salt. Cheese is listed as optional because it is the first thing to go bad and the first thing worth dropping if you are counting ounces.
