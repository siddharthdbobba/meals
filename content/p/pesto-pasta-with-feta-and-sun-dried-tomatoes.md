The original is a garden dish: fresh basil pesto, cherry tomatoes off the vine, feta from the fridge. Two of those three do not travel. Cherry tomatoes turn to pulp in a pack, so dry-packed sun-dried tomatoes go in instead and rehydrate in the pasta water on the way to done. That saves a step and gives the water some flavor.

Feta is the one dairy that earns its place out here. Sealed brine or vacuum pouches sit at ambient temperature for weeks, and the cheese is salty enough that a small amount reads as a lot. Pesto from a tube handles the fat and the seasoning at once. Drain the pasta rather than cooking it dry, or the pesto has nothing to hold onto.
