The original is a fridge salad: crisp cucumber, raw onion, feta, fresh lemon. None of that travels. What survives is the structure — chewy pearls, something acidic, something salty, and enough oil to hold it together. Sun-dried tomatoes and dried peppers rehydrate in the cooking water, citric acid does the work of the lemon, and hard parmesan replaces the feta without needing a cooler.

The rest matters. Pearl couscous straight off the boil is gummy and refuses to take on dressing, so give it ten minutes to firm up before the oil goes in. The sugar is not for sweetness — it rounds off the acid the same way the honey did.
