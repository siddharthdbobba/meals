This is a foraged breakfast, which means the real recipe is the prep. Pokeweed is toxic raw, and the toxins concentrate in the skin and roots. Three separate boils in three separate changes of water is the traditional fix and it works, but only on young plants that have not flowered yet. If you are not certain what you picked, do not cook it.

Everything after the boiling is simple. Bacon fat is the seasoning and the cooking medium both, since boiled greens come out bland and waterlogged by design. Squeezing them dry matters more than it sounds like it should: water in the pan means splattering fat and steamed greens instead of fried ones. The eggs cook on their own half of the skillet so they set properly rather than turning the whole thing into a wet scramble. Hot pepper vinegar at the end is what most people who grew up on this remember about it.
