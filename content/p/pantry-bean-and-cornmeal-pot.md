This is a bucket dinner. Every dry item in it is something people already move out of grocery packaging and into sealed containers for the long haul, which means it keeps for years and you can build the bag months before the trip. Nothing here needs a fridge or a second pot.

The cornmeal is what makes it a meal instead of soup. Bean flakes rehydrate thin and rice alone stays loose, but two tablespoons of cornmeal pull the whole pot together and hold the oil in suspension. Add it slowly off the heat or you get lumps that never soften.
