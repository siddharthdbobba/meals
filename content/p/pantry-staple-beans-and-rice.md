This is what a stocked pantry looks like after it has been packed into a bag. The base is two dry starches that rehydrate off residual heat, so the stove runs for one boil and then stops. Beans and rice together also cover the amino acids that neither one covers alone, which matters more on day four than day one.

The spice mix is the whole recipe. Dehydrated beans and instant rice are bland by design, and salt, cumin, and chili are what keep the bowl from tasting like paste. The acid at the end does the same job a squeeze of lime does at home: it cuts the starch so the last third goes down as easily as the first.
