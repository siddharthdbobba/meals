Pine needle tea costs nothing to make anywhere evergreens grow. Young needles from any temperate pine taste bright and sharp—nothing like the grim survival-manual story of boiling tree bark.

Five minutes of steeping is your practical minimum for real flavor and benefit. Sugar turns it from a medicinal obligation into something you will actually want to drink, but the real advantage is that you gathered it at camp, not carried it.
