The absorption method is the only way to cook rice when you cannot drain it. You start with exactly the right amount of water, apply heat until it boils, then let the rice absorb everything while a lid traps the steam. No burnt bottom, no excess water to deal with.

Rice by itself is calories and nothing else. It is the foundation of a hundred dinners depending on what you add before or after cooking. A can of beans, a foil packet of tuna, some dehydrated vegetables stirred in—rice becomes a complete meal with almost no added weight.
