The original is cream cheese, Cool Whip, and two hours in a refrigerator, none of which exist at camp. Instant cheesecake pudding mix plus whole milk powder does the same job: the mix carries the tang and the setting agent, the milk fat carries the body. It firms up in five minutes at ambient temperature instead of two hours in the cold.

Peanut butter and jam go in after the base thickens. Added early, the fat coats the starch and the whole thing stays soupy. The graham crackers get crushed at home and scattered on top rather than pressed into a crust, because a crust needs a pan and a pan is one more thing to carry.
