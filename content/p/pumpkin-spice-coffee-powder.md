The original is a fridge item: whole milk, heavy cream, and pureed pumpkin whisked together and poured into coffee. None of that travels. This version keeps the same flavors and rebuilds them from powders, so the whole thing weighs under an ounce and a half and does not need a cooler.

The paste step matters more than it sounds. Milk powder hits hot water and seizes into lumps that never dissolve, and you end up chewing your coffee. A little water first, stirred to slurry, and the rest goes in clean. The salt is there to keep the maple from tasting flat.
