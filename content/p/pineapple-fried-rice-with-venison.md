Fried rice at home wants day-old refrigerated rice, which is exactly what you cannot carry. Instant rice does the same job for a different reason: it is already cooked and dried, so the grains stay separate instead of turning to paste when they hit hot oil. Cook it with less water than the box says and you get something close to the dry, loose texture frying needs.

Fresh pineapple is water and weight, so this uses dried, rehydrated alongside the jerky in the same first boil. That step matters more than it looks. Jerky dropped straight into a hot pan stays leather. Ten minutes soaking in near-boiling water turns it back into meat. The four sauces are the whole flavor of the dish and none of them need refrigeration, so premix them at home and carry one bottle.
