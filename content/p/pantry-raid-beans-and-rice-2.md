This started as a pantry-clearing dinner at home, the kind you build from whatever cans are left. The trail version keeps the logic and drops the cans: bean flakes instead of a can of refried beans, dried corn instead of frozen, minced onion instead of a fresh one. Same dish, a fifth of the weight, no fridge involved.

The bean flakes are what makes it work. They rehydrate in under a minute and thicken the pot, so the rice does not sit in loose water. Add them last and slowly, or you get dry pockets. The oil matters too. Beans and rice are cheap calories but lean ones, and a tablespoon of fat is a hundred of them for almost no weight.
