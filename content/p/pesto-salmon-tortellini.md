Dried tortellini is the one filled pasta that survives a pack, and it carries more protein per ounce than plain noodles. The original called for fresh spinach, which does not make it past day one, so the spinach here lives inside the pasta. Milk powder plus the dry sauce mix gives you a cream sauce without carrying anything that needs a fridge.

Cook time is the thing to watch. Use just enough water to cover the pasta so there is nothing to drain, then kill the stove early and let the lid do the rest. Boiling tortellini hard will split the seams and leave you with cheese in the broth.
