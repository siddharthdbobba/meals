This is a home-kitchen preserve, so the trail work happens before you leave. Sugar at the same weight as the fruit plus the lemon juice is the whole preservation system — enough sugar binds the water so nothing grows in it, and the acid keeps the set firm. That is why the ratio is not the place to economize. Cut the sugar and you have a fridge jam, which is no use to you three days out.

Pears have little pectin of their own, so the set comes from evaporation. Cook it until the cold-plate test wrinkles and not a minute less, or you will carry a tube of syrup that leaks past the cap. Squeeze tubes beat jars: no glass, no lid to lose, and you can get the last of it out with a spoon handle.
