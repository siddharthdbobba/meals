The original is a moto-camping recipe built on frozen wontons, which is fine if you have a cooler and a grocery store that morning. Off the freezer, the wontons are the first thing to go. Dried shrimp do the same job: they carry the same brine-and-shellfish flavor, weigh almost nothing, and keep for months in a bag.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Shrimp and bouillon go in first and boil hard, because dried shrimp need a few minutes of real heat to release anything. Noodles and greens go in after, at a simmer, because rice vermicelli turns to paste if you boil it. Rehydrating the greens in the broth instead of separately means the water they soak up is water you already seasoned.
