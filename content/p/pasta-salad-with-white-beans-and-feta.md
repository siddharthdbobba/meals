This started as a kitchen lunch and needed almost nothing to move outdoors: one pot, one boil, no oven, no fresh dairy beyond the feta. Feta is the right cheese here because it comes packed in brine and does not sweat oil the way cheddar does in a hot pack. The beans do the real work, turning a side dish into a lunch that holds you through an afternoon.

Cool the pasta before it meets the dressing. Hot pasta soaks up all the oil and the salad goes dry, and it wilts the arugula on contact. The rinse also washes off surface starch, which matters more with chickpea pasta than wheat. Cook it a minute under package time or you will be eating paste.
