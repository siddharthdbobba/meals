Manufacturers write water amounts for the label, not for altitude, cold, or a pouch that has been sitting in a cold pack all day. Too little water and the starch on the bottom never hydrates. Adding a splash extra and waiting past the stated time costs nothing but five minutes, and it is the difference between a stew and gravel in warm broth.

The rest is arithmetic. A two-serving pouch is about one hiker dinner but is short on both fat and protein, so the meat packet and the oil go in. The onions are there for texture: everything in the pouch has the same soft, uniform bite, and one crunchy thing on top makes the whole bowl read as food.
