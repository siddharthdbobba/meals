The original is canned pumpkin simmered in milk, which is two things you are not carrying. Pumpkin powder is the whole fix: it rehydrates into real puree, weighs almost nothing, and lets the mix live in a single bag with the oats. Powdered whole milk covers the rest.

Fat is what makes this a breakfast rather than a bowl of thin porridge. Butter is the source's trick and it holds up on trail as ghee, which does not need a fridge. Add it off the heat so it stays glossy instead of cooking in. The nuts and syrup go on last, on top, where the texture still means something.
