Freezer bag cooking works when the food has already been cooked once. Everything here is boiled, browned, and simmered at home, then driven dry, so camp water is only putting back what the dehydrator took out. That is why the ten-minute soak in a cozy is enough and no pot ever gets dirty.

Writing the dry weight on each bag is the part people skip. Rehydration is roughly one part water to one part dry food by weight, and guessing gives you either soup or a bag of gravel. The pepperoni goes in before the water because the fat needs heat to bloom; the string cheese and parmesan go on after, since boiling water turns them into rubber.
