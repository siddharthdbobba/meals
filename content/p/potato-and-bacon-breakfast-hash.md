Instant mashed potatoes are the spine here, but what makes this work for winter is the fat. Powdered butter, powdered milk, and cheese powder turn the base into something substantial instead of wallpaper paste. Bacon bits add shelf-stable protein and don't need refrigeration or an actual cook fire.

Green chile cuts through the richness and finishes the dish. On a short winter day when you need to move, this breakfast delivers the calories to sustain you through the morning. The whole thing cooks in one pot in about eight minutes.
