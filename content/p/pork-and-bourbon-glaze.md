This is a trail version of a lodge-grill recipe: the protein is canned instead of fresh, the heat source is a canister stove, and the timing is ten minutes instead of ninety. The sauce—tomato paste, brown sugar, and vinegar—stays the same. Whiskey is optional; the dish works without it, though it deserves to be there if you're carrying it.

Drain the canned pork thoroughly. Wet pork simmered in its own brine is unsatisfying; the sauce needs to coat, not surround. The reduction is fast and the cleanup is one pot. This hits a real need on a long day: protein, calories, and flavor without hours of cooking.
