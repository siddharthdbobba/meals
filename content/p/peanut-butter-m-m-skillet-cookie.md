This is a bakery cookie rebuilt as one pan of shortbread-thick cookie over coals. Twenty individual cookies means minding a hot skillet for an hour, so the dough goes in flat and gets cut into wedges instead. The dry side of the recipe is unchanged: cornstarch and the heavy hand of brown sugar are what keep the middle soft rather than crisp.

The fresh ingredients had to go. Egg and milk become powders that rehydrate with a quarter cup of cold water, which is also all the mixing liquid you need since there is no creaming step to lighten. Vanilla extract is swapped for powder because a leaking extract bottle ruins a food bin. Shortening instead of butter if it is hot out; you lose a little flavor and gain a dough that is not soup at the trailhead.
