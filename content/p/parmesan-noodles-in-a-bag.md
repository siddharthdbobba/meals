Grated parmesan is one of the few cheeses that behaves on trail: it is already dry, it keeps for weeks without a cooler, and it melts into a coating rather than a puddle. The heat left in the noodles is enough to soften it, so nothing goes back on the stove.

Draining matters more than anything else here. Carry the noodles over to the cheese instead of dumping cheese into the pot, and the starch clinging to them binds with the oil into something close to a sauce. Cooking in the bag also means the pot only ever held water, which is the whole cleanup.
