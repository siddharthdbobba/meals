Wild turkey legs are dark meat from heavily used muscles that need patient, moist cooking to become tender. Smoking and braising at home breaks them down into pulled meat; dehydration then lets you carry a full meal's protein without spoilage or significant weight. At camp, rehydration takes just 15 minutes.

Fat is essential here. Lean game meat dries out fast once cooked, and fat is what carries the smoked flavor and keeps the meat from tasting stringy. A tablespoon and a half will coat the rehydrated meat and restore the richness that three hours of braising created at home.
