The original is a Christmas sauce made from fresh persimmon simmered with water, sugar, and a vanilla pod, then blended and chilled. Fresh persimmon is a bad trail passenger, so this uses dried. That changes the arithmetic: dried fruit brings its own sugar, so cut the sugar back if yours tastes like candy already, and it needs more time and more stirring to break down. The lemon is the part that matters most and the part most likely to get left at home, so it becomes dried peel plus citric acid.

The acid is not a garnish. Persimmon and sugar alone taste heavy and one-note; the sour hit is what makes it read as a sauce instead of a jam. A spoon replaces the stick blender, which means the texture stays rough. That is fine. It also thickens hard as it cools, so pull it off the heat looser than you want it.
