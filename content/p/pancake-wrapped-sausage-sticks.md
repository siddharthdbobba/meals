The original bakes these in a cornstick pan in a 400 degree oven. Camp has no oven, so the shape comes from a hot skillet instead: batter, sausage, batter, flip once. Same result, one pan.

Everything perishable got swapped out. Buttermilk powder and powdered egg rehydrate with cold water and ride in the same bag as the flour, and ghee replaces butter because it keeps at ambient temperature for weeks. The sausage has to be the precooked vacuum-packed kind; raw links will not finish through in the time the batter needs. Keep the heat moderate, since the sugar in the batter browns faster than you expect over coals.
