This is three shelf-stable ingredients doing structural work. Peanut butter alone is a mess to carry; the powdered sugar and milk powder soak up the free oil and turn it into a dough stiff enough to slice. The milk powder is not there for flavor, it is there for body and a bit more protein per ounce.

The original chilled the logs and kept them in a cooler until the trailhead. That is worth doing at home because cold dough rolls cleanly, but the chill is not what keeps it safe. Nothing here needs refrigeration. Heat is the only real enemy, and heat only costs you the shape.
