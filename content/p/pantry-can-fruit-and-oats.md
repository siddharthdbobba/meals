Freeze-dried fruit from a storage can is cheaper per ounce than trail-brand fruit and weighs about the same. The catch is that it rehydrates slowly compared to oats, so the two go in the water at the same time rather than the fruit going in last. Five minutes off the heat, lid on, does both.

The oil is not optional. Oats and fruit are close to fat-free, which means a bowl this size runs out of calories long before the morning does. A tablespoon adds about 120 of them and costs almost nothing in pack weight.
