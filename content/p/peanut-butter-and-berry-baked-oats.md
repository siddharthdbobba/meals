The original is a home recipe: fresh egg, fresh milk, a real banana, a blender, an oven. All four of those got replaced. Powdered egg and milk carry the same structure without a cooler, banana chips soaked and mashed give the sweetness and binding of the fresh fruit, and a lidded mug buried in coals is close enough to 175 °C if you keep the fire low and covered.

The baking powder is what separates this from porridge. It lifts the oats into something cakelike, so the texture is worth the thirty minutes of sitting by a fire. The peanut butter in the middle stays molten and does not need to be stirred in; the raspberries go on top because they turn to mush if buried.
