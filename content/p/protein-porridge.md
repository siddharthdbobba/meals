The original is a home breakfast with a fresh egg beaten into the pot and a sliced banana on top. Neither travels, so the egg becomes powder and the banana becomes chips. Everything else was already shelf-stable.

The protein comes from three places on purpose. Collagen dissolves without changing the texture, egg powder adds structure, and the oats carry the rest. The heat step matters: egg powder hitting boiling water goes grainy, so the pot comes off the flame first and goes back on low only long enough to set. Flax thickens it and keeps the whole bowl from going soupy as it cools.
