Cookies are the wrong shape for camp. Scooping two dozen rounds means baking in batches, and a dutch oven over coals gives you one shot at even heat. Pressing the same batter into a single pan turns it into one bake, one pot, one wash. The flavor is unchanged.

Two swaps make it work away from a kitchen. A powdered egg removes the one ingredient that will not survive a warm day in a pack, and shelf-stable cream cheese from a foil pouch stands in for the tub. The cornstarch is doing real work: camp ovens run hot at the edges, and it softens the crumb enough that overshooting by five minutes does not ruin the pan. Maple syrup and molasses carry most of the sweetness, so there is less sugar here than a bakery version and the bars stay dense rather than crisp.
