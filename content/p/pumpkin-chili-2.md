Pumpkin puree in chili is not a novelty. It is starch and pectin, which means it thickens the pot without flour and holds up to a long simmer over uneven coals. It also softens the cayenne and the fire-roasted tomatoes, which is why the cinnamon does not read as sweet.

The camp change is the dairy. Heavy cream and sour cream are a cooler liability and they break if the pot gets away from you. Shelf-stable coconut milk goes in off the heat and does the same job. Chopping the vegetables at home cuts the camp prep to opening cans.
