Rice and beans is cheap at home because both keep forever in a cupboard. On trail the same logic holds for a different reason: the dehydrated versions weigh almost nothing and rebuild themselves in one pot. The canned beans and simmered dried beans of the kitchen version are out. Bean flakes do the same job in ninety seconds.

Bouillon is the whole flavor budget. Water and salt will cook the rice, but the result tastes like it. Dried onion and garlic go in with the water rather than at the end so they have time to soften. The oil is not optional either. Rice and beans on their own are a lot of carbohydrate and not much fat, and a tablespoon of oil is the cheapest hundred and twenty calories you will carry.
