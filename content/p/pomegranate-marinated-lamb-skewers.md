Acid does the work here. Pomegranate juice is tart and tannic enough to loosen up leg of lamb overnight, which is a cut that otherwise wants a long braise. The onion and shallot go in raw and minced so they melt into the marinade rather than sitting on the meat.

This one stays in the cooler, so it is a day-one or day-two car-camp dinner, not a backpacking meal. Two details matter: dry the cubes before they hit the fire, and cook over coals rather than flame. Wet meat and open flame both give you a grey outside and a raw middle.
