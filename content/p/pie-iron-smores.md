A pie iron solves the one problem with s'mores over a fire: the marshmallow cooks from the outside in, so you get char before you get melt. Closed metal heats the whole sandwich evenly and the chocolate softens on the same schedule as the marshmallow. Nothing falls in the fire, either.

This is car camping food. The crackers are fragile and the chocolate needs a cool pack, so it does not travel far in a backpack. Coals matter more than time. Open flame will blacken the crackers in under a minute while the inside is still cold.
