The home version simmers diced potato and carrot in broth for twenty minutes and then blends half the pot to thicken it. On trail you have neither the fuel nor the blender, so both jobs move to dry ingredients. Bean flakes are already the blended half. Potato flakes do the thickening the moment the heat is off, which is also where the peeled potato went.

Tomato powder replaces the can, and it is the ingredient worth sourcing. Smoked paprika and cumin carry most of what this soup tastes like, so measure them at home rather than shaking jars in the dark. The oil is not optional at these calories — without it this is a thin 400-calorie bowl after a long day.
