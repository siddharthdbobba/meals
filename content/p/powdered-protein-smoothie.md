A powdered packet solves the shelf-life problem no fresh fruit can—it spoils never and weighs almost nothing. The trade: it tastes like reconstituted powder, not fresh fruit, which is why you're carrying it.

Mix at camp with cold water and you have breakfast in two minutes flat. No stove, no fuel burned, minimal cleanup. Plant-based protein covers most dietary restrictions, which matters when you're planning trips with a group.
