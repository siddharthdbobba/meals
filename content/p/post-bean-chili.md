This started as a stovetop pot of beans simmering with fresh peppers, onion, tomatoes, and garlic for the better part of an hour. On the trail neither the fresh produce nor the hour is available, so everything got dried: home-cooked beans dehydrated to flakes, corn and peppers freeze-dried, tomatoes as powder. What was an hour of simmering becomes twelve minutes of soaking and stirring.

Order matters more than technique. Tomato is acidic, and acid slows down rehydration, so the beans and vegetables get plain water first and the tomato powder goes in after they have softened. The oil goes in last, off the heat, where it carries the chili and cumin instead of frying them.
