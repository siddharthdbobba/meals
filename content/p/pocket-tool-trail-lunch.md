This is a knife lunch. Everything in it arrives whole and gets cut at the stop, which is why it holds up: a salami stick and a cheese wedge keep far longer than sliced versions, because the cut surface is where things go wrong. Nothing here needs heat, water, or a fridge, so it works on a break where you do not want to unpack the stove.

The crispbread carries the fat rather than adding much itself. The oil and cheese do the calorie work, mustard cuts the grease, and dried apple resets your mouth at the end. Cut on something flat, not on your leg.
