Paneer is high-protein and dehydrated paneer stays shelf-stable without refrigeration, making it ideal for the trail. The spice blend balances warmth with flavor—the heat from the chili powder is noticeable but not overwhelming, and easily adjusted to your tolerance. This works on a canister stove or alcohol burner in five minutes flat.

Individual flavors still come through even though everything is powdered: the tomato tartness, the onion sweetness, the spice complexity. The ghee ties it all together and carries enough fat to make it satisfying as a complete meal or a substantial breakfast.
