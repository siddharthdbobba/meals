The original wants fresh milk and a sliced banana. Neither survives a pack, so the milk becomes powdered whole milk and the banana becomes chips that soften back up in the hot water. Honey in a jar is a mess waiting to happen, so brown sugar does that job.

Chia and flax are the reason this holds together. They absorb water and turn a half cup of oats into a bowl that actually fills you. The peanut butter powder carries most of the protein without the weight or the grease of a real jar.
