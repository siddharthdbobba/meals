The bean flakes are the structural part. Instant rice alone drinks water and stays loose, but the flakes bind the leftover liquid into something you can eat with a spoon instead of drinking. Add them last and add them slowly, because they set faster than you expect and a clumped pot never fully smooths out.

Fat and salt carry this. The oil is most of the calorie load and the chips do the work a cheese or crema would at home, adding crunch and salt without anything that needs a cooler. Everything rehydrates off the flame, so one boil is the whole fuel cost.
