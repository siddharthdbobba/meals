Portuguese custard tarts can't be trail-adapted—they need ovens and fresh cream. This extracts what makes them appealing (warm, sweet, spiced comfort) and rebuilds it from shelf-stable pieces. Flour thickens hot water into pudding, butter powder adds richness, cinnamon provides the Portuguese connection. It's a compromise, not a replica.

Flour in hot water needs constant stirring to avoid lumps. Add the water slowly or the mix will set into clots before the flour fully hydrates. The butter powder is what keeps this from tasting like wallpaper paste—don't skip it. Butter powder is widely available in grocery stores' baking sections.
