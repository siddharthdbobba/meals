The original is a kids' breakfast plate: a canned peach half with cottage cheese in the hollow and raisins for eggs. Cottage cheese does not travel, so the filling here is milk powder and a spoon of instant pudding mix, which thickens with cold water and no refrigeration. It sets to roughly the same texture and carries the cinnamon and honey better than curds would.

Canned fruit is heavy, so this is a car-camping or short-trip breakfast rather than a long-haul one. Buy single-serve plastic cups instead of cans and you skip both the can opener and the sharp lid, which matters when you are eating on a tailgate. Drink the syrup or dump it, but do not pour it into the filling.
