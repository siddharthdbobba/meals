This started as a shelf-stable pantry list rather than a recipe, which is exactly why it works in camp. Everything in it is either canned, dried, or a fat that keeps at ambient temperature. Nothing needs a fridge except the ferment, and the ferment is optional.

The oats are the structural piece. Beans and canned tomatoes on their own make a thin, watery pot, and rolled oats pull it together into something you can eat with a spoon without adding a second dish. Fat is the other half of the job: the oil, the creamed coconut, and the cheese are where the calories come from, and the splash of vinegar at the end keeps all that fat from tasting flat. The kraut is a cooler item, so leave it home on longer trips and add a bit more vinegar instead.
