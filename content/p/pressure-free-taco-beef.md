This is the original recipe stripped of the appliance. A pressure cooker is doing two things there: cooking the beef fast and keeping it from drying out. On a camp stove you get the same result by browning hard first, then finishing in a small amount of seasoned water. The water is not filler. Taco seasoning is mostly salt, chile, and starch, and it needs liquid to dissolve and thicken into something that coats the meat instead of sitting on it as grit.

Draining the grease matters more in camp than at home, because whatever you leave in the pot ends up in your dishwater and then in your pack. Freeze the beef flat before the trip and it works as cooler ice for the first day, then cooks the same as fresh. Serve on tortillas, over instant rice, or straight out of the pot with a spoon.
