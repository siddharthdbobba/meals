This is home prep that pays off later: a saucepan and ten minutes buys you six dense blocks that need nothing at camp. The set comes from the peanut butter and chocolate firming as they cool, not from sugar cooked to a temperature, so there is no candy thermometer and no stage to miss.

Use natural peanut butter. The commercial kind carries added sugar and hydrogenated oil, and it sets softer and sweeter than you want. Honey does two jobs here, binding the mix and keeping it from crumbling in a pocket. Heat only until the chips melt; hold it longer and the fudge turns grainy.
