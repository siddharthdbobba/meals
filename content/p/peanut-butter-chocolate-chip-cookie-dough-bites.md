The store-bought freeze-dried version of this is a pouch of crumbles you eat with a spoon. Rolling it yourself gets you the same thing for a quarter of the price and in a shape you can eat one-handed while walking.

Powdered milk is the structural ingredient. Raw cookie dough gets its body from flour, which you cannot eat uncooked in any quantity; milk powder absorbs the peanut butter oil the same way and needs no heat. Oats add chew. Skip either one and you have peanut butter with chips in it, which will not hold a ball.

Nothing here needs cold storage. Peanut butter, milk powder, and sugar are all shelf-stable on their own, and mixed together they stay that way for a week of hiking easily.
