steps:
  - "Warm the pineapple juice until it is barely above skin temperature. Hot juice kills yeast."
  - "Stir the juice and honey into the dry mix in a pot or large bag, then add the reconstituted egg and the ghee. Work it until no dry flour is left."
  - "Knead ten minutes on a clean pot lid or inside a heavy bag. The dough should end up smooth and slightly tacky."
  - "Cover and let it rise somewhere warm until doubled. In sun, an hour. In cold, put the pot near the fire ring, not in it."
  - "Divide into twelve balls and set them in a greased 10-inch dutch oven. Second rise, thirty minutes."
  - "Bake with about 8 coals underneath and 16 on the lid, roughly 25 minutes. Rotate the oven and lid a quarter turn every 8 minutes or the bottoms burn."
  - "Done when the tops are deep gold and a roll sounds hollow. Brush with ghee."

Cottage cheese and a fresh egg do not travel, so both got replaced: milk powder for the dairy protein and softened fat, egg powder for the structure. The result is nearly the same crumb. Pineapple juice stays, because it comes in a shelf-stable can and its sugar and acid are doing real work on the dough.

This is a car camping or basecamp recipe and it will not shrink into a backpacking one. It needs two rises, a real dutch oven, and coals you can count. The payoff is that the entire dry side travels as one labeled bag, and the only measuring at camp is a cup of juice.
