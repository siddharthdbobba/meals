The original used spray butter, which is a can you do not want in a pack. Oil from a dropper bottle does the same job: it gives the seasoning something to grip so the powder ends up on the crackers instead of in the bottom of the bag. Water works too and adds no weight, but it also makes the crackers soften, so oil is the better trade.

Pepperoni is the reason this counts as food and not just a salty crunch. A dry-cured stick keeps for days without a cooler and brings fat and protein that crackers alone do not. Slice it at home; sawing pepperoni with a pocket knife on a windy ridge is not worth it.
