Fruit leather is the cheapest calorie you can carry that is not a candy bar, and it survives being sat on. The cornstarch is doing structural work: it binds the puree so the dried sheet bends and rolls instead of shattering into chips. The brief simmer does the same job from the other direction, breaking down the fruit so the finished sheet is uniform rather than fibrous in patches.

The honey is not there for sweetness alone. Sugar holds a little moisture, which is what keeps the strip flexible in a cold pack. Lemon juice keeps the color from going brown. Dry it too far and you get brittle shards that crumble in the bag; stop too early and the wet spots will mold on you three days in. Aim for tacky and check the pale areas, since those are the ones still holding water.
