The pasta has to be precooked and dried at home. Raw pasta wants a rolling boil and eight minutes of fuel; dried cooked pasta only needs to reabsorb water, which a covered pot off the heat does fine. That is the whole reason this works on a small stove.

Draining feels wasteful, and it is, but the pasta comes out coated rather than soupy. Everything that makes it taste like food goes in after the water leaves: oil for calories, butter powder for the flavor oil does not have, Parmesan for salt. Add the seasoning at the end so the lemon does not boil off.
