A slow cooker roast is four hours of collagen breaking down. You cannot carry that, so this rebuilds the result from the other end: freeze-dried cooked beef is already tender, and the two soup mixes supply everything a canned condensed soup was doing — salt, onion, mushroom, thickener. No fridge, no can, no cream.

Order matters more than technique. Carrots need the longest boil, so they go in first with the beef and take the full simmer. The gravy base and potato flakes are both starch, and starch dumped into boiling water seizes into lumps. Off the heat, whisked in stages, it turns into gravy instead. The oil at the end is calories the original got from a fatty chuck cut; without it this eats thin.
