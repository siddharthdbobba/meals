This is the trail version of an Italian pistachio torte, stripped down to the flavors that matter: pistachio, cocoa, and a creamy filling. We replace the ricotta with powdered milk and skip the oven entirely. The whole thing is assembled at home and arrives as a finished bar that needs no cooking in camp.

The trick is emulsifying butter into the dry mix at home so it all holds together without water or heat. The fat is doing the binding and the seasoning; cocoa gives you the chocolate element without melting anything. Eat it straight from the packet or break into pieces.
