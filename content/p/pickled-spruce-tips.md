This is a foraging project more than a dinner. Fir and spruce push out soft new tips in late spring, and for a few weeks they taste sharply of citrus and resin. Vinegar and sugar hold that flavor in place. After a week in brine the tips behave like capers: sour, faintly piney, useful anywhere a trail meal has gone flat.

Everything but the tips is shelf-stable and travels in a jar, so this suits car camping or a basecamp where the fire is already going. The caution is identification, not cooking. Yew has flat needles and red berries and will hurt you. Take a key, take a small amount, and leave the growing tip on the tree.
