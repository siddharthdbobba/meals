Freeze-dried banana and pumpkin powder rehydrate entirely in cold water with no heat required. Milk powder dissolves into the mix, creating creaminess without refrigeration. Everything combines in a cup in under five minutes.

Dessert is a morale device on long hiking days but doesn't need to be complicated. This one weighs two ounces, tastes like fall even in summer, and uses only a cup and a spoon.
