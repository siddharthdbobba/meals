Tortillas are the one bread that survives a pack. No stove, no water, no pot, and the whole thing assembles standing up at a trail junction in about a minute.

The nut butter is the calorie engine and the honey is the glue that keeps the raisins from falling out the far end while you eat. Granola is optional but it fixes the main problem with this wrap, which is that everything in it is soft. Nut butter packets are worth the money here because a jar means a spoon and a spoon means washing something.
