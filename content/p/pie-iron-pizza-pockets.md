A pie iron makes a sealed pocket, which is the whole point. The bread crisps on the outside while the inside steams, so the cheese melts and the sauce heats without anything running into the fire. Butter on the outer faces is doing two jobs: browning the bread and keeping it from sticking to the cast iron.

Keep the filling thin. The failure mode here is not undercooking, it is a fat sandwich that splits at the seam and dumps sauce into the coals. Two tablespoons of sauce feels like too little and is correct. This one needs a cooler for the cheese and meat, so it is a car camping and basecamp recipe, not something to carry for days.
