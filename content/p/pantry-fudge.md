This is a no-cook fudge: there is no sugar syrup, no candy thermometer, no soft-ball stage. Powdered sugar already carries the cornstarch that gives real fudge its set, so all the recipe has to do is wet it enough to bind and then let it firm up cold. Two tablespoons of water is the whole liquid budget. More and you get frosting.

The dry milk is not filler. It absorbs some of that water and adds enough protein and fat to keep the squares from tasting like straight sugar. Butter does the rest, and it is also the reason the fudge softens in a hot pack — it will still be edible, just messier. Because everything is made and set at home, this travels as finished food and needs no water, fuel, or pot on the trail.
