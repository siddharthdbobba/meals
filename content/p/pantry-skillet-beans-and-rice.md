This started as a pantry-preparedness dish, which is most of the way to a camp dish already: nothing in it needs refrigeration and nothing needs a knife. The only real adaptation is instant rice instead of long-grain, so one boil and a covered rest do the work a twenty-minute simmer used to.

The bean liquid is the reason this comes out as a skillet dish rather than soup with rice in it. Drain the can and you lose the starch that binds everything, and you will be adding oil and bouillon to fake it back. Dried onion and pepper rehydrate in the boil and cost almost nothing in weight, which is where the canned tomatoes earn their spot too — replace them with dried pieces if you are carrying the pot any distance.
