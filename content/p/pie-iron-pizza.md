This is a sealed sandwich, not a pizza, and the pie iron is what makes the difference. Clamping the two slices together traps steam inside, so the cheese melts before the outside burns. Grease the iron every single time. Bread welds itself to bare cast iron and you will spend longer scraping than cooking.

Coals over flames, always. Direct flame chars bread in under a minute while the center stays cold. Budget about five minutes per sandwich, which means one iron feeds one person at a time. For a group, run two or three irons in rotation or people eat in shifts. Everything here needs a cooler except the bread, so this is a car-camping recipe; the tortilla and hard-cheese variation is the version that travels without one.
