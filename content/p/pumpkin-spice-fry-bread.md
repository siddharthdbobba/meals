This started as a home donut recipe, which means fresh eggs, buttermilk, and a fridge. All three are gone here. Powdered buttermilk and powdered egg rehydrate in the dough and behave close enough, and the pumpkin puree comes from a can or pouch that sits in a pack for a month without complaint.

The hole in the middle is not decoration. Dough this thick will brown outside and stay raw at the center over a camp stove, where the flame is small and the oil temperature swings. Keep the flame low, fry in small batches, and let the oil recover between them. Frying oil is heavy and has to be packed out, which is why this belongs at a car camp or a basecamp and not in a thru-hike food bag.
