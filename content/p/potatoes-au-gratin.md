Two kinds of cheese are doing different jobs. The powder dissolves into the water and becomes the sauce; the freeze-dried shreds stay in pieces so there is something to chew. Milk powder rounds off the sharp edge that cheese powder alone has, and it adds fat that the potatoes soak up.

Use potato dices, not flakes. Flakes will work and make a passable cheesy mash, but dices hold their shape and give the bag some structure. Keep the bacon and fried onions out until the end. Anything crunchy that sits in hot water for ten minutes stops being crunchy.
