Most store trail mix is two-thirds raisins and a scattering of tired nuts. This is the opposite: pecans for fat and dates for fast sugar, nothing else competing. Pecans run over 700 calories per 100 g, the highest of the common nuts, which is why the carried weight here is so low for the calories you get.

The dates matter more than they look. They are almost pure sugar, so they hit fast, while the pecan fat keeps releasing for the next hour. Cut them at home and salt the mix — uncut dates weld together in a warm pack, and unsalted pecans taste like nothing after the third handful.
