A pie iron is a sealed cast pan on sticks, which is why this works. The two slices of bread press together at the crimped edge and become a closed pocket, so sauce and melted cheese stay put instead of running into the fire. Nothing needs a pot and nothing needs water.

Heat management is the whole skill. Flames char the outside before the cheese moves; coals cook it through. Grease matters more than it sounds like it should, and overhanging bread just burns off, so trim it before you set the iron down.
