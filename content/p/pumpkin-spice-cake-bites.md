Two ingredients do the whole job. Canned pumpkin has enough moisture and body to stand in for the eggs, oil, and water the box asks for, so the only wet thing you carry is a can that keeps forever. That also means no fridge and no cracked eggs in a cooler.

The cinnamon sugar has to go on hot. Once the outside cools it stops taking the coating, and you get plain cake balls instead. Butter first, sugar second, one batch at a time.

Dutch oven heat is the part that goes wrong. Too many coals under the oven and the bottoms burn before the middles set, so keep most of the heat on the lid and rotate the oven a quarter turn every five minutes.
