The original called for farm eggs, cream, and grated cheese out of a fridge. None of that travels. Egg powder plus powdered milk hits close to the same texture, and it is the reason this works at all: the milk fat keeps the eggs from going rubbery when they overcook, which they will.

The dried pepper needs its ten minutes soaking or it stays crunchy in a way that reads as raw. Drain it before it hits the butter. Water in the pan means steamed eggs instead of scrambled. Hard aged cheese is the one dairy that keeps for days without a cooler, so use jack or cheddar and not anything soft.
