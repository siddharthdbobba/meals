The source version is a can of whole peeled tomatoes blitzed with basil, salt, and pepper. A can weighs 28 ounces and most of that is water, so the trail version starts from tomato powder instead. Add the water back at camp and you carry about an ounce per serving.

Whole peeled tomatoes are used because they are the least processed thing in the aisle and taste sweeter for it. Tomato powder loses some of that, which is why the seasoning stays short: salt and pepper do the lifting, and the basil goes in dry so it has hot water to open up in. Control thickness with water, not with cooking time. There is no reduction happening on a canister stove worth the fuel.
