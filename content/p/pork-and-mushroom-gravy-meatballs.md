The original is a kitchen dish with heavy cream, fresh mushrooms, and homemade stock. Three swaps make it a car-camping dinner: dried mushrooms rehydrated in bouillon water do the job of fresh mushrooms and stock at once, powdered egg binds the meatballs without a carton in the cooler, and powdered milk stirred in off the heat replaces the cream. The pork is the only thing that needs cold, so this is a first-night meal.

The mushroom soaking liquid is the whole point of the gravy. Don't dump it. Pour carefully and leave the gritty bottom behind, then whisk the bouillon into what you saved. Long, slow simmering is what makes the meatballs tender, and coals hold that heat better than a roaring fire does.
