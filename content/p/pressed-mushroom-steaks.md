  - "Clean the mushrooms at home. Rinse the clusters in cold water, trim the base with a knife, and let them dry fully on a towel. Wet mushrooms steam instead of browning."

The weight is the whole recipe. Hen of the woods is all ruffles and air, so an unpressed cluster browns on a few high points and stays pale everywhere else. Compressed against hot iron, the layers collapse into a slab that makes contact edge to edge, and the caramelization runs all the way across. The pressed mushroom also comes off the fire stiff, which makes it easy to eat off a knee without a plate.

This is a car camping or basecamp dish, not a pack meal. Fresh mushrooms need a cooler and a real skillet, and there is no dried substitute that behaves this way. Clean them at home and dry them completely. Grit is what ruins this, and you cannot inspect a mushroom well by headlamp.
