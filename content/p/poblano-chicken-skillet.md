The original is a cast iron dish built on cream cheese, crema, milk, and butter, which is four refrigerated things in one pan. All four are available as powders, and rehydrated off the heat they behave close enough. Roasting and peeling the poblanos at home before drying them is the step that matters most; raw dried chile is bitter and never softens.

Rehydration order does the work here. Protein and vegetables go into boiling water, dairy goes in after the pot is off the flame. Reversed, the cream cheese powder scorches on the bottom and you spend the rest of the night scraping.
