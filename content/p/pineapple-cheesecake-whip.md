The original is a food processor job: fresh pineapple, a block of cream cheese, and whipped heavy cream, held together in a fridge for two hours. None of that survives a pack. Freeze-dried pineapple, cream cheese powder, and cream powder do the same three jobs dry, and rehydrating them in a bag replaces both the machine and the chill time.

Water is the whole game. The powders want very little, and the pineapple pulls back what the cream gives up, so the mix thickens on its own as it rests. Add water in stages and stop early — a thin whip cannot be fixed on trail, but a stiff one takes another splash fine.
