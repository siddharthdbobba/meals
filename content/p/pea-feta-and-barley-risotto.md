Real risotto needs twenty minutes of stirring and a pot of simmering stock, neither of which is a reasonable ask on a stove burning fuel you carried. So the barley gets cooked at home in stock and dried flat, which means camp is only rehydration. Pearl barley survives that better than rice does. It holds its shape and comes back with some chew instead of turning to paste.

The feta is the reason this reads as a meal rather than grain and water. Freeze-dried feta is roughly a quarter protein and a quarter fat, and it rehydrates in seconds, so it goes in off the heat where it will not turn rubbery. Ghee does the rest of the work: it is shelf-stable without a cooler, and it carries the lemon and saffron through the whole pot the way butter would at home.
