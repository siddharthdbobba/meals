Powdered eggs get a bad reputation from the cheap ones. The better whole-egg powders scramble like eggs, with one catch: the package ratios run thin, so mix richer than the label says and you get curds instead of a puddle.

Heat is the other half. Reconstituted egg sets at a lower temperature than fresh and goes to rubber quickly, so the potatoes get the hot pan first and the eggs go in after the flame is turned down. Both the potatoes and the bacon bits are already dry, which means the whole breakfast is shelf-stable and the only cold thing you carry is water.
