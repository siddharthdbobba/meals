Real pumpkin puree is a can of water you carry for no reason, and it needs a fridge once opened. Pumpkin powder does the same job at a fifth of the weight, and you add the water back at camp. No eggs are involved, which is the other thing that makes this trip-friendly: the powder plus the water gives enough moisture, and the two leaveners handle the lift.

Ghee or coconut oil stand in for butter because neither cares about a warm bear canister. Coals over and under is the whole technique. Bottom heat alone scorches the base long before the center sets, so most of the coals belong on the lid.
