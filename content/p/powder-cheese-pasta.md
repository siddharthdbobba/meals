This is macaroni and cheese with the weight removed. Butter powder and milk powder give you richness without refrigeration or ounces. The pasta cooks in just enough water that when you add the powder mix to the hot pot, everything hydrates into actual sauce instead of broth or paste. Too much water and you have soup. Too little and you have a brick.

Stirring during the boil prevents scorching on the bottom and keeps it from boiling over. The 5-minute rest lets the powders finish hydrating while the noodles soften a touch more. This is a complete dinner that tastes real, not like trail compromise.
