This started as a tea-party spread built on refrigerated block cheese, jarred pimentos, and cream cheese. None of that travels. Cheese powder and powdered cream cheese carry the same fat and salt at a fraction of the weight, and dried bell pepper rehydrates into something close enough to pimento that the difference stops mattering after a few miles.

The order matters more than the ratios. Peppers first, because they take the longest and will stay leathery if you dump everything in at once. Then water in small increments — powdered dairy absorbs slowly, so a mix that looks right immediately will be soup in five minutes.
