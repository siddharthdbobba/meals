This started as a home pantry dish built around cans and long-shelf staples, which makes it an easy move to camp. Everything travels at room temperature and nothing needs a cooler. The only real change is water discipline: the rice gets a measured 700 ml rather than a pot filled by eye, so you are not draining fuel-heated water onto the ground.

The canning liquid from the chicken is the reason to buy the canned bird instead of a dry pouch. It is a weak broth, and poured into the pot it seasons the rice from the start. Tomato powder does what a jar of sauce would at home without the glass or the weight. Adding the chicken at the end, off heat, keeps it in shreds instead of cooking it into threads.
