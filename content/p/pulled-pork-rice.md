Instant rice done right is about fat, protein, and flavor. This recipe stacks all three. The freeze-dried pulled pork contributes serious protein without weight, the oil and cheese deliver calories and satisfaction, and the ranch mix brings it all together without requiring fresh ingredients. Everything pre-packs into one bag.

The bag method asks only for boiling water and a cozy to retain heat—no pots to wash, just a spoon. The rice rehydrates in 15 minutes while you handle other camp tasks. If you lack a cozy, the one-pot version works just as well; you'll just have dishes to clean. Corn is swappable for freeze-dried peppers or peas, and any hard cheese will work instead of pepperjack.
