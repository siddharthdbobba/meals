Store-bought bars crumble because they are mostly puffed grain glued together with syrup that goes brittle. This one is built the other way around: jumbo oats, whole seeds, and enough butter and honey to hold the whole slab together as one flexible mass. It bends under pack pressure instead of shattering.

The pressing step is the recipe. Press it half-heartedly and you get granola in a bag. The toasting up front matters too, since raw oats and seeds taste flat and toasted ones do not. Butter and honey mean these are vegetarian but not vegan or dairy-free, and the walnuts are not negotiable for anyone with a nut allergy unless you use the swap below.
