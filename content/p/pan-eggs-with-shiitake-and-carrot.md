Thai skillet eggs are usually a fresh-pan dish: whole eggs cracked over vegetables in a small hot pan. Nothing about that survives a pack, so this version does the work in reverse. The vegetables rehydrate first in a measured amount of water, the water gets poured off, and the eggs go in last as a slurry. Dumping powder into a wet pot gives you mushroom soup with egg in it.

White pepper and the mushroom seasoning are carrying the whole dish, so do not economize on them. Both go in after the heat is off, since white pepper turns flat and dusty when it fries. The oil is not optional either. Powdered egg is lean and it sticks to a titanium pot without something between it and the metal.
