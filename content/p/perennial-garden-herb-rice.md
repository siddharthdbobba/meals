The source page is about growing perennial food, not cooking it, so this is the trail version of what a rosemary hedge and a strawberry ground cover actually give you: herbs and fruit that dry well and weigh nothing. Everything perishable is gone. Nothing here needs a fridge.

Red lentils do the work. They cook in the same window as an instant-rice soak and thicken the pot into something that holds a spoon, which is the difference between a dinner and a bowl of seasoned water. The oil goes in off the heat because the calories matter more than the browning.
