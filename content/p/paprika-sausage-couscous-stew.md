Couscous is the fastest carb in a pack: it does not cook, it just swells. That means the stove only has to reach a boil once, which matters most on alcohol and Esbit where fuel is measured in minutes, not hours.

The cured sausage is doing two jobs. It carries the fat and salt that plain couscous has none of, and it renders a little into the water as it sits, which is what turns this from grain-in-broth into something with a coating. Bouillon and smoked paprika cover the rest. Dried vegetables need the full soak time to stop being crunchy, so do not cut the wait short.

Total dry weight is around 105 g. No dietary tag beyond nut-free applies here: the sausage is meat and the bouillon is beef, and couscous is wheat.
