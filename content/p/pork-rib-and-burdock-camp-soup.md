Burdock is the point, so most of what a home version would carry has been left out. Cabbage, turnips, garlic, and smoked paprika all shout louder than the root does and the soup ends up tasting like them instead. Fennel takes over that volume without competing, and dried shiitake pushes the earthy side of the burdock forward rather than covering it.

Spare ribs are doing double duty as meat and as stock. The connective tissue breaks down over the hour and gives the broth body no bouillon can fake, which is why the simmer stays low and long. This one needs a cooler and a real burner, so it is a first-night car camp dinner, not something to carry up a hill.
