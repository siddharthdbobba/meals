Freeze-dried pork is one of the few dehydrated proteins that tastes like actual meat. The noodles and vegetables rehydrate completely in boiling water and the residual heat, with no active cooking required. Butter and sour cream powders thicken the broth and carry fat through the noodles so it isn't thin broth with noodles floating in it.

Pack it all together at home so at camp you are dumping one bag into boiling water. The fifteen-minute sit is completely passive and buys you time to get the stove organized and start water for the next pot. The meal is protein-dense without tasting like protein powder.
