This is home-prep, not camp cooking. You bake it in a kitchen oven before the trip and then carry it dry, which is why it costs no fuel and no water on the trail. The syrup and peanut butter are the binder: they coat the oats so the mix holds together in clusters instead of powdering into loose oatmeal in your pack.

Protein powder is doing the work that would otherwise take dairy. Half a cup across a batch gets each serving to around 13 grams without anything that needs a cooler. The original serves this over yogurt, which is not a trail option, so the milk powder is here instead if you want something wetter than a handful of clusters.

One note on tags: it is vegetarian as written, but whey protein powder makes it not dairy-free, and peanut butter makes it not nut-free. The swaps in the variations fix both.
