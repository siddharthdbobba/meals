A pie iron is a sealed pan, which is the whole point. Anything wet that would slide out of a skillet sandwich stays put between two slices of bread, and the closed edges crimp shut as the bread toasts. That is why leftovers work here when they would not work anywhere else in camp.

The butter on the outside is not seasoning. It keeps the bread from bonding to the cast iron, and scraping a welded sandwich out of a hot iron in the dark is a bad end to a night. Coals over flame, and check early: the difference between toasted and ruined is about ninety seconds. Note the cooler requirement on this one. It is a car-camping recipe and does not scale down to a pack.
