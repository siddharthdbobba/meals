Pie irons run hot and uneven, so a filling that is already set does better than one that is still liquid. Instant pudding mixed with powdered milk and cold water thickens in about two minutes with no fridge and no cooking, which is the whole point of using it here instead of a milk-based pudding cup.

The cookie is structure, not flavor. Crumbs soak up whatever the marshmallows release when they melt, so the filling stays in the sandwich instead of running out the seam of the iron. Coals, not flames: bread over open flame is charred outside and cold inside.
