This started as a stack of pumpkin cheesecakes and spice cakes, none of which survive contact with a backpack. What does survive is the part that makes those desserts work: pumpkin puree, warm spice, and something creamy to suspend it in. Instant pudding does the suspending without an oven or a fridge.

Instant pudding sets by chemistry, not cold, so it firms up at any camp temperature. The milk powder matters — pudding mixed with plain water is thin and tastes like nothing. Canned pumpkin is heavy at roughly seven ounces, which is why the dried-powder swap is listed; on a car-camping trip the can is fine.
