Pumpkin is the reason this holds together. Dehydrated pumpkin is mostly soluble fiber and starch, so it thickens the pot the way a roux would at home, without butter or flour. It also sands the edges off the chili powder, which matters when the alternative is eating straight cayenne at altitude.

Cooking the TVP with the pasta saves a step and a boil. Everything else goes in off the heat because the bean flakes and tomato powder seize into lumps if they hit rolling water. The nutritional yeast stands in for the cheese this dish usually gets, and it survives a week in a pack, which cheese powder does too but flavor-wise this one earns its weight in savoriness rather than fat. The oil is where the calories are, so measure it honestly.
