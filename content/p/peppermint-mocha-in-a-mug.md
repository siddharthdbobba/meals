The listicle version of this drink is a martini or a bundt cake, neither of which fits in a pack. What survives the translation is the pairing: peppermint against dark chocolate and coffee. All three of those ship as dry powder, which is the whole reason this works on a stove.

Whole milk powder is the ingredient people substitute and regret. Nonfat dissolves the same but the drink comes out thin, and fat is what carries the peppermint oil and keeps you warm for more than ten minutes. Crush the candy canes at home. Doing it in camp means either a rock or your teeth.
