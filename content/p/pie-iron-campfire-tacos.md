A pie iron does one thing well: it presses two tortillas into a sealed pocket and browns both faces at once. That is the whole recipe. The seal matters more than the filling, which is why you tear off the overhang and keep the load light.

Browning the beef at home is the change worth making. Raw ground meat over a fire means a skillet, a grease problem, and a temperature you cannot verify. Cooked and chilled, the beef only needs to come back up to hot, which happens in the same few minutes the tortilla needs to toast. Drain the salsa too, or the steam softens the crust you are trying to build.
