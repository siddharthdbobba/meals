This is less cooking than rehydration, and that is the point. Boiling is the only heat step, so a small stove and a one-liter pot handle the whole job, and the pot never touches food. Nothing to wash means nothing to scrape frozen off the rim at 6 a.m.

The two things that go wrong are water volume and time. Too much water and you get soup you have to drink; too little and the bottom stays crunchy. Use the pouch's own fill line rather than a habit, stir hard before sealing, and give it the full soak. Cold air steals heat through the pouch wall, so insulate it if the temperature is anywhere near freezing.
