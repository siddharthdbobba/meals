Potato and apple in the same packet sounds odd until you cook it. The onion sits between them and the apple keeps its shape if you use a firm one, so you get sweet, sharp, and starchy in the same bite. The apple also releases enough moisture to steam the potato, which is why no water goes into the packet.

The hour under coals is not negotiable. Diced potato needs that long in a sealed foil packet, and the tortilla around it acts as a buffer so nothing scorches against the metal. Cut the pieces small and even; one big potato chunk is what ruins a packet. This one stays a car-camping or basecamp meal because it depends on a real fire and raw produce out of a cooler.
