Toppings inside the bread instead of on top is the whole idea. Nothing slides off into the coals, nothing needs a pan, and each pocket is its own sealed container that heats from both sides at once. Foil turns it into a packet you can throw straight on the fire.

Use low-moisture cheese and precooked meat. Fresh mozzarella releases water that turns the pita to paste, and four minutes on coals will not take raw sausage anywhere safe. The other failure is greed: pack a pocket full and the seam tears, and then you have a sauce fire.
