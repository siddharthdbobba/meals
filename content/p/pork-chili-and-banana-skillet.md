The original is a wok dish with raw pork strips, which is a refrigerator problem. Pouched pork solves it: already cooked, shelf-stable, and it browns fine in hot oil as long as you drain it first. Everything else on the list already travels.

Banana is the part people doubt. It works because a firm, underripe one holds its shape and caramelizes, adding sweetness and body without a sauce base. An overripe banana turns to paste in the pan, so buy them green and eat this on day one or two. Cook the meat first and set it aside; the banana needs a hot dry pan to itself, and crowding it means steaming instead of browning.
