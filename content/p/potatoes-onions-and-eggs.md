The original is a car-camp skillet: real potatoes, half an onion, six eggs, butter. All of that works over a fire and none of it keeps in a pack. Swapping in dehydrated hash browns, dried onion, and egg powder gets the same dish down to about four ounces a person with nothing that spoils.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Rehydrate the potatoes first and drain them hard, because leftover water is the difference between a browned hash and grey mush. The egg goes in last and cooks in under a minute, so have it mixed and waiting before the potatoes are done.
