The original is a Romanian plum pie with a sour cream topping, which needs fresh fruit, fresh dairy, and an oven. All three are gone here. Dried plums soaked back in hot water get close to the texture of the fresh bistrița plums the pie is built on, and they carry at room temperature for a month. Sour cream powder rehydrates into something tart enough to do the job of the cream, and it weighs almost nothing.

Coal placement is the part that decides whether this works. Most of the heat belongs on the lid, or the bottom scorches before the middle sets. Rotating the pot and lid in opposite directions evens out the hot spots that any fire has. The soaked plums release a little liquid as it bakes, which is why the batter starts slightly stiff.
