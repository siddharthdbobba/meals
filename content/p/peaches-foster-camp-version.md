This started as bananas Foster made with peaches, cooked in a skillet and served over ice cream. Two of those things do not survive a trailhead. Dried fruit replaces fresh because it weighs a quarter as much and cannot bruise in a pack, and citric acid replaces the lemon because a lemon is mostly water you are already carrying.

The soak matters more than the simmer. Dried peaches that go straight into hot syrup stay leathery in the middle, so give them cold water and ten idle minutes first. The sugar goes in after the fruit is soft, not before, because sugar syrup pulls water back out of the fruit. Skip the ice cream and let the sauce be the rich part: butter, brown sugar, and a little milk powder get you close enough.
