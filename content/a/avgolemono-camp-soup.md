Avgolemono is a soup thickened with egg instead of cream, which is exactly what you want on trail: no fresh dairy, no roux, no flour. Whole egg powder rehydrates well enough to do the same work, and lemon powder carries the acid without a leaking bottle.

The tempering step is the only place this can go wrong. Egg poured straight into boiling broth curdles into strings. Warm it slowly in a mug first, off the flame, and it turns the broth silky instead. Kale flakes are not traditional but they rehydrate in the same eight minutes the orzo needs and add something to chew.
