Instant potatoes are the fastest hot dinner in a pack, but plain flakes and water make paste. The dry milk and parmesan give it fat and body, and the alfredo mix supplies the starch and salt that make it read as a sauce rather than a filler. The oil matters more than it looks: without it this comes in under 400 calories, which is not a dinner after a long day.

All of it is shelf-stable by design. Dry milk stands in for cream, the canned parmesan does not need a cooler, and everything but the oil packs into one bag. Add the oil to the water before boiling so it disperses instead of pooling on top.
