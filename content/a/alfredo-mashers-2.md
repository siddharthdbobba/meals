Instant potato flakes are the fastest hot dinner in a pack, but plain ones are thin and taste like paste. The fix is fat and dry dairy. Milk powder rebuilds the body that water alone cannot, the parmesan brings salt and glutamate, and the tablespoon of oil is where a third of the calories actually live.

Boiling the oil with the water matters. Poured on top it sits in a slick; stirred in from the start it emulsifies into the potato. Add the dry mix in stages too, because dumping it all at once gives you dry pockets that never hydrate. Potatoes carry a lot of potassium, which is worth something after a long hot day of sweating.
