This started as a refrigerator dessert and mostly survives the move outdoors because nothing in it is perishable. Chickpea brine whips like egg white, which means the mousse needs no eggs, no cream, and no cooler to stay safe. The only real constraint is chilling: aquafaba foam holds air but stays loose until it gets cold, so you need a creek, a snowbank, or a genuinely cold night.

The olive oil and syrup go in last for a reason. Fat deflates whipped protein foam, so folding them into an already-chocolate-heavy mixture costs less air than adding them early. Salt matters more than it looks — half a teaspoon against seven ounces of chocolate is what keeps it from reading flat.
