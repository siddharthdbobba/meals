The original is a Norwegian dessert built on fresh apple sauce and vanilla quark, neither of which survives a day in a pack. Dried apples simmered in a little water rebuild the sauce, and milk powder whisked with vanilla powder and only a splash of water gives you something thick enough to spoon rather than pour.

Toast the oats at home. It is the one step that makes this worth carrying, and doing it on a canister stove wastes fuel and usually scorches. The layers are not decoration either: oats sitting in the apple go soft in minutes, so assemble in the cup and eat it standing there.
