These are held together by cooked sugar, not by binding or refrigeration, which is the whole reason they travel. The butter, brown sugar, and honey are simmered long enough to thicken into a syrup that hardens as it cools, so the bar goes into your pack already set and stays set. Skip the 20 minutes on the stove and you get a crumbly mess in the bottom of a bag.

Toasting the dry mix first is not optional. Raw almond meal and wheat germ both carry oil that turns stale fast, and a few minutes of dry heat buys shelf life along with flavor. Press the mixture into the pan harder than feels reasonable, score it warm, and cool it fully in the dish. Cut cold and it shatters.
