This started as a family dinner off a budget grocery list: sausage as the anchor, rice and beans to stretch it, olives and feta for flavor. That structure is already close to trail food. The changes are the perishables. Fresh sausage becomes a dry-cured stick that keeps for weeks, canned beans become bean flakes, and long-grain rice becomes instant rice so one boil finishes the job.

The bean flakes are the load-bearing part. They soak up whatever water the rice left behind and turn a wet pot into something you can eat with a spoon, and they add protein without adding cook time. The olives matter more than they look: cured salt and acid are the two things a rehydrated dinner is always missing, and neither survives being dried. Feta is real dairy, so it is a day-one luxury and nothing more.
