The original is a summer preserve: fresh apricots and peaches, whole vanilla bean, a long boil, sterilized jars. None of that travels. Dried fruit is the same fruit with the water already removed, so you carry the flavor and add the water back at camp. The sugar is not decoration. It pulls moisture out of the fruit and gives the jam body, and at this ratio it sets on its own without pectin.

Heat control is the whole job. A canister stove puts a lot of energy into one small circle of the pot bottom, and sugar burns there before the rest of the pot knows anything is wrong. Lowest flame, constant stirring, and stop while it still looks loose. Vanilla powder instead of extract because a leaking dropper of extract ruins a food bag.
