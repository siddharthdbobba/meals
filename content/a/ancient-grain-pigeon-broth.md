Dried game meat, toasted barley, and warm spices replace the fresh ingredients of the original. The barley thickens broth and adds body that stock alone cannot carry—toast it at home for depth that raw grain will never reach.

The oil is not optional. It carries both flavor and calories into cold nights, turning thin broth into a meal substantial enough to warm you through. If you cannot source dehydrated game meat, jerky crumbled into the pot works just as well.
