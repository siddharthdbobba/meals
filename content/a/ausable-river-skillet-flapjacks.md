These are fried, not griddled, which is the whole point. Batter dropped into hot oil puffs hard against the baking powder and sets with a crisp shell, so you get something closer to a fritter than a breakfast pancake. That also means the oil depth matters more than the recipe does: shallow oil gives you a greasy flat disc.

Fresh eggs do not belong in a pack, so powdered whole egg does the binding here. It rehydrates fine in the batter and you never notice. Dried blueberries replace fresh for the same reason, and they soften in the oil. Frying takes real fuel and a heavy pan, so this is a car-camping or basecamp breakfast, not a backpacking one.
