This is a car-camping dish because frozen fries and cheese both need a cooler. The order matters more than anything: the onions take twelve minutes and the fries take about the same, so cook the onions first and let them sit. They are fine lukewarm. The fries are not.

A foil pan replaces the baking sheet and the broiler becomes coals under a grate. There is no broiler heat coming from above, so the cheese gets melted by covering the pan for a minute instead. American slices are the right call here for once, since they melt at a low temperature and do not break into oil the way real cheddar does over a fire. The sauce is cold and acidic on purpose, cutting through the butter and the fat in the cheese.
