The key to these pancakes is not overworking the batter. Lumps disappear when they cook; aggressive stirring makes tough pancakes instead. The apple cider in the batter adds subtle sweetness and a slight tang that butter and sugar alone cannot achieve.

The compote is where the recipe earns its place in the car-camping rotation. Fresh apples caramelize quickly in butter and release their own liquid, creating a light glaze that tastes nothing like flat applesauce. Spoon it over the stack warm.
