Freeze-dried chicken hydrates as the rice absorbs water, so you're cooking one dish at one temperature for one duration. This is why it works: nothing is waiting on something else. The saffron adds enough aroma that the freeze-dried vegetables stop tasting like dehydrated filler. Water must be exact, so don't guess the ratio.

This is a patience recipe, not an attention recipe. Boil water, pour it in, cover, and walk away while you set up camp. In cold weather, use a pot cozy or add time. Cheese stirred in at the end is fat, not flavor—skip it and the rice tastes lean. If your pack weight matters more than your dinner, freeze-dried cheese powder works.
