The original is a Romanian preserve: fresh apricots and walnut kernels boiled with sugar until they candy, then sealed in jars for winter. Fresh apricots do not travel, and canning in the backcountry is not a thing, so this version starts from dried fruit and skips the jar. The apricots rehydrate in the first boil and the sugar syrup forms around them, which gets you to roughly the same place in fifteen minutes.

Sugar is doing structural work here, not just sweetening. Too little and you get stewed fruit in water; the half cup is what turns the liquid into syrup thick enough to cling. The walnuts go in at the end on purpose. Simmer them and they turn soft and slightly bitter, and the point of them is the crunch against the fruit.
