The original is a home muffin recipe with butter, yogurt, and eggs from a fridge. All three are replaceable with powder, and the swaps do the same jobs: milk powder plus water gives the acid-free liquid, egg powder binds, butter powder carries the fat. Freeze-dried apples were already in the source recipe, which is what makes it worth adapting at all. They rehydrate off the batter's moisture during the bake instead of dumping water into it the way fresh apple would.

Heat management is the whole game in a dutch oven. Most of your coals belong on the lid. Coals underneath scorch the bottom long before the center sets, and a quick-bread batter this thick needs the full twenty-plus minutes. Stir the batter as little as you can stand.
