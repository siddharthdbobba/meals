The apples cut the curry heat and stay firm through rehydration. The gravy mix thickens the rice as it hydrates—you end up with a stew, not a broth with rice in it. Freeze-dried chicken gives you protein that doesn't spoil in a warm pack.

At altitude or in the cold, a pot cozy cuts the sitting time by half. Without one, 15 minutes and you're done.
