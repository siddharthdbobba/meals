The original was a slow cooker soup that got its body from boiled potatoes hit with an immersion blender. Instant potato flakes do the same job in one step: they dissolve into the broth and give it weight without milk, cream, or a blender you are not carrying.

The rest is substitution. Freeze-dried asparagus and spinach flakes for fresh greens, dried dill for the bunch, citric acid for the lemon. The acid matters more than it sounds like it should — without it the pot tastes like starch and salt. Add it last, off the heat, and taste before you commit to the whole quarter teaspoon.
