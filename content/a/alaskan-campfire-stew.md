This is a coals recipe, not a stove recipe. The two hours of low heat is what turns cheap cubed shoulder or round into something you can eat with a spoon, and a Dutch oven buried in coals holds that heat far more evenly than a burner ever will. Build more fire than you think you need and shovel fresh coals under the pot as they die.

The order matters. Bacon fat is the cooking medium for everything after it, and browning the meat in that fat is where most of the flavor comes from. Potatoes and carrots go in late because two hours of simmering turns them into paste. Nothing here is trail food: it needs a cooler, a heavy pot, and time you only have at a car site or basecamp.
