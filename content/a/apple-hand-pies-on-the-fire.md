The original is a lenten pie built on thin pastry sheets and fresh sour apples. Neither travels. Dried tart apple rings rehydrate in ten minutes and come back with the acidity intact, which is the whole point of using sour apples in the first place. Flour tortillas do the job of the pastry: thin, flat, indestructible in a pack, and they crisp over coals.

The semolina is not filler. Rehydrated apple sheds water, and without something to bind it the filling runs out the seam and into the fire. Two tablespoons is enough to hold it together without making it pasty. Coals, not flame — a tortilla goes from brown to carbon in under a minute over open fire.
