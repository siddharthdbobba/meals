This started as a kitchen snack and needed almost nothing done to it for the trail. There is no cooking, no fridge step that matters, and no water at camp. The pancake mix is doing double duty: it is the dry protein and it is the thing that turns wet nut butter into something you can hold.

The chia matters more than it looks. It pulls in the free moisture from the honey and almond butter over the first hour, which is why these set up firm instead of staying tacky. Honey is the only reason to leave this off a vegan list, and the pancake mix is wheat, so it is not gluten-free either. Almonds are in here twice, so it is squarely off the nut-free list.
