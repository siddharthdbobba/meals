This is trail food that gets built in a kitchen, not at camp. Chocolate is one of the densest calorie sources you can carry, and setting it around toasted nuts and dried fruit turns it into a bar that does not need a wrapper or a stove. Roughly 320 calories in two ounces, most of it fat, which is what you want on a cold day.

Toasting the almonds matters more than it sounds. Raw slivered almonds go soft against the fruit and taste like nothing; toasted, they stay crisp for a week and give the drop something to break against. The apricots have to be chopped small for the same reason a Ramen Bomb needs potato flakes: structure. Large chunks leave the chocolate no continuous surface to hold onto, and the whole thing crumbles into the bottom of your food bag.

The dairy question is the one to watch. Most chocolate chips contain milk fat, so this is vegetarian but not dairy-free unless you buy chips that say so.
