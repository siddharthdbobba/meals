This is a car-camping breakfast, not a trail one. Avocado, bacon, and an egg all need a cooler and none of them survive a week in a pack, so this is a first-morning meal at the trailhead or a basecamp day.

The reason it works is the fat. An avocado half is most of a stick of butter's worth of energy, and it digests slowly, which is the opposite of what a pastry does to you before a climb. The bacon fat also does double duty: it is the cooking medium for the egg, so there is no oil bottle and only one pan to wipe out. Firmness matters more than ripeness here. A soft avocado turns to paste under the egg and you end up eating it with a spoon.
