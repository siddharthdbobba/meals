Applesauce is mostly water, which makes it useless to carry and easy to fix. Dried to a leather it loses nearly all of that weight and keeps the sugar and the acid, which is the whole reason you wanted it.

The quarter-inch thickness matters more than the temperature. Spread thinner and the edges turn to brittle glass before the middle sets; spread thicker and you get a sticky center that will mold in the bag. Added peaches or strawberries carry more water than the applesauce does, so expect an extra hour or two in the oven if you use them.
