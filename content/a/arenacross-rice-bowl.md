The bean flakes are the load-bearing ingredient. Instant rice on its own drains to the bottom of the pot and leaves you drinking the broth separately. The flakes bind the water into the rice, so every calorie you carried ends up on the spoon instead of in the dirt.

Rehydrating the beef in the bouillon water before the rice goes in matters. Meat that goes in dry with the grains stays chewy in the middle. Two minutes in hot broth first fixes that, and it costs no extra fuel because the water is already off the flame.
