This started as a twelve-muffin oven recipe. Nothing about that survives on trail, so the structure changed: no egg, no butter, no fridge milk, no baking. What remains is the flavor logic — oats, apple, cinnamon, brown sugar — cooked as a single thick pot bake.

The flour and the small hit of baking powder are what keep it from being oatmeal. Flour gives the batter body; the baking powder puts enough air in it that the texture reads as cake rather than mush. Dried apple rehydrates in the batter as it cooks, so it needs no separate soak. The streusel goes on at the end, off heat, because oats and sugar on a live flame burn fast.
