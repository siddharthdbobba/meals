This is the sauce that goes on a smoked wild turkey breast, and it is the part of that meal you can actually make in camp. Vinegar and mayonnaise instead of tomato and sugar, which matters when the bird is lean. Wild turkey has almost no fat, so it dries out on a grill; a fatty acidic sauce puts back what the smoke took out.

The water is not filler. Straight mayonnaise sits on the meat in a lump instead of coating it. Thin it until it pours off a spoon in a ribbon. Mayonnaise needs a cooler for anything past the first day, so if you are carrying it without one, use shelf-stable packets and mix only what you will finish that night.
