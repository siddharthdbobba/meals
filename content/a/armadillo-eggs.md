The dough here is doing two jobs. Sausage alone would shrink and split around the pepper; the biscuit mix binds it, absorbs the rendering fat, and holds the shape through half an hour of dry heat. Cheese in the dough as well as inside the pepper means the outside browns instead of drying out.

This one stays at the trailhead. It needs raw sausage, a cooler, a mixing bowl, and steady coals, so it is a basecamp or car-camping dinner and nothing lighter. Halving the peppers into rings rather than boats matters more than it sounds — a ring is easy to seal dough around, a boat leaks cheese into the oven.
