The original is a fridge recipe: applesauce and fresh milk in a sealed container overnight. Neither travels, so the applesauce becomes dried apple chopped fine and the milk becomes whole milk powder. Both rehydrate off the same water the oats are already absorbing, which is why the liquid drops from a full cup to about 200 ml.

The spices are not decoration. Cinnamon, ginger, and cloves are what make dried apple read as pie instead of just fruit, and the pinch of salt keeps the whole thing from tasting flat. Walnuts are the fat, and fat is most of the reason this holds you until midday.
