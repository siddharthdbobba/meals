The egg white is glue, not an ingredient you taste. It foams up, grabs the sugar and cinnamon, and dries onto the nuts in the low oven into a thin shell. That shell is why this keeps: the coating seals the nut oil away from air, so the mix stays fresh for weeks instead of going rancid the way bare walnuts do in a hot pack.

Two hundred degrees for an hour is dehydrating, not baking. Go hotter and the sugar melts and slides off, and you end up with a sheet of candy and naked nuts. Stir it every quarter hour and you get even coverage. Cereal and apple go in only after everything is stone cold, because warm nuts throw off enough steam to soften both.
