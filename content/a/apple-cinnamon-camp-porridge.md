This is a commercial dehydrated apple porridge rebuilt from parts you can buy in a grocery store. The original bakes fresh apple slices before drying them, which you cannot do on trail and do not need to: store-bought dried apple rehydrates into the same soft, sweet pieces once it sits in hot water long enough.

The five-minute rest off the stove is the whole recipe. Oats hydrate fast, dried fruit does not, and if you eat immediately you get soft oats around chewy apple. Citric acid replaces the lemon; it keeps the sugar and raisins from going flat, and it weighs nothing.
