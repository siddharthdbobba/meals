The original is a grilled sausage sandwich with a whiskey remoulade, which needs a cooler, a grill, and a loaf of bread that will be gravel by day two. The sausage stays, the sauce stays, the bread and the fridge go. Dry-cured andouille and instant rice carry the meal instead, and the rice soaks up the rendered fat that would otherwise soak into a bun.

The remoulade is the point, so it is built cold in a bag and stirred in at the end. Heat splits mayonnaise. Vinegar and mustard cut the fat of the sausage, and the whiskey mostly reads as smoke once it hits warm rice. Single-serve mayo packets are the trick that makes the whole thing shelf-stable.
