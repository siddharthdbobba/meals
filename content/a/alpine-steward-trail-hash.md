Real hash needs a hot skillet and fat. Neither travels well, so this version gets its structure from bean flakes instead of a sear. They bind the loose potato and water into something you can eat with a spoon rather than drink.

The two-minute simmer after soaking matters more than it sounds. Dehydrated potatoes hold water badly and will leave you with a soup if you skip it. Jerky goes in at the start, not the end, because it takes the longest to come back.
