The oven version of this depends on radiant heat from above, which no camp stove has. A skillet with a lid gets you there another way: the pan heats the bread from below, the trapped steam heats the cheese from above. Low heat is the whole trick, since the bread has a much shorter fuse than the cheese.

Everything here except the cheese and sauce is shelf-stable, so the cooler only carries two items. Peppers and mushrooms go in raw because they only need to warm through under the lid. If you are out past the second day, drop the cheese and treat it as a hot open-faced sandwich instead of pretending it will still be pizza.
