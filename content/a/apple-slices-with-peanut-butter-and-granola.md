This is a fresh-fruit snack, which means it is a day-one item and it does not scale into a long trip. An apple is heavy for its calories, but it is also the only thing in a food bag with any water and crunch left in it, and after two days of bars that matters more than the ounces.

The peanut butter is what turns it from fruit into food: fat and protein that slow the sugar down. Granola alone will not stick to a bare slice, so spread the nut butter first and treat it as glue. Crush large clusters at home or they roll off and end up in the dirt.

An apple bruises into mush under a pack lid. Carrying it inside a mug is the cheap fix. Slice at home only if you are eating it that day, since even lemon juice buys you hours, not nights.
