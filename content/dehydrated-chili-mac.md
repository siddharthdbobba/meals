Dehydrating at home is the single biggest jump in backcountry food quality available,
and it costs one evening per trip. Commercial freeze dried meals run 110 to 120
calories per ounce for eight dollars. This runs 140 for about two.

The one rule: rinse the fat off cooked ground meat with hot water before it goes on
the tray. Fat does not dehydrate, it goes rancid, and it will find you on day four.
