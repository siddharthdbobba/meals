This started as a home kitchen dish and needed two changes to work at a fire ring. Fresh morels became dried ones, which weigh nothing and bring their own soaking liquid to use as stock. Heavy cream became cream powder, which reconstitutes thick enough to hold a sauce and does not spoil in a warm cooler.

The flour matters more than it looks. Venison is lean and the sauce has no braising liquid behind it, so without a roux the cream splits over live fire. Cook the loin rare and let it rest while you build the sauce; carryover heat from a grate is aggressive and will take it to medium on the plate.
