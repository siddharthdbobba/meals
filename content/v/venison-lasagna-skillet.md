The original is portabella caps stuffed with lasagna and baked. Camp has no oven and no ricotta that survives a day in a truck, so this rebuilds the same flavors in one pan: venison simmered in tomato, noodles cooked in the sauce, cheese melted on top under a lid instead of browned in an oven. Dried mushrooms replace the caps and carry the same earthy note without hauling eight fresh portabellas.

Cooking the noodles in the sauce rather than in separate water is the part that matters. You lose a pot, a strain, and about a liter of water, and the pasta picks up salt and tomato as it softens. Watch the bottom — starch plus a live fire scorches fast, so keep the pan off the hottest coals and keep the spoon moving.
