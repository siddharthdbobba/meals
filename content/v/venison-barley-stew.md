The original is an hour-long braise with fresh root vegetables. Neither the time nor the produce travels well, so this version leans on quick-cooking barley and dried vegetables that rehydrate in the same pot they cook in. Barley is the reason it still tastes like the original: it releases starch as it simmers and gives the broth weight that no instant starch quite matches.

Staging matters. Barley needs twenty minutes of real simmer, the dried carrot and celery need about ten, and the peas need almost none. Dump everything at once and you get mush around a hard center. Budget the fuel honestly — this is a thirty-five minute burn, which is a lot on a small canister. It is a stew for a night you are staying put.
