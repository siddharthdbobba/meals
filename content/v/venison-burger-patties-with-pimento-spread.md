Venison is lean enough that a burger made from it goes dry fast. The pimento spread is the fix. It is fat, salt, and acid landing on the patty right as it comes off the heat, which is roughly what a fattier grind would have done from the inside.

The original called for fried green tomatoes and a quart of brine, which is a lot of oil and a lot of dishes for a fire pit. Those are gone. What is left is one grate, one mixing bag, and a spread that goes together cold. The mayo and cheese still want a cooler, so this is a first- or second-night car camp meal, not a pack-in dinner.
