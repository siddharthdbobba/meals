The original sauce is boiled potato, carrot, and onion blended smooth. You cannot blend at camp, so the vegetables come dried and the potato comes as flakes, which thicken on contact instead of needing a machine. Nutritional yeast does the same job it does at home: savory depth without dairy. The mustard and vinegar are the sharp edge that makes it read as cheese rather than as vegetable puree.

The starchy pasta water is the binder. Drain it fully and the sauce goes pasty, so keep some in your mug and add it back until the texture is right. The panko is optional but it is the only part that survives from the baked version, so toast it dry and put it on last.
