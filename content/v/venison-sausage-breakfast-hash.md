Venison is lean, so the fatty pork butt is doing the work here—it keeps the sausage tender when cooked and gives you calories on the trail. Dehydrating the cooked sausage at home turns it into shelf-stable meat that travels without ice.

The trick is boiling the sausage in just enough water to rehydrate it, then thickening everything together with potato flakes and egg powder. It's a savory breakfast that tastes like someone's hunting cabin, which is the point.
