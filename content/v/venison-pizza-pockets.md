Deep frying egg roll wrappers is a kitchen job. In camp you have one skillet and a finite amount of oil, so this version trades the fryer for a pan-crisped tortilla. The filling is the same idea: lean ground venison simmered down with pizza sauce until it is thick enough to stay put, then cheese and pepperoni added at the last second.

The simmer matters more than it looks. A wet filling steams the tortilla instead of browning it, and the seam comes apart in the pan. Cook the sauce until a spoon dragged through leaves a line, then fill. Dried onion replaces fresh because it needs no cutting board and no cooler space.
