This is camp cooking, not trail cooking. Three pounds of raw meat and a cooler are the entry fee, and the payoff is a dinner that feeds six with enough left for sandwiches. Venison is lean enough to bake into a brick on its own, so the ground bacon is structural: it puts fat back in, and the bread crumbs and stock hold the moisture that fat releases.

The stuffing is what makes it worth the effort. Filling the pot halfway, laying in spinach and gouda, then sealing the rest of the meat over it puts a melted layer through the middle instead of cheese sweating out the sides. Keep the filling back from the edges. A dutch oven over coals runs hotter on the bottom than an oven does, so weight the coals toward the lid and check the center temperature rather than the clock.
