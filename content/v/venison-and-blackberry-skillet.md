The original is a seared tenderloin under a pan sauce, which needs a hot skillet, a fridge, and a cutting board. None of that goes in a pack. What survives the translation is the pairing: lean, iron-heavy meat against tart fruit, with dairy fat smoothing the edge between them. Dehydrated venison and freeze-dried blackberries carry that same combination at a quarter of the weight.

The cream cheese powder is doing two jobs. It thickens the berry water into something that clings, and its fat keeps the venison from reading as dry — lean game has almost none of its own, which is why the oil is not optional either. Cinnamon and cayenne are small amounts on purpose. They are there to keep the sauce from tasting like jam.
