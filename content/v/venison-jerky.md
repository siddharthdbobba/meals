Jerky is salt and smoke doing preservation work. Soy and worcestershire pull water from the meat, stopping spoilage before it takes hold. The ketchup provides sweetness—without it you get pure salt. Once dehydrated, jerky needs no water or cooking at camp.

Lean venison is essential because fat spoils in your pack. The cure proportions matter: enough salt and acid to preserve, but not so much that every bite tastes like brine. Slice thin so the dehydrator drives out moisture evenly.
