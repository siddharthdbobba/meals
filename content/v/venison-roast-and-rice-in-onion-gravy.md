This is a pantry dinner that survives the trip to a campsite intact. Everything except the roast and the butter is shelf-stable, and the roast keeps fine frozen in a cooler for two days. Cans of consomme and onion soup do the job a long-simmered stock would do at home, which is why an hour is enough.

Parboiled rice matters. Regular long grain turns to paste sitting under a roast in that much liquid, while converted rice stays separate and still takes on the gravy. Searing first is not for doneness, it is for the browned crust and the fond it leaves in the pan. Sliced thin across the grain, a lean venison round stays tender; cooked through and cut thick, it will not.
