The source for this one is a stunt: cook spaghetti, freeze it into balls, wrap each ball in ground venison sausage, bread it, and bake. That needs a freezer, four hours, and an oven, which rules out every camp kitchen. What survives the trip is the flavor combination underneath, so this is that combination cooked straight in one pot.

Cooking the pasta in a measured 700 ml instead of a full boil means no draining and no strainer, and the starch left behind thickens the sauce so a single pouch is enough for four people. Ground sausage does not travel without a cooler, so a cured venison link stands in. Browning it first puts fat in the pot that the pasta soaks up later.
