The original is a layered casserole with enchilada sauce, cheese, and 45 minutes in an oven. None of that travels. What survives the trip is the part that carries the dish anyway: lean venison browned hard with salt, cumin, and pepper, and corn tortillas going soft in the fat.

Browning matters more than usual here. Venison has almost no fat of its own, so the flavor has to come from crust on the meat and from the oil you add. Stir it too much and you steam it grey. Tearing the tortillas instead of stacking them means they soak up the pan drippings rather than sitting in sauce, which is what the sauce was doing in the first place.
