Real stock is scraps, water, and thirty minutes of simmering, then you throw the solids away. That is backwards for the trail, where water is the heaviest thing you own. Drying the scraps first and grinding them means you carry the flavor and leave the water at home. You are eating the solids instead of straining them out, which is why the grind has to be fine.

Skip anything bitter or already going soft. Brassica trimmings and old lettuce turn sulfurous when concentrated. The olive oil goes in at camp rather than the powder because fat in a dry mix goes rancid in a pack within a couple of weeks.
