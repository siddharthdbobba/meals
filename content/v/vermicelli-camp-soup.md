Vermicelli earns its place on trail for one reason: it is thin enough to cook off residual heat. Bring the water up, kill the stove, and the noodles soften while the pot cools. That is a minute or two of fuel instead of ten, which matters more on an alcohol stove than a canister.

The dried vegetable mix does the flavor work that a bouillon cube alone cannot. Bouillon is salt and not much else; leek, celery root, and lovage give the broth something to taste like. Buy a freeze-dried soup mix from the spice aisle or dry your own. The sausage is optional but it doubles the weight and roughly doubles the calories, so decide before you pack, not at camp.
