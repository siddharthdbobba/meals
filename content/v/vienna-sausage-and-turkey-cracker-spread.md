This is a canned-meat spread built for a day with no fire and no cooler. Vienna sausages are already cooked and soft enough to mash with a spoon, so they act as the binder while the canned turkey adds bite and most of the protein. Together they hit a decent protein number for something that takes five minutes and no fuel.

Mayonnaise from packets is the part worth being deliberate about. A jar is a liability without refrigeration; single-serve packets are shelf-stable until you tear them and add the fat that canned meat lacks. Dried minced onion stands in for fresh and rehydrates on the moisture already in the mix. Make only what you will finish in one sitting, because mixed canned meat sitting warm in a pot is not something to carry into the afternoon.
