This started as a fresh sausage recipe with casings, a grinder, and a fridge rest. None of that travels. What survives the translation is the flavor logic: lean game meat, real fat, warm spice, and fruit that cuts the richness. Cooked loose in a pot, it does the same job at camp.

The pork fat is not optional. Venison is lean enough to go dry and chalky on its own, and the fat is where most of the calories live. Milk powder does what it does in the original — it binds and emulsifies, so the fat stays in the meat instead of pooling at the bottom of the pot. Dried blueberries replace frozen ones; they rehydrate in the residual heat and hold their shape as long as you add them off the heat.
