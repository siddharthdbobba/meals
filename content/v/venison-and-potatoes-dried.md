This is a home dehydrator recipe, not a store-bought one. The meat has to be cooked lean and blotted before it dries, because fat left in ground meat goes rancid in a pack within weeks. The oil packet puts the calories back at camp, where they are not sitting in a hot car for a month.

The flour is doing the work that a long stovetop reduction would do at home. Slurried into cold water first, it thickens 500 ml into gravy in about two minutes, which is all the fuel this deserves. Without it you get meat and potatoes floating in salty water.
