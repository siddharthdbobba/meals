The original is baked, which is a problem when the oven is a canister stove. A covered pot on low heat does the same job: the cheese on top melts from steam instead of dry heat, so nothing browns, but nothing scorches on the bottom either. Stir the cream cheese in off a near-dead flame or the milk solids catch.

Dried mushrooms are the swap that matters. Fresh mushrooms are mostly water and turn to slime in a cooler within two days, while an ounce of dried rehydrates to about the same volume and packs flat. Sour cream powder replaces the tub for the same reason. Cream cheese and shredded cheese still need cold, so this is a cooler recipe, not a backpacking one.
