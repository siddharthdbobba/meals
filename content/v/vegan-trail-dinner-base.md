Vegan trail dinners fail on two counts: not enough calories and not enough protein. This one solves both with pantry items rather than freeze-dried pouches. Bean flakes and quinoa flakes together land around 24 grams of protein, and both rehydrate in minutes instead of simmering through half a fuel canister.

The oil is not optional. Plant-based dry goods are lean, so without added fat this comes in near 500 calories, which is not a dinner after a long day. Stir it in off the heat so it coats everything instead of cooking off. Nutritional yeast does the job cheese would do in a non-vegan version, and it keeps the bag dry and shelf-stable.
