This is a way to carry the parts of vegetables you would otherwise throw away. Pepper tops, carrot ends, mushroom stems, and onion trim all dry fine in a dehydrator or a low oven, and once dry they weigh almost nothing and keep for months. The bouillon covers whatever your scrap mix lacks in flavor.

Cornmeal is the right base because it rehydrates the vegetables while it cooks, so nothing needs a separate soak. The stirring is not optional at camp stove heat: polenta scorches on a thin titanium pot bottom faster than it does at home. The oil goes in off the heat, where it stays as fat and calories instead of cooking off.
