Tomatillos give this the tang that tomato sauce would not, and canned ones are already soft, so the whole roasting step the original recipe calls for disappears. Canned green chiles do the same job for the bell pepper and jalapenos. That takes an hour of fire-roasting down to one skillet and about twenty-five minutes.

The cook-down before the eggs go in is the part not to rush. A thin sauce lets the eggs slide around and poach unevenly, and you end up with green soup. Cook it until a spoon leaves a track, then make the wells. Cover the pan for the eggs; over coals the heat comes from below only, and the whites will never set on top otherwise.
