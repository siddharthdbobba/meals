Chislic is a South Dakota bar snack that happens to suit a hunting camp perfectly: cubed meat, three seasonings, and a pot of hot fat. There is no batter, no sauce, and nothing to hold at temperature. That makes it one of the few deep-fried things worth doing at a picnic table.

The cooking is all about timing. Backstrap has almost no intramuscular fat, so the window between medium-rare and dry is maybe thirty seconds wide. Small batches keep the oil hot enough to brown the outside before the center overcooks. This one stays in the car-camping and basecamp column — you are carrying three cups of oil, a deep pot, and a cooler, none of which belong in a pack.
