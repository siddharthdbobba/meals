This is a van-kitchen dinner, not a backpacking one. It assumes a real burner, a real skillet, and a locker where potatoes and a salami stick can sit for a week without a cooler. Nothing in it needs refrigeration until the salami is cut.

The method is the whole recipe. Brown first, then steam under a lid, then dry the pan out again. Skipping the steam leaves you with cubes that are black outside and raw in the middle. The salami goes in last because its fat is the seasoning, and it turns to leather if it fries the whole time.
