Kofta is a good use for ground venison because the herb paste and the soaked bread do the work that fat normally does. Deer is lean enough that a plain patty turns to sawdust over coals. The bread holds water inside the meat, and two packed cups of parsley plus the mint keep it from tasting like a lean burger.

The spice list looks long but it is one premix made at home, and every item is a dry shelf-stable powder. Skewering matters more than it looks: a flat inch-thick sausage cooks through in the time the outside takes to brown, which is the whole trick with lean meat. This one needs a cooler and a grate, so it belongs at the truck or a basecamp, not in a pack.
