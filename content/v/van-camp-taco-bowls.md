This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal, so it keeps the fresh things a backpacking recipe has to give up. The structure is simple: one hot base of meat, beans, corn, and rice built in a single pan, and one cold pile of vegetables that never touches heat. Keeping them separate is the point. Cook the tomatoes and cucumber and you get a wet stew instead of a bowl with texture.

The precooked rice pouch is doing the real work. It needs no water, no simmer, and no second pot, which means one pan gets washed instead of two. Tinned beans and corn come drained so the pan does not turn to soup. If the cooler has been running warm for a few days, cook the meat first and early — everything else in here is shelf-stable and will wait.
