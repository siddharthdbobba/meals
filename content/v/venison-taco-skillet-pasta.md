This is a car-camp dinner because of what it needs: two burners' worth of heat, a colander moment, and a cooler holding cheese and dressing. It feeds eight or nine people off one skillet and one pot, which is the reason to cook it at a trailhead camp rather than at home.

The dressing goes in after the heat is off. Hit it while the pan is still hot and it breaks into oil and grit. Cheese is the opposite — it needs the residual heat from the pasta to melt, so add it before the dressing and stir. Shredded cabbage in place of lettuce is the one real trail change here; lettuce turns to slime by day two in a cooler and cabbage does not.
