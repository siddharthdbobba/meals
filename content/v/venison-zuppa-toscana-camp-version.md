The restaurant version leans on sliced potatoes and a cup of heavy cream, neither of which travels. Potato flakes do both jobs at once: they give the broth body and they dissolve in under a minute, so the pot is off the stove faster. Powdered cream carries the same fat without a carton in the cooler.

Venison is lean enough that the bacon is not optional. Its rendered fat is what the potato flakes and cream cling to, and without it the soup reads thin and watery. Keep the fat in the pot when the bacon crisps.
