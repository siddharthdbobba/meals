The original is a puff pastry appetizer for a party kitchen. Puff pastry is useless in a cooler, so this uses refrigerated biscuit dough instead. It browns rather than laminates, but it seals around the filling and takes dutch oven heat without a fuss.

The mushroom and shallot mix has to be cooked dry before it goes in. Wet filling steams the dough from the inside and you end up with raw dough around gray meat. Sear the venison fast and pull it early too. It finishes cooking inside the dough, and backstrap goes from rare to leather in about a minute.
