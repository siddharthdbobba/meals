This is a car camp or hunt camp dinner, not a pack meal. It is heavy, wet, and cooks for the better part of an hour, which is exactly the point when you have a fire going and nowhere to be. The original is a home soup; the changes here are ones that survive a cooler: bouillon cubes instead of a carton of stock, dried herbs instead of fresh, jarred garlic instead of a head.

The pasta goes in at the very end and only for ten minutes. Add it early and it drinks the broth, then keeps drinking it overnight until the leftovers are a solid brick. Two pounds of venison across six servings is what makes this a dinner rather than a soup; the beans carry the rest of the protein and thicken the pot as they break down.
