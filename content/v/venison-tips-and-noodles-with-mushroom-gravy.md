Two things make this work over a fire instead of a range. First, the meat is dredged and frozen at home, so camp prep is one bag and a hot pan. Second, the flour left in that bag is the gravy thickener, which means no separate roux ingredient and no extra container.

Venison is lean and turns to leather fast. Brown it in batches, pull it early, and let it finish in the gravy off the direct heat. Dried mushrooms are the trail swap for fresh: they weigh nothing, and their soaking water is a stronger base than the broth is.
