This started as a campervan dinner, which means it assumed a cooler, a sink, and a jar of sauce. None of that travels well past a trailhead, so the sauce here is tomato powder and the protein is red lentils. Both weigh almost nothing and neither one needs to stay cold.

The lentils are the part doing the quiet work. They dissolve while the pasta cooks and thicken the leftover water into something that coats, so you never drain the pot and never lose the starch. Oil goes in off the heat, where it stays as fat instead of cooking into the pan.
