The original is a Romanian veal stew: meat browned with onion, tomato, and paprika, then simmered with potatoes until the starch thickens the pot. Nothing about that needs an oven or a refrigerator. Freeze-dried beef and dehydrated potato dice rehydrate on the same twenty-minute clock, which is the whole reason this works as a one-pot camp dinner.

The paprika and tomato paste get bloomed in oil before the water goes in. That is not fussiness — dumping paprika into boiling water leaves you with red water and no flavor. The potatoes are the thickener; give them the full simmer and the stew comes together on its own. Freeze-dried beef is drier and stringier than veal, so the oil is doing real work carrying fat and mouthfeel.
