The cold-start soak is the whole trick. Dried cauliflower and peas are slow to come back, and dropping them into already-boiling water leaves you chewing gravel. Sitting in cold water while the stove heats gives them a head start, so everything is done at the same moment the noodles are.

The sausage goes in off the heat because it is already cooked and already smoked. Boiling it just leaches fat into the broth and toughens the slices. Cured sausage is also the only part of this that needs no home prep at all, which makes it the piece you buy on a resupply.
