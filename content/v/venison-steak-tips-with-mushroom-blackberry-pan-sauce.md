Venison is lean enough that overcooking it once ruins the whole pan. Everything here is built around getting the meat out early and finishing the sauce without it. The tips come out at rare, then go back in at the end for one minute, which carries them to medium in residual heat and nothing more.

The original is a home kitchen dish with fresh mushrooms and fresh berries. Dried versions of both do the same job at a fraction of the weight and no refrigeration, and their soaking water becomes the stock the sauce needs. Butter and frozen venison are the only cooler items, so this stays a car camping or basecamp meal. Take the pan off the flame before the bourbon goes in unless you want to explain your eyebrows.
