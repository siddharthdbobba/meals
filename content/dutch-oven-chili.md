Car camping is the one context where weight does not matter at all, and the correct
response is to cook something that would be absurd anywhere else. A 12 inch dutch
oven weighs 20 pounds and produces dinner for six with a single pot to wash.

Chili is the right first dutch oven recipe because it is nearly impossible to ruin
and it forgives an uneven fire.
