The banana peel is the cooking vessel, which means nothing to wash and nothing to
pack out but foil and a peel. It is also the rare campfire dessert that does not
require anyone to stand over the fire holding a stick.

Edge of the coals, not in them. Directly in the fire and you get a black banana
with cold chocolate inside.
