Plain instant oatmeal is about 150 calories and almost no protein, which is why it fails as a hiking breakfast. The fix here is stacking: milk powder and a protein scoop push this past 30 g of protein, and the pine nuts add fat that oats do not have on their own. That fat is what keeps it from burning off two miles down the trail.

Built as freezer bag cooking, so the pot only ever holds water and the bag is the bowl. Measure the water short. Oatmeal thickens as it sits, and it is easy to add more but impossible to take any back out.

No dietary tag beyond vegetarian: the milk powder rules out vegan and dairy-free, the oats are usually not certified gluten-free, and pine nuts are a nut.
