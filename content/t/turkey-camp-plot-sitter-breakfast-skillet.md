Turkey season means being on the field edge before light and back at the truck by mid-morning, hungry. This is the meal for the back-at-the-truck part: one pan, no cooler, nothing that spoils in a warm vehicle over a four-day weekend.

The rehydration step is what makes it work. Dried hash browns dumped straight into hot oil stay gritty in the middle, so they soak first and fry second. Draining matters just as much — wet potatoes steam instead of browning, and the crust is the whole point. Powdered eggs go in last because they set fast and turn rubbery if they cook alongside everything else.
