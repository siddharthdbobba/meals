This is a store-bought 72-hour kit treated as a trail menu rather than a shelf item. The whole point is the arithmetic: nine pouches, about 3.6 pounds, and roughly 12 cups of water across three days. That is a known quantity, which is why the kit gets used for shoulder-season trips and boat hauls where you do not want to think about food planning at all.

The number to watch is 1,706 calories a day. That is enough to sit out a storm and not enough to walk on, so the oil and the extra carbs in the variations are not garnish. Everything here rehydrates in the pouch, so there is no pot to scrub, and cold water works if the stove fails, which is the actual reason to carry freeze-dried food in the first place.
