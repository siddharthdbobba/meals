The original version pan-seared a fresh chicken breast and poured a can of soup over it. Neither travels. Dried soup mix does the same job at a fraction of the weight, and a foil pouch of chicken carries the protein without a cooler.

The lentils are the reason to simmer at all. They break down into the broth and give it body, so you end up with a stew that coats the spoon instead of thin soup that runs off it. They also add most of the fiber here. Keep the heat low and stir, or the lentils scorch and the whole pot tastes burnt.
