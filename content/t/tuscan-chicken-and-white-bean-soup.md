Dried beans are the problem this recipe solves. Fifteen minutes of hot water will not soften them, no matter how long you insulate the pot, and undersoaked beans are gritty. The cold soak earlier in the day does the hydrating, so the boil only has to heat the soup and finish the vegetables.

The milk powder is what makes it Tuscan rather than broth with things in it. It thickens slightly and rounds off the bouillon's salt. Use the lower-sodium bouillon at a full teaspoon; regular-strength at that dose makes the whole pot inedible, so cut it to half a cube. As written this is a light dinner at about 330 calories. Treat it as a first course or add the oil.
