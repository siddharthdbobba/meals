Instant couscous needs only boiling water and a short rest to become tender. Freeze-dried sausage and tomato powder provide savory depth without requiring fresh ingredients or refrigeration. One pot, one boil, then eat.

Stir the Parmesan in after removing heat so it stays visible instead of dissolving into the sauce. In cold weather or high altitude, wrapping the covered pot in an insulating cozy for the 15 minutes helps the couscous rehydrate fully.
