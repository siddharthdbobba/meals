The whole thing turns on water discipline. Rice needs a real simmer and a known amount of liquid; the beans and cheese need almost none, because instant bean flakes and freeze-dried cheese pull moisture out of anything nearby. Cook them together and you get glue. Cook the rice first, then let the residual heat handle the rest, and you get a filling that holds its shape in a tortilla instead of running out the end.

Start under on water. Adding a splash takes ten seconds; boiling off an extra half cup takes fuel you did not plan to spend. Fresh cheese works and tastes better, but it is a two-day ingredient at best in summer, so the freeze-dried version is what makes this a resupply-box meal rather than a first-night one.
