Instant rice, freeze-dried chicken, and freeze-dried vegetables all rehydrate in one simultaneous boil with no separate cooking stages. Everything hits the water at once and finishes when the rice is tender.

The freeze-dried chicken carries the protein and calories. If you use canned chicken, open it in camp and pour the broth into the pot to add flavor without carrying extra water.
