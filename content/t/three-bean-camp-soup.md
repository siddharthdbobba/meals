Whole dried beans are a non-starter on a stove. They want an hour of hard simmer and more fuel than the meal is worth. Instant bean flakes rehydrate in minutes, so the only thing here that actually needs time is the lentils, and even those give up after 25 minutes.

The dried vegetables go in early on purpose. They need the full simmer to stop being crunchy, and the sugars they leach out are most of what makes the broth taste like something other than salt. The oil at the end is not optional either. Beans and vegetables are almost all carbohydrate and protein, and without added fat this lands well short of a dinner's worth of calories.
