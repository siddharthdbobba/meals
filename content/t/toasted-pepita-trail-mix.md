Pepitas do most of the work. Hulled roasted pumpkin seeds run near half fat by weight and still carry real protein, which is unusual — most trail sugar comes with nothing attached, and most protein comes with a pot to wash. This gets both from one ingredient, and it is nut-free, which matters if you are sharing a bag with a group.

The home toast is the only step that is not optional. Raw pepitas taste flat and grassy, and they soften in the bag. Toasting drives off moisture, which sharpens the flavor and keeps the seeds crisp for a couple of weeks. Cool them fully before mixing or the fruit clumps into one brick by the second night.
