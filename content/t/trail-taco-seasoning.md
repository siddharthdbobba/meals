Store-bought taco packets are mostly salt, anticaking agents, and sugar, and they cost more per ounce than the spices inside them. Mixing your own takes two minutes at the kitchen counter and gives you a blend you can carry in a lip-balm-sized vial. Chili powder does the heavy lifting; cumin adds the earthy bottom note; paprika is there for color and body more than heat.

On the trail this earns its weight because it rescues cheap staples. Instant beans, couscous, and rice all cook fine and taste like nothing. A tablespoon of this turns a pot of them into a meal you will actually finish, which matters more than it sounds when you are 2,000 calories short. Add it off the heat or at the end of the boil, since chili powder burns fast and burnt chili powder is bitter all the way through.
