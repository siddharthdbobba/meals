This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Flank steak is lean and tough, and the soy-and-sugar marinade is doing two jobs: salt works into the muscle so it stays juicy over high heat, and the sugar caramelizes fast in a hot dutch oven. Ninety minutes is the floor. The drive to the campground usually covers it.

Two changes from a kitchen version. Fresh ginger and fresh pineapple are out, because a jar of ground ginger and a can of rings survive a weekend in a hot car and taste close enough. The pineapple goes in at the end and stays covered, so the acid and the steam finish the meat instead of drying it. Slice across the grain or the whole thing turns to rope.
