Instant is the only coffee method that costs nothing in weight, nothing in cleanup, and nothing in fuel beyond the water you were already heating. Grounds, filters, and presses all end up as wet sludge you carry out. Granules dissolve completely, so the pot rinses with a swirl.

Water temperature is the one variable worth caring about. A hard boil scorches instant and pulls out a burnt edge; pull the pot as the first bubbles break and the cup is noticeably rounder. The pinch of salt is an old diner trick and it works on the bitterness of budget granules better than sugar does.
