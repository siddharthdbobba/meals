This is a home chili built to be taken apart and put back together. Everything wet gets driven off in the dehydrator, and beans dry well because they are mostly starch — they come back close to where they started. The quinoa is doing double duty: it toasts up nutty in the pot at home, and on the trail it rehydrates faster than any bean it is sitting next to.

The volume measurement is the whole trick. Dried chili gives you no way to judge how much water it lost, so you measure before drying and carry the number. Add the powder to the pot first and pour water in after — go the other way and you end up with soup you cannot thicken back. Nothing here needs a fridge or a cooler, and there is no dairy in it to worry about.
