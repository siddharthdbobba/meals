A vacuum food jar is a slow cooker that weighs nothing to operate. Boiling water sealed into an insulated container stays hot enough for hours, and that is exactly the window fine couscous and split red lentils need to go soft without a stove. You spend five minutes of fuel in the morning and get a hot lunch with no cooking at midday.

Preheating matters more than people expect. Cold steel steals a lot of heat from the first pour, and below a certain temperature the lentils stall out half-hydrated. The oil goes in at the start rather than the end so it carries the cumin and paprika through the whole jar instead of sitting on top.
