The original is a slow cooker doing one job: hold chicken at a low enough temperature that it stays wet. There is no slow cooker on trail, so the rehydration does that work instead. Freeze-dried chicken pulls water back in over ten minutes off the heat, which is gentler than boiling it and gives you shreds rather than rubber.

Seasonings go in dry and premixed because garlic and onion powder need liquid and time to stop tasting like dust. Citrus peel and oil go in at the end. Heat kills the peel's aroma, and oil stirred in cold coats the rice instead of cooking off.
