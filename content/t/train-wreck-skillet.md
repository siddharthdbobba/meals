This is a Scout camp breakfast built on one principle: everything cooks in the fat that came before it. The bacon and sausage render first, the hash browns brown in that fat, and the eggs bind the whole thing together at the end. Cooked in that order in one skillet, there is nothing left to wash but the skillet and the holding pot.

The syrup is not a mistake. Salt and pork against something sweet is the same logic as pancakes with a side of bacon, just collapsed into one pan. This one stays in car-camping territory: eggs and raw pork need a cooler, and a full pound each of bacon and sausage is not backpacking weight. Below freezing, serve it fast — the pan holds heat far better than a plate does.
