The original was a slow cooker dish: raw chicken breast, canned soup, Greek yogurt, and canned green beans, four hours on high. None of that travels. The rework keeps the flavor logic and drops everything that needs a fridge or a wall outlet.

Stuffing mix is already dehydrated bread, which makes it the one grocery-store ingredient here that was designed for this. It rehydrates in five minutes off-heat and thickens the gravy on its own, so you never simmer and never scorch the bottom of the pot. Milk powder replaces the yogurt for the creaminess without the tang, and the fat has to be added deliberately or this comes out lean and dry.
