The source here was a container, not a dish: an insulated food jug that holds heat for about eight hours. That is a cooking method, so this recipe is built around it. Everything gets hot water once, in the morning, and then finishes on its own while you walk.

Instant couscous and crushed dehydrated chickpeas are the right pair for retained heat. Neither needs a boil, only time and a sealed jug. Preheating matters more than people expect. The oil is not optional either, since it is most of the calories and it keeps the couscous from packing into a brick by noon.
