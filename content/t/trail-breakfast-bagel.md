This is a day-one meal and nothing else. Cooked sausage, cheese, and an egg left unrefrigerated are fine for the first morning out and questionable after that, so eat it the first day and plan something shelf-stable for the rest of the trip.

The original assumes a jet-style stove with a pot support plus a light frying pan, and that is still the setup that works. A pot alone will not toast a bagel evenly. If you are only carrying a pot, cook the egg in it, warm the patty in it, and skip the toasting step. Raw eggs travel better than most people expect in the shell, and one carried in the middle of a bagel bag usually arrives intact.
