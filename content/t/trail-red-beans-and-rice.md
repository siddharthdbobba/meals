Instant rice and freeze-dried beans share a rehydration timeline: both need about 20 minutes in boiling water. The cumin is your entire flavor budget. It should be subtle, not dominant. Dried onion adds depth without requiring fresh ingredients or a knife in the field.

A teaspoon of oil carries its weight. Without it this is a nutritious, functional meal. With it, you have something you actually want to eat on mile 15 of a long hiking day.
