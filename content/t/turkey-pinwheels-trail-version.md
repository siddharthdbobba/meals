This started as a school lunch and needed two changes to work on trail. Refrigerated cream cheese becomes cream cheese powder, rehydrated with a couple tablespoons of water at the trailhead. Deli turkey becomes a shelf-stable pouch or chopped jerky. Everything else carries as-is.

The paste has to be thick. Too much water and it soaks into the tortilla, which then tears when you roll it. Add water slowly and stop while it still holds a shape on the spoon. Rolling tight matters for the same reason a burrito does: air pockets turn into a flattened mess by mile three.
