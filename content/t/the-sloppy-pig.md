This is a grilled ham and cheese that has been buttered past the point of restraint. Buttering both sides of both slices means the bread fries rather than toasts, and fried bread goes floppy under a hot filling. That is the whole point. The original name is a promise about texture.

The quarter cup of butter in the source is more than a two-slice sandwich can absorb, so two tablespoons is the working number. Keep the skillet over coals instead of flame: the cheese needs time to melt, and direct fire will blacken the bread before it does. Ham and cheese need a cooler, so this is a car-camping lunch. For a pack trip, use the summer sausage variation.
