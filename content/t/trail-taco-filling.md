Ground beef dehydrates badly unless you strip the fat first. Rinse the cooked crumbles under hot water, blot them, then dry them at 145F until they rattle. Fat left in the meat goes rancid in a pack, and it also blocks water from getting back into the protein at camp. The oil goes back in at the end instead, where it does the same job for calories without the storage problem.

The vegetables are here for bulk and to stretch a small amount of meat, which is the whole point of the original. Mushrooms and carrot rehydrate fast; the beef does not. That mismatch is why this simmers rather than steeps in a cozy. Ten minutes of low flame is the difference between taco filling and gravel.
