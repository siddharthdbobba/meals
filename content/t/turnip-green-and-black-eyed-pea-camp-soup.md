The original takes two hours because a ham hock has to give up its stock and turnip greens have to break down. Neither is happening on a canister stove. The workaround is to move all the slow parts home: dehydrate the greens so they rehydrate in twenty minutes instead of two hours, use precooked dried black eyed peas, and replace the hock with smoked paprika plus a hard smoked sausage that needs no refrigeration.

The tomato paste and the vinegar are doing more than they look like they are. The paste, cooked briefly in the oil, gives the broth color and a bottom note the bouillon cannot. The vinegar at the end is what keeps a pot of soft greens and starchy peas from tasting flat. Make it at home a day ahead, peppers in white wine vinegar, and carry it in a small dropper bottle.
