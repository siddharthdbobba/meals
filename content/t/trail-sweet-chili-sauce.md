Sweet chili sauce is a home-kitchen condiment that survives the trail better than most. It is sugar, acid, and heat, and all three keep on their own, so nothing here needs a fridge until it is made. The fresh garlic became dried minced garlic, which rehydrates in the boil and tastes close enough once the pepper flakes are in.

The cornstarch slurry is the only step with a way to go wrong. Dumped in dry it clumps, and clumps do not dissolve once the pot is hot. Mix it into cold water first, off the heat, then return the pot. Made in camp it keeps a day or two in a bottle; made at home it holds for months in the fridge and rides out for one trip.
