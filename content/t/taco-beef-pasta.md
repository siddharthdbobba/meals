Dehydrated pasta is pre-cooked at the factory and rehydrates in just twenty minutes of boiling. The freeze-dried corn adds sweetness and texture without adding weight. The beef packet is already seasoned and partially cooked; you're just rehydrating the whole assembly in one pot.

Cheese is essential. It's the main calorie source and what transforms this from grain soup into actual dinner. Hard cheese carries fine on cool trips; freeze-dried cheese works on warm trips and prevents spoilage.
