This is a lunch that survives the middle of a day when the stove is buried at the bottom of the pack. Tuna and beans together get you real protein without heat, and the beans do double duty as a binder once you mash a third of them against the side of the bag.

The original wants a cooler and fresh greens. Pouch tuna and jarred peppers make that unnecessary, so the only cooler item left is spinach, which is optional and only worth carrying on day one. Dressing premixed at home matters more than it sounds: measuring oil and lemon on a rock is how you lose both.
