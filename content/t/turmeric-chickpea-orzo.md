The original is a home soup with cans, fresh spinach, and a lemon. On trail it becomes a thick one-pot dinner instead: the orzo cooks in the broth and soaks up nearly all of it, so you are not carrying fuel to boil water you then eat as liquid. Freeze-dried chickpeas replace the cans, dried onion and garlic powder replace the sauté, and citric acid stands in for the lemon.

The acid at the end is the part people skip and shouldn't. Turmeric and cumin go flat and dusty without it. Black pepper matters for the same reason, and the olive oil carries the spices and adds the calories that orzo and chickpeas alone don't.
