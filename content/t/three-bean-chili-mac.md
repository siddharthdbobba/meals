Freeze-dried chili mac in a pouch is a fine thing, but it costs a lot per calorie and you are paying for the packaging. This builds the same dinner out of grocery store parts. The three beans are not decoration: whole freeze-dried beans give you something to chew, and the refried flakes dissolve into the sauce and do the thickening.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Tomato powder and bean flakes added during the boil will glue themselves to the pot bottom and burn. Added off heat, they thicken the water the pasta cooked in, so nothing gets drained and no calories go on the ground. The brown sugar is there to round off the tomato acid, not to make it sweet.

Wheat pasta means this is not gluten-free. Corn or rice pasta works but goes to mush faster, so cut the boil to five minutes.
