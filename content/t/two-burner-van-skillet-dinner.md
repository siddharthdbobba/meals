Two burners is the whole point of a van stove, so this recipe uses both at once. Rice takes fifteen unattended minutes at low heat; the sausage and beans take about ten of active attention. Run them in parallel and dinner lands in twenty-five minutes instead of forty.

Everything here is shelf-stable on purpose. Dry cured sausage renders enough fat to cook the spices in, which is where most of the flavor comes from — dumping powdered spice into liquid gets you a dusty broth instead. Tomato paste from a tube keeps after opening and binds the beans without adding the water a can of tomatoes would.
