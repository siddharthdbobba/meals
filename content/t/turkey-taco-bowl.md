The original is a fridge recipe: cooked ground meat, canned beans, a tub of queso, avocado on top. None of that travels. The swaps are one-for-one, though. Bean flakes are just refried beans with the water removed, instant rice rehydrates in the same pot, and cheese powder does what shredded cheddar and queso were doing without a cooler.

Beans are the structural ingredient. They turn what would be a wet pot of rice into something that holds a spoon, and they carry the fat from the oil instead of letting it pool. Add them after the rice, not before, or they set up before the grains soften. The chips go on at the end and only at the end.
