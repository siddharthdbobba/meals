The original is an oven bake with refrigerated pasta, a jar of sauce, and a broccoli-roasting step. None of that travels. Dried tortellini, dried broccoli, and tomato powder get you the same plate from one pot, and the pasta water becomes the sauce instead of going in the dirt.

Order matters. Broccoli needs a long soak and pasta needs a hard boil, so the broccoli goes in first and rides through the whole cook. The tomato powder goes in last, off the heat, because it scorches on the bottom of a pot and because it thickens what would otherwise be a bowl of pasta soup.
