The original is a fridge dish: fresh mozzarella balls, raw tomatoes, mayonnaise. None of that survives a day in a pack, so the wet parts get swapped for oil-packed ones. Sun-dried tomatoes and marinated artichokes bring the same acid and salt while sitting in the oil that keeps them safe, and a hard aged cheese does the mozzarella's job of giving you something to chew.

The mayonnaise is gone entirely. Pesto plus the oil off the tomatoes coats the pasta well enough, and it is one less thing to keep cold. Draining well matters: dried tortellini holds water in its folds, and that water thins the pesto into a puddle at the bottom of the pot. Let it steam off for a minute before the sauce goes in.
