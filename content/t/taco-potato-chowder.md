The original is a slow cooker soup: raw potatoes, fresh corn, chicken stock, and a cauliflower puree for body, all left to sit for hours. None of that travels. The rebuild splits the potato into two jobs. Dried diced potatoes simmer and give you something to chew on; instant flakes go in off the heat and do what the cauliflower sauce did, thickening the pot without any dairy.

Order matters more than technique here. Spices added at the boil go flat, and flakes added at the boil clump, so both wait until the stove is off. The oil is not optional — it replaces the bacon grease and the cream at once, and without it the whole thing reads as thin and salty.
