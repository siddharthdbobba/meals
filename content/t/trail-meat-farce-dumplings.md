A farce is just ground meat with something in it to hold water. The bread is the binder, and the milk powder replaces the cream the technique normally calls for. Powdered milk hydrates back into enough fat and protein to do the same job, and it rides in a dry bag instead of a cooler.

Mix past the point where it looks done. The meat has to go tacky, or the balls fall apart in the pot. Simmer, never boil, for the same reason. Raw ground meat means this one stays a cooler recipe, so cook it the first night or two out.
