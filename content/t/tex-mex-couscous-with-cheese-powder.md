Couscous was made for backpacking. It absorbs water in minutes and carries flavor better than rice. This version leans into the calorie density a hard day demands: cheese powder, powdered butter, and instant beans do the work of a full pot of beans-and-rice, but they cook in one pot and weigh less.

The dried tomatoes give the dish some sharpness that cuts through all the fat and salt. The spices are Tex-Mex basics—chili, paprika, garlic—kept mild so the cheese stays the main event. Premix everything at home in a bag so you're not measuring five things by headlamp.
