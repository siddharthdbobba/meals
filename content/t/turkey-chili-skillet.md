The forum version of this starts with browning ground turkey in a pan, which needs a fridge and a sink. Neither exists in camp, so the browning happens at home: cook the meat, press the fat out, and dry it until it rattles. Fat is what turns rancid, so getting it out is the whole point of the exercise, and the tablespoon of oil at the end puts the calories back where you control them.

The rest is timing. Turkey takes the longest to come back, so it goes in first and alone; rice and bean flakes only need residual heat. Tomato powder does the work a can of sauce would, without carrying the can or the water in it.
