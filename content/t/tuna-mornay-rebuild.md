The original is a home dish built on cans and a fry pan. The rebuild moves everything to powder and one pot. Freeze-dried peas and corn go in with the pasta because they need the same boiling water the pasta already requires, so there is no second stage and no draining step.

Milk powder next to the sauce sachet is what keeps it from tasting thin. Add the powders off the heat, not into a rolling boil, or the starch grabs and you get lumps around a hot pot in the dark. The mustard and lemon are there for the same reason a home cook adds them: dairy powder and tuna both go flat without an edge.
