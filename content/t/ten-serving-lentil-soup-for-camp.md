Lentils are the cheapest hot dinner you can carry that still counts as protein. They need no soak and they hold their shape through a long simmer, which is what makes them workable on a fire where the heat is inconsistent.

The original wants a fresh onion, carrots, and garlic cloves. Dried versions of all three do the same job here and skip the cooler entirely, so the only wet thing you carry is the tomato can. Give the dried vegetables their two minutes in the oil at the start anyway. That step is where the sweetness comes from, and it is gone if you just dump everything into the water at once.
