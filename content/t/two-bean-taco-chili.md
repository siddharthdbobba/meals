This started as a skillet taco-chili with sour cream, lettuce, and tomato on top. None of that travels. The beef gets dehydrated after cooking and rinsing, the canned tomato sauce becomes tomato powder, and the garnishes become crushed corn chips, which do the same job of adding crunch and fat without needing a cooler.

Rehydrating beans is the whole problem here. Home-cooked or canned beans that have been dried will come back, but only with time, so most of the twelve minutes is the pot sitting off the flame with a lid on. Boiling harder does not speed it up and only burns fuel. Adding the oil at the end keeps it from separating out during the simmer.
