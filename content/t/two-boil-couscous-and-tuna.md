Couscous is the only grain that asks for no simmer time. It is pre-steamed semolina, so hot water and a closed lid finish it in five minutes. That matters more than it sounds: you spend one boil of fuel on a full dinner, which is roughly a third of what rice or lentils would cost you.

The fat is not optional. Couscous and tuna together are lean and land at maybe 450 calories, which is a snack after a long day. The oil roughly doubles it and carries the oregano and garlic through the whole pot. Sun-dried tomatoes rehydrate in the boiling water and add the acid the dish otherwise lacks; if you skip them, the lemon powder does similar work for less weight.
