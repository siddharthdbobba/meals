This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal. Cubed potatoes and raw poultry need cold storage, so it belongs at a truck camp or a hunt camp with a fire ring, not in a bear canister.

The order matters more than anything else here. Sausage first because its fat is what the turkey browns in, and wild turkey breast is lean enough that it will go dry if you leave it in while the potatoes finish. Pulling the meat out and returning it at the end is the difference between tender and chalky. The potatoes need the pan to themselves and need to be left alone long enough to crust, which is the one part of this that takes patience.
