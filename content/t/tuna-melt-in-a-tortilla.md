The original is a diner tuna melt on buttered sourdough with celery, red onion, and a sliced tomato. Every one of those is a refrigerator item or a crushable item, so they got swapped: dried minced onion for the red onion, sun-dried tomatoes for the fresh slices, a hard aged cheddar for the deli slices, and oil for the butter. Celery is gone entirely. It was there for crunch, and nothing shelf-stable does crunch after four minutes in a hot pan.

Tortillas replace bread because bread arrives at camp as crumbs. Folded in half over the filling, a tortilla griddles into something close to the same thing: crisp outside, melted cheese, cold-mixed tuna warmed through. No water goes into this recipe at all, which matters on a dry camp — the whole meal costs one oiled pan and one spoon.
