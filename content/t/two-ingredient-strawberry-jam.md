The original is a home-canning recipe: a kilo of fresh strawberries cooked down for an hour until the water boils off. On trail you cannot carry a kilo of wet fruit, and boiling water away is the opposite of what you want with a fuel canister. So the process runs backwards here. The fruit arrives already dehydrated and you add back only the 120 ml it needs.

Sugar is what makes conventional jam set and keeps it from sticking. With stevia or erythritol you get neither, which is why the source notes the pot stays clean and why this stays loose. The chia and the cooling time do the thickening instead. Add the sweetener off the heat, as the source does, so you can taste before committing.
