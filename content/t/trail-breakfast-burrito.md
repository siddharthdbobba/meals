The original is a freezer-and-microwave recipe, which is no help three days from a road. Everything perishable here has a dry counterpart: powdered whole egg, dehydrated hash browns instead of tater tots, freeze-dried peppers and onions, and precooked sausage crumbles that keep without a cooler. Hard cheddar is the one thing that stays as-is, because a dense aged wedge is fine for days in a pack.

Order matters more than technique. The vegetables and potatoes need their soaking water, the eggs need theirs, and if you dump everything in together you get scrambled eggs sitting in a puddle. Brown the potatoes first, add the eggs last, and take the pan off the flame while they still look slightly wet.
