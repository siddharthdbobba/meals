The source of this one is a tandoori marinade: yogurt, chickpea flour, and a long list of ground spices smeared on soy chaap and put over coals. None of that survives a pack. Yogurt is fridge food, and nobody is carrying a tandoor. So the marinade becomes a dry spice mix and the char becomes a simmer.

The chickpea flour is the piece worth keeping. In the original it thickens the yogurt so the coating clings; here it does the same job to the cooking water, turning what would be spiced soup into something that coats the soy. Toast it at home or it tastes raw and chalky. The chaat masala at the end is the other thing doing real work — its sourness and black salt stand in for the lemon juice the original finishes with, and it dies if you boil it.
