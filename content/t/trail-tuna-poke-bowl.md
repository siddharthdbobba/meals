The original is a rice bowl built on fresh cucumber and refrigerated mayo. Neither travels, so the cucumber is gone and the mayo comes from packets, which are shelf-stable and portioned so you are not carrying an open jar. Dried scallion stands in for fresh, and the nori does the work the cucumber was doing: something dry and sharp on top of something soft.

The acid matters more than it looks. Vinegar and lime cut the mayo, which otherwise turns the whole pot into one flat, fatty note. Mix the tuna in a separate cup rather than the pot so the rice stays intact instead of getting stirred into paste.
