Three instant starches doing three different jobs. Stuffing brings bread and herbs, potato flakes thicken everything into something a spoon can carry, and the gravy powder ties the two together so it does not read as two separate side dishes sharing a bag. The cranberries are the only acid in the meal and they matter more than their weight suggests.

Water is the thing to get right. Barely covering the mix is correct, because the flakes will keep pulling moisture for another thirty seconds after you think you added too little. Overshoot and there is no fixing it short of adding more dry mix you probably did not pack. The oil is optional on paper and mandatory in practice on a cold night.
