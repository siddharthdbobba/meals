Chili dehydrates better than almost anything else because it is already broken down. Ground meat, beans, and tomato paste all rehydrate evenly, and the paste is what makes the reconstituted version thick instead of watery. The original is a stovetop pot with fresh onion and jalapeno; both dry fine, so they stay.

Two changes matter. Lean turkey only, and drain the fat aggressively, because fat left in dried meat turns rancid in weeks. And the toppings the home version leans on, sour cream and shredded cheese, are gone. That costs calories, so the servings are lighter than they look on the plate. Carry olive oil or corn chips if you need to make up the difference.
