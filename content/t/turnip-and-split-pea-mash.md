This started as a list of things planted in a field: turnips, rape, winter peas, oats, sunflowers. None of that is trail food on its own, so the crops got dried and reassembled. Turnip is the reason it takes ten minutes instead of five — dense root, slow to take water back on. Cut it small at home and the wait is bearable.

The peas and oats are doing the structural work. Pea powder brings protein and body; oats keep it from separating into vegetable chunks sitting in broth. Oil and sunflower seeds are the calories. Leave them out and this is a side dish, not dinner.
