There is no butter, no egg, and no milk in this, which is the whole point. Fat is what turns a baked good rancid and crumbly in a pack. Without it the loaf is dense and slightly chewy, closer to a fruit brick than a cake, and it survives being sat on.

The overnight soak is not optional. Dry fruit in the batter pulls water out of the flour while it bakes and you end up with something dry and tough. Soaked fruit brings its own moisture in, which is also why the loaf stays edible for a week without refrigeration. Bake it at home; nothing about this works on a stove.
