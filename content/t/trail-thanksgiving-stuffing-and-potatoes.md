Thanksgiving works on trail because almost every part of it is already dry food. Bread crumbs, potato flakes, and a seasoning blend are shelf-stable by nature, so the only real home prep is drying the bread hard enough that it does not mold in your pack.

The timing is the whole trick. Stuffing and potatoes rehydrate in their bags with no heat under them, which frees the pot for the gravy. Bread takes less water than you expect, so measure it rather than eyeballing, or you get paste. The gravy is what ties the two bags together, and the pouch chicken is where the protein comes from.
