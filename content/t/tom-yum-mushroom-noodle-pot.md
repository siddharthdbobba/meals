The original is built on a shrimp stock made from heads and shells, which is not a thing you carry. The dried mushrooms, lemongrass, galangal, and lime leaves do the same job: eight minutes of hard simmering pulls enough out of them to make water taste like something. Skip that step and you get curry paste in hot water.

Order matters at the end. Coconut milk powder clumps in boiling liquid, so it goes in after the simmer with the heat low. Lime juice goes in dead last, off the flame, because heat flattens the sour edge that makes this soup work.
