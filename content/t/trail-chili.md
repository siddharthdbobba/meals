Canned chili is mostly water, and water is the one ingredient already at camp. So the cans come out: tomato powder and sun-dried bits for the tomato sauce and paste, bean flakes for the drained kidney beans, dried onion for the sauté. What is left is a spice mix, some protein, and a bag you can carry for a week.

The simmer is not optional. Chili powder and cumin taste raw and dusty until they have been wet and hot for a while, and the bean flakes need a few minutes to stop being powder. The sugar is doing real work too — tomato powder is sharper than canned sauce, and a tablespoon rounds it off. Oil goes in off the heat because that is where most of the calories are hiding.
