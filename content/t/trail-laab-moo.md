Laab is normally a salad: hot meat tossed with raw herbs and a sour dressing, eaten cold-ish. That does not survive a pack, so this version becomes a skillet-in-a-pot instead. The shallot, green onion, mint, and lime all go in dried, and the boiling water does the rehydrating that a fresh kitchen would not need.

The toasted rice powder is the part worth carrying. Grind it at home, keep it dry, and add it off the heat. It soaks up the fish sauce and lime so the dish clings to the meat rather than pooling in the bottom of the pot. Without it you have salty wet pork. Iceberg lettuce is gone for obvious reasons; the dried long beans do the job of something crunchy on the side.
