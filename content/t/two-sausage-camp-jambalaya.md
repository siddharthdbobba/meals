This is a grill-grate dinner, not a stove one. Two pounds of sausage renders a lot of fat, and that fat is what carries the tomato and the celery into the rice — a narrow backpacking pot with a screaming hot burner under it will scorch the bottom before the grains hydrate. Coals give you the low, even heat that a covered rice pot needs.

The liquid ratio is the part to respect. One cup of rice against a 28-ounce can of tomatoes plus a cup of water looks like too much, but the tomatoes are mostly solids and the pot loses steam every time you lift the lid. So don't lift it. The ten minutes off the heat at the end is not optional; that's when the rice goes from crunchy to done.
