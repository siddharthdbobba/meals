The original is a pressure cooker recipe, and pressure is what makes root vegetables give up in twenty minutes. On a stove at altitude you do not have that, so the vegetables are dried thin at home instead. Thin slices rehydrate in the ten minutes the beef needs anyway.

Tomato paste becomes tomato powder, broth becomes bouillon, and the frozen peas become freeze-dried ones that only need residual heat. The potato flakes are new. A pressure cooker reduces liquid and thickens on its own; a backpacking pot just boils, so the flakes do that job at the end. Add them slowly, because they are hard to take back out.
