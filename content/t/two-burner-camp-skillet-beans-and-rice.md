Two burners change what is worth cooking in camp. Rice needs fifteen quiet minutes of low heat and nothing else, so it occupies the flame you are not watching while the real work happens in the skillet. Run it on one burner and you are waiting twice.

Everything here is dried on purpose. Bean flakes rehydrate in two minutes and carry a fraction of the weight of cans, tomato powder replaces a jar you would otherwise haul out and back, and cured sausage does not care about temperature. The oil is not optional. It is where most of the calories live, and it keeps the bean paste from setting up like plaster.
