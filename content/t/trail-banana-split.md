The original is a bowl of fresh yogurt and fruit, which is a refrigerator recipe. Powdered yogurt rehydrated with a splash of cold water gets close enough, and freeze-dried berries replace fresh ones without the bruising. Everything else already travels.

The banana is the reason to carry this on day one and not day four. It is the plate, the bulk, and the only thing here that will not last. Use the peel as the dish and there is nothing to wash.
