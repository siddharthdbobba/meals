The original is a pressure cooker recipe: whole pork tenderloin, eight hours or thirty minutes under pressure, then shredded into a sauce. Freeze-dried shredded pork gets you to the same place with one boil, because the shredding already happened. Lettuce does not travel, so tortillas do the wrapping.

Cornstarch is the piece people skip and shouldn't. Powdered peanut butter rehydrates thin, and without a thickener the sauce runs out the end of the wrap. One teaspoon and a minute of simmering is the difference between a wrap and a mess. The vinegar and oil go in last for the same reason they do at home: heat flattens the acid, and the oil is carrying most of the calories.
