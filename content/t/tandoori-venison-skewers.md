This one stays a cooler recipe on purpose. Yogurt is the whole mechanism: the acid loosens the surface of a lean muscle so it takes spice, and the fat keeps the outside from drying out over a fire that hot. Swap it for a shelf-stable substitute and you lose most of that, which is why the powdered-milk version in the variations gets an hour and a lemon instead of a straight trade.

Heat and time are the only real decisions. Venison carries almost no intramarbling, so it goes from rare to dry in about ninety seconds of inattention. Big fire, small pieces, four short turns, off the grate. Marinate longer for cuts off the leg or neck and shorter for backstrap, which does not need the help.
