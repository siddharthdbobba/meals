The original is a freezer jam, which assumes a freezer. Out here nothing keeps, so this is built as a single-day batch: dehydrated fruit rehydrated into a spread and eaten before it can spoil. Freeze-dried strawberries carry almost no weight and rehydrate fast, and because they absorb most of the water you add, they do half the thickening on their own.

Gelatin does the rest. A teaspoon in 90 ml is enough to give body without turning the mug into jello. Dried lemon peel and citric acid replace the fresh lemon, which was there for tartness and pectin rather than lemon flavor. Honey covers sweetness and keeps the spread from tasting flat. Spread it on tortillas, stir it into oats, or eat it off the spoon.
