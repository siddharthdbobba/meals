This is a kitchen recipe carried to camp rather than cooked there. The syrup is a one-to-one sugar and water solution, which is concentrated enough to be shelf-stable and to survive days in a pack without a cooler. All the work happens on a home stove.

Toasting and blending are the two steps that matter. Whole nuts dumped in a pot give up very little; breaking them open after they have softened exposes far more surface to the syrup, and the flavor difference is not subtle. The salt goes in off the heat, where it sharpens the sugar instead of dissolving into the background.
