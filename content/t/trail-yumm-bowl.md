The whole dish is a home-dehydrator project, and there is no way around that. Rice can be bought instant, but the beans, salsa, and olives have to be dried yourself or bought freeze-dried in bulk. Dry the beans at medium heat for four to six hours. Cheese goes at 95F or lower, or it turns into a single orange disc; blot the oil off before bagging it.

The long covered soak is where this actually cooks. Dried beans and salsa rehydrate slowly, and the fifteen minutes off heat does more than any additional simmering would, without burning fuel. The sauce goes in cold at the very end because heat splits it. It is the one item that will not keep for a long trip — figure a few days out from a resupply, and skip it past that.
