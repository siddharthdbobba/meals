The store packet is mostly the same seven spices plus filler, and it costs several times as much per pot. Mixing your own at home also means you control the salt, which matters when the rest of the meal is already salty instant food.

The trail version leans on the blend doing all the flavor work, because the base is instant refried bean flakes and tomato powder rather than simmered ground beef and canned tomatoes. Those rehydrate in minutes off the heat, so you spend one boil instead of twenty minutes of fuel. Two tablespoons of blend per serving is heavier-handed than the pound-of-beef ratio; dried starch swallows spice.
