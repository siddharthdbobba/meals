Powdered egg has a bad reputation, most of it earned by people cooking it too hot. It sets faster than a fresh egg and turns rubbery the second the pan gets away from you. Low flame, one minute undisturbed, then move it. Reconstituting in cold water first matters too: hot water cooks the powder into grit before it ever reaches the pan.

The cheese and oil are the calories. Egg powder alone is close to pure protein and will not carry a morning of riding. The tortilla makes it portable, which means you can be moving while you eat instead of standing over a pan waiting for it to cool.
