Blending isn't an option on trail. Rice and lentils absorb the broth as they cook, turning thin stock into something with body. The spices—curry, cumin, turmeric—are the only luxuries, but they're necessary on cold nights.

Rice and lentils are load-bearing ingredients here. They expand with water and turn a simple broth into dinner. Olive oil is where the calories actually go.
