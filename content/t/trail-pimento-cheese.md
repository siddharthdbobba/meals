Pimento cheese at home is cream cheese, mayonnaise, and grated cheddar beaten together and chilled. None of that travels. This version rebuilds the same thing from powders: cheddar cheese powder for the sharpness, cream cheese powder for the tang and body, powdered mayonnaise for the fat that makes it a spread rather than a paste.

The water amount matters more than the ratios. Go in under 60 ml and add the rest slowly, because the powders drink for several minutes after they look finished. Too much water and you have cheese soup with no way to fix it. The pimentos need to be squeezed dry first for the same reason.
