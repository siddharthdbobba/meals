The original is a stovetop soup built on frozen vegetables and refrigerated pasta, neither of which survives a pack. Dried tortellini and freeze-dried vegetables do the same job with no cooler, and they rehydrate in the same pot of broth that becomes the soup. Nothing is drained, so nothing is wasted.

Sequence matters more than the ingredient list. Vegetables go in first because they need the water more than the pasta does, and tomato powder early gives the broth time to stop tasting like dust. Dried tortellini takes real time, closer to twelve minutes than the two a fresh one needs, so plan the fuel for it. The oil goes in off the heat, where it stays as fat instead of boiling off into the broth.
