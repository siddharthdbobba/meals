This is a recipe for the night the power is out or the stove will not light. Everything in it is shelf-stable, opens with a hand can opener, and cooks over coals in one pot. Nothing needs a fridge and nothing needs a plug.

Instant rice is the part that matters. Raw rice wants twenty minutes of steady simmer, which is hard to hold over an uneven fire without scorching the bottom. Parboiled rice only needs the pot to be hot when it goes in, so the fire does the easy half of the work and the lid does the rest. The canned bean liquid is doing real work too: it carries starch, and it turns a pot of soup into something that stays on a spoon.
