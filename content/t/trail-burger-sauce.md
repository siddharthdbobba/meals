The original is a fridge recipe built on jarred mayonnaise, which is a problem three days from a road. Powdered mayo mix plus water rebuilds the base close enough, and vinegar powder carries the tang without a bottle sloshing in your pack. Everything else in the list was already a dry spice.

Hydrate slowly. Powdered mayo grabs water fast and goes thin before you notice, and thin sauce runs off a burger and onto your hands. The rest time is not optional either: dried relish needs a few minutes to soften, and mustard powder tastes sharp until it sits.
