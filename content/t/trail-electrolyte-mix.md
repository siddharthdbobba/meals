This is the thing the gear roundups are selling, minus the sachet. The ratio is what matters: sodium in the 500 to 700 mg range per liter, a smaller hit of potassium, and enough simple sugar that the water actually moves out of your gut instead of sitting there. Salt alone tastes like seawater and absorbs slowly. Sugar alone leaves you drinking sweet water while your sodium keeps dropping.

Citric acid does the flavor work. Without it the salt sits right on top and most people quit after two sips, which defeats the point. Mix it into a full bottle, not a half-full one, or the concentration gets unpleasant. Shake rather than stir, since cold filtered water dissolves sugar slowly and the last of it otherwise settles into a syrup at the bottom.
