Home chili simmers thirty minutes because raw beef and canned tomatoes need that long to turn into one thing. On trail nothing here is raw, so the time collapses. The rehydration is the only real wait, and it happens off the stove with the lid on, which saves fuel.

Tomato powder does the work of both the canned tomatoes and the paste without carrying their water. Bean flakes replace the drained can and double as the thickener, so you can dial the texture by how much you add rather than by boiling liquid off. Sour cream and cheese don't travel, so the chips carry the salt and fat instead.
