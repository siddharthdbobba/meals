Every part of a margarita that needs refrigeration has been removed. What remains is acid, sugar, salt, and alcohol, which is most of what the drink was doing anyway. The powdered mix supplies the lime and the sweetness in a form that weighs almost nothing and cannot leak.

Salt is not garnish here. Sweet drinks taste flat and cloying when you are dehydrated, and a quarter teaspoon stirred into the body of the drink fixes that and replaces some of what you sweated out. Cold water matters more than you would think, since there is no ice to do the work; a creek-chilled bottle is the difference between this and warm syrup. Carry the liquor separately and account for the weight honestly before you commit to it.
