Freeze-dried meat delivers substantial protein in minimal weight. When rehydrated in boiling water, it becomes tender and complete, so a half-cup portion of each meat actually fills you. The rice would be bland and thin without the broth powder, butter, and oil—those three ingredients are where the meal gets body and salt.

In cold weather or at altitude where water doesn't boil as vigorously, cover the pot with an insulator (a pot cozy, an extra down layer, even a sleeping bag) while it sits. The retained heat ensures the rice and meat fully rehydrate. The 15-minute sitting time isn't optional—it's when the cooking actually happens.
