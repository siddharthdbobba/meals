The source version starts with frozen raw beef, which only works on a day hike out of a cooler. Canned beef does the same job with no temperature risk and no cooking time, so the pot only has to get hot, not cook anything through.

Whole canned tomatoes are worth the extra crushing. Diced tomatoes are treated with calcium chloride to hold their shape, so they stay in cubes no matter how long the pot simmers. Whole ones collapse into broth in about ten minutes, which is the whole point here.
