The original is chicken on the bone, marinated overnight in fridge-cold yogurt and finished in an oven. None of that travels. What does travel is the spice blend, which is where the flavor actually lives, so this version keeps the ratios and swaps everything else. Powdered yogurt plus lemon powder plus a little water rebuilds the acidic, slightly tangy coating that the marinade provided. Pouch chicken is already cooked, so the fire is only there to dry the paste onto the meat and char it a bit.

Skip the marinating wait and you get a wet spice sauce instead of a coating. Five minutes of hydration is the minimum. Keep the heat moderate too: the spices in this blend scorch fast, and burnt garam masala tastes like ash all the way through the pot.
