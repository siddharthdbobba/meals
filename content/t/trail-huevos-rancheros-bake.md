The original is an oven casserole with fresh tomatoes, chiles, cilantro, and a blender. None of that survives a weekend in a pack, so the sauce here is tomato powder and dried aromatics rehydrated in hot water. Same cumin, thyme, and oregano doing the work, no blending and no produce to bruise.

The layers matter more than they look. Tortillas on the bottom soak up sauce and keep the beans off the iron, which is where things scorch. The egg goes on last and unbaked so it sets into a lid instead of scrambling into the beans. Cheese is the only thing here that will not last a long trip; on anything past two nights, use a hard aged cheese and accept that it will not melt the same way.
