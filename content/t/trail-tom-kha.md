The original is a pressure-cooker recipe with fresh onion, peppers, and a block of tofu that has to be pressed under a stack of cast iron for twenty minutes. None of that survives a pack. Dried vegetables and freeze-dried tofu rehydrate in the same pot of boiling water, and they skip the pressing entirely because they are already dry and will take on whatever flavor the water is carrying.

Coconut milk powder replaces the can, which is the real weight savings and the reason this works at all. It goes in off the heat: the fat separates and clumps if you boil it. Fish sauce is gone, so this stays vegetarian, but check the curry paste anyway since some brands include shrimp. The sugar, lime, and salt are doing balance work, not sweetening it. Skip the lime and it tastes flat and heavy.
