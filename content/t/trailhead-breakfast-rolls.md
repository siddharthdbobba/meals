  - "Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt, and milk powder in a bowl or a large zip bag. Keep the salt away from the yeast until the flour is stirred through."
  - "Add warm water a little at a time and work it into a shaggy dough, then knead in the softened butter. Ten minutes of kneading on a clean lid or cutting board."

Camp bread usually fails in one of two directions: too crusty to bite through, or so soft it collapses under a hot filling. Enriching the dough with milk powder and butter is what splits the difference. The fat coats the gluten strands and keeps the crumb tender, while bread flour gives it enough structure to survive being loaded with grease and egg yolk.

The original recipe uses warm milk, which is not a thing you carry. Whole milk powder mixed into the dry blend does the same job and rides in the same bag as the flour. The rise is the only part that takes patience, and it is mostly waiting, not work. Bake them the night before if you want breakfast to be fast.
