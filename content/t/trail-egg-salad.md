Egg salad is a refrigerator recipe, so the trail version has to rebuild it from powder. Whole egg powder cooked into curds and then broken apart gives you roughly the texture of chopped hard-boiled egg, which is the only thing the dish really needs from the eggs. Cooling matters more than it looks: mayo folded into hot curds turns greasy and separates.

The rest is substitution. Fresh celery and green onion become dried flakes rehydrated in a splash of the same hot water, which gets back most of the flavor and none of the crunch. Mayonnaise and dijon travel as condiment packets, which are shelf-stable, portioned, and free at most delis. Salt lightly at first, since the packets already carry some.
