Toasting is the whole point. Raw cornmeal in cold water tastes like paste; toasted cornmeal tastes nutty and mixes without that chalky edge. Do it at home in a dry skillet and it costs you nothing on trail, since nothing here needs heat once you are walking.

The chia is structural, not decorative. It swells and holds the corn in suspension so the drink does not separate into water on top and sludge at the bottom in the first two minutes. Ten minutes of soak time is the difference between grit and something you can actually finish. Sugar and salt are there because plain corn slurry is hard to drink in volume, and volume is the point.
