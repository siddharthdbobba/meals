The point of this one is the second cooking. Meat that was already braised soft has no water left to fight you, so it hits hot oil and crisps on the outside while staying tender inside. That is why the pouch of leftover roast beats fresh ground meat here. Ground meat just browns and goes grey.

The two rules that break this dish are wet vegetables and a crowded pot. Rehydrate first, then drain hard, then fry. If you dump soaking water and vegetables into oil together you get a stew, not a hash. Cook it in two rounds if your pot is small. The herbs, lemon, and mayo all go on at the end because heat kills the first two and splits the third.
