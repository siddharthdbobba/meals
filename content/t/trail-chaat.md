Hemp hearts make this salad protein-rich and shelf-stable. Tamarind brings the distinctive flavor—sour, assertive, and weightless—while cumin, coriander, and ginger establish the chaat character. Rehydrate in cold water for ten minutes and you have a textured salad that works as a light lunch or as a side dish at camp.

Tamarind is essential here. It delivers flavor without volume or refrigeration, and cuts through the richness of the hemp hearts. Lime powder adds brightness that cold water alone cannot provide, and coconut sugar softens the sharpness. Everything keeps indefinitely on the shelf, and nothing in this recipe requires heat to taste right.
