This started as a kids' party dessert made in a 9x13 pan, which is not a trail recipe. The adaptation is that all the cooking happens in your kitchen and the trail only sees a wrapped bar. Marshmallow and cereal are already shelf-stable, the butter is bound up in the sugar, and nothing here needs a cooler.

Press harder than feels reasonable. A loosely packed bar shatters into cereal dust by the second day, and dust at the bottom of a food bag is not dessert. The two colors are cosmetic. The candy eyes fall off eventually and that is fine.
