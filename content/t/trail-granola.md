This is a home granola recipe that already suits the trail, so nothing had to be substituted out. Every ingredient is shelf-stable, there is no dairy anywhere in it, and coconut oil holds up in heat better than butter would. The only trail change is portioning: bagged by serving at home so you are not measuring anything in camp.

The 250°F bake matters more than it looks. Higher heat browns the sugar before the oats dry out, and granola that is not dry goes stale in a food bag within days. The oil and syrup coat the oats so clusters form on cooling, and the dried fruit goes in after baking because an hour in the oven turns raisins into gravel.
