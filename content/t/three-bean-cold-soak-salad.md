The original is a picnic salad built on three tins and a fridge. Tins are dead weight and the fridge does not exist out here, so the beans go in dry and the water goes in at breakfast. Four hours of walking does the rehydrating for free.

The dressing goes in last, after you drain. Add it early and the oil coats the beans before the water gets in, and you end up with dry centers. Vinegar and mustard also slow rehydration, which is the same problem from the other direction.
