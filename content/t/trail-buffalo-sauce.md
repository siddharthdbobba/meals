Buffalo sauce is just hot sauce cut with fat. The fat does two things: it takes the vinegar edge off so you can eat more of it, and it makes the sauce cling instead of running off. That is the whole mechanism.

The home version uses melted butter, which does not travel. Ghee is butter with the water and milk solids cooked out, so it sits in a pack for weeks without turning. Butter powder is the lighter option and rehydrates straight into the hot sauce, no melting step. Either way, mix off the heat. Boiling this just drives off the vinegar and leaves you with hot grease.
