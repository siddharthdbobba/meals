The original is a van kitchen dish built on fresh onion, tomato, and a fridge. None of that travels, so the aromatics come out of a spice bag instead: dried onion and garlic powder do the base, tomato powder does the acid and body. Red lentils are the reason this works at all. They need no soaking and give up their structure in about ten minutes, which is roughly one small canister's worth of simmer.

The rice goes in off the heat because it only needs steam, and cooking it alongside the lentils would just mean more fuel and a scorched pot. The oil and the lemon powder go in last for the same reason they would in a real kitchen. Fat carries the spice, acid keeps a pot of lentils from tasting flat and heavy at the end of a long day.
