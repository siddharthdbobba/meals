Boxed stuffing is already dehydrated bread with seasoning attached, which makes it one of the few grocery-aisle items that needs no adaptation for the trail. It takes hot water and time, not simmering, so the stove runs for one boil and then stops. Butter powder goes in before the bread does, because fat spread through the water coats every crumb instead of sitting in a slick on top.

The popcorn is not cooked with anything. It is there for texture, since stuffing rehydrates into a uniform soft mass and a bowl of that gets dull halfway down. Crush it in at the last second. Any earlier and it goes limp.
