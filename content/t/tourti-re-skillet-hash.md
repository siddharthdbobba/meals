The original is a meat pie under hot water pastry, which is four cups of flour, a stick of butter, and an oven. None of that travels. The filling, though, is already a one-pot dish: meat, potato, onion, oats, and a short spice list simmered until dry. So the pastry is gone and the filling is the whole recipe.

The oats are the part worth keeping. They swell and bind the loose hash into something that holds on a spoon, which is the job the crust used to do. Allspice and cinnamon in a savory meat dish look like a mistake on paper; they are what makes this taste like tourtière and not like generic camp hash. Measure them, do not eyeball them.
