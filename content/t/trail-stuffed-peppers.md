  - "On trail, one to two hours before dinner, add just enough cold water to the meat bag to cover the crumbles. Seal it and keep walking."

This is a burrito filling without the tortilla. The pepper is the point: it goes in raw at the end and stays crisp, which is the one texture a dehydrated dinner cannot fake. It is also the only thing in the bag that can spoil, so buy it in town and eat it in the first day or two.

Water discipline decides whether this works. The rice gets barely covered, not flooded, and the meat rehydrates cold in its own bag while you hike so it does not need pot water at all. Too much and you get soup with peppers in it. This needs a 1.3 L pot or bigger; two servings is a lot of volume.
