This came out of a conversation about cooking in a van with two gas rings and no freezer, and it survives being cut down to one ring. Couscous is the reason: it never boils, it just sits in hot water with the lid on while you do nothing. That frees the burner and means the pot is off the flame for most of the cook.

The salami does double duty. It is the protein, and the fat it renders is what the couscous absorbs instead of plain water. Dried onion and sun-dried tomatoes replace the fresh vegetables you would buy every couple of days with a shop nearby — they rehydrate in the same pot and add nothing to your cooler because you do not need one.
