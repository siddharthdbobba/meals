Two ingredients, because self-rising flour is already flour plus baking powder plus salt, and cream is already fat plus liquid. That covers everything a biscuit needs. No cutting butter into flour with a cold knife at a picnic table.

The hood is what makes it a bake instead of a fry. A copy-paper box wrapped inside and out in heavy foil and taped, inverted over the pan, traps enough heat to cook the tops. A foil roasting pan turned upside down does the same job and packs flatter. Shelf-stable UHT cream is the substitution that gets this off the fridge and into a food bin.
