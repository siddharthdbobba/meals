The original is a leftovers dish: roast turkey dropped into stock with fresh chili, lemongrass, and ginger. Nothing about that survives a pack, so the fresh aromatics become dried ones and the leftover meat becomes a pouch. Dried ginger and garlic granules are blunter than grated fresh, but in a broth simmered ten minutes the difference mostly closes.

The star anise and lemongrass are the whole point. They need real steeping time in hot liquid, which is why they go in first and the vegetables go in second. Skipping the simmer leaves you drinking salted water with meat in it. This is a light dinner by trail standards, around 320 calories, so pair it with something dense or double the meat.
