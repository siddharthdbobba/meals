This started as a Thanksgiving leftovers dish, which means it assumed a fridge full of roast turkey and a can of cranberry sauce. Neither travels. Freeze-dried turkey rehydrates in ten minutes and weighs almost nothing, and dried cranberries with a little sugar and hot water break down into something close enough to sauce once you mash them. The sweet-and-salty contrast is the whole point of the dish, so the cranberry step is not optional.

A dry pot on low heat is the closest thing to a skillet you have. Keep the flame low and the tortilla will brown before the cheese has a chance to run out the sides. Hard cheese matters more than the type: soft cheese turns to grease in a pot, while gouda or a firm jack melts and stays put.
