A vacuum jug is a stove you already carried. Boiling water plus instant couscous is a retained-heat cook: the grain only needs to sit in water above about 70 C, and a decent jug holds that for hours. You get a hot lunch on a day when stopping to set up a stove in the wind is the last thing you want.

The details that matter are headspace and preheat. Fill it to the brim and you cannot stir, so the top layer stays dry. Fill a cold jug and the steel drinks your heat before the couscous does. Dried chickpeas rehydrate on the same schedule as the grain, which is why they are here instead of lentils — lentils would still be gravel at noon.
