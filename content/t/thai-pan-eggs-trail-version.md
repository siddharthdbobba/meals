The original is a tabletop skillet breakfast: eggs cracked into a hot pan with pork, sausage, and mushroom, cooked until the whites set around the edges. None of that survives a pack. Fresh eggs break, pork needs a cooler, and a shallow pan is not what you brought. So this is the same flavor built from powder and freeze-dried pieces in one pot.

Two things carry it. White pepper and mushroom soy are the whole seasoning profile, and both weigh nothing, so bring them and skip the rest. Low heat is the other. Powdered egg goes tough fast, faster than shell eggs, and a canister stove on full throttle will weld it to the pot bottom before you have stirred twice.
