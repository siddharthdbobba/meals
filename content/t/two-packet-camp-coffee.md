Two packets in one mug is the whole idea. A single packet in 2 cups of water is thin, and doubling the coffee instead of halving the water means you get a full mug that stays hot long enough to actually finish.

Instant is the trail-worthy choice because there is nothing to clean and nothing to pack out. A press or a drip cone makes better coffee, but both leave you with wet grounds at 7 a.m. The mug matters more than the method: insulated plastic or double-wall steel holds heat while you wander around camp, which is the point of the ritual.
