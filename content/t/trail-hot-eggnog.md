This is a return to what commercial eggnog packets should have been: real dry milk, real freeze-dried egg, and real spices instead of creamer and artificial flavoring. It won't be thick like store-bought eggnog with stabilizing gums, but that trade is worth it for actual ingredients and cutting the weight.

The freeze-dried egg powder is the hesitation point—it is technically undercooked egg. If you are pregnant, immunocompromised, or simply uncomfortable with it, leave it out and you still have a good spiced hot milk drink. The bulk recipe makes five servings and splits into bags for choices between this and hot cocoa on longer trips.
