The original was a pressure-cooker recipe built on fresh peppers, canned tomatoes, and a fridge full of toppings. None of that travels. Dried peppers and tomato powder carry the same flavors at a tenth of the weight, and red lentils replace the long bean cook entirely: they break down into the body that a canned chili gets from time under pressure.

Cocoa and cinnamon are not garnish. A quarter teaspoon of each is what keeps the spice from reading as flat heat, and the sweet potato balances the chipotle from the other side. Dry the sweet potato thin. Thick pieces stay hard in the middle no matter how long you soak them, and there is no fixing that at 9,000 feet.
