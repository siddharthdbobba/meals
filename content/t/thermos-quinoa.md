You boil water at lunch and dinner cooks for free. Thermal mass handles the work while you hike or break camp. At mealtime, there's nothing but opening a jar and fluffing.

Prewashed quinoa is nonnegotiable—unwashed seeds have bitter saponins that require real rinsing and will wreck a meal. Dried vegetables and broth powder add flavor without weight. The base is plain, but it carries seasoning well.
