The source here was a two-burner van stove rather than a dish, so this is what that stove is actually good for: something that simmers unattended for ten minutes while you do anything else. It runs on one burner if that is all you have.

Split red lentils are the reason the timing works. They collapse into a thick base fast enough to cook and eat in one sitting, and they carry the tomato paste and fat instead of leaving a thin broth. The instant rice goes in last and finishes off the heat, which keeps it from turning to paste and keeps the whole thing in one pot.
