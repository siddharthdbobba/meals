A cheesy pasta bake needs an oven, a casserole dish, and a refrigerator's worth of soft cheese. None of that goes in a pack, so the browning step is gone and the ricotta is gone. What is left is the part that mattered: pasta, tomato, herbs, and enough fat to make it a dinner instead of a side.

Cooking the tortellini in just enough water and never draining it is what holds the whole thing together. The starch left in the pot is the sauce base, and the milk powder gives it the creaminess ricotta was doing. Mozzarella goes in off the heat and covered, or it turns stringy and sticks to the pot walls instead of the pasta.
