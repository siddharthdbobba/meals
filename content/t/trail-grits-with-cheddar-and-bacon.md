Instant grits are corn flour that turns creamy when you add hot water—it's trail polenta without the stove time. The powder ingredients carry flavor in half the weight of real cheese or butter, and they disappear into the grits rather than clumping on the surface.

The bacon bits and cheese are not optional. They're where the calories come from. Without them you have beige mush with some garlic. With them, you have breakfast with enough fat and salt to keep hunger off until lunch. The fried onions go on top after cooking so they stay crisp.
