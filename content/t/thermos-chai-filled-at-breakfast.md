An insulated mug is a slow-release stove. You spend one fuel boil at breakfast and get a hot drink at ten, at noon, and at the top of the climb, without unpacking anything. On a cold day that changes how the whole morning goes.

Two details do most of the work. Fill the mug nearly to the top, because the trapped air is what cools the drink, not the walls. And premix the powder at home so all you do in camp is dump one bag into boiling water. Powdered whole milk stands in for the fresh milk this normally wants; it dissolves fine in water that has stopped bubbling, and it will scorch if you keep the flame on.
