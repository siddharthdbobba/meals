An insulated bottle is a stove you already paid for. One boil in the morning covers coffee at the trailhead, coffee at the first long break, and something to hold at lunch when it is too cold to sit still. The only cost is carrying the bottle, and you were carrying water anyway.

The milk powder and the pinch of salt exist because instant coffee tastes thin once it drops below scalding. Fat and salt give it body at drinking temperature instead of boiling temperature, which is the temperature you will actually meet it at. Wetting the powder with a small splash before filling the bottle keeps it from setting into a lump at the bottom that no amount of shaking fixes.
