The syrup is the binder, not the sweetener. It coats the oats and cereal, and the low oven drives the water out of it so what is left is a hard sugar shell. That shell is why this stays crunchy in a pack for a week instead of going soft.

The fruit goes in after cooling for the same reason. Freeze-dried fruit put in a warm pan pulls moisture out of the air and turns leathery, and it drags the rest of the batch down with it. Cold pan, dry fruit, sealed bag, in that order.
