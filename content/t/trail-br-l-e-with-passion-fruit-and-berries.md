A real crème brûlée needs eggs, cream, an oven, and a torch. None of that goes in a pack. What survives the translation is the structure: cool vanilla custard underneath, something sharp and fruity on top, and a layer of sugar you break through. Instant pudding plus whole milk powder gets you the first part cold, with no stove and no fridge. Full-fat milk powder matters here — skim sets up thin and tastes like paper.

The fruit layer is doing the job the caramel does at home: cutting the sweetness. Freeze-dried berries rehydrate in about two minutes and hold their shape, and the passion fruit and orange powders bring back the acid you would have gotten from a squeezed orange. The turbinado never gets torched, so scatter it at the last second. Sitting in the fruit juice, it dissolves in under a minute.
