The original is a pressure-cooker roast: tough meat, salted broth, an hour under steam until the connective tissue gives up. None of that travels. What does travel is the part actually doing the work, which is the broth. Three bouillon cubes in half a liter of water is a heavy hand on purpose, because the rice absorbs most of it and unsalted rice tastes like nothing.

The pot stays on the flame only long enough to soften the vegetables. Instant rice does not need boiling, it needs hot liquid and time, so the lid replaces the stove for the last eight minutes and saves fuel. The oil goes in at the end. Added early it just coats the grains and slows down how fast they take up water.
