Nettles are foraged greens with more body than spinach, and they don't wilt to nothing when dehydrated. They rehydrate quickly in hot broth and hold their flavor better than blanching-then-drying would preserve. The butter and coconut milk powder do the heavy lifting here—they turn thin broth into something dense enough to justify the fuel.

The potato flakes thicken without turning gummy because you're not cooking them raw in liquid. They just need to absorb the hot broth and swell. This is the same trick that works in the Ramen Bomb: starch added to something already hot becomes tender instead of grainy. Season carefully at the end, since dried broth powder is salty.
