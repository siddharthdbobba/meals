Tequila is not doing much for flavor here. It is a solvent: it carries the taco seasoning and the lime into the meat faster than water would, and most of the alcohol leaves on the grate. What you actually taste is lime and cumin, with chicken that stayed juicy because the salt in the seasoning packet had four hours to work.

This one is car camping or basecamp only. Raw chicken means a cooler, and a cooler means a vehicle. The trail-friendly move is to do the whole cook at home, cut the strips, and vacuum-seal them for a one-day carry, but at that point it is leftovers rather than a recipe. Rest the meat before you cut it or the juice ends up on the cutting board.
