steps:

The original is oven work with raw meat and a fridge, neither of which exists at camp. The rebuild dries the beef and sausage at home, then rehydrates them and binds the result with breadcrumbs and powdered egg. Fully cooked meat means the meatballs only need browning, not a temperature probe, which is the part campfires are bad at.

Dried ground meat drinks more water than looks reasonable. Underdo the soak and the middles stay gritty. The fat matters too: rinse the cooked meat well before drying or it goes rancid in the bag, and add the oil back in the pan where it belongs.
