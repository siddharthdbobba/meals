Couscous is the one starch that cooks without cooking. It swells off the heat, which means the burner runs for about two minutes total and the pot is off the flame while dinner finishes itself. In a van galley that matters less for fuel than for space: you get the stove back.

The sun-dried tomatoes and bouillon go into the water first because both need a boil to give anything up. The oil goes in last, off heat, so it coats the grains instead of frying into them. Skip it and you have a dry, salty pilaf with a third fewer calories.
