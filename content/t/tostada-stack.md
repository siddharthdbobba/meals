The original is fast food built on refrigeration: fresh beef, sour cream, lettuce, tomato, shredded cheese blend. The structure survives that being stripped out. Two crisp shells with something soft between them is the whole idea, and instant bean flakes do that job better than canned refried beans because they hydrate to whatever thickness you want. Too thin and the shells go soggy in a minute. Keep it stiff.

Freeze-dried beef rehydrates in five minutes and carries the seasoning fine. Hard grated cheese replaces the melty blend since nothing here gets hot enough to melt anyway. The enchilada sauce goes on last and stays on top, not soaking in. Assemble one stack at a time and eat it standing up; there is no version of this that keeps.
