The original is a slow cooker dish built on fresh cranberries and squeezed oranges, which is four hours and a refrigerator you do not have. Dried cranberries do the same job in ten minutes because you are only putting back the water that was taken out. They arrive already sweetened, so the maple drops from the usual pile of sugar to a quarter cup.

Orange drink powder is the honest substitute for half a cup of juice, and the dried peel is what actually carries the orange flavor. The syrup goes in after the heat is off. It scorches on a stove burner in a way it never does in a crockpot, and once it scorches the whole pot tastes like it.
