A wide-mouth vacuum jug is a slow cooker that runs on one boil. Couscous and precooked lentils need heat and time, not sustained heat, so 400 ml of boiling water sealed into insulated steel does the whole job while you walk. Six hours later it is still hot enough to steam.

The preheat step matters more than the recipe. Cold stainless steals a real share of your boil before the food ever gets it, and a jug that starts cold is a jug that serves lunch lukewarm. Oil goes in at the top so the fat sits on the surface and slows evaporative cooling on top of adding the calories.
