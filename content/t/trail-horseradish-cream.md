Horseradish cream is a dairy sauce, which normally rules it out for a pack. Milk powder and buttermilk powder solve that: rehydrated they give you the body and the sour edge that sour cream was providing, and neither needs a cooler. The acid matters as much as the heat, so do not skip the vinegar.

Dried horseradish is milder than freshly grated root, and it needs time in liquid before it does anything. Mix it, then wait. If you dig your own root, do it in cold weather and dry it soon after; a summer root will taste like nothing and the drying only takes it further down.
