A twice-baked potato is two separate things: a fluffy loaded filling and a crisped shell. On trail you can only have the first one, so this recipe stops pretending otherwise and builds the filling in a pot. Potato flakes stand in for scooped potato, and the sour cream, cheddar, milk, and butter all come as powders that rehydrate in the same water.

The powdered sour cream is doing more work than it looks like. Cheese powder alone reads as boxed macaroni; the sour cream is the tang that makes the bowl taste like a baked potato instead. Bacon goes in at the end and stays dry until then, because that is the only texture contrast left once the shell is gone.
