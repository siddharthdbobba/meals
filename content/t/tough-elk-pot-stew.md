Elk that came off the animal tough stays tough under fast heat. The fix at home is a long wet braise, which is not something you do on a canister stove. So the braise happens before the trip: cook the grind down, then dry it. Grinding solves the chew problem outright, and drying it in a thin sheet means the pieces are small enough to take water back in twelve minutes instead of an hour.

The onion soup packet is doing double duty as salt and body. Green chile and chipotle are there because lean game reads flat without something sharp against it. Potato flakes at the end pull the whole thing together into a stew instead of meat sitting in broth. Skip the oil and you lose about 120 calories and most of the mouthfeel; dried lean elk brings almost no fat of its own.
