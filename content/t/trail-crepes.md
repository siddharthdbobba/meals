Crepe batter is mostly liquid, which makes it a good candidate for powder. Egg powder and whole milk powder rehydrate into something close enough to the original, and the flour does not care that it came out of a bottle. Nothing here needs a cooler, so this works on day three as well as day one.

The two things that go wrong are heat and grease. Too hot and the batter sets before it spreads, leaving you with a thick lump in the middle of the pan. Cook over low coals or a stove barely on, and re-grease every single time or the crepe tears when you flip it. Resting the batter five minutes lets the flour hydrate, which is why the first one is usually the worst one.
