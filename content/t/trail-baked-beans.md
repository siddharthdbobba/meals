Real baked beans need hours in a slow cooker and a can of beans in liquid. Neither travels. Instant bean flakes rehydrate in three minutes and land in roughly the same place, so the sauce does all the work: tomato powder for body, maple sugar and molasses for the sweet backbone, smoked paprika standing in for the hours of smoke you are not going to get.

Mustard powder replaces the jar of prepared mustard and carries the same sharpness dry. The bacon is doing double duty as fat and salt, so if you cut it, add oil back or the whole pot goes flat. Add water slowly at the end. Bean flakes keep thickening for a minute after you stop stirring.
