The original is a two-burner scout recipe: skillet for the beef, pot for the pasta, cans of corn drained into the dirt. On the trail that becomes one pot and no cans. Freeze-dried beef and corn rehydrate in the time the water takes to boil, and they weigh a fraction of what they replace.

The box wants butter and milk. Neither travels, so oil does the job instead. Keeping a splash of pasta water in the pot matters more than the fat does, because the cheese powder and taco seasoning need something to dissolve into. Too dry and you get gritty orange dust in the corners.
