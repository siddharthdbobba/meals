Freeze-dried chicken rehydrates in minutes and tastes far closer to fresh than sun-dried or home-dehydrated versions. Instant rice does the same. Both are already optimized for one-pot trail cooking, so this is less an adaptation than a collection of ingredients designed to work with nothing but a stove and boiling water.

The curry and apples seem unusual together, but they balance each other. Curry powder in the broth gives the whole pot a backbone of flavor that pushes beyond just hot salty rice. The dried apples soften into the broth and add sweetness that tames the spice instead of making it clash. It tastes intentional.
