Potato salad is a cooler dish, so this version rebuilds it out of dry parts. Dehydrated potato dice rehydrate into something with actual texture instead of mush, which is why they beat instant flakes here. Freeze-dried peas come back in the same soak. Powdered egg is optional but it is where most of the protein lives.

Draining matters more than anything else in the method. Rehydrated potatoes hold a lot of surface water, and mayo thinned by it turns into a grey puddle in the bottom of the pot. Pour the water off, let the potatoes steam dry a minute, then dress them. Cooling is worth the wait: warm mayo tastes like nothing.
