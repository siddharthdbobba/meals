The original is a springform casserole in a 400°F oven, which is not a thing you have. A covered pot on a low flame gets to the same place: the tortillas soften from the moisture in the filling, the cheese melts, and the whole stack fuses. The reason it works is that the filling goes in already cooked and already thick. Wet filling steams the tortillas into paste.

Almost everything here is dried at home or bought dry. Bean flakes rehydrate instantly and thicken the pan, which does the job the simmered-down beer did in the original. Cheese is the one item still on cooler time, so this is a first- or second-night meal.
