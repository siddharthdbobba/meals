Freeze-dried meals often taste underseasoned because flavor compounds evaporate with the water. This one doesn't have that problem—it leans on salt and sugar the way bulgoji beef does in restaurants. The sesame oil is why this tastes like something interesting instead of like rice and salt.

The fifteen minutes isn't wasted time. Freeze-dried vegetables need water to come back to life, and rice needs time to absorb. The brown sugar isn't about sweetness; it balances salt and adds depth. Under-season it and it tastes like nothing. Over-season it and it tastes like a soup packet. The balance is the whole thing.
