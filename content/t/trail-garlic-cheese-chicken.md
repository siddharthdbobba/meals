This adapts a home-oven recipe to a canister stove. The garlic-brown sugar combination caramelizes slightly as water reduces, coating the chicken. Cheese dissolves in, adding fat and umami.

Freeze-dried chicken carries well for its concentrated calories. Hard aged cheese like parmesan travels better than fresh cheese and doesn't spoil. The whole meal cooks in eight minutes once water boils.
