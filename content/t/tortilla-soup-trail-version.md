A slow cooker recipe is mostly a schedule, and the schedule does not survive a trailhead. Six hours on low is really just rehydrating beans and pulling chicken apart, so both get bought that way instead: bean flakes and a foil pouch. Tomato powder replaces the can, which is the single biggest weight cut here. What is left is one boil and a rest.

The soak, not the burn, does the work. Freeze-dried corn and bean flakes need time in hot water more than they need a flame under them, so covering the pot off-heat saves fuel and keeps the bottom from scorching. Cheese and crushed chips go on last, at the table, because both turn to sludge if they see the boil. Fresh cilantro and lime were garnish in the original; dried cilantro is a fair trade, and the lime is not optional since the acid is what keeps a pot of cumin and beans from tasting flat.
