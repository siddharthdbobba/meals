The source recipe sets with gelatin in a cold fridge, which does not exist on the trail. Cornstarch does the same job in the other direction: it thickens with heat and holds once cool, so the pudding sets sitting on a rock while the stove goes out.

Fresh milk is out too, so powdered milk carries the body and the protein. The chips matter more than they look. They are the fat, and fat is what separates pudding from sweet chocolate soup. Keep the flame low and keep stirring, because milk powder and sugar catch on a thin pot floor fast.
