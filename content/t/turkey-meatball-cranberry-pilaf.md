The original is a plated dinner: hot meatballs, cold salad, a spoon of yoghurt. None of that travels. The yoghurt and cucumber are gone, the spinach is dried, and everything now finishes in one pot on one boil. What survives is the part that made the dish work — sweet cranberries against sour and salt, with something crunchy on top.

Rehydrating the cranberries with the grain matters. Dropped in dry at the end they stay leathery; boiled alongside the rice they plump and give their sugar to the water. Lemon powder replaces the yoghurt's job, which was acid, not creaminess. The pepitas go on last and stay dry, because anything stirred into hot pilaf stops being crunch and becomes texture.
