This is a chip bag doing the work of a bowl. The chips are sturdy enough to hold hot meat without collapsing, and eating out of the bag means the only thing to wash is the skillet and a spoon.

The seasoning step matters more than it looks. Cooking the spice mix into the fat with a splash of water gives you a coating that clings to the beef, so the flavor ends up on the meat rather than in the bottom of the bag. Beef browned at home and packed cold cuts camp time to about two minutes, but it needs cooler space — that is the one thing here that will not sit out.
