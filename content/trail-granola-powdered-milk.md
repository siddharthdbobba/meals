The fastest possible camp breakfast that is still a breakfast. Milk powder mixed
into the granola at home is the entire trick: it means one bag, one pour, and no
separate step in the cold.

Full fat milk powder is worth seeking out. Nonfat is common, cheap, and takes a
third of the calories out of this for no benefit.
