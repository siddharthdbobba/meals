The coal ratio is the only technical part of dutch oven baking: more coals on the
lid than underneath, because you are baking, not simmering. Get that backwards and
the bottom burns while the top stays raw.

Rotate the oven a third turn every ten minutes, and the lid a third turn the other
way, and you will not get a hot spot.
