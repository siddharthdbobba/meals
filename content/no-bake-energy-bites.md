Commercial bars run four dollars for 250 calories in a wrapper you carry out. These
run about fifty cents for the same thing, taste better, and go in a reusable bag.

In summer heat they soften and merge. That is a texture problem, not a food safety
one, and freezing them the night before buys you most of a day.
