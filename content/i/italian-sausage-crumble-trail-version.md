The original is a five-pound sausage grind with fresh garlic, suet, and a day in the fridge. None of that travels. What does travel is the seasoning ratio, which is the actual recipe: fennel leading, oregano behind it, red pepper and a little sugar underneath. Bind that to dried meat and a starch and you get the same flavor without a grinder or a cold chain.

The sugar is not there to sweeten. It rounds off the salt and the crushed pepper, which read harsh when there is no fat emulsion carrying them. The parmesan and oil go in last, after the burner is off — heat and hard cheese make a stringy mess, and the oil is where a third of the calories live.
