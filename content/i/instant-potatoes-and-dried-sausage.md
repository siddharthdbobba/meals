Instant potato flakes are the whole reason this works on a stove that only really boils water. They rehydrate on contact, so the fuel goes to the vegetables, not the starch. Blanching the vegetables before they go in the dehydrator is what lets them come back in five minutes instead of staying gravel.

The sausage is cured, not cooked, so it keeps for days without a cooler and adds fat the potatoes can carry. It goes in off the heat — hot enough to soften and release its oil, not hot enough to render out what you carried it for.
