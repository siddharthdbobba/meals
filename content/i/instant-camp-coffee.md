Instant coffee kills the thermos. You carry nothing all day, then brew fresh coffee the moment you stop. A good instant coffee from a specialty roaster tastes nothing like the industrial powders from years past. The weight is negligible and the caffeine wake-up happens when you actually want it.

Buy quality instant before you leave. Cheap instant tastes hollow and stale. Premium costs more per serving but you use less of it, and the difference matters after a long day. Black is fastest and lightest. If you need milk, powdered coconut milk adds almost nothing and transforms the taste.
