This is a coal-bed recipe, not a stove recipe. Everything inside the packet steams in its own fat and moisture, which is why no water goes in. The sausage renders and that grease coats the potatoes; that is the whole mechanism.

The only real failure is undercooked potato. Sausage is done in twenty minutes, potato cubes take thirty or more, so the cut size is what keeps them on the same schedule. Small cubes, doubled foil, and a bed of coals rather than open flame. Raw pork means this one needs a cooler and a same-day cook, which is why it is car camping and not backpacking.
