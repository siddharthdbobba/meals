Leeks dry well and weigh almost nothing once they do. Slice them thin, dehydrate at home, and a cup of them takes up less room than an apple. Stored airtight they last years, which is longer than the fresh ones would last in your kitchen.

The potato flakes are the thickener, not the bulk. A third of a cup turns two cups of broth into soup with body; much more and you get wallpaper paste with leeks in it. Add them after the boil, off a hard flame, and stir the whole time — they clump if you dump and walk away.
