This is the thru-hiker breakfast: everything shelf-stable, everything lightweight, everything ready in three minutes. The instant breakfast handles flavor and protein without needing fresh milk or a stove. The coffee provides the actual function—waking you up so you can move. The Nido makes it thick enough to feel like a meal instead of powdered water.

Shake it hard and drink it cold. The powder will separate if you wait too long, and you shouldn't wait anyway. Just drink and move on.
