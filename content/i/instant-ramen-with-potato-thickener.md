Potato flakes do the real work here. Ramen alone is broth with noodles in it—light and quick to pass through your system. Add the flakes and the whole thing thickens into a dense paste. That thickness is what makes your stomach feel full.

Oil is the point. Without it you are carrying carbohydrate that burns hot and fast. Add fat and you have something that stays with you, something that justifies the ounces in your pack after a long day walking.
