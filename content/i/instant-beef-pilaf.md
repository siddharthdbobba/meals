Freeze-dried ingredients pack calories without weight and rehydrate in minutes once hot water hits them. The beef and vegetables plump back up almost completely, and the rice carries the whole thing. Vegetable soup mix does the seasoning work, so you don't have to measure salt, bouillon, and spices separately in low light.

The pot cozy is optional but worth the space for high mountains or cold weather. Wrapped in insulation, the water stays hot enough to finish rehydration without any fuel burned after the boil. This meal sits well at altitude where appetite is uncertain and comes together in the time it takes to set camp.
