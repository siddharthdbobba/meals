Stamppot is potato, a green, and a piece of meat mashed together, which is already most of the way to a stove-bag dinner. Instant flakes were never really a compromise here. The dish wants the potato broken down anyway.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Kale needs a real boil to come back from dried; potato flakes only need to be stirred into hot water. Boil the greens first, then thicken. Do it the other way and you get a stiff pot with leathery leaves in it. The sausage fat and the oil are the calories, and they are also what keeps the whole thing from tasting like paste.
