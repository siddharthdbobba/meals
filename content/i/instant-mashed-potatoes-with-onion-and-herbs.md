The original called for half a cup of boxed milk, which means carrying liquid. Powdered whole milk does the same job dry: it goes in with the boiling water and rehydrates on the spot, so the pot weight is water you were carrying anyway.

Simmering the onion flakes before the potatoes go in is the part worth keeping. Dried onion dropped into finished mash stays crunchy and tastes like nothing. Given a minute in boiling water it softens and gives up flavor. Add the flakes off the heat, or the starch scorches on the pot bottom and you are scrubbing in the dark.
