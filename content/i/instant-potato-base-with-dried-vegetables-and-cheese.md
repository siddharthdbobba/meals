This is a base, not a fixed dinner. Instant potato flakes rehydrate in off-boil water in under a minute, which is the whole reason they are the backbone of so many trail meals, but on their own they are bland starch. The dried root vegetables are what give it a savory floor. Grinding them to powder matters: whole dried carrot chips are still leathery after two minutes in a pot, powder is not.

Drying the grated cheese at home is the substitution that makes this work without a cooler. A 45 percent hard cheese loses most of its water in a low oven and then keeps for weeks, and it dissolves back into hot potato instead of sitting in it as a lump. Salt goes in the mix at home so you are not carrying a shaker for one meal. Scale it by making one bag per person rather than doubling into one pot; the flakes thicken too fast for large batches to stir evenly.
