This is a canned-goods soup that happens to have meat in it. The sausage does double duty: it browns first and leaves fat in the pot, so everything after it cooks in seasoned grease instead of plain broth. That fat is most of the flavor, which is why nothing here needs a spice kit.

The undrained beans matter. That starchy liquid is what keeps the soup from being broth with vegetables floating in it. Spinach goes in with the heat off because a covered pot holds plenty of residual heat, and boiled spinach turns to sludge. Carried weight is high for what it is, so this belongs at a truck or a boat, not in a pack.
