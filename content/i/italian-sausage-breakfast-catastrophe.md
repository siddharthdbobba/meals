A breakfast bake is the cheapest way to feed eight people something hot when the morning is cold and everyone wants to break camp. The hash browns are the structure: they soak up the egg and hold it together so you can cut squares instead of scooping curds.

Thawing matters more than anything else here. Frozen potatoes dump water into the eggs and you end up with a soggy middle after a full hour of coals. Pull the bag from the cooler early. The 18-over-9 coal split puts most of the heat on the lid, which browns the cheese instead of scorching the bottom.
