Store-bought instant packets are mostly sugar and cost more per ounce than they should. The fix is a tablespoon of old-fashioned oats run through a blender until it is flour. That powder dissolves instantly and thickens the water around the quick oats, so you get porridge instead of oats sitting in a puddle. It is the same trick commercial packets use, minus the price.

Dry milk carries the fat and most of the protein. Leave it out and the packet is thin and forgettable; use soy or coconut milk powder if dairy is a problem, and drop the milk-based variations. Sugar is deliberately separate from the base so you can build sweet and plain packets from one bowl. Use certified gluten-free oats if that matters to you, but the recipe as written is not tagged gluten-free.
