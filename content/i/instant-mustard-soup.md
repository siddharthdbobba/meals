Mustard is doing more here than flavor. Dry mustard powder is sharp enough to carry a whole pot on its own, which matters when the rest of the bag is beige powder and bouillon. Cold-start the mustard in the water: it hydrates evenly and the raw heat mellows as it comes up to a boil.

The potato flakes replace a flour roux. A traditional leek soup thickens with flour cooked in fat, which needs a second pan and attention you do not have at camp. Flakes do the same job off-heat with a spoon. Add them slowly. Too many at once and you have a bowl of stiff potato with mustard in it, which is a different and worse dinner.
