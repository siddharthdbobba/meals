The note that came with this one was a dead link and a backpack thread, so treat the numbers as the recipe and nothing else. What it is: two dry starches that rehydrate at different speeds, staged so you only boil once.

Rice goes in first because it needs the full heat of the water. Bean flakes go in after, off the flame, or they seize into a paste before the rice is done. The oil is not a garnish. Beans and rice are lean, and without added fat this lands closer to 500 calories than 700.
