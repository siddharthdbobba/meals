Most trail bolognese asks for twenty-five minutes in a cosy while the sauce and pasta come back to life separately. This version skips that by drying the sauce hard enough to shatter and pairing it with pasta that cooks as fast as the sauce rehydrates. Everything goes in the same water. The tomato leather does the thickening as it dissolves, so there is no separate sauce step and nothing to drain.

Angel hair matters more than it sounds. Anything thicker needs four or five minutes, and by then the fuel bill is worse than the meal. The oil and the parmesan are the calorie load — a bare pot of pasta and tomato is about 450 calories and not a dinner. Hard-dried parmesan keeps for weeks without a cooler, which is the only dairy that survives this trip.
