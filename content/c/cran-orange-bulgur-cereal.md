Bulgur is already parboiled, which is the whole reason this works without a stove. Cold water and eight hours are enough to soften it all the way through; rolled oats go to mush on the same schedule, and bulgur keeps a bit of chew.

The orange powder does the acid, so no fresh citrus and no fridge. Walnuts stay out until morning for two reasons: they hold their crunch, and their oil would otherwise sit in the soak going stale. Toast them at home and they carry better.
