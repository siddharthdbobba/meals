This is a bakery cookie dragged onto a grill grate, and the changes are mostly about heat and refrigeration. Fresh egg whites and stick butter do not survive a cooler-free weekend, so the whites are powdered and the butter is ghee, which is stable at camp temperatures because the milk solids are already gone. The oven becomes a skillet over low coals: the batter is thin enough that direct pan contact cooks it through in the same few minutes.

The folding window is the whole difficulty. A fortune cookie is pliable for maybe fifteen seconds after it leaves the heat, and rigid after that. Cook two at a time, keep the mug within reach, and accept that the first pan is a test batch. Everything else about the recipe is forgiving; that part is not.
