Bacon and oatmeal is a standard canoe-trip breakfast, and it works because the two halves fix each other's problems. Oats alone are cheap fuel with no fat and no salt. Precooked bacon brings both, and it keeps at ambient temperature, so the cooler stays shut.

The changes from the home version are the milk powder and the oil. Fresh milk is not happening on day five, and powdered whole milk rehydrates into something with enough fat to keep the oats from going gluey. The teaspoon of oil is not for flavor, it is for the calorie count on a long paddling day. Add the bacon at the end or it softens into the porridge and you lose the one bit of texture.
