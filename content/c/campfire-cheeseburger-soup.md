steps:
  - "Chop the bacon into bite-sized pieces and cook it in the bottom of your pot over the fire until crispy at the edges. Remove and set aside."
  - "Add the diced onion to the bacon fat and cook for 3–4 minutes, stirring, until it softens."
  - "Add the ground beef in chunks and break it apart as it browns, about 5–7 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed."
  - "Pour in the broth and bring to a simmer. Add the ketchup, mustard, garlic powder, paprika, and the cooked bacon back in."
  - "Simmer for 5 minutes so the flavors marry. Taste and adjust salt and pepper."
  - "Mix the corn starch with 2 tbsp of cold water to make a slurry. Stir it into the soup and simmer for 2–3 minutes until thickened."
  - "Remove from heat. Stir in the cheese until melted. Fold in the pickles."

This soup is three parts: bacon fat for flavor, ground meat for protein, and cheese plus ketchup for the burger profile. The corn starch thickener gets you from raw to finished in 25 minutes over a fire.

The pickles cut through the richness and remind you this is cheeseburger, not just beef soup. Pack them whole in a jar and stir them in at the end so they stay crisp.
