Baked dressing needs an oven and an hour. This does the same job on a fire because the cornbread is already dry going in. Stale, cubed cornbread will absorb hot broth in about fifteen minutes without an oven doing anything, so the pan only has to soften vegetables and hold heat.

The heavy cream from an oven version is gone; butter and rendered sausage fat carry the richness instead, and neither needs a fridge for a weekend. Tart apple matters more than it sounds. It is the only sharp thing in a pan of bread, fat, and salt, and without it the whole thing reads as one flat note.
