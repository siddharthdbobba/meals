This is a chili and a pasta dish cooked at the same time in the same pot, which works because the pasta finishes in the chili liquid instead of in water you would otherwise pour off. That starch is what thickens the sauce. There is no draining step and no second pot.

The original wanted fresh onion, refrigerated beef, and sour cream. Freeze-dried beef and onion flakes carry that without a cooler, and the cheese melted under a lid at the end does the job sour cream was doing. Use a wide, heavy-bottomed pot over coals rather than open flame — thin walls and direct fire will scorch the bottom before the pasta is done.
