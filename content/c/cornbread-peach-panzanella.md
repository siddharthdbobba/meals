The original is a summer salad: fresh peaches, heirloom tomatoes, torn basil, cornbread croutons. None of that travels. Dried fruit and sun-dried tomatoes rehydrate into something close enough, and 180 ml of hot water does in ten minutes what a fridge and a farmers market did before. Bake and cube the cornbread at home, then dry it out in a low oven so it survives the drive without molding.

Browned butter is the part worth doing carefully. The solids in the butter toast and turn nutty, and that is the whole dressing. Honey balances the salt from the tomatoes, and the reserved soaking liquid thins the sauce enough to coat every cube. Add the bread last and eat it fast, because cornbread in a warm pan has maybe ten minutes before it stops being croutons and starts being pudding.
