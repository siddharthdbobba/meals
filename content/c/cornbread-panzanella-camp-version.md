The original is a leftover-cornbread salad built on fresh tomato, cucumber, yogurt, and steak drippings. None of that survives a night in a pack, so the wet vegetables become dry-packed sun-dried tomatoes and onion flakes rehydrated in hot water, the yogurt is gone, and the drippings are replaced by olive oil carried in a bottle. The vinegar and the dried mint do the work the lemon and fresh herbs used to do.

Cornbread is the weak point. It falls apart when it is damp and it burns when it is hot, which is why it gets dried hard at home, toasted over low heat, and dressed last. The five-minute rest is not optional: the outside of a dried cube takes dressing immediately and the inside takes a while, and eating early gets you a bowl of oily gravel.
