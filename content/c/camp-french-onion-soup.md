Onions are mostly water and sugar. The whole recipe is the hour it takes to drive off the first and brown the second, which is why this belongs at a basecamp and not on a pass at dusk. Fire is actually good for it: you want steady low heat off to the side of the coals, not a burner running hot.

The bread goes on top of the bowl, not under the soup, so it soaks without dissolving. Melting the gruyere over it takes a lid and a couple of minutes of residual heat, which is easier than trying to broil anything outdoors.
