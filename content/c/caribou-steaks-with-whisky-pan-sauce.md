Caribou is lean enough that it punishes you for looking away. There is almost no intramuscular fat to buy you time, so five minutes a side and off the heat is the whole technique. The sauce exists to put back the richness the animal does not have: butter for fat, jelly for sugar, citrus and mustard for acid to cut through it.

This is a cooler recipe, not a backpacking one, but the sauce side of it travels well. Boxed orange juice and bottled lemon replace fresh with no real loss once they hit a boil, and the shallots can be chopped days ahead in a sealed jar. The cornstarch slurry has to go in with cold water or it clumps, which is why the 2 tbsp is listed as its own ingredient.
