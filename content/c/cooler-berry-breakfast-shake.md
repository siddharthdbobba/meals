This started as a blender recipe, which is fine at a kitchen counter and useless at a picnic table. The fix is mechanical: soak the oats overnight in the peach syrup so they go soft on their own, then mash the soft fruit by hand. Canned peaches do the heavy lifting because the fruit is already cooked down and the syrup is liquid you do not have to carry separately.

Frozen berries earn their weight twice. They keep the cooler cold on the drive in, and they keep the shake cold once it is mixed. The yogurt is the one thing here that needs real refrigeration, so this is a car camping or first morning recipe. If you are walking in, use the powdered milk version and leave the cooler at home.
