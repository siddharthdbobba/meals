Heart is not offal in the way liver is. It is a working muscle, lean and dense, and it fails the same way a cheap steak fails: overcooked and chewed forever. Pounding it flat and cooking it fast fixes both problems at once. Two minutes a side is not a suggestion.

The onions are doing more than flavor. Their sugar browns and coats the meat, which softens the mineral edge that puts people off organs the first time. Apple pushes that further if you want it. Trimming matters more than seasoning here — every piece of valve or silverskin you leave in is a piece you will spit out later.

This is a kill-day meal. It travels in a cooler, not a pack, and it does not wait.
