The original is built on homemade stock, a rotisserie bird, and fresh egg noodles. None of that travels. Bouillon paste does the job of stock at a fraction of the weight, dried carrots and celery rehydrate in the same pot, and a foil packet of chicken is shelf-stable protein you do not have to cook.

Order matters more than anything else here. Vegetables first, noodles second, chicken last and off the heat. Dump it all in at once and you get mushy noodles floating next to carrots that are still crunchy. The oil replaces the butter and keeps this from being a thin, salty broth.
