This is a stovetop kheer built from a source recipe that used fresh milk. Milk powder replaces it, which is the only real change: reconstituting in the pot means you carry the dairy dry and add water at camp. The sugar and fat ratios stay put.

Toasting matters more than it looks. Ghee-fried cornflakes hold their structure in liquid instead of dissolving into sludge, so you get a pudding with texture rather than sweet paste. Cook the flakes past golden and the whole pot tastes scorched. Watch them.
