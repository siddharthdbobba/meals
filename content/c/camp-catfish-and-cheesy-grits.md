Shrimp and grits with catfish instead is a skillet dish with a roux, a fridge, and half a dozen fresh vegetables. None of that travels. What actually carries the dish is the grits, the cheese, the garlic, and something smoky and salty in it. Instant grits thicken on their own, so the flour roux comes out and the powdered cheese and milk do the creaming.

Browning the sausage first is the one step worth the extra two minutes. Rendered fat coats the grits and keeps them from tasting like wet cornmeal, and it seasons the pot before the water goes in. Add the fish off the heat or you will stir it into paste.
