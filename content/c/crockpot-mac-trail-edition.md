The original sits in a slow cooker for two hours because it starts with liquid dairy that needs time to melt and thicken. On the trail there is no slow cooker and no cold dairy, so the milk, cream cheese, and evaporated milk all become powders that rehydrate in the pasta water instead. Same job, one boil.

The part worth getting right is not draining. That starchy water is what turns the powders into sauce rather than gritty paste, and it is also why you take the pot off the heat first — powdered cheese scorches on a hot bottom fast. Add the powder in stages and stir between each one.
