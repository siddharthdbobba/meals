The original wanted four ripe bananas, a cup of whole milk, and three quarters of a cup of heavy cream. None of that rides in a pack. Dried banana replaces the fruit, whole milk powder and coconut milk powder replace the liquid dairy, and cold water does the rest. Instant pudding sets by hydration, not heat, so the swap works as long as the water is genuinely cold.

Shaking in a sealed bottle is the whole method. It beats air in and breaks up the starch faster than a spoon does, and nothing ends up on a plate you have to wash. The coconut milk powder is not optional if you want it to taste like pie. Milk powder alone sets fine but reads thin.
