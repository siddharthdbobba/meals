Chess pie is a pantry custard: sugar, eggs, milk, butter, and a spoon each of flour and cornmeal. The cornmeal is the part that matters. It sets into a slightly grainy top layer that separates from the custard underneath, which is what keeps a pie made of sugar from tasting like nothing but sugar.

The crust is gone here, along with the fresh eggs and milk. Powdered whole egg and milk powder rehydrate into the same custard base, and the dutch oven does the work an oven would. Coals go mostly on the lid so the bottom does not scorch before the middle sets. Six servings out of one pot; it will not slice, so bring bowls.
