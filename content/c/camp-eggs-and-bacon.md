The order matters more than anything else here. Bacon goes into a cold pan so the fat renders instead of scorching, and that rendered fat becomes the cooking medium for the eggs. No oil to carry, no butter to melt into a soft mess in the cooler.

Beating the eggs lightly leaves visible white, which sets faster than the yolk and holds the whole slab together for the flip. Skip the flip if the pan is crowded and just scramble it; the difference is texture, not doneness. Without a cooler, powdered egg and precooked bacon give you the same breakfast at room temperature for a week.
