The original is a picnic salad built on fresh mozzarella, ripe cherry tomatoes, and torn basil. None of that survives a day in a pack, so all three get swapped: dry-packed sun-dried tomatoes for the cherry tomatoes, a wedge of hard parmesan for the mozzarella pearls, dried basil for fresh. What is left is the part that actually carried the dish, which is oil, acid, and salt on hot starch.

Adding the tomatoes to the boiling pasta does double duty. They soften enough to chew, and they give the water a tomato edge that ends up back in the noodles. Drain hard but not bone dry. A spoonful of leftover water helps the oil and cheese pull together into something that coats the pasta instead of pooling at the bottom of the pot.
