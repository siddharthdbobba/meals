This is a home-prep recipe with no camp step at all, which is the point. Oyster mushrooms are 90 percent water, and the oven drives that off while the thin flour coat and the oil fry the outside dry. What is left is mostly structure: light, crisp, and shelf-stable for a few days without a cooler.

The oil is where the calories are, so do not cut it. Three tablespoons across five ounces of mushrooms sounds like a lot until you remember the mushrooms shrink to about a quarter of their starting volume. Single layer on the sheet matters more than anything else here.
