Meatloaf is a panade problem. Oats soaked in liquid trap fat and water inside the beef, which is why a loaf stays moist where a plain patty does not. That mechanism does not care whether the heat comes from an oven or a skillet, so the oven step is the part that gets cut for camp.

The milk is powdered and the egg comes from a jar, so the only cooler item is the beef itself. Rehydrating the oat mix before the meat goes in matters: dry oats added straight to ground beef pull moisture out of it during cooking and leave you with gravel. Oats also mean no breadcrumbs, so this stays gluten-free as long as the soy sauce does.
