This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. The marinade does its work in the cooler on the drive out, which is the only fridge you have, so the bag goes in frozen and the meat marinates as it thaws. That removes the one-hour wait at camp entirely.

Flank is cheap and lean, which means it goes tough fast. Two things save it: high heat for a short time, and slicing across the grain at the end. The rest ten minutes off the fire is not optional either, or the juice ends up on the cutting board instead of in the taco.
