Chocolate seizes when it gets hot fast or when water gets into it, which is what makes this a lowest-flame recipe rather than a boil-something recipe. The cream is doing two jobs: it buffers the heat and it keeps the sauce loose enough to coat fruit after it comes off the stove. Without it you get a stiff block in the bottom of the pot.

This one needs a cooler, so it is a car-camping or basecamp dessert. Shelf-stable UHT cream in a small carton is the one substitution worth making, since it removes the part of the recipe most likely to spoil. Cut the fruit while the chocolate melts so nothing sits around browning, and wipe the pot before the chocolate sets hard.
