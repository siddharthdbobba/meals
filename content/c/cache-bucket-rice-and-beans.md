This started as pantry advice rather than camp cooking: pick one starch, build every meal off it, keep it all in one place. Rice is the right base for that, and it is the right base on trail too. Instant rice and dehydrated refried beans both rehydrate off residual heat, so the stove runs for the boil and nothing else.

The changes from a home version are the wet ingredients. Canned beans and canned tomatoes are mostly water and steel, so they become bean flakes and tomato powder. Cheese is gone, and the oil replaces the calories it would have carried. The fried onions are there for texture, since a pot of this is otherwise uniformly soft.
