The original is a Romanian ruffled pie with fresh apricots, strawberries, and a sour cream custard. Neither the fruit nor the sour cream survives a weekend in a car, so both got swapped: dried apricots and freeze-dried strawberries, and a custard built from whole milk powder and powdered egg. The cornstarch is insurance. Powdered egg sets a little looser than fresh, and a spoonful of starch pulls it back together.

The wrinkled coil is the whole point of the technique. Rolling the phyllo loosely and bunching it leaves air pockets that the custard soaks into, so you get layers that are soft in the middle and crisp on top instead of one uniform slab. Coals on the lid do the browning; coals underneath will only scorch the bottom, which is why most of the heat goes up top.
