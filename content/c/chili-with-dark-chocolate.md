Chocolate in a savory pot is doing the same job as a long simmer you do not have time for. The cocoa solids are bitter and the tiny amount of sugar rounds off the tomato acid, so twenty minutes on a camp stove tastes closer to an hour on a stove at home. Twenty grams is the whole dose. More and it turns into a dessert sauce.

The original is a home recipe with fresh onion, pepper, and garlic. Dried versions of all three go in with hot water first, which is where the 250 ml goes, and the canned tomatoes and beans bring the rest of the liquid. Cans are heavy, so this one is for a car or a basecamp rather than a long carry. Add the chocolate off the heat: at a simmer the cocoa butter separates and you get grease slicks instead of body.
