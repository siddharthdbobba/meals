This is a restaurant-style creamy slaw moved into a cooler and a freezer bag. Nothing here needs heat, and nothing needs a bowl. The dressing is dissolved first, before the cabbage goes anywhere near it, because sugar will not dissolve into mayonnaise once the vegetables start pulling water out.

The cooler time is not optional. Cabbage sheds water for the first couple of hours and the dressing thins out into something usable rather than a paste. It also softens the shreds enough to eat next to grilled food. Mayonnaise is the one cooler-dependent item, so if you cannot keep the bag cold, this is not the recipe to bring.
