The original is an Instant Pot soup built on two cans of condensed cream of chicken and a tub of whipped chive cream cheese. Neither travels. Powdered soup base plus powdered cream cheese does the same job dry, and dried chives replace the green onions. Shelf-stable gnocchi is the one item worth hunting for: it keeps for months unopened and cooks in four minutes of simmer, which is faster than any pasta of comparable heft.

The gnocchi also sheds starch into the pot, which is most of what makes this a chowder rather than broth with things in it. Add the powders off the heat. Cream cheese powder seizes into gluey knots if it hits a rolling boil, and once that happens no amount of stirring undoes it.
