The original blends cooked cauliflower into its own cream. You cannot carry a blender, so the potato flakes do that job: they thicken the pot and hide the fact that the cauliflower stayed in pieces. Crushing the freeze-dried florets at home gets you closer to the smooth texture without any work at camp.

Half and half and fresh cheese are the other two things that do not travel. Whole milk powder covers both the fat and the body, and it rehydrates better if you stir it in off the heat. Add the potato flakes last and slowly, or you get lumps you cannot break up with a spoon.
