This is a vegan birthday cake adapted for the trail by eliminating the oven and using water instead of fresh soy milk. The batter mixes like a home cake would—margarine and water replace dairy and eggs, baking powder does the rising—but it bakes in a cast-iron skillet over campfire coals rather than in an electric oven. At camp, everything combines in one mixing bowl and cooks in one vessel.

The skillet method browns the bottom and sides while coals heat the top, creating a drier crust than an oven would. Heat management matters: too hot and the outside burns before the inside sets; too cool and you stand around waiting. Aim for steady coals and 25 minutes, but check doneness with a stick rather than trusting time alone. The cake will keep overnight in its skillet if covered, though it's best eaten warm.
