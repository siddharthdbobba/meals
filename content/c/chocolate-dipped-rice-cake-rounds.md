This is a make-at-home snack, not a camp recipe. The store-bought versions of chocolate rice cakes are usually made with palm oil and never come with toppings, and a batch of your own costs less and lets you decide what goes on top.

The coconut oil is the part that matters. A teaspoon thins the melted chocolate enough to spread in a thin layer and makes it set with a clean snap instead of a dull, soft film. Keep the layer thin: too much chocolate and the rice cake goes soggy underneath and the whole thing breaks in the pack. Toppings have to be chopped small, because the only thing holding them on is chocolate that is already starting to set.

No dietary tags here. The chocolate you pick decides whether this is dairy-free or vegan, most rice cakes are gluten-free but not all, and the toppings are wide open.
