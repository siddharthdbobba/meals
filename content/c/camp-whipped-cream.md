The original is three things in a cold bowl and an electric mixer. Neither the bowl nor the mixer travels, and fresh cream is a cooler item, so this version starts from heavy cream powder and rebuilds it with a little water. Reconstituted at about a 4:1 water-to-powder ratio it has enough fat to trap air, which is the only thing that actually matters.

Temperature is the whole game. Fat globules need to be firm to hold a foam, so warm cream just makes sweet soup no matter how long you shake. Use the coldest water on hand and keep the bottle in shade or moving water while it hydrates. Sugar goes in from the start here rather than partway through, because a shaken bottle mixes it evenly on its own.
