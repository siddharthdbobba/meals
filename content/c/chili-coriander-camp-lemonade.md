The original is a muddled drink: fresh chili, fresh cilantro, lemon juice, soda water. None of that survives a pack. Dried chili flakes and cracked coriander seed carry the same two flavors in a form that weighs nothing and does not rot, and lemon powder replaces the juice at a fraction of the load.

The cracking and the shake matter more than the sitting time. Both chili heat and coriander aroma live in oils that only come out under pressure, so the sugar-and-splash shake at the start is doing the work a cocktail shaker would. Skip it and you have sour sugar water with debris in it.
