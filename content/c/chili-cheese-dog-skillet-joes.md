This is a home skillet dinner that survives the trip to a campsite mostly intact, so it stays a car-camping recipe rather than a backpacking one. The beef needs a cooler and the cheese does too, which is why it is a first-night meal.

The chili sauce and salsa carry both the liquid and the seasoning, so no water goes in and nothing needs measuring. Simmering it down matters more than it looks: a thin mix soaks straight through a bun and you eat dinner off your boots. Stirring the cheese in off the heat keeps it melting into the meat instead of seizing into strings on the pan bottom.
