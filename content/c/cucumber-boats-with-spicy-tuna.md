This is a lunch you build rather than cook, which makes it worth carrying on a day out or a paddle with a cooler. Nothing here needs heat. The only real technique is salting the cucumber and letting it drain, and skipping that step is how you end up eating soup out of a limp shell.

The rice is what turns a snack into food. Cook it at home the night before and it keeps fine for a day in a cooler or a cold pack. Tuna, mayo, and rice together get you protein, fat, and carbohydrate in one bite. The jalapeño and lemon are there because cold mayo needs something sharp against it.
