The original of this is a fridge recipe: fresh eggs, fresh milk, diced onion, cooked ham. All four are cooler items, and a cooler is the thing you leave behind first. Egg powder, milk powder, dried onion, and a shelf-stable ham pouch do the same jobs and add up to about five ounces a person.

The milk powder is not filler. Powdered egg alone sets tight and dry; the extra fat and protein slow that down and keep the scramble soft. Low heat matters more than anything else here. Rehydrated egg has no forgiveness in it, and a hot spot in a titanium pot will make one part of your breakfast into rubber while the rest is still liquid.
