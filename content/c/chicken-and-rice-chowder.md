The original is butter, a flour roux, and half and half. None of that travels. Whole milk powder does the same job as the cream once it is rehydrated, and the flour still thickens as long as you slurry it into cold water first. Dump dry flour into a hot pot and you get lumps you cannot whisk out with a spoon.

Parboiled rice is worth seeking out. It cooks in the broth without shedding starch, so the soup stays a soup and the grains stay grains, even the next morning if you carry leftovers. Regular white rice works but will turn the pot to porridge. The foil pouch chicken goes in at the end because it is already cooked and simmering only shreds it into nothing.
