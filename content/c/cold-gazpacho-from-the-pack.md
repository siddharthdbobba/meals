The original is a blender recipe: canned tomatoes, jarred peppers, oil, and two hours in the fridge. None of that travels. Tomato powder and dried pepper flakes carry the same soup at a fraction of the weight, and the cold soak does the work the blender used to do — twenty minutes of hydration gets you a thick, drinkable soup instead of grit in water.

The oil is not optional. It is most of the calories and it is what keeps a tomato drink from reading as a snack. Vinegar is the other half of the seasoning; without acid this tastes flat and sweet, which is the usual failure mode of powdered tomato. Add it last so you can judge how much you need.
