The trick is precooked potatoes braised in broth at home. They arrive at camp already seasoned and tender. Over fire they fry to crispy edges without risk of a raw center. Bacon renders fat to cook everything in; peppers and onion soften around it; eggs arrive last and bind the whole thing; cheese finishes it. One skillet, one fire, one pot to wash.

This is inherently a cooler meal—eggs and fresh peppers need cold storage—so save it for car-camping or a basecamp where you have a working fridge. Do the potato braise the night before so nothing goes to waste.
