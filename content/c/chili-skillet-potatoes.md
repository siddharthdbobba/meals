Potatoes are heavy and they don't dehydrate well at home, so this one stays in car-camping territory. That is fine. The tradeoff you get is a real fried potato, which no rehydrated flake will ever be.

The two things that decide how this comes out are dryness and crowding. Wet slices drop the pan temperature and steam themselves grey, and a pan packed edge to edge does the same. Dry the cut faces, cook in batches, and leave them alone for the full five minutes per side. Two teaspoons of coarse pepper reads high on paper but it is the backbone of the seasoning here, not a garnish.
