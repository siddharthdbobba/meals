Kheer is a milk pudding simmered for an hour, which is an hour of fuel nobody carries. This version gets to the same place by boiling the rice in water first and building the milk back in at the end with whole milk powder. The powder rehydrates instantly and never has to sit over a flame, so there is nothing to scorch and no long simmer to pay for.

The ghee and the toasted cashews are doing most of the calorie work, and the cardamom is what makes it read as kheer rather than sweet rice. Toast the nuts first in the same pot you cook in — it costs one minute and one less thing to wash.
