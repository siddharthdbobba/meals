Bean salads dehydrate well because the parts that matter, texture and acid, come back with cold water. The corn and onion stay a little firm no matter how long they sit, which is the point: after three days of noodles you want something that resists the spoon.

Beans and corn are the slow items. Chickpeas are the slowest, so use a bean mix without them or plan on a much longer soak. Add the oil at the end rather than at home, since fat coats the pieces and slows rehydration. The lemon powder is doing the work fresh juice would, and it weighs nothing.
