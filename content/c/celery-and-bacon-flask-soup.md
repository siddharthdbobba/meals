The home version leans on butter, stock, and double cream, none of which travel. Whole milk powder plus butter powder gets close on fat and body without a cooler, and potato flakes do the job a blender does at home: they take a pot of loose vegetable pieces and make it one smooth thing.

The long simmer is not optional. Celery is stringy and it turns bitter if you rush it, and dried celery in particular needs the time to take water back on. Everything dairy goes in after the flame is out.
