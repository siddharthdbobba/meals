The original is a fridge-clearing pot of fresh vegetables poached with whole chicken breasts. None of that travels. Dried vegetables and freeze-dried chicken carry the same soup at a fraction of the weight, and the bouillon does the work the poaching liquid used to do.

Tomato powder is what keeps this from tasting like hot salt water. It thickens the broth slightly and adds the acid that dried vegetables lose. The oil goes in off the heat because this is a lean meal otherwise, and a bowl of vegetables with no fat will not hold you until morning.
