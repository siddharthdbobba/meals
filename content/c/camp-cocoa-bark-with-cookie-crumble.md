This is a home-kitchen bark reworked into a carry item. The original used butter, which goes rancid in a pack, so coconut oil takes its place. It thins the chocolate the same way and sets firmer at room temperature.

The cookies and candy have to be pressed into the chocolate before it sets, or they shear off in the bag and you carry crumbs. Quarter-inch thickness is the compromise: thin enough to snap cleanly, thick enough to survive a day of being sat on. Cold weather is where this shines. Warm weather is where it becomes a mess.
