Instant grits thicken into paste when hot water sits on them for fifteen minutes. The freeze-dried sausage concentrates fat and salt into a form that packs small. Cheese powder dissolves straight in, turning the whole thing from porridge into something with body.

This is not gentle grits and does not try to be. It is fuel, and the fat is the point. Boil one pot, pour one bag, and you have a hot meal in twenty minutes that will carry you through a cold morning or a long day.
