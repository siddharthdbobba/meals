Couscous is the laziest starch on the trail: it does not cook so much as swell, so a pot only has to reach a boil once and then it can sit off the flame. That leaves fuel and time for nothing else, which is the whole argument for it. The problem is that plain couscous reconstitutes thin and bland.

Whole milk powder fixes that. It has enough fat to give the pot body and to round off the curry, and it does it without a cooler. The nuts and the meat stick are calorie ballast rather than flavor: 50 g of couscous alone is a snack, not a dinner. Add the meat stick at the end so it warms through instead of turning greasy.
