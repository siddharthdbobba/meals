This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Raw chorizo and eggs both need to stay cold, so it belongs at a car campsite or a basecamp with a working icebox. What it gives you in exchange is a breakfast that feeds four out of one skillet with almost no seasoning work, because the chorizo carries the spice and the fat for everything else in the pan.

The potatoes are the part people get wrong. Cut them small at home, dry them off, and then let them sit still against hot cast iron. Browning is contact time, not stirring. Eggs go in at the end so the yolks are still soft when the pan comes off the coals.
