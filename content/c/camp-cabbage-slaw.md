Cabbage is the one vegetable that earns its weight on a trip without refrigeration. A whole head has its own packaging, it does not bruise, and it stays crisp days after lettuce has turned to slime in the bottom of a dry bag. Cut only what you need for a meal and the rest keeps.

The dressing is packets rather than a jar. Single-serve mayo is shelf-stable until you open it, which is the whole trick here, and the mustard and celery seed cut through the fat so it reads as a side dish and not a bowl of mayonnaise. The original called for frozen orange juice concentrate, which is not a thing you carry; a spoonful of sugar in the soy-and-oil version does the same job.
