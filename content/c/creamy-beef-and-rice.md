Freeze-dried beef is dense with protein and light to carry. Sour cream powder does the real work—it dissolves into the broth and turns plain rice into something with body and richness, without the weight or fridge requirements of actual sour cream.

Fifteen minutes of sitting time means you're not hovering over a stove. Wrap the pot in a cozy if you're at altitude or in cold weather, or just use your rain jacket. The rice absorbs the liquid passively while the sauce builds itself.
