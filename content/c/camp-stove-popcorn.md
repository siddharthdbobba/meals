The original version of this uses butter and an Instant Pot on saute. Butter does not travel without a cooler, and coconut oil is the better trail fat anyway: it is solid in a jar, it has a high enough smoke point to run a pot at popping temperature, and it does not go rancid the way an open bottle of olive oil will over a week.

The lid gap is the part people get wrong. Popcorn is mostly steam escaping a kernel, and if that steam stays in the pot it condenses back onto what already popped. Leave a vent and the finished popcorn stays dry. Keep the pot moving too, since unpopped kernels sitting on a thin backpacking pot burn in seconds over a canister stove's narrow flame.
