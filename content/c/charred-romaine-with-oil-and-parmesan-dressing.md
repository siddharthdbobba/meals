Grilling lettuce sounds like a mistake until you do it. The heat pulls water out of the cut face and leaves a bit of smoke behind, which gives romaine something to say next to grilled meat. The core matters: cut through it, not around it, or you are chasing loose leaves across the grate.

The dressing is oil and acid rather than egg and mayonnaise, which is the whole reason this one belongs in camp. Nothing in the jar needs refrigeration, so it can be mixed at home a week out and ride in a pack pocket. The lettuce is the only cooler item, and it is the only thing that will not wait.
