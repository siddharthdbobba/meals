The original of this is a party platter, which does not survive a car trip. Cups fix that. Each person gets their own, nothing slides off, and there is no board to wash or wind to fight over. Assembly is the whole recipe.

Cube the cheese at home. Cutting hard cheese on a picnic table with a pocket knife is slow and produces a mess, and a sealed bag of cubes takes the cooler cold better than a block does. Everything else is layered dry, so the cups do not go soggy sitting out through an afternoon.
