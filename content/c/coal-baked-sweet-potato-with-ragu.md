The original of this is a frozen batch of ragu thawed at camp, which assumes a cooler and a short trip. Dehydrating the sauce instead drops most of the weight, removes the cooler entirely, and gets you the same thing after ten minutes of soaking. Dry it at home after the sauce has already cooked down hard — thin sauce dries into flakes that never come back together.

The triple foil wrap is not padding for its own sake. A single layer scorches through against the coals and the burnt skin tastes its way into the flesh. Three layers hold a pocket of steam around the potato so it bakes rather than chars. The oil at the end matters too: sweet potato is mostly starch and sugar, and the fat is what turns it into a dinner you stop thinking about an hour later.
