This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. The loaf is baked and frozen at home, which does two jobs: it keeps the cooler cold on the drive in, and it means the coals only have to heat the thing through and cook the vegetables, not cook raw ground beef in the dark. Forty-five minutes is honest for a thawed loaf and quartered potatoes.

The foil is the actual technique. Three layers, seams turned different directions, because a packet that splits in the coals dumps the rendered fat into the fire and you end up with dry meat and ashy carrots. Fat from the 85/15 chuck runs down into the potatoes while it cooks, which is why the vegetables go around the loaf and not under it. Cleanup is one crumpled ball of foil packed out.
