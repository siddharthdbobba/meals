Freezer cooking works because you cook once and eat six times. The trail version is the same idea with the freezer removed: batch the assembly at home, carry the result dry. Nothing here needs cold, so a session at the kitchen counter buys you a week of dinners that weigh five ounces each.

Red lentils are what make it a one-pot. They cook in the same window as small pasta and they thicken the water into sauce as they collapse, so there is no straining and no pot of grey starch to dump in camp. The powdered dairy goes in off the heat because cheese powder in boiling water turns grainy. Tomato powder does the work a jar of sauce would, at about an ounce.
