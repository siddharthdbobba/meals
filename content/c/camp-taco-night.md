This is a car-camping meal, so the cooler does most of the work. The only real change from the home version is cabbage instead of lettuce. Lettuce wilts into slime after a day on ice; shredded cabbage stays crisp for most of a week and takes salsa better anyway.

Drain the fat before the seasoning goes in. Taco seasoning is mostly starch and salt, and it will bind whatever grease is in the pan into a slick coating that ends up on your hands and the shells. A few tablespoons of water after draining gives the starch something to thicken instead.
