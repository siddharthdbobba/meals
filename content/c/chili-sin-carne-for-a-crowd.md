This is group food. Ten people, two pots, one long simmer, and almost all of it comes out of cans and jars. The soy crumble goes in dry and rehydrates in the tomato instead of in plain water, which is the whole point: it drinks the stock and spice rather than diluting them.

The sugar is not there to sweeten it. Canned tomato is sharp, and two tablespoons flattens that out so the cumin and paprika read. Corn chips are the last ingredient because they are the only crunch in the pot, and they keep the whole thing gluten-free as long as you check the bag.
