The original is a baked casserole made from leftovers, which is two things camp cannot supply: an oven and a fridge. Canned chicken replaces the leftover roast, instant rice replaces cooked rice, and freeze-dried vegetables replace the steamed mix. Evaporated milk and condensed soup were already shelf-stable, so those stay as they are. Ghee stands in for butter because it does not care how warm the bin gets.

Everything cooks in one pot instead of a baking dish. The rice absorbs the soup and milk directly, which is why the water is measured rather than eyeballed: too much and it goes to porridge, too little and the grains stay chalky. Coals on the lid brown the top the way an oven would, but the pot has to come off direct heat first or the bottom scorches before the rice is done.
