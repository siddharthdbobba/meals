The original is ice cream in a cone, which is not a thing you can carry. What survives the translation is the actual structure: something rich and cold against something tart, scooped into something crisp. Powdered coconut milk hydrated with as little water as it will take gives you the fat and the body without a cooler, and it whips up thicker cold than warm.

Freeze-dried berries do the work the fruit did. They rehydrate on contact with the cream, so the tartness stays sharp and localized instead of bleeding out into a uniform pink. Add the water slowly. Overshoot it and you have a drink, and there is no way back.
