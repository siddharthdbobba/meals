This is a car-camping dessert, not a trail one. Nothing here survives a pack, so the work happens in your kitchen and the cooler does the rest. The freezer pop is the point: coconut fat plus cream freezes soft instead of into a solid ice brick, and the maple syrup keeps the texture from going grainy since sugar lowers the freezing point.

The salt matters more than it looks. Coconut and maple both read sweet and one-note, and a pinch pulls them apart. The chocolate shell is optional, but the coconut oil in it is not — it thins the chocolate enough to coat and then snaps hard against the frozen pop instead of sliding off.
