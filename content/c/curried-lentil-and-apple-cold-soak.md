The source version leans on a cooler: greek yogurt dressing, fresh apple, fresh celery. None of that travels. The swap is powdered milk plus citric acid, which rehydrates into something tangy and thick enough to coat the lentils, and dried celery, which gives the same background flavor without the weight of a vegetable that is mostly water.

Dehydrating the lentils at home is the part that matters. Raw lentils will not cold soak into anything you want to eat, but cooked-then-dried lentils come back in about ninety minutes of cold water and hold their shape. Freeze-dried apple goes in at the end because it stays crisp for a few minutes and then does not, so there is no point stirring it in early.
