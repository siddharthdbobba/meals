This started as a home biscuit recipe, which means fridge butter and fresh milk. Both are swapped here: powdered shortening cuts into flour dry and rehydrates when the water goes in, and milk powder mixed into the flour at home means one bag and one pour at camp.

Heat placement is the whole game with a dutch oven. Most of the coals belong on the lid so the tops set before the sugar on the bottom scorches. A thin ring underneath is enough. The twisting is not decoration either — it opens up the surface so more of each piece gets coated.
