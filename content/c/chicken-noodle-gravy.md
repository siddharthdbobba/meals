Freeze-dried chicken and vegetables rehydrate while the noodles cook in the same pot of boiling water. The gravy mix both seasons and thickens everything as it hydrates. Mozzarella disappears into the sauce instead of staying visible.

Freeze-dried chicken is protein with minimal water weight—a handful delivers thirty grams. The gravy mix is fat and seasoning concentrated into powder. This is built for weight counting.
