Home french toast needs eggs and milk, which is two refrigerated things you are not carrying. Whole egg powder and milk powder solve both, and they hydrate into a batter that behaves close enough to the real one. The ratio matters more than the brand: too much water and the bread turns to mush in the pan.

Stale bread is the other half of it. Fresh bread is already saturated with its own moisture and falls apart on the flip. A dry, dense loaf soaks up the batter and holds together. Cook it slower than you think, over coals rather than flame, or you will get a burnt shell around a raw middle.
