This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one, because a raw whole chicken needs a cooler and a Dutch oven needs a fire pit. Cook it night one, while the ice is still solid. The method is a slow cooker's, moved onto coals: onions as a rack, a shallow pool of water, and low steady heat from above and below rather than a hot oven blasting one side.

The water is doing two jobs. It keeps the bottom of the pot from scorching over uneven coals, and by the end it is stock. Pour it into a bottle, drop it back in the cooler, and it turns into the base for rice or noodles the next day. Coals on the lid matter more than coals underneath — that is what browns the skin instead of steaming it grey.
