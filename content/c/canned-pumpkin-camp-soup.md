This is a home soup with the fridge taken out of it. Fresh onion becomes dried minced onion, fresh milk becomes milk powder mixed into a slurry off the heat, and the immersion blender goes away entirely — canned pumpkin is already smooth, so the only lumps come from the onion, and those are fine.

The baking mix earns its tablespoon. Without cream, that starch is what keeps the soup from separating into water and pumpkin sludge halfway through the bowl. Note the can weight before you commit: 15 ounces of wet puree is car-camping and short-paddle territory, not something you carry uphill.
