Bannock is a flat quick bread that leans on baking powder instead of yeast, so there is no proofing and no timeline. The cornmeal and oats do more than add flavor: they cut the gluten, which keeps the dough from turning rubbery when you knead it inside a bag by hand.

The two failure modes are both heat. Too hot and the outside browns in three minutes with a wet seam through the center; too thick and the same thing happens even on low. Keep the patty thin, keep the flame down, and flip often. Powdered milk stands in for the fresh milk this kind of bread usually gets, and it carries at ambient temperature all week.
