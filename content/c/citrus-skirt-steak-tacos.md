Skirt steak is the right cut for a fire because it is thin. It cooks through before the outside burns, which is the whole problem with grilling over coals you cannot dial down. Cut it across the grain when it comes off or it eats like rope.

The original called for a crema of sour cream thinned with heavy cream. Two dairy items in a cooler for one sauce is not worth it, so lime juice does the thinning instead and the acid earns its place. The slaw gets a spoonful of that same crema plus honey, which is the only thing standing between grilled meat in a tortilla and something you want a second of. Grilling the orange wedges is not decoration; the heat pulls out sugar and the juice hits the steak warm.
