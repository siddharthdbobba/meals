This is car-camping food, and the cans are the reason. Three of them plus a rice bag runs close to eleven ounces per person before you add a drop of water, which is fine when the cooler is twenty feet away and pointless if you are ten miles in. The payoff is that nothing needs rehydrating and nothing spoils.

Draining matters more than it sounds. Canned beans and mushrooms both carry a lot of packing liquid, and if it goes into the pot you end up with soup that will not thicken. Dump it, then let the rice absorb what steam is left. The chili powder and seasoned salt are doing the heavy lifting on flavor; canned protein is bland on its own and will stay that way if you skip them.
