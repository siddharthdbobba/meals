This is a hot-water chocolate cake, which is why it works in a dutch oven at all. Boiling water blooms the cocoa and thins the batter enough that it self-levels over uneven coals, so you do not need a mixer, a whisk, or a flat surface. The batter looks broken and soupy going in. Leave it alone.

Two things changed for camp. Fresh eggs became powdered egg, which rehydrates in the water you are already boiling and does not become a problem in a warm cooler. The milk in the frosting became milk powder mixed with water at camp. Heat management is the whole skill here: coals on top do the baking, coals underneath only burn the bottom, so keep the ratio lopsided and keep turning.
