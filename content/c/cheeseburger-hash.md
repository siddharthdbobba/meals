The original is a casserole: browned beef under cheese under a layer of frozen tater tots, forty minutes in an oven. None of that survives a pack. Frozen potatoes need a freezer, Velveeta needs a fridge, and a 13x9 pan needs a kitchen. What is left once you strip those out is the actual idea, which is beef and potato bound by cheese and sharpened with mustard and Worcestershire.

Dehydrated hash browns do the potato work and rehydrate in a covered pot off the flame, which saves fuel. Cheese powder and oil replace the melted block cheese; the powder alone tastes thin, so the oil is not optional. The pickles matter more than they look. Everything else in the pot is soft, salty, and the same temperature, and a cold sour bite at the end is what keeps it from reading as mush.
