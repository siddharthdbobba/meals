A holiday ham is an oven project built on refrigeration, which is exactly what a campsite lacks. Canned ham solves both problems. It is already cooked and already cured, so the fire is only warming it and setting the glaze, not cooking meat to temperature.

The glaze is the part worth getting right. Instant cider mix carries the apple and the spice without a jug of liquid, and the brown sugar gives it something to caramelize with. Opening the packet for the last ten minutes matters: sealed foil steams, and steam will not build a crust. Dried apples rehydrate in the runoff and end up as the side dish.
