The original was baked in a slow cooker with eggs, block cream cheese, and a bottled iced coffee drink. None of that travels. Cream cheese powder and butter powder carry the same fat and tang dry, instant coffee replaces the coffee milk, and instant pudding mix does the structural work the eggs did in the heat.

Water is the variable to respect. Too much and it never sets, and there is no oven to save it. Start at 120 ml for two servings and add a teaspoon at a time only if the powder refuses to hydrate. The crust is pressed rather than baked, so pack it hard or it stays loose crumbs under the filling.
