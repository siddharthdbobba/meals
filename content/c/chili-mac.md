The work here happens at home. Pasta that has already been cooked and dried rehydrates off the heat, so camp is one boil and a soak instead of a rolling pot and a strainer. That saves fuel and it saves you from dumping starchy water somewhere it shouldn't go.

The beans and corn are small amounts on purpose: they are there for body and sweetness, not bulk. Oil and cheddar carry the calories and the chili powder, which is fat-soluble and tastes flat without them. Cheese travels fine on a cool trip; in real heat, swap in freeze-dried cheddar and add it with the spices.
