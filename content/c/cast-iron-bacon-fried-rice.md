Fried rice at home starts with cold leftover rice, which is the one thing camp never has. Instant rice fills in: hydrate it first, then get it out of the pan so the skillet can run dry and hot. Wet rice in a wet pan steams and turns to paste.

Cast iron is the reason this works over a fire. It takes a while to come up to temperature but then holds it while you keep adding cold food, so the rice actually browns instead of stalling. Preheat it gradually and off to the side of the coals; a screaming hot spot will scorch the seasoning and stick everything to it.
