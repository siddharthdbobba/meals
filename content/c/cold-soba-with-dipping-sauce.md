Zaru soba is the anti-stew: noodles served cold without broth, meant for summer and quick meals. The dipping sauce does all the work—intensely salty and sweet, meant to coat each bite. On the trail, boil your noodles, drain them well, and the water you save becomes your sauce. You're not eating this to warm up.

Soba noodles cook in four minutes flat and cool just as fast. The salty-sweet sauce is strong enough that a little goes a long way; you'll dip each noodle, not pour broth over them. This is as close as you get to a fresh, cold lunch without carrying ice.
