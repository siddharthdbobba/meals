The original is cream cheese and whipped cream, neither of which survives a day in a pack. Instant cheesecake pudding plus milk powder does the same job: the pudding's starch sets cold, and the added cream powder puts the fat back so it tastes rich instead of chalky. Citric acid replaces the lemon juice and does the tang that makes cheesecake taste like cheesecake rather than sweet paste.

Water ratio matters more than anything else here. At 120 ml for two servings it sets thick enough to layer; go past 150 ml and you have a drink. Mix in the bag, not the mug, so you can knead out the lumps without a whisk.
