The home version starts with live lobsters and a pan of fresh tomatoes. Neither travels. Canned lobster meat is already cooked and shelf-stable, and dry-packed sun-dried tomatoes rehydrate in the pasta water, which means one pot and no cutting board.

Reserving the starchy cooking water is the part worth doing. Without it the oil sits on the noodles and the seasoning slides to the bottom of the pot. Lemon powder covers what fresh juice would do without a lemon rolling around your pack.
