The source here was a nutrition write-up rather than a recipe, so this is built from the one thing it actually establishes: guava carries well as dried fruit and is unusually high in vitamin C. Fresh guava is not a trail item. Dried slices are, and they rehydrate into something worth drinking.

The acid and salt are doing the real work. Sugar water alone tastes flat after a few miles; a little citric acid brings the fruit forward and the salt makes it read as a drink instead of a syrup. Do not throw the soaked fruit away when the bottle is empty.
