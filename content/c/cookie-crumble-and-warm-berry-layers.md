This is a Norwegian leftover-cookie dessert reworked for a pack. The original leans on frozen berries and a tub of vanilla quark, neither of which travels. Freeze-dried blackberries rehydrate in a few minutes of simmering and throw off the same tart syrup, and full-fat yogurt powder mixed thin gives you the soft layer without a cooler.

The layering is not decoration. Cookies go soft the moment they meet liquid, so they stay dry until the mug is assembled. Sugar and the short heat pull juice out of the berries, which is what soaks into the crumbs on the way down.
