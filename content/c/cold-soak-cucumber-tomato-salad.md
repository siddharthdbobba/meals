The original is a fridge salad: fresh cucumber, ripe tomatoes, raw onion. None of that survives a pack. Dehydrating the vegetables at home solves it. Cucumber rehydrates into something closer to a quick pickle than a fresh slice, which is fine, and sun-dried tomatoes come back sweeter and more concentrated than the fresh ones ever were.

Draining matters more than any other step. The vegetables release water for a while after they look done, and if that water is still in the jar the oil and vinegar just float on top of it. Pour it off, then dress. The honey is not for sweetness so much as to keep the vinegar and lemon from reading as sharp on their own.
