Dried carrot is stubborn. Whole diced pieces will still be crunchy after a normal oatmeal soak, which is why both the oats and the carrots get broken down in a blender before the trip. Smaller pieces rehydrate in the same five minutes the oats need, so one pot and one boil covers both.

The milk powder is doing more than flavor. It gives the mix enough body that the sugar and cinnamon hang on the oats instead of settling into the bottom of the pot. Coconut goes on at the end on purpose: stirred in early it turns limp, on top it stays dry and gives the bowl some texture.
