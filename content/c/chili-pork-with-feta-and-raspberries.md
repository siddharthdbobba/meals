This is a car-camp dinner, not a pack-in one. Pork loin needs a cooler, and there is no way around that. What makes it worth carrying is the ingredient count: four things on the grate and two things sprinkled on after.

The shallots on either end of the skewer are structural. They hold the steaks pressed together so the meat cooks as a block instead of curling and drying at the edges. Finishing over indirect heat matters for the same reason: lean pork goes from done to tough fast, and the cool side of the grate lets the middle catch up without punishing the outside. Chili paste goes on off the heat so it does not burn, and the feta stays raw because it does not melt so much as leak. Fresh raspberries do not survive a cooler, so the freeze-dried version does the job here. They soften on contact with the hot meat and give you the sour note the fat and salt need.
