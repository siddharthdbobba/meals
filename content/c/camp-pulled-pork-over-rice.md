This started as a slow cooker recipe and got compressed. Canned pork is already cooked through, so the thirty minutes on the stove are doing one job: reducing the sauce and breaking the meat down enough to shred. There is nothing to tenderize.

The fresh onions and garlic became dried, and the fresh pork became canned, which is where most of the weight savings live. Cook the tomato paste in the oil before any liquid goes in — that step is not optional, and skipping it is the difference between a sauce and a bowl of sharp tomato. The vinegar goes in at the same time as everything else and cuts the brown sugar.
