This is a fast food sandwich rebuilt out of a cooler, and the only real engineering problem is water. Lettuce, tomato, and pickle brine all leak, and bread absorbs everything. Cheese against the bread on both faces slows that down, and draining the vegetables at home does the rest.

The herb topping that normally goes on the bread does not survive a cooler, so it goes inside as a dry shake of oregano, garlic powder, and parmesan. Same flavor, no baking. Rated day-one because the meat and cheese need cold; the salami-and-hard-cheese variation in the notes is the version that travels past that.
