This is a bechamel with the fridge taken out of it. The roux is the whole recipe: flour cooked in fat until the raw taste is gone, then loosened with liquid a little at a time. Cook the flour too briefly and the soup tastes like paste. Add the liquid all at once and it clumps.

The camp changes are the dairy and the vegetables. Evaporated milk sits on a shelf for a year and thickens better than fresh milk anyway. Fresh celery becomes dried celery flakes, which rehydrate in the simmer and weigh almost nothing. Canned chicken goes in last because it is already cooked and boiling it just shreds it into strings.
