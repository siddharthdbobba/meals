This requires cast iron and a hot fire, which means basecamp only. The reason is simple: you get melted cheese and a browned crust, which a stove cannot produce.

The filling adapts fresh ingredients for shelf life: powdered cheese and tomato powder, dried pimentos, bacon bits. The cooking challenge is heat balance. Too hot and the bottom burns before the top melts; too cool and nothing happens. Cover with foil if the top threatens to scorch. Twenty-five minutes gets you pizza for two.
