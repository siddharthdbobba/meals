The source version is raw tartare with egg yolks, which is a fine restaurant dish and a bad idea on a tailgate. Field-dressed meat, a cooler that opens twenty times a day, and no sink is the wrong combination for raw diced protein. So this keeps the flavor structure — garlic, capers, chives, lemon, good oil — and applies it to seared cubes instead. The sauce goes on off the heat so the garlic and herbs stay sharp rather than toasting.

Backstrap has almost no intramuscular fat, so it goes from tender to dry in under a minute. Cook it hot and fast in two batches, pull it pink, and let the oil in the sauce do the work fat would normally do. Everything but the meat is shelf-stable and premixed at home, so camp is two bags and a skillet.
