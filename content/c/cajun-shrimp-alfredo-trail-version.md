The original is a skillet dish leaning on heavy cream, cream cheese, and fresh shrimp, none of which survive a pack. Whole milk powder plus grated parmesan plus a teaspoon of cornstarch does the same job: fat, salt, and body. The starch already in the pasta water helps, which is why you never drain.

Freeze-dried shrimp is the one ingredient worth buying specially. It rehydrates in cool water in about ten minutes and does not turn to rubber the way a boiled retort pouch does. Soaking it in the Cajun seasoning means the spice ends up in the shrimp instead of only in the sauce.
