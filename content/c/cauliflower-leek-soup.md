The original is a home-kitchen soup: fresh leeks, a whole head of cauliflower, and an immersion blender. All three are problems on trail. Dehydrated cauliflower and leek carry the same flavor at a fraction of the weight, and the back of a spoon does what the blender did, just coarser. It will not be silky. It will be thick and closer to a chowder, which is arguably better after a long day.

The roux is the part worth not skipping. A tablespoon of flour cooked in oil is what makes this read as creamy without any cream, and it is why the soup has body at all. Cook it the full minute or you get a floury aftertaste that no amount of pepper covers. If a stove-side roux is more fuss than you want, the potato-flake variation gets you most of the way there.
