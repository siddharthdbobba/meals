The original was a home pot of chili built on cubed deer meat instead of ground, browned in fat with fresh onion, garlic, peppers, and celery. Cubes hold their texture where ground meat disappears, which is the whole point, and it survives the trip to the trail better than most chili does. Everything that came out of a garden here comes out of a dehydrator instead.

Cubed meat is the one thing that will not shortcut. Dry raw venison and you get gravel that no amount of simmering fixes, so cook it through at home before it goes in the dehydrator. The tomato powder is doing the thickening; without it you have spiced broth with meat in it. Hot sauce goes in at the end so you can set the heat per person rather than per pot.
