Freeze-dried ingredients pack a full meal into the weight of a granola bar. The cheese does the real work here—it carries fat into rice and meat in a way that makes this feel like actual food instead of warm broth with bits. The dish will look watery halfway through the rest period. That is normal. Stir it well and it comes together.

Cold nights are when you want this. You get protein and calories with no thought and minimal cleanup.
