Hot brewing exists to pull tannins and caffeine out of leaf fast. Cold brewing does the same thing slower, and cold brew bags are just cut finer so the slow part takes minutes instead of hours. On trail that trade is free: no fuel, no boil, no twenty minutes waiting for a pot to cool down enough to drink.

The other half of the point is that it fixes water you are already carrying. Filtered creek water sitting in a warm bottle tastes like the bottle. Two bags and a pinch of citric acid cover that, and the caffeine is a real argument for an afternoon climb. Pull the bags on schedule — steeping all day is where this goes wrong.
