This is a home kitchen sandwich moved outdoors with almost nothing lost, because everything in it already cooks in one pan. The bacon renders the fat, the fat fries the eggs, and the same fat toasts the bread. Three components, one skillet, one wash-up.

Covering the pan instead of flipping is the part worth keeping. A flipped egg on an uneven camp burner breaks and runs out the back of the sandwich. Trapped steam sets the top of the yolk just enough to survive being picked up. Arugula over lettuce is a packing decision, not a flavor one — it wilts instead of shattering, and it fits in a container the size of a fist.
