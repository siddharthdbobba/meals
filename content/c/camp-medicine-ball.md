The coffee-shop version pours in bottled lemonade, which is mostly water and glass. Powdered lemonade does the same job at a fraction of the weight, and you are already boiling water anyway. Two tea bags in 400 ml is a deliberately strong steep, because the lemonade and honey both dilute what you started with.

This is not food. It is 130 calories and no protein, and it earns its place in the bag for other reasons: warm liquid before bed, something to hold in cold hands, and a sore throat that stops being the only thing you think about. Honey coats; mint numbs a little. Both are doing more than the flavor suggests.
