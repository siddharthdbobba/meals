The original is a leftovers dish: boiled potatoes and cold roast beef, fried down together. Canned corned beef replaces the leftovers, and dried diced potatoes replace the boiling pot, so the whole thing collapses into one pan and one heat source. The potatoes rehydrate in the same water they will later fry in, which is why there is no second pot to wash.

Browning is the part worth waiting for. Wet potatoes steam and go grey, so drain hard and then leave the pan alone between stirs. The corned beef goes in last and barely gets cooked; it is already cooked, and the only thing more heat does is break the chunks into mush. Season at the end, if at all, since the can carries most of the salt this needs.
