This is a casserole taken apart and rebuilt in one pot. The roux and milk do the work a can of soup usually does, and the wild rice is the reason it eats like dinner instead of gravy — it holds its chew even after sitting in liquid.

The camp changes are small. Fresh milk becomes powdered milk mixed with water, and broth comes from bouillon rather than a carton. Cook the rice at home and carry it cold if you want to cut 45 minutes off camp time; otherwise start it before you touch anything else. The chicken is the only thing that truly needs the cooler, so freeze it solid and pack it deep.
