Couscous is the only grain that cooks off the stove. You boil water, dump it in, and let the pot do the rest under a lid while you deal with your feet. That matters more than it sounds on a day you walked twenty-five kilometers.

The chorizo is doing two jobs. It carries the protein, and the fat it renders becomes the sauce, which is why it goes in the water before the grain rather than on top at the end. Tomato powder and paprika stand in for the vegetables you cannot carry. If you skip the olive oil you get a dry pot of grain.
