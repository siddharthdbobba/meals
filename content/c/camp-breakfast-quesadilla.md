The home version fries raw bacon and cracks fresh eggs, neither of which belongs in a pack. Precooked bacon crumbles and whole egg powder do the same job at a fraction of the weight and with no cold chain. Hard cheese grated at home is the last piece: it keeps for days unrefrigerated and melts well enough to glue the tortillas together.

Low heat matters more than anything else here. A camp stove concentrates its flame in a small circle, so a hot pan scorches the tortilla before the cheese has softened. Keep the flame low, expect the middle to brown first, and move the quesadilla around the pan if you have no diffuser.
