Millet is the underused trail grain. It costs about the same as rice, cooks in the same pot, and holds more fat without turning to paste, which matters when the calories are coming from oil and seeds rather than the grain itself. It is also free of wheat, so this one goes to people who cannot eat couscous or ramen.

The black-eyed peas go in late on purpose. Dehydrated legumes rehydrate faster than millet cooks, and adding them at the start just means twenty minutes of them falling apart. Sunflower seeds off the heat keep some crunch; stir them in early and they soften into the mash.
