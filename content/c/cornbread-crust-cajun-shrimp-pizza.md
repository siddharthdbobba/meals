Cornbread makes a better camp crust than a wheat dough does. It is forgiving about kneading, it does not need a long rise, and the coarse grind holds up under wet toppings instead of going to paste. The small amount of yeast and the twenty minutes near the fire are enough to lift it; you are not chasing a real proof out here.

The original used fresh peppers, onions, and raw shrimp, all of which are a cooler problem. Dried vegetables rehydrated in a cup do the same job at a fraction of the weight, and canned shrimp is already cooked, so it goes on last and only needs to heat through. Drain the vegetables properly. Water left in them will soak into the crust and you will pull a flat, wet disc out of the pot.
