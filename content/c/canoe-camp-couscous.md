Couscous is the only grain that cooks on residual heat. It needs one boil and five minutes covered, which means the stove runs for under two minutes and the fuel canister lasts the trip. That matters most on the water, where you land late and want dinner before the mosquitoes find you.

The salami and oil carry the calories. Dry-cured salami sits in a pack for a week without a cooler, and the sun-dried tomatoes rehydrate in the same water as the grain, so nothing gets drained and nothing gets a second pot.
