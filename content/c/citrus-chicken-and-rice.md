The original is a fresh-produce dutch oven meal: raw chicken, a whole onion, an apple, an orange. Dried fruit and canned chicken carry the same job without a cooler. Dried apple and orange rehydrate in the cooking liquid and give the rice back the moisture and acid the fresh fruit would have, and the water measurement stays at 3 cups because the fruit is drinking some of it.

Heat control is the whole recipe. Ten minutes of real frying heat is what gets flavor into the oil before the rice goes in; after that the oven has to come down to a bare simmer or the bottom layer of rice scorches while the top stays raw. Moving coals from under the oven to the lid is the fastest way to make that drop.
