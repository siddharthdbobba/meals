This is a home-oven bake that travels, not a camp recipe. The dough is stiff enough to press rather than scoop, which means one pan and one bake instead of tray after tray of cookies. The cookies stirred in stay crunchy at the edges and go soft where the dough surrounds them.

The part that matters for the trail is the bake time. Pull them early. A bar that is slightly underdone in the middle stays chewy for days in a pack; one baked until fully set becomes a dry brick by day two. Butter and sugar do the preserving work here, so the bars keep at ambient temperature without a cooler. The white chips will soften above about 80°F but they set again overnight.
