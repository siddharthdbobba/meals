This is a fat-and-sugar brick with enough structure to survive a pack. Peanut butter and honey are the binder; the oats, coconut, and hemp hearts are filler that soaks up the excess oil so it does not leak. Chia seeds do the same job on a smaller scale, pulling in loose moisture over the first hour and firming the whole batch up.

The original is a boat-galley snack that lives in the refrigerator. On trail it does not get one, so the ratio matters more: keep it on the dry side of sticky at home and it will hold through a warm afternoon instead of turning into paste. Made stiff and packed cold, these last about a week out of a fridge, longer in cool weather.
