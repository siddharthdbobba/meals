The original is a blender drink with raw egg and two cartons of whipping cream, which is three problems at once in camp. The egg is out on principle. The cream is replaced by whole milk powder rebuilt with cold water, which gets you the fat and the body without anything that has to stay at 38 degrees. Condensed milk does the rest of the heavy lifting: it is already sweet, already thick, and the can keeps unopened for a year in a bin.

Shake, do not stir, and do not overdo it. The milk powder needs real agitation to hydrate or you will find grit at the bottom of the last glass, but once the whiskey goes in, hard shaking just whips air into it. Alcohol and sugar make this keep for a few days in a cool bottle. Without the whiskey it is a one-day drink.
