Pressure-canned venison is already cooked through, which is the whole point. All the heat here is doing is rehydrating mushrooms and pulling the herbs together, so it goes from cold pot to bowl in about fifteen minutes even with a stiff wind on the stove.

The original is a home-canned soup with fresh onion and mushroom sealed into the jar. Dried mushrooms and onion flakes do the same job at a fraction of the weight, and tomato paste from a tube replaces the can that would otherwise rattle around half-used in your bin. The jar of meat is the one heavy item, which is why this stays at the truck or the wall tent.
