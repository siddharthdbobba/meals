This is a no-bake bar, which means the binder does all the structural work. Coconut butter and honey cook down to a syrup that sets firm when cold, and the ten-minute rest lets the oats absorb it instead of sitting in it. Pack the pan harder than feels necessary.

There are no peanuts or tree nuts here, which is the point of the hemp seeds: they add protein and fat without the usual allergen. The tradeoff is heat. Coconut oil softens around 76F, so these turn into a pocket of paste on a hot afternoon. Save them for shoulder season, winter, or a bag that lives inside the pack rather than strapped to the outside.
