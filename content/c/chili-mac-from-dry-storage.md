The pasta is cooked at home and dried, so camp is just rehydration. Three minutes of fuel gets you a boil; the standing time does the rest with the burner off. That is the whole reason to carry dried cooked pasta instead of raw — you trade a little home prep for most of your fuel back.

Drain the leftover water before the cheese goes in. Cheddar and oil will emulsify into what is left in the pot and coat the pasta; dump them into a soup and you get grease floating on chili water instead. The oil is also where a third of the calories live, so do not leave the packet at home.
