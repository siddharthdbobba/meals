The source made these as waffles with chili ladled over. In camp a waffle iron is dead weight, so the same batter goes straight on top of the chili and bakes in one pot. Cornbread over a wet base is forgiving: the chili steams the bottom while the coals on the lid brown the top.

Milk powder and egg powder do the work of the fridge ingredients, and neither needs any special handling. Keep the batter thick and pour it in a single sheet. If it sinks into the chili it will turn to mush instead of setting up as a crust.
