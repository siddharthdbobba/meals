This started as a stovetop dinner with a seared chicken breast and a pan sauce. On trail there is no pan and no second burner, so the chicken becomes a foil pouch and the whole thing happens in the pasta pot. Butter becomes olive oil because butter does not travel.

The part worth keeping is the pasta water. Leaving a shallow layer in the pot instead of draining it gives the oil something to bind with, and the starch turns it into a sauce rather than a slick. Olives and capers are doing the flavor work; they are salty and shelf-stable, which is a rare combination worth carrying.
