This is camp bread with nothing in it that needs a cooler. The milk powder is the whole reason it works away from a fridge: it puts back the fat and protein that fresh milk would, and it weighs almost nothing. Everything else is flour, leavening, and salt, so the mix keeps for months in a bag.

The two failure modes are heat and thickness. Baking powder gives you one rise, and it happens as soon as the water hits, so mix and cook without stalling. Then keep the flame low and the round thin. A pan hot enough to brown the outside in two minutes will hand you a raw center every time.
