Couscous is already cooked and dried before it reaches the box, so it does not need heat, only time and water. That makes it one of the few grains that works cold. The vegetables are the slow part: dried tomato and onion take longer to come back than the couscous does, which is why this soaks for two hours rather than twenty minutes.

The oil and pine nuts go in last, and they are most of the calories. Soaked couscous with vegetables is about 300 lean calories and not enough to walk on. Fat added at the end also stays where you can taste it, instead of being absorbed into swelling grain. Use a jar that seals by thread, not friction, and carry it upright.
