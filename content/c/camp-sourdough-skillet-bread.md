This is a car-camping or basecamp loaf, not a trail one. The dough is a long-fermented, no-knead type: hydration does the work that kneading would, and the wild yeast takes hours to do it. Time is the constraint, not effort, which is why it fits a day you are staying put.

The starter is the fragile piece. It needs to be at peak when you mix, meaning fed two or three hours earlier and domed on top, so feeding it before you leave the house is the whole plan. Everything else on the list is flour, salt, and water and rides in a bag indefinitely. Baking in a covered pot with coals on the lid is what replaces an oven: heat from both sides, or you get a raw middle under a burnt bottom.
