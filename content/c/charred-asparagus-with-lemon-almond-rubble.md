This one stays a fresh-vegetable recipe, which means a cooler and a first or second night out. Asparagus is mostly water and it wilts fast, so there is no dehydrating around the problem without losing the point of it.

The work moves home. Chopping almonds and mincing garlic in camp is a knife-and-board job nobody wants at dusk, so the topping gets made in a jar days ahead. Oil and lemon hold it together and the salt keeps the garlic honest. Coarse is right here — you want gravel on the spears, not paste, and a food processor is not coming along anyway. Foil makes the packet the only dish.
