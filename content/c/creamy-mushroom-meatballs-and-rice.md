The original is a pressure cooker dish: canned broth, canned soup, frozen meatballs, and a cup of heavy cream. None of that travels. The soup can becomes a dry envelope, the cream becomes milk powder plus oil, and the frozen meatballs become freeze-dried ones or a sausage you can carry warm for a week.

Order matters more than technique here. Mushrooms and meatballs rehydrate slowly, so they boil first; rice rehydrates fast and turns to paste if it sits in heat. The thickeners go in last, off the flame, because starch and milk powder scorch on a stove that only knows one setting.
