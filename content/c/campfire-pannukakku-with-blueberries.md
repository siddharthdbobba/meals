Pannukakku is a baked pancake, not a griddled one, so it lives or dies on even heat from underneath and enough trapped heat above. An upturned dutch oven lid over settled coals gives you both. Flame does not — it scorches the bottom before the center sets.

The batter looks wrong. It is thinner than pancake batter and it should be: the high liquid ratio is what makes the middle set custardy instead of cakey. The rest before baking lets the flour hydrate, which is the other half of that texture. This one needs a cooler for eggs, milk, and butter, so it is a car-camping recipe. The powdered-milk and dried-berry version in the variations travels without one.
