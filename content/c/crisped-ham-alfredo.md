Packet pasta goes down easy and gives you nothing to chew on. The whole point of the ham is texture. Frying it dry drives the moisture off, and the crucial part is pulling it out of the pot early, because cured ham stiffens as it cools rather than in the heat.

Cook the sauce down before it meets the pasta. Packet directions run wet, and thin alfredo slides off noodles and pools at the bottom of the bowl. A minute of reduction over low heat fixes that. Cured ham keeps unopened at ambient temperature, but once the pack is open, use it that night; for anything longer than a weekend out, carry bacon chips or hard sausage instead.
