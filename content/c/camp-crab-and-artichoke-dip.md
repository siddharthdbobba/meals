The original is a casserole dish full of cream cheese, mayonnaise, and sour cream, which is three refrigerated things too many. Powdered milk plus cream cheese powder plus olive oil rebuilds the same body without a cooler, and grated parmesan from a canister sits on a shelf for months. The result is thinner than a baked dip but holds a cracker fine.

Draining matters more than anything else here. Artichokes carry a lot of brine and freeze-dried spinach will drink whatever water you give it, so keep the water low and add more only if the pot goes stiff. Low heat throughout: the cheese and milk powder will catch and scorch on a hot aluminum bottom faster than you expect.
