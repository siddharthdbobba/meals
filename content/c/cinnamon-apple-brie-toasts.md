This is an oven appetizer moved onto a single skillet. The oven step exists only to melt cheese and crisp bread, and a covered pan over low heat does both. Cooking the apples first matters: raw slices on toast stay crunchy and watery, while a few minutes in butter and sugar drives off moisture and softens them enough to eat in one bite.

Brie needs a cooler, so this is a car-camping or basecamp dish, not something to carry on day three. Keep it cold until the pan is ready — soft brie smears rather than slices, and it will find its way into everything in the bin.
