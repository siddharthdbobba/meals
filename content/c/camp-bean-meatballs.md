The original is a home kitchen mix-and-match: any ground meat, an egg, an oven at 400. None of that travels. Canned beans replace the meat because they are shelf-stable and already cooked, and dried breadcrumbs plus dried onion replace the egg because they absorb rather than bind. Beans hold together well enough on their own once the excess water is gone.

The two things that go wrong are moisture and heat. Rinse and drain properly, and let the crumbs sit before rolling. Then keep the fire low. High heat sets the outside while the inside is still loose, and the ball splits when you turn it.
