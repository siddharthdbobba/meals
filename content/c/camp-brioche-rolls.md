Brioche is a bread problem solved with fat. The eggs and butter coat the gluten strands, which is why the crumb comes out tender and slightly flaky instead of chewy. It is also why the dough resists kneading and takes longer to rise than a lean loaf.

The home version wants fresh milk and whole eggs. Neither survives a weekend in a pack, so this uses whole milk powder and egg powder mixed into the dry blend and hydrated with plain water. The dough behaves the same. Butter is the one thing worth carrying cold, and ghee is the honest swap when the cooler stays home. This is a basecamp recipe: two rises and a bed of coals mean you need a morning with nothing else scheduled.
