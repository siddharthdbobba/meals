Most bagged trail mix is nuts and oil in a wrapper, and it runs close to 150 calories an ounce with over half of that from fat. Fat is dense, but it digests slowly, which is the wrong shape for energy you need in the next twenty minutes. This mix flips the ratio: roughly 77 percent of the calories come from carbohydrate, 18 percent from fat, 6 percent from protein.

Pretzels do the salt work that peanuts usually do, which also makes this safe for a nut-free group. Drying your own apples and bananas costs a day of dehydrator time and cuts out the sugar coating and preservatives on the store-bought stuff. Ten ounces is about a thousand calories, which covers roughly eight to ten miles of walking for most people. Eat it in small handfuls, continuously, with water.
