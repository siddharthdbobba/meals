Coleslaw is a fridge dish made portable by removing the two things that need cold: fresh cabbage and a jar of mayonnaise. Home-dried cabbage and carrot rehydrate in under an hour in cold water and come back with most of their crunch, and single-serve mayo packets are shelf-stable until you tear them.

The dressing is mostly a sugar-and-acid solution, which is why it wants to be whisked before the mayo goes in. Dissolve the sugar in the vinegar first or you will be chewing grit. The rest time is not optional. Freshly rehydrated cabbage tastes like wet paper until the vinegar and salt pull water out of it.
