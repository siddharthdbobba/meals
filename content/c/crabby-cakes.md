Crab is one of the few proteins that stays convincing out of a pouch, and boxed stuffing already contains the two things a crab cake needs: dried bread crumb and poultry seasoning. That removes the flour, egg, and breadcrumb work entirely. The mayonnaise packets are the binder, which is why they matter more than they look. Shelf-stable mayo in single servings survives heat far better than a squeeze bottle you opened last week.

The ten-minute rest is the whole recipe. Dry stuffing crumb needs time to pull in that small amount of water; skip it and the patties crumble the second you flip them. Keep the flame low and the cakes thin. This runs heavier per calorie than dehydrated food, so it belongs on a paddle trip or a first night out of the car rather than day nine of a thru-hike.
