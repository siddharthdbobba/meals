The original is a roasted side dish, which does not travel. Dehydrated green beans do. They rehydrate fast, and the second pass over dry heat is what keeps this from being boiled vegetables — the sauce needs a hot, mostly dry pot to stick to the beans instead of pooling under them.

The lime goes in last, off the heat. Acid cooked into the sauce flattens out, and this dish is thin on fat and salt, so the sour edge is doing most of the work. Bottled juice is fine; nothing here depends on it being fresh.
