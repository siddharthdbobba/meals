Brie is the useful part here. It softens at ambient temperature and melts fast, so it forgives a fire you cannot really control. Hard cheeses need sustained heat to flow; brie is most of the way there when it comes out of the cooler.

Foil is the fix for open-fire cooking. It spreads heat, keeps the cheese off the grate, and means cleanup is one crumpled square. Skip the foil and you get scorched bread over cold cheese. The last minute unwrapped is what gets you the crust.
