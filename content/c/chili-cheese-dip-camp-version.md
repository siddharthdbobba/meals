The original is a party dip built on a fridge: cream cheese, shredded cheese, canned chili. Two of those three do not survive a weekend in a bear box. Powdered cheese sauce replaces the shredded cheese because it melts on demand and carries dry, and shelf-stable cream cheese in foil holds unopened at ambient temperature for weeks.

Order matters more than technique. Hydrate the cheese powder before anything else goes in the pot; once the chili is in there, the mixture is too thick to break up lumps. Low heat throughout. This is mostly starch and fat, and it will weld itself to the pot bottom the moment you walk away.
