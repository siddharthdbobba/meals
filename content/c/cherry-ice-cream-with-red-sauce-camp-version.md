The original is ice cream in a bowl with a berry sauce, which means a freezer and a fridge. Neither travels. What survives is the structure: a cold, fatty, sweet base under sharp fruit and something red and runny on top. Whole milk powder plus solid fat plus powdered sugar and very little water gets you there. The water ratio is the whole trick — 60 ml, not 150. Too much and you have a milkshake.

Freeze-dried cherries do double duty. Crushed, they color and flavor the base without adding liquid the way frozen or fresh fruit would. Left whole, they give the top something to bite. The cornstarch in the sauce needs no heat; cold water and a hard shake plus five minutes of sitting is enough to move it from juice to something that clings.
