This is a condiment, not a meal, and it exists because camp food is usually short on fat and sharp flavor at the same time. Mayonnaise fixes both. Single-serve mayo packets are shelf-stable and small enough that you can carry sauce without carrying a cooler, which is the whole reason this version works on trail.

The home kitchen version sits in the fridge for an hour so the flavors settle. You do not have a fridge, so the rest gets shortened to whatever you can stand while dinner heats. Drain the relish before packing it: the brine is most of its weight and it thins the sauce into something that runs off a tortilla. Mixed sauce keeps a couple of days in cool weather, less in heat, so mix what you will finish.
