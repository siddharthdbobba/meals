Steam is simpler than baking over a camp stove. A covered pot with an inch of boiling water cooks the cake evenly and keeps it fudgy inside. No timer anxiety, no risk of a dry cake.

Cold weather dessert is practical. The calories genuinely fuel your body through the night, and warm cake morale is real. A commercial mix makes this doable at camp—mixing from scratch would be fiddly and ingredient-heavy.
