The original is a restaurant sauce built on half-and-half and a pound of fresh shrimp. Neither travels. Whole milk powder does the same job once you slurry it in hot liquid, and freeze-dried shrimp comes back close enough in ten minutes of cold water. Cherry tomatoes became sun-dried, which stops them from turning the pot to soup.

The pasta water is the sauce. Cooking the noodles in a measured amount of bouillon water and letting it reduce means there is nothing to drain and nothing to throw away, and the released starch is what keeps the milk powder from separating. Add the cheese off the heat or it goes grainy.
