Couscous is fast and light but needs fat and protein to be dinner. Oil and nuts carry the calories; cranberries add tartness and balance. Dried mint and lemon pepper give brightness—the flavor profile that works in summer when cream feels too heavy.

Vegetarian as written, it feeds two hikers. Add a chicken pouch or instant hummus in the last minute for more protein.
