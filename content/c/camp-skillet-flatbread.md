The original is a loaf, and a loaf needs an oven and a two-hour rise you cannot babysit at a campsite. The dough survives the translation better than the shape does, so this cooks flat in a skillet instead. The long soak is the part worth keeping: whole wheat flour sitting in an acidic liquid softens the bran and makes the finished bread less dense and easier to digest. That soak happens in a freezer bag on the drive in, which costs you nothing.

Fresh buttermilk is not an option without a fridge, so buttermilk powder rehydrates in the soak and does the same acid work. Ghee replaces butter because it is already clarified and keeps unrefrigerated. The yeast, salt, and soda go in after the soak, not before: acid and a long wet rest will spend the leavening before it ever sees heat.
