A s'more is a structure, not a recipe: something crisp on the outside, something that melts in the middle, and a hot marshmallow to melt it. Once you see it that way the graham cracker and the plain milk chocolate bar stop being required. Any sturdy cookie works as the shell, and any candy bar works as the melting layer.

The part that actually matters is heat management. A marshmallow held in flame chars in ten seconds and stays cold inside; held over coals it browns through and softens all the way down, which is what melts the chocolate on contact. Cookies with a coating soften faster than bare graham, so press gently or the whole thing squirts out the sides.
