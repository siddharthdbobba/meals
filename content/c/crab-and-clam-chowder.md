Canned shellfish is the rare protein that needs no cooler and no rehydrating, and the clam juice is a free broth base, so drain it into the pot rather than the dirt. Potato flakes do the thickening that a roux would do at home, and powdered milk stands in for cream. Neither one needs to simmer, which is why this is one boil and a five-minute rest rather than twenty minutes of stirring.

The parmesan is there for salt and body, not for a cheese flavor you would notice. The oil matters more than it looks: without added fat this reads thin and watery, since neither canned shellfish nor milk powder brings much. Two cans are heavy for a backpacking dinner, so this earns its place on a short trip, a paddle, or a first night out of the car.
