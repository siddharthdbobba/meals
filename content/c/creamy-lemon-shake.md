The original is sweetened condensed milk whipped with lemon over ice, which is a fridge recipe and a blender recipe at once. Neither travels. Whole milk powder plus sugar rebuilds condensed milk from the dry side, and shaking in a bottle does what the whip did: it aerates and it dissolves.

Order matters more than technique. Milk powder hydrates in a small amount of water and clumps in a large one, so the first shake is a paste, not a drink. The pinch of salt is not for flavor exactly; it keeps the sugar from reading flat once the mix is cold.
