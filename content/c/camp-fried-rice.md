Real fried rice starts with cold leftover rice, which is the one thing you cannot have on trail. Instant rice is the workaround: it rehydrates fast and stays in separate grains instead of turning to porridge, as long as you keep the water tight. Four hundred milliliters is the ceiling. Anything more and you are eating soup.

The last two minutes over low heat are the part that matters. That is where the oil hits the pot and a bit of the rice browns, which is most of what separates this from steamed rice with things in it. Egg powder is doing double duty here, adding protein and binding the whole thing loosely together. The dish is dairy-free but not vegetarian; drop the meat and egg for the vegetable version.
