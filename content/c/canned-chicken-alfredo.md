The original of this is a scout-camp recipe that leans on a can of condensed milk. Canned milk is heavy and there is nothing to do with the leftovers, so powdered whole milk goes in instead. Mix it into cool water first or you get lumps that never dissolve.

The noodles cook in the water that becomes the sauce, so nothing gets drained and no starch gets thrown away. That starch is what keeps a powdered alfredo from separating. The last five minutes on low is the part people skip, and it is where the sauce actually comes together — stir the whole time, because the pot bottom scorches fast on a canister stove.
