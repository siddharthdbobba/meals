Soda bread is the one loaf that makes sense on a stove. There is no yeast and no rising time, so the whole thing goes from bag to pot in fifteen minutes. The lift comes from baking powder plus the acid reaction between baking soda and soured milk, and that reaction starts the moment the wet hits the dry. Mix, shape, and get it on the heat.

The buttermilk is reconstructed rather than carried: dry milk plus water plus a teaspoon of vinegar, rested ten minutes total. Real buttermilk would need a fridge. Knead briefly and no more, because worked dough goes tough without yeast to rescue it. Baking times swing hard between stoves and pots, so run this once at home on your actual setup before you trust it at camp.
