The original is an oven casserole held together by cream, creme fraiche, and an egg yolk. None of that travels. Milk powder plus cheese powder rebuilds the sauce, and potato flakes do the egg's job of thickening it, so the whole thing sets in the pot instead of in a baking dish.

Drying the vegetables at home is the real work. Cut them thin and they come back in ten minutes of soaking; leave them thick and you will be chewing damp cardboard. The parmesan and the fat go in last, off the heat, so the cheese melts rather than scorches to the bottom.
