The original is a home kitchen soup: fresh mushrooms browned in batches, a flour roux, a cup of white wine, heavy cream. None of that packs. Dried mushrooms replace fresh ones and actually concentrate the flavor, hard salami replaces raw Italian sausage without a cooler, and whole milk powder plus potato flakes stand in for the cream and the roux at once.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Simmer the meat and mushrooms long enough to build a broth, then kill the heat before the dairy goes in. Milk powder added to boiling liquid separates into grit, and there is no fixing it once it does.
