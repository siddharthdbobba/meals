Canned potatoes are already cooked, which is the entire point. All the pan has to do is drive off surface moisture and build a crust, so this goes from cold cans to a set skillet in about fifteen minutes. Drain them hard and dry them if you have a bandana to spare, because wet potatoes will never brown.

Two swaps make the original work without a fridge: whole egg powder in place of fresh eggs, and dried onion flakes rehydrated in a splash of water instead of a brown onion that rolls around the bin for a week. The sausage is already shelf-stable and does not need changing. The low, covered finish is what sets the egg without turning it into scramble.
