Stuffed cabbage rolls are a folding project. The soup version already skipped the folding; the trail version skips the fridge. Fresh cabbage is 92 percent water and dead weight in a pack, so it gets dehydrated at home, where it shrinks to almost nothing and rehydrates in the pot without turning to mush. Tomato powder replaces the can for the same reason.

The fat matters more than it looks. Ghee carries the oregano and mint, and it keeps the rice from going gluey. Simmer rather than boil hard, and stir: tomato solids sink and burn onto a thin titanium pot faster than you expect.
