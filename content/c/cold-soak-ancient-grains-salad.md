Grains and lentils that were already cooked, then dried, will rehydrate in cold water given enough time. Three hours of walking does the job with no fuel and no pot. The trade is planning: you have to remember to add water mid-morning, not at lunch.

Lentils are the part that matters. The grains are carbohydrate and bulk; the lentils and the freeze-dried cheese are where the protein comes from. Puy and black lentils stay whole through the soak, which is why the salad still eats like a salad instead of like porridge. The oil and the acidic dressing go in last so the fat does not coat the grains and slow the water down.
