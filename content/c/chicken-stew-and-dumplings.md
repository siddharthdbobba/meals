Freeze-dried chicken and vegetables rehydrate in the simmering broth, so you never touch raw meat or chop anything at camp. The dumplings are made in their freezer bag: add water, seal, knead until uniform, snip the corner, squeeze into hot broth. It is faster and cleaner than pulling out a mixing bowl.

Soak the vegetables for 15 minutes before boiling. This breaks them down enough that they taste like something, not crunchy sawdust. The starch from the dumplings thickens the broth naturally, and bouillon carries all the flavor without fresh stock. One pot simmers for five minutes after the dumplings go in, then you eat.
