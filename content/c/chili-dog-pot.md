This is a car-camping dinner because the dogs and the cheese need a cooler. Two cans, one pot, one burner, and eight people eat. The chili does double duty as sauce and cooking liquid, so nothing gets added and nothing gets drained.

Low heat matters more than it seems. Canned chili is thick and camp pots are thin, so a hot flame welds a black layer to the bottom before the middle is warm. Slicing the dogs into coins speeds it up and makes portioning easier. For a trip without a cooler, canned hot dogs and a wedge of hard cheese get you the same pot with nothing that spoils.
