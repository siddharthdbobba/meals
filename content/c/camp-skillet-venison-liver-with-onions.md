The soak is the whole recipe. Liver holds blood, and blood is what makes it taste like iron filings; a day or two in cold water, changed a few times, pulls most of that out. Skip it and no gravy will save the pan. This one stays in the cooler until the first night, since liver is the fastest-spoiling thing you will carry.

Everything else is a dredge and a hot pan. The flour crust sets fast in the oil, which keeps the inside from going gray and grainy, so pull each slice while the center is still faintly pink. The beer and the browned bits from the bottom of the skillet become the gravy, thickened with the dredge you already made. One pan, one plate, no second pot.
