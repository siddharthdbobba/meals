This one stays in the cooler category, and it should. Cream cheese and sour cream are the whole texture, and there is no shelf-stable version that holds up. Cook it the first or second night while the ice is still solid.

The spice mix gets split for a reason. A quarter of it goes in with the corn so the heat cooks into the base; the rest goes into hot butter at the end, which pulls the color and the smoke out of the paprika and floats it on top. Dumping all of it in at once gives you a flat, muddy pot. Low heat when the cheese goes in matters too — a hard boil breaks it.
