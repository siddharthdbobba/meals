Car camping means you get a cooler and a real pan, so the constraint is not weight, it is burner count. This is built for one pan and one burner. The order matters more than the recipe: potatoes take three times as long as everything else, so they go in first and everything else joins them as they finish.

Most of the work happens at the kitchen counter. Dicing potatoes on a picnic table with a dull knife and no cutting board is the part of camp breakfast people quit over. Prepped vegetables and pre-cracked eggs turn this into twenty-five minutes of stirring. The sausage is already cooked, so it is there for fat and salt rather than food safety.
