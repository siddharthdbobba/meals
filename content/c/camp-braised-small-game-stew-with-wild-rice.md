This is a long braise, so it belongs at a basecamp or a car-camping site where the fire is going anyway. Tough, lean game meat needs hours of low wet heat and an acid to work on it. The overnight wine soak does the acid part, and it doubles as your packing liquid — the meat rides in the cooler in the same bag it marinates in.

The wild rice flour is the piece worth carrying. It thickens the broth without a roux and without flour dust everywhere, and it tastes like the rice already in the bowl. Bacon and butter cover the fat that game doesn't have. Keep the pot at a bare simmer; a hard boil over open coals will tighten the meat and no amount of extra time will loosen it again.
