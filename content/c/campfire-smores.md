There is no technique here beyond heat management. A marshmallow held in open flame carbonizes in ten seconds while the inside stays firm. Held over coals it browns evenly and the sugar inside liquefies, which is what melts the chocolate on contact. That contact is the whole mechanism: the marshmallow is the only heat source the chocolate gets.

Quantities scale straight up by headcount. Two marshmallows and one cracker sheet per person is the standard unit, and one 1.5 oz bar covers two people. Pack the crackers hard-sided; they are the fragile part, and crushed grahams make a mess rather than a s'more.
