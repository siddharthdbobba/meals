Freeze-dried protein and vegetables solve the weight and shelf-life problem. Everything here carries for months without refrigeration and rehydrates completely in simmering broth. The chicken gravy mix does the heavy lifting: it thickens the soup and seasons it all at once, so you're not carrying a spice cabinet.

Butter powder and milk powder matter more than they sound. They create the creamy flavor you expect from pot pie soup without requiring cream at camp. Stir constantly as you add the dry mix to avoid lumps—once it's in the hot water, lumps won't break down. This feeds four people in one pot with no dishes after.
