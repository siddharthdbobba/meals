This is a home dinner-roll dough with the refrigerator taken out of it. Powdered egg and butter replace the fresh egg and dairy, and the whole dry side gets measured into one bag at home so camp work is water, kneading, and waiting. It is a car-camping or basecamp recipe, not a backpacking one, because two rises take two hours you will not want on a walking day.

The two things that go wrong are heat. Water hot enough to feel hot kills the yeast, and coals piled under the pot burn the bottoms before the tops set. Keep most of the coals on the lid, and turn the pot against the lid so no hot spot sits in one place.
