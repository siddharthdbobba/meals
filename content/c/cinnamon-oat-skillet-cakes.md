A waffle iron is not going in a pack, so this keeps the batter and drops the appliance. Ground rolled oats are the whole flour here, which means the batter behaves differently from wheat: it thickens as it stands and it will not tolerate a hot pan. Low heat and a lid, every time.

The swaps are the fridge items. Powdered egg and powdered milk rehydrate in the bag with the water, and ghee replaces butter because it keeps for weeks without cooling and has no water to spit. Maple sugar in place of syrup cuts a few ounces if you care about that.
