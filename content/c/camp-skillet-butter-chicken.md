Butter chicken survives the move outdoors better than most curries because nothing in it needs to be fresh. Onion, garlic, and ginger all work in dried form here, and the cream comes out of a bag. The chicken is precooked and shelf-stable, so the pan time is about building the sauce, not cooking meat through.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Spices go into hot fat before any liquid touches the pan, which is where the flavor actually comes from. Add the passata first and you get a thin tomato sauce with sand in it. The butter and cream powder are also the calories, so keep both.
