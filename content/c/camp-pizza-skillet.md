This is a casserole rebuilt as a skillet dish, because nobody is baking a layered pan of pasta at a campsite. Everything goes in one pot and the cheese melts under a lid off the heat instead of under a broiler. The residual heat does the same job with none of the fuel.

Drain the pasta short of soft. It keeps cooking in the sauce, and mushy macaroni is the usual failure here. Draining the beef fat matters too: with a full jar of sauce and two cups of cheese already in the pan, the extra grease has nowhere to go and pools on top.
