Instant rice and freeze-dried chicken rehydrate at the same rate, which is why you only need one boil. Sitting for fifteen minutes lets everything absorb the water while your stove stays off. The gravy mix, milk powder, and Parmesan build a sauce-like coating that wouldn't happen with plain broth.

This meal fits in a freezer bag, which saves weight and cleanup. You can eat it straight from the bag or transfer to a pot. All ingredients store shelf-stable for months, making this a reliable dinner that doesn't need a fridge or fresh supplies.
