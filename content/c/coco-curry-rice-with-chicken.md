Coconut milk powder is the whole reason this works without a cooler. It carries fat and body the way canned coconut milk does at a tenth of the weight, and it keeps indefinitely. The catch is that it clumps in boiling water, so it goes in at the end, once the pot is off the flame.

A hot stove is a liability here, not an advantage. Rice and coconut both scorch, and a four-kilowatt burner will weld a black ring to your pot in under a minute. Boil hard, then kill the heat and let residual temperature do the rehydrating. The freeze-dried chicken needs the head start because it takes longer to soften than the rice does.
