The home version of this leans on a can of condensed soup, a brick of cream cheese, and a cup and a half of milk. None of that travels. The rebuild uses milk powder, powdered cream soup base, and cream cheese powder to get the same fat and body out of a bag that weighs a few ounces. Flour still does the heavy lifting, exactly as it does at home.

The order matters more than the ingredients do. Powdered dairy hitting boiling water clumps into rubber, so the pot comes off the heat first and the powders go in gradually. Bean flakes and freeze-dried corn rehydrate in the same liquid, which means no draining and no second pot. Add the cheddar at the end with the burner off or it breaks and pools grease on top.
