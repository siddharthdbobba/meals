Real salsa is a cooler problem: tomatoes, onions, peppers, and a knife. This version moves all of that to the dry side. Tomato powder rebuilds the base, flakes and dried pepper give it something to chew, and the acid powder replaces the lime you did not want to carry.

The wait is the recipe. Everything hydrates at a different rate, and the flavor you taste at minute two is not the flavor at minute twenty. Salt and heat go in at the end for the same reason. Scale it up freely, since the only limit is bag size.
