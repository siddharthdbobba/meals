The original is a stovetop side dish built on canned beans. Cans are heavy and the acid comes from frozen juice concentrate, so both had to go. Dehydrated bean flakes rehydrate in three minutes with no simmer, and powdered orange and lime do the same job as the wet versions at a fraction of the weight.

The acid is the whole point. Beans and cumin alone are flat and muddy; the orange, lime, and vinegar cut through and make the spices read as separate things. Add all three off the heat, because boiling drives off the sharpness you carried them for. The oil goes in at the same time and does double duty as calories and as the thing that keeps the paste from setting into a brick.
