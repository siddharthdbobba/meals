This started as a home salad built around fresh green-lipped mussels, which is not a thing you carry to a campsite. Tinned smoked mussels do the same job: briny, meaty, and already cooked, and the smoke covers for the ones you would have gotten off a grill. Everything else already keeps. Whole peppers last a week in a pack, and the dressing is shelf-stable oil, acid, and sugar.

The char is the part worth doing properly. Skin that is merely warm will not peel, and you end up chewing on it. Blacken it, cover it, wait, then rub the skin off dry. Rinsing it under water takes the smoke with it, which is the whole reason you built a fire. Fold the mussels in last and gently, or they come apart into shreds.
