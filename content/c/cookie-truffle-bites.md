The home version binds cookie crumbs with cream cheese, which is fine for a party table and useless in a pack. Full-fat milk powder plus coconut oil does the same job: the powder supplies the dairy solids and the oil is solid below roughly 76°F, so it sets the balls firm instead of leaving them soft and perishable. Nothing here needs a fridge.

Candy melts over real chocolate is a deliberate choice. They set with a harder shell and tolerate warm pockets better, which matters when the dessert rides in a pack all afternoon. This is all home prep. In camp you are opening a container, not cooking.
