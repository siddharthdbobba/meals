Raw cauliflower is one of the few vegetables that keeps its texture without refrigeration for a couple of days, which is why this works away from a cooler at all. Cutting it small matters more than it sounds: small florets take on dressing, large ones stay dry in the middle and taste like nothing.

The original leans on Greek yogurt, fresh eggs, and jarred pickled onions. All three are replaced here. Powdered yogurt or buttermilk plus oil rebuilds the creaminess, egg powder does the protein, and dried onion soaked in vinegar for fifteen minutes gets close enough to the pickled version. Let the finished bowl sit twenty minutes before eating so the dried herbs and celery pull water out of the dressing and soften.
