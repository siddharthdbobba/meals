Milk powder does real work here. It thickens the grits into something with structure instead of leaving you with a salty slurry. Freeze-dried corn adds fiber and nutrients without adding weight, and Parmesan provides the fat and salt that make this worth eating at mile 15.

In cold weather or at high altitude, wrap the bag in a cozy or tuck it into your jacket while it rehydrates. The corn takes longer to soften in thin air.
