This started life as a baked casserole with fresh eggs, canned chiles, and heavy cream. None of that travels. Freeze-dried chiles and cheese carry the same flavor at a fraction of the weight, powdered egg and cream powder replace the fridge, and a covered skillet does what forty minutes at 350 was doing.

The order matters more than the technique. Rehydrate the chiles and cheese first and drain them, because any water still hanging in them will thin the eggs and you will end up with curds in a puddle. Low heat and a slow spoon keep the eggs soft; a hot pan sets them into something you could patch a boot with.
