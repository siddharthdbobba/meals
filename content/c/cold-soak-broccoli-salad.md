Raw broccoli salad does not travel, but the dried version gets close. Broccoli rehydrates into something with actual bite rather than mush, which is why this works cold-soaked when most vegetables do not. Give it the full ninety minutes: start it at a break and eat it at the next one.

The original dressing leans on mayonnaise, which is a fridge problem. Oil, vinegar powder, and a mustard packet do the same job and weigh less. The tamari-and-paprika coating on the nuts is worth the dehydrator time at home, since it is the only smoky, savory thing in the jar. Add the crunchy parts at the end or you have soft nuts and no contrast.
