The cold soak saves fuel. Ten minutes in water softens the noodles and rehydrates the chicken before you ever strike a match, so you only need two minutes of actual boiling. When you carry fuel in ounces, this matters.

Curry powder replaces the flavor packet. Freeze-dried chicken and vegetables do the real work—they're calorie-dense and they rehydrate reliably in cold water. The olive oil is essential: without it you have seasoned noodles; with it you have dinner.
