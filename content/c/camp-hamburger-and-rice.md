Freeze-dried meat and vegetables weigh almost nothing and rehydrate in minutes. The instant rice is the real trick: it swells to bind everything together and turn watery ingredients into an actual meal. The vegetable soup mix pulls its weight on salt and flavor, replacing the aromatics you can't carry.

Fifteen minutes of sitting is all the cooking this needs. No watching the pot or adjusting heat. The freeze-dried meat carries 25 grams of protein in a half-cup, same as a real hamburger patty but worth less than an ounce.
