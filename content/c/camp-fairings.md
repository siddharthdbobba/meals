These are old Cornish market biscuits, and the whole point is the crack across the top. That comes from an absurd amount of baking soda plus a physical shock partway through baking: you drop the hot pan, the risen dough falls, and the surface splits. Skip the drop and you get a plain domed cookie.

A dutch oven handles this fine because the recipe is short and forgiving on temperature. Golden syrup is the one ingredient worth carrying rather than substituting, though corn syrup does the same job of keeping them chewy in the middle. Shortening instead of butter if the cooler is not part of the trip.
