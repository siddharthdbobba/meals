Adobo is a braise that survives the trip because it is mostly acid and salt. The original runs a cut-up bird for six hours in a slow cooker; here the chicken arrives already cooked in a pouch, so all that is left is reducing the sauce and letting it grab on. Twenty minutes on a burner does what an afternoon did at home.

The reduction is the whole point. Straight out of the pot the liquid is thin and aggressively salty, and it just pools under the rice. Cook it down by half and add the cornstarch and it coats instead. Fresh ginger becomes ground, and the sauce loses a little brightness for it, which the vinegar mostly covers.
