This is a home-kitchen step that pays off on the trail. Dry curry powder shaken over nuts just falls to the bottom of the bag. Warming it in a skillet blooms the spice and lets the nut oils and coconut fat pick it up, so the flavor stays on the food instead of in the corner of the bag.

Nothing in it needs a stove, water, or a cooler once it is toasted, so it packs as a straight grab bag. The peanut and cashew fat carries most of the calories and the coconut adds more; the raisins are there for sugar that hits faster than fat does. Salted nuts are worth the trade. Salt is what makes curry taste like curry rather than like powder.
