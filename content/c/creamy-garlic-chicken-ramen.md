The home version of this leans on butter, heavy cream, and leftover chicken, which is three refrigerated things too many. Whole milk powder does the cream's work: enough fat and milk solids to turn the cooking water cloudy and thick, with none of the weight of liquid. Olive oil replaces the butter and adds back the calories the swap costs.

Add the milk powder off the heat. Dairy powder scorches on a hot pot bottom and will scald into the finish before you notice. Keep the water low too. Ramen wants to swim, but a swimming noodle leaves you with thin broth instead of sauce, and there is no colander out here.
