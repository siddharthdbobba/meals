The original is a home stew: fresh onion, carrot, peppers, two cans, and a long simmer. On trail the cans are the problem, so tomato powder does the work of both the paste and the tinned tomatoes, and instant bean flakes replace the drained can. Everything that used to be wet is now powder, and the water goes in at camp instead of on your back.

The order matters. Vegetables first, because dried carrot is stubborn and needs the full boil. Bean flakes last and off the heat, or they clump and scorch on the pot bottom. The oil is not optional either — the source browned the chicken in two tablespoons of it, and without that fat the whole thing reads as thin and salty.
