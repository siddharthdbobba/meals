The original is a fridge salad built on cucumber, fresh peppers, and cherry tomatoes. None of that carries. What survives the swap is the structure: a bean with real protein, a lot of crunchy vegetable, and an acidic dressing holding it together. Freeze-dried edamame does the first job, dried peppers, corn, and carrot do the second, and lemon powder plus mustard and oil do the third. Cucumber is gone entirely; there is no dried version of it worth eating.

Dressing goes in last, after the soak. Oil coats the vegetable pieces and slows rehydration if you add it early, and the acid makes everything taste flat once it has been sitting in water for an hour. Salt is the same story in reverse: a little in the dry mix helps the soak, the rest belongs at the end where you can taste it.
