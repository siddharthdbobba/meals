The original is a fridge salad: canned chickpeas, raw vegetables, feta, and a dressing made fresh. None of that carries. The swap is dehydrated chickpeas and dried vegetables, which rehydrate on the same water they would have been packed in, and a dressing built from powders plus oil added at the end.

Oil goes in last on purpose. Chickpeas coated in fat rehydrate slowly, so water first, then the oil once they are soft. The feta is gone and the honey is replaced with sugar, which keeps this vegan; add powdered cheese back if you would rather have the salt than the tag.
