The original is oven fries buried under chili and broiled cheese, which needs a fridge, a sheet pan, and a broiler. None of that fits in a pack. Dehydrated hash browns rehydrate into something between a fry and a mash, and they take the chili the same way the fries did. Tomato powder replaces the sauce and weighs almost nothing.

Order matters more than technique. The beans and corn need real simmering time to come back; the potatoes need almost none and will glue to the pot if they go in early. Wait until the heat is off before the cheese, or it breaks and you get oil slicks instead of a bound sauce.
