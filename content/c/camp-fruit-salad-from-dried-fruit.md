Fresh fruit salad is mostly water and bruises, which is a bad deal in a pack. The dried version carries the same fruit at a fifth of the weight and rebuilds itself in the bag. Cold water is enough; the apples and pineapple just need time, so start it before you set up the tent.

The citrus crystals and the pinch of salt are doing the work the fresh lime and orange used to do. Without acid, rehydrated dried fruit tastes flat and one-note sweet. The berries and banana chips go in at the end because they soften in minutes and turn to mush if they soak the whole hour.
