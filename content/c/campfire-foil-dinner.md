This is a car-camping meal and it stays one. The potatoes are the timer: sliced thin they cook in the fifteen minutes the beef needs, and left in chunks they will still be raw when everything above them is done. Layering matters for the same reason. Potatoes touch the hot foil, the meat sits above them, and the canned vegetables ride on top where they only need warming.

Two layers of foil is not a suggestion. A single sheet tears when you turn the packet and you lose the whole dinner into the coals. Keep the cheese light inside the packet and save the rest for the bowl, because cheese against hot foil turns into a layer you scrape off and throw away.
