Heart is a working muscle with almost no fat, which means it goes from tender to rubber in about a minute. Slicing it thin and frying hot is the whole trick: the crust forms before the inside overcooks. The overnight soak in reconstituted buttermilk softens it further and cuts the iron edge.

The fresh dairy is the only thing that would normally keep this off a camp menu, so both the soak and the gravy use powder mixed with cold water. Buttermilk powder is shelf-stable and rehydrates fine for this; you lose nothing but the trip to the store. The heart itself still needs a cooler, which is why this is a truck-camp or basecamp dinner rather than something you carry.
