Brownies are forgiving about heat but not about heat from below. Cast iron over settled coals gives you a slow, spread-out bake; open flame gives you a burnt floor and raw middle. Cover the pan so the top sets from trapped heat instead of waiting on the bottom to catch up.

Two changes from a kitchen version. Powdered egg replaces cracked shells, which means nothing leaks in the food box and the wet side of the recipe is a measured pour. The dry ingredients get mixed at home, so at the fire ring you are combining three bags instead of measuring cocoa and spices by headlamp. The chili is not decoration either. It cuts the sugar enough that a second square still goes down after dinner.
