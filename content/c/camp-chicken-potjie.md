Potjiekos is a South African fire-pot dish, and the whole method rests on one rule: layer everything and leave it alone. Stirring breaks the vegetables down into a uniform stew. Left in place, the carrots stay carrots and the top layer steams while the bottom braises in fat and stock.

This is a car camping or basecamp meal, not a pack meal. It needs a heavy pot, two hours of coals, and a cooler. The trail concessions are small: stock concentrate and tomato paste instead of liquid stock and fresh tomatoes, granulated garlic if you would rather not carry cloves. Keep the liquid shallow. The chicken and vegetables release plenty on their own, and a flooded pot loses the layering that makes this worth the time.
