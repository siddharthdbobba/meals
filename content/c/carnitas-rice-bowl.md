Real carnitas is eight hours of pork shoulder in its own fat, which is not a trail move. What survives the translation is the spice profile: cumin and coriander for the base, oregano for the edge, a small amount of cinnamon to round it out, and pepper flakes for heat. Those are the parts you can carry in a bag the size of a thumb.

Bay leaves get dropped because they need long wet cooking to give anything up, and the stock becomes bouillon. The oil matters more than it looks — the original dish gets its texture from rendered fat, so a tablespoon stirred in at the end keeps the rice from tasting like dry seasoned grain. Lime goes in off the heat or it flattens out.
