A café au lait is just half coffee, half hot milk, which is easy at home and awkward in camp because milk is the part you cannot carry. Whole milk powder fixes it. Reconstituted at four tablespoons to a cup it has enough fat to soften a dark roast the way real milk does, and it weighs nothing.

Heat matters more than technique here. Milk powder scorches and skins over if you push it to a boil, so warm the water first, then stir the powder in off the flame. The coffee gets brewed separately because grounds and milk in the same pot make a mess you have to carry out.
