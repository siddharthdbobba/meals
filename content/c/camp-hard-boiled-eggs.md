This is a car-camping and basecamp recipe, not a backpacking one. Eggs are not shelf-stable and there is no honest way around that, so they ride in a cooler and get boiled the first morning while the ice is still solid. What you get back is two days of protein that needs no stove and no dish.

The salt in the water is doing real work. It seeps through the shell and makes peeling less of a fight, which matters when you are doing eight of them with cold hands. The cold-water dunk matters more: skip it and the shell fuses to the white, and you spend breakfast picking gravel-sized flecks off a mangled egg.
