Chimichurri is mostly acid, oil, and herbs, which means it survives a cooler better than most sauces. Fresh parsley wilts to slime in two days, so this uses dried and lets it sit in the vinegar and oil for twenty minutes before serving. That rehydration step is the whole trick; skipped, the sauce tastes like dust.

Everything happens in one skillet, in order. The steak leaves fat and browned bits behind, and the eggs cook in that with a little butter. Rest the steak while the eggs go, and both land on the plate hot.
