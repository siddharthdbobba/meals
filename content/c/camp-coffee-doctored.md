Instant coffee at camp fails for two reasons: boiling water scalds the grounds bitter, and milk powder dropped into a full mug turns into floating lumps. Both are fixable without carrying anything extra. Let the water sit half a minute off the flame, and hydrate the powder as a paste before you flood it.

The salt is not a garnish. A small pinch flattens the harsh edge that cheap instant coffee has, and it does more for the cup than more sugar would. Premixing at home matters more than the ingredients do — five small containers by headlamp in the cold is how people end up drinking plain hot water instead.
