Rice pudding at home means simmering rice in milk for forty minutes and stirring so it does not scorch. None of that works on a canister stove. Instant rice and powdered milk do the same job with one boil, and the pudding mix supplies the starch and sugar that would otherwise come from long cooking.

The ten minutes off the heat matter more than the boil does. That is when the rice finishes absorbing and the pudding starch sets. Pull the lid early and you get sweet soup. It also comes out fine cold the next morning, which makes it breakfast if you cook a double batch.
