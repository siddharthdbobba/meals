Zucchini is 95 percent water, which makes it a bad thing to carry and a good thing to dry. Peeled into ribbons with a vegetable peeler, it dries fast and rehydrates in cold water in under an hour, coming back with enough bite to still read as a raw salad rather than mush. That is the whole reason this one works without a stove.

The fresh version leans on goat cheese and just-torn kale. Neither survives a pack, so the cheese becomes a hard aged wedge that keeps at ambient temperature and the kale gets dried with everything else. Drain the soaking water before dressing. Two tablespoons of oil is most of the calories here, and it will slide off a wet salad instead of sticking to it.
