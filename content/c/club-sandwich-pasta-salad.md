This is cooler food, not pack food. Mayonnaise, deli meat, and cheese all need to stay cold, so it belongs at a trailhead or a basecamp with ice, and it should be eaten on day one. Cook the pasta on the stove or over a grate, then rinse it cold — that step is not optional, because dressing hitting hot pasta separates into an oily mess.

The dressing is thinned with milk so it coats every spiral instead of sitting in clumps; ranch seasoning does the flavor work that a jar of bottled dressing would. Lettuce and tomatoes go in last since both give off water and turn the whole pot soupy if they sit. If you want the same thing without a cooler, use shelf-stable meat and mayo packets and build it the hour you eat it.
