This is a sugar sauce, which is why it goes on late. Brushed on early it burns black before the inside of the chicken or pork is done. The tomato paste is doing structural work rather than flavor work: it thickens the sweet chili sauce enough to cling to a curved surface instead of running through the grate into the fire.

Nothing here needs heat or a fridge, so it mixes at home and rides in a jar. The vinegar and sugar keep it stable for a trip. Fresh garlic and chili are the only perishable part, and dried versions of both work well enough that they are the default for anything past a weekend.
