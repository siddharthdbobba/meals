The original is baked cheesecake: cream cheese, eggs, sour cream, a low oven, then four hours in the fridge. None of that travels. What makes cheesecake read as cheesecake is tang, fat, and thickness, so this version gets all three from powder — instant cheesecake pudding mix for the tang and the set, whole milk powder for the fat. No eggs, no oven, no cold chain.

The cookie does the rest. One left whole at the bottom of the cup stands in for the crust, and the crushed ones stirred through carry the cookies-and-cream part. Use cold water and add it slowly; warm water and a fast pour both give you a lumpy, loose cup that never firms up.
