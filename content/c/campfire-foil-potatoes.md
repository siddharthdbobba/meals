This started as an air fryer recipe, which is no use in a campsite. The mechanism transfers anyway: oil on the skin, salt on the oil, dry heat all around. Coals do what the fryer's fan does, just slower and less evenly, so the fix is turning the packets and testing by feel rather than by clock.

The foil is not optional over a fire. Bare potatoes in coals burn through on one side before the center gives up, and heavy foil holds enough steam to cook the inside while the skin still crisps against the metal. Potatoes are heavy for what they deliver in calories, so this is car-camping and basecamp food. Nobody should carry four russets uphill.
