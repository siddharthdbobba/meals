This is a recipe for asparagus you found too late. Spears that went woody in the field will never soften on a quick sear, so the whole method is aimed at time: an hour in fat and cream, covered, over low coals. The cream keeps the pan from scorching while the heat works through the fibers, and the parmesan thickens what would otherwise stay thin.

Coals, not flame. Cast iron over direct fire will burn the bottom of the cream long before the stalks give up. Pull a ring of coals around the skillet and feed it slowly. This one needs a cooler for the cream and bacon, which puts it at car camp or basecamp — the boxed-cream variation is the version that goes further from the truck.
