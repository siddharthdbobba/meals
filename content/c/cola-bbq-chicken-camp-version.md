The original called for two hours of simmering bone-in chicken, which is a fine backyard project and a bad idea on a canister. Canned chicken is already cooked, so the only thing that still needs time is the sauce. Reducing the cola first is the whole trick: it concentrates the sugar into something that clings, and it means you are not trying to boil off a can of soda while the meat sits in it turning to threads.

This is heavy, and the cans make it car camping or paddle food, not pack food. Watch the heat once the honey goes in. Sugar on a thin aluminum pot over an unregulated flame goes from glaze to carbon in about a minute.
