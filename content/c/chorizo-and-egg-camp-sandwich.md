Two changes make this work away from a kitchen. Smoked chorizo is already cured, so it needs heat but not refrigeration, and it renders enough fat to cook everything else in the pan without carrying oil. Fresh eggs and fresh tomato both lose an argument with a pack, so whole egg powder and oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes take their place. The dried tomato is more concentrated than a raw slice, which matters here since nothing else on the sandwich is acidic.

The eggs are the only step with a way to fail. Powdered egg sets faster and tighter than fresh, so keep the flame low and pull the pan early. Rubbery scramble is a heat problem, not an ingredient problem. Everything happens in one pan in sequence: sausage, then bread, then eggs, each step using the fat left by the last.
