This started as an oven dish and most of the oven work stays at home. The chips are the whole reason it travels: baked wonton wrappers are dry, light, and stable for a few days, which fried ones are not. Bake them the night before you leave and the camp job is one skillet of pork and a sauce.

The cornstarch matters. Straight soy and vinegar is dipping sauce, and it runs off the chips into the bottom of the plate. Thickened, it clings long enough to eat. The cabbage goes on raw and cold for crunch, since it is the only thing here fighting the softening chips.
