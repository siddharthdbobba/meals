This started as an herbalist's tea preparation and it survives the trail almost unchanged, which is rare. Dried leaf and root weigh nothing, keep for a season in a sealed bag, and need only hot water. The one thing worth carrying that a home kitchen would not bother with is the refillable bag or mesh ball. Loose herbs floating in a pot become loose herbs in your teeth.

The method is the whole recipe. Steep off the heat, not at a boil, and keep the lid on. Boiling shreds mint's oils and turns chamomile bitter; five minutes under a lid gets you the flavor and the ginger warmth without either. Honey goes in last for the same reason. Sweetener aside, this is 350 ml of hot water you will actually drink, which on a cold night is the point.
