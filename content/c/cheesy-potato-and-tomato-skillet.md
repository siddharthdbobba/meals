The source was a family grocery run: potatoes, onions, canned crushed tomatoes, and two kinds of cheese from a warehouse store. Those are good bones for a cheap dinner and terrible things to carry. Baking potatoes became flakes, the #10 can of crushed tomatoes became tomato powder, and the onions became dried mince. The romaine did not survive the translation.

Two cheeses do two jobs. Freeze-dried mozzarella dissolves into the potatoes and thickens them. The colby jack cubes stay whole, so you hit pockets of melted cheese instead of a uniform paste. Add the cubes off heat or they break and leave grease on top.
