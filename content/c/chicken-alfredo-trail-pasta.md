Store-bought alfredo pouches solve this with one boil, but you can build the same thing from grocery-shelf parts for less money and more food. The order matters more than the ingredients: chicken and mushrooms need water and time, pasta needs less, and the sauce needs no heat at all. Dumping everything in at once gives you crunchy chicken in a scorched sauce.

The milk powder is the piece most people skip. Packet alfredo mix on its own thins out into something gluey; the extra dairy fat and the tablespoon of oil give it enough body to coat the noodles. Add the powders off the heat and slowly, or they clump and no amount of stirring fixes it.
