The original is a skillet dish: fresh beef, eggs, charred tomatoes and onion blackened in a pan and blended into salsa. None of that survives three days in a pack, so this version rebuilds each part from a powder. Freeze-dried beef takes the place of ground chuck, powdered egg does the binding, and tomato powder plus chipotle powder stand in for the blender step. You lose the char. You keep the shape of the dish.

The uncooked instant rice in the meatball mix is doing more than it looks like. Rehydrated beef has no fat holding it together, and the rice swells during the simmer, gripping the crumbles from the inside. Brown them hard before the sauce goes in or they will dissolve into a chipotle hash, which still eats fine but is not what you came for. Simmering uncovered is deliberate: the sauce has to lose water to cling.
