Dried cheese and milk powder let you make a real sauce without the weight or spoilage risk of fresh dairy. This matters on longer trips. The pasta absorbs the sauce without getting waterlogged.

The sauce thickens after the heat comes off. Pull it early when it still looks thin, because it will set up as it cools. Cook it longer and it becomes a glob instead of coating the pasta.
