Home-canned meat is already cooked through, so the only real work is getting the vegetables soft. Everything else is warming up. That makes this a good first-night meal when you rolled into camp late and nobody wants to think.

The original called for a bag of frozen vegetables, which no cooler holds past day two. Dried peas, corn, and carrots do the same job and rehydrate in the juice while it heats. Potato flakes replace the missing starch: without them the juice stays thin and drinks more like a hot beverage than a dinner.
