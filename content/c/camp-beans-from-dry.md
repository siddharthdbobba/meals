Dried beans are the cheapest protein you can carry and the slowest to cook, so the whole recipe is about moving the waiting off your stove. The soak happens in a bottle strapped to your pack while you walk. By the time you stop, the beans have taken on water for eight hours and the simmer is 45 minutes instead of two hours.

Draining the soak water matters. The sugars that cause the gas leach out into it, and dumping it takes most of them with it. Salt and acid both go in at the end: add either one early and the skins toughen, and you will burn fuel on beans that never soften. The lemon juice replaces the whey the home version uses, since nothing fresh and dairy survives a pack.
