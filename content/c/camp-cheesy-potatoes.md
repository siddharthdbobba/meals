The commercial cups are just instant hashbrowns and cheese powder in expensive packaging. Mix your own for a third the cost and double the portion. Cheese powder is doing the real work—it thickens the hydrated potatoes into something with body instead of leaving you with soup.

Ten minutes of resting matters. It gives the potatoes time to absorb the water and the powder to hydrate fully. Stir well before eating or everything settles into a brick at the bottom. Add bacon or dehydrated chicken if you want this to count as dinner instead of a side.
