This started as a copycat of the mayonnaise at a sandwich chain, which meant raw eggs and a refrigerator. Neither travels. Powdered whole egg rehydrates into something that emulsifies about as well as the real thing and keeps at ambient temperature for months, so the whole build moves to the picnic table.

The order matters more than the technique. Everything acidic and dry goes in first, oil last, and the blender stays pinned to the bottom so it works on a small pocket of liquid at a time. Break that rule and you get oil with egg suspended in it. Made without a cooler, the finished jar keeps about a day in warm weather, so mix what a lunch will actually use.
