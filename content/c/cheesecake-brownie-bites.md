This is a home dessert carried out rather than a trail recipe, and that is deliberate. Cream cheese does not travel without a cooler, so this one belongs on car-camping trips, paddle trips, and short overnights where a cold bag comes along. The filling is stiffened with butter and chilled hard so it behaves like a slab instead of oozing out the sides of the sandwich.

All the work happens at the kitchen counter. Assembly and chilling at home means camp effort is zero: open a tin, hand them out. The chopped chocolate in the filling is structural as much as anything, giving the soft center something to hold onto once it warms up in your hand.
