This is a home dessert that only needed two changes to work at a campsite: the milk becomes powdered milk and water, and the flour gets toasted before it leaves the kitchen. Nothing here is baked, so there is no oven and no stove beyond a pot warm enough to melt chocolate.

Butter temperature is the whole game. If it is cold, the sugar stays gritty and the dough never binds. Pull it out of the cooler an hour before you start, or set the wrapped stick in the sun while you eat dinner. The chill at the end is short on purpose — an hour firms the pie, and much past that the chocolate layer cracks instead of slicing.
