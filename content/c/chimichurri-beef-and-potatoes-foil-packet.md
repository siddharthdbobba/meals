Chimichurri is normally a fresh-herb sauce, which does not survive a cooler in August. Dried parsley and cilantro bloomed in olive oil for twenty minutes get most of the way there. The vinegar and the crushed red pepper do the rest of the work, and both keep indefinitely.

Potatoes and beef want very different times on the fire, so they get separate packets. Twenty-five minutes for quartered potatoes, ten for cubed sirloin. Combine them only at the end, off the heat, so the sauce goes on warm rather than cooking down into the foil.
