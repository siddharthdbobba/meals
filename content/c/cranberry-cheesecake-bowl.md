The original is baked in a springform pan inside a slow cooker, which is not a thing you carry. Eggs, sour cream, and a water bath all disappear here. What replaces them is cornstarch: it sets the filling without heat, so you get pudding texture instead of custard. Cream cheese powder and milk powder rehydrate into something close enough to the real thing that nobody complains.

The cranberry sauce does most of the work. Dried cranberries simmered with sugar and acid rebuild into a real compote in two minutes, and the acid is what keeps the whole bowl from tasting like sweetened chalk. Cook the sauce first so the pot only needs a wipe between steps.
