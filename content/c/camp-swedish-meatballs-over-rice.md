This is a two-pot meal that runs on a clock, not on attention. The rice takes 15 to 20 minutes covered and the sauce takes 20 to 30, so if you start both at once they finish together and nobody stands over anything. That matters on arrival night, when the stove is the only thing working while everything else in camp is still in a bag.

The canned soup is the sauce. Condensed cream of mushroom brings the fat and the thickener, and the consommé thins it back out and adds beef flavor the frozen meatballs do not have on their own. Nothing gets browned and nothing gets a roux, which is why this works on a camp stove with one burner of real control. Frozen meatballs need a cooler, so this is a car trip, not a carry. The variations cover the shelf-stable version.
