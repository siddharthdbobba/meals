The original is a fridge recipe: fresh eggs, half-and-half, sour cream, raw potatoes, a pound of bacon. Every one of those has a dry equivalent that behaves the same way, so the swaps are powdered milk for the cream, egg and sour cream powder for the eggs, dehydrated hash browns for the raw spuds, and precooked bacon crumbles for the strips. Ghee replaces butter because it does not need cold and does not scorch as fast.

Order matters more than technique here. Potatoes take the longest and hold heat the longest, so they go first and sit covered while the eggs and cakes cook in the same skillet. The cornmeal is what keeps the cakes from going gummy without buttermilk, and the fat cut into the dry mix gives them a short, crumbly edge instead of a rubbery one.
