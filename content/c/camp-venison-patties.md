Venison is lean and needs binding—breadcrumbs and egg keep the mixture moist during cooking. Worcestershire, onion, and garlic powder replace the fresh vegetables of the home recipe, delivering the same savory notes without the water weight or spoilage risk.

Foil-packet cooking is the simplest option: the patties steam inside the foil and char slightly against the coals. They won't dry out and hold heat without overcooking, which matters when you're not eating immediately after the fire cools.
