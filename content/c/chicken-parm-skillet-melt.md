This is a home skillet dish moved to a fire ring with two swaps. Powdered egg replaces the beaten egg so nothing has to survive a warm cooler, and canned chicken replaces the butterflied breast, which means no raw poultry riding around and no doneness guessing by headlamp. The patties are already cooked through, so the frying step is only about crust.

The fire roasted tomatoes carry the sauce. They come out of the can with char already on them, which is the flavor a long stovetop simmer normally builds. Fifteen minutes over coals is enough to drive off the canning water and nothing more. Everything happens in one skillet, in order, so the fond from the chicken ends up in the sauce.
