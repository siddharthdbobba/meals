This started as an oven recipe and a party appetizer. The move outdoors is mostly about heat control: a covered dutch oven in coals does what a 375 degree oven does, as long as you turn the pan so the hot side keeps moving. Everything else survives the trip unchanged.

Two swaps carry the load. Powdered milk plus water replaces the half cup of whole milk, which is the only fridge item in the original besides the sausage and cheese. Dried chives stand in for green onion, since scallions turn to slime in a cooler by day two. The sausage fat is what binds the dough and keeps the cornbread from drying out, so do not lean it out.

The dipping sauce is the part worth carrying the extra bottles for. Maple, chili crisp, and lime hit sweet, hot, and sharp against a very rich ball.
