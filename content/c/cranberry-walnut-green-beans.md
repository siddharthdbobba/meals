This started as a holiday side dish made with frozen green beans and an oven. The trail version keeps the parts that actually matter: beans, dried cranberries, walnuts, and a sweet-sour dressing. Frozen anything is out, so the beans go in dried and come back with hot water.

The craisins do double duty. They rehydrate alongside the beans and bleed sugar and acid into the water, which seasons the whole pot before you add anything else. The walnuts are the fat and the texture, and they are the only reason this eats like a real side and not a bowl of wet vegetable. Keep them out of the water.
