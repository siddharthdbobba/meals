The original is a car-camp soup: raw chicken breast, a pound of carrots, a whole onion, eight cups of water for eight people. None of that travels. This version does the same job with dehydrated mirepoix and freeze-dried chicken, scaled to one pot and one person, with the water cut to what a small pot actually holds.

The order matters more than anything else here. Vegetables and chicken go in first because they need real simmer time to soften. Noodles go in last because they release starch, and starch on a stove with a hot spot means a scorched pot. The tablespoon of oil is not seasoning, it is the calories: broth alone will not carry you through a cold night.
