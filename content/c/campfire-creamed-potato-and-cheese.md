The original is a casserole: onions, potato chips, and cheese layered in a baking dish and baked under cream soup. This camp version keeps the same bones—vegetables, cheese, creamed soup—but ditches the oven and baking time. Dehydrated potatoes soften in the soup without fussing, and you stir everything together in one pot.

Cream of chicken soup is doing the heavy lifting as broth and thickener both. The cheese melts into the sauce and makes it rich enough for a full dinner. This recipe doesn't scale down well (a full can is your minimum), so it's car-camping food where weight matters less than ease.
