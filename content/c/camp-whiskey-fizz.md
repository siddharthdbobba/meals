The original is a shaken bar drink: fresh lime, simple syrup, ice, soda. None of that travels. Lime crystals carry the acid at almost no weight, plain sugar dissolved in a little water does the job of syrup, and lemon-lime powder replaces the soda without a can. What you lose is carbonation. What you keep is the sour-sweet-strong balance, which is the part that matters.

Dissolve the sugar first, in a small amount of water, before anything else goes in. Cold water and a full bottle will not carry sugar into solution and you will end up with a sweet slug at the bottom and a sharp drink on top. The pinch of salt is not for flavor alone; it rounds the acid so the lime crystals read less like a supplement.
