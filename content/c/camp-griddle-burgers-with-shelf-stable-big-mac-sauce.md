This is a fast food burger rebuilt for a fire ring. The technique that matters is the smash: a loose ball pressed hard onto very hot cast iron for the first ten seconds, then untouched. That contact makes the browned crust, and the crust is most of the flavor. A patty formed at home and carried as a puck will never get there.

The sauce is the other half. Mayonnaise, French dressing, relish, vinegar, and sugar mixed at home keeps in a jar through a weekend, and dried minced onion replaces the grated fresh onion so nothing wilts in the pack. American cheese is not a compromise here — it melts at a lower temperature than real cheddar, which matters when the patty comes off the grate in 90 seconds.
