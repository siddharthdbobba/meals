The original is a fridge dessert built on cream cheese and whipped heavy cream, neither of which survives a day in a pack. Instant cheesecake pudding plus whole milk powder gets close: the pudding starch sets without cold, and the milk fat carries the flavor the cream used to. Mix it with cold water, not hot, or the starch seizes and you get lumps.

Fresh cranberries become dried ones, which means they need liquid back before they taste like anything. Simmering them with sugar and a little acid rehydrates and candies them at once, and the cornstarch turns the leftover syrup into something that sits on the pudding instead of running through it. Cook the compote first and the cheesecake second — the pudding needs the pot cool anyway.
