This started as a slow-cooked whole chicken with the previous night's roasted vegetables stirred in. None of that survives a pack. What survives is the shape: broth, rice, vegetables, and a lot of chicken in one pot.

The substitutions are all about water. Instant rice hydrates off residual heat instead of burning fuel, dried carrot needs a real simmer while freeze-dried beans do not, and the potato flakes replace the body a long-cooked bird would have given the broth. Foil-packet chicken carries the protein without a cooler.
