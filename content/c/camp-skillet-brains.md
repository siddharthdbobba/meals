This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Brains are perishable and mostly water, so there is no dry version of them and no shelf-stable substitute worth pretending about. What makes it a camp dish is that all the fussy work happens at home the night before, and the fire only has to do two simple things.

The overnight soak is the part people skip and then regret. Brains hold a lot of blood, and blood is what turns them grey and gives them the metallic edge that puts people off. Cold water and a few changes pull it out. The short poach afterward is structural: raw brains are too soft to dredge or turn in a pan, and eight minutes in acidulated water firms them enough to hold a crust. Fry them straight from the cooler and you get scrambled custard instead.
