This is a make-at-home job with a cook-at-camp payoff. Store sausage is fine, but mixing your own means you control the fat and the salt, and two pounds of ground pork costs less than the equivalent in patties. The maple is there to caramelize on the pan surface, not to sweeten the meat.

Freezing the patties flat does double duty: they're part of your cooler ice on the drive in, and they thaw on their own schedule. Cook over coals, not flame. Half-inch patties need about seven minutes total, and a fire that's still flaming will blacken them before the center is safe.
