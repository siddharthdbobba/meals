The original is a blender soup: sauté mirepoix, simmer chickpeas in broth, purée smooth. No blender on trail, so the purée is built in instead. Dehydrated hummus mix is already ground chickpea, which means it dissolves into the broth and gives you the same velvety body without a motor.

The lemon is not a garnish. Chickpea and cumin go flat and heavy without acid, and citric acid powder weighs nothing. Cornstarch does the rest of the work on texture, and the oil is where most of the calories live — a soup this thin is otherwise a starter, not dinner.
