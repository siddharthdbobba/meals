Pudding powder hydrates with cold water to create a creamy base without heat or refrigeration. Powdered milk adds protein and body. The result tastes like cheesecake because commercial pudding is engineered that way, even without actual cream cheese.

The trick is precise water—200 ml makes it creamy, not soupy. Don't stir in crumbs until just before eating or they'll go soggy. Keep the prepared dessert in your coolest spot while it sets.
