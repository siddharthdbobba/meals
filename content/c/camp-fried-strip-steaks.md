A whole strip loin runs cheaper per pound than ribeye, and breading it lets a lower grade eat well. The flour coating does two things: it holds seasoning against the meat, and it forms a crust that keeps the juices in a cut that has less marbling to spare.

This one stays a car-camp or basecamp recipe. It needs a cooler, a cast iron skillet, and enough fat to shallow-fry, none of which go in a pack. Fry in batches and keep the oil hot — a cooled pan steams the coating off instead of setting it.
