Foil packets work because the seal traps steam from the vegetables and cooks the inside while the bottom picks up direct heat from the coals. That is why the potatoes have to be small and the packet has to be closed properly. A loose fold vents the steam and you get scorched bottoms and raw centers.

Use two sheets. One sheet tears on a rock or a stick and then dinner is in the fire. Smoked sausage is the easy protein here since it is already cooked and only needs to warm through, which means you can time the whole packet around the potatoes and stop worrying about it.
