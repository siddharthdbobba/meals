This is a cooler meal, not a stove meal. Everything that needs cooking happens in your kitchen, so at the site you are pouring three bags into one and shaking. That is the whole appeal after a long paddle or a late arrival at the campground.

The dressing is mostly oil by volume, and it should be. Oil coats the cabbage so it stays crisp for hours instead of weeping into a puddle, and it carries the ginger heat across the noodles. Rinsing the noodles cold at home matters too: warm noodles keep absorbing dressing until the salad goes dry.
