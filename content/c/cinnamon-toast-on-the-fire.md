This started as a toaster-oven recipe, which is not a piece of gear anyone carries. The fix is foil on a grill grate over coals, which does the same job: dry heat from below, no pan to scrub. Coals, not flame — sugar scorches fast and a flare-up takes the slice with it.

The butter is the other change. A stick needs a cooler, so ghee or canned butter does the work instead and keeps for weeks in a jar. Cutting the cinnamon back to a teaspoon matters too; a full tablespoon against one slice of bread is dry and bitter, and the sugar cannot cover for it.
