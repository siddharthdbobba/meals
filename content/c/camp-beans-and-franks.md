This is a car-camping dinner, not a trail one. Two large cans and a package of franks weigh close to five pounds, and the bacon and hot dogs need a cooler, so it belongs at a site you drove to.

The sauce is doing the work. Canned baked beans arrive already sweet, and the mustard, hot pepper, and molasses are what keep them from tasting like a side dish out of a can. Cook the bacon short of crisp and finish it on top of the pot: it stays chewy and its fat renders down into the beans over that last 15 minutes instead of staying on a paper towel.
