This is a day-one food. Avocados do not keep, do not tolerate being sat on, and go brown within hours of being opened, so it earns its place only on trips where you are eating it the first afternoon. Car camping, a paddle day, a basecamp you walked into that morning.

The short ingredient list is the point. No onion, no cilantro, no jalapeño means nothing to chop and nothing to wilt. Granulated garlic and cumin come from the same spice bag you already carry, and the lime does double duty: acid for flavor, and it slows the browning. Mashing in the freezer bag means no bowl, no cutting board, and one thing to pack out.
