This is Eton mess with ice cream standing in for whipped cream, which is the whole reason it works away from a kitchen. Whipping cream at a campsite is a chore and fresh cream is a cooler risk. Ice cream is already whipped, already sweet, and already frozen, so it doubles as cooler ballast on the way in.

The meringues are structure, not decoration. They stay crisp for the first minute and then dissolve into the melt, which is what turns three separate things into one dessert. Assemble right before eating — a mess that sits gets soupy, and the berries are the only thing holding acidity against all that sugar.
