Bean flakes rehydrate faster than anything else that delivers this much protein, and they thicken on their own, so there is no draining and no second pot. The freeze-dried corn is the only ingredient that needs real soak time, which is why it goes in first while the water is still hot.

Add the flakes in stages. Beans keep absorbing for a minute or two after they look done, and a batch that seemed thin at the stove will be mortar by the time you sit down. Aim for slightly looser than you want.

Tortillas do the work a fork would otherwise do, and they are the cheapest calories in the bag.
