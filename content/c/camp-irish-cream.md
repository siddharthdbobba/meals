The original is eggs, sweetened condensed milk, and whipping cream in a blender, which is three refrigerated things and a wall outlet. None of that travels. Full-fat milk powder plus coconut milk powder gets you the same body without a cooler, and the raw eggs go away entirely, which is a food-safety win as much as a weight one. Powdered sugar replaces the condensed milk's sweetness and dissolves in cold water; cocoa and a little espresso powder replace the chocolate syrup at a fraction of the carry weight.

The hour of standing is not optional if you want it smooth. Cocoa and milk powder both hydrate slowly in cold liquid, and shaking alone leaves grit on the tongue. The alcohol keeps the mix stable overnight without a cooler, so a bottle made at camp on night one is still fine on night three.
