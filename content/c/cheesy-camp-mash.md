The original is a baked casserole with four pounds of real potatoes, a stick of butter, and a cup of cream. None of that travels. Flakes replace the boiled potatoes, powdered whole milk replaces the cream, and butter powder or ghee replaces the stick — all of it shelf-stable, all of it in one pot.

The cheese does the work in two stages. Parmesan is dry enough to dissolve straight into the hot mash and season the whole thing. The cubed hard cheese goes in off the heat and melts slowly under the lid, so you get pockets of it instead of a uniform paste. Skip the fat and you have wallpaper paste with cheese in it.
