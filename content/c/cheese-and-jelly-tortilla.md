This started as a punchline about what you eat when nobody in camp can cook. It holds up because every part of it is stable at any temperature, needs no water, and takes two minutes with cold hands. Processed cheese spread is the whole trick: it is emulsified to sit on a shelf, so it behaves nothing like real cheese in heat and does not sweat oil in a pack.

The layers matter more than the ingredients. Cheese under, jam over, crackers on top gives you fat, sugar, and salt in separate bites instead of one sweet smear. Roll it tight and the crackers stop it from sliding apart while you walk.
