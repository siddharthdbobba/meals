The original is made with fresh milk and topped with whipped cream, which is a fridge problem. Whole milk powder solves it: the fat is still there, so the chocolate has something to melt into and the drink comes out thick rather than watery. Instant cocoa mix would not work the same way — it is mostly sugar and starch, and it cannot carry 50 grams of chocolate.

Heat control is the whole game. Milk on a canister stove goes from steaming to scorched in seconds, and burnt milk solids ruin the pot and the drink. Take it off the flame before it boils and let residual heat do the melting. Chili flakes steep as it sits, so the longer it waits, the hotter it gets.
