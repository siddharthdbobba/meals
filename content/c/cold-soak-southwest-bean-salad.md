The original is a can of beans, a can of corn, and a jar of salsa dumped in a bowl. That is three pounds of water you would be carrying. Every wet ingredient here has a dried counterpart that costs almost nothing in weight: bean flakes for canned beans, freeze-dried kernels for canned corn, tomato powder plus onion, chili, and lime for the salsa.

The acid matters more than it looks. Beans rehydrated in plain water are flat and pasty, and powdered lime is what makes the whole thing read as a salad instead of cold refried beans. The oil goes in at the end because fat coats the flakes and slows how fast they take up water.
