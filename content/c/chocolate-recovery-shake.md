The four-to-one carb-to-protein ratio is the point. Maltodextrin refills muscle glycogen fast because it digests almost as quickly as straight glucose, and the protein rides along to start repair. Whey isolate is the version that stays smooth in cold water instead of turning gritty.

The source version leaned on fresh milk and ice, neither of which exists on the trail. Powdered whole milk stands in for the body and the fat, and cold creek or bottle water does the rest. Unsweetened cocoa is not decoration here: maltodextrin is bland-sweet and a spoonful of cocoa keeps 120 grams of it drinkable.
