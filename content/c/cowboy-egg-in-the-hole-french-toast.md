This is one slice of bread doing two jobs. The hole gives the egg a wall to set against, so it cooks flat and stays put through the flip instead of sliding around a bare skillet. Sweet egg bread is the right choice because it is dense enough to hold together when you turn it over, and enriched enough to brown fast.

Heat control is the whole game. Coals raked flat or a stove on low give the egg time to set while the bread browns. Rip-roaring heat gives you a black slice with raw white in the middle. Eggs and butter mean this one needs a cooler, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp, not in a pack.
