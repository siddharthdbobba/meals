A boxed cake mix is already flour, sugar, and leavening measured out and sealed against humidity, which is most of what makes cookie dough annoying to carry. Add fat, eggs, and chips and you have skipped every dry measurement at the campsite.

The dutch oven does the rest. Coals on the lid are what browns the tops; coals underneath alone will burn the bottoms before the middles set, so keep the bed under the oven thin and pile the heat above. Pull them while the centers still look underdone, because they keep cooking on the hot cast iron for a few minutes after they come off the fire.
