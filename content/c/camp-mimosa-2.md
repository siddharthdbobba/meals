This is a brunch drink dragged outside. The real version is orange juice and sparkling wine, and neither travels: juice needs a cooler, and a full bottle of anything is a bad thing to carry. Powdered drink mix solves the first problem and a 187 ml split solves the second. One split makes two honest cups.

Mix the powder into plain water before the wine goes anywhere near it. Powder dumped into carbonation foams up and out of the cup, and you lose both the drink and the bubbles. Cold matters more than it seems here — the mix tastes flat and syrupy warm, and the wine goes dull within a minute of leaving the shade.
