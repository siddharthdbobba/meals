This started as a butter-and-eggs cake baked in a skull-shaped pan in a home oven. Neither the butter nor the four eggs survive a weekend in a car, so both are powders here: buttermilk powder and whole egg powder rehydrate with cold water, and oil takes over the fat job. The result is slightly denser than the original and holds together better when you cut it with a pocket knife.

The hot coffee is not a flourish. Pouring boiling liquid over cocoa powder blooms it, which is where the depth comes from, and the chili and cinnamon read as warmth behind the chocolate rather than as spice. Coal placement matters more than time: more coals on the lid than underneath, or you get a burnt bottom and a wet middle.
