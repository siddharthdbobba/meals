This started as a horse-packing meal, where a cooler and a can opener are both fair game. The can is heavy and the calories per ounce are mediocre, so this is a car-camp, basecamp, or boat dinner, not a backpacking one. Everything in it is shelf-stable, which is the point: no cooler, no ice, no timeline.

The dried hash browns are doing the work the source recipe left to fresh potatoes. Rehydrate them first, then drive off the water before the hash goes in, or the whole pot stays wet and never browns. The crust is the entire reason to cook this over a fire instead of eating the can cold, so resist stirring it.
