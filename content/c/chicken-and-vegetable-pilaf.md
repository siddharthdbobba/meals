Instant rice is the vehicle. Dehydrated mushrooms and tomatoes add umami and depth, while butter powder and Parmesan deliver fat—the ingredient doing the real work of making this taste like dinner. Freeze-dried chicken is lightweight protein that needs no refrigeration. Everything mixes dry at home and travels weightless until you need it.

Fifteen minutes of passive steeping does the actual cooking. The mushrooms soften, the rice absorbs liquid, the chicken rehydrates, and all the flavors meld together. The vinegar is optional but worth finding—a deli packet adds tartness that cuts through the richness without adding weight. In cool weather or at high altitude, wrap the pot in your fleece to keep everything hot while it steeps.
