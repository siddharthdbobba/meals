  - "Get a bed of coals going and set the grate about four inches above it. You want steady heat, not flames."

This is a car-camp burger, not a trail meal, and there is no honest way to shrink it into a pot. It leans on a cooler and about twenty minutes of knife work, so do the cutting at home and treat camp as assembly plus fire.

The blackberry sauce is doing the heavy lifting. Fruit and honey against BBQ sauce and chili gives the patty something acidic to push back on, which matters more over coals than on a stove because grill smoke flattens everything it touches. The cabbage cooked in vinegar, mustard, and bacon fat plays the same role from the other side. Both can be made ahead, which is the only reason the whole thing lands in twenty-five minutes of actual cooking.
