The original was a carbonara pretending to be primavera: raw eggs and cream tipped over hot pasta, plus fiddleheads that are a two-week window in spring and not something you carry. On trail the whole thing collapses into one pot. Freeze-dried mushrooms, peas, and broccoli rehydrate in the pasta water, so the vegetables and noodles finish together and nothing gets drained away.

The sauce is the part worth getting right. Cook the water down instead of pouring it off, then take the pot off the flame before the powders go in. Residual heat sets the egg powder into something creamy; direct flame scrambles it and you get grit stuck to the pot floor. The oil at the end is not garnish, it is a third of the fat in the bowl.
