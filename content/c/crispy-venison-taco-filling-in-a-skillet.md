Venison is lean enough that a hot pan turns it to gravel. The baking soda trick is worth the ten minutes it costs: it raises the pH of the meat so it holds onto water while it browns, and dried onion rehydrated in the pan juices puts back some of the moisture a fresh onion would have carried.

The source deep-fried these in a pot of oil. That is a lot of fuel and a lot of hot oil to dispose of at a campsite, so this pan-fries them folded in a shallow film instead. You get the same stiff, browned shell without carrying a quart of oil in and a quart of used oil out. Keep the oil moving and the tortillas flat against the metal, and they crisp in about the same time.
