This is the potluck carrot salad, minus the grater and the refrigerator. Fresh carrots are mostly water, so drying them at home cuts the carry weight by roughly nine tenths and turns the soak into the whole method. The raisins and pineapple rehydrate on the same clock, and the sugar they give up ends up in the dressing.

Mayonnaise packets are the part people doubt. They are pasteurized, acidic, and sealed, and they hold fine in a pack for a few days. Add them at the end, after you drain, or the dressing thins out into soup.
