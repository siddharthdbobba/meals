The canned version of this is malt liquor, cream, and sugar, and none of those travel. Concentrate, syrup, and a flask do. The whole thing is three small bottles and a mug, which is why it belongs on a trip at all.

The salt is doing more than it looks like. Shelf-stable cold brew concentrate is aggressive on its own, and a pinch flattens the bitter edge so you can use less syrup. Caffeine plus alcohol makes you feel less drunk than you are, so treat this as the drink it is: one, sitting down, after camp is set.
