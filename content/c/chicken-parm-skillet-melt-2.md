The original is a slow cooker recipe: frozen tenders, four hours on low, then a broiler for the cheese. Neither of those exists at camp. Canned chicken is already cooked and already shredded, so the whole first half of the method disappears, and a lid over low heat does what the broiler did.

Toasting the buns first is not optional. Sauce hits bread hard, and an untoasted bun collapses before you finish it. Provolone is the pick over mozzarella because it comes sliced and vacuum-sealed, which means it survives a day or two out of a cooler; the parmesan is shelf-stable indefinitely and carries most of the salt.
