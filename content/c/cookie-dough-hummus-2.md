The original is butter, white flour, and sugar eaten raw, which is fine at a kitchen counter and useless in a pack. Chickpeas do the same job here: mashed fine they give the dough body and a neutral base, and they bring protein instead of raw flour and its bacteria problem. Peanut butter replaces the softened butter, so nothing has to stay cold to stay workable.

Powdered milk stands in for the four tablespoons of fresh milk and thickens rather than thins, so you add water yourself and control the texture. Mixing inside the bag means no bowl, no mixer, and nothing to wash. Chop the candy at home; on a warm afternoon it will not chop, it will smear.
