A stir-fry needs a hot pan and a fridge, so neither survives the trip. What survives is the parts list: quinoa instead of rice, tofu for protein, cashews for fat and crunch, and a soy-ginger seasoning. Cooked as a single simmer, it lands in roughly the same place.

Drying tofu takes a freeze-thaw first. Freezing coarsens the curd, so the thawed block presses much drier and the cubes rehydrate spongy rather than chalky. Skip that step and you get gravel. The cashews go in after the heat is off for the same reason they go in last in a wok: fifteen minutes in water turns them soft and pointless.
