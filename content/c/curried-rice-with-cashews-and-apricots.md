Milk powder makes this work. Whisked into the water before the rice goes in, it gives the pot enough body to carry curry powder and melted cheddar instead of leaving both sitting on top of plain rice. Mix it as a paste first or you will spend the whole simmer chasing lumps.

The apricots go in late on purpose. Add them at the start and they dissolve; add them with ten minutes left and they soften into something you can still bite. Cashews and cheese go in off the heat, which keeps the nuts crisp and the cheese from breaking. Two ounces of cheddar rides fine for a few days in cool weather, and it is where most of the fat comes from.
