The original is an oven recipe with rolled pizza dough and an egg wash, neither of which survives a weekend in a car. Tortillas do the same job: they hold sauce and cheese, they seal when pressed, and they brown over coals. Olive oil replaces the melted butter because it lives in the same bottle you already carry.

Low-moisture mozzarella matters more than it sounds. Fresh mozzarella releases water as it heats and you end up with a soggy packet. The block kind melts instead of leaking. Tomato paste rather than jarred sauce for the same reason — less water going in means less water pooling in the foil.
