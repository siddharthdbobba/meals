The original is a crock pot roast: cola, chili sauce, onion soup mix, eight hours on low. The sugar and acid in the soda break down a tough cut while the soup mix supplies the salt and the browned-onion flavor. None of that survives a backpack, so this version keeps the flavor triangle and drops the eight hours.

Freeze-dried beef rehydrates in ten minutes instead of breaking down over an afternoon, which means the sauce has to do the heavy lifting. Brown sugar and a little acid stand in for the cola. Potato flakes replace the potatoes that would have cooked in the pot and thicken the sauce at the same time, so nothing pools at the bottom of the bowl.
