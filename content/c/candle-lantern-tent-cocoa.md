A candle lantern will lift a small tent about four degrees. That is real but it is not warmth. What actually raises your core temperature on a cold night is calories and hot liquid, and this mug delivers both faster than anything you can burn.

The milk powder and the fat are the point. Plain cocoa is sugar water and it fades in twenty minutes. Whole milk powder brings protein and enough fat to keep the heat working, and the pinch of salt keeps the sweetness from going flat. Make it a paste before adding the rest of the water or you will drink lumps.
