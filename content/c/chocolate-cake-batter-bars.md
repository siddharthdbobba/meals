This is home-kitchen assembly, not trail cooking. Nothing gets heated, but the pressing and the chill are what turn a bowl of crumbs into a bar you can carry, so do them properly. Honey and nut butter are the entire binding system: honey is sticky enough to glue puffed rice together, and the fat in the nut butter keeps the slab from setting up as hard as a brick.

The ground oats matter more than they look. Whole rolled oats stay separate and the bar falls apart in your pocket; ground fine, they act as flour and fill the gaps between the puffed rice. The original keeps these in the refrigerator, which is no help on trail. Press harder than feels necessary, cut them small, and wrap each one so a soft bar in August is still one bar and not a smear.
