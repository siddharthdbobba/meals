Potato salad is a refrigerator dish, so the trail version has to solve two problems: no cold storage and no stove. Freeze-dried potato dices handle the first, since they come back to something with structure in cold water instead of collapsing the way dehydrated slices often do. Mayonnaise packets handle the second. They are shelf-stable until opened, and they carry the fat that makes this a meal rather than a side.

The water is the whole game. Barely covering the mix leaves the potatoes to pull in what they need and no more; overshoot and the dressing thins out into soup you cannot fix. Thirty minutes beats ten by a wide margin, so start it soaking when you drop your pack and go do something else. The five-minute rest after the dressing goes in is not padding either, the dill and garlic need a moment in the fat before they taste like anything.
