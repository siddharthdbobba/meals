This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Nearly all the weight is water sealed inside cans, so it is heavy to carry and cheap to cook. The payoff is that no rehydration step exists to get wrong: the bean and corn liquid is the sauce, and undrained chicken keeps the base loose enough that the dumplings poach instead of scorching.

The dumplings need the two-stage lid. Uncovered first so the surface liquid boils off and the dough sets from below, then covered so trapped steam finishes the middle. Cover them from the start and you get a raw seam through the center. Coals go on the lid only for the cheese at the end, since heat from above before that will brown the tops long before the dough cooks through. Milk powder replaces fresh milk; it hydrates fine in the dough and needs no cooler.
