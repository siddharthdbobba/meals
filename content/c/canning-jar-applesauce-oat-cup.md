Canning season leaves you with more applesauce than anyone eats at a table, and a half-pint jar happens to hold the right amount of liquid to soften three quarters of a cup of oats. That is the whole idea. No stove, no measuring cup, no pot to scrub.

The chia matters more than it looks. Applesauce alone is thin enough that oats float in it; the chia binds the water and the pectin together so what you get is a thick spoonable cup instead of fruit soup with grain in it. Sunflower seeds go in last because anything crunchy stirred in early is soft by morning.
