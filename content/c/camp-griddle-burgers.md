steps:

  - "Split the chuck into six portions and shape half-inch patties a little wider than the buns. Handle the meat as little as you can. Press a dent in the middle of each so they do not dome up."

This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Two pounds of raw beef needs ice, so it belongs at a car campsite or a basecamp with a hard cooler and a short drive behind it. Nothing here works out of a pack.

The spice mix is premixed at home for a reason: measuring seven things onto a picnic table in wind is how you end up with half of it on the ground. Season the patties at the last second, since salt sitting on raw meat pulls water out and firms up the surface. The thumb dent matters more over a fire than over a stove, because uneven coals make patties swell in the middle and slide off the bun.
