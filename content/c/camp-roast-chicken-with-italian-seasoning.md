This is a car camping recipe and it stays one. A whole bird needs a cooler and an hour and a half of managed heat, so it belongs at a site you are not leaving. The payoff is the same reason people do it at home: one cook session, three dinners. Breast side down matters more over coals than it does in an oven, because the heat from below is uneven and the breast is the part that dries out first.

The rub is dry spices and oil only, which is deliberate. No butter, no marinade, nothing that needs its own container or its own cold space. The 10-minute rest is not optional at camp either, and skipping it costs you the moisture you will want on nights two and three, when the meat is being reheated in something else.
