The original is a fridge recipe: block cream cheese, whipped heavy cream, six hours to set. None of that travels. Instant cheesecake pudding plus whole milk powder gets you the same thickened, tangy body from two dry powders and cold water, and the starch does the setting instead of gelatin and refrigeration.

The crust does not get baked or pressed here, so treat it as crumbs rather than a base. Working the coconut oil into the crumbs at home is what keeps them from turning into dust, and it puts most of the fat and calories in the bag you can eat by the handful if the pudding somehow fails to set.
