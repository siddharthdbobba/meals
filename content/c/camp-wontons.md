This is car-camp food, not pack food. Half an inch of hot oil and forty-eight individual wraps means a table, a chair, and someone else on dish duty. The payoff is that the filling does the hard work at home: chop the onion and pepper in your kitchen, measure the spices into the same bag, and camp becomes browning meat and folding paper.

The refried beans are structural. They bind the beef into a paste that stays put inside the skin instead of leaking into the oil, and they stretch a pound of meat across four dozen wrappers. Keep the filling to two teaspoons a wonton — overfill and the seam splits mid-fry. Wet the last corner properly and press it, or you get the same result.
