Damper is a chemically leavened bread, so nothing has to rise and nothing has to stay alive in your pack. Baking powder does all the lifting the moment liquid hits it, which means the clock starts when you mix and the dough should go on the heat immediately.

The original called for fresh milk, which is a non-starter without a cooler. Powdered whole milk and water gets you the same fat and sugars for browning, and it keeps in the dry mix all trip. The cross cut in the top is not decoration — a six-inch round is thick enough that the middle stays raw unless you open a path for heat.
