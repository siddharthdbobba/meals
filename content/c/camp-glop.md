The original was a skillet dish: fresh onion, a pound of ground beef, a drained can of corn. All three are water and weight, so all three got swapped. Freeze-dried beef and corn rehydrate in the same pot the noodles cook in, and dried onion gives you the savory backbone without carrying a vegetable that bruises.

Cook the beef and corn first, before the noodles. If they go in together the noodles are done while the beef is still leather. The cheese and oil go in off the heat, where they thicken the leftover starch water into sauce instead of splitting into grease.
