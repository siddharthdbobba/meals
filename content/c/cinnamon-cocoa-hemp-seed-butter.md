Hemp hearts hit roughly 10 g of protein per ounce and they are already soft, so they break down into a butter without the long grinding a nut needs. That makes this the rare trail spread you can make in ten minutes and hand to someone with a nut allergy.

The source recipe is a home condiment for a jar in the pantry. For the trail it changes in one way that matters: it ships in a squeeze tube instead of a jar, so there is no utensil and nothing to wash. Coconut oil is what sets it up. It is solid below about 76°F, which keeps the butter from separating in the pack, and liquid in a hot tent, which is why the tube needs headspace.
