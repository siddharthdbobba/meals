This is a car-camping dinner because it needs a hot skillet with real surface area and a cooler that holds pork. The work is front-loaded: slicing, zesting, and mixing two liquids happen at the kitchen counter, so camp is searing and stirring. The marinade doubles as transport — the pork sits in it the whole drive, which is why 30 minutes in a fridge is not part of the plan.

The sear order matters more than the timing. Pork out first keeps it from steaming grey while the sprouts render. The sprouts need direct, undisturbed contact with hot iron to char, and stirring them early gets you boiled sprouts instead. The honey-lemon sauce goes in last and off the pork's fond; add it earlier and the sugar burns before anything is cooked.
