Corn grits do the thickening and the soy crumbles do the protein, which is the whole trade this dish makes. Grits alone are cheap starch and go down like wallpaper paste; the crumbles need a soak but rehydrate in the same water you are already boiling, so they cost nothing in fuel or dishes.

The freeze-dried corn goes in late on purpose. It keeps a little bite instead of dissolving, and that bit of texture is what separates this from porridge. Nutritional yeast stands in for the cheese that a home version would use, and the oil at the end carries the calories that neither the grits nor the corn can.
