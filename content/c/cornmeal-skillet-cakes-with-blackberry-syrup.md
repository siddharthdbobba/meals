This started as a home griddle recipe with fresh milk, a real egg, and fresh blackberries. All three come out on the trail. Milk and egg powder rehydrate into batter that behaves the same, and dried blackberries actually make a better syrup than fresh ones — they release concentrated fruit as they soften instead of watering the pot down.

Cornmeal is the reason this needs a skillet and not a pot. It has no gluten to hold anything together, so the wheat flour and the egg powder do the structural work, and the batter needs a five-minute rest or the grit stays gritty in the middle. Cook them over coals rather than a roaring flame; cornmeal burns before the center sets.
