Scout camps have been making chili mac over fire for decades, and the reason is arithmetic: pasta, meat, and beans in one pot is the cheapest way to move a lot of calories into tired people. The trail version changes almost nothing except the shopping. Canned chili and ground beef become freeze-dried crumbles and bean flakes, and the tomato base becomes powder.

The important part is not draining the pasta. That starchy water is the sauce, and the bean flakes are what turn it from soup into chili. Add them off the heat and a little at a time, because they thicken more than you expect and a scorched pot bottom is a long cleanup with a scrap of grass.
