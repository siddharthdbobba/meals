The original is a Norwegian weeknight plate: steak, baked tomatoes, and a dressing made from cultured quark. Quark does not travel, so the dressing here is powdered whole-milk yogurt mixed with cold water. It comes out thinner than the real thing, which is fine — it is going over hot meat, not holding a shape. Give it five minutes to hydrate before you judge the texture.

Steak means a cooler, so this is a car-camping or basecamp dinner and a night-one dinner at that. The tomatoes are the part worth not skipping. Roasting concentrates them into something acidic enough to cut the fat, and the foil packet keeps that job off your only pan.
