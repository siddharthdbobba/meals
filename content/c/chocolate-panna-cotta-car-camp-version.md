Panna cotta is a set custard, so it needs cold time and a fridge is the one thing camp does not have. A cooler does the job if you make it right after dinner and eat it after the fire dies down. Everything else is stove work you were already doing.

Fresh cream is the part that will not travel, so this uses heavy cream powder rebuilt with water. The fat percentage lands close enough that the gelatin still sets and the chocolate still emulsifies. Chop the chocolate at home — in a dark camp, whole squares sink and refuse to melt evenly. Gelatin is an animal product, which is why this is vegetarian and not vegan.
