No stove means no fuel, no windbreak, and no waiting for a boil. Cold water will soften rolled oats and dried fruit on its own in about half an hour, which happens to be the length of a walking stretch between breaks. You start it and forget it.

Water is the part that takes practice. Too much and you are drinking oatmeal out of a bag; you can always add more, so start short. The fat matters as much as the sugar here. Oil and nuts are what turn a bag of fruit and starch into a breakfast that holds until noon. Honey and oil go in after the soak, not before, since they coat the oats and slow the water down.
