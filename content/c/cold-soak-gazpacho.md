Gazpacho is a soup that was never heated, which makes it one of the few cold home recipes that survives the trip to a backpack intact. Fresh tomatoes and cucumbers are out on weight alone, so tomato powder and dried vegetable flakes do the work. The powder rehydrates into a real base rather than the thin red water you get from bouillon.

The soak time is the actual cooking. Dried pepper and onion need at least an hour in cold water to soften, so mix this at a break and eat it two hours later. Oil and vinegar go in last and stay separate at home, because fat in a dry bag turns the powder into a paste that will not dissolve. The sugar and salt are not garnish either; without them the acid has nothing to sit against.
