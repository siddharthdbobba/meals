This is a one-skillet breakfast that uses the potatoes as the base and everything else as flavor layers. The potatoes go in first and fry until their edges are crispy and brown—this is where most of your cooking time goes, and it matters. Once they're golden, you're just finishing everything else.

Bacon fat is doing the work here. If you use something leaner than bacon, add a bit more oil. The poblano peppers are mild and slightly sweet; if you can't find them, bell peppers work fine but lose the earthiness. The chipotle is the heat and smoke—start with one pepper if you're cautious. Fresh eggs scrambled into the hot hash cook in seconds and bind everything together. The cheese melts into the crevices and adds richness. This is a real breakfast, not a light meal.
