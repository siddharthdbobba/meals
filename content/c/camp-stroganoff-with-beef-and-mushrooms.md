The home version leans on searing fresh steak and finishing with full-fat sour cream, neither of which survives a pack. Freeze-dried beef and dried mushrooms carry the same savory weight at a fraction of the load, and sour cream powder rebuilds the tang and the fat once the heat is off. There is no flour here, same as the original — the noodle starch and the dairy powder do the thickening on their own.

Two details decide whether this works. Rehydrate the beef and mushrooms first, in their own water, or they stay leathery while the noodles turn to paste. And add the sour cream powder off the heat: boiled, it splits and goes grainy. The tomato paste and mustard are small amounts doing large work, giving the sauce a floor of acid that dried ingredients otherwise lack.
