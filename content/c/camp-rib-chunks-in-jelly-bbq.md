Venison ribs have almost no intramuscular fat, and what fat is there tastes like tallow gone wrong. That is why people grill them once and give up. The boil is not optional: it renders out the last of the fat and breaks down connective tissue that dry heat would only tighten. Do that step at home, where you have a big pot and a drain.

Grape jelly is doing two things. It gives the sauce enough sugar to glaze a lean meat that would otherwise stay dull, and pectin thickens it so it clings instead of running off into the fire. The foil packet replaces the slow cooker — the meat is already cooked, so camp is just reheating and setting the glaze.
