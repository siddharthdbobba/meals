This is camp cooking, not backpacking cooking. It wants a skillet, a real fire or a liquid-fuel stove, and enough oil that you will be packing a bottle in and packing the used oil out. The payoff is that fresh-killed small game gets handled the day it is taken, with no cooler and no kitchen.

The two changes from the home version are the buttermilk and the gravy milk. Powdered whole milk soured with lemon juice does what buttermilk does here: it wets the meat so flour sticks and it takes a little edge off older animals. The same powder, mixed thicker, becomes the gravy. Hold back half a cup of flour before you dredge, because flour that has touched raw meat cannot go into the pan sauce.
