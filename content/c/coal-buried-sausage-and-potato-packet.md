This is the campfire foil-packet dinner from every Scout cookbook, with the cooler taken away. Fresh potatoes and raw sausage need refrigeration and twenty-five minutes of real heat. Dehydrated potato slices and a cured summer sausage stick need neither, and the sausage keeps for weeks unopened.

The measured water is the whole trick. Foil traps steam, and the potatoes rehydrate in it rather than sitting in a puddle. Too much and you get soup with nowhere to drain. Coals matter too: flames scorch the bottom seam before the inside ever heats through.
