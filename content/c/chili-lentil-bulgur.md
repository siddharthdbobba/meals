Lentils take time but reward patience. Bulgur swells as it drinks the broth and fills the bowl without demanding attention. Brown sugar quiets the chili just enough—sharp without being punishing.

Butter and walnuts carry the calories. Milk powder is optional; add it if you want creaminess, skip it if you want to save weight. Cornstarch helps thicken a thin broth but is not required. The lentils themselves will thicken everything eventually.
