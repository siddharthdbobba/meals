The mistake here is rushing. Fifteen minutes feels long when you are hungry, but freeze-dried chicken and vegetables genuinely need that time to fully absorb water and soften. Cut it short and you end up with crunchy cores and a grainy texture. The powdered milk and oil thicken the potatoes into something creamy without fresh dairy. Everything is shelf-stable and travels light.

This works best at basecamp where you have time, or when you are already boiling water for something else. The ratio scales linearly if you want to double it for two people.
