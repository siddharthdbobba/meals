This is one pan doing four jobs in sequence, which is the only reason it is worth cooking at a campsite. Toasting the muffins dry first gives the bread a crust that stands up to the egg. Everything after that lands in the fat the meat leaves behind, so there is nothing to wash between stages.

The cheese melts off residual heat under a lid, not over flame. Cheddar left on the burner splits and slides out the back of the sandwich. Cut it from a block rather than carrying slices; the block survives a warm cooler and a long drive, and it grates into the egg if you would rather melt it that way.
