This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Cream cheese and bacon both need a cooler, and there is no shelf-stable version worth eating, so the honest move is to keep it in the cooler and cook it the first night or two.

The filling does two jobs. Fat and sugar cut the capsaicin, which is why the pineapple matters more than it sounds like it should. The bacon wrap is structural as much as anything else: it holds the cheese in while the pepper softens and gives you something to grab with tongs. Coals rather than flame is the whole game here, since bacon fat dripping into a live fire will char the outside long before the pepper gives up.
