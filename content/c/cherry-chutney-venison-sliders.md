This is a freezer-cleanout dish, which is why the cut is left vague. Braising does not care whether the roast came off a shoulder or a hind leg. Five hours in a covered pot over low coals turns connective tissue to gelatin, and lean game meat needs exactly that, since it has almost no fat of its own to keep it moist.

The chutney and slaw are doing the balancing. Game is dense and savory; the cherries and honey push sweet against it and the lime slaw cuts through both. Shelf-stable mayo in a jar or packets replaces anything that would otherwise need real refrigeration, and dried cherries stand in for frozen ones because they rehydrate in the pot anyway. This is car-camping food. The cooler and the long fire are the price of admission.
