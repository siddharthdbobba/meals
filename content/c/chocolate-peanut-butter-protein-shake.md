The original is a blender drink: frozen banana, liquid almond milk, protein powder. None of that travels. Freeze-dried banana crushed to a coarse powder rehydrates in the bottle and gives the same sweetness and body, and powdered plant milk replaces the carton. No blender, no cooler, no stove.

The shaking matters more than it sounds. Protein powder and PB2 both clump on contact with water, so you shake, rest, and shake again. A minute of sitting lets the banana soften and the whole thing thickens on its own.
