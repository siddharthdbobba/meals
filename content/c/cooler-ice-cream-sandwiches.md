This is a camp dessert that depends entirely on cold, so it belongs where a cooler does: car sites, a basecamp, a boat with a hard cooler in the bow. Nothing here is cooked, which means the whole thing lives or dies on how fast you work. A pint pulled straight from the ice cuts into clean discs; a pint that has sat out five minutes smears.

Cutting the carton in rounds instead of scooping is what makes it fast. You get four even slabs the width of a cookie in one pass, and no soft scoop to wrestle onto a cookie one at a time. The hour back in the cooler is not optional — it re-freezes the seam where the cookie meets the ice cream so the sandwich holds together in your hand. Dietary tags are left off on purpose: the ice cream is dairy, the cookies are wheat, and store-bought cookies routinely share equipment with nuts.
