Condensed soup is the entire mechanism here. It is a shelf-stable roux with mushrooms in it, so you get gravy without flour, butter, or a whisk, and it thins itself out on the fat the beef leaves behind. Do not add water to the can.

The original calls for two burners. One works if you cook the rice first, set it aside covered, then use the same pot for the beef. Rice holds heat fine for the ten minutes that takes, and you wash one thing instead of two.
