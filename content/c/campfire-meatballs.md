The mustard and jelly pairing might seem strange on paper, but it's what makes the sauce work. The jelly isn't there to be sweet—it adds body to the liquid and rounds the sharp edges of both the mustard and the ketchup. Simmering for 40 minutes is the whole point. The flavors need time to meld, and you need time to reduce thin sauce into something with substance.

For the trail, this recipe stays lightweight and shelf-stable. Dehydrated beef is your best protein-to-ounce ratio. The long cook time is actually a feature of base camp cooking—you can set it simmering and tend the fire or set up camp while the pot does the work. This is a dinner for when you're staying put.
