The original is a casserole: frozen tots, raw eggs, cheese, an oven, and 45 minutes. None of that travels. Dehydrated hash browns and egg powder do the same two jobs at a fraction of the weight, and both go in dry, so nothing has to stay cold overnight.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Potatoes rehydrate first and get fried before the egg goes in, because egg cooks in under a minute and potatoes that went in wet stay mushy. Cheese powder goes on last off the heat; over direct flame it scorches and turns grainy.
