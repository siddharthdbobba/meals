The original is a fridge dessert built on canned pie filling and tubbed frosting, neither of which survives a trailhead. Powdered milk and powdered sugar do the frosting's job, and dried cherries rehydrated in a tablespoon of water do the pie filling's. The result is drier and less sweet, which is the right direction for something you eat out of a cup with a spoon.

The chocolate is structural, not decorative. As it cools it locks the crumbs together, so the mix goes from wet sand to something that holds a spoon. Soften the chips gently with body heat rather than any real heat, because seized chocolate will never bind and you cannot fix it in camp.
