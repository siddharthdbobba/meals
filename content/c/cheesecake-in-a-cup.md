The original is a chilled dessert that leans on a refrigerator for eight hours and on three separate dairy products for structure. Neither travels. What does travel is the powdered versions: heavy cream powder rebuilds into something that whips, and cream cheese and sour cream powders carry the tang and the fat without a cooler.

The whipping step is not optional. Loose cream is just liquid, and liquid never sets, so the filling stays a puddle over wet crumbs. Shake the cream in a bottle until it thickens, then fold the rest in gently. Without a fridge it will not slice, but it will hold on a spoon, which is all you need.
