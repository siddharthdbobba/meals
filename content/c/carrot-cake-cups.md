The original is an 8x8 sheet cake scooped into plastic cups. Without an oven this becomes a steamed cake, which is what the low flame and the covered ten-minute rest are doing. Steam cooks a small batter through without scorching the bottom, and a backpacking pot on a stove is a bad oven but a decent steamer.

The swaps are all about water and refrigeration. Fresh shredded carrot and drained crushed pineapple are mostly water, so the dried versions plus the 120 ml rehydration step put that water back where it belongs. Cream cheese, butter, and eggs all exist as powders that keep at ambient temperature for months; the frosting comes together with cold water and no fat left out overnight. Oil stays liquid because the batter needs the fat and butter powder alone will not make it tender.
