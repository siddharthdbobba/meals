The original is a fridge salad built on cucumber and cherry tomatoes, neither of which survives a pack. Dried bell pepper and sun-dried tomatoes do the same job: they rehydrate into something with bite, and the sun-dried tomatoes bring more concentrated sweetness than fresh ones did. Cucumber has no shelf-stable equivalent worth carrying, so it is gone.

The soak matters more than the mixing. Beans do not need it, but the dried vegetables do, and adding the oil early coats everything and slows the water down. Dress it after the soak, not before. Lemon juice powder is lighter than a lemon and does not leak, though a real lemon is better if the trip is short.
