The original is a russet baked in an oven for an hour, which is not a thing that happens on trail. Potato flakes get you to the same place in five minutes, and once you accept that, the rest of the toppings translate cleanly: cheese powder for shredded cheddar, jarred bacon bits for bacon, dry ranch mix for the bottled dressing. Nothing here needs a fridge.

Ranch powder is doing more work than it looks like. It is buttermilk solids, herbs, and a lot of salt, so it seasons the potatoes and adds tang that the cheese powder alone does not have. Add the oil at the end rather than the start — fat stirred into hot potato coats better than fat boiled in water.
