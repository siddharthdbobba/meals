This started as a slow cooker recipe, which is three hours of low, even heat and no attention. A dutch oven on coals gets you the same thing faster and less politely. The heat is hotter and comes from one side, so the stirring is not optional. Ten minute intervals, scraping the bottom, or the sugar on the floor of the pot turns bitter before the top layer has done anything.

Two changes for camp. Raw egg white is a cooler ingredient with a short life, so meringue powder and a splash of water do the same job: it is the glue that holds the sugar to the nut, and it dries to that crust. The quarter cup of water at the end matters more than it looks like it should. Without it the coating stays grainy and falls off in the bag.
