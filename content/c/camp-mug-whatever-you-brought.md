There is no dish here to reproduce. The source was a gear roundup from a trade show, and the only edible thing in it was a line about your favorite beverage, so that is what this is: the honest floor of camp cooking, written down.

It still earns a page. Hot water is the most useful thing a stove makes, and the difference between a good mug and a bad one is technique, not ingredients. Dissolve powders in the pot, not the mug. Let the boil settle before it hits tea. And carry a spare packet, because the one night you want it is the night you are out.
