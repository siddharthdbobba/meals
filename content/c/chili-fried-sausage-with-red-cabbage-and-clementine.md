This is a Norwegian Christmas plate, and it survives the move outdoors mostly intact because three of the five ingredients are already preserved. Pickled cabbage keeps in a jar. Sausage is cured or precooked. The only real cooler dependency is the butter, and ghee solves that.

The structure is heat against acid. Browned sausage in chili butter is fat and salt, and the cabbage with citrus cuts straight through it — that is the whole point of the dish, so do not skip the clementine and do not rinse the cabbage. Assemble the salad first and let it sit while the sausage browns; the citrus loosens up and the cabbage stops tasting sharp.
