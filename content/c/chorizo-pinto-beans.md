The original is a dutch-oven dish with fresh peppers, canned tomato paste, and raw chorizo that cooks down into paste. None of that travels. Cured Spanish chorizo does the same job dry: it renders real fat into the pot and carries the smoke and chile on its own. Instant refried bean flakes replace hours of simmering pintos, and they thicken on contact, so the whole thing is one boil.

The sweet-sour-smoke balance is what makes baked beans read as baked beans. Brown sugar and a little molasses on one side, vinegar powder on the other, smoked paprika bridging them. Add the acid off heat or it flattens out. Skip the sugar and you have spiced beans, which is a different and lesser dinner.
