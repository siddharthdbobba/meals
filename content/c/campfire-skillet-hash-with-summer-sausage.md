A hash needs three things: dry potatoes, hot fat, and patience. Dehydrated hash browns get you the first one for free, since you control exactly how much water goes back in. Drain them well. Wet potatoes steam instead of browning, and steamed potatoes taste like nothing.

The home version of this uses fresh potatoes, onion, and breakfast sausage out of a refrigerator. Dried vegetables and hard summer sausage do the same work with no cooler, and the sausage renders enough fat that the olive oil is mostly insurance. Coals matter more than the recipe here. Flames will scorch one side of the pan and leave the rest raw.
