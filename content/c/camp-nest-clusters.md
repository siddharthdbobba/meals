This started as an Easter table decoration and it turns out the structure is exactly right for a pack. There is no water in it, so nothing spoils, and the chocolate is the binder rather than an icing — it sets into a solid puck instead of smearing. Toasted coconut and oats give it enough body to hold a shape after eight hours of getting jostled.

The coconut oil matters more than it looks. Straight chocolate goes brittle and shatters when cold, which is what happens in a pack at dawn. A little added fat keeps the set soft enough to bite without turning greasy. Chill them fully before packing or they will slump into one shared mass.
