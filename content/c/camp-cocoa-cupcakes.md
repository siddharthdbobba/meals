This started as an oven recipe and it survives the move because the batter is small and forgiving. Coals do the work an oven thermostat would, so the coal count matters more than the clock: twelve on the lid and six underneath puts most of the heat above the batter, which is what makes cupcakes rise instead of burn on the bottom.

The two swaps are eggs and butter. Powdered whole eggs rehydrate close enough to the real thing for a batter this rich, and ghee or canned butter sits in a pack all weekend without a cooler. Fresh milk is gone too — powdered milk and cold water only show up if the batter refuses to pour. Mix the icing in a bag and you skip a bowl and a spatula.
