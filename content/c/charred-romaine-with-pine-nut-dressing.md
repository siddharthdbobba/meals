This is a car-camping side, not a pack meal: romaine is mostly water and it lives in the cooler. What makes it worth the space is that heat changes lettuce. The cut face caramelizes, the outer leaves go smoky, and the core stays cold and crisp. You get two textures out of one vegetable.

The dressing is built to survive a jar instead of a food processor. Chopping the pine nuts at home does the work the blade would have done, and the mustard holds the oil and vinegar together while you shake. Toast the nuts at home too. Over a fire they go from pale to burnt in about ten seconds and there is no recovering them.
