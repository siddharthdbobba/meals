Freeze-dried chicken cuts pack weight while keeping protein high—no fresh meat to spoil, no cans to pack out. Everything rehydrates from one boil, making a single pot and no extra stove time.

The apples cut the concentrated, slightly salty taste of rehydrated chicken; the rice provides bulk and carbs. It serves one large appetite comfortably or stretches to two smaller ones without waste.
