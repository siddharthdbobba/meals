The original starts with a butter and flour roux and four cups of fresh milk, neither of which survives a night in a pack. Whole milk powder and instant potato flakes do the same two jobs: fat and body. Potato flakes thicken cold or hot, they never lump the way flour does, and they cost nothing in fuel.

Add the milk powder off the boil. Powdered milk dropped into a rolling pot will catch on the bottom of a thin titanium pot and turn the whole thing scorched. Dried onion and celery need the water hot first, so they go in ahead of everything else and rehydrate while the noodles cook.
