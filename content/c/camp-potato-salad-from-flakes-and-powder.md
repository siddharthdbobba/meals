Potato salad is a cooler dish, and coolers are the thing you do not have. The workaround is to rebuild it from parts that already survive a warm pack: dehydrated potato dices instead of boiled Yukons, egg powder instead of hard-boiled eggs, and single-serve mayo packets, which stay sealed and stable until you tear them open. Diced potatoes matter here. Flakes will absorb everything and give you mashed potatoes with pickles in them.

The brine is doing more than the mayo is. Acid is what keeps a starch-and-fat salad from reading as heavy, and a spoonful of pickle liquid does it without any fresh ingredient. Cool the potatoes before the mayo goes in, and treat this as a same-meal dish. Opened mayo out of a cooler has a short window and it is not worth testing.
