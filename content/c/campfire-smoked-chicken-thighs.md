Smoking is just cooking with the meat parked away from the heat. The coals make hot air and smoke, the smoke does the flavoring, and the meat cooks slowly enough that the fat and collagen have time to break down. That is why thighs work here and breasts do not: dark meat forgives a fire you cannot dial in.

The two things that go wrong are heat and flame. Coals directly under the meat turn this into grilling, and dry wood chunks catch fire instead of smoldering, which coats everything in bitter soot. Soak the wood, keep the coals to one side, and cover the meat so the smoke has somewhere to sit.
