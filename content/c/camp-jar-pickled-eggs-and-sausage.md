This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Eggs and sausage in vinegar need to stay cold and they need time, so the whole thing gets built at home and hauled out whole. Give it three days minimum before the first jar opening. The brine has not reached the yolks before that.

Grilling the sausage before it goes in the jar is the step worth keeping. Char adds smoke that survives the vinegar, and rendering some fat out first keeps the brine from going greasy. The sugar is not there to make it sweet, it just takes the edge off four cups of vinegar. Keep everything under the liquid line and the jar holds for weeks in a cold cooler.
