One pot, mostly canned goods and cooler ingredients. Brown the chicken and peppers, add the cans, and let it simmer while you set up camp. Most of the flavor work is done by the peppers and spices, not technique.

The smokiness comes from two sources: chipotle peppers in the sauce and smoked paprika in the spice mix. They add depth that reads as hours of simmering. Twenty minutes over heat is enough for everything to meld.
