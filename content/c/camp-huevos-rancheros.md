This is a cooler breakfast, not a trail one. Eggs and a 28 oz can of tomatoes are heavy and neither one packs down, so it belongs at a car campsite or a basecamp where weight is somebody else's problem.

The method is worth knowing anyway. Poaching eggs directly in the tomato means no second pan and no water to boil, and the sauce insulates the eggs so they set evenly over an uneven fire. Fry the tortillas first while the oil is clean, then pour most of it off — cooking the onion in three tablespoons instead of an inch and a half is the difference between breakfast and a grease slick. The used oil cools in a can and goes home with you.
