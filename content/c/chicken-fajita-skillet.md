This is a home fajita pan moved onto a grate, and almost nothing had to change. Chicken strips, peppers, and onion all cook in the same time window over live fire, and the pan does the whole job, so cleanup is one skillet and a spatula.

The spice blend is the part worth doing at home. Eight jars is eight chances to spill something in the dirt, and premixed spice keeps for months in a film canister. The lime at the end is not garnish; the acid cuts through the char and wakes the whole pan back up. Skip it and the fajitas taste flat and dusty.
