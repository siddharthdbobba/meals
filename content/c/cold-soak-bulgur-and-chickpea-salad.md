The original is a chopped salad that lives on fresh herbs, cucumber, and feta. None of that survives a pack, so the structure stays and the perishables get swapped: dried herbs, dried cucumber, and no cheese at all. Bulgur is the reason it works on trail. It is parboiled at the mill, so cold water alone is enough to soften it, which is not true of rice or most grains.

Acid and fat are doing the rest. Citric acid replaces the lemon and weighs nothing, and the oil is where most of the calories come from. Add the pistachios at the end rather than at the soak, or you get two hours of soft nuts and no texture left in the bowl.
