Blending is the step people skip and then regret. Whole dried tomato pieces will not rehydrate in ten minutes of off-heat soaking, so you end up chewing tomato leather in thin broth. Ground to a powder, they thicken the water instead, and the tomato powder pushes it the rest of the way to something with body.

The milk powder and butter powder are doing the creamy part without a cooler. Both rehydrate cold, so a boil is enough and you never simmer this. If you cook it in a pot instead of a bag, bring it to a boil, kill the heat, and cover it for five minutes; the shorter soak works because the pot holds heat and the bag does not.
