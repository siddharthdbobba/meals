This is a fat delivery system with enough structure to eat off a tortilla or a cracker without dripping. Peanut butter alone is already dense, but the bacon grease pushes the fat fraction higher and the honey keeps it from tasting flat. Milk powder is the part doing the engineering: it soaks up free oil and turns a loose mix into something that holds shape in a warm pack.

The texture is entirely up to how much powder goes in, so add it slowly and stop where you like it. Two cups gives a thick spread; four gives a dense block you can slice. Nothing here needs refrigeration, but strain the grease well and keep the container closed, since browned bacon solids are the one ingredient that will turn on you.
