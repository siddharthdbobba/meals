The original is an oven recipe: a baked almond shortbread crust, then a cream cheese and egg custard baked a second time. Neither step travels. Instead the crust is pressed rather than baked, using graham crumbs that are already cooked, and the filling is instant cheesecake pudding rebuilt with milk powder and powdered yogurt. No egg, no fridge, no second bake.

The pudding starch is what makes this hold a cut edge. Use cold water, not hot, or the mix seizes into lumps before it can set. The stove is only doing two small jobs here, melting butter and warming a bag of chocolate, which is why the whole thing works over a campfire just as well as a burner.
