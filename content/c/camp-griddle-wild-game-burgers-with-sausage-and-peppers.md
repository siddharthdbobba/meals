Wild game is lean enough that a straight burger goes dry and crumbly before it goes done. Two things fix that here. The patties get cooked to medium and no further, and the sausage-pepper-onion mix on top brings back the fat and moisture the meat does not have. The smoked sausage is doing most of that work; a plain grilled onion would not.

This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Nothing in it is shelf-stable and the meat has to stay cold, so it belongs at a trailhead camp or a hunting camp with a truck nearby. Frozen patties buy you a day or two of cooler life and keep everything else around them cold in the meantime.
