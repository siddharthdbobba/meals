The original is a stovetop soup built on ground beef, diced potatoes, and a cheese sauce. Both of those go dry without much loss: potato flakes thicken faster than cubed potatoes ever did, and cheddar powder does the work of grated cheddar without a cooler. The mirepoix stays, just dehydrated, and it needs the full simmer — carrot and celery rehydrate slower than everything else in the pot.

Ketchup and mustard are what make this read as a burger instead of generic beef soup. They are one packet each and weigh nothing, so do not leave them home. Same for the pickles: the acid cuts the fat, and without it the whole pot goes flat by the third spoonful.
