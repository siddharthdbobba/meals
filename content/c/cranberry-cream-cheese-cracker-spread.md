The original is a fridge recipe: a block of cream cheese, thawed juice concentrate, and a bowl. Neither of those travels. Cream cheese powder rehydrates into the same thing at about a fifth of the carried weight and does not care how warm your pack gets, and orange drink powder covers the acid and sweetness the concentrate was there for.

Water control is the whole job. Add it slowly, because the powder keeps drinking for a minute after you stop stirring, and a spread that is too loose slides off the cracker. The cranberries get soaked first so they pull their water from the bag instead of from the finished spread.
