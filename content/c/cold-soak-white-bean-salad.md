The original is a fridge salad: tinned beans, cut tomatoes, cucumber, a whisked vinaigrette. None of that survives a pack. Dehydrated beans and vegetables do the same job and weigh a quarter as much, and the soak time is dead time you spend walking anyway.

The dressing goes in last on purpose. Oil coats the beans and slows how fast they take on water, so a jar dressed at the trailhead is still chalky at lunch. Lemon powder and mustard carry the acid; the honey is there to keep the acid from dominating, not to make it sweet.
