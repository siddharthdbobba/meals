The original is a stovetop roux with fresh celery, fresh milk, and stock — three things that do not ride in a pack. Dehydrated celery, milk powder, and bouillon cover all three, and the flour behaves the same whether the fat is butter or ghee.

Order matters more than technique here. Celery rehydrates in plain water; once flour is in the pot the liquid thickens and the celery stops drinking. Boil the celery first, then build the sauce around it.
