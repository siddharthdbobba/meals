Wild turkey breast has almost no fat, which is why the source method browns it in oil and then simmers it in seasoned water instead of just frying it through. That short simmer is the whole recipe. It puts moisture back into meat that would otherwise cook up like sawdust, and the starch in the seasoning packet thickens the pan liquid into something that clings.

This is a car camping meal, not a pack meal. The oven step for crisping shells is gone; a skillet already hot from the meat blisters tortillas in under a minute and you keep one pan. Cabbage replaces lettuce because lettuce turns to slime on day two in a cooler, and everything cold goes on after the pan is off the heat.
