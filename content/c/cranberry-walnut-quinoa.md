Quinoa is a good trail grain because it cooks in twenty minutes and most of that is off the stove. Five minutes of boil and fifteen minutes sitting under a lid gets you the same result as simmering the whole time, on a fraction of the fuel. The 1:2 grain-to-water ratio is the one number worth holding to; everything after it is by feel.

The original called for a bunch of fresh scallions, which is a fridge item. Dried chives carry the same sharp note and weigh nothing. Oil and vinegar are the real reason this works cold the next day: the fat keeps the grains from clumping into a brick, and the acid keeps it from tasting flat once it cools.
