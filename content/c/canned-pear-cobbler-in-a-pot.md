The original bakes at 350 for 50 minutes, which nobody has in camp. A covered pot over a very low flame does the same job in half the time, because the pot is smaller and the batter is shallower. The lid traps steam and cooks the top; the flame cooks the bottom. Low heat is the whole trick. Anything above a simmer scorches the sugar before the middle sets.

The canned syrup is the liquid, so you carry no extra water and waste nothing. Oats give the batter enough body to hold the syrup instead of letting it pool. Butter is out with no cooler, so coconut oil or shortening does the fat work; both are stable in a jar for weeks. Skip olive oil, it fights the fruit.

Canned fruit is heavy, which is why this is a car-camping and basecamp recipe. Eight servings out of two cans is the payoff.
