Bagels are a lot of standing around for one dutch oven of bread, which is exactly why they suit a basecamp with a fire that has to burn down anyway. The dough rises while you do something else. The only real timing pressure is the twenty minutes at the end.

The molasses bath is not decoration. Boiling the dough sets the crust before it bakes, which is the difference between a bagel and a round roll. Keep the dip short; leave them in longer and the outside turns to leather. Kneading in a freezer bag replaces the mixer and the greased bowl and means one less thing to wash in cold water.
