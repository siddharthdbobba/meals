This started as a scout dutch oven recipe built on raw chicken breasts, which means a cooler and 45 minutes of worrying about whether the middle is done. Canned chicken removes both problems. It is already cooked and already shelf-stable, so the long cook is doing one job only: boiling the cola, ketchup, and barbecue sauce down into something thick enough to cling.

The cola is not a gimmick. Twelve ounces of sugar syrup plus the acid is what turns two cups of thin sauce into a glaze. Diet cola has no sugar to reduce and will leave you with soup. Stir often — sugar on the bottom of a hot cast iron pot goes from brown to burnt fast, and once it burns the whole pot tastes like it.
