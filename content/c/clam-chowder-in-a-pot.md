Chowder is normally thickened with a butter-and-flour roux and finished with cream, neither of which travels. Instant potato flakes do the same job in one step: they thicken and they add body, and they go in off the heat so nothing scorches on the bottom of the pot. Whole milk powder replaces the cream and carries the fat that makes it read as chowder instead of salty water.

The canned clams are the only heavy thing here, and they are worth the weight. Pour the packing juice in with them; that liquid is where the shellfish flavor actually lives. Add the flakes slowly. Too many at once and you get a paste that no amount of stirring will loosen.
