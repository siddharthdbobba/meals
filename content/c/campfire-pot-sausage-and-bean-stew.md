This is built around a wide, deep pot on a grate over coals, which is a slow and uneven heat source. Everything here tolerates that. The sausage is already cooked and cured, so it only needs to render and brown. The beans are already soft, so overcooking them just makes the stew thicker.

The canning liquid is the reason there is no flour or roux. Simmered down, it turns starchy and coats the sausage fat into something closer to a gravy. Skip the paprika and the tomato paste and you have beans in hot water; those two are carrying the whole flavor.
