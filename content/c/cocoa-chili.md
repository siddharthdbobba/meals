Cocoa and chili powder come from the same shelf of flavor, which is why this works. Unsweetened cocoa adds bitterness and body without sugar, and the chocolate at the end brings fat that carries the chile heat instead of letting it sit sharp on your tongue. Add the chocolate off the heat or it will seize and go grainy.

The original called for queso fresco, crema, and avocado on top. Those are cooler food and this recipe does not need them, so they are gone. Vegetable broth became bouillon in the chile soaking water, which saves you carrying a carton and puts the chile flavor back in the pot. Everything else is cans and dry spice, so the only perishable is the onion.
