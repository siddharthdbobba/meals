The original is a slow cooker recipe, which is fine at home and useless at a campsite. This version keeps the parts that matter — the browned crust, the mushroom gravy, the ketchup and mustard doing the sweet-and-sharp work — and compresses five hours into thirty minutes in one skillet. A covered simmer finishes the patties the same way the slow cooker did, just faster.

The binder is where the trail substitutions happen. Egg yolks and fresh milk are cooler liabilities, so the panko gets soaked in reconstituted milk powder instead and holds the patties together on its own. Dried mushrooms replace 16 oz of fresh, and their soaking liquid becomes most of the gravy, so nothing is wasted. Venison itself is the one item that needs a cold cooler; freeze it solid and cook it the first night out.
