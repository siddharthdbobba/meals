This started as a bulk feed recipe: meat scraps boiled soft, salted hard, then thickened with cornmeal until it set into a mush. The logic scales down to one pot on a stove. Grain plus fat plus whatever protein you have is a complete dinner, and cornmeal is cheap, dense, and cooks in water alone.

The trail changes are about weight and safety. Fresh scraps and eggs become dried beef and egg powder, and the greens are dehydrated instead of pulled from a produce bin. The egg goes in off the heat because it is doing two jobs, protein and binding, and boiled egg powder curdles into grit. The oil at the end is not optional: without it this is a bowl of starch.
