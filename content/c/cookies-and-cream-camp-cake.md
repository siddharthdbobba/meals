The original of this is a two-layer birthday cake with a raw-egg-white buttercream. Neither the layers nor the raw eggs belong in camp, so this is one Dutch oven, powdered egg whites, and a shortening glaze that does not care about temperature. Shortening holds its shape at 85 degrees in a way butter never will.

Coal management is the whole recipe. Cake mix is forgiving about mixing and unforgiving about heat, so keep most of the coals on the lid and rotate often. A ring of coals directly under the center is how you get a burnt disc with raw batter on top of it.
