This started as cornstick pan cornbread, baked at 450 in a preheated cast iron mold. No oven on a trip, so it becomes griddle cakes: same batter, same ratios, cooked flat in a skillet. Self-rising cornmeal already has the leavening and the acid from the buttermilk activates it, so the cakes rise on the pan instead of in an oven.

The fresh stuff is the part that had to change. Buttermilk, sour cream, and egg all come as powders and rehydrate fine if you give the batter five minutes to sit. Keep the batter thick. Thin batter spreads and burns before the middle sets, and the cheese is what browns the outside, so a hot pan and a slow flip matter more than the timer.
