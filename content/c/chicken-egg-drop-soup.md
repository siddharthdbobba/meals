The freeze-dried eggs do the real work here. They don't just add protein—when you stir them in off-heat, they scramble into soft curds and thicken the broth instead of leaving you with weak soup floating in water. The instant soup handles the salt and flavor; the chicken pouch handles the weight that makes this feel like dinner instead of a cup of broth.

This is ten minutes from start to eating on a winter night. Everything is shelf-stable and light. No fresh ingredients, no guessing on rehydration, and only one pot to wipe clean. That is why you carry it.
