This is lunch when you do not want to stop long enough for a stove. Crackers, something fatty on top, eat, walk. The whole thing lives or dies on which cracker you buy: knäckebröd and hardtack survive a week of being sat on, and thin butter crackers turn to dust by day two.

The soft cheese matters more than it looks. It glues the toppings down, adds fat the crackers do not have, and keeps the whole thing from being dry crumbs and salt. Shelf-stable spreadable cheese and cured sausage both hold at ambient temperature, which is the only reason this works without a cooler. What breaks in the bag is not waste — cracker dust thickens a thin soup better than most things you would carry on purpose.
