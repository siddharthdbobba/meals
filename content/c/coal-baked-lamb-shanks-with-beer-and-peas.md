The beer does two jobs. It is the braising liquid, so you carry no extra water, and the sugars in a malty beer cook down into something that coats the meat. A hoppy beer goes bitter over three hours in coals. Pick accordingly.

Dried peas and carrots rehydrate in the pot instead of taking up cooler space, and they thicken the liquid as they go. This one is not backpacking food despite what the original claimed: lamb shanks are heavy, bone-in, and need a cooler. Cook it where you park.
