The original wants heavy cream, which has no business in a cooler for three days. Evaporated milk does the same job: it is concentrated, canned, and stable until you open it, and the extra milk protein keeps the sauce from splitting when the cheese goes in.

The part that decides whether this works is heat control. Cheese breaks when it boils, so the pan comes off the fire before any cheese touches it. Residual heat in cast iron is enough to melt two cups of shredded cheddar and mozzarella, and it fades slowly enough that the dip stays dippable while people work through the pretzels.
