The original is boiled potatoes, sour cream, and shredded cheddar, all of which need a fridge and fifteen minutes of stove time. Dehydrated potato dices solve both. They rehydrate in cold water while you do something else, and they hold their shape, which is the whole point of a potato salad. Flakes would turn this into mash.

The two powders go in before the wet dressing on purpose. Cheese powder hitting straight mayo clumps and stays clumpy. Hitting damp potatoes, it dissolves into a coating. The bacon stays in its own bag until the last minute for the same reason it does at home: once it gets wet it is just salt.
