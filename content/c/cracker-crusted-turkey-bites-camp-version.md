Pickle juice is a brine that already has salt, acid, and dill in it, so the turkey comes out seasoned through rather than seasoned on the surface. Wild turkey breast is lean and dries out fast; the brine buys you a margin of a minute or two before that happens. Cheese crackers crush into a crumb that browns quicker than breadcrumbs, which matters when you are cooking small cubes and want the inside done at the same moment the outside is.

This is a car camp recipe and it stays one. The oil volume and the cooler space rule out anything you carry on your back. The only real change from a kitchen version is the sour cream, which becomes powder plus water so nothing has to survive on ice for three days. Frying over a fire means unstable heat, so run smaller batches and let the oil come back up between them.
