The original is a late-summer soup built on fresh cobs, and the fresh corn is the whole point. Freeze-dried corn is the only honest substitute on trail: it rehydrates sweet and stays in whole kernels, which is more than can be said for canned or dehydrated corn.

The home version gets its body from pureeing a few cups of soup and stirring them back in. No blender in camp, so potato flakes do the job. Add them with the rest and they dissolve into the broth over the simmer. The dill and ginger go in dry from the start because dried herbs need heat and time to give up anything at all.
