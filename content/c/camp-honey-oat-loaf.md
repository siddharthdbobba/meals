This is a home bread moved outside with one change that matters: the egg wash is gone. Egg is the only refrigerated thing in the original and it does nothing structural, just gloss. Without it the crust comes out matte and the loaf is the same. Everything else already travels dry.

The timing is what makes this a basecamp recipe rather than a backpacking one. Two rises and 50 minutes of coals is a whole afternoon, so mix it early and let it work while you do something else. Coal count is the real control here; too many underneath and the bottom carbonizes long before the middle sets.
