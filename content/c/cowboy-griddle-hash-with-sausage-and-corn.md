Camp griddles have hot spots, and this recipe uses them instead of fighting them. Each ingredient gets its own patch of metal, browns properly, and then slides to a cooler zone to wait. Crowding everything into the center at once is what turns a hash into a wet pile.

Frozen hash browns do the work of peeling and cubing potatoes at a picnic table, and they go into the cooler frozen, which buys you a few hours of ice. The sausage is fully cooked before it leaves home, so all it needs is a crust on two sides. The original was served with garlic toast; that is left off here so the meal stays gluten-free and one-surface, but a skillet-toasted bun catches the oil at the bottom of the pan if you want it.
