This is a home dessert dragged out to a fire pit. The original bakes on a sheet pan at 350; a dutch oven with coals top and bottom hits close enough, and the coal ratio is the only real technique here. Cream cheese in the dough is not decoration. It keeps the crust soft after it cools, which matters when you are eating it an hour after it comes off the fire instead of warm from an oven.

Everything perishable stays perishable, so this is a cooler recipe and a day-one-or-two recipe. Butter and cream cheese need to be soft to mix and cold enough not to have separated, which is a narrow window on a hot afternoon. Pull them out twenty minutes before you start. The lemon juice in the frosting cuts the sugar and keeps it from tasting like straight fat.
