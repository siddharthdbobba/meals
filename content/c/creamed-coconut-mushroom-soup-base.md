Grated creamed coconut is doing the job powdered milk usually does. It is solid coconut flesh, not a milk powder, so it carries the fat that makes the soup read as cream and it stays shelf-stable without a cooler. The potato starch is the body: it swells as the water heats and holds everything in suspension instead of letting the coconut oil float off on its own.

Start the mix in cold water rather than dumping it into a rolling boil. Starch dropped into boiling water seizes into lumps that never break up, and a hard boil after thickening breaks the gel back down. Low flame, constant stirring, off the heat early. The dried shiitake rehydrates in the same few minutes and is the only real mushroom flavor here, so crumble it small enough to soften.
