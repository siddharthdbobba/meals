Yogurt is one of the few high-protein breakfasts that costs almost nothing per serving, and buying it as powder in bulk instead of in cups is what makes it work on trail. Rehydrated yogurt powder is thinner and tangier than the tub version, so the oats and milk powder are here to give it body. The oats soften in cold water on their own; the yogurt powder just makes them taste like something other than wet oats.

This is assembled the night before and eaten cold, which means no stove, no fuel, and no waiting around in the morning. The sugar and salt matter more than they look — without them the tang reads as sour rather than as yogurt. Freeze-dried berries hold their texture better than raisins do, but they cost more and crush in a pack, so pick based on what your food bag is already carrying.
