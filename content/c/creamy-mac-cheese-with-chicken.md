Freeze-dried dairy powder replaces fresh butter and milk, so you can leave the cooler at home. Cooking in minimal water lets the pasta hydrate with its own starch instead of boiling in excess—saves weight, saves fuel.

Powdered dairy needs stirring to blend properly, not just sitting. Freeze-dried cheese melts in at the end, and the chicken pouch adds protein without any refrigeration. The proportions are tight—full simmer time is non-optional.
