This is the overnighter meal, not the trip-of-a-lifetime meal. Everything comes out of a can already cooked, so the only real job is getting it hot. That matters more than it sounds: after a long drive and a tent setup in the dark, a three-course plan is a way to be eating at ten o'clock.

The potatoes are the part worth the extra four minutes. Browning them in oil first gives the pot some texture instead of a uniform wet stew, and they carry the curry sauce. It is heavy food to carry, so this belongs in a vehicle, a boat, or a base camp you drove to. The same cans stored permanently in the truck double as the emergency box if a night out turns into three.
