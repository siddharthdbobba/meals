This is a condiment, not a meal, and it earns its weight by making dry trail food edible. Fried potatoes over a fire, rehydrated chicken, tortilla-wrapped anything: the honey and barbecue sauce do the sweetness, the mustard and lemon cut the fat of the mayo. Ratios matter more than technique.

The trail adaptation is packet mayonnaise instead of a jar. Single-serve packets are pasteurized and sealed, so they ride in a pack without a cooler, and mixing at camp means nothing spoils on the way in. Once combined it behaves like any opened mayo, so make what you will eat that day rather than carrying a jar of it around.
