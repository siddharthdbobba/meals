This is a candy bar you build in your own kitchen, and the reason it travels is fat. Chocolate and peanut butter set into a solid binder at room temperature, so the noodles stay locked in place instead of rattling loose in the bag. No baking, no water, no stove.

Chow mein noodles are already fried, which is why they hold their crunch under a chocolate coating. Dry ramen goes soft and chalky. The mini eggs are decoration and sugar; leave them out and you have the same snack, just plainer. The whole batch is made at home and carried ready to eat, which is the point.
