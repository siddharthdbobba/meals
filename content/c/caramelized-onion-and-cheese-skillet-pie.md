Caramelizing onions is the whole recipe and it takes about 40 minutes, not the 15 that most instructions claim. Low heat, frequent stirring, and yellow onions. Sweet varieties like Vidalia carry too much water and sugar in the wrong balance; they slump into sweetness without ever browning.

The original is a baked pie, which is not happening on a camp stove. Crushed crackers pressed into the pan give you the same salty base without pastry, egg and milk powders replace the fresh custard, and a lid over low heat sets it the way an oven would. The cheese is structural here — it holds the slices together when you cut them.
