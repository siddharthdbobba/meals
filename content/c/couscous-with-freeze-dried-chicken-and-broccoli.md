Couscous rehydrates in minutes without cooking, which matters when you're carrying minimal fuel. Freeze-dried chicken and broccoli come back to life in the same time, giving you actual protein and vegetables in shelf-stable form rather than just grains.

The dry milk and broth powder are doing real work: they carry fat into the grain and build seasoning that makes the dish taste intentional rather than assembled. Freeze-dried ingredients need a good seal if you're storing them past a few days, so a vacuum sealer is worth owning if you cook like this regularly.
