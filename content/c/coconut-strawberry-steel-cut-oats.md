Slow cookers and steel cut oats go together because steel cut grain needs an hour it will never get on a stove that is burning fuel by the minute. Quick-cook steel cut oats are the fix. They are pre-steamed, so they hydrate in about ten minutes and still keep the chew that makes steel cut worth carrying over rolled oats.

Canned coconut milk becomes coconut milk powder, butter becomes ghee, fresh strawberries become freeze-dried, and the condensed milk drizzle becomes milk powder mixed thick. The fat is not optional. Coconut milk powder is where most of the calories live, and the spoon of ghee at the end is what keeps this from tasting like thin porridge. Keep the flame low and stir; steel cut oats will weld to a titanium pot given the chance.
