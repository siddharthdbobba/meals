The home version leans on refrigerated cream cheese, Greek yogurt, and aerosol whipped topping, none of which survive a pack. Instant cheesecake pudding mix plus whole milk powder does the same job dry: the mix carries the modified starch that sets the filling, and the milk powder brings back the fat and the tang the pudding expects. Cold water is enough to set it, which means no stove and no fuel spent on dessert.

The crust becomes a topping instead of a base. There is no way to keep a pressed graham crust intact in a pack, so the crackers ride separate and go on last, where they stay crisp for the few minutes it takes to eat. Dried apples need a head start in water or they stay leathery; the caramel bits soften alongside them and thin into something close to a sauce.
