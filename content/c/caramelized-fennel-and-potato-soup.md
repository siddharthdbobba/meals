Fennel tastes like licorice until you cook the sugar out of it. An hour on low heat over a thick-bottomed pot turns it sweet and dark, and nobody at the table names the vegetable. That hour is the whole recipe, and it happens in your kitchen, not at a windy campsite.

The original leans on boiled potatoes and an immersion blender for creaminess. Neither travels. Instant potato flakes do the same job in one minute and carry dry, so the trail version is a single boil and a stir. No milk or cream is needed here, and none was needed in the kitchen either.
