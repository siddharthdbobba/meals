The spice bag is the whole reason this works in camp. Nine small containers of seasoning is nine chances to forget one, so they get combined at the kitchen table and arrive as a single pour.

Tomato paste rather than sauce is deliberate. It ships in a small can, needs no refrigeration until opened, and the one cup of water you add back is water you control — so the sauce thickens on schedule instead of boiling down for an hour over a fire you would rather be eating next to. The 30-minute simmer is not optional; that is where the dried onion rehydrates and the chili powder stops tasting raw.

This one stays car-camping. Hot dogs, ground beef, and cheese all need a cooler, and there is no honest way to backpack it.
