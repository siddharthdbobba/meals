Fresh berries are a cooler-only food. They bruise in a pack, they mold in two days, and they are mostly water you are already carrying. Freeze-dried berries hold the same flavor at a fraction of the weight, and cold water puts the texture back in about twenty minutes.

The lime powder and salt do the real work. Freeze-dried fruit rehydrates sweet and flat, and acid plus salt is what makes it read as fruit instead of candy. Dried mint is weaker than fresh, so it goes in at the start with the water rather than at the end.
