This is not a real rum cake—it's a workaround using instant mixes and foil to approximate one over a campfire. The pudding mix provides richness without fresh dairy, and the pecans anchor the structure when the batter would otherwise just spread and bake unevenly. The rum is there for flavor, not hydration, so you don't need to carry much.

Foil-packet cooking over fire is approximate. The bottom can scorch, the top can stay gummy, and the middle is sometimes both at once. Rotate often and accept inconsistency. It still tastes like rum cake and it's still better than the alternatives.
