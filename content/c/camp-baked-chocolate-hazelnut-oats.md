Baked oats work because the oats, egg, and baking powder behave like a batter instead of a porridge. On a stove you get the same result with a lid and a low flame: the trapped steam does what an oven does, and the whole thing sets rather than staying loose. The fresh ingredients are the only real problem, so the milk and egg become powders and the banana becomes crushed banana chips, which rehydrate in the batter and bring the sugar with them.

Low heat is the entire technique. Anything above a simmer scorches the bottom before the middle sets, and there is nothing to stir it back into. The chocolate buried in the center melts into a seam; the chocolate and hazelnuts on top stay separate, which is the point.
