Venison is lean enough that a thick steak goes tough before the crust browns. Pounding it thin fixes both problems at once: the meat cooks through in the same few minutes the breading needs, and the fibers are already broken up, so an older animal still cuts with a fork. This is the whole trick, and it is worth the noise.

The camp changes are in the wet ingredients. Fresh eggs and milk do not belong in a pack, so the wash is powdered egg rehydrated in a cup and the gravy is built on powdered whole milk stirred into hot water. Both behave the same in the pan. The gravy uses the oil already in the skillet, which means one pan for the whole meal and one pan to scrape out afterward.
