Couscous does not need to boil. It only needs to sit in hot water with the lid on, which is why this dinner burns almost no fuel. The vegetables are the part that actually needs heat, so they go in first and get the whole boil to themselves while the couscous waits in its bag.

Spices carry the meal, because dried leek and bouillon on their own taste like salt and not much else. The olive oil is the other half of the argument: it is the densest calorie you can carry per ounce, and it keeps the grain from packing into a dry brick. Keep the couscous separate from everything else at home so you can time it right in camp.
