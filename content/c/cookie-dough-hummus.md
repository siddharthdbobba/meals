This is a home-blender job, not a camp one. The beans are the whole structure: cooked chickpeas puree into something dense and dough-like, and the cashews and oats absorb the maple syrup so it thickens instead of running. Blend it long enough and nobody guesses what is in it.

It is not shelf-stable. Canned beans with no acid and no preservative belong in a cooler, which makes this a car-camping and paddle-trip food rather than a pack food. Five days is the outside limit. Chocolate chips go in at camp because they soften and bleed if they sit in the jar.
