Grits are one of the few real breakfast starches that cook well over a fire. Stone ground takes the full 25 minutes, which is the point: instant grits go slack and the coarse grind holds its texture under a wet topping. Water alone makes them thin, so the half and half, butter, and cheddar are structural, not garnish. They also carry the fat that makes this hold you until lunch.

The fish is the part that pins this to a cooler. Fresh fillets fried in seasoned cornmeal are worth the setup at a car camp or a paddle put-in where you caught them that morning. Away from a cooler, swap in a foil pouch of tuna and stir it straight into the finished grits; you lose the crust and keep the meal.
