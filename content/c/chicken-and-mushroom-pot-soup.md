The original is a stovetop soup built on a butter roux, half and half, and a cutting board's worth of fresh vegetables. None of that travels. The fat comes from olive oil instead of butter, the cream from whole milk powder, and the mirepoix from dried flakes that rehydrate in the same water everything else cooks in.

The roux is the part that needed rethinking. You cannot brown flour in a pot you also want to boil in without scorching it, so the flour goes in as a cold slurry with the milk powder at the end. Cold water first is not optional here: flour dropped into boiling stock seizes into lumps you will be chasing with a spoon for ten minutes. Eight minutes of simmer before that is what gets the dried mushrooms from leathery to actually edible.
