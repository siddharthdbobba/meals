Wok sauce is four ingredients and a minute of stirring, which is why buying it in a jar makes little sense on the trail. The trail version swaps the diluted stock for bouillon powder and water carried separately, so nothing wet rides in your pack except the soy sauce.

Cornstarch is the part people skip and then wonder why the sauce runs to the bottom of the pot. Half a teaspoon is enough to make it cling to noodles. Slurry it in cold water before it meets heat, or you get lumps that never dissolve.
