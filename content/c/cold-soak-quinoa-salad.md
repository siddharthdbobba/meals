The original is a fridge salad built on fresh cucumber, tomato, and feta. None of that travels. What survives the swap is the structure: a neutral grain, something acid, something fatty, and enough dried vegetable to give it texture. Quinoa is the reason this works cold at all, but only if you cook it at home first and dry it. Raw quinoa will sit in cold water all afternoon and stay gravel.

The dressing does the heavy lifting. Powdered lemon and mustard rehydrate into something sharp enough to cut a grain that has been sitting in a jar, and the oil is most of the calories. Feta is gone because soft cheese and a warm pack are a bad pair; the salt and the seeds cover for it. Honey became plain sugar, which does the same job in the dressing and keeps the whole thing vegan.
