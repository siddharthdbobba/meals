Oven fries do not exist on the trail, so this trades crisp edges for a crust. Cornstarch is why it works at all: dusted onto rehydrated potatoes, it dries the surface and browns hard against the pot in a way plain potato will not. The trick is draining thoroughly first, then leaving the potatoes alone between stirs.

The fresh parts of the original are all swapped out. Whole potatoes become dehydrated dices, shredded cheddar becomes freeze-dried cheese that melts fine with residual heat, and the Worcestershire is a powder or a few drops of soy sauce. Skip the oil and you lose both the calories and the browning.
