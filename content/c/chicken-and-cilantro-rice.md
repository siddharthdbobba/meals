The source of this was a grocery-budget dinner built around a whole chicken breast, two dollars of rice, and an onion. The economics survive the trip to the backcountry; the fresh ingredients do not. Freeze-dried chicken replaces raw breast, dried minced onion replaces the fresh one, and freeze-dried cilantro goes in at the end where its flavor is not boiled off.

The order matters. Chicken needs a few minutes in hot water before it stops being styrofoam, and rice added too early soaks up everything the meat wanted. Simmer the chicken first, then dump the rice and treat the pot as a cozy. The oil is not optional — rice and lean poultry give you almost no fat, and the calorie count falls apart without it.
