Winter backcountry trips already have a stove going for dinner. This repurposes that fire into calories and hydration at once. The no-bake cheesecake mix is shelf-stable and dissolves instantly in hot liquid. Bringing actual cream cheese would be impossible.

Adjust the consistency at camp to your preference: thin it with more water if you want to drink it, keep it thick if you want it to feel substantial. The milk powder adds the fat that makes this actually satisfying rather than just sweet and watery.
