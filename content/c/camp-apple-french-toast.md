The original wanted fresh eggs, fresh milk, and a real apple. None of that keeps past the first warm afternoon, so this version runs on powdered egg, powdered milk, and dried apple slices that get soaked back to life in cold water while the stove warms up. The custard comes out about the same. Nothing here needs a cooler.

Two things decide whether this works. The bread has to be stale and thick, because fresh soft bread turns to mush the second it hits liquid. And the pan has to stay at medium, since the sugar in the mix will scorch well before the egg has set. Dip fast, flip once, keep moving.
