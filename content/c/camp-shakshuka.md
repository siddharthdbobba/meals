Real shakshuka is 750 grams of tomatoes cooking down for a quarter hour. Tomato powder does that job in five minutes, because the water was already removed at a factory. Add it gradually and whisk, or you get lumps of paste in thin water. The sun-dried pieces are there for texture; powder alone makes a sauce with no body to it.

Eggs are the reason this needs a cooler and a rigid case, and they are why the tag list has no vegan on it. The poach is the only fussy part: the sauce has to be thick enough to hold a well, and the flame has to be low enough that the bottom does not scorch while the whites set. A lid speeds it up by cooking the tops with steam. If your stove only has one setting, pull the pot half off the burner.
