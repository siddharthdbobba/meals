The original bakes these in a 425°F oven, which you do not have. A pot over a stove does the same job because crisping a taquito is a surface reaction, not an oven one: seam side down in hot oil, two minutes, done.

Everything perishable got swapped. Dried mushrooms rehydrate into something close to the sautéed original and weigh a fifth as much. Chipotle powder replaces the canned chipotle in adobo, since a half-used can is dead weight and a fridge problem. Hard grating cheese replaces the queso fresco. The one rule worth obeying is cooking the filling dry before it goes in the tortilla.
