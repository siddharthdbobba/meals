This is a layered pan, not a scramble. Each thing goes in when the thing under it is finished cooking, so the onions get soft before the tomato ever hits heat and the beans only need to warm through at the end. Adding a splash of water with the oil at the start steams the onions first, then boils off and lets them fry — one pan doing two jobs.

Breaking the yolks and dividing the eggs into quadrants is what makes it serve four cleanly. You flip four pieces instead of fighting one sheet. This one needs a cooler for the eggs and produce, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp rather than in a pack; if you want it on the trail, powdered eggs and dried onion get you most of the way there.
