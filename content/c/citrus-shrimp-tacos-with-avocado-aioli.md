The original is a cast iron grill recipe with fresh shrimp and a blender aioli, which is a lot of refrigeration for a taco. Everything cold-dependent got swapped: freeze-dried shrimp for fresh, mayo powder for jarred, freeze-dried avocado and pico for the produce. The shrimp rehydrate in ten minutes and then get seared dry, which is the step that matters. Wet shrimp steam and turn to rubber.

The aioli is thin at first and thickens as the powder hydrates, so mix it before you cook and let it sit. Lime juice powder is doing the acid work that the fresh lime did; without it the whole plate tastes flat and salty. No dietary tags beyond nut-free here, since the mayo powder carries egg and the tortillas carry wheat.
