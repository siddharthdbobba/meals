The original is a frozen skillet meal, which is useless past the trailhead. Everything in it dries well, though, so the trail version is the same idea rebuilt from components: dried cauliflower for the rice, pouch chicken for the protein, dried peas and carrot for the vegetables. It stays one pot and roughly the same fifteen minutes.

Two things decide whether this works. Drive off the extra water before you add fat, or you get soup with cauliflower in it. And add the oil last, off the heat, because sesame oil loses everything it has if you boil it. The egg powder is optional but it is doing the structural work that makes the dish taste like fried rice rather than steamed vegetables.
