This is a fire-and-stick recipe, so almost everything that can go wrong is heat management. Flames scorch the outside and leave the hips raw. Coals with a low, steady flame cook a small carcass through in about twenty-five minutes without turning it into jerky. Keep it high and turn it often.

Squirrel is very lean, which is why the seasoning is heavy and why a little oil is worth carrying. The twine matters more than it sounds: use cotton, not synthetic, because synthetic melts into the meat over a fire. The seasoning blend is premixed at home so the only thing you handle in camp is a knife, a vial, and a stick.
