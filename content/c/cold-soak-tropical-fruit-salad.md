Fresh tropical fruit is a car-camping luxury and a backpacking joke. The original is watermelon, papaya, and dragon fruit cut fresh, which is mostly water you would otherwise be carrying. Dried fruit and a cold soak get you to the same place: the water comes from the creek, not your pack.

The lime and salt are the parts that matter. Dried fruit soaked in plain water tastes flat and one-note sweet. Acid and a little salt put the edges back. Bananas and coconut go in last because they turn to mush in the soak, and mush is the failure mode of every rehydrated fruit dish.
