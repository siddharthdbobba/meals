Couscous is the fastest carb you can carry. It does not cook so much as rehydrate, which means the stove runs for two minutes instead of fifteen and you are not scraping starch off the bottom of a pot afterward. The lid does the rest of the work off the flame.

The oil goes in at the end for a reason. Added to boiling water it coats the grains and they hydrate unevenly. Added after, it stays where you can taste it and carries most of the calories in the bowl. Sun-dried tomatoes stand in for anything fresh: they soften in the first boil and put acid and salt into the water before the couscous ever sees it.
