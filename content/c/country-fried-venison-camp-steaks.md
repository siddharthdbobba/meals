This is a car-camping dinner, not a pack-in one. It needs a cooler, a deep skillet, and enough oil to shallow-fry, which is three things a backpacker will not have. What it gives back is a way to turn a lean, unforgiving cut into something with a crust.

The queso fresco in the breading is the part worth keeping. It is dry and crumbly rather than melty, so it browns into the crumb layer instead of sliding off. Keep the oil hot between batches and give the meat room; a crowded pan steams the crust rather than frying it.
