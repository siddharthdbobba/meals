This is a bread that keeps its schedule on your side. Yeast, one rise, one shape, one bake, and no starter to feed. The cooked rice is the part worth keeping: the gelatinized starch holds water in the crumb, so the loaf stays soft an extra day in a hot car. Instant rice gets you there in ten minutes instead of forty, which is the whole reason it is in the trail version.

Two swaps came from the original. Fresh milk or buttermilk becomes powdered whole milk in the dry mix, with a teaspoon of vinegar in the beer to get the same tang and a little more lift. Everything except the beer is dry and premixed, so the only cooler item is gone. Beer wants to be flat and room temperature going in — cold beer stalls the rise, and the carbonation does nothing here since the yeast is doing that job.
