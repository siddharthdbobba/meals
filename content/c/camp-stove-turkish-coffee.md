This is a boil-in-the-pot coffee, which is the point of carrying it. Everything goes in cold together, comes up together, and the grounds settle out in the mug instead of in a filter you have to pack out wet. The only gear is a pot you already have.

Low heat matters more than anything else here. Coffee brought up slowly foams once, gently, and that foam is what holds the aroma; rushed on a wide-open burner it just boils over and tastes scorched. A narrow, heavy-bottomed pot climbs faster than you expect, so stand there with your hand on the handle.
