This is a scout-camp dutch oven standby, and the reason it holds up is that only one ingredient is perishable. The chili and chips are shelf-stable, so the cooler is only carrying beef and a block of cheese.

The corn chips go in late on purpose. Twenty minutes under the lid softens them into something closer to a dumpling than a chip, which is the texture you want. Stir them in at the start and they dissolve completely.

Everything happens in the one oven, so cleanup is a scrape and a wipe. Lean beef is worth the money here since drained grease has nowhere to go at a campsite.
