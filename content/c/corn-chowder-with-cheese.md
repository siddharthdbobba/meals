Freeze-dried vegetables fully rehydrate in boiling water, and the potato flakes do the work of thickening the soup into something with body rather than thin broth. The dry milk and cheese carry most of the calories and protein, which is what you need on a cold day when you are fueling a body that is staying warm.

Both the freezer bag method (in a cozy) and one-pot method produce the same result in about 10 minutes from boil. The thickened chowder is substantial and warm. Add more water if you want it thinner, or leave it as is—this recipe skews thick by design.
