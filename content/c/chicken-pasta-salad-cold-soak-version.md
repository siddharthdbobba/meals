Home versions of this lean on a fridge: fresh mozzarella, cucumber, mayonnaise, yogurt. None of that travels. The dressing here is milk powder, parmesan, and oil rehydrated by the water clinging to the pasta, which lands close to the same place — thick, tangy, and sticking to the spirals rather than sliding off.

Cold soaking pasta is slower than people expect. Two hours is the floor for fusilli, three is safer, and spirals thicker than a pencil lead may never get there. Start it at your morning break and it is lunch. The sun-dried tomatoes and pepper flakes rehydrate on the same schedule, so nothing needs separate attention.
