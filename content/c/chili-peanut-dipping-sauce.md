This is a thin, vinegar-forward dip rather than a creamy peanut sauce. Nothing is cooked. The sugar dissolves in the vinegar and water on its own if you keep stirring, so the whole thing works cold with no stove.

The fresh chili and garlic from the original become flakes and powder. Both rehydrate in the liquid over ten or fifteen minutes, which is why it is worth mixing before you start grilling instead of when you sit down. Peanuts go in last and stay crunchy; if you mix them in early they soften and the sauce loses its texture.
