This is a bean pot with a bread lid, and the lid is the reason it works. Baked beans and barbecue sauce are already sweet and already cooked, so the oven is not really cooking them, it is holding them at a simmer while the cornbread sets on top. That top layer soaks up steam from below and turns into something between cornbread and dumpling.

Browning the beef at home is the real trick. It removes the messiest step from camp, it kills the grease-disposal problem, and a flat frozen bag of cooked beef is a better cooler block than a bag of ice. The powdered milk and powdered egg replace what the muffin box assumes you have in a fridge. Heat from above matters more than heat from below here, so keep most of your coals on the lid or you will get a pale, wet top.
