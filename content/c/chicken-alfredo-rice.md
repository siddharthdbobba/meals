Freeze-dried chicken and vegetables rehydrate completely in 15 minutes of hot soak. You need only one boil and one stir, then you're done. The powdered milk and alfredo powder make a cream sauce without fresh dairy—lighter weight and indefinite shelf life. It sits easier at altitude than heavier fare.

The rice is the ballast. It absorbs the sauce instead of leaving you with broth that runs out. The freeze-dried chicken is already cooked and densely concentrated, so you get real protein for the ounces in your pack.
