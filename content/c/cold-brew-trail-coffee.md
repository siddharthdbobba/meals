Cold water pulls less acid out of coffee than hot water does, which is why cheap grounds taste better this way than they do out of a pot. It also means you do not spend fuel or a boil on your first cup. The steep happens while you sleep, so the whole cost at camp is a strain and a pour.

Ratio is the only thing to get right: one ounce of grounds per cup of water makes a concentrate, not a cup. Drink it undiluted and it will hit hard. Coarse grind matters too, since fine grounds slip through a bandana and turn the bottom of the mug into sludge. The creamer is powdered on purpose, because there is no cream on a trail.
