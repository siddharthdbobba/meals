This is a car camping breakfast, not a pack-it-in one. Eggs, shredded carrot, and cream cheese all need a cooler, so the recipe stays where the cooler goes. In exchange you get a hot breakfast with real bulk to it.

The carrot is doing two jobs: it adds moisture, which means less water in the batter and less chance of a soggy center, and it holds the pancake together while it cooks over uneven fire heat. Keep the heat lower than you would at home. Camp skillets have hot spots, and a sugared batter finds every one of them. The cream cheese goes on last, off the heat, so it softens instead of breaking.
