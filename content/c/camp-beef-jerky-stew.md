Gravy mix thickens everything here. The tomato powder and Worcestershire stop it from tasting like salt and cornstarch. They're working hard to make jerky and freeze-dried vegetables taste intentional.

This swaps fresh beef and produce for jerky and dehydrated vegetables. Fresh would brown and taste better, but these pack light and don't spoil. On a week-long trip without resupply, that tradeoff is easy to take.
