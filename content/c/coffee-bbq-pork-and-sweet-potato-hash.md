Coffee in barbecue sauce sounds like a stunt and is not. Roasted beans carry the same bitter, savory compounds that make a sauce taste like it has been reduced for an hour, and a teaspoon of instant dissolves into the sauce with no brewing and no grounds in your teeth. Instant is the point here. Ground coffee stays gritty.

The order matters more than anything else. Sweet potato needs a boil to get tender, but boiling it in the same pot first means one pot and one wipe-out for the whole meal. Sauce goes on at the very end, off the main heat, because it is mostly sugar and will char to tar long before a chop is cooked through. This one needs a cooler, so it is a car-camping or basecamp dinner, not something to carry.
