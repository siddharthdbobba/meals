The original is an hour-long simmer, and that is the one thing a stove cannot afford. Real wild rice needs 45 minutes or more of fuel, so this uses a parboiled blend that comes up in about twelve. Everything else follows from having no fridge: freeze-dried mirepoix stands in for the celery, carrot, and onion, bouillon replaces the stock, and the chicken arrives already cooked in a pouch.

The fat matters more than it looks. Broth soups are thin on calories, and a tablespoon of ghee is most of what makes this a dinner instead of a warm drink. Salt at the end, not the start — bouillon and the pouch liquid are already salty, and how much water actually boiled off is anyone's guess.
