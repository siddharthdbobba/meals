steps:
  - "Stir 1/4 cup powdered milk into 1 cup of warm water. Warm, not hot. Water above about 120F kills the yeast."
  - "In the pot, mix flour, yeast, dough sugar, salt, and powdered egg. Pour in the milk water and 1/3 cup shortening. Add the raisins."
  - "Knead in the pot for eight minutes. It goes from shaggy to smooth and slightly tacky. Add flour a spoonful at a time only if it sticks to your hands."
  - "Cover and let it rise until doubled, about an hour in warm sun, two or more if it is cold. Set it near the fire, not on it."
  - "Punch it down and press it flat on a clean surface or a lid, roughly a foot square. Brush with half the melted ghee, then scatter the two sugars and cinnamon over the whole thing."
  - "Roll it up tight, pinch the seam shut, and lay it seam down in a greased dutch oven. Let it rise again for 30 minutes."

Home bread recipes lean on cold milk, eggs, and softened butter, none of which survive a weekend in a car. Powdered milk and powdered egg do the same job dry, and shortening or ghee replaces the butter because both sit at 90F without turning into a puddle. The sugar and cinnamon filling is unchanged, since it was already shelf-stable.

The real variable is heat, not ingredients. Coals under a dutch oven scorch the bottom long before the middle sets, so most of your fuel goes on the lid and you keep rotating. Rise time is the other one: yeast reads temperature, not the clock, so a cold morning means two hours of waiting and there is no shortcut for it.
