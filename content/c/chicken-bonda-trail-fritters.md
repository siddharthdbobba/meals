This started as a fried tea-time snack and it survives the move to camp because nothing in it needs a fridge. Canned chicken replaces fresh mince, dried onion and powdered aromatics replace the fresh shallot, ginger, and garlic, and the roasted gram does exactly what it did at home: it drinks up moisture and binds the filling so no egg or breadcrumb is required.

The two failure modes are both about water. Filling that is too wet slumps off the ball and into the oil; batter that is too thin slides away and leaves you frying bare chicken. Add water in splashes, not pours. Keep the oil at a steady moderate heat and fry in small batches, because a crowded pot drops the temperature and the coating turns greasy instead of crisp. This is a car-camp or basecamp job — you are carrying two cups of oil and dealing with it afterward.
