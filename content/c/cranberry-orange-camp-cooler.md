The original was two juices poured from cartons, which is not a thing you carry. Powdered drink mix does the same job at a fraction of the weight, and the flavors survive the conversion better than most fruit does. Cranberry is the reason this works: it is sour and slightly bitter, so it keeps the orange from tasting like candy.

The lemon is not a garnish. Three drops per cup sharpens the whole thing and cuts the flat, powdery edge that drink mixes get when they sit in a bottle. Keep the sugar small or skip it. Most commercial mixes are already sweet enough to be tiring after a liter.
