Couscous is not really cooked so much as rehydrated, which is why it works on a stove that only has one good boil in it. The milk powder and the cream cheese wedge are what keep it from being wet semolina. Fat carries the herbs and the bouillon salt, and it gives the whole thing enough body to feel like dinner instead of a side.

Hold the cheese until the heat is off. Boiling it just makes it break and stick to the pot wall. The five minutes under the lid matter too: that is when the last of the water goes into the grain rather than pooling at the bottom.

The bouillon is beef, so this is not vegetarian as written. Vegetable bouillon swaps in cleanly. The milk powder and cheese mean it is never dairy-free, and couscous is wheat.
