This started as an oven recipe, and an oven is the one thing camp does not have. Foil on coals gets you most of the way there. It steams more than it roasts, so the wedges come out soft rather than crisp, which is why they are cut thick instead of into fries. A thin fry in a foil packet turns into mush.

The dip is doing the work the crisp texture would otherwise do. Mayo alone is flat; lemon and raw garlic cut through it and give the sweet potato something to push against. Single-serve mayo packets keep at ambient temperature until you tear them, which is the whole reason they are here instead of a jar. Mix the dip right before you eat and use it all.
