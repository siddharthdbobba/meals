The original was a casserole that went into a 450 degree oven. There is no oven at a campsite, so the whole thing moves to one pot on a flame. Nothing is lost: the bake was only heating already-cooked food through, which a few minutes of stirring over heat does just as well.

Tomato paste is the ingredient carrying the weight. Cheese powder alone thins out once you add a pound of beef and a can of corn, and the paste puts the body back so it clings to the pasta instead of pooling. Keep the corn liquid for the same reason you drained the pasta — you want a little starch and salt in the sauce, not a bowl of water.
