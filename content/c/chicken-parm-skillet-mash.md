Baked chicken parm needs an oven, a sheet pan, and three bowls. None of that fits in a pack, so the structure moves instead of the flavor: potato flakes carry the sauce, the crumbs get toasted dry in the pot before anything wet goes in, and the cheese goes on at the end where it still tastes like cheese.

Toasting the crumbs first is the part that matters. Untoasted crumbs stirred into sauce turn to paste and you lose the only crunch in the meal. Tomato powder replaces the cup of marinara because a jar of sauce is mostly water you are already carrying.
