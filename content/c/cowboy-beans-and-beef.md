This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal, and it is best on the first night out while the ground beef is still solidly cold. Everything else on the list is a can or a condiment, so the only thing you are managing is the meat.

The brown sugar and mustard are doing more than sweetening. Canned baked beans are already sweet and one-note; the mustard's acid and the ketchup's tang give the sauce edges, and the beef's rendered fat carries them. Cook it down long enough that the liquid thickens and clings, or you are eating soup with a spoon.
