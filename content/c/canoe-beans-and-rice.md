This is a two-starch dinner that only asks for one boil. The rice does its work off the flame, so a canister lasts most of a week of these, and the bean flakes turn the leftover water into a sauce instead of leaving it in the bottom of the pot. Nothing here needs a cooler.

The oil and cheese powder are load-bearing. Beans and rice on their own come in light on calories for the volume they take up, and after a long day of paddling or walking that shortfall shows up around nine at night. The tortillas double as the utensil, which is the real reason there is only one pot to scrub.
