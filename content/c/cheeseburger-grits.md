This is cheeseburger-flavored polenta made from ingredients that live in a ziplock. Freeze-dried hamburger rehydrates into something indistinguishable from fresh beef. The cheese powder carries the fat, and the dry milk rounds it out into something that actually tastes like food instead of camping math.

Grits don't need to cook. Pour boiling water, pull the pot off heat, and wait fifteen minutes. Relish and ketchup packets are free—ask nicely at any burger counter. Collect them at home or pocket a few on the trail. They're the reason this tastes like something you meant to make.
