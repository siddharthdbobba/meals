The onion soup mix flavors the beef itself, so the soup character runs through the burger rather than sitting on top. Two cheeses are not overkill: Swiss brings gentle sweetness, Provolone adds smoke. The combination works better than either alone.

Caramelized onions take 12-15 minutes and demand patience, but they are the point of the dish. The wine reduces their sharpness and lifts their sweetness. This is why French onion soup translates to burgers: the caramelized onion, melted cheese, and good beef are already a complete idea together.
