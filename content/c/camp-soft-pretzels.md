The baking soda bath is the whole recipe. Thirty seconds in alkaline water gelatinizes the surface starch, which is what gives a pretzel its brown skin and its chew. Skip it and you have baked bread in a knot.

Butter and the egg wash both went in the trash for trail purposes: neither survives a cooler-free weekend, and olive oil does the same job in the dough and on the crust. The rise is the real cost here, not the bake. Mix the dough when you get to camp, go do something else for an hour, and come back when the coals are grey.
