This is home-prep work, not camp work. The oven does the toasting and the hot nuts do the coating, so there is nothing to cook once you are out. Timing is the whole trick: the pecans have to come out of the oven straight into the butter, and the sugar has to go on while they are still hot enough to hold it. Let them cool first and the sugar just falls off in the bag.

The butter is the shelf-life limit. Fat this exposed goes rancid, so plan on two weeks and keep the bag out of a hot pack lid or a car dash. In real heat, leave these home and carry plain nuts instead.
