The original is ice cream, which is a non-starter without a freezer. Instant pudding does the same structural job: it is sweet, cold-ish, and thick enough to hold toppings without them sinking. Milk powder plus cold water sets it in about five minutes, no stove and no fridge.

The caramel popcorn has to be coated and dried at home. Toss it in warm caramel sauce, spread it out, let it firm up, and it travels as a dry crunchy thing instead of a sticky mass. That crunch is the whole point of the dish against a soft base, so assemble at the last second and eat it fast.
