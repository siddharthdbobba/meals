This is a cooler recipe, not a stove recipe. The smoking happens at home where you have hours and a real fire; camp only does the mixing. That split is what makes it work — smoked fish and cream cheese both keep several days on ice, and there is nothing to cook, heat, or wash.

Mix it in the bag it traveled in. Cream cheese welds itself to a bowl and a spoon, and you are not scrubbing that in a creek. Let the cheese warm up first or you will spend ten minutes fighting a cold brick. The rub and hickory salt carry the smoke past what the fish alone gives you, which matters because carp is mild to the point of blank.
