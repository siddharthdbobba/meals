Instant couscous is already cooked and dried, so it does not need heat, only hot water and time. That means one boil, no simmer, and almost no fuel — which is the entire reason it belongs in a pack instead of rice or pasta.

The couscous alone is thin eating. Salami, oil, and hard cheese are shelf-stable fats doing the work fresh dairy would do at home, and they are where most of the thousand calories come from. Cut them and you have a hot side dish, not a meal.
