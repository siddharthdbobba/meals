This came off a crock pot recipe from 1984, and the original ran eight to twenty-four hours on low. A Dutch oven over spent coals does the same job in ninety minutes if you keep the heat low and stir often enough that the sugar does not scorch on the bottom.

The fish is the strange part and it is the point. Canned salmon dissolves into the sauce and thickens it; the smoked oysters and their oil are where the smoke and most of the richness come from. Add both at the end, because anything that simmers hard for an hour turns to paste. A full cup of sugar against a quarter cup of chili powder is not a typo either, and it is why this needs more salt than you expect.
