This started as a blender drink built on ice cream, which is three things the trail does not have: a blender, a freezer, and fresh milk. Whole milk powder and instant vanilla pudding mix do the same job cold. The pudding is the important part — it has enough starch and gum to thicken plain water into something with body, so you get a shake instead of blue milk.

Use gel coloring, not the liquid kind. A drop of gel colors the whole bottle without adding volume, and it weighs nothing. Add the cookies last and drink it soon after; leave it half an hour and the pudding sets hard enough to stand a spoon in.
