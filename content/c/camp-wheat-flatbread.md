The original is a two-loaf oven bread with Greek yogurt in it, which is two things you do not have in camp: an oven and a fridge. Flatbreads solve the oven problem, since a skillet over coals heats one thin round all the way through in a few minutes. Powdered whole milk covers the yogurt, adding the fat and milk solids that keep a whole wheat crumb from going dense and crumbly.

The vital wheat gluten is not optional. Whole wheat flour carries bran that cuts the gluten strands as they form, and the added gluten gives the dough enough structure to trap gas and stay soft. Instant yeast goes straight into the dry mix, so the only wet step at camp is pouring water into a bag.
