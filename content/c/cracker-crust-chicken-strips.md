The crackers do two jobs at once. They are already seasoned and already salted, so the breading needs nothing else, and the fat baked into them browns the crust without a fryer. Crush them fine at home; large shards fall off the moment you turn a tender.

Powdered egg white replaces fresh eggs here, which is the one real change from a kitchen version. It rehydrates into a thin coating that grips crumbs just as well and lets you carry the binder in a jar instead of a carton. The chicken still needs a cooler, so this is a first-night car-camping dinner, not a backpacking one.
