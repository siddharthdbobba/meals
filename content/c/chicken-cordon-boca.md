This one earns the cooler weight. The chicken is doing two jobs at once: cooking through, and dripping fat and mustard down into the spinach and mushrooms underneath, which cook down into a broth thick enough to serve on its own. One pot, three things out of it.

Most of the fiddly work belongs at home. Pounding, rolling, and pinning eight thighs at a picnic table costs you daylight, and raw chicken plus a camp table is a cleanup problem you do not want. Do it in a kitchen, pack the rolls flat, and camp prep drops to chopping mushrooms. Coal management is the only real skill here — more on the lid than underneath, or the bottom scorches before the centers are done.
