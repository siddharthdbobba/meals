Freeze-dried egg is the whole reason this works. Real eggs are fragile, heavy for the calories, and go bad in a pack; the powdered version rehydrates into something with actual curd if you give it a full ten minutes off the heat instead of stirring it over the flame. Stirring while it cooks turns egg into rubber, so the rest is not optional.

The sausage and peppers do double duty. Sausage carries the fat and most of the twenty-odd grams of protein per serving, and the peppers put back some texture that egg powder alone does not have. Two servings out of one pouch is honest for a rest day, but if you are moving hard, eat the whole thing and count it as one meal.
