This is a car-camping chili and it stays that way. Eight pounds of wet ingredients and a two-hour simmer do not go in a pack. The dutch oven earns its weight here: cast iron holds the low, even heat that breaks brisket collagen down without scorching the sugar in the BBQ sauce and ketchup.

The beer is doing more than flavor. Pale ale is bitter and carbonated, which cuts through the brown sugar and the fat rendering off two pounds of chorizo. Beans go in last because they have already been cooked once, and an hour in acidic tomato turns them to paste. Everything else in the pot only improves with time on the coals.
