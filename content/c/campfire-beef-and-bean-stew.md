This one is honest about what it is: a can of beans doing most of the work, with a pound of beef for protein and fat. The baked bean sauce is already sweet and thick, so it needs no thickener and no added water. Salt late, because the beans arrive salted.

The fire is the variable. Cook over coals with the pot on a grate, keep the beef moving until it browns rather than steams, and pour the fat off only if there is enough to pool. That rendered fat is what carries the onion and keeps the beans from tasting flat.
