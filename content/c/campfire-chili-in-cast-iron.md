Dutch oven chili is car camping at its most practical. You prep once, walk away for an hour, and have hot food for everyone. The long simmer breaks down the peppers and beans into a thick sauce rather than a soup, and the meat browning step builds flavor up front so the gentle heat later just blends everything together.

The peppers do the work here. Fresh peppers carry more vitamin C than tomato sauce alone, and they taste brighter. Use whatever the campground store has if your usual combination isn't there. Poblanos are nice if you find them, but red and green bells work just as well. The canned beans and tomato sauce are shelf-stable standbys that pack light and last, making this recipe flexible for remote camps where you can't pick and choose.
