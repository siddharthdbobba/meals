Frozen hash browns are the quiet trick in this recipe. They go in the cooler frozen
solid, they keep everything else cold for a day and a half, and by the morning you
want them they have thawed to exactly the right point.

The no-stir instruction is the whole recipe. Stirring hash browns produces a pile of
steamed potato. Leaving them alone produces a crust.
