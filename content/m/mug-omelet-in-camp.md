The original is a mug in a microwave, which no one has at 6 a.m. in a tent. A pot over a low flame does the same job if you accept the one rule that matters: keep stirring and pull it early. Eggs carry heat after the burner is off, so a pot that looks done on the stove is dry by the time you eat it.

Powdered whole egg replaces the shells. It rehydrates into something close to scrambled eggs as long as you mix it into cool water first and let the lumps break down before any heat touches it. Hard aged cheese and cured meat are the two dairy-case items that survive a few days out of a fridge, and they carry most of the fat here.
