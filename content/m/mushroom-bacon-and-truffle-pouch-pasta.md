Emergency pouch meals are engineered to survive a decade in a bin, not to taste like anything. The fix is not more water. It is fat, salt, and something with actual texture. Dried mushrooms rehydrate into real bite, the bacon adds smoke and grease, and truffle oil gives the whole pot a smell the pouch could never carry.

The original is a home kitchen version with fresh mushrooms, butter, baby spinach, and a skillet. Everything here is the shelf-stable stand-in: dried mushrooms with their soaking liquid used as broth, freeze-dried spinach, precooked bacon crumbles, and ghee instead of butter. Add the truffle oil after the heat is off. It is volatile, and boiling it just wastes it.
