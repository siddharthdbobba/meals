This started as an Australian Scout drink made with two liters of fresh milk and a liter of prepared custard. Neither travels. Full-fat milk powder and instant custard powder do the same two jobs dry: fat and protein from one, thickening starch from the other. You carry powder and add water at camp.

The rule is no boiling. Milk powder solids stick and burn to a thin pot fast, and custard starch that gets too hot breaks instead of setting. Heat to just under a simmer, stir, and pull it. The sugar is halved from the original because Milo already brings its own.
