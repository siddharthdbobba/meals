The powdered cream and butter are where this works. They rehydrate into enough of a sauce to coat the noodles instead of making soup, and the freeze-dried chicken carries real protein without weight. The peas and carrots add structure and the broth powder adds salt.

Fifteen minutes covered is not decorative. The powders need time to fully hydrate, and the noodles need the moisture to soften. In cold weather, a pot cozy or your sleeping bag speeds the rehydration. Skip the sit and you have crunchy noodles in thin broth.
