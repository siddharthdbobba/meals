Slow-cooker versions of this run on heavy cream, fresh aromatics, and four hours. None of that travels. Cream powder rehydrates into something close enough, dry-packed sun-dried tomatoes carry the same concentrated flavor without a jar of oil, and shelf-stable gnocchi cooks in three minutes because it is already cooked once.

The soak step matters more than it looks. Sun-dried tomatoes and onion flakes are the slowest things in the pot, and if you add gnocchi at the same time you get soft dumplings floating around leathery tomato. Give the hard stuff a head start, then boil once more. Cream powder always goes in off the heat; boiled, it separates.
