The source version starts with a jug of store-bought cider, which is water you have already carried and a fridge you do not have. Instant cider powder gets you the same drink at a fraction of an ounce, and the whole spices do the part the powder cannot: simmering releases oils that a shelf mix flattens out.

Simmer time is the whole recipe. Ten minutes is the floor for the cinnamon and cloves to give anything up. Add the powder at the end instead of the start so the sugar does not scorch on the pot bottom while the spices work.
