The source made this with fresh mint, a cut lime, and ice, which is a cooler recipe, not a trail one. Dried mint and lime crystals do the same job at a fraction of the weight and survive a week in a pack. Honey keeps indefinitely and squeezes out of a tube in any weather above freezing.

The point is not flavor for its own sake. Water that has been sitting in a bladder or a scratched bottle picks up a flat plastic taste, and people drink less of it than they should. Acid and mint cover that, the sugar gives you something to absorb, and the pinch of salt replaces what you sweated out. Mix it in the bottle you are already carrying and there is nothing to wash.
