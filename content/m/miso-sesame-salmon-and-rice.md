Scallops need a hot cast iron surface and a cold chain, which is two things a stove and a pack do not provide. The glaze is the part worth carrying: miso, honey, vinegar, tamari, and sesame oil is a sauce that keeps at ambient temperature and does most of its work raw. Pouched salmon stands in for the shellfish, and instant rice gives it somewhere to go.

Mix the glaze before the rice, not after. Miso is stiff and clumps into pale lumps if you try to stir it into a hot pot of anything, and heating it past a simmer flattens the flavor and makes it taste harsh. Salt is measured last because both the miso and the tamari already bring plenty, and the amount varies by brand.
