This is a pantry dinner rebuilt around one constraint: it has to survive a year in a sealed pouch and still cook in one pot. Instant rice and bean flakes do the heavy lifting because both are already cooked and dried, so the stove only has to reheat them rather than simmer anything for an hour.

The oil goes in last and off the heat. It is most of the fat in the meal, and stirring it into a boiling pot mostly loses it to the pot walls. Bean flakes thicken as they cool, so err on the wet side when you add water.
