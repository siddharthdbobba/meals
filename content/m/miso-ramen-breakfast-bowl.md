The source version uses a fresh egg, which is a car-camping ingredient. Powdered egg poured into off-boil broth does the same thing structurally: it sets into soft ribbons and thickens the broth slightly. Slurry it in cold water first, or you get lumps of dry powder floating on top.

Miso goes in last and off the heat. Boiling it flattens the flavor and drives off most of what you carried it for. Adding it after the pot comes off also means the noodles stop cooking at the same moment, which is the only real timing decision in this recipe.
