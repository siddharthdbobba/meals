The original is a fresh-mushroom soup built on scratch dashi. On trail neither of those travels, so the mushrooms come dried and the dashi comes from powder. Dried mushrooms are actually the better carrier here: drying concentrates the aromatic compounds, and the soak water becomes part of the broth instead of being poured off.

Two things decide whether this works. Keep the kombu below a hard boil, and add the miso off the heat. Miso is a live ferment with a lot of volatile aroma in it; boiling strips that out and leaves you with salt water. Everything else is timing you can do by feel.
