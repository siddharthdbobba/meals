The home version starts with sauteed onion and a can of pintos, mashed in the pan. On trail the can is the problem: a pound of water you already have to carry more of. Instant bean flakes give you the same mashed texture from four ounces of dry weight, and the dried onion and garlic go in with the boiling water so they rehydrate instead of staying gritty.

Cumin is the ingredient doing the flavor work here, so be heavier with it than a home recipe would suggest. The oil matters too. Beans are protein and fiber and almost no fat, which makes for a dinner that fills you up at seven and leaves you awake and hungry at eleven.
