The original is a Romanian shortbread cookie baked around a boozy cherry. Baking is not happening on trail, so this is the same three flavors, cocoa, butter, and sour cherry, assembled as a no-bake dough at home. The flour gets toasted first, which is not optional: raw flour carries the same risk as raw egg, and toasting also gives the dough a nutty backbone it would otherwise lack.

Fat and sugar are what make this worth its weight, roughly 420 calories in two bites. The dried cherry replaces the jam or liqueur-soaked fruit in the original; it stays acidic enough to cut through the butter, and unlike jam it will not weep through the dough on day three.
