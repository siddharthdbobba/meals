This is a griddled cheese sandwich that has been dipped in custard, so the batter has to stay thin. Too long in the egg and the middle turns to paste before the cheese ever melts. Two seconds a side, then straight into the butter.

The cheese runs the timing. Cheddar and Swiss both melt slower than the bread browns, so the pan stays at medium and the sandwich gets pressed flat on the second side to close the edges and push heat into the center. Camp version: cheese is pre-sliced at home, the milk is boxed or powdered, and powdered egg makes the whole thing cooler-free if you want it that way. The jam is not garnish. Sweet against smoked cheddar is the point of the dish.
