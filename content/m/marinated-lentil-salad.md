Lentils are one of the few dry legumes worth cooking on a stove. No soak, one pot, and about 25 minutes of fuel. That is a lot for a canister but reasonable on a basecamp night when you are not racing daylight.

The dressing is doing the heavy lifting. Vinegar, mustard, and maple syrup hit hot lentils and get absorbed instead of pooling at the bottom, which is why this gets stirred off the heat rather than at the table. The sun dried tomato oil replaces the olive oil the home version uses, so there is one less bottle to carry. Fresh parsley and fresh lemon were the only things that needed the fridge, and dried parsley plus bottled juice cover both.
