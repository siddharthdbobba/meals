This is a Tom Collins with a spoon of marmalade in it, which sounds wrong and isn't. The peel in the marmalade carries bitter oils that plain sugar syrup doesn't have, and those oils give the drink something to push against the lemon. It also thickens the texture slightly, so the soda has body to sit on.

The jar is the shaker. That is the whole reason this belongs in a camp kit rather than a bar: no tin, no strainer, no measuring beyond a spoon. Everything except the ice is shelf-stable, so the only cooler dependency is the ice itself, and superfine sugar matters more than it sounds. Regular granulated sits at the bottom of a cold jar and stays there.
