The original is an oven dish with a stick of butter and a cast iron pan. Neither travels well, so this runs in foil at the fire's edge, and olive oil replaces the butter. Shelf-stable precooked bacon takes the place of raw bacon, which needs a cold box and a real pan to render.

The cuts matter more than the topping. Slicing most of the way through opens up the potato so heat gets to the middle and the sugar and oil run down into it instead of sitting on the skin. Cut all the way through and you have a pile of coins; leave it whole and it takes an hour.
