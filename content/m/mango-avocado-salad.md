This one only works on the first day out, and that is fine. It is the meal for the drive-in night when the cooler is still cold and nobody wants another pot of noodles. Everything past day one, use dried mango and skip the avocado entirely.

The dressing is premixed at home for a reason. Zesting a lime and mincing garlic on a picnic table is possible but slow, and once the avocado is cut you are on a clock. Mixing the acid first means the fruit goes straight into it. Fold, do not stir: the avocado is doing the work of a creamy dressing and it only does that while the cubes are still cubes.
