The original is a picnic salad built on cooked rice, fresh cucumber and tomato, and feta. None of that survives a warm pack. What survives is the structure: starch, acid, fat, salt, and something briny. Instant rice cold-soaks in about ninety minutes, and the sun-dried tomatoes and pepper flakes rehydrate on the same water, so the vegetables come back on their own instead of riding in a cooler.

The fresh lemon becomes lemon juice powder and the feta comes out entirely. Feta is the one thing that does not have a good shelf-stable stand-in, so the oil and the olives carry the fat and the salt instead. Add the oil at camp, not at home, or the dry mix goes greasy and the rice soaks unevenly.
