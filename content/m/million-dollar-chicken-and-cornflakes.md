The original is a slow cooker casserole finished under a broiler, which is two heat sources you do not have. What survives the translation is the structure: a creamy chicken base, salt from bacon and cheese, and a crunchy lid. Freeze-dried chicken replaces raw breast, powdered milk and sour cream powder replace the dairy, and the soup mix does the job of the can.

The topping is the part people skip and should not. Everything under it is one soft texture, and a spoonful of buttered cornflakes is the only contrast in the pot. Add it at the very end — stirred in early, it just dissolves into thickener.
