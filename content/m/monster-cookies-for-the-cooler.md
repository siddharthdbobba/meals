This is a baked cookie, which means it belongs at a car camp or basecamp with a dutch oven and a real fire, not in a pack. The dough carries no water and almost no chemical leavening. Everything rises on the air you whip into the eggs and sugar, so that step is where the cookie is won or lost.

Coal placement does the rest. Bottom heat alone scorches the undersides before the centers set, so most of the coals go on the lid. Chilling the dough matters more over a fire than in an oven, because a cold ball spreads slower and gives the edges time to crisp while the middle stays chewy. The candy eyes will melt if you add them before the bake.
