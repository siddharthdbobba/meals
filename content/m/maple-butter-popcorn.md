The original was a home recipe: melt butter, stir in syrup, pour it over popcorn someone else already popped. On a trip you have to do the popping too, so this uses one pot for both jobs. Pop first, empty the pot, then melt the butter in it. That order matters, because sugar hitting a hot dry pot burns fast.

Ghee is the swap worth making in warm weather. It behaves like butter here and does not need a cooler. Maple syrup is heavy for what it is, so this is a car camp and basecamp dessert, not something to carry up a hill.
