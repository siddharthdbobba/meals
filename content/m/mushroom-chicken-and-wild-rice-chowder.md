The original is a roux soup: butter, flour, fresh milk, stock. None of that travels. The flour comes out and potato flakes go in, which thicken off the heat and never go pasty the way a raw-flour slurry does. Whole milk powder rebuilds the cream, and bouillon does the stock's job at a fraction of the weight.

Freeze-dried mushrooms are the ingredient worth spending on. They rehydrate in the simmer and give back the savory liquid that makes this taste like it took an hour. Add the milk powder off the boil — dumped into a rolling pot it seizes into lumps you will be chasing with a spoon all night.
