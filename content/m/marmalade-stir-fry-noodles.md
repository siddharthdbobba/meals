The original is a wok recipe built on fresh vegetables and a jar of orange marmalade. Neither travels. Dried vegetables rehydrate in the same water that cooks the noodles, and the marmalade splits into its two working parts: sugar and orange. Brown sugar plus ground peel or orange drink powder gets you close enough.

The cornstarch is what makes this a stir fry instead of noodle soup. It needs heat to set, so add the sauce mix after you drain and give it a minute back on the flame. Drain too thoroughly and it will glue itself to the pot, which is why you keep a splash of the cooking water.
