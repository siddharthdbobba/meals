This is a car-camp recipe because it needs a cooler, real eggs, and a tin. The point of the muffin pan is geometry: eggs baked in a cup come out as a disc that fits bread, so nothing hangs off the edge and nothing slides out on the first bite. It also cooks six at once without you standing over a skillet flipping one egg at a time.

The heat is the part that goes wrong. Coals, not flames, and a lid or foil over the top so the eggs cook from all sides instead of browning on the bottom. Every ingredient here is a topping bar, so lay it out and let people build their own. No dietary tags on this one: eggs, cheese, meat, and wheat muffins rule out all five.
