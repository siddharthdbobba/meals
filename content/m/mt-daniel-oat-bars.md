Butter and honey are the binder here, not a flavor note. Sugar syrup plus fat coats the oats and sets as it cools, which is why the pressing step matters more than the cooking step. The microwave drives off enough moisture that the whole thing firms up instead of staying a wet pile.

These are a few-day proposition, not a season's worth. Fat and honey do most of the preserving, but there is no reason to push it past a long weekend in a warm pack. For anything longer, cut and wrap them, freeze the batch, and pull out what the trip needs the morning you leave.
