Matsutake are expensive and the smell is the whole reason to carry them, so nothing here competes with it. The trick from the restaurant version survives the trip intact: the peelings you would throw away make a broth stronger than the sliced caps do. Dry both at home, carry them together, and you get a pot of broth out of scrap.

Dehydrating solves the field problems too. Fresh matsutake bruise in a pack and the stems arrive full of grit. Sliced and dried, they weigh almost nothing and rehydrate in the same three minutes the noodles need. The oil goes in off the heat because heat and time are what strip the aroma out.
