This is car-camping cooking, so it keeps the eggs and dairy the original recipe called for. Doing the mixing at home is the whole point: cracking six eggs at a picnic table means a bowl, a whisk, shells, and a wash-up. In a jar it is one shake and one thing to rinse.

The cinnamon and vanilla need time in the liquid, which they get overnight in the cooler. Thick bread matters more than the type — thin sandwich slices go to mush before the center takes on any custard. If you have no cooler, swap in powdered eggs and powdered milk and mix in the same jar with water at camp; the texture is a step down but the method is identical.
