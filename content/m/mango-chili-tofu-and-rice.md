The original is a skillet dish: press the tofu, fry it in oil until crisp, stir-fry fresh pepper, then coat it all in sauce. None of that survives a trail kitchen. Crisp does not keep, and fresh mango and bell pepper are water you carry uphill. So this version dries the tofu and the produce at home and rebuilds the dish as one pot of hot soak.

Pressing still matters, just for a different reason. Tofu that goes into the dehydrator wet dries slowly and rehydrates into something spongy. Press it hard, dice it small, and it comes back chewy enough to read as protein. The chutney and chili sauce go in at the end, off heat, because sugar scorches on a stove that only has one setting.
