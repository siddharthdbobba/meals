Onions and sweet potatoes are the two vegetables that do not need a cooler. They sit at room temperature for weeks, which makes this the rare camp dinner with actual fresh produce in it. The original used canned tomatoes and canned chickpeas; both are mostly water and mostly weight, so tomato powder and dried chickpeas carry the same dinner at a fraction of the load.

The couscous goes first and then gets ignored. It rehydrates on residual heat while the stew cooks, so the stove only runs once. Crushing part of the sweet potato at the end is the whole texture trick: starch comes out, the broth turns into sauce, and it stops sliding off the couscous.
