This stew doesn't turn into gray broth because the beef pieces and vegetable chunks are small enough to fully rehydrate in 12-15 minutes without disintegrating. The high protein-to-weight ratio means you're packing real nutrition into 3 ounces. Beef is the engine: it carries most of the calories and protein, and it outlasts any other protein form you could carry.

Dehydrating at home takes time and equipment, so this recipe makes sense if you process several batches at once or buy freeze-dried beef in bulk. If you're only making one or two pouches, a commercial product saves the setup work. Either way, you get a real dinner instead of something that tastes like powder.
