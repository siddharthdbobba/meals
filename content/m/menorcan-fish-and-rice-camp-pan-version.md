This is a Menorcan take on paella scaled down to what fits in a camp pan. Two pans is a compromise: the rice needs to sit undisturbed to set its crust, and frying fish in the same pan would break that up. If you only have one pan, fry the fish first, set it aside under foil, and build the rice in the leftover oil.

The fish stock does the work. Rice cooked in water is filler; rice cooked in stock is the dish, and the fried fish on top is protein and crunch. Fresh fish means a cooler and same-day cooking, so this is a truck or boat meal, not a pack meal. The dried-vegetable variation is what makes it carryable.
