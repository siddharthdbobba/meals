This is a carry-along seasoning, not a meal. Ten minutes of grinding at home buys you a jar that fixes bland camp protein for a whole season, and it weighs almost nothing.

The coffee is the part that makes it work. Slippery jacks dry down into something loud and woodsy that can take over a plate on its own; the ground coffee sits under it, bitter and roasted, and pulls the mushroom back into range. The real constraint is heat. Everything in the jar is already dried, so it burns long before meat gets a hard sear. Cook lower and slower than you would over a bare grate, or mix the rub into oil and add it at the end.
