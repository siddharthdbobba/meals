This is a cooler breakfast, not a pack-weight breakfast, so it stays close to the original. The order matters more than anything else: bacon first because its fat is the cooking medium, potatoes second because they take the longest and want direct contact with the pan, vegetables last because they release water and will steam the potatoes soft if they go in early.

Eggs on top are the one part that gets fussy over a fire. Coals throw uneven heat, so cover the pan and let steam do the work instead of trying to fry them. If the pan runs hot, pull it off the coals entirely after the eggs go on. Residual heat in cast iron will finish them.
