Cookies are a bad trail food and a good camp food. This one moves to the fire by swapping two things: butter becomes shortening or ghee, which does not need a cooler, and the fresh egg becomes powdered egg plus water. Nothing else changes, because bread flour, brown sugar, maple syrup, and pecans are all already shelf-stable.

The bread flour is not a substitution to undo. Its higher protein gives the dough enough structure to survive uneven coal heat without collapsing into grease. Coal baking runs hotter on the bottom than the top, so put more coals on the lid than under the oven, and pull the pot early. Underbaked centers set as they cool; overbaked ones do not come back.
