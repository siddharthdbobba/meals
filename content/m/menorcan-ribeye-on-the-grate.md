steps:
  - "Score the fat cap in a diamond pattern, cutting through the fat only. Stop at the meat."

  - "Burn charcoal or hardwood down to a solid bed of coals and let the grate heat over it until a drop of water skitters off."
  - "Stand the steak on its fat edge first, propped against a rock or held with tongs. Render it 4 to 5 minutes until the scored fat is brown and crisp."
  - "Lay it flat and sear both sides hard, 3 to 4 minutes each, moving it off any flare-ups."

The fat cap is the whole recipe. Scoring it lets the fat render out and crisp instead of shrinking into a rubber band that curls the steak off the grate. Standing the steak on that edge first buys the fat the extra minutes it needs, since it takes longer to break down than the meat takes to cook.

Salt goes on early so it has time to draw moisture and come back in. Pepper goes on late because it scorches over live coals and turns bitter. This is a day-one, cooler-dependent meal: it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp with ice, not in a pack.
