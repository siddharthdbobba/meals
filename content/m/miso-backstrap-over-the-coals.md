Miso is fermented soybean, and what it brings to venison is salt plus the savory depth lean game meat does not have on its own. Backstrap is nearly fat-free, which is why it goes dry and flat when you just salt and sear it. The marinade's salt pulls moisture in, and the paste itself clings where a thin liquid marinade would run off.

This one stays a cooler recipe. There is no shelf-stable version worth eating, so it belongs at a truck or a basecamp with ice, not in a pack. Wipe the marinade down before it hits the grate: honey and mirin both burn well before the middle of a 1 1/2 inch steak is done, and burnt miso is bitter.
