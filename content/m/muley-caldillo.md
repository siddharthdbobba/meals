Caldillo is a chile stew built around tough meat and time. Venison is very lean, so the browning step and the ninety-minute simmer are not optional — that is the only thing turning a shoulder roast into something you can chew. Two salsas and canned roasted chiles do the work a long aromatics prep would do at home, which is why this scales to a camp kitchen without much cutting board.

Everything but the meat and potatoes is shelf-stable, so the cooler only carries two items. The corn goes in at the end on purpose; boiled for an hour it turns to mush and gives up the sweetness that cuts the chile.
