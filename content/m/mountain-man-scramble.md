This is a lot of eggs in one pot, and the order of operations is what keeps it from turning into a wet gray mass. Sausage and bacon come out first so their rendered fat can cook the vegetables, then most of that grease gets poured off. Leave it all in and the eggs sit in a pool of it.

The temperature change between frying and baking matters. Frying heat under the pot will scorch the egg bottom before the middle sets, so move coals to the lid and let the eggs cook from above. Cheese goes on after the bake, off the heat, or it separates. Milk is the one fridge item here — powdered milk works and buys you a day.
