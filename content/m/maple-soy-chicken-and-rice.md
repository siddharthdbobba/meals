The original is a marinated grilled chicken breast, which needs a fridge, two hours, and a grill pan. None of that packs. What actually makes the dish is the glaze: maple and soy reduced until the sugar goes sticky and the salt concentrates. That part works fine in a titanium pot over a low flame, and pouch chicken is already cooked, so it only needs to get coated.

Reducing in the empty pot after the rice is the whole trick. There is almost no liquid, so it thickens in a couple of minutes and the residue on the pot walls goes into the sauce rather than into your dishwater. Watch it — two tablespoons of syrup will scorch if you walk away. The oil goes in at the end for calories, since a chicken breast and rice on their own is a light dinner by trail standards.
