This is a cook-it-down sauce, not a recipe with a finish time. The ketchup carries sugar and acid already; the vinegar and brown sugar sharpen both ends so it does not read as warm ketchup. What actually takes time is water leaving the pot, and a fire gives you that for free while you do other things.

Simmer it at the edge of the grate, not over the coals. Sugar on a hot bottom scorches fast and the burn goes through the whole pot. Skim or pour off the beef fat before the liquid goes in, or the sauce breaks and slides off the dog instead of sitting on it. This one needs a cooler for the beef and butter, so it is a car-camping dinner, not a pack-in.
