The original bakes this. On trail there is no oven, so the polenta does all the work in the pot and the cheese goes in off the heat. Instant polenta thickens in ten minutes on a low flame, which is about the limit of what a canister will happily hold at simmer.

The two dairy swaps matter. Cream cheese becomes milk powder, which carries the fat without a cooler, and crumbled gorgonzola becomes blue cheese powder, which dissolves instead of sitting in cold lumps. Dried mushrooms are the other half of the trade: their soaking water is the cooking liquid, so the mushroom flavor ends up in the grain rather than down the drain.
