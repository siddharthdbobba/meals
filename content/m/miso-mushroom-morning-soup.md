The source version wanted a cooler, a knife, and fresh eggs. None of that survives a pack. Dried shiitake do the same job better anyway, because the soaking water becomes the broth instead of getting poured off. Egg powder stands in for the soft-boiled egg and gives the pot most of its protein.

Miso goes in last, off heat. Boiling it flattens the flavor and turns it harsh. Same with the sesame oil, which is aromatic and volatile and does nothing if it sits over a flame. The greens are dried, so they hydrate in about the time it takes the mushrooms to finish.
