This started as a home oven dish and mostly survives the move outdoors, because the only real requirement is even heat from above and below. A dutch oven in coals does that. The base is ground meat and egg pressed flat, which behaves like a crust once the protein sets, and unlike dough it does not care whether your temperature was 375 or 425.

Two things go wrong. The meat renders a lot of fat during the first bake, and if you leave it in, the passata thins out and the whole slab sits in grease. Pour it off. Second, meat shrinks as it cooks, so press the base wider than you think you need. Cold wedges the next day are the real payoff here: dense, flat, and they do not get crushed in a pack.

Dietary note: the tags cover gluten and nuts only. It is meat and cheese throughout.
