This is a lunch with no stove, no water, and no pot, which is the whole point. Fat and protein carry the calories, parched corn adds the carbohydrate, and the blueberries keep it from being three textures of the same thing. Everything here is preserved by drying, so nothing needs a cooler.

The part people get wrong is the jerky. If a strip bends and shows fibers, it still holds moisture, and moisture is what turns pemmican rancid in a pack. Dry it until it snaps and grinds to powder. Tallow, not oil: it sets solid at camp temperatures and stays that way in a side pocket.
