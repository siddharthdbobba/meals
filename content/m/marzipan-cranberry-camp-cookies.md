This is a home baking recipe pushed onto coals, and the only hard part is heat control. A Dutch oven with most of the coals on the lid behaves like an oven with a top element: the tops brown, the bottoms do not scorch. Four cookies per round is the honest limit. Crowd it and they run together into one sheet.

The marzipan is the reason to bother. Grated cold, it stays in visible pockets instead of dissolving, so you get almond paste against tart cranberry rather than one flat sweet note. Browning the butter first costs five minutes and is what keeps 250 g of sugar from tasting like nothing but sugar. Butter, eggs, and marzipan all need the cooler, which is why this is a car-camping dessert and not a backpacking one.
