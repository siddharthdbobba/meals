Instant noodles usually get their flavor from one salt packet. This gets it from four things that are also food: miso, bonito, kelp, and seaweed. The result is a broth with actual depth, and about twice the protein of a plain ramen dinner, mostly from the tofu and the fish powder.

Order matters more than technique here. The mushrooms and tofu need a head start in hot water or they stay leathery, and the miso goes in after the boil because heat kills what makes it taste like miso. The seaweed is doing the job of a vegetable, not a garnish, which is why there is so much of it. Cut it back if the flavor is too strong for you.
