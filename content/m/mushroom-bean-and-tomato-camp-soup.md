The original is a fresh-mushroom soup, and fresh mushrooms are a bad trail item. Dried ones are better here anyway. Their soaking water is a concentrated stock, so it goes into the pot instead of the ground, and that single move replaces most of what a long simmer would have built.

The rest is about fat and salt. Oil-packed tomatoes bring both, plus acid to cut the broth, and the bean liquid gives the soup enough body that it eats like a meal rather than hot water with things in it. Save half the olive oil for the end: heated oil disappears into the broth, cold oil sits on top where you taste it.
