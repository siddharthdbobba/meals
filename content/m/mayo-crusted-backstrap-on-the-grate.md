Mayonnaise is not a flavor here, it is a binder and a fat source. It glues the seasoning to the meat and browns hard on the grate, which matters with venison because backstrap has almost no fat of its own to crisp. What comes off the fire has a dark crust and a still-red center.

This one needs a cooler, so it is a car camp or basecamp dinner rather than a pack-in. Pull the meat at 125°F and let it rest. Venison goes from rare to dry in about ninety seconds of extra heat, and there is no fat in the muscle to hide the mistake.
