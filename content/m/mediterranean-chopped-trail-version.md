This started as a way to use up grilled chicken from the fridge, which is not a thing you have on trail. A foil pouch of chicken does the same job at the same protein and needs no cooler, so that is the one substitution that makes the whole recipe work away from a kitchen.

Greens are the weak link. Bagged salad is a day-one food and nothing else, so this is a first-night car-camping lunch or a day-hike bowl. For anything longer, shredded cabbage is the right call: it survives being sat on, and it does not go slimy when the vinaigrette hits. Everything else here is already shelf-stable or close enough. Assemble in the bag and there is nothing to wash.
