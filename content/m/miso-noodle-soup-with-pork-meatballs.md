This is a fire-and-Dutch-oven soup, not a stove-and-freezer-bag one. It needs a quart of broth per person, which means a big pot and a real heat source. Everything except the pork and the fresh chiles is shelf-stable, so the cooler load is one package of ground meat.

Two changes from a kitchen version. Bouillon replaces boxed stock, because carrying half a gallon of liquid is absurd. Dried garlic and green onion replace fresh, which loses some sharpness but survives a week in a bin. The miso goes in off the heat: it is a live ferment and boiling it strips the flavor you carried it for.
