Nobody drinks Tang at home. In a cold camp it is sugar, vitamin C, and a reason
to finish a litre of water you would otherwise leave in the bottle, which is
the actual job.

An ounce of powder per day is nothing to carry and it makes treated water taste
like something other than treated water.
