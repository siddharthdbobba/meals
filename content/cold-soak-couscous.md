Fine couscous is the one starch that genuinely rehydrates in cold water, which is
why it anchors nearly every cold soak kit. Pearl couscous does not. Neither does
rice, and people keep trying.

The whole point is fuel: on a long trail, skipping the stove for lunch and dinner
saves more weight in canisters than most gear swaps ever will.
