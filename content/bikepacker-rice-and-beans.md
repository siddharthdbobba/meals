Dehydrated refried beans are underrated: they rehydrate instantly, they are cheap,
they are sold in most grocery stores, and they turn loose rice into something with
body. Together with instant rice this is a complete protein for about a dollar.

The crunchy topping matters more than it sounds like it should. Five days of soft
food gets old, and texture is the thing you start to miss first.
