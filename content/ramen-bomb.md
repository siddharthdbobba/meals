The Ramen Bomb is less a recipe than a physics problem: how many calories fit in
one pot with one boil and one spoon. The potato flakes are the trick. They thicken
ramen into something with body, and they carry fat far better than broth does.

Cheese and oil are where the calories actually come from. Skip them and you have
a wet, salty snack rather than a dinner.
