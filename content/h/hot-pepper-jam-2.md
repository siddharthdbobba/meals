Sugar and vinegar together are a preservative, which is the only reason a jar of cooked peppers can ride in a pack in July. The sugar ties up water the way it does in any jam, the vinegar drops the pH, and nothing much grows in that. Cook it until it sheets off the spoon and it will hold for months sealed.

This is a condiment, not a meal, and it exists because trail food is bland by design. A spoonful turns instant rice, ramen, or a tortilla with cheese into something you notice. Weighing the peppers whole and uncleaned is how the original recipe does it, so a pound of small thin-walled chiles yields less finished jam than a pound of fat ones. Fine either way; you are not canning to a shelf label.
