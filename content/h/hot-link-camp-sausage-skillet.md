Hot links are a home-prep project that pays off in camp. All the work happens at a kitchen counter days ahead: grinding, spicing, stuffing, twisting. What you carry is a frozen bundle that thaws in the cooler and needs nothing but heat and twenty minutes.

The cold matters more than the spice blend. Meat and beer both go in near freezing so the fat stays in solid pieces instead of smearing into paste, which is what makes a link juicy rather than crumbly. Over coals, keep the skillet between the links and the flame; direct fire blows the casings open and the fat you worked to keep ends up in the coals.
