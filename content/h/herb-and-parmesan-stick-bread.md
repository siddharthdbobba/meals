Stick bread is the one camp bread that needs no pot, no oven, and no lid. The dough is stiff enough to cling to a stick, which is the whole design constraint: too wet and it drips into the coals, too dry and it cracks off in pieces. Keep the spare flour handy and adjust in camp rather than trying to get it perfect at home.

Both baking powder and yeast are in the mix on purpose. The yeast does slow work while the dough rides in your pack; the baking powder covers you if it is cold and the yeast never really wakes up. Hard grated parmesan is shelf-stable and salty enough to season the bread on its own, so no butter or fresh dairy is needed. Cook over coals, not flame, or you get a black shell around raw dough.
