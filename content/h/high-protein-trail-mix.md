Trail mix earns its place by having no failure mode. No stove, no water, no waiting, and it does not care if it rides in a hip belt pocket for four days. This one leans on three nuts plus sunflower seeds and pepitas, which is where the protein comes from — dried fruit and chocolate are close to zero on that front.

The unsweetened coconut is doing more than it looks like. It is mostly fat, so it pushes the calorie density up without adding weight, and the big chips give the mix some texture against the seeds. Chocolate is the bribe: without it most people stop reaching into the bag by day two.
