Hutspot is a Dutch mash of carrot, onion, and potato, and almost all of it dries well. The potatoes become instant flakes, the vegetables go through a dehydrator for six to eight hours, and the whole dinner drops to about 6.5 ounces dry.

The order matters more than anything else here. Carrot is the slow ingredient, so it rides through the boil while the potato waits outside the pot. Flakes go in last, off heat, or they turn to paste and the carrot stays crunchy. Nutmeg and white pepper are not garnish in this dish; without them it reads as plain potato.
