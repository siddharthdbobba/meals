Canned salmon is lightweight and keeps for months, making it reliable trail protein. A honey glaze bridges the gap between fresh and canned—the heat caramelizes the honey slightly, and the pepper cuts through the richness. The foil packet seals in moisture, so the salmon stays tender and doesn't need added water or complicated cookware.

This works because you're not trying to grill a fresh fillet. You're warming seasoned salmon that's already cooked and starting from brined or oiled liquid. The heat just needs to warm it through and caramelize the glaze at the edges. Eight minutes and you're done.
