The original is a home casserole: poached chicken, a cup of mayonnaise, a can of soup, fresh celery and pepper, butter-soaked stuffing on top. Most of that does not survive a weekend without a fridge. The mayonnaise and canned soup are replaced by powdered soup mix, powdered milk, and oil, which get you the same fat and body from a bag. Dried celery and pepper flakes rehydrate in the gravy and do the aromatic work the fresh vegetables did.

The stuffing layer is the part worth protecting. Butter the crumbs off the heat and spread them dry on top, and the coals on the lid crisp them while the base bubbles underneath. Stir them in and you get mush. Coal count matters more than time here: heavy on the lid, light underneath, or the bottom burns before the top colors.
