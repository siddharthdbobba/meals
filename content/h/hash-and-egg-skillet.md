An oven casserole does not travel, so this is the same five things cooked flat in a pot instead of baked. Everything that was refrigerated becomes powder: eggs, milk, and cheese all come dried, and the sausage is the shelf-stable precooked kind. The seasoning blend is there because plain crumbles taste like nothing.

Order matters more than technique. Potatoes have to soak first or they stay gritty, and the eggs go in last over low heat because powdered egg turns rubbery fast. Pull the pot before the eggs look finished; they carry enough heat to set on their own.
