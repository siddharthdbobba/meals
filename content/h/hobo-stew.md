This is a stew that cooks itself. Everything goes in cold, gets barely covered, and sits on coals until the hardest vegetable gives. Carrots are the timer — they take the longest, so when they are soft, nothing else is undercooked.

The water level is the whole technique. Just covering means the vegetables release their own liquid into a small volume instead of thinning out into a broth, and the sausage fat floats up and stays in the pot. Add a cup too much and you get soup that tastes like water. This one needs a cooler for the sausage, so it belongs at a car camp or basecamp, not in a pack.
