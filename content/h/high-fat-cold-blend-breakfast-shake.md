The source version leans on raw egg yolks, canned coconut cream, and avocado, none of which belong in a pack. Coconut cream powder does the same job at a fraction of the weight, and cocoa powder replaces the chocolate square without melting into your gear. The raw yolks are gone for the obvious reason: no fridge, no shell eggs, no gamble.

The point of the drink is fat, not sugar. Coconut fat plus the optional oil is where most of the calories live, and the protein keeps it from acting like a snack. Mix the powder to a paste before adding the rest of the water, or you will be drinking around lumps for the next mile.
