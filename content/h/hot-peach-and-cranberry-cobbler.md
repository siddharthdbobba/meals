This is a cobbler with the baking removed. Nothing is dough here except the biscuit, which is already made, so there is no dutch oven, no coals to manage, and no way to burn the bottom of anything but syrup. What you are actually doing is building a hot fruit sauce and finding something absorbent to put it on.

The heavy syrup does two jobs. It is the sauce, and it rehydrates the cranberries as it heats, which is why they go in the pot rather than on top. Cranberries stay tart even after they plump, and that tartness is what keeps the whole bowl from tasting like straight sugar. Wet-pack fruit is heavy, so this belongs in a cooler-side kitchen or a boat, not a pack.
