This one is car camping only, and not by accident. Crescent dough and deli ham both need a cooler, so there is no honest way to make it shelf-stable without turning it into a different recipe. If you have the cooler anyway, the payoff is bread baked at camp for about twenty minutes of attention.

The pineapple is doing more than flavor. It releases steam inside the dough as it heats, which keeps the middle from going dry while the outside browns. Draining it matters for the same reason in reverse: pack wet fruit in there and the bottom of the roll never sets. The reserved juice plus mustard is the dip, so nothing goes in the fire pit.
