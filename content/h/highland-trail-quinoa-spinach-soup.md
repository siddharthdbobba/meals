Quinoa is the rare grain that cooks in a backcountry pot without a half hour of fuel. Twelve minutes of simmer plus five minutes covered gets it there, and the residual heat does the last of the work for free.

The original was a home version with fresh spinach. Dried spinach rehydrates in the broth in about two minutes and weighs a tenth of what the fresh leaves do. The oil goes in at the end because fat added to a boil mostly just films the pot; added off the heat it stays in the soup, where the calories are the point.
