Heat kills appetite before it kills anything else, and a stove makes the problem worse. This is the same bowl you would build hot, rearranged so cold water does all the work. Instant hummus powder is the load-bearing part: it hydrates in minutes at any temperature and turns loose beans and corn into something thick enough to hold on a chip.

Freeze-dried corn is the limiting ingredient on time, so give it the full soak rather than the ten minutes the beans need. Acid and oil go in at the end, both because the lime keeps its edge and because oil coats the dry powder and slows rehydration if you add it early.
