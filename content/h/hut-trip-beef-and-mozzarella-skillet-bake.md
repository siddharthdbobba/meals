AMC hut dinners are cooked from scratch by a crew hauling real food up a mountain. Nobody carrying their own pack gets to do that, so this is the same idea rebuilt from things that keep: pasta, freeze-dried beef, tomato powder, and low-moisture mozzarella instead of a refrigerated brick.

The method matters more than the ingredients. Cooking pasta in exactly enough water and never draining it means the starch stays in the pot and turns the tomato powder into an actual sauce. Add the powder while the water is boiling and you get a scorched layer on the bottom. Add it off the heat and you get sauce.
