This is a protein-heavy dinner that carries well and cooks in one pot with one boil. The dehydrated vegetables rehydrate in the pasta water, eliminating the need for extra liquid. Tuna is the cheap protein anchor; oil is where the calories come from.

The starchy water left in the pot after draining is essential. Don't drain completely, or you'll end up with dry pasta that tastes like cardboard. Leave just enough liquid so everything clings together when you stir in the tuna and oil.
