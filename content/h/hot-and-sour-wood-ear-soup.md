Wood ears are the reason this works on trail. They dry hard and come back almost exactly as they went in, with the same snap, which is more than most dried vegetables manage. The rest of the pot is a mushroom broth that hydrates in ten minutes.

The source version used a homemade dark mushroom stock and black vinegar. Both go muddy once egg hits the pot, so this uses light chicken bouillon and rice wine vinegar instead, which also happen to be the two things easiest to carry. Acid and chili go in after the heat is off. Boiling either one strips out what you brought them for.
