steps:
  - "Brine the wings: dissolve salt and sugar in 1 cup cold water, add wings, refrigerate in your cooler for 1–2 hours before leaving home or right when you arrive at camp."
  - "Pat wings bone-dry with paper towels or a clean cloth."
  - "Set your grill grate over medium-hot coals. Oil the grate to prevent sticking."
  - "Grill wings for 15–20 minutes, turning every 3–4 minutes, until the skin is golden and the meat is cooked through."
  - "While wings cook, combine butter, hot sauce, cider vinegar, honey, minced garlic, dill, lemon juice, and Tabasco in a small pot over low heat."

Grilled wings beat fried wings when you're heating over coals and fuel is limited. The brine makes cooking fast even over variable heat, and the meat stays juicy through the whole process.

The sauce is just butter and hot sauce with dill to cut the richness. This is a first-night meal—your cooler keeps everything safe for 24 hours, and having dinner sorted before fatigue sets in is worth the weight.
