This started as a home kitchen recipe using fresh halibut trim, which is not something you carry up a hill. Freeze-dried or pouched white fish gets you to the same place: protein, mild flavor, something that binds. Powdered egg replaces the fresh one and weighs almost nothing.

The whole recipe turns on how dry the fish is. Drain it further than feels right, then drain it again. Breadcrumbs can only absorb so much, and a wet mix falls apart the second you flip it. Press the patties hard and leave them alone in the pan until the crust sets.
