This is a game recipe, not a trail recipe, and it stays that way. Squirrel front legs are small, lean, and full of connective tissue, so they need either long moist heat or hot fast fat. Frying is the fast route: the seasoned flour crust protects the thin meat while the interior finishes, and ten minutes at 350F is enough to loosen it from the bone.

The glaze is a reduction, so the bourbon's alcohol cooks off and leaves the wood and sugar behind. Vinegar and lemon are load-bearing here, not garnish. Half a cup of honey plus sorghum is a lot of sugar, and without acid the whole thing coats your mouth and quits. Sauce the legs at the last second; the crust starts surrendering the moment it goes wet.
