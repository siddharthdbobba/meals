The original is a freezer meal: raw thighs, fresh peppers, and pineapple that go in a bag and come out of a slow cooker. None of that travels. What survives the swap is the sauce, which is really just soy, sugar, vinegar, garlic, and ginger, and all five of those exist as powders that weigh nothing.

Dried pineapple does more work than it looks like. It rehydrates into the acid and sweetness the dish is built on, and the water it gives back becomes the sauce. The cornstarch is the only addition to the source list, and it is there because instant rice releases very little starch of its own. Without it the sauce sits at the bottom of the pot.
