Store-bought instant oatmeal is mostly sugar and a small amount of oats. Building your own packet flips that ratio and costs about a quarter as much. The powdered milk is what makes it work off a single boil: it turns plain water into something creamy, so you do not need a stove long enough for real cooking.

Grinding half the oats is the trick that lets you use old fashioned rolled oats instead of quick oats. The ground half thickens fast and the whole half keeps some chew. Powdered fruit does more for flavor than the dried pieces do, since the pieces mostly rehydrate back into fruit while the powder spreads through the whole bowl. Start with less sugar than you think you want. You can add more; you cannot take it out.
