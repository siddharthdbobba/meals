This is home prep, not camp cooking. You dry tomatoes in October so that in July a quarter-ounce of chips can turn a pot of couscous into something that tastes like food. Fresh tomatoes are about 94 percent water, which is why they are the single worst thing to carry and one of the best things to dehydrate.

Two details decide whether it works. Scooping the seed jelly out removes most of the remaining water before the machine ever starts, and flattening the halves cuts the drying time again. Dry them until there is no give at all in the thickest part. Anything short of that molds in the jar three weeks later, usually right before the trip you dried them for.
