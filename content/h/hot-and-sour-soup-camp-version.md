This started as a home soup with fresh mushrooms, a carton of broth, and diced tofu, none of which travel. Dried mushrooms and freeze-dried tofu rehydrate in the same pot the broth is made in, so the whole thing collapses into one boil. The vinegar is the part people skimp on. Do not. Hot and sour soup is mostly acid and black pepper, and both fade when the pot cools, so go heavier than seems reasonable.

Cornstarch and egg white are the two texture moves. The starch has to be slurried in cold water first or it will seize into lumps the second it hits the pot. The egg white has to go in slowly while you stir, otherwise you get one rubbery mass instead of ribbons. Both take thirty seconds and both are the difference between soup and salty mushroom water.
