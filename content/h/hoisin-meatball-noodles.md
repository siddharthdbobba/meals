The original is a supermarket-run dinner: fresh noodles, a chilled tray of meatballs, a bag of cut vegetables. None of that keeps in a pack. Retort-pouch meatballs are the swap that makes it work, since they are fully cooked and stable at ambient temperature. Freeze-dried vegetables stand in for the stir-fry mix and rehydrate in the same water the noodles use.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Draining the noodles before the sauce goes in is the difference between stir-fry and soup, and hoisin is thick enough to coat without any liquid help. Browning the meatballs in dry oil first builds the only real depth in the dish, so do not skip straight to the sauce.
