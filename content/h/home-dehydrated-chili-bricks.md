This is not a recipe so much as a method for turning any Sunday pot of chili into a week of trail dinners. Ground beef dehydrates well because the crumbles are already small; chunk chicken, tried the same way, sits in your pot for half an hour and stays leathery. Everything here is chopped or mashed for the same reason. Surface area is the whole game.

Fat is the constraint. It does not dry, it coats the flakes, and it goes off in a warm pack, so the beef gets rinsed and the oil gets carried separately and added at the end. Weigh the batch before you split it — that number is how you know 400 ml is right rather than a guess. Add water short of that and top up. You can fix a dry pot; you cannot un-flood one.
