The original is a home stew: two pounds of chuck browned in a pan, cans of gravy, fresh carrots and potatoes. None of that carries. Freeze-dried beef and dried diced potatoes get you the same shape at a fraction of the weight, and gravy powder does what canned gravy did without the tin.

The order matters more than the recipe looks. Vegetables rehydrate first in plain broth, gravy powder goes in last and slowly, and the oil goes in off the heat. Browning fresh beef left fat in the pot; here nothing does, so you have to add it back or the stew tastes thin.
