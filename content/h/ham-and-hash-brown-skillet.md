The Dutch oven original leans on frozen potatoes, a can of soup, and half-and-half, none of which survive a day in a pack. Dehydrated hash browns and an instant potato soup packet do the same two jobs: starch and a thick, creamy base. Milk powder plus oil replaces the cream without a cooler.

The eight-minute simmer is not optional. Dried potato shreds need real time in hot water or they stay gritty in the middle, and adding the soup mix early would thicken the pot before they finish. Cheese goes in off the heat so it melts instead of splitting.
