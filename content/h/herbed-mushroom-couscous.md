Couscous rehydrates almost instantly, which is why it anchors this meal. The freeze-dried mushrooms provide real flavor and rehydrate as fast as the grain itself. The Parmesan is the calories—an ounce of cheese carries more fat than almost anything lighter, and it doesn't dissolve into broth the way salt does.

Freezer bag cooking eliminates the pot, which saves weight and means no dishes. The insulated cozy works as a thermos, keeping residual heat working while you set up or rest. Without a cozy, dinner is ready in ten minutes. With one, it's fifteen, which sometimes matters on a tired evening.
