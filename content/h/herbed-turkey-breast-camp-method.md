The source cooks this in a circulating water bath, which is the right answer at home and a nonstarter in the woods. A sealed foil packet does the same two useful things: it traps every drop of moisture and it puts a layer between the meat and direct heat. Coals instead of an immersion circulator means you lose the temperature guarantee, so a thermometer does that job.

Wild turkey breast is very lean and has almost no fat of its own. The bacon grease is not seasoning, it is the fat the bird does not have, and it bastes the meat as the packet heats. Honey helps the rub stick and gives the surface something to brown against once you open the foil. Pull it at 160F. Ten degrees past that and it turns to rope.
