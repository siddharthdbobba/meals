This is a car-camping dinner because there is no way around it. Strip steak and a butter log both need a cooler, and neither dehydrates into anything worth eating. What travels well is the butter itself: mix it at home and it holds for weeks cold, so at camp you are only managing a fire and a timer.

The compound butter does two jobs. Harissa is a dry spice blend of chiles, garlic, cumin, coriander, and caraway, and fat is what pulls those out. It also replaces a pan sauce, which you cannot make on a grate. The asparagus goes on during the rest so the fire, the grate, and the resting time all get used once.
