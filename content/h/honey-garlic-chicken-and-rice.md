The original is an Instant Pot dinner built on raw thighs and a cornstarch slurry. Neither travels, so the meat becomes freeze-dried chicken and the sauce becomes a powder you mix at home. The cornstarch still earns its place: without it the honey and soy just run to the bottom of the pot and the rice tastes like plain rice with a wet spot.

Slurry the starch in cold water before it meets heat. Dumped in dry it seizes into lumps that never dissolve. The chicken needs its own soak ahead of the rice — freeze-dried meat rehydrates slower than grain, and if you cook them together you get done rice and chewy chicken.
