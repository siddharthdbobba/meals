This is a two-minute cook with a fifteen-minute setup, and the setup is the part that matters. Scallops are mostly water. If they go on the grate wet they release steam, the ham stays limp, and you end up with something boiled in its own liquid. Dry them, wrap them tight, and the cured ham acts as a fat jacket that browns while the scallop inside cooks gently.

The lemon goes into the oil rather than onto the finished skewer. Acid on the raw surface helps the oil cling and season, and lemon juice squeezed over hot seafood at the table just runs off into the fire. This is car-camping food: scallops need a cold cooler and get eaten the first night out. Nothing here reheats well, so cook what you will finish.
