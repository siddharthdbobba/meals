This is a coal-cooking recipe, not a stove one. The whole thing hinges on rendering the bacon first and then building everything else in that fat, so the peas pick up smoke and salt from the bottom up instead of getting it dumped on at the end.

The hour of simmering is where the texture happens, and a dutch oven under coals holds a low, steady heat better than a pot on a burner does. Pull heat off the lid once it boils. If it is rattling the lid, you have too many coals and the peas will blow apart before the flavor gets anywhere. The ham and green onions go in late on purpose: the ham is already cooked and only needs warming, and the onions keep some bite.
