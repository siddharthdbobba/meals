Heat problems usually start before anyone notices them. Nausea, cramps, a thirst that a few gulps will not touch, or a kid who suddenly says they are done playing are the signs worth watching for. This drink exists so there is something in the pack that is easy to keep drinking once those show up, and easier still to drink before they do.

Sugar and salt are both doing work. Sodium is what sweat takes, and plain water alone dilutes what is left. A little sugar helps the sodium and the water move out of the gut faster, and the lemon powder makes it taste like something rather than like a saline drip. Keep it cool rather than iced. Very cold drinks get sipped, and sipping is how people end up short a liter by the end of the afternoon.
