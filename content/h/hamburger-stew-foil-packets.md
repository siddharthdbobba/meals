This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Raw ground beef and a pound of fresh vegetables need a cooler, so there is no honest way to make it a backpacking meal. What it does well is turn one fire and four sheets of foil into four dinners with no pot to scrub.

The soup mix is doing double duty. It seasons the beef, and its starch and salt pull enough liquid out of the onions and carrots to make gravy inside the packet. No water gets added; the vegetables supply it. That is also why the seal matters more than the heat: a packet that vents steam bakes instead of stewing, and the potatoes come out chalky.
