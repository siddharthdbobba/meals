Drying is the least fussy way to keep a mushroom haul, and for backpacking it is the only one that matters: a pound of fresh mushrooms comes down to about an ounce that stores for years at room temperature. The soaking water is not waste. It is concentrated broth, and pouring it out throws away most of what you dried the mushrooms for.

Two things ruin a batch. Dirt that was not brushed off in the field never rehydrates clean, and mushrooms packed shoulder to shoulder on a tray get warm before they get dry, which is decomposition rather than dehydration. Space them out, dry them the day you pick them, and let them cool before sealing the jar.
