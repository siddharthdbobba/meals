This started as a kitchen recipe with a frying pan and a saucepan. It collapses into one pot with no real loss: brown the lamb first, pull it out, build the broth in the same pot, and the fond from the meat ends up in the soup where you want it anyway.

The order matters more than the technique. Hoisin burns fast over camp heat, so it goes in at the very end of the searing, off the hottest part of the flame. Stock concentrate replaces carrying liquid stock, which is most of the weight of the original and none of the flavor. Ginger and chili do the actual warming work after a cold walk, and the salt in the stock is why this beats a hot drink for recovery.
