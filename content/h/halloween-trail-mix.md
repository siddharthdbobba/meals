This is a sugar-and-starch snack, not a meal, and it works on trail for exactly one reason: the pieces are all about the same size and none of them need water. Cereal and Bugles are mostly air, so a cup weighs almost nothing while carrying real calories. That is a good ratio for a pocket snack and a bad one for pack volume, which is why you portion it small.

No trail adaptation was needed beyond the packing. It is already shelf-stable and no-cook. The only real failure mode is heat: the mellowcreme pumpkins soften and glue everything together, so keep it out of direct sun and eat it in the first week. Nothing here is gluten-free, dairy-free, or nut-free, so read the labels before handing a bag to someone else.
