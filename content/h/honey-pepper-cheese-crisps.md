The original was baked and frozen first, neither of which happens at a campsite. Flattening the cheese into discs and frying them in a pan does the same job in eight minutes: the panko crust sets before the inside runs, which is the whole trick. Freezing was only there to buy time in a 475-degree oven, and a pan over a grate gives you more control than that anyway.

Goat cheese needs a cooler, so this is a car-camping or paddle recipe and not a backpacking one. The honey is not decoration. It cuts the tang of the cheese and the pepper cuts the honey, and without both you have fried cheese and nothing else.
