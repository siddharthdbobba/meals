This is a home kitchen recipe pulled toward a camp stove. The original leans on fresh basil, oregano, and thyme, which wilt to slime in a cooler by day two. Dried herbs in the crumb mix do the same job and weigh nothing. Fresh grated parmesan becomes the shelf-stable shaker kind, which browns just as well inside a crust.

The pork has to be thin. Pounding the cutlets at home means they cook through in under five minutes a side, so you are not standing over a dark stove guessing at doneness. Keep the oil coming between batches. Bread crumbs in a dry pan go from pale to black with nothing in between, and there is no second chance once the crust is gone.
