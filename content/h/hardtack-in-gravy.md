Hardtack is flour, water, and salt baked until it stops being bread. On its own it is a jaw exercise. Gravy is what makes it food: the hot liquid gets into the biscuit and turns it back into something you can chew, and the starch holds the whole thing together instead of leaving crumbs in a puddle.

The gravy is doing double duty as flavor and calories. Bouillon and onion carry the savory end, the mustard and nutmeg keep it from tasting like plain salt, and the fat is where most of the energy comes from. Skip the ghee and you have soggy crackers. Total dry weight is around four ounces, which is the point of this one.
