steps:
  - "Trim the fat and the tough collar off the top of the heart. Split it down one side and open it flat. Cut out the pale fibers running through the middle. Skipping this is why people call heart chewy."

  - "Get the skillet hot over the fire or the grate. Oil, then the heart strips in one layer. Brown the outsides and pull them out. They finish later, so undercooking here is correct."

Heart is muscle, not organ meat in the liver sense, and it tastes close to a steak off the same animal. The two things that ruin it are leaving the internal fibers in and cooking it past medium. Trim carefully, sear fast, and finish it in the gravy off direct heat.

This is a first-night camp meal because the heart should be cooked fresh and the whole thing needs a cooler. Everything else in the pan is forgiving: onions and mushrooms travel fine unrefrigerated for a couple of days, gravy mix is a dry packet, and boxed stock does the work of a sauce you would otherwise have to build. One skillet, one fire, four sandwiches.
