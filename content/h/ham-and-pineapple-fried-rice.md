This started as a way to use up a frozen Easter ham and a discount can of pineapple, which is a fine reason to cook but not a thing you can carry. The trail version keeps the working parts: salt, sweet, fat, and a starch that soaks all three up. Dried pineapple replaces the can, a foil pouch replaces the freezer, and instant rice replaces the leftover rice that made the original a fried rice in the first place.

Nothing here is actually fried. The oil goes in at the end instead, off the heat, where it coats the rice and does the same job for a fraction of the fuel. Chop the pineapple small before you leave home; whole rings sit in the pot like rubber and pull water away from the rice.
