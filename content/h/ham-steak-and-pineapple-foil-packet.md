The original is an oven dish, which is a problem exactly once: nothing here needs an oven. Ham steak is already fully cooked, so the fire is only warming it through and reducing the pineapple juice into a glaze. That works as well over coals in a sealed foil packet as it does at 350.

The pineapple is not garnish. Its juice, plus the brown sugar, is the only liquid in the packet, and it keeps the ham from drying out while the acid cuts a very salty cut of meat. Mustard on the side does the same job from the other direction. Keep the packet off open flame — sugar burns long before ham is hot.
