This is a first-night meal and it is built like one. The pasta is boiled at home, so at camp you are running one pot and one long simmer instead of juggling two burners and a colander in the dark. Frozen ground chuck rides in the cooler as a block of ice that turns into dinner.

Three tomato products sounds like overkill, but each does a different job: sauce for volume, puree for body, paste for the concentrated flavor that a 30-minute camp simmer would not otherwise build. Keep it off direct flame while it reduces, or the paste scorches to the pot and you taste it in every bowl. It does not pack down for backpacking, and it does not want to.
