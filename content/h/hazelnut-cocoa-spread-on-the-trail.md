Chocolate hazelnut spread runs about 200 calories per 2 tablespoons, roughly 55 percent of that from fat and the rest from sugar. That mix is the reason it works on trail: the sugar moves fast and the fat keeps working an hour later. Olive oil is denser at around 240 per 2 tablespoons, but it is pure fat and you cannot eat it off a spoon.

Protein is the weak spot, about 2 grams per serving against 7 or 8 for the same volume of peanut butter. Treat it as a calorie and morale item, not a protein source, and do not carry both jars unless the trip is short. Pair it with the tortilla or a bar so you are getting some starch behind it. The spread is shelf-stable in the heat and does not separate the way natural nut butters do.
