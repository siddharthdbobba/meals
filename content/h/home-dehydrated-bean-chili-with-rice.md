The whole point of drying your own is control. Store meals are built around a sodium number that has nothing to do with you, and they cost four dollars an ounce. A pot of chili you already made, spread thin on trays overnight, becomes the same thing for the price of the beans.

Fat is the one thing that will not go on the tray. Oil does not dry, it goes rancid in the bag and takes the rest of the meal with it, so the chili gets dried lean and the calories go back in at camp from a dropper bottle. The pineapple is there for acid and sugar against a long stretch of salty dinners, and it rehydrates at roughly the same rate as the corn.
