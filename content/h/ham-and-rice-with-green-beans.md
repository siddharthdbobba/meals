This started as a budget grocery list, not a trail meal: ham, brown rice, canned green beans, carrots, edamame, bouillon. The bones are good — cheap protein, cheap starch, cheap vegetables — but cans and refrigerated ham do not go in a pack. Dried vegetables and freeze-dried ham carry the same meal at a fifth of the weight, and instant rice replaces the forty-minute simmer regular brown rice demands.

The bouillon cube is doing most of the flavor work, so it goes in the water first rather than getting sprinkled on at the end. The off-heat soak is what makes this a one-fuel-burst meal: the vegetables and rice finish in the residual heat instead of on the stove. Skip the oil and the calorie count drops by about a hundred and twenty, which matters more than the taste does.
