Kettle corn burns for one reason: sugar hits the pot floor and stays there. The fix is mechanical, not culinary. Keep the pot moving over the flame the whole time and the sugar caramelizes on the kernels instead of the metal. A wide pot with a real lid works far better than a tall narrow mug.

Butter and a stovetop popper do not travel, so ghee replaces the butter and your cook pot replaces the popper. Ghee has no milk solids left to spoil, which is why it sits in a pack for a week without complaint. The hot sauce goes in the fat rather than straight on the popcorn, because vinegar alone makes it soggy while fat spreads it evenly and drives the heat into every piece.
