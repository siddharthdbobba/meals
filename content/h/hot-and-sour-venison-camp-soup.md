The original is a two-hour braise built on searing a whole chunk of venison. You cannot do that in a pot on a stone, so the browning moves home: sear the meat in one piece, shred it, dry it, and carry the flavor with you. Rehydrating shredded meat takes half an hour, not ninety minutes.

Mushrooms do the thickening here. Honey mushrooms give a broth real body without flour, and dried ones give most of it back once they swell. The vinegar is not optional. Cabbage soup without acid tastes like nothing at 8,000 feet, and adding it at the end, in stages, is the only way to land it.
