The original is a muffin tin recipe: tortilla quarters pressed into cups, beans and salsa spooned in, an egg cracked over each, half an hour in the oven. None of that survives a stove and one pot, so the shape changes and the flavors stay. Torn tortillas do the same job loose that they did as cups, soaking up bean and salsa liquid instead of holding it.

Order matters more than technique. Beans first, because they rehydrate off the heat while you do something else; eggs last and low, because powdered eggs go rubbery faster than fresh ones. Adding the tortillas at the end keeps them chewy rather than dissolving them into the beans.
