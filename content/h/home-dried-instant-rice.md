Rice is cheap, keeps forever, and takes twenty-five minutes of simmering that a backpacking stove cannot afford. Drying cooked rice moves that fuel cost into your kitchen. What you carry is already cooked, so the pot only has to rehydrate it, and the stove can go out the moment the water boils.

The safety part is not optional. Uncooked rice carries Bacillus cereus spores that survive the pot, and they multiply fast in rice sitting at room temperature. Load the trays while the rice is still steaming and dry at 150°F or higher, so it never idles in that window. Dry until the grains are genuinely hard, not just cool to the touch. Any soft grain is moisture that will spoil the bag.
