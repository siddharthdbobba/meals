The order matters more than the ingredients. Browning the ham and softening the vegetables before the eggs go in builds a savory base that raw ingredients cooked together never develop. It also drives off water, which is the difference between a frittata and scrambled eggs sitting in a puddle.

The trail version swaps fresh eggs for powdered and fresh vegetables for dried, so nothing needs a cooler and nothing breaks in your pack. Canned ham is already the shelf-stable choice, so it carries over unchanged. Rehydrate the vegetables first and drain them properly; if you dump them in wet, the pan never gets hot enough to brown anything. A wide pan beats a deep pot here, since a thick frittata sets on top long before the middle cooks.
