This is the meal for the night the driving ran long. Instant rice is the whole point: it rehydrates off residual heat, so the burner runs for about three minutes total and the pot does the rest under a lid while you deal with camp.

The oil is not optional. Bouillon and dried vegetables give it flavor but almost no calories, and rice alone leaves you hungry at midnight. Cooking the aromatics in the boiling water rather than adding them at the end is what keeps the dried onion from staying crunchy.
