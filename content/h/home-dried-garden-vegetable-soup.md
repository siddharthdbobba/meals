This is what a spring garden looks like after it goes through a dehydrator. Cabbage, carrots, onions, and hardy greens all dry well and rehydrate fast, which makes them the useful ones to grow if some of the harvest is meant to end up in a pack. Tomatoes and lettuce do not survive the process in any form worth carrying.

The potato flakes are the structural part. Dried vegetables alone give you a thin, weak broth with things floating in it, and no real calories. Flakes turn it into a stew, and stew carries oil. The oil is where most of the 540 calories actually come from, so do not leave it home.
