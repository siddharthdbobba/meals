The original is a slow cooker recipe: drumsticks braised for hours in soy, honey, and broth, then grilled for crust. None of that survives a stove and one pot, so what carries over is the sauce. Soy, honey, ginger, garlic, and sesame oil are the whole flavor, and every one of them travels dry or in a tube.

Cornstarch is worth the teaspoon. Without it the sauce sits at the bottom of the pot and the rice on top stays plain. Add it after the rice has absorbed most of the water, while the pot is still hot, and it thickens what's left into a glaze instead of a puddle. Bone-in drumsticks become freeze-dried chicken here, which loses the fat but keeps the protein; the sesame oil puts some of the calories back.
