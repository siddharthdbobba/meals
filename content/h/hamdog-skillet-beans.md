This is a group-feeding recipe pretending to be a skillet dinner. One pound of beef alone would not feed eight people; the hot dogs and the beans are what stretch it, and both arrive already cooked, so the only real cooking is browning the meat. Everything after that is just getting the pan hot.

The bean sauce is doing the binding, which is why the can goes in undrained. Drain it and you get dry meat with beans rattling around in it. Bread is not a side here either — it is the plate, and it is how the skillet gets most of the way to clean before you touch it with water.
