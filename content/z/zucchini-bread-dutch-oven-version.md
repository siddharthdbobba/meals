Home zucchini bread runs on two things a campsite does not have: a grated fresh squash and a thermostat. Dried shredded zucchini fixes the first. It weighs almost nothing, and when you soak it the water it gives back is the same water the batter needed anyway, so the soaking liquid goes into the bowl instead of down the ground. Powdered egg handles the second problem, which is that three eggs in a cooler is three eggs you will eventually sit on.

The coal count is the whole recipe. Sixteen on top and eight underneath puts most of the heat above the batter, which is what a quick bread wants, and it keeps the bottom from scorching before the center sets. Rotating the oven and lid in opposite directions cancels out the hot spot in your fire. The oil is not optional richness here, it is what keeps the loaf edible on day three.
