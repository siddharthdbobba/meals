Zucchini has almost no flavor of its own, which is the whole point. Cooked slowly in sugar and acid it takes on the lemon and vanilla and ends up tasting like canned pineapple. The texture does the same trick: the cubes go translucent and hold their shape instead of collapsing.

This is a preserving recipe stripped of the preserving. No jars, no sterilizing, no water bath. You are making one pot of jam to eat over a day or two, so it keeps in the cooler rather than on a shelf. The 20-minute rest before the burner is the step people skip and shouldn't. Without it you will add water, and water is what stands between you and a set.
