This is a braise pretending to be a fry. The chops get seared hot for crust, then the heat moves to the lid so the sauce reduces around them instead of boiling underneath. That coal shuffle between the two steps is the whole recipe. Skip it and you get chops that are either raw in the middle or dry through.

Honey and soy do the same job here that they do in a glaze: sugar to catch color, salt to season the meat past the surface. The chili and curry powder are there for warmth, not heat. The bacon fat is the cooking medium, so pour off the excess but not all of it. This one stays a car-camping recipe. Six pork chops and a cooler do not go in a pack, and there is no honest shelf-stable version of it.
