Camp baking fails in two places: the fat and the eggs. Both want a cooler, and a cooler on day three is a warm box of water. Shortening holds its shape at 90 degrees and creams the same way butter does, and powdered egg rehydrates in a minute. The dough that comes out is slightly less rich and slightly more forgiving, which is the correct trade when your oven temperature is a guess.

The rocks under the pie tin are the part people skip. Set a tin flat on the dutch oven floor and the bottoms are black before the middles set. Raise it an inch and the heat circulates. Rotating oven and lid in opposite directions cancels out the hot spot that every coal bed has.
