  - "Get a fire going early. You want a bed of coals, not flames, and it takes 30 to 40 minutes to get there."

  - "Set the Dutch oven over coals, add the oil, and brown the roast hard on every side. Six or seven minutes total. Do not skip this; it is where the flavor in the broth comes from."

This is a home pot roast moved outdoors with almost nothing changed, because almost nothing needed changing. A Dutch oven buried in coals holds temperature better than most people expect, and the sealed lid means the roast braises in its own steam rather than drying out. The lean round cut is the point: it is cheap, it packs dense protein, and ninety slow minutes turns the connective tissue soft. Cook it fast and it is shoe leather.

The heat management is the only real skill. Coals under the oven cook the bottom, coals on the lid cook the top, and if you leave them alone the side facing the fire scorches. Rotating the oven and the lid in opposite directions every fifteen minutes is what keeps it even. Everything else is chopping. This one needs a cooler for the meat, so it is a car-camping or basecamp meal, not something you carry in.
