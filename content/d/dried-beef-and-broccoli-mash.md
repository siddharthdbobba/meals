Meat and vegetables need a long soak. Potato flakes need about ten seconds. Doing them together gives you either raw broccoli or potato glue, so the beef and vegetables get the full rehydration window first and the flakes go in at the end to thicken whatever water is left.

Dehydrate the ground beef lean: 5% fat, cooked with onion and garlic, dried at 63°C for seven to eleven hours. Fat is what turns dried meat rancid in storage, and there is no fixing it in the field. The butter powder puts the fat back at camp, where it cannot spoil first.
