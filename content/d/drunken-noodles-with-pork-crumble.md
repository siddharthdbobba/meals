The original is a wok dish: high heat, hard sear, everything moving. None of that survives a backpacking stove, so this version trades the sear for a soak. Pork dried at home and rehydrated for twenty minutes gets close enough, and the sauce carries most of the flavor anyway.

Sugar is not optional. Drunken noodles run sweet against the salt of the soy and fish sauce, and without it the whole pot tastes flat and briny. Dried basil is a real downgrade from a cup of fresh leaf, but adding it off the heat under a lid gets more out of it than boiling it would. Note this is not vegetarian or gluten-free: pork, fish sauce, and oyster sauce all stay in, and soy sauce contains wheat.
