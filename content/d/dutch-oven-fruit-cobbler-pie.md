Biscuit mix is the whole reason this works in a camp kitchen. The leavening and fat are already in the powder, so the only wet ingredient is milk, and powdered milk covers that with no cooler. Reserve a half cup of the mix instead of dumping all three in at once. Humidity changes how much liquid the dough wants, and dry dough you can fix is easier than wet dough you cannot.

The canned syrup is not a garnish. Poured over the top crust it soaks in and browns, which is what keeps a plain biscuit lid from baking into a cracker. Coal placement matters more than time here. Most of the heat belongs on the lid, or the bottom crust burns before the top sets.
