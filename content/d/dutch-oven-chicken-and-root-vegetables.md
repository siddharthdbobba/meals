This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Eight chicken breasts and half an inch of frying oil do not belong in a pack, and there is no shelf-stable version of raw chicken. If you are walking to camp, use the canned-chicken variation and treat the rest as a braise.

The flour dredge does two jobs. It browns into a crust in the hot oil, and what falls off thickens the small amount of water in the bottom into a gravy over the hour. That water level is the whole recipe. Too much and the chicken poaches; dry and the flour scorches onto the cast iron. A quarter inch, checked every twenty minutes, is the target.
