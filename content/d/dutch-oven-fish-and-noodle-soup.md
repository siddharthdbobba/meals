This is a cooler-dependent recipe and it does not pretend otherwise. Frozen fish and frozen vegetables are the whole point: they ride as ice on the drive in, and they go into the pot straight from the cooler on night one. Cook it the first evening out.

The order matters more than anything else here. Fish first, in a low boil that will not tear the cubes apart. Noodles second, because they need only five minutes and turn to mush if they sit. Vegetables last, since they are already cooked and only need to thaw and warm. Reverse any of those and you get shredded fish in noodle sludge.
