Condensed soup is the whole engine here. Undiluted, it is thick enough to hold rice in suspension so the grains cook evenly instead of settling into a scorched layer on the bottom. Three different cans is not fussiness, it is depth: mushroom brings the body, celery the vegetal note, chicken the salt. The onion soup mix on top seasons downward as it melts.

This one stays a car-camping recipe. Cans are heavy and raw chicken needs a cooler, so there is no honest way to shrink it into a pack. What you get for that weight is eight servings out of one pot and no strainer, no second burner, no dishes beyond the oven. Watch the water, not the clock. Rice absorbs at its own pace and a dry pot goes from fine to burnt in about ten minutes.
