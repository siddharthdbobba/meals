This is a car-camping recipe, not a trail one. Five kinds of protein and an hour of simmering will not happen out of a titanium pot, so the pot stays a Dutch oven and the cooler does the work. The substitutions are all about what survives two days without power: canned crab, canned chicken, pouched ham, jarred oysters. Only the vegetables are fresh, and celery and peppers are the hardiest ones there are.

The okra is structural. Gumbo without a roux needs something to give the broth body, and okra releases enough to thicken four cups of water on its own if you cook it low and slow before the liquid goes in. Rushing that 15 minutes leaves you with soup. The seafood goes in last and short — twenty minutes is already generous for shrimp and oysters that came out of a can cooked.
