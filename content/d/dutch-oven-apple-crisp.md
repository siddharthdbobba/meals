Coal placement is the whole recipe. Most of the heat goes on the lid, not underneath, because a crisp browns from above and the bottom crust will scorch if you stack coals under it. Eight below and seventeen on top holds a 12-inch oven near 350 for about 45 minutes without touching it.

The oat layer does double duty as base and topping, which is why there is no separate pastry to make in the dirt. Pressing half of it up the sides keeps the apple juice off the cast iron, so cleanup is a scrape rather than a soak. Ghee swaps in for butter at the same volume and skips the cooler entirely.
