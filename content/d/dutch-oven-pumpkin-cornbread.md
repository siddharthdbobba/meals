The original is a filled muffin baked in a muffin pan with cream cheese, fresh milk, and eggs. None of that travels. Powdered milk and powdered egg do the same job out of a bag, and one dutch oven replaces twelve muffin cups, so there is a single pot to scrape instead of a tray to scrub in cold water.

Canned pumpkin is the concession. It is heavy and wet, but it is also the fat-free moisture that keeps a cornbread mix from baking into a brick, and dried pumpkin powder does not rehydrate into anything close. Coals on the lid matter more than coals underneath: heat from below alone gives you a burnt bottom and raw middle.
