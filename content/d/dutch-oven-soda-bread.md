Soda bread does not need yeast, which means it does not need warmth, time, or a place to rise. Baking soda plus acid makes the gas the instant they meet. That is the whole trick, and it is why this is one of the few breads worth attempting in a fire ring.

The original calls for fresh milk. Powdered milk and water does the same job and survives a hot car, so that is the swap here. The vinegar sours it on contact and gives the soda something to react with. Do not mix the wet into the dry until the coals are ready, because the rise starts immediately and waiting costs you loft. The 10-minute knead is not optional either. Whole wheat is most of this loaf, and without the gluten worked up it bakes into a brick.
