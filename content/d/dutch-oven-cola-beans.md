This is a Scout dutch oven recipe with the fridge taken out of it. Fresh onion, peppers, tomatoes, and mushrooms all became dried or canned, which means the whole meal rides in a bin and survives a hot car. Summer sausage does the pork work that bacon did, and it keeps unopened at ambient temperature.

The cola is not a gimmick. It boils down over forty minutes into a thin syrup that thickens the sauce and adds acid, so the beans get a glaze instead of staying soupy. The tomato paste holds the rest together. Drain the pineapple properly, or the extra syrup pushes the whole pot past sweet and into candy.
