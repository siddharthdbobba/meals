The pie tin is the whole trick. Dough laid straight into a dutch oven sticks and burns, and scraping it out afterward ruins the evening. In the tin, on foil wads, the crust bakes in trapped air instead of frying against cast iron. Cleanup is the tin, not the oven.

Coal placement matters more than the recipe does. Most of the heat belongs on the lid, because pizza cooks from the top down; too much underneath gives you a black bottom and raw cheese. Turning the oven and lid in opposite directions cancels out the hot spot that every fire has.
