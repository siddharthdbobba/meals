Zucchini is 95 percent water, which makes it useless in a pack and ideal in a dehydrator. Sliced thin and dried for five or six hours it turns into brittle chips that weigh nothing and come back to something like texture in boiling water. If you do not have a dehydrator, buy dried vegetables rather than fighting your oven for six hours.

The sauce is milk powder plus a cream cheese wedge. Milk powder alone goes thin and chalky. The cream cheese carries fat and holds the whole thing together, and it is stable at ambient temperature for the length of most trips. Stir it in off the heat so it emulsifies instead of breaking.
