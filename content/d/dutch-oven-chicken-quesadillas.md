This is a scout troop recipe and it stays one: it feeds six off two cans and a bag of cheese, and nobody has to touch raw meat. Canned chicken is already cooked, so the pot step is only about getting the chili powder and garlic to bloom into the canning liquid. Skip it and the spice sits on the outside of the shreds instead of through them.

The foil is the whole trick. Building each quesadilla on its own square means you can stack two in the oven and pull them out by the corners without reaching into a 400-degree pot. It also means the dutch oven comes home with nothing baked onto it. Cheese is the one thing here that needs a cooler, which is why this is a car-camping meal and not a backpacking one.
