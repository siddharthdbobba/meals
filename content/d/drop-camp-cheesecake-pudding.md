Instant pudding sets by chemistry, not cold. The modified starch and phosphate salts in the mix grab calcium from the milk and thicken it at ambient temperature, which is why this works with cold creek water and no cooler. Whole milk powder matters more than it looks: nonfat sets thin and chalky, and the fat is also where half the calories live.

The berries go in first with a splash of water because dried fruit dropped into pudding stays leathery and pulls moisture out of the set. Kneading through the bag instead of stirring keeps the whole thing to one dish you can burn or pack out. Skip the graham crumbs until the end or they turn to paste.
