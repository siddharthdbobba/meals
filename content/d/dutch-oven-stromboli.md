This is car-camping food, not pack food. Everything in it needs a cooler, so cook it the first night out while the ice is still solid.

The order of operations is the whole recipe. You fry bacon hot, then you bake bread at a lower temperature in the same pot, and cast iron does not cool down on request. Pull the oven off the coals and let it sit while you build the loaf. That gap is not wasted time, it is what keeps the dough from scorching the second it lands. Rolling the loaf into a ring means it fits the round oven without a coiled end pressing on the wall, and the half-inch bare edge gives the seam something to grab.
