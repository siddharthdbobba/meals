Raw shredded potato oxidizes and goes grey-black in a dehydrator unless you blanch or acidify it first. The shortcut is to skip raw entirely: frozen hash brown patties are already cooked and already treated, so they dry clean and rehydrate in about five minutes with boiling water. Break them up before drying, then crumble them again once brittle. Small pieces take on water evenly; a dried slab stays hard in the middle.

Gravy mix does the work that eggs and butter would do at home. It is powdered, shelf-stable, and thickens on its own heat, which turns dry potato crumbs into an actual meal instead of damp shreds. The beef is there for protein and for salt; if you use chipped beef, soak it first or the gravy will be inedible.
