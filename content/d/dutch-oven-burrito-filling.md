This is a large-group dutch oven dinner and it stays that way here, because the design only works at volume: two pounds of meat and three cups of rice is what fills twenty tortillas. The rice goes in last and finishes off the broth already in the pot, so there is no draining step and no second vessel. Get that ratio wrong and you either have soup or crunchy rice, which is why the recipe tells you to check at five minutes and add water rather than guessing up front.

The tortilla trick at the end is the part worth keeping. Steam coming off the filling warms the whole stack under the lid, which means no griddle, no foil, and no cold tortillas cracking as people roll them. Everything but the meat and cheese is shelf-stable, so the cooler only has to hold two items.
