This is a home cookie moved onto coals. Butter and a fresh egg are the two things that will not ride in a pack, so shortening handles the fat and powdered egg handles the bind. Neither one misses much here, because the pumpkin is already doing the work of keeping the crumb wet.

Pumpkin is mostly water, which is why these bake soft and stay soft for two days in a bag. It also means they will not crisp, so stop at 20 minutes and let the residual heat finish them. Coal count matters more than time: too many underneath and the bottoms go dark before the middles set.
