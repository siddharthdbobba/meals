Camp biscuits live or die on heat distribution. Coals go mostly on the lid — 17 up top against 8 below — because these bake from above, and a Dutch oven bottom sitting in a coal bed will scorch long before the tops color. The turning is the other half. Rotating oven and lid in opposite directions keeps any one hot spot from parking under the same biscuit for fifteen minutes.

The source used fresh buttermilk and a beaten egg, which is a refrigerator recipe. Powdered buttermilk and powdered egg hydrate to the same thing and ride in the dry bag, so the only cold-sensitive item left is the butter. Club soda is doing a small job: the carbonation adds lift the mix's own leavening doesn't quite provide. If the can is a nuisance, water is fine.
