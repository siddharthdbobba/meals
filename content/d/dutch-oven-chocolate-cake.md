Camp baking fails in two places: heat control and dairy. The heat is solved by counting coals rather than guessing, and by keeping most of them on the lid, since a Dutch oven browns from the top and burns from the bottom. Rotating the pot and lid in opposite directions evens out the hot spots the fire leaves.

The dairy is solved with powder. Buttermilk, eggs, and cream all come as shelf-stable powders that rehydrate well enough for a batter and a topping, so nothing in this needs a cooler. The hot coffee is not a flavor gimmick — it blooms the cocoa and thins the batter so it bakes through in the middle before the edges set hard. This is a 12-serving dessert for a group at a car campsite; it does not scale down to one pot of anything useful.
