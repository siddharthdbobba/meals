Fresh berries are mostly water, which is the wrong thing to haul to a campsite. Dried fruit and a measured pour of water get you to the same place: the berries drink the water back and the cornstarch turns what is left into filling. Citric acid replaces the lemon juice, and milk powder plus shortening replaces the heavy cream in the biscuit, so nothing here needs a cooler.

The coals are the part people get wrong. A dutch oven sitting in a fire bakes the bottom and steams the top, and you end up with pale wet dough over burnt jam. Most of the heat goes on the lid. Give the biscuits their twenty minutes without lifting it to check.
