The original is a home kitchen trick: bake the batter all at once in a muffin tin instead of standing at the stove flipping two pancakes at a time. A dutch oven does the same job with coals, and it lets you feed four people from one vessel and one bake.

Three things had to change for the trail. Fresh bananas became dried banana chips rehydrated in a little water, which gives back the moisture and sweetness they were there for. Fresh eggs became powdered egg, mixed with cold water before the dry goods so it has a chance to dissolve. The mix itself is already shelf-stable and carries most of the protein. Foil in the bottom means the pan comes out clean and the squares lift out whole.
