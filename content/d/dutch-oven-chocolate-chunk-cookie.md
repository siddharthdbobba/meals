The original is a home recipe with a stick of butter and two cold eggs in it. Neither survives a cooler-free weekend, so the butter becomes coconut oil and the eggs become egg powder rehydrated in the pot. Coconut oil is nearly all fat where butter is about 15 percent water, so the dough runs a little richer and spreads a little less. That is fine here.

Heat placement is the whole game. Most of the coals go on the lid, because a dutch oven sitting in a bed of embers scorches the bottom long before the middle sets. Pull it while the center still looks wet. Carryover heat in that much cast iron will finish the job while you argue about who gets the edges.
