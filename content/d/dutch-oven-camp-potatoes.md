This is a side dish that eats like a dinner, which is why it earns the fuel. The bacon grease is the cooking medium. Nothing else in the pot brings fat, and without it the potatoes stick and scorch before the middle softens.

The frozen peas do double duty. They go in still frozen and release enough water as they thaw to steam the potatoes, so no liquid gets added. Thin slices matter more than anything else here. Coal heat is uneven and 35 minutes is not long, so thickness decides whether this works.
