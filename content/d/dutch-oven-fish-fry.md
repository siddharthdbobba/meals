This one does not travel. Two cups of oil and a cooler full of fish is a car-camping dinner, and there is no shelf-stable version worth eating. Cook it the first night out, while the ice is still solid.

The flour-then-rest step is the part people skip and regret. Dry fish sheds batter into the oil and you end up with bare fillets and a pot of scorched crumbs. Five minutes on a paper towel lets the flour pull surface moisture and grip. The club soda does the other half: the bubbles keep the pancake batter loose and open instead of dense, and beer works for the same reason. Keep the oil near 400°F. Cooler than that and the crust drinks oil rather than sealing.
