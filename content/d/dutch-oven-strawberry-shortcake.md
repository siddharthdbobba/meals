Shortcake is a biscuit that happens to be sweet, which makes it one of the few desserts that survives the trip to a fire pit intact. The fresh parts are the problem, not the baking. Freeze-dried strawberries with sugar and a little acid poured over them make their own syrup in about twenty minutes, and that syrup is what soaks into the split biscuit. Butter, milk, cream, and eggs all come out of bags here: shortening for the fat, milk powder and powdered egg in the dry mix, and instant topping whisked with cold water instead of whipping heavy cream.

Lid heat is what browns the top, so most of your coals belong up there. Rotating the oven and the lid in opposite directions is the whole technique for avoiding a scorched patch where the fire runs hot. Keep the dough shaggy. Overworked biscuit dough bakes dense, and a dense shortcake will not take on syrup.
