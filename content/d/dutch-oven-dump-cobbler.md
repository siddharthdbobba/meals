The no-stir rule is the recipe. Left alone, the dry mix sits on top and drinks the soda from above and the fruit juice from below, so it bakes into a crust rather than dissolving into batter. Stir it and you get one gluey layer that never sets.

The soda is carbonation and sugar doing the job of eggs and milk, which is why this works with no cooler. Margarine over butter for the same reason. The foil liner means the cleanup is pulling out a sheet and burning it down, not scrubbing caramelized fruit off cast iron in the dark.
