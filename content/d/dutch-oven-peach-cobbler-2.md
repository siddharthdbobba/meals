The original is a slow cooker recipe built on fresh peaches and cold butter. Neither survives a trip, so this version starts from dried peaches and rebuilds their juice with water and sugar over the fire. Ten minutes of simmering does what maceration does at home: it pulls sugar into the fruit and leaves syrup behind for the topping to sit on.

The dry mix goes on unstirred on purpose. Flour that stays dry on top bakes into a crust while the underside soaks up syrup, which is the whole texture of a cobbler. Butter on top melts down through it. Ghee is the shelf-stable swap and works the same, just without the milk solids browning.
