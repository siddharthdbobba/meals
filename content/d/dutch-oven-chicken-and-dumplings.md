Everything here comes off a shelf, which is the point. Canned chicken and a soup envelope give you a stew base with no cooler and no knife work, and the biscuit mix turns into dumplings with nothing but water.

The half-water rule matters. Soup mix is written for a thin broth, and dumplings release starch and steam as they cook, so a full pot of water leaves you eating soup with lumps in it. Cut the water and the stew thickens up under the lid. Keep the heat mostly on top once the dumplings go in, or the bottom scorches before their centers set.
