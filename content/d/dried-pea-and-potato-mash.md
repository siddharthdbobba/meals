Split peas are cheap, dense, and light, and the packet directions asking for a long simmer are the only thing wrong with them. They do not need it. Bring them to a boil, kill the stove, and let the covered pot coast for twenty minutes. You get soft peas for roughly two minutes of fuel, which matters more than the peas themselves on a long carry.

Potato flakes turn the result from soup into food and take no cooking at all. The protein powder is here because meat, eggs, and beans are all heavier per gram of protein than a scoop of plant powder; add it off the heat so it dissolves instead of seizing. Processed cheese slices are the deliberate choice over real cheese — they sit in a pack for a week without a cooler and still deliver fat.
