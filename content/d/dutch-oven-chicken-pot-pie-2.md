This started as a home casserole and it survives the trip because almost all of it is already shelf-stable. The only swap that matters is the topping. Refrigerated biscuit tubes need a cooler and pop open on their own in the heat, so dry biscuit mix and a half cup of water does the same job out of a bin.

The canning liquid in the vegetables is not waste. Condensed soup straight from the can is thick enough to seize and burn against the cast iron, and that liquid thins it into a gravy without adding any water you had to carry. Heat gently before the lid goes on so the bottom is not already set when the biscuits start baking.
