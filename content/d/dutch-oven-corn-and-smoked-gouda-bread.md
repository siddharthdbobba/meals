This started as an oven recipe with fresh milk, a real egg, and sour cream, none of which survive a weekend in a car. Milk powder, egg powder, and sour cream powder rehydrate into the same batter and weigh a fraction as much. The creamed corn stays canned because it is the only thing here supplying moisture and sweetness, and there is no dried version worth carrying.

Heat management is the whole job. A dutch oven with coals only underneath burns the bottom before the middle sets, so most of them go on the lid. Smoked gouda is the right cheese because it is firm enough to travel unrefrigerated for a couple of days and it does not turn the crumb greasy the way a soft cheese would.
