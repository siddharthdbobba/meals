Cooking this over coals instead of in an oven means the heat comes from two directions and you control each one. Most of the coals go on the lid because marshmallows brown from above; too many underneath and the crust burns before the tops color.

The two-stage bake matters. Chocolate needs to melt and spread before marshmallows go on, or you end up with browned marshmallow sitting on hard chips. Cooling is the other half — the butter in the crust is liquid when it comes off the fire and the whole thing slices cleanly only once that sets.
