Sun-dried tomatoes are doing most of the work. They are concentrated acid and salt in a form that does not need a cooler, and they rehydrate in the broth while the beans warm through. Without them this is just beans in liquid.

Canned beans are already cooked, so the twenty-five minutes is about reduction, not softening. Mashing a few against the pot wall releases starch and turns thin broth into something that stays on a spoon. Keep the coals moderate and stir from the bottom, because cast iron holds heat long after you stop paying attention.
