This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal. Two cans of beans weigh close to two pounds, so it belongs in a car or a basecamp and nowhere near a backpack. In exchange you get six real dinners out of one pot and one heat source.

The order of operations is doing the work. Bacon fat is the cooking medium for the onion, and frying the hot dog pieces before the beans go in gives them browned edges the sauce cannot supply later. The 25 minutes under the lid is not about cooking anything through, since the beans arrive cooked. It is about driving off enough liquid that the ketchup and brown sugar tighten into a glaze. The bacon goes on at the end so it stays crisp.
