Olives are mostly water and salt, which is exactly what makes them heavy and exactly what makes them dry well. Pulled down to a coarse dry crumble they lose the weight but keep the brine, and 10 minutes of cold water at lunch puts the texture back. No stove, no pot, no waiting on a boil in wind.

The oil is held back on purpose. Fat does not dehydrate, and if you dry it into the mix it goes rancid on a long trip and coats the bag. Add it at camp from the same bottle you already carry. Drain the artichokes hard for the same reason: their marinade is oil, and oil left in the mix keeps the tapenade from ever drying properly.
