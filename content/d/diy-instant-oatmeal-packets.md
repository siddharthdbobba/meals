This is a pantry version of a grocery-store oatmeal packet, and the only real work happens at home. Quick oats hydrate in about a minute in hot water, which is why they are the base instead of rolled or steel cut. The salt is not optional. A quarter teaspoon is what separates oatmeal from wet cardboard.

One packet as written is small for trail breakfast, so double it if you are walking all day. Nothing here needs a cooler, and the dried fruit softens in the same minute the oats do, so there is no soaking step and no second pot.
