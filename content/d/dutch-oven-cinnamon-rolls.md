The original is a yeast dough that wants a mixer, a warm kitchen, and two hours of rising. None of that is happening at a campsite, so this is a baking powder biscuit dough instead. It goes together in twenty minutes and it is forgiving about temperature, which matters when the ambient is 40 degrees and windy.

The other swaps are about the cooler. Powdered milk and water stand in for fresh milk and eggs, and ghee or shortening replaces butter because both sit at ambient temperature for a week without going off. Most of the heat belongs on the lid. Coals underneath scorch the bottoms long before the middles set.
