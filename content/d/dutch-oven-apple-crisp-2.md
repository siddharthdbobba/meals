This is the cake-mix crisp trick, moved to a Dutch oven. Dry cake mix over fruit does what an oat topping does: the butter and the fruit's own juice wet it from both sides, and it sets into a crust without anyone measuring flour at a picnic table.

Heat placement is the only part that takes attention. Most of the coals go on the lid so the top browns while the bottom just simmers the apples. Too many underneath and the fruit scorches before the topping is done. Rotating the oven and lid in opposite directions evens out the hot spots your fire will inevitably have.
