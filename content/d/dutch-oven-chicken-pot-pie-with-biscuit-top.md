The original is a fridge recipe: cream, fresh mirepoix, refrigerated croissant dough. All three are replaced here. Dried vegetables rehydrate in the same water that becomes the broth, so nothing is wasted and there is no second pot. Whole milk powder plus the flour roux gets you the body that cream was doing, and it rides in a bag at ambient temperature.

Drop biscuits go on instead of pastry because they tolerate uneven coal heat. A rolled crust wants an even oven; a lump of biscuit dough just needs top heat and time. Keep two thirds of your coals on the lid — heat from below only will burn the gravy and leave the top raw.
