This is a car-camping recipe and stays one. Two pounds of fresh beef and twelve ounces of cheese need a cooler, and a dutch oven full of coals is not a backpacking stove. What makes it worth the hauling is that it feeds eight out of one pot with almost no knife work.

The layering is doing something real. Corn chips baked under wet meat turn to mush, so only the middle third is meant to soften; the top layer stays under the cheese and keeps its crunch. Holding the liquid to one cup matters for the same reason. More than that and the bottom is a paste by the time the cheese melts.
