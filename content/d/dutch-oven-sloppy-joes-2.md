Chicken gumbo soup is the odd ingredient and the load-bearing one. It brings tomato, rice, okra, and salt in one can, and the rice and okra thicken the sauce as it simmers so you are not standing over a pot cooking down water. That is why the mix goes from soupy to spoonable in 15 minutes with nothing added.

This one stays a car-camping recipe. A pound of raw beef needs a cooler, and buns need space, so there is no honest way to make it a pack-in dinner. Heat management is the only skill involved: hot coals to brown the meat, most of them raked away to simmer. Leave it on frying heat the whole time and the bottom scorches before the sauce sets.
