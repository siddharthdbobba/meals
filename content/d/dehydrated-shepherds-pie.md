This single-pot meal weighs 4 ounces, delivers 32 grams of protein, and requires only boiled water and a 20-minute wait. The dehydrated beef carries flavor that meat powder can't, and instant potato flakes transform broth into something with body.

Dehydrated vegetables chew tougher than freeze-dried alternatives, which matters if you're tired at the end of a hard day. The sodium content runs high at 1,200 mg, but on the trail that's useful—your body will thank you for it at altitude.
