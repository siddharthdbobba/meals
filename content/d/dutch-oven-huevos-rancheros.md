This is a car-camping breakfast because eggs and cheese need a cooler, and eight eggs for four people needs a pot with a lid and steady heat. The dutch oven gives you both: fry the bacon over full coals, drop the heat for the sauce, then move most of the coals up top so the eggs bake from above instead of scorching from below.

The order matters more than the recipe looks. Cheese goes down before the eggs so it melts into a barrier between the wet sauce and the whites, which is what keeps the eggs from going watery. Warming the tortillas on top at the end costs five minutes and saves you a second pan. If you're carrying everything on your back instead, see the freeze-dried variation below.
