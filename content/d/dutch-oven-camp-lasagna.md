Oven-ready noodles are the whole reason this works over a fire. They are dry enough to absorb liquid from the sauce and the cottage cheese as they bake, so you never boil a pot of water, never drain it, and never deal with a second dish. Break them to fit a round Dutch oven and stop worrying about the shape.

The coals matter more than the recipe. Heat only from below burns the bottom layer before the noodles hydrate, so most of the coals go on the lid. Browning the beef at home is not laziness — it removes the greasiest, longest step from camp and means the pot only ever holds one thing.

This one stays in the cooler. Cottage cheese and grated mozzarella are not shelf-stable, so it is a car-camping or basecamp dinner, best on night one or two.
