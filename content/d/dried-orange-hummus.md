Wet hummus is heavy and spoils. Drying it removes the only part you can replace for free at a stream. Chickpeas rehydrate well once they have been pureed, because there is no cell structure left to soften back up, so it comes back as a paste rather than a bag of gravel.

The orange juice and zest are doing more than flavor. Acid keeps the mash bright through the dehydrator, which otherwise turns it a dull grey. Tahini is the fat, and fat is what slows drying, so keep the layer thin and check the middle of the sheet before you call it done. The olive oil goes on at camp instead of into the blender for the same reason.
