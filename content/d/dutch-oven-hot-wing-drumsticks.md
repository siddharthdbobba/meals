Skinning the drumsticks first is what makes this work in a dutch oven. Skin needs dry radiant heat to crisp, and a covered oven over coals traps steam instead. Without it, the oil and garlic go straight onto the meat and the sauce has something to grip.

The hot sauce goes on last for a reason. An hour of heat cooks the vinegar off and leaves a dull red coating. Fifteen minutes is enough to set it on the meat and keep the bite. This one needs a cooler and real coals, so it is a car-camping or basecamp dinner, not something you carry.
