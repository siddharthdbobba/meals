This is a meatloaf and a side dish stacked in the same pot, which is the whole point of a dutch oven. The meat renders fat and the soup runs down through the potatoes, so the potatoes braise instead of drying out and the loaf never sits in its own grease.

Coal placement is the part people get wrong. Most of the heat belongs on the lid; too much underneath scorches the meat layer long before the potatoes give up. Slice the potatoes thin and even, because an hour is exactly enough time for eighth-inch slices and not nearly enough for quarter-inch ones. Boxed shelf-stable milk swaps in for fresh with no difference once it is in the mix.
