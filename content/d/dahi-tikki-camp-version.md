The original is a street snack built on eight boiled potatoes and hung curd, neither of which survives a pack. Instant potato flakes get you the same mash in one pot with one boil, and the gram flour and corn flour from the source recipe still do the binding so the patties hold together in the pan. Yogurt powder rehydrated thick replaces the drained curd, which is really just yogurt with the water taken out anyway.

Ghee is the reason this works over a stove instead of a tawa. It has a high smoke point and no milk solids to burn, and it keeps at ambient temperature for weeks. The sev goes on last and only last, because it goes limp within a minute of touching the yogurt.
