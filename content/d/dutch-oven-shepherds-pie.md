Coals, not flames. A dutch oven bakes by radiating heat from cast iron, so you set it on embers and pile more on the lid. Most of the heat belongs on top here, because the job in the second half is browning a potato crust, not scorching the meat underneath.

The potato lid is structural. Spread edge to edge with no gaps and it traps steam, which is what finishes the vegetables and keeps the filling from drying out over thirty minutes. Instant flakes are better than real potatoes for this: no peeling, no boiling pot of chunks, and they set up stiffer, which holds the cheese instead of letting it sink. This one needs a cooler, so cook it the first night out.
