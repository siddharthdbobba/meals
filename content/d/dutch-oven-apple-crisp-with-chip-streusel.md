The original version of this bakes fresh Pink Lady apples with cold butter and finishes with whipped cream. None of that survives a night in a car, so the fruit is dried apples that rehydrate in their own spiced liquid, the butter is shortening or the canned kind, and the cream is gone. What is left still works because the structure was never the cream. It was salt and fat against sweet fruit.

The crushed chips are the part worth keeping. They bring salt and grease into the topping and they stay crisp longer than oats alone, which matters in a dutch oven where steam has nowhere to go. Coal placement is the only real skill here: more heat on the lid than under it, or the fruit scorches before the streusel colors.
