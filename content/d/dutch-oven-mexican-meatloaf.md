  - "Build a bed of coals and get a 12-inch dutch oven ready. You want about 350°F: roughly 8 coals under the oven and 17 on the lid."
  - "Chop the onion and jalapenos. Keep half the onion and all the jalapenos aside for the topping."

This is two recipes stacked in one pot. The meat layer bakes first because it needs the direct bottom heat and takes twice as long; the cornbread goes on top later and finishes in the steam trapped under the lid. Doing it in that order means one pot, one fire, and no juggling.

Draining the tomatoes is the step people skip and regret. Canned tomatoes carry enough liquid to keep the meat from setting, and a loose meat layer means the batter sinks into it instead of sitting on top. The bread crumbs and egg are structure, not filler. For the trail version, use boxed shelf-stable milk or powdered milk reconstituted at camp — this is a cooler recipe either way, since the meat and eggs need to stay cold until they hit the pot.
