This is a coals recipe, not a stove recipe. The heat has to come from above as well as below, or the top layer stays raw while the bottom scorches. Most of your coals go on the lid.

The cream does the work. There is no water in this dish, so the potatoes steam in their own moisture and the cream reduces around them into something that holds the layers together. Slice thin and the hour is enough; slice thick and it is not. Season every layer, because garlic salt on top only seasons the top.
