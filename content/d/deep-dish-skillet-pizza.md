Chicago deep dish is a good camp pizza for one structural reason: the cheese goes under the sauce. That layer shields the dough from the wet sauce for the entire cook, so a crust that is baking slowly over uneven coals still comes out set rather than soggy. The order is not a gimmick.

The home version wants fresh yeast and an oven. Instant dry yeast does the same job out of a bag and survives a week in a pack. Low-moisture mozzarella replaces the fresh stuff, which sheds too much water and will not hold in a cooler. Coals under a lid run cooler than an oven, so give it the full 35 minutes and judge by the bottom crust, not the top.
