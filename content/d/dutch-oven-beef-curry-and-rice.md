This is a one-pot skillet-and-rice dish more than a curry. The rice cooks in the beef fat and the vegetable liquid, which is why draining the pan is a mistake: fat is what keeps the grains separate and carries the spice through the pot.

The spice step matters. Two minutes of curry powder in hot fat before the water goes in tastes like something; the same powder stirred into boiling water tastes like dust. Keep the simmer low and stir every fifteen minutes, because a dutch oven bottom over coals will scorch rice quietly and you will not smell it until it is too late.
