This is a long-simmer sauce, which is exactly why it belongs at a car camp and nowhere else. The work is all in the first twenty minutes; after that the dutch oven sits on low coals and the fire does the rest. Nothing here is fast, and nothing here is light — two pounds of meat and a cast iron pot is not backpacking food.

Two changes from a kitchen version. Fresh milk becomes powdered whole milk reconstituted at camp, which does the same job of rounding off the tomato acid without a carton going warm in the cooler. Fresh basil becomes dried, added at the end, because a bag of leaves turns black in a pack by day two. Chop the onion, carrot, celery, and garlic at home; a cutting board on a picnic table in the dark is the worst part of this recipe.
