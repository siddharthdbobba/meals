This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal, and it stays that way. Crescent dough and raw ground meat both need to be cold, so it belongs at a car site or a basecamp with ice. Everything happens in the one oven: brown, dump the grease, cool it down, build, bake.

The cooling pause matters. Dough dropped into a 375°F oven scorches on the bottom before the cheese ever melts. Coal placement does the rest — most of the heat on the lid so the top browns while the bottom just sets. Grate the cheese off a block instead of buying it shredded; the anti-caking starch in bagged cheese turns gritty in a sealed bake.
