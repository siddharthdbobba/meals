This is a reheat, not a cook. Everything in the pot is already done, so the only job over the fire is getting the cheese to melt without scorching the bottom. That means low, spread-out coals and a spoon in your hand. Cast iron holds heat long after you pull it off, which is exactly the problem here.

Cooking the macaroni at home is the change that matters. It saves a pot of water, the fuel to boil it, and the mess of draining pasta in the dark. Oil the noodles before they go in the bag or they weld into a brick overnight. The alfredo and the half and half do the same job from two directions: the sauce carries fat, the cream keeps the melted cheese from turning grainy.
