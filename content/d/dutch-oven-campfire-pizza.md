This is car camp cooking, not trail cooking. Refrigerated dough, shredded cheese, and cured pepperoni all need a cooler, so the recipe stays where a cooler goes. The trade is that you get real bread out of a fire, which nothing dehydrated will give you.

The heat management is the whole recipe. Coals under and coals on the lid means the crust and the cheese finish around the same time; flame under the pot alone will scorch the bottom while the top is still raw. Rotating the oven and the lid in opposite directions evens out the hot spots that build up wherever the coals bank up.
