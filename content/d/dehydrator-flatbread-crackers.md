This is a cracker with no flour in it. The sunflower seeds and ground flax do the structural work: flax turns gluey when it hits the moisture in the mushrooms, and that gel is what holds the sheet together once the water leaves. Skip the flax and you get a tray of crumbs.

Mushrooms are mostly water, which is why the mix goes on wet and dries for half a day. Low heat matters — 125°F pulls water out slowly enough that the fat in the seeds does not go rancid and the sheet dries evenly instead of case-hardening. Pesto is oil and salt as much as it is flavor, and both help these keep. Three tablespoons is already assertive; four takes over.
