This is a scout-camp dutch oven recipe pushed toward shelf stability. Powdered egg and powdered milk replace a dozen eggs and a quart of milk, which is the whole cooler problem solved in two bags. Reconstitute them cold and let the mix rest before the pour, or you get lumps that bake in.

The bread is structural. It soaks up the custard and keeps the eggs from sheeting into rubber against the hot cast iron. Cheese goes on late for the same reason it always does: forty minutes under coals turns it to grease, ten minutes turns it into a crust.
