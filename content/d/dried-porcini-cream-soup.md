Dried mushrooms and cream are an old pairing, and the trail version loses less than you would expect. Whole milk powder carries the fat that makes porcini taste like porcini, and it rehydrates without a cooler. The soaking water is the stock here, so nothing gets drained off.

The original leans on a boiled russet potato for body. Potato flakes do the same job in thirty seconds and weigh an ounce. Add the milk powder off the boil, though. Boiled milk powder goes grainy and no amount of stirring brings it back.
