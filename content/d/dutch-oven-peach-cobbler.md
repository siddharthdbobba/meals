The original is a summer kitchen recipe built on fresh Georgia peaches and a carton of buttermilk. Neither travels. Dried peaches rehydrated in hot water give you the same fruit volume and more concentrated flavor, and buttermilk powder plus water is a straight one-for-one for the real thing in a batter this sweet.

The unstirred layers are the whole mechanism. Butter on the bottom, batter poured over it, fruit on top: as it bakes the batter climbs through the fruit and the butter fries the underside into a crust. Stir it together and you get one dense pan of sweet bread instead. Heat management is the only hard part, so keep most of the coals on the lid and turn the oven regularly.
