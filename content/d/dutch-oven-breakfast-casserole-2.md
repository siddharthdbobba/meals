Camp breakfast for a crowd is a heat management problem, not a cooking one. The ratio that matters is two-thirds of your coals on the lid and one-third underneath. Eggs set from the top down that way instead of welding to the bottom.

The hash browns do double duty. They insulate the eggs from direct bottom heat and they soak up sausage fat that would otherwise pool. Bread pieces take up the rest. Rotating the oven is not optional — coals burn unevenly and a stationary pot gives you one scorched quarter and one raw one.

This one stays as written: it needs a cooler and a fire ring. There is no backpacking version of twelve eggs.
