This started as a car-camping menu built around fresh steak and sausages kept frozen in the middle of a pack. That works for two nights in Tasmania in July. It does not work for a week in summer, so the meat here is hard summer sausage, which needs no cooler and keeps its fat where you want it.

The rest is unchanged in spirit: instant potato, dried vegetables, and a gravy packet doing the seasoning. Gravy mix is the part people skip and shouldn't. Plain potato flakes and water taste like nothing, and the mix is a few grams that turns the whole pot into a meal. Add the flakes slowly or you end up with dry pockets you cannot stir out.
