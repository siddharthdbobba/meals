steps:
  - "Start a fire and let it burn down. You want about 20 coals for the lid and 8 underneath."
  - "Cut the feta into thick fingers, roughly the size of your thumb. Melt the butter in a small pot at the edge of the fire."
  - "Lay out a sheet of phyllo, brush it with butter, set a finger of feta near one edge, fold the sides in, and roll it up tight. Half a sheet is enough if your phyllo is thick."
  - "Line the dutch oven with parchment or foil. Set the rolls in seam side down, brush the tops with butter, and scatter sesame seeds over them."
  - "Lid on, coals on top and under. Bake about 15 minutes, rotating the oven a quarter turn every 5, until the phyllo is browned. Check early; coals run hotter than an oven."
  - "Stir the honey and chili crisp together in a mug. Dip and eat while the phyllo is still crisp."

This is a home oven recipe moved onto coals, and coals are the whole problem. Heat comes from above and below unevenly, so the coal count and the quarter turns matter more than the timing. Parchment in the bottom of the oven is not optional unless you enjoy scraping burnt butter off cast iron in the dark.

Block feta is the other thing to get right. It holds its shape under heat instead of running out of the pastry, and its salt is doing all the seasoning work here. The dip is just honey and chili crisp stirred together, which sounds like nothing but is what keeps four ounces of salty cheese from wearing you out halfway through.
