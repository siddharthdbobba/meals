Two starches do two different jobs here. The grits set up fast and give the bowl its body; the bean flakes carry the protein and keep it from tasting like plain porridge. Both rehydrate off the heat, so the stove only runs long enough to soften the dried corn.

The corn is the part worth waiting on. Add it with the powders and it stays leathery. Simmer it first and it comes back sweet, which is the only contrast in an otherwise flat bowl. The oil goes in at the end because stirring fat into boiling starch makes it separate.
