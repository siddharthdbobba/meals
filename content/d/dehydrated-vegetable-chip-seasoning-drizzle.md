The point of this one is the dressing, not the vegetable. Vinegar, oil, a little syrup, and smoked paprika stick to a wet slice and stay stuck once the water leaves. Dry seasoning sprinkled on bare vegetables mostly ends up on the tray.

Keep the syrup to a teaspoon. More sugar and the slices go tacky instead of brittle, and tacky chips absorb humidity in a pack and turn to leather by day three. The same rule applies to the oil: enough to coat, not enough to pool, or you carry rancid grease up the hill.
