The original is a sheet-pan dinner built on frozen chicken fingers, which is a non-starter anywhere without a freezer. Canned chicken bound with breadcrumbs and oil does the same job: it gives you a firm, browned base that holds sauce instead of dissolving into it. Draining and pressing the chicken matters more than anything else in this recipe.

The dutch oven handles the two-stage bake the oven version needs. Bottom heat first to set the crust, then most of the coals on the lid so the dough browns from above before the cheese underneath breaks. Cheese and dough are the only cooler items, so this is a first- or second-night car camp meal, not a long trip.
