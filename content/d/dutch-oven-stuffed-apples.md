This is a car-camping dessert and it stays one. Whole apples need real, sustained heat from all sides, which is what a Dutch oven under coals gives you and a canister stove does not. The lid coals matter more than the bottom ones here: you want the apples steamed and roasted, not fried on the base.

The liquid is doing two jobs. It keeps the bottoms from scorching over the coals, and it reduces down into a syrup that goes back over the apples at the end. Orange juice concentrate is the whole reason it works undiluted sugar and acid, so the sauce has body without simmering it down separately. Skip the honey and the syrup stays thin.
