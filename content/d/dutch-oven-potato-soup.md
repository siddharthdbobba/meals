Potatoes take forever over a fire and burn on the bottom while the middle stays raw. Parcooking them at home fixes that, and it turns a 90-minute pot into a 30-minute simmer. Cube, boil or microwave until a fork goes through, cool, bag.

The fresh milk and sour cream from the original are the parts a cooler can't be trusted with on day three, so both go powdered. Milk powder rehydrated at one-third cup to a cup of water carries the same body, and sour cream powder brings the sourness back without a tub sloshing around. The bacon fat left in the pot is doing most of the work either way.
