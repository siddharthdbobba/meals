This started as a kitchen recipe with a mixer, an oven dial, and three refrigerated ingredients. Butter, eggs, buttermilk, and milk all got replaced: shortening for butter, powdered egg and buttermilk powder in the dry mix, and a spoonful of powdered milk to loosen the frosting. Nothing here needs a cooler, and the dry side is measured at home so camp work is stirring and heat management.

The hard part is the fire, not the batter. Cake burns from below long before it sets on top, so keep two thirds of your coals on the lid and rotate both oven and lid so hot spots do not park in one place. Let it cool all the way down before frosting; shortening frosting slides straight off a warm cake.
