The soda does two jobs at once. Its carbonation lifts the batter the way eggs would, and its liquid is the only water the cake mix needs. That removes eggs, milk, oil, and a mixing bowl from the list, which is most of what makes baking at a campsite annoying.

Keep the layers separate. The cake mix has to sit on the fruit, not in it, or the top never sets and you end up with hot fruit soup. Half a can of soda is enough to wet the mix through; the rest is insurance if a dry pocket shows up while you fork it.
