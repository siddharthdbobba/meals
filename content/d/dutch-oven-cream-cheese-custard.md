This is car-camping food, not backpacking food. Cream cheese and eggs need a cooler and there is no shelf-stable substitute that sets the same way, so it stays a trailhead dessert. What travels well is the crust: crush the grahams at home and it becomes one jar and one stick of butter.

The coal ratio matters more than the timing. Two thirds of the heat goes on the lid so the custard sets from above instead of scorching against the bottom. Pull it while the center still wobbles. Custard finishes cooking on residual heat, and an overbaked one cracks and weeps.
