Mushrooms are mostly water, which is the whole problem and the whole point. The pre-bake drives off enough of it that the slices hold together, and once they are firm they soak marinade instead of falling apart in it. Skip that step and you get shreds.

The long soak matters more than the drying time. Two or three days in the marinade gets salt and sugar all the way through, and that is what makes the finished jerky read as food rather than dried fungus. Dry at low heat and stop while it still bends. This is not vegan as written: Worcestershire contains anchovy. Use a fish-free version if that matters to you.
