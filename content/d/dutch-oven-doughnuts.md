This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Two inches of hot oil is not something to carry or manage on a backpacking stove, and a dutch oven over coals gives you the one thing frying needs: a heavy pot that holds its temperature when cold dough hits it.

Cream of tartar plus baking soda is the leavening here, which is why there is no yeast and no rising time. The dough is mixed and cut and fried inside half an hour. Keep the oil hot between batches. Oil that drops below about 350F soaks in instead of searing, and you get heavy, greasy rings. Milk is the only cooler item worth mentioning, and powdered milk replaces it cleanly if you would rather not deal with that.
