This is the classic rolled-and-toothpicked cordon bleu taken apart and stacked flat, which is the only version that works in a dutch oven. Nothing gets pounded, rolled, or breaded in a separate dish. The cutlets sear on the bottom, the ham and cheese go on after the flip, and the crumbs land last so they toast instead of steaming.

Toasting the crumbs in butter first is the step that matters. Dry crumbs sprinkled over melting cheese just turn to paste. Buttered ones hold their texture through a 35-minute bake. Moving the coals to the lid for that bake keeps the bottom of the chicken from burning while the cheese and crumbs get heat from above. This one needs a cooler, so it belongs at the trailhead or a basecamp, not in a pack.
