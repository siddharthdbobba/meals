The original is a copycat of a bakery bundt cake and leans on sour cream, fresh eggs, and a cream cheese frosting. None of that survives a cooler for long, so the wet ingredients here are all powder: whole milk powder does the tenderizing the sour cream did, and powdered egg binds the same way three eggs would. The instant pudding mix stays, because it is the reason a boxed cake comes out dense and damp instead of dry and crumbed.

The frosting is the real change. Cream cheese and butter beaten together is a refrigerator product, so it becomes a thin powdered sugar glaze mixed at camp with a spoonful of water. Heat control matters more than the batter does. Coals on the lid do most of the baking, and if you do not rotate the oven you get a scorched arc on whichever side faces the fire.
