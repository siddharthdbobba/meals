The original version of this was a stovetop pot of canned beans over simmered rice, which is cheap at home and useless in a pack. Cans are water you are already carrying, and long-grain rice burns twenty minutes of fuel. Instant rice and dehydrated bean flakes get you the same plate for about five ounces dry and one boil.

The bean flakes do the structural work. They turn what would be loose rice in broth into something that stays on a spoon, and they carry the oil rather than letting it pool on top. Add them slowly. Dumped all at once they set into gravel and never rehydrate evenly.
