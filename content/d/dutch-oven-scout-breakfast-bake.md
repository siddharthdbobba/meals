This is a car-camping breakfast, not a pack meal. Twelve eggs and half a pound of cheese need a cooler, and a 12-inch dutch oven is a heavy piece of iron. Carried to a site you can drive to, it feeds six people off one fire with one pot to scrub.

The order matters more than the recipe does. Sausage first, so the potatoes fry in its fat and actually brown. Eggs last, and only after the oven comes off the bottom coals, or they scorch to the iron before the top ever sets. Baking means heat from above: most of your coals belong on the lid. Cheese goes on at the end with the oven off direct heat, because it needs to melt, not cook.
