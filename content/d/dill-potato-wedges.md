This is two techniques in one pot. The boil cooks the potatoes through, which frying alone will not do without burning the outside first. The frying afterward is only for browning, so it goes fast and the seasoning has fat to stick to.

Red potatoes matter here. Waxy varieties stay intact when you cut them hot; a russet falls apart and you end up with skillet hash. Dried dill is the right call anyway since it holds up better in butter than fresh does, so nothing was lost adapting this for camp. Only the butter needs thought in the heat, and ghee solves that.
