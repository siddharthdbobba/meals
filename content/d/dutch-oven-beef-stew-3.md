This is the whole cooking-in-a-hole-in-the-ground argument in one pot. Beef cubes need long, wet, moderate heat to stop being chewy, and a cast iron oven under coals holds that heat steadier than any stove you can carry. The first 20 minutes with just a little water is not browning, it is a covered braise that starts breaking the meat down before the vegetables arrive.

Order matters more than seasoning here. Vegetables added at the start turn to mush over 40 minutes; added at the halfway point they finish exactly when the beef does. This is a cooler recipe, not a shelf-stable one, so it belongs to night one. Nothing about it survives a warm cooler on day three.
