This is a mush-then-bake, not a batter. Cooking the cornmeal in milk first gelatinizes the starch, which is why the finished cake holds together without flour or leavening. Skip the simmer and you get grit suspended in egg.

Everything here travels dry except the eggs, and powdered egg covers that if you would rather not run a cooler. The syrup does double duty as sweetener and moisture, so do not cut it and the sugar both. Coal count matters more than time: too many underneath and the bottom sets into a crust before the middle cooks.
