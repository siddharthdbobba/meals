Couscous does not cook so much as rehydrate, which is the whole point on a stove where fuel is measured in grams. One boil, no simmer, then the pot sits there doing the work while you deal with your feet.

The bouillon keeps it savory and the dried fruit keeps it from being flat. Dice the dates and figs small at home so they soften in the same ten minutes the grain does; whole ones stay chewy in the middle. The oil is not optional. Plain couscous is close to fat-free, and without a tablespoon stirred in this is a bowl of starch that will not hold you until lunch.
