This is a car-camping breakfast, and it stays one. Two pounds of sausage and two dozen eggs need a cooler, so there is no honest way to shrink it into a pack. What it does buy you is eight people fed at once off one pot and one lid.

The grease is the reason the eggs work. Sausage fat left in the oven keeps them from welding to the cast iron, and half a cup is about the line between rich and greasy. The final bake is not cooking anything, it is melting the cheese so the burritos hold together when someone eats one standing up. Coals on the lid, not just underneath, or the top layer stays cold.
