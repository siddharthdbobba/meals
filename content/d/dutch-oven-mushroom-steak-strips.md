Round steak is cheap because it is tough. The fix is the same one every braise uses: low heat and a long time, until the connective tissue gives up. That is an hour and a half of doing nothing but keeping coals under a lid, which is why this is a basecamp meal and not a trail one.

The flour coating does double duty. It browns on the meat in the butter, and the rest of it dissolves into the tomato sauce and mushroom liquid to make gravy. Skip the dusting and you get thin, watery pan juice instead. Mushrooms release a lot of water in the first ten minutes, so do not add liquid until after they have cooked down.
