This is a cooler-and-can-crate meal, not a pack meal. The only refrigerated item is the steak, and cubing it at home means it thaws faster and browns evenly in one layer. Everything else comes out of a can, so the water is already in the pot and nothing needs measuring.

The long simmer is the whole point. Round steak is cheap because it is worked muscle, and two to three hours over coals is what turns it from chewy to spoonable. The rice mix goes in last for the same reason the vegetables are canned: it only needs half an hour, and any longer and it turns to paste. The corn goes in undrained because that starchy liquid does most of the thickening.

If you want this in a pack, the canned-roast-beef variation is the way. Fresh beef and a dozen cans do not go up a trail.
