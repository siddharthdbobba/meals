The point of this dish is texture, not chocolate. Kataifi is shredded phyllo, and when it is fried in butter until it is properly dry it stays crisp inside a fat-heavy filling for days. Stop toasting early and it turns to paste within the hour.

Camp changes two things. There is no tempering, so the bark will be soft and matte rather than snappy; a cold rock or the cooler is enough to set it. And pistachio butter from a jar replaces the sweetened pistachio cream, with tahini thinning it out so it folds instead of clumping. It contains wheat and pistachios and butter, so it is dessert for a group that has cleared all three.
