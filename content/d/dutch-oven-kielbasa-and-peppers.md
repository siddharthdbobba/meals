Fully cooked smoked sausage is the reason this works over uneven coals. Nothing here has to reach a safe temperature, so a fire that runs hot or cool only changes how fast the vegetables give up their water. The 35-minute stew is about melding, not doneness.

The pineapple juice is the only liquid, and that is deliberate. It carries acid and sugar into the rendered sausage fat, which is what turns a pan of sausage and peppers into something with a sauce. Drain the can and you get a dry skillet dish instead. If you are packing this without a cooler, dried peppers and shelf-stable summer sausage do the same job with a cup of added water.
