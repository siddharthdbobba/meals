Pimento cheese is a mayonnaise-and-block-cheddar job at home, which means a cooler and a knife. Neither travels. Cheddar powder plus oil gets you the same place: the powder carries the flavor and the protein, the oil supplies the fat that mayonnaise would have, and the two emulsify into a spread once you add just enough water.

Water is the whole difficulty. Cheese powder drinks it fast and then keeps drinking, so a spread that looks right in the bag can slump into sauce two minutes later. Go slow, and rehydrate the dried peppers separately first so they take their share before the powder gets any.
