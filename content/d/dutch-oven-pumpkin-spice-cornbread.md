Cornbread is the right camp bake because it needs no rise time and no gluten development. You mix it, you cook it, it is done in half an hour. Canned pumpkin does two jobs here: it carries the spice, and it holds enough moisture that the crumb survives uneven coal heat without drying to sand.

The home version wants butter, eggs, and buttermilk. Powdered whole egg and oil replace all three and none of them need a cooler. Coal placement is the only real skill involved. Most of the heat goes on the lid because the pot bottom sits closer to its heat source and will burn first if you load it evenly.
