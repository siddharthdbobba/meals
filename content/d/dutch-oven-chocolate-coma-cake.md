The bottom layer is not batter. Cocoa, sugar, oil, and water go in loose and stay loose, so as the cake sets above it the liquid sinks and turns into a fudge sauce under the crumb. That is why the batter gets poured on top and never stirred in.

The marshmallows are a barrier. They keep the wet layer and the batter from mixing during the first ten minutes of heat, then melt into the sauce. Coal placement matters more than time here: heavy on the lid, light underneath, or the bottom scorches before the top sets. The quarter turns every 15 minutes are what keep one side from burning.
