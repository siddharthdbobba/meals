Layering is what makes this work over a fire. Coals give you uneven heat, and a stack of tortillas, sauce, and cheese spreads that unevenness out instead of concentrating it in one spot. The lid does the rest: top coals melt and brown the cheese while the bottom heat cooks slowly enough not to burn the base layer.

The rest is timing. Fewer coals underneath than on top, and rotate both pot and lid so hot spots keep moving. The ten-minute rest off the heat matters more than it sounds, since the layers are soupy straight off the coals and hold together once they cool a little. Corn tortillas, not flour, both because they take sauce without turning to paste and because they keep the dish gluten-free.
