This is a fridge recipe that only needs one adjustment for camp: the wet ingredients. Tomato and lettuce leak, and a soggy tortilla comes apart in your hands around hour six. Put the cheese down as a barrier layer, blot the pickles, and slice the tomato thin so it has less water to give up.

The sauce spread edge to edge is doing structural work, not flavor work. It seals the tortilla shut so the roll stays a roll in the bottom of a pack. Mayonnaise and deli meat both want cold, so these live in the cooler under the ice and get eaten by day two.
