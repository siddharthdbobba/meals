This started as an oven loaf with fresh bananas, eggs, and buttermilk, none of which survive a weekend in a car. Freeze-dried banana crushed to powder carries the same flavor at a fraction of the weight, and egg and buttermilk powders rehydrate with the water you were adding anyway. The result bakes and tastes close enough that nobody asks.

Heat is the whole game here. Coals on the lid do the browning and coals underneath do the cooking, so a lid stacked too heavy gives you a scorched top over raw batter. Rotate both the pot and the lid on a schedule; a dutch oven sitting still in a fire ring cooks one side twice as fast as the other.
