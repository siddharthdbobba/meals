This is a dump cake, and the no-stir rule is the whole method. The dry mix has to sit as a lid over the fruit so the bottom steams while the top bakes into a crust. Stir it and you get one uniform pan of wet batter that never sets.

The Coke does two things at once. It wets the mix enough to bake, and its acid plus the sugar gives you browning without eggs or oil, which is what makes this work with no cooler. Heat placement matters more than time: most of the coals go on the lid, because heat from below only scorches the fruit. Car camping only. Two cans of pie filling is over three pounds of water weight, and nobody carries that.
