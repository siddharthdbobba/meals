This is a bar cookie that never gets mixed. Butter binds the graham crumbs into a crust, the condensed milk soaks down into it and sets like caramel as it bakes, and the chips melt into a layer on top. Nothing needs a bowl, a mixer, or a measured ratio, which is why it works in a dutch oven on the ground.

Heat placement is the whole game. Most of the coals go on the lid so the top browns and the chips melt without the crust scorching — six under, eighteen over, and rotate the oven and lid a quarter turn every five minutes if your fire runs hot on one side. The cooling wait is real. Condensed milk is liquid at bake temperature and firm at ambient, and nothing you do speeds that up.
