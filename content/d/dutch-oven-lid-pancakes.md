The original calls for a fresh egg and a jug of milk. Both are replaced here with powders, which means the whole dry side of the recipe premixes at home and rides in a bag for a week without a cooler. Reconstituted milk powder and egg powder behave close enough to the real thing in a pancake, where the flour is doing most of the structural work.

The upturned lid is the point of the method. Propped on two sticks over spread coals, it gets air underneath and heats evenly instead of scorching in one spot, and it gives you a real griddle without carrying one. Mix in the bag, cut a corner, and pour straight onto the lid. Nothing to wash but the lid and a spatula.
