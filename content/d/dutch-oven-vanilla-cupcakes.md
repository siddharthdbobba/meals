This is a home kitchen recipe pushed onto coals. Two swaps make it work without a fridge: powdered whole egg reconstituted with water, and powdered buttermilk instead of the carton. Butter stays butter, because shortening tastes like nothing and butter holds a day or two in a shaded bin at car camp.

Coal count is the oven dial. Twice as many on the lid as underneath gives you top heat without a burnt bottom, and rotating the oven against the lid every eight minutes keeps hot spots from setting in one place. Creaming the butter and sugar by hand is the part people skip; without a mixer it takes patience, and without the air you beat in, these come out dense.
