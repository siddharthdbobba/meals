Bread at camp usually means someone sawing at a loaf on a dirty table. Muffins skip that. They come out of the oven already portioned, they hold together in a lunch kit, and they can be eaten cold with no assembly.

The pickle juice is doing chemistry, not just flavor. It is acidic enough to kick the baking soda, which is what lifts a batter this heavy with cheese. Drain the chopped pickles well anyway. Extra brine turns the crumb gummy, and gummy is what you get when a Dutch oven runs cooler than you thought.
