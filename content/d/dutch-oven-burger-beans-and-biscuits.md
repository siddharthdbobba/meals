This is a car-camping meal, not a pack meal. Two pounds of beef and two large cans are heavy and wet, so it stays where a vehicle goes. What it buys is one pot feeding eight with no side dishes.

The biscuit lid is the part that needs attention. Dough dropped on hot beans steams from below and bakes from above, so the coals have to move to the lid or the middle stays raw. Cheese goes on at the end, after the biscuits set, otherwise it burns against the lid instead of melting.
