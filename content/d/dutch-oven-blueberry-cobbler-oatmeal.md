The original does this with fresh milk and frozen berries out of a cooler. Neither is needed. Powdered whole milk rehydrates in the pot and dried blueberries swell back up in the cooking water, which means the whole batch is shelf-stable and the cooler stays shut.

Steel cut oats are the reason this takes a Dutch oven and not a saucepan. They need thirty minutes of low, even heat, and cast iron holds that better than a burner does. Keep the coals sparse and the pot moving, or the bottom layer turns to carbon while the middle is still hard. The granola goes on at the bowl, not in the pot, so it stays crisp.
