The original is a fresh-vegetable gumbo, which means a cooler full of onions, peppers, tomatoes, and two pounds of raw chicken. Cans and dried vegetables do the same job with no cold storage. Okra is the one ingredient that has to stay: it thickens the broth as it cooks down, and the roux alone will not carry a pot this size.

The roux is the whole recipe. Oil and flour cooked until brown is where the color and most of the flavor come from, and it takes ten unglamorous minutes of stirring over steady coals. Dried onion and pepper rehydrate in the simmer, so they go in early. Canned chicken is already cooked, so it goes in last and only long enough to heat through.
