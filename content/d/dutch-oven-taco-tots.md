This is a car-camping dinner, not a pack-in one. Everything about it is heavy and cold: two pounds of beef, four cans, a bag of frozen tots, and the dutch oven itself. That is fine when the cooler rides in the trunk.

The layering is the whole method. Meat on the bottom holds the heat, corn buffers the cheese so it does not scorch on the iron, and the tots sit on top where they can dry out and crisp instead of steaming. Moving all the coals to the lid at the end is what browns them. Stir it together early and you get a pan of grey mush that tastes the same but eats worse.
