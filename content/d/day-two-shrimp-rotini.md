This is a second-day meal, which is a specific problem. Day one you eat what is fresh. Day two the cooler has lost its edge and nobody wants another cold slice of something. Cooking the shrimp at home solves it: they are already safe, already seasoned, and they need nothing but a bag and a spoon.

The fat is doing the preserving. Oil-based dressing coats the pasta and keeps it from drying into a brick, and it does not break down the way a creamy dressing does after a day in a warm cooler. Chill the shrimp completely before packing, and drain the artichokes or the whole bag turns to brine.
