This cooks the noodles directly in the stock instead of boiling them separately and draining. That means one pot, no strainer, and the starch the noodles shed stays in the pan doing the thickening. The condensed soup goes in last, off the heat, because it will scorch on cast iron if it sits over coals.

Heat control matters more than timing. A Dutch oven holds heat long after you pull it off the fire, so cook the noodles a touch shy and let the residual heat finish them while it rests. Browning the beef at home cuts the camp work down to about twenty minutes.
