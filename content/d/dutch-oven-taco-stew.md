This is a car-camping dish, so it stays a car-camping dish. The vegetable juice is the whole trick — it is the cooking liquid, the salt, and the tomato body all at once, which is why no water goes in. The dry rice goes in raw and drinks it down over two hours until the pot turns from soup into stew.

Low and slow matters more than the exact coal count. Six under and four on top holds a simmer rather than a boil; a hard boil scorches rice against cast iron long before the middle of the pot is done. Stir on a schedule and keep the lid on the rest of the time.
