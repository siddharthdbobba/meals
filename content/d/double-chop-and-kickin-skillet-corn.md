This is a car-camping dinner, not a pack-in one. Two thick chops, one pan, and a cooler. The order matters more than anything else: the chops sear first so the pan is full of rendered fat and browned bits, and then the corn goes in and picks all of that up. Cooking the corn in a clean pan would waste the best part.

The finishing ingredients go in off the heat. Butter added to a screaming pan breaks and the lime juice cooks off, which leaves you with hot corn and nothing bright against the spice rub. Pull the pan, then stir. Cutting the kernels at home is the one real change from a kitchen version — whole ears are mostly cob, and cob does not fit in a cooler you also want beer in.
