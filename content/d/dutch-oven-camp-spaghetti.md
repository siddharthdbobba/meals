The noodles never see a colander. They cook in plain water first, then finish in the sauce, so the starch stays in the pot and thickens everything instead of going down the drain. Five cups sounds like too much water and it isn't quite. The pasta drinks most of it during the bake.

Timing matters more than temperature here. Pull the noodles off the boil while they still have a spine, because thirty minutes under a lidded oven keeps cooking them. Most of the coals go on the lid for that last stretch, which browns the cheese instead of scorching the bottom.
