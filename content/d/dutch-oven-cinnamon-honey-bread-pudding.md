Bread pudding at home leans on fresh eggs, milk, and cream, none of which survive a weekend in a car. Powdered whole egg and milk do the same job, and coconut milk powder covers the fat that double cream was supplying. The custard sets the same way; you just mix it in a bottle instead of a bowl.

Stale bread is not a compromise here, it is the requirement. Dry crumb pulls in liquid and holds its shape, where fresh bread turns to paste. Leave the top layer sticking up out of the custard so those edges dry and caramelize under the lid coals. That contrast between the crisp top and the soft middle is the whole reason to bother.

Coal count matters more than time. Too many underneath and the bottom burns before the center sets, which is why the heat is weighted two to one toward the lid.
