This is a boxed mix doing what a boxed mix does, with two additions that matter. The syrup adds sugar and moisture without adding another egg, which is what keeps a dutch oven brownie from drying out over uneven coals. The chips stay whole and give you pockets of melted chocolate instead of a uniform slab.

Coal placement is the whole recipe. Ten under and eighteen on the lid puts the heat above the batter, so it bakes rather than fries against the cast iron. If you smell scorching, lift the oven off the bottom coals entirely and let the lid finish it. This one needs a cooler for the eggs, so it belongs at the car, not in a pack.
