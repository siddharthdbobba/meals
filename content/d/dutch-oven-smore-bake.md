This is s'mores without the sticks, the queue, or the marshmallow you dropped in the dirt. Everything goes in the Dutch oven in layers and the heat does the assembly for you. It feeds eight from one pot, which is why it belongs at car camp and not in a pack.

Coals, not flames, are the whole game. The oven holds heat evenly enough to melt the top layer of marshmallows before the bottom layer of crackers burns, but only if the heat is steady and moderate. Mini marshmallows matter for the same reason: they melt in the time the chocolate needs, so nothing waits on anything else. The parchment is not about flavor. Sugar welded to cast iron is a genuinely bad end to the night.
