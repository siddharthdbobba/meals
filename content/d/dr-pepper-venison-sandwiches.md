This is a car-camp dinner because raw sliced venison needs a cooler and the sauce needs a real reduction. Both are easy at a truck and impossible in a pack. The sauce is doing the heavy lifting: cola for sugar and acidity, soy for salt, vinegar to keep it from going syrupy-sweet, tomato paste for body. Cooked down it turns into something that clings instead of running off the meat and into the roll.

Venison round is lean and gets tough fast, so the searing window is short. Thin slices across the grain plus a few seconds per side over hot coals is the whole technique. Reduce the sauce first and hold it off heat, because the meat cooks faster than you can react and you do not want to be babysitting two pans at once.
