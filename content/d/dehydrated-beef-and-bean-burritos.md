The whole recipe happens in your kitchen. Browning and seasoning at home means the trail version is soak, simmer, roll — and the dehydrator is what makes a pound of ground beef weigh almost nothing. Fat-free beans are not a health choice here. Fat is what makes dried food go off and what keeps a bean sheet tacky instead of brittle.

The hour-long cold soak matters more than the simmer. Dried beef rehydrates slowly and unevenly, and five minutes of boiling will not save you from gritty crumbs. Start the soak when you get to camp, set up the tent, then cook. String cheese is the only thing here that will not last a week, so treat it as a first-night luxury.
