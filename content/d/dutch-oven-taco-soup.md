This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Nearly everything in it is shelf-stable canned goods, so the only cooler load is two pounds of beef and a block of cheese, which is why it belongs on night one. Nothing gets drained except the grease. The can liquid from the corn and beans is half the body of the soup, and pouring it out means adding water back later.

The two seasoning packets are doing all the seasoning work. Taco mix brings the chile and cumin, ranch brings salt, buttermilk powder, and enough dried herb to keep it from tasting like a single note. Both are dry and weigh nothing. The chips are not a garnish either: they add the fat and crunch that a bean soup does not have on its own.
