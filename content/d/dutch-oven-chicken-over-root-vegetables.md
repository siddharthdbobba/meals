This one does not get adapted for the trail. It is a cooler recipe, a fire recipe, and a heavy-pot recipe, and it stays that way. What makes it worth the weight is the vegetable bed. The sliced sweet potato and parsnip hold the chicken up off the iron, so the bird roasts in circulating heat instead of frying on the bottom, and every drop of fat that renders lands in the vegetables.

The hard part is heat control, not technique. Coals under a dutch oven run hot and uneven, so the quarter-turn rotation is the whole job. Skip it and you get one burnt side and one raw one. The thermometer is not optional either: a five-pound bird in a pot you cannot see into is exactly the situation where guessing goes wrong.
