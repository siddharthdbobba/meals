The original is a home quick bread with fresh smashed pawpaw, five eggs, and two sticks of butter. None of that rides in a pack. Dried fruit rehydrated in the pot gets you the same wet, dense crumb, powdered egg and buttermilk carry the binding and the acid, and shortening replaces butter without a cooler. The recipe is also cut in half here, because one loaf feeds six and two Dutch ovens is a lot of coals.

The hour of rest before baking does the real work. Baking soda hitting buttermilk acid produces gas immediately, and a batter this heavy needs that reaction to run before it goes near heat, otherwise you get a brick with a burnt bottom. Rotate the oven while it bakes for the same reason you rotate anything over coals: campfire heat is never even.
