This is a scout dutch oven standard, and the reason it works is the crescent dough. It bakes into a crust that holds the egg custard instead of letting it soak into a soggy bottom layer, so you get something you can cut and hand out rather than scoop.

Rotating the oven matters more than the exact coal count. Coals burn unevenly and a stationary oven will run hot on one side and leave raw dough on the other. Everything else is prep you should do at home: grating, dicing, and slicing by a picnic table in the dark is the slow part, not the baking. Cooler required, so this one stays at the trailhead.
