Round steak is lean and full of connective tissue, which means fast heat turns it into shoe leather. The whole recipe is built around avoiding that. You brown hot for the crust, then drop the temperature to a bare simmer and hold it there for two hours while the collagen breaks down. A Dutch oven under a thin layer of coals is one of the few camp setups that can actually hold a low temperature that long.

The ketchup is doing more than it looks like. Its acid helps soften the meat and its sugar thickens into a sauce as the liquid cooks off. Watch the pot every half hour and add water when it goes pasty, because scorched sauce on cast iron is a long night of scrubbing. This one needs a cooler, so it is a car-camping or basecamp meal, not something you carry.
