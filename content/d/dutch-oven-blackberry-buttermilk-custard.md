A buttermilk tart is really a custard held together by a small amount of flour and a lot of acid. That makes it a good candidate for camp, because everything in the custard except the butter and the berries is available as a powder. Buttermilk powder and whole egg powder rehydrate cleanly in cold water, and neither one needs a cooler.

The crumb crust replaces rolled pastry, which is not a thing worth attempting on a fire. Press it in and pre-bake it briefly so it sets before the wet filling hits. The rest is heat management: too many coals under the oven scorches the crust before the center sets, so keep most of them on the lid.
