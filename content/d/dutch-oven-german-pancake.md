This is nine eggs held together by a little flour, so it behaves more like a baked custard than a stack of pancakes. The dramatic rise up the sides comes from steam, not leavening, which is why the lid stays on and the oven has to be hot before the batter goes in. Cold pot, no climb.

It needs a cooler, so it is a car-camping and basecamp breakfast rather than a backpacking one. Milk powder replaces the milk cleanly if you would rather not carry a jug, but there is no honest shelf-stable stand-in for the eggs — powdered egg makes something flatter and rubbery. Butter is doing double duty: flavor, and keeping the whole thing from welding itself to cast iron.
