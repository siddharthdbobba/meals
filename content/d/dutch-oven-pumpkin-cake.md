The original is a cupcake recipe with cream cheese frosting, which means muffin tins and a working refrigerator. Neither travels. Batter baked as one cake in a dutch oven gets you the same thing with one vessel, and the frosting becomes a powdered sugar glaze that keeps at ambient temperature indefinitely. It is sweeter and thinner than the frosting. It also will not spoil in the bin on day three.

The pumpkin is doing double duty: moisture and structure, which is why there are no eggs and no milk in the list to begin with. Coal placement matters more than the recipe does. Most of the heat belongs on the lid, or you get a burnt bottom and a raw middle, and the quarter turns are not optional over a real fire where one side always runs hotter.
