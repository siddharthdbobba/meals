Split peas do not need soaking, but they do need time, which makes this a car-camping or basecamp meal rather than a trail dinner. The payoff is that everything in the pot is dry and cheap, and one pound of peas turns into six real servings.

The fresh vegetables and refrigerated ham from the original are the parts that do not travel. Dried potato, carrot, onion, and celery rehydrate in the same two hours the peas need, so nothing is lost by swapping them in. Canned or vacuum-sealed ham handles the heat without a cooler. Watch the bottom of the oven near the end: the soup thickens fast once the peas break down, and a scorched dutch oven is the usual way this goes wrong.
