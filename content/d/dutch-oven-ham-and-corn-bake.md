This is a one-vessel casserole that builds itself in layers and never needs stirring. The ham goes on the bottom because it can take direct bottom heat without drying out, and the batter goes on last because it needs to sit on top of the fill to rise into a lid. Pour it evenly and it bakes into a single crust rather than pooling in one corner.

The camp changes are the dairy. Fresh milk does not travel, so powdered whole milk plus water goes into the batter instead; it hydrates fine and carries the fat the biscuit needs. Powdered egg works too if the cooler is not part of the trip. Coal count matters more than time here: too many under the oven and the ham layer burns before the batter sets.
