This is a Scout dutch oven recipe and it stays one. Two pounds of beef, eggs, and cans of soup mean a cooler and a car, so it is not going in a pack. The browning step is what separates it from a pot of boiled meatballs: the crust holds the balls together through 40 minutes of simmering, and the fond that sticks to the pot ends up in the gravy.

Bread crumbs and egg do the binding, and the amount of crumbs is a judgment call, not a measurement. Ground beef varies in moisture. Add until the mix stops sticking to your palms. Draining the fat after browning is the step most people skip, and skipping it gives you an orange slick on top instead of gravy.
