This one stays as-is instead of getting the trail treatment. It is a car camping and basecamp dinner, and the whole method depends on a heavy lidded pot, real chops, and a cooler. Trying to make it packable would leave you with a different recipe entirely.

The layering is the part to get right. Condensed soup goes on undiluted because the potatoes and onion release plenty of liquid as they cook, and that liquid is what turns the soup into gravy. All the coals go underneath so the bottom simmers and the top steams. Put coals on the lid and the soup scorches before the potatoes give up. Slice the potatoes thin or you will be checking every ten minutes for an hour.
