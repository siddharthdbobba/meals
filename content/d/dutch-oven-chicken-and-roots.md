This is a coal-fired roast, and the coals are the whole skill. Bottom heat cooks the vegetables and boils the broth; top heat browns the bird. Weight the lid heavier than the base and you get an oven instead of a scorched pot. Lifting the lid to peek dumps that heat, so check twice and no more.

The broth is doing more than flavor. Two cups in a covered oven turns to steam, which keeps a lean bird from drying out over 90 minutes and cooks the potatoes without a second pot. Fresh herbs are worth carrying if you have a cooler anyway, but a teaspoon of dried thyme goes in the spice kit and does most of the same work.
