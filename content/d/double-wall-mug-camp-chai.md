The mug this came from was built to keep heat in and hands cool, which is a fair description of what chai needs on a cold morning. On the trail there is no double wall, so the answer is to make it strong and drink it fast.

Boiling the spices before the tea is the part people skip. Whole cardamom and cinnamon need a few minutes in hot water to give anything up, and dropping them in with the tea leaves means you get color without much else. Milk powder replaces fresh milk here and goes in off the heat, because boiling reconstituted milk scorches on a thin backpacking pot faster than you expect.
