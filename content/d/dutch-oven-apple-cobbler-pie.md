This is a cobbler wearing a pie's name. Dry muffin mix on top of wet filling does the same job a crumb topping does: the fruit's own liquid soaks up from below, the butter melts down from above, and the middle sets into a crust without anyone measuring anything.

The pebbles matter more than the recipe. A pie tin flat on cast iron sits right against the bottom coals and scorches in ten minutes. Lift it a half inch and the heat turns into oven heat. Keep most of the coals on the lid for the same reason.
