This is car camping food, not pack food. Four and a half pounds of pork shoulder and three hours of coal management do not fit in anyone's backpack, and the payoff is that you feed eight people from one pot.

The liquid smoke is standing in for a fire pit lined with banana leaves, which is the honest version of what happened here. It works because pork shoulder is mostly collagen and fat: hold it around a low simmer long enough and it falls apart on its own, no technique required. The slaw exists to cut through it. Vinegar and lime against a pot of salty pork is the balance the whole plate depends on, so do not skip it.
