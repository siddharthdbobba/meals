This is a car-camping dessert and it stays one. Refrigerated dough needs a cooler and hot oil needs a heavy pot, so there is no trail version worth carrying. The dutch oven earns its weight here: cast iron holds temperature when cold dough hits the oil, which is the whole problem with frying over a fire.

The seal is the part that goes wrong. Chocolate melts before the dough browns, and any pinch you left open turns into a slick of sugar in the oil that burns and taints the next batch. Pinch hard, fry a few at a time, and sugar them warm so it sticks.
