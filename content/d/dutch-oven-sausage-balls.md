This is a dutch oven recipe and stays one. The sausage supplies both the fat and the leavening liquid, so the baking mix hydrates off the meat instead of off water you have to carry or heat. That is why there is no water in the ingredient list at all.

Heat placement matters more than time. Baking means most of the coals go on the lid; pile them underneath and you get black bottoms and raw centers. One layer only, for the same reason. This one needs a cooler, so it is a car camping or basecamp breakfast, not something to walk in with.
