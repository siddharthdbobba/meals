This is a one-pot dinner for a group, and it works because the pasta cooks in the tomato liquid instead of in a separate pot of water you would have to drain and dump. The starch coming off the macaroni thickens everything into a sauce. That means no strainer, no second pot, and no wastewater to carry away from camp.

The fat from the beef stays in on purpose — it carries the chili powder and keeps the pasta from going gluey. The canned tomatoes with chilies do the seasoning work of a spice rack you did not bring, though a real dose of chili powder and cumin is what separates this from plain tomato noodles. Ground beef is the one cooler-dependent part, so cook it the first night out.
