This is a camp-oven recipe, so it stays one. The heat comes from coals above and below, which is why the beans thicken instead of scorching: the lid holds steam long enough for the sauce to reduce evenly rather than boiling dry against the bottom.

The vinegar and mustard are doing the actual work. Canned beans arrive sweet and one-note, and an hour under coals concentrates that. Acid and sharpness give the sauce somewhere else to go. Bacon fat carries all of it, so render it first and leave it in the pot.
