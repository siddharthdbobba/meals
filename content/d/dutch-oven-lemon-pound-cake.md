Pound cake is a fat-and-egg structure, which is exactly what a camp kitchen usually cannot supply. Butter-flavored shortening and powdered whole egg cover both without a cooler, and they behave close enough to the real thing that the crumb still comes out tight and heavy. Bottled lemon juice is another option, but crystallized lemon packets weigh nothing and do not leak.

The coals do the real work. Too many on the bottom and you get a burnt ring with raw center, which is why the count is lopsided toward the lid. Rotating the oven and the lid in opposite quarters evens out the hot spots from wind. The icing is just sugar, fat, and water, so it sets up in cool air and travels fine on leftovers.
