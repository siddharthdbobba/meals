This one is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Canned biscuit dough needs to stay cold, so it belongs in car camping and basecamp kitchens and nowhere near a backpack. There is no trail version worth writing: dry mixes make a pancake, not pull-apart bread.

The method matters more than the ingredients. Heat from below browns the bottom fast and does almost nothing for the middle, which is why most of the coals go on the lid. Rotating both the pot and the lid every ten minutes evens out the hot spots a fire always has. Butter in the bottom first means the sugar coating melts into a syrup instead of scorching dry against the iron.
