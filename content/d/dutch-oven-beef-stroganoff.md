steps:
  - "Build a fire and let it burn down to coals. You want a moderate frying heat under the oven, roughly 350F, which is about 12 coals underneath a 12 inch oven."
  - "Shake the flour, salt, and pepper together in a gallon zip bag. Add the beef cubes and shake until every piece is coated."
  - "Melt the butter in the dutch oven or a deep skillet set over the coals. Brown the beef in batches, turning often. Crowding it steams the meat instead."
  - "Add the onion and garlic. Cook about 5 minutes, until the onion goes soft and gold."
  - "Pour in the water and broth, then add the mushrooms. Pull coals away until it is just simmering and hold it there about 25 minutes, stirring now and then. The mushrooms will give up a lot of liquid, so it will look thin before it thickens."

This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Beef, butter, and sour cream all need a cooler, and the sour cream is what makes it stroganoff, so there is no honest way to shrink it into a pack without swapping to powders. Cook it the first night, before the cooler ice starts losing.

Two details decide whether it works. The flour coating on the beef is not breading, it is the thickener, and it dissolves into the broth during the simmer. And sour cream curdles above a simmer, so the oven comes off the coals before it goes in. Residual heat is enough to warm it.
