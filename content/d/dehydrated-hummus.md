Store-bought dried hummus is expensive and thin. A ten-ounce tub of the real thing costs less, tastes like what you already buy, and dries down to about four and a half ounces total. The only equipment you need is a dehydrator or an oven that goes low enough not to cook it.

Rehydration is where people ruin it. Chickpea flakes drink slowly, so water added all at once sits on top and then turns the whole bag to soup once it finally absorbs. Cover the flakes, wait, stir, and add more only if it needs it. Olive oil at the end does what the oil in the original tub did before it dried out.
