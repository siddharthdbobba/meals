This is assembly, not cooking. The dutch oven is just a small oven here, and the only real variable is heat management: most of the coals go on the lid so the cheese melts from above before the bun bottoms scorch. Bottom heat is what burns bread, so keep it light down there.

The bun is the reason it works over a fire. It is already baked, so nothing has to rise or cook through, and eight minutes is only about melting cheese and warming sauce. Go easy on the sauce. A wet bun collapses, and there is no fixing it once it is in the oven. Six halves per round is the honest capacity of a 12-inch oven, which means three rounds to feed eight people, so start early and eat in shifts.
