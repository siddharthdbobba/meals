This is a scout dutch oven recipe, mostly unchanged, because it was already built for a fire. Everything but the dough is shelf-stable: canned chicken, canned soup, canned or frozen vegetables, powdered milk. The potatoes and onion keep for a week in a crate without ice.

Two heat settings matter. The frying stage wants coals underneath so the potatoes actually soften, and the baking stage wants them mostly on the lid so the dough browns instead of scorching on the bottom. Move the coals when you put the lid on. The flour and milk slurry is what turns two cans of soup into gravy that holds a crust; add it as a liquid, never dry, or you get lumps you cannot stir out.
