Round roast is cheap and lean, which is why the tenderizer and the wine both earn their place. A real burgundy braise runs two hours; fifteen minutes of simmer will not break down connective tissue on its own, so the tenderizer does that work chemically and the small cubes keep the distance heat has to travel short.

The instant gravy is the camp shortcut. It thickens without a roux, without flour dust, and without whisking over uneven coals. Sour cream goes in last and off the heat because the acid and the low fat content will curdle if it sees a boil. If you are more than a day from a cooler, use the powdered version in the variations and nothing about the method changes.
