  - "Get a bed of coals going. You want about 20 briquettes or an equivalent pile of hardwood embers."

Apple cider doughnuts are a fryer trick, and nobody is hauling a fryer to a campsite. The part worth keeping is the cider reduction. Boiling two cups down to one concentrates the sugar and the acid, so the cake tastes like apple instead of tasting like flour that met an apple once. Doing that step in the same dutch oven you bake in saves a pot and a wash.

Whole wheat pastry flour is the whole point of the gentle mixing. Its bran cuts gluten strands but also soaks up liquid fast, so an over-stirred batter goes to brick. Mix until it is barely combined and no further. The butter brush and cinnamon sugar at the end are not decoration; they are the crust the fryer would have given you.
