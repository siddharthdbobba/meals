Lean beef is the whole point of the 5% fat rule. Fat does not dehydrate, it just sits in the food and turns rancid over the weeks the bag spends on a shelf, so the leaner the meat the longer the meal keeps. The calories you lose that way come back at camp as a tablespoon of olive oil, which you can carry safely and add at the last minute.

Rice and sauce dry at very different rates, which is why they go on separate trays. Dried cooked rice rehydrates in the same twelve minutes the sauce needs, so they can share a pot once both are brittle. Dry the sauce until it snaps rather than bends; anything still tacky has water left in it and will spoil.
