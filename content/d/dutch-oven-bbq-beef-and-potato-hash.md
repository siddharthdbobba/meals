This is camp cooking that trades weight for simplicity. Three pounds of beef, a couple of potatoes, and one pot going over coals for half an hour. It is not backpacking food and it does not pretend to be — the meat and eggs need a cooler, so this is a car-camping or basecamp dinner cooked the day you drive in.

The eggs mixed into the raw beef are the part that looks odd and matters most. They bind the meat so it browns in clumps rather than dissolving into gravel, which gives the hash texture once the potatoes go in. Cut the potatoes small; anything bigger than half an inch will still be raw when the beef is done. The BBQ sauce is a seasoning here, not a sauce — two teaspoons across three pounds is background, so taste before you salt.
