This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe, and it earns the weight. The marinade does the work overnight: pineapple juice carries bromelain, which breaks down connective tissue, and tri tip is a lean cut that needs exactly that. Twelve hours in the bag is the difference between tender and chewy.

The lid matters more than the fire. A dutch oven traps the steam coming off two cups of sauce, so the roast braises in its own marinade instead of drying out over coals. Sugar plus coals means scorching, so rotate the oven and the lid in opposite directions every quarter hour and nothing sits over one hot spot long enough to burn. Leftovers slice cold into tortillas the next day.
