Puttanesca without tomatoes is mostly a salt delivery problem, and that suits the trail. Olives, capers, and anchovy are all preserved by design, so the flavor base carries at room temperature for weeks. The anchovies dissolve into the oil and stop reading as fish. What is left is depth.

The pasta cooks in just enough water to be absorbed, so nothing gets drained and the starch stays in the pot to bind the sauce. Measure the 700 ml. Too much and you are eating pasta soup; too little and the noodles seize before they soften. Fresh spinach and scallions do not travel, so freeze-dried greens and dried chives stand in and rehydrate in the last minute of heat.
