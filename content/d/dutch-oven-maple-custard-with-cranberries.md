The original is an oven pie with sour cream, heavy cream, and five fresh eggs, none of which survive a weekend in a car. Powdered egg, cream, and buttermilk rebuild the same custard from bags, and citric acid does what the lemon juice was there for: cut the maple and keep the dairy from tasting flat. Ghee replaces the melted butter because it does not need a cooler.

The crust is pressed rather than rolled. Rolling out dough on a picnic table is a losing project, and a cornmeal crumb base pressed straight into the Dutch oven bakes to the same purpose. Coal placement is the part that actually decides whether this works: most of the heat belongs on the lid. Too many coals underneath and the bottom scorches before the center of the custard sets.
