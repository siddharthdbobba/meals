The original calls for twelve potatoes, two onions, celery, and a quart of half-and-half. None of that survives a night in a pack. Dried potatoes and milk powder do the same job at a fraction of the weight, and they rehydrate faster than raw cubes simmer.

Heat control is the whole recipe after the cheese goes in. Cheddar breaks above a simmer and separates into oil and rubber, so move the pot off the coals before it goes in and keep stirring. The butter is not optional either — it is most of the calories and it keeps the flakes from setting into paste.
