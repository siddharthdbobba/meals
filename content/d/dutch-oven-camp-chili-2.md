Dutch oven cooking runs on coals, not flames, and this recipe is built around that. An hour of steady low heat is what turns dry beans soft, and a cast iron pot buried in coals holds that temperature far better than a stove ever will. Everything else is timed around the beans.

The overnight soak is the one step you cannot skip at camp, so it happens the night before. Salt and tomato both go in at the end for the same reason: acid and salt slow the beans from softening, and adding them early means an hour of cooking that still leaves you chewing. Masa at the finish thickens the pot without adding another pan.
