The original wanted six eggs, a cup of half and half, and a ripe tomato. None of that survives a weekend in a car. Whole egg powder and milk powder rebuild the custard from dry goods, and olive oil puts back the fat the cream was carrying. Sun-dried tomatoes and dried greens go in dry and pull moisture out of the egg mix, which is why the five-minute rest matters.

Coal count is the whole game. Roughly two thirds on the lid gives you top heat that sets the surface while the bottom cooks slower, which is what keeps the eggs from turning into a burnt disc with a raw middle. Rotate both oven and lid or you will find the hot spot the hard way. Sausage and feta still need a cooler, so this is a first- or second-day breakfast.
