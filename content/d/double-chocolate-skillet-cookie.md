Twenty-four separate cookies on two sheet pans is an oven problem. A fire is not an oven, so this bakes as one thick round instead, which forgives uneven coals: the center stays soft while the edge sets against the hot metal, and that is the texture the original was chasing anyway.

The substitutions are the load-bearing part. Butter is fridge food and creaming it needs a mixer, so oil goes in cold and does the same job of coating the flour. Powdered egg plus a little water replaces the fresh egg. Because unsalted butter is gone, salt gets added back on purpose, and cocoa this dark needs it.
