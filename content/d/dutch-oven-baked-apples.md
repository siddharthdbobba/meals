Nothing here is really candied. The brown sugar melts into the pineapple juice in the bottom of the oven and steams the apples from below while the coals on the lid work on them from above. That is what makes them collapse instead of just going warm.

The juice matters more than it looks. A dry dutch oven scorches the sugar and the apple skins split before the middles soften. Keep half an inch of liquid on the floor and you can leave the oven alone for the full thirty minutes. This one is car camping only: whole apples and a can are heavy, and a 12-inch dutch oven is not going in a pack.
