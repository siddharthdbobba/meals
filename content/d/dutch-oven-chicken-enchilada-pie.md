This is a group dinner that scales to a patrol and cooks itself once the coals are set. Everything in the sauce comes out of a can, so the only cooler items are the cheese and the onion. The condensed tomato soup is not filler: it thickens the enchilada sauce enough to hold the layers together and takes some of the sharp edge off the chile.

Build the layers from the bottom up with sauce first, or the tortillas weld to the cast iron. The finished pie is deliberately loose. If you want it firmer, hold back half the water and check it at twenty minutes.
