This is a shelf-stable sandwich filling from the Great Depression that turned out to be trail-perfect. Deviled ham is intensely salty—exactly what your body needs to replace sodium and electrolytes—and the mayo and peanut butter buffer that saltiness while delivering dense calories. None of it requires refrigeration if you use packets.

The real trick is the combination. Canned ham alone is too dry; mayo alone is too bland. Together they become something that sticks to bread and stays interesting for a full day of hiking. Mix fresh at camp to avoid a stale sandwich, or pre-mix at home and work through it in the first few days of a trip.
