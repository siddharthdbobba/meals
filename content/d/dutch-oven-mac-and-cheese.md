Three cheeses do three jobs. Cheddar is the flavor, mozzarella is the pull, parmesan is the salt. Drop any one and the pot goes flat in a way more of the others will not fix.

The jarred alfredo is the reason this works over a fire. A scratch roux wants steady heat and constant attention, and coals give you neither. The jar is already emulsified, so the cheese has something to melt into instead of breaking into grease and curds. Keep the heat low and stir often anyway, because cast iron holds more heat than you think and scorched cheese does not come off easily.
