Instant rice is what makes this work over coals. Regular rice wants twenty-five minutes of steady heat and a lot of guessing about your coal bed; instant rice just needs the pot to reach a boil once and then hold warm. The shrimp cook in the same window, which is why they go in last and why nobody stirs after that.

Coals underneath only for the frying stage, then a small number under and most on the lid for the simmer. Too much bottom heat scorches rice into the cast iron and you will be scraping it in the dark. The tomato juice counts toward the liquid, so do not drain the can.
