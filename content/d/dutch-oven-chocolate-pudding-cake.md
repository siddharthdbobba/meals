This bakes into two layers from one pan. The sauce mix goes on top as a thin liquid, sinks through the batter as it sets, and ends up as a pudding under a cake. That only works if you leave it alone after pouring. Stir it in and you get one muddy layer that never sets.

The original calls for fresh milk, which is a fridge item. Powdered milk plus water does the same job and rides in the dry bag all trip. Oil instead of butter for the same reason, though butter is worth carrying if you have a cooler running anyway. Coal placement matters more than time here: most of the heat belongs on the lid, or the bottom scorches before the middle sets.
