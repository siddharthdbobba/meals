This is a dump crisp, so the only technique in it is the butter. Cutting cold butter into the dry cookie mix leaves solid pieces that melt in the oven and steam the crumble apart. That is what turns a bag of cookie mix into a crunchy, granola-like top instead of one flat cookie sheet sitting on wet fruit. Warm butter smears and you lose that.

Heat management matters more than the recipe does. Roughly twice as many coals on the lid as underneath keeps the bottom from scorching while the top browns. Charcoal loses heat over 50 minutes, so plan on refreshing partway through. Foil lining is not optional if you want to leave the site without scrubbing caramelized fruit sugar out of cast iron.
