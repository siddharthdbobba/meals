Coffee cake is forgiving to bake in the field because the crumb does not need to be light or delicate. The appeal is all in the topping—the brown sugar and cinnamon caramelize at the edges as the cake bakes, creating a crust that contrasts with the tender cake underneath. The butter keeps the crumb moist even if you slightly overbake it.

Baking in a Dutch oven over coals comes down to patience and coal placement. Arrange coals around and underneath the oven, not directly against it. If the bottom is browning too fast, lift the oven up and move it slightly away from the heat. If it is taking more than 25 minutes, your coals are probably cooling—add a few more. A smaller Dutch oven will bake faster and more evenly than a large one.
