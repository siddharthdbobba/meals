Fresh peaches are mostly water, and water is the one thing you do not haul. Dried peaches carry the same sugar and flavor at a fraction of the weight, and rehydrating them in the pot builds the syrup the original recipe gets from the fruit's own juice. The measured 1 1/2 cups replaces both the fruit juice and the splash of water in the home version.

The cake mix does two jobs: it thickens the fruit into a sauce and forms the crust on top. Stirring it in rather than sprinkling it dry means no chalky pockets, which is the usual failure with dump cobblers. Coals on the lid are what browns the top; heat from below alone will burn the bottom before the middle sets.
