Cornbread mix already contains the flour, leavener, and salt a cookie needs, which is why this works with so little added. The mix is dry and heavy on cornmeal, so the dough comes out stiff and holds its shape on the lid instead of spreading into one sheet.

The home version calls for softened butter and two fresh eggs. Ghee replaces the butter without a cooler and behaves the same when creamed, and powdered egg rehydrated in a cup covers the binding. Coals run hotter than an oven, so watch the bottoms rather than the clock and take the lid off early the first time.
