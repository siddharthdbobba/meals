Bread pudding is a good camp dessert because it wants stale bread and it forgives bad temperature control. The original called for eggs and half and half out of a cooler. Powdered whole egg and milk powder mixed rich do the same job with no fridge, and they let you build the custard in a bottle instead of hauling a mixing bowl.

The soak matters more than the bake. Twenty minutes gives the croissant layers time to pull the liquid in; skip it and you get wet bread sitting in scrambled custard. Coals on the lid are what brown the top, so keep more heat above than below or the bottom scorches before the middle sets.
