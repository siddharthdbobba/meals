This is a home oven recipe pushed into a dutch oven, which is where it wants to live anyway. Butter and brown sugar in the bottom of the pot melt into a caramel that the peaches sit in, and the whole thing turns over at the end so the fruit is on top. Cornmeal is the reason it works over coals: it bakes into a coarse, sturdy crumb that tolerates uneven bottom heat better than a fine flour cake would.

The fridge ingredients are gone. Whole milk powder and powdered egg replace the milk and the egg, and both go into the dry premix at home so camp is one bag plus water. Butter stays, because nothing else browns sugar the same way; keep it in the cooler or use it the first two days out. Coal placement matters more than time here. Twice as many on the lid as underneath, or the bottom scorches before the batter sets.
