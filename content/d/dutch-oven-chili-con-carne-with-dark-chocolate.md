This is a car camping meal and it is honest about that. Two hours of coal management, a cooler full of raw meat, and a pot heavy enough to be its own workout. There is no trail version of it worth writing.

The chocolate is doing structural work, not dessert work. Cocoa solids are bitter and tannic, which is exactly what a pot of fat and tomato paste needs to stop tasting flat after the third bite. Add it at the end so it melts into the sauce rather than scorching against the iron. The vinegar is there for the same reason from the other direction.

The dietary tag is gluten-free only in spirit: check your beer, since most of it is barley. Use a gluten-free beer or broth if that matters to anyone eating.
