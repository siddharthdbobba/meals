Cheese fries are a two-problem dish on the trail: frozen fries and cold milk. Dehydrated hash browns solve the first, and they crisp better than frozen shoestrings ever will over coals, because you control how much water goes back into them. Whole milk powder solves the second. Reconstituted at full strength it makes a bechamel that behaves like the real thing, and the fat in it keeps the gruyere from breaking.

Country ham and duck confit are both preserved by design, so neither needs a cooler for a weekend. The mustard is not a garnish. Cheese sauce without acid reads flat and heavy after a mile of walking, and a teaspoon of whole-grain cuts through the whole thing.
