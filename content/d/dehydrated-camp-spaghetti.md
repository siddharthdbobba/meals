Fat is what ruins dried meat, so the beef gets drained hard before it ever sees the dehydrator. Fat goes rancid in a bag; lean crumbles do not. That is also why the beef needs the longest lead time in camp, a half hour cold soak before the stove even lights, and why it still takes fifteen minutes of simmering after that.

The other trick is not draining the pasta. Sauce leather needs liquid to become sauce again, and the starchy noodle water does that job better than fresh water while saving you a second boil and a strainer you did not bring. Cook for four and it is one pot, one burner, and one spoon.
