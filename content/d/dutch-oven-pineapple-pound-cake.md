Self-rising flour is the reason this is a camp cake and not a project. There is no baking powder to measure, no butter to cream, and no soft-vs-cold temperature to manage. Oil replaces butter throughout, which also means nothing in the cooler has to survive the drive.

The canned pineapple does two jobs. Its juice is the only liquid the batter gets besides the reconstituted egg, and the fruit keeps the crumb wet enough to forgive an uneven fire. Heat placement is where this goes wrong: most of your coals belong on the lid. Coals underneath scorch the bottom long before the middle sets.
