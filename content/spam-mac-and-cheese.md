Boxed mac is cheap, light, and about 250 calories a serving of almost pure
carbohydrate. The can of Spam is what makes it dinner: it roughly doubles the
calories and takes the protein from eight grams to the mid thirties.

Fry it hard. Warm Spam stirred into pasta is limp; crisp Spam holds its edges
and reads as an ingredient rather than an apology.
