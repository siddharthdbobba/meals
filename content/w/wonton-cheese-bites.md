Rangoon is a cooler problem, not a cooking problem. A block of cream cheese is heavy, spoils, and turns to soup at 80 degrees, so the filling here is cream cheese powder rehydrated at camp with a quarter cup of water. It comes out stiffer than the block, which is what you want: loose filling blows the seams open in the oven.

The wrappers are the one thing that still needs cold, and they are why this is a car-camping recipe. They keep about a week refrigerated and longer frozen, so pack them frozen and use them the first two days. Dried chives and garlic powder do the aromatics; powdered egg does the sealing. Nothing else in the bag cares about temperature.
