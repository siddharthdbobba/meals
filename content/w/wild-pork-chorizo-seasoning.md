Chorizo is a seasoning problem, not a sausage problem. There is no casing here and no grinder in camp: you buy or bring ground meat, add a blend you already measured at home, and the acid plus salt does the curing work in the ten minutes it takes to get the fire honest. Wild hog is leaner than store pork, so the extra fat is not optional if you want it to fry rather than steam.

The two paprikas and two chiles are doing different jobs. Smoked paprika and chipotle carry the smoke, ancho and sweet paprika carry the color and body, and the brown sugar keeps the whole thing from reading as pure heat. Mix a double batch of spice at home; it keeps in a jar indefinitely and turns any ground meat you find on the road into dinner.
