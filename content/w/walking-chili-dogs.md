This one is built around the bag, not the pot. The onion rings are the bun, the bowl, and the crunch, so nothing gets soggy and there are no plates to wash — you scrape one skillet and you are done. That makes it a good move for a group at a car campsite when the fire is already going.

Velveeta is doing the structural work. Real shredded cheese breaks into grease and strings when it sits over a fire, while processed cheese melts into the chili and stays there, which is what lets the mixture sit warm at the edge of the grate while people fill their bags. The sour cream is the only thing here that needs a cooler; drop it and the rest holds at ambient temperature until the can is opened.
