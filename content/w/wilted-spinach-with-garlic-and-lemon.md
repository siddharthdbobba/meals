Spinach stems are food, not trash. Left whole, a young cluster cooks like a small vegetable: the stem holds some snap while the leaves collapse. Older spinach with thick stems needs a head start, so cut the stems off as a bunch, give them a minute in the pan, then throw the leaves on at the end.

The garlic oil is the whole dish. Toast the slices slowly in a neutral oil that can take the heat, then kill the flame before they darken past gold. The good olive oil and the lemon go on off-heat, where they still taste like themselves. This is cooler food, not backpacking food — fresh greens hold two days at best, so cook it early in the trip.
