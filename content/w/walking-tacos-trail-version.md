The original is a car-camping meal built on fresh lettuce, shredded cheese, and sour cream, none of which travel. So the whole topping bar gets replaced with powders: cheddar powder, sour cream powder, and dried salsa all rehydrate off the same boil that softens the meat, and none of them need a cooler.

The bean flakes are the structural piece. Rehydrated ground beef alone stays loose and runs to the bottom of the bag; the beans thicken it enough to sit on a chip. Crushing the chips before you open the bag matters too — whole chips shatter into shards you can't spoon, and the bag is your only bowl.
