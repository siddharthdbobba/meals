This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Steak, butter, and a hot cast iron skillet do not travel in a backpack, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp where you can carry weight and keep meat cold.

The original flames the whiskey in the pan for show. Off the heat and near a fire ring that is a bad idea, so the glaze here reduces on residual heat instead. It takes a minute longer and ends up the same: the sugar thickens, the alcohol cooks off, and the fond from the sear dissolves into the syrup. Preheating the skillet slowly matters more than people expect, since cast iron over uneven coals will scorch one side of a filet and leave the other grey.
