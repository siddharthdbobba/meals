The scout-camp version cooks ground beef in a skillet and heats canned beans. Neither travels. Freeze-dried beef and refried bean flakes give you the same bowl of chili from a quarter of the weight and one pot of hot water, and the flakes are what thicken it — pour the water in all at once and you get soup that soaks the chips flat.

The chip bag doing double duty as the bowl is the whole point. Cut it along the side rather than the end so the chips lie flat and you can reach the bottom. Nothing to scrub, and the bag packs out as your trash either way.
