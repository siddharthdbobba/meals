This is the pie taken apart and reassembled in a bag. Crumbs stand in for crust, pudding cups stand in for the filling and the curd, and canned cream replaces the meringue nobody wants to whip over a picnic table. Nothing bakes, nothing sets, and nothing gets washed afterward.

The only cold-chain item is the cream, so keep it in the cooler and treat the rest as pantry. Use a heavy freezer bag rather than a sandwich bag — pudding finds every weak seam, and a leak in the bottom of a food bag ends the dessert early.
