The original is a six-hour crockpot recipe built on canned beans, fresh chicken, and dairy stirred in at the end. None of that travels. Bean flakes replace the cans and do double duty as the thickener, freeze-dried chicken replaces the shredded breasts, and dried onion, celery, and chile stand in for the vegetables that were only there to give the broth a base.

The two things to get right are heat and order. Sour cream powder goes in after the pot is off the boil, or it separates. Acid goes in last, because lime added early cooks flat. The bean flakes keep swelling for a few minutes after you stop stirring, so leave it looser than you want it.
