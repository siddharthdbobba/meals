The original is a whole roasted chicken, three hours, and a dutch oven. What survives the translation is the spice load. Cumin, smoked paprika, coriander, and oregano do most of the flavor work, and they weigh nothing, so they come along at full strength.

The substitutions are the recipe. Dried beans will not cook on a canister stove without burning a whole fuel can, so bean flakes stand in and thicken the pot at the same time. Freeze-dried chicken rehydrates in the same eight minutes the corn needs. Sour cream and shredded cheese are the two things that cannot travel, so the fat comes from oil and the crunch comes from corn chips crushed in at the end.
