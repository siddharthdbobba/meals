The original is a boiled preserve: fresh plums stuffed with walnut and simmered in syrup until they candy. That needs a stove, a jar, and an hour you do not have. Prunes are already the finished product of the drying half, so the recipe collapses into stuffing and coating. No cooking, no water, nothing to spoil.

The sugar coat is doing two jobs. It pulls surface moisture off so the prunes do not weld together in the bag, and it puts fast carbohydrate next to the walnut fat. That pairing is the whole point on a long climb: the sugar hits now, the nut keeps working for the next hour.
