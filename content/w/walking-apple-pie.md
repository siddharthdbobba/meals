This is a dessert built around a container you already have. The cracker bag is the bowl, the crushed cookies are the crust, and nothing gets dirty except a spoon. That matters more at a campsite with one wash tub than the flavor does.

The canned filling is the reason it works cold. The apples are already cooked soft and spiced, so there is nothing to heat and nothing to rehydrate. Whipped cream is the one item that needs a cooler, which is why this stays a car-camping recipe. Drop it and use a pudding cup if you want the same thing out of a pack.
