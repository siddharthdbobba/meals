Everything here is a powder, which is the whole point. Milk powder and egg powder do the job of the fridge ingredients in a home pancake recipe, and they weigh almost nothing once the water is gone. The mix goes together at home in five minutes and then sits in a bag until you want it.

Whole wheat is the reason this holds you past mid-morning, but it also drinks more water than white flour and browns sooner. Let the batter rest a couple of minutes before it hits the pan, and run the stove lower than feels right. Three tablespoons of baking powder across four cups of flour sounds heavy; it is what keeps a whole-grain batter from cooking flat.
