This is a layered dessert built inside the packaging it came in, which means no bowl and no plate. The crumbs stay dry until you eat them, so the crust never turns to paste the way it does in an assembled pie.

Canned pie filling is doing all the work: it is already sweetened and spiced, so nothing has to be measured in the dark. The catch is that an opened can does not keep, so treat this as a night-one dessert or scale the servings to finish the can. Powdered topping mix is the version that survives a whole trip without a cooler.
