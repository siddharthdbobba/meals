Ramps are a foraged, day-one ingredient, so this stays a car-camping or basecamp dish rather than a trail dinner. The only real technique is washing. They grow in leaf litter and hold grit down in the sheath, so a lazy rinse means sand in every bite. Two changes of water, and split the fat bulbs open before the last one.

Butter does the rest. The bulbs are sharp raw and go sweet as they soften, and fat is what carries that flavor instead of boiling it off. Keep the heat moderate: ramps scorch faster than onions do, and burnt garlic is what they taste like when you push them. Ghee holds up better than butter if the cooler is not a real cooler.
