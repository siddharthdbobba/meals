The whole point is that the bag is the bowl. Crushed graham cookies coat a hot marshmallow the way a cracker sandwich never quite does, and they also do the work of pulling it off the roasting stick, which is the part of a normal s'more that ends up on someone's hands.

Order matters. Marshmallow first, chocolate second, because the residual heat is what melts the chocolate. Reverse it and you get a cold bar sitting on the bottom of the bag. Everything here is shelf-stable, though the chocolate will soften above roughly 85F and set again into a lumpier shape. That costs nothing but looks.
