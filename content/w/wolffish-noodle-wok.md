The original is a hot pan, fresh fish, and crisp vegetables. None of that survives a pack, so this version leans on freeze-dried fish and dried vegetables and gives them a proper soak before anything else happens. Rushing that step is the one way to ruin this: undersoaked vegetables stay leathery and undersoaked fish goes stringy.

Sweet chili sauce is the whole flavor budget, and it goes in off the heat. Boiled, it turns thin and loses its edge. The oil matters more than it looks like it should, since a wok normally supplies fat and boiling water supplies none.
