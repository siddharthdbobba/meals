The slow-cooker original spends four hours doing one thing: getting beans, chicken, and dairy to melt into each other. On trail you have ten minutes and one pot, so the work moves to the shopping list. Instant bean flakes thicken on contact instead of simmering, and freeze-dried chicken rehydrates in the same five minutes the chiles and corn need. That is the whole trade.

Cream cheese and heavy cream do not travel, so powdered whole milk plus a tablespoon of oil stands in. The powder brings the milk solids that make it read as creamy; the oil brings the fat and most of the calories. Add the powder off heat and slowly, or you get white lumps that never dissolve. Skip the oil and this becomes a thin bean soup with chicken in it.
