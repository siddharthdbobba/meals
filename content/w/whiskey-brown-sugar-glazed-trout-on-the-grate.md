This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe. Fresh fish and butter both need ice, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp where the cooler stays shut.

The glaze is a reduction: butter and sugar for body, soy sauce and lemon for the salt and acid that keep it from reading as dessert. Reduce it before the fish goes on. Brushed thin over a fillet that is already mostly cooked, it sets into a lacquer instead of running off into the coals. Brushing it on cold fish at minute zero just burns the sugar.

A soaked cedar plank keeps the fillet off the bars so it lifts in one piece. Foil does the same job with less ceremony.
