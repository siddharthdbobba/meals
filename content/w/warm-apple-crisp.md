Soak the fruit during dinner and you're only five minutes from warm dessert. The apples soften without falling apart. The cranberries are tart enough to keep the whole thing from cloying.

Graham crackers replace the fat and crunch of a crisp topping but fit flat in a bag. Powdered butter distributes evenly instead of pooling at the bottom. Three bags and one pot make this work on the trail.
