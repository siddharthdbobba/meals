steps:
  - "Wipe the inside of a 12-inch dutch oven with oil. Scatter a third of the jalapeno slices across the bottom."
  - "Tear two tortillas into rough one-inch pieces and spread them over the peppers. Add a cup of cheese, then a third of the ham."
  - "Repeat twice more. Three layers, ending with ham."
  - "Beat the eggs with the milk, cumin, pepper, salt, onion powder, and garlic powder. Pour it evenly over the stack."

This is a strata, which means the bread does the structural work. Tortillas are the camp version: they hold their shape in a hot dutch oven better than sandwich bread, and they do not turn to paste. The rest is layered so every serving gets peppers, cheese, and ham instead of the fat all pooling at the bottom.

The 20-minute soak is not optional. Dry tortilla under baked egg is chewy in a bad way, and the wait is free time while the coals come up to temperature anyway. Keep more heat on the lid than under it or the bottom scorches before the custard sets. This one stays a cooler recipe by design — ten real eggs and a pound of cheese have no shelf-stable equal worth eating.
