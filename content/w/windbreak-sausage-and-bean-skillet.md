Camp stoves lose more heat to wind than most people expect, which is why this cooks in one pot on low and never asks for a rolling boil. A windscreen or a propped lid buys back most of the fuel a gusty site steals.

The tomato paste is doing the work. Fried in oil before liquid goes in, it turns from sour to savory and gives canned beans something to taste like. Skip that minute and you get warm beans in pink water. The optional polenta is there for the night the drive ran long and two servings need to become three.
