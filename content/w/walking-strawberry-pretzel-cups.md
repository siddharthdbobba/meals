This is a layered pretzel-and-berry dessert with the pan removed. Nothing gets baked, nothing gets cut into squares, and the bag is both the mixing bowl and the dish. Ten of them assemble in the time it takes to cut one pan.

The order of the layers is the whole trick. Pudding goes in first so the gelatin sits above the pretzels instead of soaking straight through them, and the crushed pretzels on top stay dry until the last bite. Assemble one at a time as people ask for them. A tray built twenty minutes early comes apart into sweet mush.

This one needs a cooler. Pudding, whipped topping, and cut strawberries are all cold-chain items, so it is a car camping and basecamp dessert, not something to carry up a hill.
