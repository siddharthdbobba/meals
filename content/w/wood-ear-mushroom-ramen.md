The original is a two-hour project built on a roasted chicken carcass. That broth does not travel, so this version rebuilds it out of bouillon and miso: bouillon for the salt and body, miso stirred in off the heat for the fermented depth that a spice packet never has. Add the miso to boiling water and it goes flat and one-note, which is why it goes in last.

Dried mushrooms are the real reason this works on trail. They weigh almost nothing, they rehydrate in the same pot as the noodles, and the wood ears keep a rubbery snap that nothing else in a backpacking dinner has. Give them a five-minute head start before the noodles or they stay stiff.
