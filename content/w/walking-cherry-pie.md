Everything about this recipe is a decision to not wash anything. The cracker bag is the crust, the bowl, and the trash. A can of pie filling is the only heavy item, and a squeeze pouch removes even that.

Order matters more than it looks like it should. Crumbs first, filling second, topping last. Stir it and you get sweet mush; layer it and the crackers stay crisp long enough to finish. The whipped topping is the one part that needs thought away from a cooler, so the powdered mix is here as the real answer and the aerosol can as the car-camping shortcut.
