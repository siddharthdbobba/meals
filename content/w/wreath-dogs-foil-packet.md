This started as a holiday party centerpiece baked in an oven. The wreath shape survives the move to a fire because it is actually useful: sausages packed shoulder to shoulder in a ring hold each other up, so the dough sets before anything tips over, and the middle of the packet is empty where a fire runs hottest.

Crescent dough is the one thing here that needs a cooler, which makes this a car-camping recipe and a first-night one. Everything else is stable — vacuum-packed smokies, shredded parmesan out of a can, dried herbs. The butter and seasoning go on after cooking, not before; brushed on early they scorch against the foil. Foil heat is uneven, so rotate the packet once and expect the side that faced the coals to be darker.
