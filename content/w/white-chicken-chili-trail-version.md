The original is a slow cooker recipe: eight hours, a block of cream cheese, a pile of fresh vegetables. None of that travels. What survives the move is the flavor structure, which is really just salsa verde, cumin, and white beans. Dry the salsa into a leather at home and it carries in three tablespoons instead of a jar.

Bean flakes replace both the whole beans and the cream cheese. They rehydrate off the heat in a minute and thicken the pot at the same time, which is the job the dairy was doing. Milk powder and oil put the fat back. Add the lime at the end, after the heat is off, or it flattens out.
