This exists because a watermelon at camp leaves behind more rind than melon, and the white layer between skin and flesh is firm, bland, and takes sugar the way a fruit rarely does. It has almost no flavor of its own, so lemon and vanilla do the actual work.

The five-minute pre-boil is not optional. Raw rind stays chalky and never goes clear no matter how long it sits in syrup. Cook it low after that; a hard boil turns the sugar to candy and the cubes to gravel. Watch for translucency, not the clock.
