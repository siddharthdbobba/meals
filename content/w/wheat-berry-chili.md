Wheat berries do the job ground beef usually does in a chili: they hold their shape, chew back, and soak up the spice without turning to mush. They also cost almost nothing and keep forever in a bag, which is more than you can say for meat in a cooler.

The honey and the ras el hanout are what keep this from tasting like plain beans. A tablespoon of sweetness pulls the acid out of the canned tomatoes, and the spice blend covers ground that chili powder alone does not. Soak the wheat berries overnight before you leave and the pot time drops to something a campfire can handle without a long fuel burn.
