Tough game meat is why the original recipe simmers for two hours. Drying it at home does that work early: the meat is already cooked through, so camp is just rehydration and twenty minutes of holding heat. Cook it, shred it, dry it, and it comes back soft instead of chewy.

The canned tomatoes, fresh onion, celery, and baking potatoes all became dry equivalents, which is most of the weight savings. Flour still thickens the broth the way it does at home, but potato flakes finish the job without the extra hour that real potato chunks demand. The vinegar at the end stands in for the wine and cuts the fat.
