Jam usually gets stirred into pulp. This one does not, and that is the whole point. The syrup is cooked to thickness first, then the berries go in off the heat and are never touched with a spoon again. Swirling the pot moves the syrup around them instead of through them, so they come out intact.

This is a car-camping recipe, not a backpacking one. Fresh raspberries do not survive a pack, and the sugar load makes it heavy for what it is. Cooked properly it is shelf-stable in a sealed jar at home, but a camp jar opened with a camp spoon is not sterile, so treat it as a two-day food and keep it cool. The lemon juice at the end is for set, not flavor; the acid is what makes the syrup grip.
