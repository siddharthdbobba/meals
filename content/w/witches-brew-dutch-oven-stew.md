This is a scout-camp pot meal built around a fact most people fight: canned liquid is free water. Nothing gets drained here. The tomato juice, bean liquid, and mushroom liquid add up to about enough to cook two cups of dry noodles, which means no measuring cup and no second pot. The noodles also thicken the whole thing as their starch comes out, so it finishes as a stew rather than soup with things floating in it.

The bacon fat is the other structural piece. Pour off all of it and the beans and noodles taste flat; leave it all and the top goes greasy. A spoonful or two is the middle. Cayenne at a full teaspoon across six servings is warm, not hot, but cut it in half if you are feeding a mixed group — you can always pass hot sauce, you cannot take it back out.
