Mushrooms carry a chili the way beef does, but only if you treat them like meat. The soak and the sear matter. Skip the sear and they stay spongy and release water into the pot for the whole simmer, which leaves you with bean soup.

The soy sauce looks out of place and is the most important thing here. Meat brings glutamates; without it the chili tastes flat no matter how much chili powder goes in. Two tablespoons fixes that. The mushroom soaking water is doing the same job, which is why it goes in the pot instead of on the ground. Original calls for fresh peppers roasted over a burner and a food processor for the onion and garlic; dried pepper and onion flakes get close and remove the two things you cannot do in camp.
