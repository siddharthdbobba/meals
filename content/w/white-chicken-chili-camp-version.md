The original simmers a whole chicken breast in a box of broth for an hour. That is an hour of fuel and four cups of water you have to carry or filter, so both go. Freeze-dried chicken rehydrates in five minutes and arrives already shredded, and bouillon turns plain water into broth at a fraction of the weight.

Instant refried bean flakes do double duty. They stand in for the canned white beans and they thicken the pot, which is what canned beans and a long simmer were doing together. Sour cream and shredded cheese are out with no cooler; the olive oil covers the fat they were adding, and the corn chips keep the crunch.
