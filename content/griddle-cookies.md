A dutch oven bakes better cookies. A griddle makes them in ten minutes without
burying anything in coals, which is the difference between dessert happening and
dessert being discussed and abandoned.

Coals, never flame. The sugar in the dough will scorch long before the centre
cooks if the pan is too hot.
