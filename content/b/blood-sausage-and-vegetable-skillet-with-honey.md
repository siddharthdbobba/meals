This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal. Blood sausage and five fresh vegetables have no shelf-stable version worth pretending about, so it belongs at a car camp or basecamp where you have a cooler and a real skillet.

The honey is the part that makes it work. Blood sausage is heavy and mineral, and a couple of teaspoons of honey caramelizing against the pan cuts through that instead of just sweetening it. Fry the vegetables in batches for the same reason: browned edges give the dish something to push against. The fennel and paprika go in dry, briefly, so the fat picks them up before the tomatoes release their liquid.
