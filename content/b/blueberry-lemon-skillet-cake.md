The original leans on ricotta, three eggs, and a stick of butter, none of which survive a weekend in a hatchback. Whole milk powder and buttermilk powder together do most of what the ricotta did, which is add fat and a little acid, and the acid is what makes the baking powder work. Egg powder rehydrates fine in a batter this wet. Ghee replaces butter and does not care about temperature.

Soaking the berries first is the part people skip. Dried blueberries are thirsty, and dropped in dry they steal water from the crumb and leave you with a scone. The quarter cup scattered on top stays dry on purpose, so it firms into something closer to candy against the lid heat.
