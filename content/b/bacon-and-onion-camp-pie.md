The original was a refrigerator recipe: chilled crumb crust, fresh eggs, a home oven. Camp version drops the chilling step because 30 minutes of refrigeration is not happening, and the crumb base holds together well enough once butter and heat set it. Powdered eggs and canned evaporated milk replace the two things that would otherwise force a cooler run.

Coal placement is the part worth getting right. More heat on the lid than under it sets the custard from above and keeps the crumb base from scorching before the middle cooks. If you smell burning at the 15-minute mark, pull coals from underneath, not from the top.
