Restaurant fried rice needs day-old rice because fresh rice is too wet to fry. On the trail the problem is the same in reverse: rehydrated rice comes out soaked. Draining it and then letting it sit in the pot dry is what gets the grains to separate instead of turning to porridge.

The bacon is doing double duty as protein and as the cooking fat, so use the fully cooked shelf-stable kind and let it render before anything else goes in. Fresh eggs, ginger root, and scallions do not travel, so this uses egg powder and dried aromatics. Press the rice against the hot metal and leave it alone between stirs. That contact is where the browning happens.
