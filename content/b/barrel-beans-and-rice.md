The order matters more than the ingredients. Rice first, off the heat, with the lid on: it drinks most of the water while you stand around. The bean flakes go in second because they thicken instantly, and if they hit the pot first the rice never rehydrates. What you end up with is closer to a wet burrito filling than to a bowl of soup.

Calories come from the oil and the chips, not the starch. Both are the first things people leave out to save weight, and both are the reason this eats like dinner. The chips also give it texture, which matters more than it sounds like after a week of things that dissolve.
