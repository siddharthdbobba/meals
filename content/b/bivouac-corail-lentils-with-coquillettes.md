Red lentils and small pasta finish in roughly the same window, which is why they share a pot here. The lentils dissolve as they cook and do most of the thickening work; the polenta at the end is there to catch whatever water is left instead of pouring it out. Draining pasta on a slope, in the dark, is how you lose dinner.

Everything is dry goods from a supermarket, so it packs cheap and leaves no foil pouch to carry out. Fat is the weak point: pasta and lentils are lean, and without the oil this reads as a thin bowl rather than a day's end meal. Bouillon does the seasoning, so bring an extra cube if you like it salty after a long day.
