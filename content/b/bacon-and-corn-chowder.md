Potato flakes are the body of this. They thicken instantly and hold a lot of fat, which is why the oil and the parmesan go in rather than getting skipped as luxuries. The freeze-dried corn rehydrates in the same five minutes and gives the chowder something to chew on, which matters more than it sounds when everything else is a smooth starch.

Shelf-stable bacon is the substitution that makes this work off a cooler. It is already cooked and dry-cured, so it needs no heat and no ice, and it goes in at the end so it does not turn to mush. Four cups of water is a lot to boil for two people, but under that the potato sets up like paste.
