Slicing the backstrap thin is the whole plan. Bear carries trichinosis risk, which means cooking it through, and a thick steak cooked through turns to leather. Quarter-inch strips hit 160F in under a minute a side, so the meat is safe and still tender. Use a thermometer, not color.

The marinade does double duty. Oil carries the spices onto every surface of the sliced meat and keeps it from sticking to a hot camp skillet. Turmeric and cumin are there for the earthy backing note; the sesame oil is the odd one out and the reason these do not taste like every other camp taco. Mixing it at home means camp work is fire, skillet, thermometer.
