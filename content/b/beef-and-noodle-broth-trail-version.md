The original is a home soup with fresh steak, whole mushrooms, and noodles boiled in a second pot. On the trail there is one pot and no second boil, so the noodles cook in the broth and the broth gets cloudy. That is fine. It also means the soba absorbs some of the liquid, which is why the water goes in high at 600 ml.

Order matters more than technique here. The beef, mushrooms, and carrot need the longest soak, so they go in first; kale turns to sludge if it boils, so it goes in off the heat. Tamari and oil go in at the end because boiling flattens the tamari and the oil is doing calorie work, not cooking work.
