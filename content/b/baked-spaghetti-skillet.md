The original is a casserole: boil pasta, brown meat, layer it into a dish, and bake it under cheese for half an hour. No oven at camp, so the pot does both jobs. Cooking the pasta and then simmering the sauce onto it in the same water gets you the same thick, clingy texture the bake was after, and it saves you a drain and a second pan.

The fresh things are the parts that had to change. Onion and bell pepper became onion flakes and seasoning, mozzarella became hard aged cheese that survives a warm day in a pack. Do not skip the low-heat simmer at the end. That is where the starch and the sauce come together instead of sitting in a puddle at the bottom of the pot.
