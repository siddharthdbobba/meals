The original is a fridge recipe: fresh berries, a tub of Greek yogurt, eaten right away. None of that carries. Powdered Greek yogurt rehydrates into something close enough, and freeze-dried berries weigh almost nothing and come back with real flavor once they hit the moisture in the yogurt.

The chia is structural. Powdered yogurt mixed with water is thin, and the chia pulls it back to spoonable in about ten minutes. Nuts and seeds go in at the end because anything crisp stirred in early stops being crisp. Protein here is around 22 g, most of it from the yogurt powder, which is why this holds as a breakfast and not just a snack.
