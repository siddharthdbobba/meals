The bag is doing the cooking, so the pot never gets dirty. Instant rice only needs hot water and time, and a sealed freezer bag holds heat well enough to finish the job while it floats in the pot. The canned chicken and vegetables are already cooked — they are just warming through in the second sit.

The leftover boiling water is the point of the quart. It stays hot in the pot, does the dishes you barely have, and makes drinks after. Cans make this heavy, so keep it near the car or the boat; the dehydrated version in the variations is what goes in a pack.
