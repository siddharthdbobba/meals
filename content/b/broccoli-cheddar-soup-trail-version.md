The original is a stovetop soup built on butter, chicken stock, and two cups of half-and-half. None of that rides in a pack, so the dairy becomes whole milk powder and a chunk of block cheddar, and the fresh vegetables become freeze-dried. Everything else survives the translation intact, including the nutmeg, which sounds fussy and is not — it is what keeps a cheese soup from tasting flat.

Order matters more than technique here. Cornstarch dropped into hot liquid seizes into lumps, so it goes in premixed with the milk powder and off the heat. Cheese added to a boiling pot separates into oil and rubber, so that goes in last with the flame already out. Use block cheddar and cut it small; pre-shredded bags are dusted with anti-caking starch that turns the pot gritty.
