Fresh shrimp is a cooler food with a short clock. Freeze-dried shrimp is not, and it comes back close enough once hot water gets into it. Save the soaking liquid; it is shrimp stock and it keeps the pan from going dry when the oil starts to smoke.

The ginger has to hit the oil before anything else. Frying it first pulls the heat out of the dried slices and into the fat, which is what carries flavor onto the shrimp. Dried lime powder replaces the lime juice at the end, and it goes in off the heat so the sourness stays sharp.
