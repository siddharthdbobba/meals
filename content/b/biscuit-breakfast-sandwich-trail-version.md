This started as a make-ahead kitchen sandwich, which is useless on trail: no fridge, no freezer, no toaster. Everything perishable got swapped. Powdered whole egg scrambles close enough to the real thing and weighs almost nothing. Shelf-stable precooked bacon needs warming, not frying. Hard aged cheese sits in a pack for days where sliced American turns to grease.

The biscuit is the one part worth the time. Pan-baking dough under a lid over low heat works because the trapped steam cooks the top while the bottom browns, so you get bread instead of a scorched disc. If you are moving fast, tortillas do the same job for none of the effort, and the sandwich is still mostly protein and fat, which is the point of it.
