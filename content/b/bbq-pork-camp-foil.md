Grilled pork chops don't fit in a pack, but canned pork in a foil packet gives you the grill taste without the grill. The rub and BBQ sauce build flavor quickly and stay in the foil—the moisture doesn't evaporate like it would over open fire.

Canned pork is lean, which is why you need the oil and sauce. The foil packet steams everything evenly and holds the moisture, something regular camp cookware struggles with. Eat straight from the foil; cleanup is throwing it away.
