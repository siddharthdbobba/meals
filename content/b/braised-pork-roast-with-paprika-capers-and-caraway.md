This started as a home oven recipe and mostly survives the move to coals, because a Dutch oven is an oven. The real problem was the dairy. A cup of sour cream will not last a weekend in a cooler, so it becomes sour cream powder reconstituted at the end. Whisk the flour into the powder while both are dry or you will be chasing lumps around the pot.

The onion and carrot get thrown out after the braise anyway, so dried versions do the job fine and save cooler space. Paprika, capers, and caraway all go in late for the same reason: long heat flattens them, and the sauce is the only part of this dish that has anything sharp in it. The pork itself is just protein and fat.
