Dried beef is the whole reason this holds together on trail. It is salt-cured and shelf-stable, it rehydrates in the hot potato without any simmering, and it brings the protein that instant mash alone never will. Chop it fine at home. Big pieces stay leathery.

The gravy powder does more than flavor. It thickens the mix so a modest amount of water still turns into food you can eat with a spoon instead of drink. Butter powder replaces the fridge butter the home version would use, and it is where the fat comes from. Four servings out of one bag means one person carries dinner for the group.
