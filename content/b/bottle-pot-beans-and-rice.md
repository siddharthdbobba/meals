Presoaking is the whole point. Dry rice and bean flakes will take a real boil and several minutes of fuel to soften, but water does that work for free while you are still walking. By the time the pot goes on the stove it needs heat, not cooking, and one short simmer finishes it.

The oil and chips are not garnish. Beans and rice on their own land around 450 calories, which is not a dinner after a long day. Fat and fried corn close the gap and add the only crunch in the pot.
