This is a first-day-out-of-town lunch and nothing else. Lettuce, tomato, and avocado are all day-one food in warm weather, so plan to eat both wraps within about twenty-four hours of the resupply. The bacon is the exception: precooked shelf-stable bacon sold in a box holds unrefrigerated for a week, which is why it also works as a resupply-box item.

Tortillas carry the calories and survive the pack better than bread as long as they lie flat. Mayo packets are the cheap fat here, and the avocado does the same job on the second wrap with more body. Both wraps are heavier per calorie than a dinner bag, which is the whole point of eating them early.
