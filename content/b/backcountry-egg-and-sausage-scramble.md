Freeze-dried ingredients are the only way to build high-protein breakfast without carrying a cooler. Rehydrating sausage, cheese, and eggs in separate containers is the move—each absorbs water at its own pace, and you avoid the pulpy mush that happens when you mix everything together. The garlic and mushroom season the sausage; the cheese adds fat and structure; the eggs bind it all.

Drain everything thoroughly after rehydration or you get soup. Cook low and slow because overcooked eggs from powder turn rubbery in seconds. The whole thing is 15-20 minutes from lighting the stove, and most of that is waiting for things to rehydrate while you break camp or drink coffee.
