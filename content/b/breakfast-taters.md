Potato flakes rehydrate the instant they meet hot water, which is the whole point on a cold morning when you do not want to stand over a stove. There is no simmer and no soak time. The pot goes off the heat before the dry mix goes in.

The milk powder does more than it looks like. Cheese sauce powder alone goes thin and salty; the milk solids give it enough body to hold onto the potatoes. Bacon rides in its own bag because the fat migrates through anything it touches, and 3 tbsp is where most of the protein comes from. Potatoes carry potassium, which is worth something on hot days when leg cramps start showing up.
