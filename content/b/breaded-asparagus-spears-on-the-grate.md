This started as an air fryer recipe, which is fine at home and useless in camp. A foil pan on a grate does the same job if you respect two things: dry spears and space between them. Moisture is what turns breadcrumbs into paste, and a crowded pan traps every bit of it.

The two breadcrumbs are doing different work. Panko gives the crunch, the fine crumbs fill the gaps and actually stick to the egg. Use only panko and half the coating ends up in the pan. Coals matter too. Flames scorch the crumbs long before the stalks are cooked, so wait until the fire has burned down.
