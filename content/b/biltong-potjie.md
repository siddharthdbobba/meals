Potjie is a slow pot over coals, and that part carries to camp fine. What does not carry is the fresh cream and fresh produce, so both got swapped: dried carrot, potato, and mushroom rehydrate in the same water that becomes the sauce, and powdered cream slurried with cornstarch does the job of a reduction without a fridge.

The fire is the actual skill here. Coals under the whole pot will scorch the bottom and split the cream. Bank them around the sides instead and let it tick over for an hour. Biltong goes in late because it only needs to soften and give up its salt, not cook.
