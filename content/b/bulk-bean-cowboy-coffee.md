Beans bought a few pounds at a time and ground at home cost a fraction of single-serve packets, and the difference compounds over a long trip. The catch on trail is that you have no filter, so the pot has to do the separating.

The two tricks are the thirty-second wait and the cold splash. Water off a rolling boil pulls bitterness out of the grounds; letting it drop a bit first keeps the cup drinkable. The cold water at the end changes the density enough that the grounds drop to the bottom and mostly stay there. Powdered milk goes in the mug, not the pot, or it foams over the grounds and drags them back up.
