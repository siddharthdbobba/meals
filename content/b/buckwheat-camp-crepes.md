The original of this is a blender recipe with eight eggs and two cups of milk, which is a non-starter without a fridge. Whole egg powder and milk powder do the same job by weight and turn the whole batter into one dry bag plus water. Fresh cherry tomatoes become sun-dried, fresh greens become dried flakes rehydrated in a cup, and butter becomes ghee, which does not need a cooler.

The half-buckwheat, half-wheat ratio is doing structural work. Buckwheat has no gluten, so an all-buckwheat crepe wants to fall apart in the pan. The wheat holds it together enough to fold three times without cracking. The 20-minute rest matters for the same reason: a batter that has not hydrated tears on the flip and you end up serving scrambled crepe.
