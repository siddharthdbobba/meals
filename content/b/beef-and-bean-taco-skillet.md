The original is a home cook's budget trick: cut the ground beef in half and make up the bulk with a can of refried beans. It works just as well on trail, and for a better reason than money. Beans carry the fat and seasoning, stretch the protein, and turn loose taco meat into something that stays in a tortilla instead of falling out the bottom.

The can and the fresh beef are the only things that had to change. Freeze-dried beef and dehydrated bean flakes hit the same ratio at a fraction of the weight, and both rehydrate in the same pot in one pass. No cheese here — it is the one thing that keeps poorly and the one thing you will not miss once the beans are in.
