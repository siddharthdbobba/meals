This is a base, not a finished dish. Forty grams of oats, milk powder, and salt runs about 290 calories, which is thin for a hiking breakfast on its own. The point is that it packs down to almost nothing and takes whatever you add to it at resupply.

Whole milk powder is doing two jobs: it makes the oats creamy instead of watery, and it carries the fat that the oats do not have. Skim powder saves a few grams and costs you most of the calories. The salt matters more than it sounds like it should. Without it the whole bowl tastes like wet paper.
