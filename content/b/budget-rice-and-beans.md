This is a physics problem in reverse: how to make rice taste like food with only ingredients that pack flat and never spoil. Tinned tomatoes carry the flavor that fat usually carries; beans add protein and texture. Without them, you're eating shelf-stable paste.

Dehydrated mixed vegetables replace the frozen veg in the source list and rehydrate in the cooking liquid, saving weight and pack space. Rice is just calories; tomatoes and beans are where the actual meal happens.
