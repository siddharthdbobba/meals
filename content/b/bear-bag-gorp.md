This is a fat-and-salt mix rather than a sugar bomb, which is why it holds up over a long day: the peanuts and seeds carry most of the calories, and the fruit is there for the sugar hit, not the bulk. Coating the dry side in melted coconut oil before the fruit goes in keeps the spice on the food and adds calories that weigh almost nothing.

The other design goal is odor. Chocolate, cured meat, and anything cheesy broadcast through a stuff sack; roasted nuts and dried fruit are comparatively quiet, and a sealed liner does the rest. Eat it out of the bag, hang it at night, and do not snack in the tent.
