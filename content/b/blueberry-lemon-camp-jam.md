The source recipe is a canning project: fresh berries, Pomona's pectin, calcium water, a boiling water bath, four half-pint jars. None of that travels. This version does the same job with dried fruit that rehydrates into pulp and chia seeds that gel on their own, so there is no pectin, no calcium solution, and no jars to sterilize.

The trade is shelf life. This is not canned, so it keeps a couple of days in a screw-top container and no longer than that in warm weather. Make what you will eat over one trip. Honey and lemon carry most of the flavor, and the lemon also keeps the whole thing from tasting like syrup.
