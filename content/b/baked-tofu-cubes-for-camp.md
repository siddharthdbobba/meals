Tofu tastes like whatever you put on it, which is the point. Two tablespoons of tamari, a little sesame oil, and a neutral oil that can take the heat are enough to give the cubes a savory edge without deciding what the rest of dinner has to be. Then they go into rice, noodles, wraps, or a bowl of soup.

The oil is not optional. It is what lets the outside firm up and brown instead of drying out over the fire. Foil on the grate replaces the sheet pan and keeps the cubes from dropping through the bars. This one needs a cooler, so cook it the first night out; the leftovers keep another day if the ice holds.
