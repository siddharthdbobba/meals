The original is a cupcake with a pudding core, which needs an oven, eggs, cold cream, and a refrigerator. None of that travels. What actually makes banana pudding work is simpler: a set custard, soft banana, and something starchy to soak. Instant pudding mix plus powdered whole milk gives you the first two-thirds with cold water and no stove.

The milk powder matters more than it looks. Instant pudding sets on the proteins in milk, so mixing it with plain water gives you sweet soup that never thickens. Freeze-dried banana pulls moisture out of the mix as it rehydrates, which tightens the pudding further, so give it the full twenty minutes before you judge the texture.
