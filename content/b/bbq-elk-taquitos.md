This is a leftovers recipe. Smoke a shoulder on the first night of a basecamp trip and you will have more meat than anyone wants to eat as meat again, so it gets rolled into something else. The beans are structural: they glue the filling to the tortilla and keep the meat from falling out the ends when you fry it.

Warming the tortillas is the step people skip and then regret. Corn tortillas straight from the bag crack down the middle as soon as they curve. Ten seconds of heat makes them pliable, and the same skillet you fry in does the job. The chiles are there for moisture and a little heat without needing anything fresh.
