Cornbread is one of the few real baked goods that survives the trip to camp intact, because self-rising cornmeal carries its own leavening and does not care how long it sat in the bin. The original called for whole milk, sour cream, and three fresh eggs. All three become powders that weigh almost nothing and mix back with cold water in the pan, which is the only change that matters here.

The blue cheese is the one thing still riding in the cooler, and it is worth the space. It melts into pockets rather than blending in, so you get salt in bursts instead of evenly. Bacon crumbles come precooked so you are not rendering fat over a fire before you can start baking; the oil covers what the rendered fat would have done for the crust.
