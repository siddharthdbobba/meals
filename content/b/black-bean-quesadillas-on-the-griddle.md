The mash is what makes this work over a fire. Whole beans roll out the open side of a quesadilla the second you flip it, so crushing part of the batch turns the filling into a paste that grips the cheese. Cheese on both sides of the filling does the same job from the outside — it melts into glue and keeps the whole thing shut.

Changes from the home version: the cilantro, avocado, and sour cream are gone, since none of them survive a cooler for long and none of them are load-bearing. The lime stays because acid is the only thing cutting through a pan of fat and starch. Cook the filling first, then wipe the pan — leftover onion bits scorch under the tortilla and taste like it.
