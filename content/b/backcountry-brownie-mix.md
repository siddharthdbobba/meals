The home version of this is a pantry mix plus eggs and butter. Two of those three do not travel, so the eggs become powdered egg and the butter can become coconut oil, which does not turn to soup in a hot car. Everything else was already shelf-stable, which is why brownies work in camp at all.

Cocoa powder and melted fat, with no melted chocolate and almost no leavening, is what makes these fudgy rather than cakey. The half teaspoon of baking powder is there for lift, not rise. Pull it early: a dutch oven keeps cooking after you take it off the coals, and an overbaked pan of this goes dry and crumbly fast.
