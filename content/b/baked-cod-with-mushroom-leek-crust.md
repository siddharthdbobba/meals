The original is an oven dish with fresh mushrooms, fresh leeks, and peeled tomatoes. All three rehydrate well, so the trail version carries them dry and brings them back with one mug of hot bouillon. Dried black trumpets in particular stay tender instead of going rubbery, which is why they hold up to this treatment better than most.

The topping is not a crunchy crust. It is a wet layer that sits on the fish and keeps it from drying out in the packet, where the heat is uneven and hard to read. The breadcrumbs and oil are on top to soak up what steams up out of it. Fish is the one thing here that needs a cooler, so this is a first- or second-night meal, not a week-out one.
