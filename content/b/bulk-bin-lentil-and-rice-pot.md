This started as a bulk-pantry list rather than a recipe: rice, lentils, oats, pasta, popcorn, the things worth buying by the sack. Rice and lentils are the pair that actually becomes trail dinner. Bought in bulk they cost pennies a serving, and neither one needs refrigeration at any point.

The catch is fuel. Dry lentils want twenty minutes of simmer, which is most of a canister over a week. So they get cooked at home and dried flat on a sheet tray, which turns them into something that rehydrates in the same few minutes the instant rice needs. The cozy step is doing half the cooking with no fuel at all. Oil goes in at the end, off heat, where it stays fat instead of turning into a scorched film on the pot.
