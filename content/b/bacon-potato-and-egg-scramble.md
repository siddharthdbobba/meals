This is a cooler recipe, not a backpacking one. Bacon and raw eggs need to stay cold, so it belongs at a car site or a basecamp with a fridge you drove in.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Bacon first because the rendered fat is the cooking medium for everything after it, and thin potato slices because a whole spud will burn outside before the middle softens. Eggs go in last and cook in under a minute, so pull the pan early. Residual heat in the skillet finishes them, and eggs that look done in the pan are dry on the plate. If you want this shelf-stable, see the powder variation below; it is a different breakfast, but it works out of a pack.
