The home version blends roasted squash and cauliflower in a machine you are not carrying. On the trail the blender's job is done before you leave: dry the roasted squash into a leather, grind it, and grind dried cauliflower florets separately. Rehydrated, the two come back as a thick soup without a fresh dairy ingredient anywhere in it. Cauliflower is the reason it reads as creamy.

Apple, vinegar, and maple sugar are all doing the same job from different directions. Squash alone goes flat and sweet in a dull way. The acid and the tart apple hold it up. Add the salt at the very end, since bouillon varies and the powder concentrates as the pot reduces.
