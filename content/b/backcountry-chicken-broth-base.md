A slow cooker running for a full day is doing one thing: hydrolyzing collagen out of bone into water. The vinegar helps, and the long low heat matters more than the vegetables do. What you get is mostly water, which is exactly the wrong thing to carry, so the trail version adds two steps the original does not need. Defat, then reduce and dry.

Skimming the fat is not optional. Fat does not dehydrate, it goes rancid, and it will spot through a paper bag in a week. Salt is left out until camp for the same reason the source leaves it out at home: reducing twelve cups to two concentrates whatever salt is already in there, and you cannot take it back out. Eight tablespoons of powder weighs under six ounces total and replaces a gallon of liquid stock.
