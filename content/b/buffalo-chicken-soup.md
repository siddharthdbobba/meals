The original is a Dutch oven recipe built on fresh broth, cream cheese, and Greek yogurt. None of that rides in a pack. Bouillon replaces the broth, powdered whole milk replaces both dairies, and potato flakes do the thickening that cream cheese used to do. Pouch chicken skips the twenty-five minutes of poaching entirely.

Order matters more than technique here. Milk powder goes into hot water off the boil and gets whisked, not dumped, or you get pockets of dry powder that never dissolve. Once the dairy is in, keep the pot below a boil — a hard boil will curdle the milk powder and split the fat out of the buffalo sauce.
