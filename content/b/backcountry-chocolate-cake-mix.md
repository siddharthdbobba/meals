Boxed mix is mostly flour, sugar, and cocoa with leavening. Mixing it yourself costs ten minutes at home and lets you fold the wet ingredients into the dry bag list too. Powdered whole milk and powdered eggs are the change that makes this a camp recipe: both go in the same bag as the flour, so nothing needs a cooler and there is one container to open.

Heat placement is the whole game with a dutch oven. Coals underneath cook the bottom fast and burn it faster; most of the heat belongs on the lid so the cake rises and sets from above. Rotating in opposite directions evens out the hot spot that every fire has. Dutch-process cocoa is worth carrying because it is neutral, and the baking powder here is doing the lifting on its own.
