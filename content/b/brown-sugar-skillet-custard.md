This is a Canadian butter tart with the pastry taken away. The crust is the part that does not travel: cold butter, shortening, ice water, a rolling pin, and a fridge rest. The filling is the part worth carrying, and it sets on its own in a buttered pan.

Powdered egg and powdered milk replace the eggs and heavy cream, hydrated with a splash of hot water before the sugar goes in. Doing it in that order matters. Dump powder into brown sugar dry and you get lumps that never dissolve. Keep the heat low and pull the pan while the center is still loose, because carryover heat will take it the rest of the way and an overcooked custard turns grainy.
