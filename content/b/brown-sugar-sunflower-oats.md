The dry milk is what separates this from plain oatmeal. Oats plus water is thin and grey; the milk powder gives it body and adds protein without adding a cold-chain problem. Brown sugar and cinnamon go in the bag at home so nothing gets measured by headlamp.

The sunflower butter goes in last and off the heat. It is the fat that makes the bowl hold you until midday, and it stirs in far more evenly into something already hot than it would into cold oats. It also keeps this workable for a group with a peanut allergy, which is the main reason to choose it over peanut butter.
