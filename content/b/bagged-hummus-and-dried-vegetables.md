The vegetables need the head start. Hummus powder hydrates in minutes, but dried carrot and pepper take the better part of an hour in cold water, and if you add them all at once you get a smooth paste with gravel in it. Two stages, one bag, no stove.

The oil is not optional. Instant hummus is mostly chickpea flour and sesame, which means protein and not much fat, and a lunch that leaves you hungry two miles later. A tablespoon of olive oil roughly doubles the calories and makes the texture read as hummus instead of wet powder.
