This started as a slow cooker soup, which is a machine nobody carries. The translation is mostly about dehydration order: squash is dense and slow, tortellini is fast, and boiling both together gives you either raw squash or pasta paste. Ten minutes apart fixes it.

Canned tomatoes and heavy cream are the two things that will not ride in a pack. Tomato powder plus a little smoked paprika covers the fire-roasted flavor at a fraction of the weight, and milk powder stirred in off the heat gives the same body without curdling. Add the dairy while the pot is still boiling and you get grit instead of cream.
