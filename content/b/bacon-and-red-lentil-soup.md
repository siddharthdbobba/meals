Red lentils are the only legume worth carrying that cooks without soaking. They break apart on their own, so the soup thickens itself and you skip the flour, the milk, and the immersion blender the home version leans on.

The source called for fresh onion and raw streaky bacon, neither of which survives a warm pack. Dried minced onion rehydrates in the broth and precooked bacon crumbles come sealed and shelf-stable, and since the bacon is already rendered you add a little oil at the start so the onion has something to fry in. Half the cook time is the lentils softening, so keep the flame low once it's simmering or you'll burn the bottom and scrub it later.
