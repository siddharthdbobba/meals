This is a home tapas dish, and it stays one. Fresh ground pork and a soft cheese are not trail food, so this is a first-night car-camping dinner cooked out of the cooler while the ice is still solid. Everything else has been moved home: the chili minced, the lime zested, the cheese cubed, so camp work is mixing and rolling.

The cheese in the middle is the whole point and also the failure mode. Brie melts to liquid well before pork is cooked through, so the seam has to be closed properly or you end up frying cheese in the pan. Lime juice in the mix keeps the pork from going dense, and the zest carries more of the citrus than the juice does.
