Buckwheat groats are seeds, not wheat, and they bake into something much harder than an oat flake. That is the point. Commercial granola turns to dust after two days of being sat on; a slab built around groats and pumpkin seeds breaks into chunks instead of powder.

The two rules are press and wait. Packing the mix flat before it goes in the oven is what fuses it, and stirring mid-bake undoes that. Coconut oil sets solid at cool temperatures, which helps the clusters hold, but it also means the pan needs to cool all the way before you touch it. Honey instead of maple syrup works the same and drops the vegan claim, which is why there is no vegan tag here either way.
