Butter chicken is really a spice-and-tomato braise with fat stirred in at the end, which makes it a good frame for small game. Squirrel is lean and tough, and three hours in an acidic sauce over low coals is what turns it into something that leaves the bone.

The cream is the part that had to change. A carton of heavy cream is a bad passenger, so this uses powdered cream reconstituted in a mug and stirred in off the heat. Adding it late and cold-side matters: dairy that boils in an acidic tomato base curdles, and you get grit instead of sauce. Butter goes in at the same stage for the same reason, and because emulsified fat is what gives the dish its weight.
