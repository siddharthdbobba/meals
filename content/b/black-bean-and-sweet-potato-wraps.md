The original is an oven recipe: fresh sweet potato, cream cheese, a wire rack at 425F. None of that travels. The rack is doing one job, keeping the bottoms from steaming, and a dry pot over low heat does the same thing if you leave them alone and only turn them once.

Everything perishable got swapped. Dehydrated beans and sweet potato rehydrate in the same ten minutes, so they cook in one pot with one boil. Cream cheese is gone; the filling gets its body from the beans thickening instead. Hard pepperjack replaces the grated cheese because it survives a warm pack, and it is also what glues the roll shut in place of toothpicks.
