This started as an oven recipe and coals do the same job with less control, so the foil matters more than the fire does. A doubled sheet with air above the pockets turns the grate into a small oven: the bottom browns, the top sets, and nothing scorches while the dough cooks through. Set them directly on the grate and you get burnt bottoms and raw middles.

Canned biscuit dough is the one item here that needs a cooler, which is why this is a car-camping or basecamp meal and not a backpacking one. The chicken does not have to be: foil pouches are shelf-stable and drain-free, and they save you from cooking meat first. Keep the sauce to a spoonful per pocket. More than that and the dough goes soggy at the seam and lets go on the grate.
