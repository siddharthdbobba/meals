This is a bird hunt recipe, which means it is written around a small pile of meat rather than a large one. A few doves will not feed anyone as an entree. Spread across toast with browned butter and garlic, the same few breasts feed a camp.

Browning the butter is the whole point. Melted butter is fat; browned butter is fat plus toasted milk solids, and those solids are what stand up to lean dark meat. Two things will ruin it: burning the butter, and overcooking the dove. Both happen fast over a fire, so keep the pan off the hottest coals and pull the meat earlier than feels right. It is a cooler recipe, not a backpacking one — real butter and fresh bread do not travel any other way.
