This one needs a cooler and a real skillet, so it is a car-camping breakfast, not a trail one. The orange juice does two jobs: it thins the egg so the bread soaks instead of sitting in custard, and the acid keeps the whole thing from tasting flat.

The graham crumbs are the point. They form a crust that browns fast and gives the slice enough structure to survive a flip on uneven camp heat, which is where ordinary french toast falls apart. Let the extra egg drip off before you crumb, or the coating slides off in the pan.
