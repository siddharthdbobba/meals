The original is a slow cooker cheesecake: eggs, sour cream, a springform pan, four hours in the fridge. None of that travels. What survives the trip is the flavor combination, so this rebuilds it from shelf-stable powders and instant pudding instead of baking anything.

The pudding mix is doing the structural work. It sets with cold water and no heat, which is what lets the cream cheese powder read as cheesecake rather than as a paste. Use less water than feels right at first; you can always add another splash, and a runny cup never recovers.
