This is a canning recipe, not a backpacking one, so treat it that way. It is a car-camping or basecamp project: two ingredients, one pot, and forty minutes of standing over a fire with a spoon. No pectin and no preservative means sugar and evaporation are doing all the work, which is why the ratio matters and why you cannot rush the boil.

The hour of maceration before heat is the part people skip. Dry sugar on the bottom of a pot over open flame burns before the fruit releases anything, and a burnt batch tastes burnt all the way through. Without a canner and sterile seals, this is not shelf-stable — keep it in the cooler and finish it in a week.
