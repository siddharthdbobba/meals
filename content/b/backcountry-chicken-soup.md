Fifteen minutes and one pot give you a complete hot meal. Freeze-dried chicken and vegetables rehydrate completely in broth with no simmering needed. Couscous swells in the same water.

The speed is built into the ingredients: everything is pre-cut and dried, so boiling water is enough. Broth powder carries all the flavor in two teaspoons. If you travel with a freezer bag and insulated cozy, you can boil water and oil at camp, pour into the bag with dry ingredients, seal it, and let it rehydrate while you pitch your tent.
