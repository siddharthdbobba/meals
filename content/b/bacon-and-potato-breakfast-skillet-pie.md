The original is a pie: pastry crust, raw eggs and milk, frozen potatoes, a woven bacon lid, an hour in an oven. None of that travels. What survives is the filling, which is a crustless quiche and cooks fine in a covered skillet over coals.

The substitutions are the whole job. Powdered egg and powdered milk replace the fridge ingredients and rehydrate into something that sets the same way. Dried hash browns replace frozen and need a soak first, or they stay gritty in the middle. Shelf-stable bacon is precooked, so it is there for fat and salt rather than rendering. Keep the heat low; egg at a hard boil goes rubbery and weeps water.
