This is the rare trail dessert with structure. The no-bake filling sets from powder and cold water alone, which is the whole reason it works out here: no oven, no fridge, no fuel burned. The milk powder is doing double duty, adding the fat that makes it read as cheesecake instead of sweet paste.

Two things decide whether you get cake or soup. Measure the water honestly, and give it time to firm up before it touches the crust. Cold weather speeds the set. If you run out of patience, it still eats fine as a pudding over crumbs.
