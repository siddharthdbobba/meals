Damper is bread for people who have no yeast and no patience. Self-raising flour brings its own lift, and the beer adds more: the carbonation puts bubbles into the dough the moment it hits the flour, so the loaf rises in the oven instead of in the shade for five hours. That trade is the whole recipe. Mix it, shape it, bury it in coals.

The bacon and onion are doing more than flavor. Rendered fat keeps the crumb from going dry and chalky, which is the usual failure with a quick bread cooked over uneven heat. The trivet matters too. Set the dough straight on the cast iron and the bottom is charcoal before the middle is cooked, so lift it and load most of your coals on the lid instead. This one needs a cooler and a real fire, so it stays a car-camping and basecamp recipe. There is no backpacking version worth writing.
