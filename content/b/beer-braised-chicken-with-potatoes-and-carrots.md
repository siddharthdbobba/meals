This is a cooler recipe, not a pack recipe, and it stays that way. Chicken thighs and bacon need cold, and the whole point is the braise — vegetables cooking in liquid that already has fat and fond in it. Freeze-dried versions of this exist and none of them are worth the trade.

The beer is doing two jobs: liquid, and enough bitterness to cut the bacon fat. Mustard and honey go in at the same time on purpose, one sharp and one sweet, so the sauce lands somewhere in the middle instead of tasting like beer that got hot. Thighs are the right cut here because they tolerate the long simmer; breasts turn to rope.
