The original simmers lamb for an hour and adds vegetables in stages as each one softens. On a stove burning fuel you carried in, that is not happening. Freeze-dried lamb and dried vegetables collapse the whole thing into one boil and one rest, and the staged additions stop mattering because everything rehydrates on roughly the same clock.

The cornstarch is the part worth keeping. Without it this is meat and vegetables floating in salty water. Slurried into cold water first and then stirred into the hot pot, it pulls the liquid into something that coats the spoon. Dumping the powder straight into boiling water gives you lumps and nothing else. The oil goes in last for calories, since freeze-dried lamb carries almost no fat.
