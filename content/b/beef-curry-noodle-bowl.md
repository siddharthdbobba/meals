The jerky is doing double duty. It rehydrates into something chewable over the ten minutes of boiling and resting, and the salt and fat it sheds season the broth from the inside. That is why it goes in with the cold water instead of at the end.

Chuka soba is worth seeking out because it is steamed rather than fried, so it soaks up broth instead of floating in grease. It also holds a bite after five minutes off the heat, which ramen does not. The raisins look out of place until you eat it: mild curry powder with no acid and no heat needs something sweet to push against, and dried fruit is the version that travels.
