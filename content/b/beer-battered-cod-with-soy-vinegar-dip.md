  - "Cut the cod into strips the width of two fingers. Pat them dry with a bandana or paper towel."

This came off a food-truck challenge dish and it survives the move to a campsite better than most fried food, because everything in it except the fish is shelf-stable. The cod is the only cooler item, and frozen fillets buy you the whole drive in.

Cold beer in the batter is not a gimmick. The carbonation and the low temperature both slow gluten from developing, so the crust stays thin and brittle instead of turning bready. The dip does the work a tartar sauce would, without needing anything refrigerated: soy for salt, rice vinegar for acid, and dried tuna flakes steeped in hot water for the savory backbone. Fry in a deep pot rather than a skillet. Less oil surface means less spatter, and a lid nearby is the fastest way to end a grease fire.
