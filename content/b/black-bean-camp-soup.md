The original is a pressure cooker soup built on soaked beans, fresh onion, and six cups of stock. None of that travels. Instant bean flakes do the same job in two minutes because the cooking already happened in a factory, and dried peppers and carrot rehydrate straight in the broth with no extra step. The fresh toppings are gone too: sour cream and avocado are cooler food, so the fat comes from oil instead.

Two things carry the flavor. The lime is not optional, acid is what keeps a pot of beans from tasting flat and heavy, so bring it as powder if you cannot bring the fruit. The other is mashing half the pot. Whole beans in thin broth read as watery; a half-smooth base clings to the spoon and makes the same ingredients feel like dinner.
