The original is a make-ahead freezer recipe, which is useless three days from a road. Everything perishable got swapped: powdered egg for fresh, instant bean flakes for a can of black beans, freeze-dried corn and dried onion for the sautéed vegetables. The spice blend is the one part carried over intact, because cumin and smoked paprika are what make eggs and beans taste like something other than eggs and beans.

Order matters more than technique here. The beans and vegetables need boiling water and a soak; the egg needs low heat and constant motion. Do the egg first and it turns to rubber while the beans rehydrate. A can of beans is mostly water and would be roughly a pound of dead weight — the flakes get you the same paste for about two ounces.
