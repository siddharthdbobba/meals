The can is a stand, not a flavor delivery system. Standing the bird upright puts the thighs closest to the heat and lets the breast finish last, which is the right order for a chicken. The beer steams slowly and keeps the cavity humid, but most of what you taste is the rub and the fire.

This one does not travel past the trailhead. A whole raw chicken needs a cooler, and the whole method needs a lid or a foil tent to hold heat around a tall bird. Freeze it solid before you leave and it does double duty as ice. The skillet underneath matters more than it sounds: it catches the fat, and it stops a top-heavy chicken from going over into the coals.
