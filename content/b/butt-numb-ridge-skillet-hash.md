This is a fried-potato breakfast rebuilt so nothing in it needs a refrigerator. Fresh potatoes, raw sausage, and butter all go; dried shreds, freeze-dried crumbles, and olive oil take their place. The result behaves the same in the pot and weighs a fraction as much.

The soak is the part people skip. Dried potato shreds that hit boiling water dry stay chewy in the middle no matter how long you cook them. Ten cold minutes first fixes that. The low-heat crust at the end is optional in the sense that a hash without one is just wet potato.
