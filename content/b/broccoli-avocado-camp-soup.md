An immersion blender is not going in your pack, so the avocado does the blending. Mashed into hot broth it breaks down into a thick green base with real fat in it, which is the whole point of the original recipe and the part that survives the trip. Everything else here is dried, because fresh broccoli and a bunch of beet greens are mostly water and mostly air.

Buy the avocado hard and unripe two or three days out. It will be ready about when you want dinner, and it bruises less than a ripe one. Keep half the olive oil for the top of the bowl: unheated oil tastes like something, and heated oil mostly tastes like nothing.
