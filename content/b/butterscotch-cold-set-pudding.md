The original is a baked pie: crust, egg yolks, evaporated milk, a stovetop custard, and a cream topping that all wants a refrigerator. None of that travels. Instant ClearJel does the one thing that mattered, which is set the filling, and it does it in cold water without heat or eggs. That is the whole substitution.

Crushing the chips at home is not optional. Whole chips sit in cold pudding as hard little pellets and never give up their butterscotch. Broken up, they soften into the base and carry most of the fat and calories. The brown sugar and salt push it back toward the caramel edge the baked version got from browning.
