This came out of long-boat provisioning, where the fresh food runs out on day three and the back half of the trip is cans and dry starch. The glaze is the whole point. Canned ham is salty and one-note, and an acidic sugar syrup gives it something to push against.

The juice is powdered drink mix, not a carton, because a cup of liquid orange juice is half a pound of water you would be carrying for no reason. Reduce it hard. If the glaze is still thin when the ham is hot, keep the burner on and stir, since the sugar has to concentrate before it will coat anything.
