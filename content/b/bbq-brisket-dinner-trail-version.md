The original is a three-pound brisket under pressure for over an hour. None of that travels. What does travel is the flavor logic: fatty beef, a sweet and sour sauce, onion, and enough time for the two to meet. Freeze-dried beef gives you the protein at a fraction of the weight, and barbecue sauce powder rehydrates into something close enough.

The potato flakes are the substitute for hours of reduction. A pressure cooker leaves you thin liquid you boil down at the end; on a stove with limited fuel you thicken instead. Add them slowly. Too many and the whole pot goes stiff and pasty.
