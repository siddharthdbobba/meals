The original is a cooler recipe: pre-cooked rice, canned beans, a bag of shredded cheese. That works from a car and nowhere else. Swapping in instant rice and dehydrated bean flakes cuts most of the weight and all of the refrigeration, and the beans rehydrate faster than the rice does, so nothing waits on anything.

Salsa is doing the seasoning here, which is why it is worth carrying wet. A block of hard cheese keeps for days out of a cooler and melts better than pre-shredded, which is coated in starch to keep it from clumping. Bring the avocado only if a car is involved.
