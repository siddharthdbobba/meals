Store-bought instant oatmeal packets are mostly sugar and salt, and there are four flavors. Mixing your own costs less per serving and lets you pick what goes in. The base is oats, oat bran, powdered milk, sweetener, cinnamon, and a little salt. Everything after that is decoration.

Three ingredients earn their weight. Oat bran thickens the bowl and keeps it from turning watery at the bottom. Chia or flax adds fat and fiber and disappears into the texture. Powdered milk is where most of the protein comes from, and it does more for the mouthfeel than sugar does. Freeze-dried fruit rehydrates in the mug; regular dried fruit stays chewy, which some people prefer. Build a week of packets at once and morning becomes one bag and one pour.
