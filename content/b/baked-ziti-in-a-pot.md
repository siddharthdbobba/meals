Baked ziti is a casserole, and casseroles do not travel. What survives the trip is the structure: starch, tomato, something creamy, and enough cheese to hold it together. The oven step exists to brown the top, which is worth nothing on trail, so it goes.

Cauliflower powder is doing the job cream would do at home. Dried and ground, it rehydrates into a thick neutral base that carries fat and keeps the pasta from swimming in thin tomato water. Add it off the heat and slowly, or it clumps. Cook the pasta in the sauce rather than draining it, because draining means a second pot and a liter of hot water thrown into the dirt.
