This is a game stew, so the long simmer is not optional. Squirrel is lean and worked hard for a living. The only thing that makes it tender is time in water, and that same water becomes the stock the rest of the stew is built on. Boil it, cool it, boil it again if you have to, but do not pour it out.

The canned vegetables are doing double duty: they are shelf-stable, and the packing liquid adds body without extra fuel. Corn and butter go in last because both are already cooked and long heat just flattens them. Sugar sounds odd but it pulls the acid out of the tomatoes, which otherwise dominate a pot this size.
