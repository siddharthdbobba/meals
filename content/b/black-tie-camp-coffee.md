This is a Thai iced coffee scaled down to a camp kitchen. The original wants brewed espresso, fresh half and half, and a can of condensed milk, none of which survive a weekend without a fridge. Instant espresso and powdered whole milk cover both, and the condensed milk moves into a squeeze tube so you are not carrying an open can around.

The layering is not decoration. Condensed milk is dense enough to sit under the coffee, and the reconstituted milk poured last is light enough to sink slowly instead of mixing, so the first sips are bitter tea and the last are sweet. Stir it and you get one flat sweet cup, which is fine, just not the point.
