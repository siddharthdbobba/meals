This is a diner burger sauce built out of things that do not need a cooler. Mayonnaise packets are the whole trick: sealed single-serves are shelf-stable, so the fridge step the home version depends on disappears. Everything else already lives at room temperature.

The original chills it for half an hour, which is about hydration and not temperature. The vinegar and mustard have to move through the mayo before it tastes like one thing instead of three. Twenty minutes in a warm pack does the same work. Mix it in the bag and the cleanup is a bag.

Once a packet is opened it is on the clock like any other opened condiment. Mix what you will eat that night.
