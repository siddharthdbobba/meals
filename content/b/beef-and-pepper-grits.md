This works because grits rehydrate in minutes—same speed as instant potatoes. That means you need fast-hydrating ingredients or you end up with mushy grits and crunchy bits of meat. Freeze-dried beef and cheese soften on the grits' schedule, not their own. The dried milk powder dissolves into boiling water and thickens the whole dish, turning it from thin paste into something with texture.

A quarter-cup of freeze-dried beef isn't much by weight, but it rehydrates to real protein and real calories. Pepper jack carries flavor the whole way, which matters when everything else came from a freezer bag.
