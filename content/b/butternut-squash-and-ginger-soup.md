Squash soup on the trail means dehydrated squash powder, vegetables, and spices cooked in one pot in under twenty minutes. It won't be as rich as oven-roasted squash soup, but ginger and mace fill in, and butter and brown sugar add body. The payoff is that it's shelf-stable and portable.

Mace is the move here. It's less common than nutmeg, subtly bitter, and stops the whole thing from tasting sweet. Ginger and mace together make this feel intentional, not like you're eating instant soup at a trailhead. They do the work.
