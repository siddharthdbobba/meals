Venison is almost pure lean muscle, which is exactly what jerky wants. Fat is what turns homemade jerky rancid in a pack, and a deer roast barely has any. The bulgogi marinade solves the other half of the problem: soy and sugar season deep into a thin slice, and the grated pear carries enzymes that break down connective tissue so the finished strip chews instead of fighting back.

The cure is not optional if this is going to ride around in a warm pack for a week. Insta Cure No. 1 is nitrite, and it is what keeps a low-temperature dry from being a slow incubator. Measure it by the teaspoon, never by eye. Sprinkling the sesame and pepper flakes on right before drying keeps them on the surface, where you taste them, rather than dissolved into the marinade you pour off.
