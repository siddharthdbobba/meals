This is a muffin recipe that gave up on muffin tins. A dutch oven holds even heat far better than six small cups do over coals, so the batter goes in one layer and comes out as a cake you cut into wedges. Same crumb, fewer things to scrub.

The streusel is the reason to bother. It sits on top and takes the direct lid heat, which browns it and keeps the interior from drying out. Two adaptations from the home version: buttermilk powder plus water removes one cooler item, and dried blueberries soaked in a little water work when fresh ones would turn to mush in a pack. Butter and egg still need a cooler, which is why this stays a car-camping recipe.
