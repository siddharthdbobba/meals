The original is a drop cookie made with two eggs and a tub of sour cream, which is three refrigerated ingredients and forty small cookies you have to bake in batches. Neither fits a camp kitchen. Baking it as one slab in a dutch oven takes one round of coals instead of four, and the powdered egg and powdered buttermilk carry the same job without a cooler.

Brown sugar and shortening are doing most of the structural work, so cream them properly. Skip that and the batter bakes dense instead of cakey. The glaze is where the flavor sits: butter cooked until the milk solids toast is the whole point, and it sets hard within a few minutes, so mix it only once the slab is cool and spread fast.
