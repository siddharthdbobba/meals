The original is a picnic salad that lives in a fridge for two hours. Trail version keeps the structure and drops everything that needs cold. Dried broccoli rehydrates into something with real bite, which is the whole point of the dish, and the raisins and onion soak alongside it so they soften at the same rate.

The dressing is the part that changed most. Yogurt and mayonnaise are out, so the acid and sugar carry the flavor and powdered mayo or a spoon of oil carries the fat. Drain the soak water before dressing or you end up with soup. Seeds and bacon bits go in last for the same reason they exist: texture, which dies the moment they get wet.
