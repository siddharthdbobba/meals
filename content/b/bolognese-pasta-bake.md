This is lasagna with the layers collapsed into one pan. Two things carry it. The pasta comes off the boil undercooked, so it finishes in the bolognese and takes on the sauce instead of sitting in it. And the sauce goes in deliberately loose, because the pasta drinks water for the rest of the cook and a tight sauce turns to paste in five minutes.

The cheese layer stays on top. Poured to the edges and left alone under a lid on low heat, it sets into a skin rather than dissolving into the pasta. Milk powder does the work fresh milk would do at home, and the resting time is not optional — straight off the heat it runs, and five minutes later it holds.
