Fruit leather is dehydration you do yourself, and blueberries are a good candidate because they have no pit and no seeds worth chewing around. The simmer removes most of the water before the dryer ever sees it, which is what keeps the drying time down to hours instead of a full day. The maple syrup is there in small amounts for flavor and a little pliability, not to make candy.

Thickness is the whole game. Spread it too thin and the edges go brittle and crack; too thick and the center stays tacky and will mold in your food bag a week later. Aim for even, check early, and pull it while it still bends. Carried this way, roughly a pound and a half of berries becomes a few ounces you can eat walking.
