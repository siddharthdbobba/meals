The point here is cost per calorie. Bulk rice and bean flakes run a few cents an ounce, which is less than any freeze-dried pouch selling the same dinner. Buying the components loose and mixing them yourself is the whole savings.

Whole dried beans never soften at altitude in a reasonable time, so this uses flakes, which are just beans already cooked and dried. They also thicken the pot, which is what turns rice and water into a meal instead of soup. The oil is not optional. Beans and rice are lean, and fat is where the calories per ounce actually live.
