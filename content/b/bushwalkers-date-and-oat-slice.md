This is a home-oven job that pays off on the track. The dates are the structure: soaked with bicarbonate in boiling water, they break down into a sticky paste that holds oats, bran, and seeds together without eggs or butter. Leaving out the egg and butter matters, because it means the finished slice sits in a hot pack for a week without going off.

The oil and honey carry the energy. Oats and bran alone would be dry and slow to eat; a half cup of oil turns each bar into a real block of calories you can chew while walking. Press the mixture into the tray hard and let it cool all the way before cutting, or you carry crumbs instead of bars.
