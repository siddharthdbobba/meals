Hearts are the one organ that eats like muscle meat: dense, mild, no liver funk. They are also small, which is the whole problem on a grill. Skewering three or four together turns a dozen fiddly pieces into one thing you flip once, and nothing drops into the coals.

This one stays a cooler recipe. Cream cheese and raw bacon have no shelf-stable stand-in that behaves the same, so treat it as a first-night camp meal or a hunting-camp appetizer rather than something you carry. Doing the filling at home is the real time saver — stirring cold cream cheese with a stick at camp is worse than it sounds.
