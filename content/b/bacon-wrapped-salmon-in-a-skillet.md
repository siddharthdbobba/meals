This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal, so it belongs to night one at a car site or the first camp of a paddle trip. Everything else about it is trail-friendly: one skillet, no added water, no oil, no dish beyond the pan.

The bacon is structural. It bastes the salmon as it renders, which keeps a lean fillet from drying out over an uneven fire, and the fat left in the pan is why you skip oil entirely. Foil over the top traps enough heat to finish the inside without scorching the outside — a lid you already carry. The syrup goes on off the heat, because sugar in a hot skillet burns before the fish is done. Lemon zest gets grated at home into the spice jar; carrying a whole lemon for one teaspoon is not worth the weight.
