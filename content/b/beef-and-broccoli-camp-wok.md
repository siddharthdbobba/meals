The baking soda is not optional if you are using a cheap cut. It raises the surface pH of the meat and keeps the proteins from seizing into rope over high heat, which is the only way flank steak works in a hot pan. Fifteen minutes is enough; an hour is fine.

The original steams fresh broccoli in the pan with a third of a cup of water. Dried florets do the same job with none of the weight and none of the produce rot, and rehydrating them separately means the pan stays dry enough to actually sear. Drain them well. The cornstarch needs to hit a hot pan with a little liquid, not a puddle, or the sauce never tightens.
