This is a car-camping breakfast, not a trail one: eggs, dairy, and a bottle of liqueur are all cooler weight. The batch size is the point. A dozen eggs and a loaf covers about ten people, which is roughly what a group site produces at seven in the morning.

Stale bread is not a shortcut, it is the requirement. Dry slices absorb custard and stay intact on the flip; fresh bread turns to paste in the batter and shreds on the spatula. The alcohol mostly cooks off on the griddle, so what you get is the sweet cream-and-vanilla side of the liqueur, not the burn. Evaporated milk in place of fresh is worth doing — it is shelf-stable until the can opens and it makes a richer custard.
