The pre-soak is the whole reason this works. Dried tomato and onion need longer to soften than couscous needs to cook, so giving them five cold minutes before the boil means everything finishes at the same time instead of leaving you chewing leather in a bowl of mush.

Couscous never really cooks. It swells off the heat, which is why the stove goes out before the pot sits. That saves fuel and keeps the bottom from scorching. Note it is wheat, so this one is not gluten-free, and the parmesan is real dairy, shelf-stable or not.
