The original is a slow cooker recipe built on raw chicken breast, fresh celery and carrot, and lettuce leaves. None of that travels. Pouch chicken is already cooked and already shredded, which removes the only real work in the dish, and freeze-dried celery and carrot give the same snap without anything that wilts by noon on day two.

Dressing powder does the job the blue cheese did at home. It is acidic and salty enough to cut the vinegar in the wing sauce, and it stays dry until it hits the chicken. Letting the bag rest a few minutes matters more than it sounds: the dried vegetables need that time to rehydrate on sauce alone, and the sauce thickens as they do, so the wrap holds together instead of running out the bottom.
