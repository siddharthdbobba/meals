The original is a leftover-prime-rib soup, which is not a thing you have on trail. Freeze-dried beef and bouillon do the same job at a fraction of the weight, and dried mushrooms bring back most of the savory depth that gets lost when the fresh vegetables go. Butter becomes ghee or oil because neither needs a cooler.

Barley is the reason this simmers instead of steeps. Quick-cooking pearl barley gets there in about twenty minutes; regular pearl barley takes closer to forty and will cost you a canister. The potato flakes at the end are doing what the diced potatoes did in the original, thickening the broth into something that coats the spoon.

Note on tags: barley is wheat-adjacent enough that this is not gluten-free, and the beef rules out the vegetarian tags.
