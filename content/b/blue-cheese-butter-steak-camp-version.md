This is a home recipe with an oven step, and the oven is the part that does not travel. The oven exists to finish a thick steak without burning the crust, so camp swaps in the cool side of the grate plus a foil tent. Same idea, radiant heat instead of convection, and it takes about the same time.

The compound butter is home prep on purpose. Creaming butter with cheese in camp is a mess, and a frozen log is easier to carry, portion, and keep cold than a tub. It also keeps the cheese out of the pan, where blue cheese scorches. Salting the steaks the night before dries the surface, which matters more over a fire than a stove because coals run uneven and a wet steak steams.
