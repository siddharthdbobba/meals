The original is a cold picnic salad built on sour cream, mayo, fresh lettuce, and a refrigerator. None of that survives a pack. The dressing gets rebuilt from dry ranch mix, powdered milk, and shelf-stable mayo packets, which lands in roughly the same place: tangy, thick, a little fatty. Sun-dried tomatoes replace grape tomatoes because they carry the same acid at a fraction of the weight, and freeze-dried peas or spinach replace lettuce, which turns to slime in a bag by hour two.

Draining is the only fussy part, and the fix is to under-water it rather than dump good starch on the ground. Leave a couple of tablespoons behind and the powdered dressing has something to grab. Bacon bits go in last so they keep some texture instead of dissolving into the sauce. It is fine warm, better cool, and no dietary tags apply here since it has both bacon and milk powder in it.
