This is a fish-camp dinner, so it assumes you have fish and a cooler. The lemon bed is the working part: it keeps the fillet off the foil so the skin does not weld itself down, and it steams the fish from below while the seasoning takes heat from above.

Honey goes on after the fire, not before. Sugar over coals burns black and bitter well before the fish is done. Off the heat it thins out on the hot fillet and cuts the cayenne in the spice blend. Foil replaces the cedar plank from the original method — less smoke, but nothing to pack out except the packet.
