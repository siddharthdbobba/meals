The source page was a list of grocery-budget tips rather than a dish, so this is built the way that page argues food should be bought: bulk staples, no packaging, nothing that costs money for the convenience. Instant refried beans and instant rice are two of the cheapest dry calories per ounce you can find, and both rehydrate on residual heat.

Order matters. Rice first, because it needs several minutes of soak and a lot of water. Beans second, because the flakes thicken fast and will lock the whole pot into a paste if the rice is still thirsty underneath them. The oil is not optional at these prices; without added fat this is a large volume of starch and not much of a dinner.
