This is a candy mix that happens to have nuts in it, and that ratio is the point. Coated chips carry fat and sugar at about 150 calories an ounce with no water weight and no fridge, which is what you want in a pocket snack on a cold morning. The cashews and cranberries are there so it is not pure confection.

The only real failure mode is heat. Butterscotch and yogurt coatings soften around 80°F and set back up into one solid block, so keep the bags buried deep in the pack rather than in a lid pocket in the sun. If it does fuse, break it up before it cools rather than after.
