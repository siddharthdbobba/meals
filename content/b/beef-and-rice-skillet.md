This recipe is for dry camps far from resupply. Freeze-dried ingredients are shelf-stable and carry light. Everything rehydrates in one pot under a tight lid—you boil once and let it sit.

Parmesan and oil are not optional here. They carry all the savor. Without them this is bland rehydrated grain. Mix the oil through the whole pot instead of leaving it on the surface so it actually seasons something.
