Stuffing mix rehydrates fast and soaks up gravy without turning to mush. Powdered butter carries calories without weight, and the gravy packet thickens the whole thing into something warm and solid.

Shelf-stable chicken pouch is ready-to-go protein. Craisins add sweetness and chew without water weight. A plate of hot stuffing and gravy on the trail is the kind of straightforward, filling dinner that makes sense after a long day.
