Stroganoff normally leans on sour cream and fresh beef, neither of which survives a pack. Whole milk powder covers the first job — it goes creamy in hot water and carries fat, which skim milk powder does not. A dry cured sausage stick covers the second, and since it is already cooked it only needs to warm through.

Cook the pasta in the same water that becomes the sauce. The starch it sheds is what keeps the milk powder from separating, so there is nothing to drain and nothing to rinse. Crush the mushrooms at home; whole dried caps take longer to soften than the vermicelli takes to cook, and you end up chewing them.
