The original is a baked side dish held together by cream and cheese instead of a flour roux. That part travels well. Everything else does not, so the fresh heads become freeze-dried florets, the double cream becomes cream powder, and the cream cheese becomes a mix of cheese powder and a block of aged cheddar that survives a warm pack.

No roux means the sauce depends on fat and evaporation to thicken, which is why the water goes in short and the rest only if needed. Rehydrate the vegetables first and off the heat before the cheese goes in. Cheese powder added to a hard boil turns grainy, and it will not come back.
