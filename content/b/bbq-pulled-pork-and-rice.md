This is the trail version of a slow-smoked pulled pork dinner. Fifteen hours over oak is not happening at a campsite, so the smoke comes from paprika and chipotle instead, and the meat comes freeze-dried. What you are really rebuilding is the sauce: tomato, sugar, vinegar, salt. Get those four in the right ratio and the rest is seasoning.

The order matters. Pork first, in water alone, because dried meat rehydrates slowly and rice will steal the water it needs. The oil goes in last, off heat, so it coats rather than fries. Dried minced onion is the stand-in for pickled red onion, which is mostly there for sharpness, and the vinegar powder covers that anyway.
