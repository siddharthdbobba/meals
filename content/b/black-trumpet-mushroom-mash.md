Black trumpets dry down to almost nothing and rehydrate fast, which makes them one of the few foraged foods worth the pack weight. Half an ounce is a season's worth of walking slowly through wet oak woods, and it weighs less than a fuel canister lid.

The method is built around not wasting the soak. Most home recipes strain the mushroom liquid and use it separately; on trail you have one pot, so the potato flakes go straight into the broth and pick up everything that leached out. Nutritional yeast and oil stand in for the butter and cream this would get at home.
