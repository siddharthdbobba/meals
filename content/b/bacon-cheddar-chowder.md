This is a real roux soup, which is unusual on a stove that only knows two settings. The flour cooked in oil is what gives it body; without that minute of stirring you would need twice the cheese to get the same thickness, and freeze-dried cheddar is the expensive part of the bag.

The order matters more than the technique. Milk goes in before the boil so it thickens, cheese goes in after the heat is off so it melts instead of splitting. Rehydrate the onion first, in cold water, while you are setting up camp. It costs nothing but time you were spending anyway.
