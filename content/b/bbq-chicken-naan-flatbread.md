This started as an oven recipe, which is fine at home and useless in camp. The whole thing translates to a grate over coals because naan is already a cooked bread — you are only reheating it and melting cheese, not baking dough. The one step worth keeping from the original is toasting the naan naked first. Sauce hits bread that has already firmed up, so it does not soak straight through.

Heat control matters more than timing. Direct flame burns the bottom before the mozzarella gives up, so build the fire, let it fall to coals, and work off to the side. The lid, or a foil tent, is what actually melts the cheese: with an open top the heat leaves and you get a warm flatbread with cold cheese on it.
