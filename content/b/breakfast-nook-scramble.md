Powdered egg fails when it goes into the pot dry. Mixed with cool water first and given a minute to hydrate, it scrambles close enough to the real thing. Cook it over the lowest flame the stove will hold and take it off early, because residual heat in an aluminum pot will keep cooking it after the burner is off.

The potatoes carry the calories and the fat carries the flavor, so do not skip the oil. Draining the potatoes before the egg goes in matters more than it sounds like it should. Leftover water turns the whole pot into soup, and there is no fixing it at that point.
