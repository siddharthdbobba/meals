Steak and eggs is a reliable breakfast because it's protein in multiple forms. Fresh steak needs refrigeration, so jerky substitutes. It's shelf-stable, needs no prep, and softens quickly in a hot pan. Powdered eggs reconstitute into proper scrambled eggs—fluffier than they sound—and cook faster than any real prep would allow.

The tarragon is the original recipe's connection to béarnaise, the traditional steak sauce. On trail, you skip the fussy emulsion and just add dried herb. This isn't fancy plating; it's a jerky scramble that delivers 38 grams of protein in one pot in five minutes.
