Holiday dinner is cheap because the parts are cheap: cured pork, potatoes, whatever vegetable was in season. All three have shelf-stable versions that weigh almost nothing, so the whole plate collapses into one bag and one pot. The ham pouch does the protein and most of the salt. The gravy packet does the work a pan drippings would have done at home.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Vegetables need real simmering time, potato flakes need none, and flakes dumped in early turn the pot into paste before the beans are soft. Cook the slow thing first, kill the heat, then thicken. Gravy mixed separately keeps it from seizing into lumps you cannot stir out.
