Commercial electrolyte tabs are salt and sugar sold at a heavy markup. The ratio is what matters, not the brand. Roughly 6 percent sugar with about 500 mg of sodium per liter pulls water across the gut wall faster than plain water does. Below that concentration you are just drinking sweet water; well above it, the drink sits in your stomach and sloshes.

The potassium salt substitute is the part most homemade mixes skip. Sweat carries potassium out too, and it tastes faintly metallic on its own, which is why the drink powder is in the list. It is masking, not flavoring. Skip the powder and this is drinkable but unpleasant enough that you will drink less of it, which defeats the point.
