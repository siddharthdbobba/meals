Chuck is the point of this one. It is a working muscle full of collagen, and it needs a long wet simmer to break down into something tender. There is no shortcut, so this belongs at a basecamp or a car-camping site where the fire is burning for hours anyway and nobody is waiting on dinner to hike.

The chocolate and the sugar at the end are not a garnish. Two cans of tomatoes plus tomato paste land sharp and acidic after a long cook, and a small amount of unsweetened chocolate flattens that out and adds body. The cinnamon stick does similar work from the other direction. The original serves this with sour cream, which is a cooler liability, so season harder at the end instead.
