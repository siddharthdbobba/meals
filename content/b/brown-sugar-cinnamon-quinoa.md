Quinoa flakes are rolled the same way oats are, so they rehydrate on residual heat instead of a long simmer. That is the whole point: one pour, no boil-over, no pot to scrub. They also carry more protein per ounce than rolled oats, which matters on a morning that ends with a climb.

The minute of stirring is the step people skip. Flakes clump the second they hit hot water, and once a lump forms the inside stays dry. Stir it through, then let the sealed bag do the rest of the work while you pack up.
