steps:
  - "Warm 300 ml of water on the stove until it is barely above body temperature. Too hot kills the yeast."
  - "Stir the honey into the water, sprinkle the yeast over it, and leave it ten minutes. It should foam. If it does not, the yeast is dead and you are making flatbread."

  - "Knead on a clean stuff sack or cutting board for eight minutes, until the dough stops tearing when you stretch it."

Bread on a stove is a long job with a short ingredient list, which makes it a basecamp or car-camping project rather than a trail dinner. The original used a slow cooker and a fixed low heat. A pot on a simmering stove does the same thing badly enough to work: trapped steam, gentle sides, no direct radiant heat from above.

The vital wheat gluten matters more here than in an oven. Without dry heat setting a crust, the loaf depends on gluten structure alone to hold its shape, and plain all-purpose flour does not have enough. Coconut oil replaces butter because it survives a week in a pack without a cooler. Expect a soft, pale loaf with a browned bottom, not a crusty one.
