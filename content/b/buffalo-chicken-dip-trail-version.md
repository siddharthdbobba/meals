Party dip is a chafing dish full of cream cheese and shredded rotisserie chicken. Neither of those travels. Cheese powder and powdered whole milk do the same job dry, and a spoonful of potato flakes replaces the body the cream cheese was providing. The result is thick enough to hold on a chip instead of running off it.

Water is the variable that ruins this. Start at 180 ml and add more only if it seizes, because you cannot take water back out. The hot sauce goes in last and off the heat: it thins the mix, and boiling it flattens the vinegar that makes the whole thing taste like buffalo anything.
