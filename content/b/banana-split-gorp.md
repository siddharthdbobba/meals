The whole thing is an assembly job, but the ratios decide whether it works. Peanuts and walnuts are the bulk and most of the fat, the pecans and M&M's carry the sugar, and the three fruits do the flavor work. Banana chips are fried, so they land closer to a chip than a fruit and add more calories than their weight suggests.

Freeze-dried strawberries are the fragile part. They pull moisture out of the air and go leathery, and they turn to red dust if the bag gets crushed. Adding them at the last minute keeps them crisp, and packing in small bags keeps the crushing to one serving instead of the whole batch.
