The source is a lattice-crust pie with cold butter and ice water, which is a kitchen project. On a fire you cannot control temperature well enough to protect a bottom crust, so this drops a cornmeal biscuit topping over the fruit instead. Same cornmeal grit and lemon against sweet berries, none of the chilling and rolling.

Dried blueberries replace six cups of fresh ones. They weigh a fraction as much and they pull the added water back out of the pot, so the cornstarch has less work to do. The shortening stands in for butter because it does not melt in a pack, and it still cuts into flour cold enough to make a tender topping.
