The home version drains the noodles and saves a splash of pasta water to bring back. On trail you skip both moves: cook in just enough water that none is left over. Less fuel, no strainer, nothing to dump in a place you should not dump starchy water. Getting the ratio right matters more than timing — 600 ml to 4 oz of noodles lands close to dry with a thick starchy film that behaves like sauce once fat goes in.

Butter is swapped for ghee, which has the milk solids cooked out and does not need a cooler. Garlic powder gets bumped up because a pot cooling in cold air dulls everything. Powdered parmesan is shelf-stable and worth the ounce; it thickens the coating along with the starch.
