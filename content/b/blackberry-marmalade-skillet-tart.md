The original is a home-kitchen tart with a rolled crust, fresh berries, and an egg wash. None of that survives a trip. Powdered shortening replaces cold butter, so the crust mix rides in a bag at ambient temperature and comes together with cold water. Dried berries replace fresh ones, and the marmalade does double duty: it is sugar, acid, and the moisture the dried fruit needs to plump back up.

The egg wash is gone because carrying an egg for one brushstroke is not worth it. The crust browns fine without it under a lid of coals. Press the dough in rather than rolling it out; a pressed crust is thicker and less fussy, and it holds fruit that is heavier and wetter than what the recipe was designed for.
