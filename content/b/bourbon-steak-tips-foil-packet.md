This is a night-one meal and it is honest about that. Steak tips need a cooler, so they get eaten before the ice goes. The marinade is what makes a cheap cut worth carrying: acid from the vinegar and mustard, salt from the soy sauce, and sugar that caramelizes on the coals. Cutting the tips small doubles the surface the marinade reaches, which is why the long soak actually pays off.

The foil packet is the trail change. Direct grilling over a campfire means flare-ups from all that oil and sugar, and a grate you then have to scrub in the dark. Sealed foil traps the juices, cooks evenly over uneven coals, and gets balled up and packed out. Freeze the bag flat at home and it works as a cooler block for the drive in.
