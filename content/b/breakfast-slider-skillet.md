This started as a make-ahead brunch tray baked in an oven. On the trail there is no oven, no fridge, and no reason to assemble six sandwiches before eating them, so it collapses into one pot: crisp the bacon, cook the eggs in the fat, stir the cheese in off the heat, spoon it into the rolls.

Three swaps make it travel. Shelf-stable precooked bacon replaces the refrigerated kind. Whole egg powder replaces fresh eggs and hydrates in the same pot. Ghee replaces butter because it keeps at any temperature. The ranch seasoning is the one thing worth carrying that is purely flavor, and it does a lot of work against otherwise flat powdered eggs.
