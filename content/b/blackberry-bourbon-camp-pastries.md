The source made these with fresh blackberries, which is fine in a kitchen in July and useless at a campsite. Dried berries simmered with sugar and cornstarch land in the same place: a thick filling that will not soak through the crust and run out the seams. Cornstarch matters more here than it does at home, because a dutch oven over coals runs hotter and less evenly than an oven, and thin filling boils out fast.

Cream cheese in the icing comes from a foil tube, not a tub, so the whole thing works without a cold box. Bourbon is optional and mostly cooks off anyway. The real skill is coal management: more on the lid than underneath, and a quarter turn every eight minutes, or the bottoms burn while the tops stay pale.
