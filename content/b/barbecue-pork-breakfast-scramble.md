This started as a diner omelet built on leftover barbecue, which assumes a fridge and three fresh eggs. Powdered whole egg and a retort pouch of pulled pork get you the same plate with nothing that spoils. Dried onion stands in for sautéed, rehydrated in a spoonful of hot water while the pan heats.

The fold is the only part with any technique to it, and it is optional. What matters is heat control: reconstituted egg has less fat and water than the real thing, so it goes from set to rubber in about a minute over a hot canister flame. Keep the burner low and pull the pan early.
