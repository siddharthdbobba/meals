The original is an oven casserole with fresh squash, half and half, and a cheese sauce. None of that travels. Roasting and dehydrating the squash at home does the same flavor work in a fraction of the weight, and whole milk powder plus dry parmesan rebuilds the cream sauce with hot water instead of a fridge.

Cooking the pasta in the same water that rehydrates the squash and greens is the point. The starch it sheds is what makes the sauce cling rather than pool. Add the dairy off the heat: milk powder on a live flame will scorch to the bottom of the pot and you will be scrubbing it in the dark.
