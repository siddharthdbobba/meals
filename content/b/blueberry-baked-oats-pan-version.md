Baked oatmeal is a custard that happens to be full of oats. Take away the oven and you still need the custard, so the eggs and milk come along as powder and the pot lid does the work the pan used to. Low flame and a covered pot is close enough to baking, as long as you accept a softer edge instead of a browned one.

Three swaps matter. Maple syrup and honey are heavy and they leak, so dry sugar goes in the premix instead. Coconut oil is solid at most temperatures you will carry it at and it greases the pot before anything else goes in, which is the difference between one-pot cleanup and scraping. Dried blueberries rehydrate on their own in the simmer, and unlike fresh ones they do not turn the whole pot purple.
