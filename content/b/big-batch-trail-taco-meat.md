Bulk cooking is the whole point here. One skillet at home turns into six dinners that weigh five ounces each, and the work happens in a kitchen instead of in the dark with a headlamp. Lean beef matters more than flavor at this stage: fat left in the crumbles will turn on you in a warm pack within a week.

Dried ground meat rehydrates unevenly, which is why the bean flakes go in last. They pull the loose water into a paste and hide any crumbles that stayed stubborn. Tomato powder does the job canned sauce would, at a fraction of the weight, and the oil at the end puts back the calories that draining removed.
