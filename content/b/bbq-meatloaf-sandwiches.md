This is an RV recipe adapted for a cooler and nothing else. The whole point is that the cooking already happened. You do an hour of oven work at home and buy yourself several no-stove meals at camp, which matters most on the first night when you arrive late and on rest days when you do not want to deal with a stove at noon.

Cooling the loaf fully before wrapping is the step people skip. Trapped steam softens the glaze and gives bacteria a warm pocket to work in. Chilled solid, the loaf slices clean instead of crumbling, and it is good for about three days packed against ice. Vegetables and coleslaw are left off on purpose, since neither survives a cooler as well as the meat does.
