The original is made with fresh apples baked whole, which is a two-hour oven job and a cooler full of fruit. Dried apples get to the same place faster because they are already concentrated. You are just putting back a controlled amount of water instead of driving off an uncontrolled amount.

The acid matters more than it looks. Sugar and long heat alone give you brown syrup; the lemon keeps the apple flavor sharp and helps the whole thing gel. Citric acid powder does the job of a lemon at a fraction of the weight, and it does not leak in your pack. Keep the flame low once the sugar goes in, since sugar scorches on a thin pot bottom before you smell it.
