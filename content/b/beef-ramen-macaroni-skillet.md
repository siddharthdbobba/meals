Macaroni alone needs a lot of water and gives back nothing but starch. The ramen block fixes both problems: it cooks in two minutes, the seasoning packet is a full beef broth base in a quarter ounce, and the noodles thicken the leftover water into sauce instead of leaving you draining a pot in the dark.

The vegetables go in with the pasta because they need the whole boil to rehydrate, and dropping them in late leaves you chewing gravel. Oil goes in off the heat, at the end. It is most of the fat in the bowl, and heat only drives the flavor off.
