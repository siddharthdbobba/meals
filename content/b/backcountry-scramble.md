The eggs and beans are doing the real work here—they rehydrate into a vegetarian protein punch that fuels the day. Freeze-dried ingredients are light and shelf-stable, so you're not carrying water weight. The cheese and potato flakes thicken the whole thing from soup-like to something with body.

The scramble comes out very wet, which is the point. That moisture carries fat and seasoning all the way through. Smoked paprika does the heavy lifting on flavor. Make it at home, eat it at camp, one pot.
