Pheasant has almost no fat, so it goes from cooked to dry in about five minutes of inattention. The pickle brine is the insurance. Salt and acid hold water in the muscle, and the leftover juice in a jar is already the right strength, so there is nothing to measure.

The glaze is the counterweight. Sorghum and soda bring the sugar, blackberries and vinegar bring the sour, and the tomato paste gives it enough body to cling instead of running off onto the coals. It only goes on at the end. Sugar over a live fire turns bitter fast, and fifteen minutes is about all it will take.
