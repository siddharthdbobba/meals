This is raw dough as a food group. Brownie mix is already sugar, cocoa, and flour in the right ratio, so it needs nothing but water to become something edible. The oats are structural: they soak up the free water and turn a batter into a dough you can hold. Without them you have a bag of chocolate slurry.

Add water slowly. Too much and the only fix is more oats, which stretches the batch into something bland. Commercial brownie mix is heat-treated flour in most current boxes, but check yours, and if raw flour concerns you, toast the mix in a dry pan at home before packing. Nothing here spoils, so leftovers ride in the bag until they are gone.
