The original is a scout dutch oven recipe: three pounds of beef rolled into meatballs, fried in oil, then simmered with canned soup for a dozen people. Cans and a twelve-inch cast iron pot are not going in a pack. Canned vegetable soup becomes dried soup mix plus bouillon, the meatballs become freeze-dried crumbles, and long-grain rice becomes instant rice so the whole thing finishes on one fuel burn instead of twenty minutes of managed coals.

Staging matters more than anything else here. Vegetables rehydrate slowly and instant rice rehydrates fast, so the vegetables go in over heat and the rice goes in after. Add them together and you get chalky carrots in gummy rice. The oil goes in last because fat coats the grains and slows absorption if it is in the pot early.
