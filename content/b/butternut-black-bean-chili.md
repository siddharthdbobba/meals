The original is a slow cooker recipe: raw squash, canned tomatoes, and a cup of porter left to sit for eight hours. None of that travels. Dehydrated squash cubes rehydrate in about ten minutes of simmering, tomato powder replaces the 28-ounce can at a fraction of the weight, and instant bean flakes give you the beans plus the thickening without a long cook.

The beer was doing a specific job — porter brings roast and chocolate. Instant coffee and cocoa powder, a quarter teaspoon each, get you the same dark bottom note dry. Add the bean flakes off the heat and slowly, or you get lumps that never break up.
