Romesco is a Spanish pepper-and-nut sauce, and it survives camp better than most sauces because nothing in it needs a fridge or a blender. The original is pulsed in a food processor. Chop the nuts fine at home instead and mash the pepper with a spoon, and you get a coarser sauce that does the same job on the plate.

Backstrap is very lean, so it wants high heat and a short stay on the grate. The sauce is where the fat is: half a cup of oil plus two cups of nuts carries the meal. Rest the meat before slicing or the juice ends up on the board instead of in the venison.
