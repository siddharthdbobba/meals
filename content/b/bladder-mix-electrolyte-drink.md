Sweat costs you water and sodium in roughly fixed proportion, and plain water replaces only one of them. This mix puts both back, with sugar along for two reasons: it moves sodium and water across the gut wall faster than water alone, and it makes the salt drinkable. Salt water without sugar tastes like the ocean and you will stop drinking it.

The ratios are chosen to land near the concentration your gut absorbs fastest. Doubling the powder into one liter instead of two does not get the electrolytes in quicker; it slows absorption and can sit heavy. Citric acid and the flavor powder are there to make you keep sipping, which is the part that actually matters.
