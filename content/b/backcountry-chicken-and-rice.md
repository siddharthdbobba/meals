This is freeze-dried chicken's best use case: a full dinner in 15 minutes with no active cooking. Boiling water is all you need—no simmering, no stirring. The instant rice and chicken provide protein; oil and cheese provide the calories that actually fill you up.

Freeze-dried works because it stays shelf-stable until the trail. Seal your freezer bag tight at home, especially for trips longer than a weekend. Either the freezer bag method or one pot on the stove will work equally well.
