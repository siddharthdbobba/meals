The cereal is the reason this works as a walking snack. Oat rings are bulky and dry, so they break up the density of nuts and seeds and keep a handful from feeling like eating a candy bar. That matters over a long day, when a mix that is too rich stops going down.

The peanut butter chips are the fat and the sugar in one piece. Currants carry the fast carbohydrate, almonds and sunflower seeds the protein and staying power. Nothing here needs a cooler or a stove, and the whole mix survives being crushed at the bottom of a pack, which is more than most snacks manage.

Note the nuts: almonds and peanut butter chips both disqualify this for anyone avoiding nuts, and the chips contain milk, so this is not dairy-free.
