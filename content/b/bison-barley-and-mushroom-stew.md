The original is a two-hour braise on raw chuck, which no stove is going to give you at camp. Freeze-dried cooked meat and parboiled barley do the same job in thirty-five minutes and one pot. The mushrooms are not garnish. Dried shiitake and their kin release enough starch and glutamate to give the broth body, which is what a long braise would otherwise have built.

The barley wants the full simmer, so start it before the meat goes in. Adding freeze-dried bison early just turns it stringy. The oil at the end is where most of the fat calories live, and it also carries the thyme and pepper. Skip it and you have soup instead of dinner.
