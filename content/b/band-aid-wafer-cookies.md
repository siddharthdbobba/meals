This is a kitchen craft project moved outdoors with two changes. The original shaves the cookie corners round with a zester, which is a fussy indoor step, so it is gone. And tub frosting scooped into a piping bag means carrying a spoon and a bag you have to clean, so a squeeze tube does the same job with nothing to wash.

The rest works on the trail because all three parts are already shelf-stable. Wafers, icing, and jam sit at ambient temperature for months. The only real constraint is crushing: assemble at camp, never at home, and keep the cookies in something with walls.
