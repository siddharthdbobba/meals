The original is a fresh-beef, soft-boiled-egg dinner cooked at home. On trail both of those go away. Jerky replaces the steak and rehydrates in the hot broth in about two minutes, which is enough to soften it without turning it to string. The egg becomes powder or nothing; boiling one in its shell costs seven minutes of fuel and gets you one egg.

The broth is where the recipe lives. Bouillon, soy, and sesame oil do the work a seasoning packet does, with more fat and more depth. Add the sriracha off heat so the vinegar in it does not cook flat. Nothing else needs salt, and adding some will wreck it.
