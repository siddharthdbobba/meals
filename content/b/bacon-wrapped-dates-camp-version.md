This is a party appetizer that happens to suit a fire pit better than an oven. Bacon renders its own fat, so the dates fry in it rather than sticking, and a foil sheet on the grate means the only thing to clean up is the fire.

The order matters. Sugar burns long before bacon crisps, so the honey and sriracha go on after the dates come off the coals. Bacon is the one cooler item here; the dates and honey keep indefinitely, so this is a first-night dish on a car-camping or paddle trip and not something to plan for day four.
