The source version runs on a cooler: raw eggs, fresh potatoes, sliced bacon. Everything here is the shelf-stable equivalent, which matters most in the second half of a trip when the ice is gone. Dehydrated hash browns rehydrate in ten minutes of cold soak and brown better than fresh ones because there is less water left in them to boil off.

The order of operations is the whole recipe. Bacon first so its fat seasons the pan, potatoes second with enough room to actually contact metal, eggs last because powdered egg sets fast and turns rubbery if it sits over heat. Tortillas mean no plates, which on a group morning is the difference between one dish bin and four.
