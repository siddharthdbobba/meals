Grits offer what oatmeal does not: savory breakfast with a smooth texture that doesn't turn to mush. The butter and dry milk supply calories and fat, and they taste like breakfast instead of just hot grain. It settles the stomach well even after a hard day.

Three cooking methods work. Freezer-bag cooking in a padded cozy removes all cleanup. An insulated mug keeps breakfast warm while you break camp. Or boil water in a small pot and eat right away. All three take five minutes, one spoon, and no skill.
