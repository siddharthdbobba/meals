Carrot cake is built on oil and sugar—they make it travel and bake well in a Dutch oven. The cream cheese frosting is gone (too heavy and spoils at camp). Dehydrated carrots replace fresh ones; they soften in the batter and you won't notice. Egg replacer handles binding. The oil stays the same because it's doing the real work.

This is a basecamp recipe, not something you bake while moving. You need a Dutch oven, sustained coals or heat, and fifty minutes of patience. That means a multi-day camp or car-camping setup. On your last night at a site with wood to spare, this cake feeds eight people and uses equipment you already have. Cold cake slices better and travels better—that's your snack for hiking out.
