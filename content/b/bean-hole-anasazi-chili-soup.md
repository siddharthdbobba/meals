This is a car-camping recipe and stays one. Dry heirloom beans need hours of steady low heat and a lot of water, which is exactly what a dutch oven over coals gives you and exactly what a canister stove does not. Everything else is shelf-stable; only the hocks and the soaked beans need the cooler.

The long simmer is what pulls the ham fat and gelatin into the water, and that is where the body comes from. Salt goes in late on purpose, after the beans are already tender, and the raw onion and chile on top exist to put something sharp against three hours of soft. The lemon does the same job from the other direction.
