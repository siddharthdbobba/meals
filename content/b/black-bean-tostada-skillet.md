The original bakes tortillas in an oven and uses fresh zucchini, neither of which travels. Toasting on a dry pan does the same job in a minute: the tortilla loses enough moisture to hold weight without going to cracker. Dehydrated beans, corn, and zucchini rehydrate in the same pot with the seasoning, which means the spice ends up inside the vegetables instead of sitting on top.

Watch the water. Too much and the topping slides off the tortilla and into your lap. Cook it down until it holds a shape on the spoon. Hard cheese grated at home is the swap for the refrigerated shredded blend, and it melts better anyway.
