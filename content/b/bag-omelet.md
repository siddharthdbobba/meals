Boiling the bag instead of frying it means the eggs cook by steam at a steady 212F, so they cannot scorch. That matters on a camp stove, where the flame is either off or blasting and nothing in between. It also means the pot stays clean. The water is just a heat bath, and you can reuse it for coffee or dishes.

Use freezer-weight bags. Thin sandwich bags soften and can split at boiling temperature, and slider seals leak. Push all the air out before sealing or the bag balloons and the eggs cook unevenly. The original version leaned on fresh eggs and a cooler; egg powder and dried vegetables get you the same breakfast with nothing that needs to stay cold.
