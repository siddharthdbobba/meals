The pasta water is the sauce here. Draining it on the trail means a second pot and a wasted cup of hot water, so instead you cook the penne down until the starch is thick enough to hold fat. Milk powder and parmesan stand in for the cream, and they only work in that concentrated liquid.

The shrimp cannot go in the boil. Freeze-dried shrimp turns to rubber if it sits in hot water, so it soaks cold on the side and gets stirred in at the end. Blackening seasoning is just the four powders mixed at home; on a stove there is no dry sear to bloom them, so they season the sauce rather than crusting the shrimp. This one is not gluten-free or dairy-free.
