Raw beet holds up in a cooler better than any leafy green, and it does not wilt when it sits in dressing. That makes this one of the few salads worth carrying past the first day. Grating happens at home, where the sink is, because beet juice on a camp table stays there all trip.

The vinegar is not just flavor. Acid softens the shredded beet over about five minutes, which is what turns a pile of raw root vegetable into something you want to eat. Toast the seeds before you leave; a stove at camp is not needed for anything here, and toasted seeds go stale slower than they go soft in a bag.
