The beer does two jobs. Its carbonation lifts the batter before the leavening catches up, and its sugars brown the outside faster than water does. That matters over a fire, where heat is uneven and you want color before the inside overcooks.

The original called for fresh buttermilk and a real egg. Both are replaced here with powders so the whole dry side rides in one bag at ambient temperature. Whole wheat flour is the other structural piece: straight white flour in a camp iron tears when you open it.
