Canoe trips carry weight the way car camping does, but the barrel is still finite and nothing in it can be wet or fragile. So this is a chili with the can taken out of it. Tomato powder does what a can of crushed tomatoes does at a tenth of the weight and none of the dent risk, and the bean flakes rehydrate in two minutes instead of the hour dry beans want.

The order matters more than the ingredients. TVP and dried corn need real simmer time; rice needs a covered soak; bean flakes only need to be stirred into hot liquid at the end. Dump it all in at once and you get crunchy corn in bean paste. Adding the flakes last also lets you steer the thickness — stop early for something you drink from the pot, keep going for something you eat with a spoon on a rock.
