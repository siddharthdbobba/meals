Red lentils are the whole argument. They cost almost nothing per pound, they cook in under ten minutes because they are split and hulled, and they collapse into a thick base that carries fat and salt. Whole lentils will not do this on a canister stove without burning half your fuel.

The instant rice goes in last and off the heat, so it rehydrates on stored warmth instead of more gas. The oil is not optional. Lentils and rice together are cheap protein and cheap carbohydrate, but they are close to fat-free, and a day of walking wants the calories.
