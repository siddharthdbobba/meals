Every ingredient here except the onion is already cooked, so the stove is only rehydrating. That is why the cold soak before the boil matters: dried hash browns and diced carrots are the slowest things in the bag, and five minutes of head start keeps them from staying gritty in the middle.

The pot method beats a freezer bag for this one. Tomato sauce powder and potato starch need a real boil to thicken into something that clings to rice, and a bag will not get you there. Cook the rice first and let it sit off-heat while the stew works; one pot, one stove, two things done.
