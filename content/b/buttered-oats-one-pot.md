The original is a pressure cooker recipe, which is not a thing you carry. Boiling water and a covered pot get you the same place: oats absorb water fine off the heat, and five minutes of steeping costs no fuel at all. Old-fashioned rolled oats are the only ones that work here. Instant turns to paste and steel cut never softens.

Butter is the point and it is also the problem, so it becomes ghee. The milk solids are already cooked out, which means it sits in a pack at 80 degrees without going rancid. The fat is what turns a cup of oats from a thin breakfast into something that carries you a few hours, and the salt is what keeps it from tasting like wallpaper paste.
