The original is an oven recipe with goat cheese and spring mix, neither of which survives a weekend in a cooler. The cheese is gone here and the greens are swapped for kale or cabbage, which take a day of jostling without going to mush. What is left is the part that actually carries the dish: caramelized squash, a sharp dressing, and something crunchy.

Heat is the whole game. A foil packet on a grate does not roast the way a 425-degree oven does, so the squash steams unless you leave the top open and stir it. Dumping the cubes on the greens while they are still hot wilts them just enough, which is the only thing the cheese was doing for texture anyway.
