This started as a muffin recipe and lost the oven. There is no fridge on a trip, so the bananas come as chips rehydrated back into mush, the eggs and the Greek yogurt come as powder, and the whole thing cooks flat in one pan instead of twelve paper cups. The chemistry is unchanged: acid-free batter, baking powder and soda doing the lift, oil keeping whole wheat from setting up like a brick.

Low heat is the only real skill here. Whole grain batter over open flame burns on the bottom before the middle sets, so keep the coals thin and the lid on so heat comes from above too. The nuts on top toast while it bakes, which is worth more than mixing them all in.
