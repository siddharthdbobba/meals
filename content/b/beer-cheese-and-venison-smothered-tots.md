This is a car camp dish and it does not pretend otherwise. Frozen potatoes, cold beer, real cheese, and a cooler that has to hold for the whole trip. There is no backpacking version of it worth eating.

The sauce is a plain roux, and the only place it goes wrong is the cheese. Melt shredded cheddar into liquid that is still boiling and the fat separates out into a greasy slick. Move the skillet off the coals first and add it in handfuls. The beer thins the sauce enough to pour, which matters because the tots need to stay crisp under it rather than drown.
