  - "Burn the fire down to coals and spread them flat. You want to hold a hand a hand's width above the grate for five or six seconds before it hurts. Flames will split the casing."
  - "Slice the onion. Heat the oil in a pot on one side of the grate and cook the onion until it goes soft and brown at the edges, about 8 minutes."

Boerewors is a coarse-ground sausage in a long coil, and the whole recipe is heat management. Fat renders slowly at medium heat and stays inside the casing. Over open flame the casing bursts, the fat drips out, and you get something dry and gray. So bank the coals down and be patient with it.

The relish exists to cut the fat. Onion cooked long enough to sweeten, tomato reduced until it stops being watery, a splash of vinegar for edge. It is also the reason this stays a one-pot job: cook the relish first, park the pot at the cool side of the grate, and the sausage never needs a pan. This one needs a cooler, so it is a car camping meal, not a pack meal.
