Bread soup exists because stale bread is not garbage. Dry the cubes hard at home — an hour or two in a low oven, or a dehydrator — and they keep for months without a fridge and weigh almost nothing. Wet bread molds; bone-dry bread does not.

The loaf matters more than anything else here. Sandwich bread collapses into paste the moment it hits hot water. Sourdough or a dense rye holds its structure long enough to eat with a spoon. Dried sausage is the only real protein in the pot, so carry it unless you are deliberately going meatless.
