Brown butter carries the toasted, nutty flavor that campfire heat would add to marshmallows, so these bars taste like s'mores at their best instead of just sugar and chocolate melting apart. Make them several days before your trip so they have time to set completely—they won't turn gooey in your pack even on warm days.

They're shelf-stable and durable in a way fresh baked goods aren't, which means you get a real dessert on the trail instead of crumbs and mess. The butter preserves well and keeps the bars flavorful through a full week of backcountry travel.
