Freezer-bag cooking exists so you never wash a pot. Boil water, pour it in, close the bag, wait. The bag insulates well enough that instant rice finishes without a second application of heat, which is the whole point on a small fuel canister.

The soup mix alone is thin, so the tomato powder and the oil are doing the work. Tomato powder adds body and acid; the oil carries the calories that a vegetable soup otherwise has none of. Skip both and you are drinking hot water with rice in it.
