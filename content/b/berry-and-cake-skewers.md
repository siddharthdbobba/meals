The source for this is a scratch pound cake: six eggs, a pound of butter, ninety minutes in an oven. None of that happens at a campsite. Boxed shelf-stable pound cake is the same dense, close-grained crumb, and dense is what matters here — an airy cake tears apart on the skewer and falls into the fire.

Dried berries replace fresh for the obvious reason, and they toast better anyway. Fresh strawberries collapse into water over coals. Freeze-dried ones just warm through and stay put. Keep the skewers over coals rather than flame; cake is mostly sugar and goes from browned to black fast.
