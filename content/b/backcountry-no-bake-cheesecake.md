Home no-bake cheesecake leans on cream cheese and a fridge, neither of which exists on trail. Instant pudding mix and powdered whole milk do the same job: the starch and the protein set up in cold water alone, no gelatin and no chilling below air temperature. Ten minutes of standing is enough for it to hold a spoon.

The crumb goes in last and stays dry until then. Butter powder gives it enough fat to read as crust rather than cracker dust, and freeze-dried berries pull water out of the mix as they rehydrate, which tightens the whole thing further. Warm afternoons make it looser; it still eats fine, just closer to pudding than to cheesecake.
