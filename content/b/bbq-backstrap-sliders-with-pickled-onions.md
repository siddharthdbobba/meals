Backstrap is the leanest cut on the animal, which is the whole problem and the whole point. There is no fat to render, so every minute past medium-rare costs you. Cook it hot and fast, pull it while the center is pink, and let it rest before slicing. Sauce goes on after the slice, not before the grill, or the sugar in it burns.

The pickled onions do the work the fat cannot. Sweet BBQ sauce on lean meat is one-note, and acid cuts through it. They need to sit at least overnight, so make them at home and let the jar ride in the cooler. This stays car camping because of the cooler and the buns; there is no way to make raw backstrap shelf-stable.
