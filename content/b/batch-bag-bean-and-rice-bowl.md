Meal prep at home usually means cooked food in containers in a fridge. On trail the same idea works better dry: do the measuring once, at a table, with a scale, and then every night on the trip is one bag and one boil. The variable that ruins camp dinners is not skill, it is fumbling five separate containers in the dark.

Bean flakes and instant rice are both parcooked, so they rehydrate off residual heat rather than a rolling simmer. That is the whole reason this runs on one short burn. The oil and the chips are not garnish. They are most of the fat, and without them the bowl comes in around 450 calories, which is not a dinner after twenty miles.
