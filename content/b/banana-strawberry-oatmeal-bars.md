This started as a muffin recipe with yogurt and fresh fruit in it, which is two problems on a trip. Milk powder plus a little lemon juice covers what the yogurt was doing: liquid, a bit of protein, and enough acid to wake up the baking soda. Soaked banana chips replace the mashed ripe banana, and freeze-dried strawberries go in dry so they do not water down the batter.

Bars instead of muffins, because a flat foil pan packs better than a muffin tin and bakes more evenly under a dutch oven lid. Keep the batter shallow and take the heat mostly from the top — coals underneath will scorch the bottom long before the center sets. The oats are structural here; they hold the bars together well enough to survive a few days in a lid pocket.
