Freeze-dried chicken in small dice rehydrates fast—these soften in 15 minutes instead of the 30 that larger chunks need. The powdered cream cheese and buttermilk do what mayo normally does in a chicken salad: bind the ingredients into something cohesive that stays together in a wrap. Without them you have a loose pile of meat.

This is shelf-stable and no-cook, making it practical trail lunch that doesn't compete for stove space. Blackened spice, smoked paprika, and cayenne deliver the hot-wing heat without weight or fresh ingredients. You eat it cold, spooned straight from a bowl or wrapped in a tortilla.
