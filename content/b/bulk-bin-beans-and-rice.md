This is what bulk dry storage is actually for. Rice, beans, and cornmeal are the three things worth buying by the bucket, and they happen to be the three things that make a trail dinner. The catch is that whole dried beans need forty minutes and a lot of fuel, so the bean flakes do that job instead — same shelf, tenth of the burn time.

The cornmeal is the part people leave out. Two tablespoons turn a pot of loose rice and water into something that holds together on a spoon, and it thickens without any dairy. Salt and oil go in at camp, not in the storage bag: fat is the first thing to go rancid in long-term dry goods.
