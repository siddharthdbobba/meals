Broccoli cheddar soup is normally a cream-and-fridge dish, so most of the work here is replacing what you can't carry. Powdered whole milk plus a teaspoon of cornstarch does what the heavy cream did: body and mouthfeel without a cooler. Freeze-dried broccoli rehydrates in the same water that becomes the broth, so nothing gets drained and no water is wasted.

The cheese is the part people get wrong. Aged cheddar melts into a hot pot, but held over a flame it splits and you end up with orange oil on top of thin soup. Kill the heat first, add it in pieces, stir. Soy sauce stands in for Worcestershire — same job, a squirt of salt and umami, in a bottle that survives a pack.
