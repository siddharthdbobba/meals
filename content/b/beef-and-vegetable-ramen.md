Freeze-dried vegetables and beef rehydrate in the hot broth and add texture and seasoning that plain ramen lacks. The oil and parmesan are where the calories come from; ramen and broth alone are mostly starch.

One pot, one boil, and everything happens at once. The freeze-dried mix replaces the meat and vegetables you'd normally carry fresh, at nearly nothing for weight.
