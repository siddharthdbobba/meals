This is a car-camp dinner, not a trail dinner. Fresh fish, butter, and a lemon all need a cooler, so cook it the night you drive in.

The flour is doing two jobs. It gives the fillets something to brown against, and what comes off in the pan thickens the beer into a gravy thin enough to soak into the rice. Cook the rice first and leave it covered; fish takes ten minutes and waits for nobody, while rice holds its heat fine. The beer mostly cooks off. What stays behind is malt and a little bitterness, which is what keeps the butter from tasting flat.
