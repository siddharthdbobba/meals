This is a home-prep dessert, not a camp one. Everything happens in a blender on your counter, and what you carry is finished food. The oats do double duty: blended to flour they bind the mix without any egg or baking, and they keep absorbing the butter as the balls sit, which is why a chill in the fridge turns a sandy crumb into something that holds a shape.

Butter is the binder and most of the calories. It is also the failure point in heat — above room temperature these go soft and greasy. Coconut oil or shortening has a higher effective firmness in a warm pack and swaps in one for one. There is no fresh dairy and no water, so they keep for about a week out of a cooler and much longer if you freeze them before the trip.
