The original wants a mixer, a fridge, and four hours. None of that travels. Cream cheese powder plus milk powder does the same job in a bag: enough fat and protein to thicken when it meets water, no whipping required. The trade is that you get a thick pudding rather than a set cheesecake, which is fine when you are eating it out of a titanium mug.

Cold water matters. Hot water makes the mix slimy and thin. The five-minute rest is not optional either, since powdered dairy hydrates slowly and the difference between grainy and smooth is entirely that pause. Freeze-dried strawberries are the only fruit worth carrying here because fresh ones bruise and dried ones are chewy and too sweet against the filling.
