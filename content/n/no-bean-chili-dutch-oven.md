No beans means nothing is soaking up liquid, so the tomatoes and the fat have to do the thickening on their own. That is what the hour at 300F is for. The chuck breaks down slowly and the connective tissue turns the liquid into something that coats a spoon. Rush it and you get chunks of tough beef sitting in tomato juice.

The bacon grease is not a garnish, it is the cooking fat for two pounds of lean meat. Do not pour it off. Six jalapenos sounds aggressive but seeding them takes most of the heat out; leave the seeds in a couple if the group wants it to bite. This one needs a cooler, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp, not in a pack.
