This is a home kitchen recipe with the fridge taken out of it. The original leans on a jar of mayo and fresh dill; packets and dried dill do the same work and survive a warm pack all day. Canned chickpeas are heavy, which is why this belongs in a car, a boat, or a day pack rather than a week of backpacking.

Mashing only half the chickpeas matters. Fully mashed it turns into paste and slides out the end of the tortilla. Left whole it will not hold together at all. The lemon juice and mustard cut the mayo, which otherwise tastes flat once everything has warmed to ambient.
