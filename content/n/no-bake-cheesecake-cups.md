The source version wants a cooler: fresh cream cheese, fresh yogurt, and jars kept cold until dessert. That works for one night in a car and nothing else. Swapping in cream cheese powder and Greek yogurt powder gets you the same tang and the same body from two bags of dry mix that ride in a bin all week.

Water is the whole game. The crust needs just enough to clump and the filling needs just enough to whisk smooth, and both go wrong fast if you dump it in. Add it in small amounts and stop early. Assemble at camp rather than at home, because a pressed crumb crust sitting under a wet filling for six hours is no longer a crust.
