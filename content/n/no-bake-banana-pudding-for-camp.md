Instant pudding is one of the few desserts that works with cold water and no fridge, because the starch sets chemically rather than by chilling. Whole milk powder does the job of the milk the box asks for and adds the fat that makes it read as pudding instead of sweet paste.

Banana chips replace fresh banana, which is a non-starter on trail. They go in soft-side-up early so some of them hydrate and some stay crunchy. Graham crackers go on last for the same reason: once they are in the wet pudding, the clock is running.
