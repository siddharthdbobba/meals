The original is a fridge dessert built on fresh peaches and whipped cream, neither of which survives a weekend outside. Two swaps fix that. Freeze-dried peaches rehydrate in cold water in about fifteen minutes and hold their shape better than canned. Whipped topping powder whips with cold water instead of cream, so nothing needs to stay at 40 degrees.

The set comes from the condensed milk and lemon juice, not from heat. Acid thickens the milk on contact, which is why the filling firms up in the pot without any cooking. Drain the peaches properly before folding them in. Leftover soak water thins the filling and it will never recover.
