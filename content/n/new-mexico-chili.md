The mashed beans are the whole reason this holds together. Chili without a thickener sits as meat in broth, and flour or masa is one more thing to carry and one more thing to lump. A third of a cup of beans crushed to paste and stirred back in does it with an ingredient already in the pot.

This one wants a cooler and a real fire, so it is a car camping or basecamp dinner, not a backpacking one. The dicing is done at home. In camp you are browning meat and waiting, which is what a Dutch oven is for. Keep the fire low under it once the lid goes on; cast iron over direct flame scorches the bottom long before the carrots are done.
