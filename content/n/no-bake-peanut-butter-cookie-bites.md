The original is a baked cookie with eggs and flour, which is a problem three days from a trailhead. Powdered milk and oat flour do what the flour and eggs were doing: they bind the dough and give it structure without an oven. Peanut butter and butter were already the fat, so nothing changes there.

Skip natural peanut butter. The stabilized kind holds the dough together under pack pressure, while the oily kind separates and leaves you with grease and crumbs. The two-hour air dry matters too: it firms the outside so the bites do not weld into a single mass in the bag.
