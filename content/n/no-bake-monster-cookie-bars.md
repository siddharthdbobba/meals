The source made drop cookies chilled on a sheet pan. Cookies break. Bars pressed into a pan and cut hold together, so this version trades the shape for durability and drops the butter, which is the only ingredient here that needs a refrigerator. Peanut butter alone carries the fat and does the same binding job at room temperature.

Melted peanut butter chips are the structure. They set up firm as they cool, which is why these hold their edge in a pack instead of turning into a bag of oats and sugar. Candy-coated chocolate is deliberate over plain chips: the shell keeps it from smearing across everything at 85 degrees.
