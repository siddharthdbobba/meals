This is assembly, not cooking. The point is a breakfast you can eat standing up while the stove is busy with coffee, or skip the stove entirely on a morning when you want to be walking by six.

Roll them at breakfast rather than the night before. Honey migrates. Given eight hours it will find its way through the tortilla and leave you with something limp and translucent. Keeping the spread thin and the fruit in a separate bag until the last minute is the whole trick. The seeds are there for texture and a little more fat, and they hold up better than anything crunchy that has starch in it.
