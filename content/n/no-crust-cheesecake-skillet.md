The original is a crustless Easter cheesecake: fresh farmer cheese, eggs, sour cream, butter, held together by a little flour and semolina. None of those first four survive a trip without a cooler, so all four come out of a bag here. Whole milk powder and sour cream powder rebuild the dairy body, powdered egg does the binding, and butter powder or ghee carries the fat.

The semolina is the part that matters most. It swells as it heats and takes up the loose water, which is what turns a wet batter into something that holds a slice instead of running off the spoon. Coals on the lid do the rest — heat from above browns the top the way an oven would, and without it you get a pale, gummy surface.
