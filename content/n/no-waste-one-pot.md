This is the meal you make on the last night, when the food bag holds a cup of this and a scoop of that. The structure matters more than the exact contents: a starch that rehydrates fast, a fat, a salt source, and something that used to be a vegetable.

The order is the part worth keeping. Dried vegetables need real boiling time and instant rice needs almost none, so the vegetables go in first and the rice goes in last, off heat. Reverse that and you get mush around gravel. The sausage is doing double duty as protein and as rendered fat, which is why the oil is only a tablespoon.
