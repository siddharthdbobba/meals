The original is a Turkish-style nettle soup: fresh greens, mirepoix, a potato, chicken stock, half-pureed. None of that travels. The fix is to dehydrate the greens and mirepoix at home and replace the potato with flakes, which do the same job of thickening the pot without twenty minutes of boiling a tuber.

The part worth keeping is the finish. Fat goes in after the heat is off, not at the start, so it sits on the surface and carries the mushroom and onion flavor instead of dissolving into the broth. Dried mushrooms rehydrate in the cooking water and pull double duty as the stock. If you dry your own nettles, blanch them first so they lose the sting.
