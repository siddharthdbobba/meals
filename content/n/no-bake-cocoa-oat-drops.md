This is a cooked-sugar candy that happens to contain oats. The one-minute boil is the whole recipe: it drives off enough water that the syrup sets firm as it cools. Boil less and you get sludge. Boil more and the drops turn dry and crumbly.

The original uses fresh milk, which is a problem away from a fridge. Powdered whole milk and 80 ml of water do the same job and ride in a bag. Butter still needs to be reasonably cool, so this is a car-camping and basecamp dessert unless you swap in coconut oil. Everything sets faster in cold air, so give it a shaded rock rather than a sunny table.
