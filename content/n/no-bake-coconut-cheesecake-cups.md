The original is a fridge dessert: real cream cheese, a can of condensed milk, eight hours to set. None of that travels. This version rebuilds the same flavor from powders, which rehydrate into a thick paste instead of a pourable batter, so it holds its shape without chilling.

Water is the whole game. Cream cheese powder wants far less than it looks like it does, and once it goes soupy there is no way back on the trail. Add it in splashes and stop early. The freeze-dried strawberries stay crisp on top until the moment they hit the filling, which is the closest you get to fresh fruit out here.
