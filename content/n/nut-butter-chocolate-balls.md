Calorie density is the point. Nut butter and chocolate deliver the energy per gram that matters when you need to minimize pack weight. The oats bind everything; the dried fruit adds flavor without adding significant weight. Unlike energy bars, these don't harden unpleasantly as they warm in your pack.

Make them at home and bring by the handful. They stay shelf-stable for weeks and carry cleanly. Store cold before packing if you prefer them firm; in warmer conditions they soften to almost fudgy.
