The original is a fridge dessert: block cream cheese, whipped topping, two hours of chilling. None of that travels. Cream cheese powder does the same job dry, and milk powder plus a careful amount of cold water rebuilds the body that whipped topping was providing. It sets softer than the pie it came from, so serve it in the pot rather than trying to slice it.

Water is the failure point. The powder will take more than it needs and turn to soup, and there is no way back from soup without another bag of powder. Add it in small pours and stop while the mix still holds a ridge from the spoon. Crushed cookies stirred in absorb a little of the excess if you overshoot.
