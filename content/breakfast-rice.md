This is a leftovers recipe, which is the point: it costs one extra cup of rice
and two minutes at dinner, and it buys you a hot breakfast that needs no second
ingredient list.

It only works on day two of a given rice pot. Cooked rice left warm for longer
than a night is not something to gamble a trip on.
