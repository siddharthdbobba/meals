This started as a home skillet supper and lost the parts that need a fridge. Fresh Italian sausage becomes dry-cured sausage, which is already cooked and keeps for days in a pack. Fresh peppers and onions become dried ones, and their soaking water goes back into the pan rather than down the ground, because that is where the flavor went.

Polenta is the reason this is one pan instead of two. It cooks in the same liquid the sausage rendered into, so the fat ends up in the grain instead of pooling. Add it slowly and keep stirring; instant polenta thickens fast and lumps are permanent.
