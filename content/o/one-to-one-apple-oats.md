The whole recipe rests on the water ratio. Standard oatmeal is two parts water to one part oats, which gives you a bowl of glue. One to one gives you oats that are cooked through but still separate, and it means carrying and boiling less water.

Dried apple is the trail version of a fresh sliced apple. It goes in dry with the oats and pulls water back out of the pot while everything sits, so it does double duty: it softens itself and it keeps the mix from getting soupy. The oil and seeds are the calories. Without them this is a light snack, not a morning that gets you over a pass.
