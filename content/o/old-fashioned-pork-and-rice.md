The original is a two and a half hour oven braise on skin-on pork belly, which is not a thing you carry to a campsite. Canned pork is the substitution that makes it portable: it is already cooked, so the long braise collapses into a 30-minute simmer whose only job is reducing the sauce and getting it into the meat.

The sauce is the recipe. Brown sugar, vinegar, mustard, and soy give you sweet, sharp, and salty in one pot, and the bourbon carries the orange and whole spices into everything. Pull the cinnamon stick and cloves before serving or someone bites one. Instant rice goes in at the end so it soaks up the reduction instead of diluting it.
