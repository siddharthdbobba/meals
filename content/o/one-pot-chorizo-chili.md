The original is a car-camping dish: fresh tomatoes, a canned steak, a tin of beans in their liquid. All of that is water you would be carrying. Sun-dried tomatoes plus tomato powder rebuild the sauce from about two ounces, and instant beans skip the hour of boiling that whole dried beans demand.

Cured chorizo is the ingredient doing the most work. It is shelf-stable for weeks, it renders fat into the pot, and it seasons everything without a fridge. The chili packet is the trap: those are portioned for four and will strip the enamel off your teeth if you dump the whole thing in. Measure two teaspoons at home and leave the rest behind.
