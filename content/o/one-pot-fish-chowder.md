The original is a scout-camp chowder built on fresh potatoes, an onion, and a can of evaporated milk, simmered a half hour in a big pot. The trail version keeps the structure and drops the weight: potato flakes instead of diced potatoes, milk powder instead of the can, foil-pouch fish instead of fillets, and bacon crumbles instead of raw bacon you would have to render and then carry the grease from.

Order matters more than technique. Milk powder goes into thin liquid or it seizes into lumps, and the potato flakes go in last because they are the thickener and you cannot take them back out. Off the heat for both, which also solves the source's warning about scorched milk on the pot bottom. The ghee is not optional — without added fat this reads as thin and salty rather than as chowder.
