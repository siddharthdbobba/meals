The home version browns beef in the pot and drains the fat. On trail there is no fat to drain and nowhere to put it, so freeze-dried beef rehydrates in the water first and the oil goes in at the end where it stays in the food. Tomato powder replaces the six-ounce can of paste — same sauce, four ounces lighter, and no lid to pack out.

The pasta cooks in its own sauce, which means no straining and no wasted water. That also means it scorches easily. Keep the flame low and the spoon moving; the starch coming off the spaghetti is what thickens the whole thing, and it will grab the bottom of a titanium pot the second you stop stirring.
