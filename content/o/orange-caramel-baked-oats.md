This started as a blender-and-oven breakfast: fresh milk, a raw egg, half a banana, orange slices on top. None of that carries. Milk powder, whole egg powder, and crushed dried banana do the same three jobs — liquid, binder, sweetness and body — and they ride in one bag at ambient temperature. Dried orange slices replace the fresh fruit; they soften as the batter bakes and give up most of their aroma into it.

The egg powder is what makes this a baked good rather than porridge. It sets the middle so the whole thing holds together when you spoon it. Baking powder gives it enough lift that it does not turn into a dense puck. Keep the coals moderate: too much bottom heat and the edges scorch before the center firms, which is the one way this fails.
