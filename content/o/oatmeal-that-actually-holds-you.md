Plain instant oatmeal is thin and gone by mid-morning. The milk powder is what fixes it. It adds protein and fat, and it turns the whole thing opaque and creamy instead of leaving a bowl of loose grain in hot water. Two packets rather than one is the other half of the fix.

The dried fruit goes in dry so it rehydrates in the same five minutes the oats do. Chop it small at home; whole raisins soften, but a big chunk of dried apple stays leathery. Everything here is shelf-stable, and the bag is the bowl, so there is nothing to wash.
