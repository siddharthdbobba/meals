The original bakes this in an oven for forty minutes. On a stove you get the same result faster, because the pasta cooks directly in the sauce instead of waiting for a covered dish to come up to temperature. The starch that comes off the noodles is what thickens everything, so do not drain anything and do not use more water than the recipe calls for.

Fresh milk becomes powdered milk, and the meatballs come from a can or a vacuum pouch rather than a freezer. Both changes are about temperature control you do not have. The mozzarella is the one weak link: it goes greasy above about 70 degrees, so leave it home on hot trips and lean on the parmesan.
