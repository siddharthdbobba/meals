This is the Lipton packet rebuilt out of your own spice drawer, which matters on trail for two reasons: you control the salt, and one small jar replaces four separate seasonings. The celery seed is the part people skip and the part that makes it taste like the packet. Grind it before you leave.

The onion flakes are the reason this needs boiling water and a rest, not just a stir. Dropped into lukewarm water they stay hard and the broth tastes thin. Four minutes covered fixes both. Bouillon granules already carry salt, so measure the added salt against your brand rather than the recipe.
