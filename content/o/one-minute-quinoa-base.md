Quinoa comes coated in saponin, a bitter compound that tastes like dish soap. Rinsing removes it. That is the whole reason bad quinoa is bad, and it is also why this is a home-prep recipe: you rinse and dry the grain on a sheet pan before the trip, not with your filtered water at 9,000 feet.

The 1:1 ratio is what makes it a trail grain. Rice wants twice its volume in water; quinoa needs the same volume it occupies, which is less to carry and less to boil. The long off-heat rest does most of the cooking, so the actual fuel burn is about two minutes. Overcooking is the only real failure mode here, and the lid prevents it.
