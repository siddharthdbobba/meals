Fried rice on the trail starts with shelf-stable rice pouches and freeze-dried components that don't need a cooler. The vegetables and eggs rehydrate in cold water before you even light the stove, so the cooking is just assembly and heat. One pot, no timing, done in minutes.

Freeze-dried eggs and vegetables do the real work. They bulk out the rice and take on the oil better than wet leftovers. Canned chicken is already cooked, so you're mostly there to bind everything together. Not gourmet, but it fills you up after a long day.
