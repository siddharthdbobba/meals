This is a dish for a small, powerful, single-flame stove: one burner, one pot, twelve minutes. The sausage is doing double duty. It is the protein, and its rendered fat is the cooking oil for everything after it, which is why it goes in cold and comes out first.

The rice never actually boils. It steams off the heat, which matters on a stove that runs hot and concentrated — a covered pot coasting on residual heat cooks rice more evenly than a hot spot the size of a coin. Tomato paste from a tube instead of a can because you will not use a whole can and you cannot carry the rest.
