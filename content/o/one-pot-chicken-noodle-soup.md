This is a one-pot meal that trades technique for calorie density. The oil is essential—not as seasoning, but as fuel. Thin broth alone will not sustain you through a cold night, but broth enriched with oil will. Canned chicken is already cooked, so you are really just rehydrating pasta and vegetables.

Pack this at home in a single bag for breakfast-level friction at dinnertime, which matters when you are tired and cold. The recipe scales easily: double the ingredients for two people. Watch closely as it cooks. A pot of soup bubbles up fast on a camp stove and will boil over if you look away.
