This is a plating trick more than a recipe. Hollowing an orange gives you four disposable bowls that do not need washing and do not blow off the table, which is most of what a camp dessert asks for. The flesh you scoop out is not waste — cut into wedges it cuts the sweetness of the ice cream.

The limit is the cooler, not the technique. Ice cream only works when the cooler is packed hard with block ice and has not been opened all afternoon, so this is a car-camping or basecamp dessert and nothing else. Flatten the bottom of each half before filling it; a round shell rocks, and the scoop goes over the side.
