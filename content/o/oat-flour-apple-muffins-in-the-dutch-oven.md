There is no flour in this. Rolled oats blended fine enough behave close to soft wheat flour, and the little bit of texture they leave is the point. Oat flour also drinks liquid slowly, which is why the batter rests before it goes in the oven. Skip the rest and the muffins come out wet in the middle.

Applesauce carries most of the moisture, so the butter is down to a quarter cup for twelve muffins. The diced apple is there because oat batter tends toward uniform and dense, and pockets of fruit break that up. Coals are the variable you actually control: too many underneath and the bottoms scorch before the centers set, which is why most of the heat sits on the lid.
