Cooking pasta in its own sauce is the whole point. No straining, no second pot, and the starch that would go down a drain at home stays in the pot and thickens everything. On a stove where every boil costs fuel, that matters more than it does in a kitchen.

The home version starts with browned ground beef and a jar of marinara, neither of which travels. Tomato powder plus water reconstitutes to about the same thing at a fraction of the weight, and freeze-dried beef crumbles rehydrate in the simmer without needing to be cooked. Watch the bottom of the pot — thin titanium scorches tomato in seconds, so keep the flame low and the spoon moving.
