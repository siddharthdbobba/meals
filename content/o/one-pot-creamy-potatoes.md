Powdered milk and butter replace fresh cream cheese and sour cream, keeping the dish shelf-stable and lightweight. The powdered milk prevents the potatoes from separating into soup as they cool, which matters when you are eating out of the pot.

One pot, one boil, five minutes total. It is a warming side that does not monopolize your stove.
