This started as a full holiday spread with a roast bird and separate pots for everything. On trail there is one pot and one boil, so the components collapse into each other: stuffing does the bread, potato flakes do the thickening, gravy powder does the seasoning. Foil-packet chicken stands in for the roast, since a whole bird is not a backcountry item.

Order matters more than technique. The stuffing needs standing time in hot water before the flakes go in, or you get dry cubes suspended in paste. Margarine replaces butter because it keeps at ambient temperature for weeks, and the fat is what keeps this from eating like wallpaper glue. Cranberries rehydrate on their own in the pot and cut the salt.

Note on tags: the gravy mix and stuffing mix are wheat-based and commonly contain dairy, so no gluten-free or dairy-free claim here.
