This started as a kitchen sheet cake and the only real trail problem was the fresh dairy and eggs. Powdered egg replaces the eggs one for one, and canned evaporated milk does the topping's job better than fresh milk would since it is already concentrated. Butter is fine unrefrigerated for a few days in cool weather; in heat, use a canned or ghee substitute.

The oats soaking in boiling water is the step that matters. It softens them and holds moisture in the crumb, which is why the bars survive being wrapped in foil and shoved into a pack for days. The broiled coconut topping is done on the lid with coals on top only, which is as close as a dutch oven gets to a broiler.
