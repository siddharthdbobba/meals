This is the standard make-ahead brunch casserole with the refrigerator taken out of it. Fresh eggs and a half gallon of milk are the two things that fail first in a cooler, so both come from powder here, mixed to the same ratio the original used. Powdered egg sets a little firmer than fresh, which is an advantage in a dutch oven where the edges cook faster than the middle.

The overnight soak is not optional. Dry bread needs hours to absorb the custard, and skipping it leaves you with a wet layer under a browned top. Onion and bell pepper get rehydrated and drained first so they do not release water into the eggs. Cheese still has to ride in the cooler, so this one stays a car camping or basecamp recipe.
