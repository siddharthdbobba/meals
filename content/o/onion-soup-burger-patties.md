The onion soup mix is doing two jobs. It seasons the meat all the way through instead of only on the crust, and the dried onion pulls a little moisture out of the beef and holds it there while the patty cooks. Salt and bouillon in the mix mean no separate salting step, which matters when you are working next to a fire in the dark.

Fat percentage is the whole ballgame. 80/20 renders enough to keep the middle moist over the dry, uneven heat of coals; 90/10 will be gray and tight no matter what you add. The butter at the end is not seasoning, it is insurance — it melts into the surface while the patties rest and covers for anything you overcooked. This one needs a cooler and stays a car-camping meal; there is no shelf-stable version of raw ground beef.
