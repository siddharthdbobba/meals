This started as a weeknight vegetarian dinner, the kind that leans on canned beans and a block of cheese from the fridge. Neither travels. Instant refried bean flakes do the same job at a fraction of the weight, and they thicken the pot instead of sitting in it. Tomato powder replaces the can of sauce.

The beans are the whole structure here. Pasta and water alone give you a thin, sad bowl; the bean flakes bind the cooking water into something that coats. Cook the pasta in the minimum water you can get away with and skip the drain, so the starch stays in. The oil and cheese go in off heat because that is where the calories are, and boiling them just makes a greasy film.
