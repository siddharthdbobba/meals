The original is a skillet of canned tomatoes, canned beans, and cooked rice. None of that carries. Instant rice replaces the cooked rice, tomato powder plus sun-dried tomatoes replaces two cans of stewed tomatoes, and the greens go in dried. What survives the swap is the structure: a tomato broth that rehydrates everything, then starch and protein stirred in at the end.

Order matters. The dried vegetables and tomatoes need real boiling time to soften, and the rice does not — put the rice in first and the broccoli stays crunchy while the rice turns to paste. The beans go in last because they are already cooked and only need warming. The oil at the end is where most of the fat comes from; without it this is a lean dinner for the calories it weighs.
