This started as an oven pound cake with a pound of butter, four eggs, and fresh orange juice. None of that survives a weekend in a car. Oil replaces the butter and behaves better warm, egg powder and milk powder replace the fridge items, and extract plus dried peel carry the orange without a piece of fruit going soft in the food bin.

The dutch oven is the whole method. Most coals go on the lid so the heat comes from above and the bottom does not scorch before the middle sets. Rotating both oven and lid is not optional over a real fire, where one side always runs hotter than the other.
