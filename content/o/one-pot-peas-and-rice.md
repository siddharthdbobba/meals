Freeze-dried peas and instant rice work because they rehydrate at the same speed. The powders—butter, sour cream, Parmesan—dissolve into the liquid and add fat and flavor. The 15-minute sit time is when everything comes together. You boil once and insulate.

The recipe halves easily for a light appetite or doubles for groups. In cold or high-altitude conditions, wrap the pot in a sleeping pad or cozy; heat loss slows hydration significantly. Carry the powders premixed in a bag at home—they're less likely to clump or absorb moisture that way.
