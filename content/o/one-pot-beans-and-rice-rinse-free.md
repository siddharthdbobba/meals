The water ratio is the whole point. Instant rice takes about twice its volume, bean flakes take a little more, and 420 ml lands close enough that everything absorbs and the pot comes out nearly dry. A dry pot means a wipe with a spoon and a splash, not a basin of dishwater you have to carry away from camp and strain.

Bean flakes are also the thickener. They turn what would be loose rice into something that holds on a spoon, and they carry the oil instead of letting it pool. Add them after the rice has already softened, or the starch seizes around the grains and the middle stays hard.
