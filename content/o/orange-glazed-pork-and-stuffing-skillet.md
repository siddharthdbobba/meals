The original is a Dutch oven project: pockets cut in ten pork chops, stuffed, seared, baked an hour. Nothing about that survives a trip without a fridge, so the stuffing came out of the chop and went under the meat. Same components, one pot, a quarter of the time.

The glaze is where the shortcuts show. Fresh orange zest and juice become sweetened drink powder plus cornstarch, which thickens on contact with heat and gives you the sticky, sharp counterweight the fat needs. Mix it cold first or the starch will seize into lumps you cannot break up. Butter becomes oil because butter goes rancid warm and oil does not.
