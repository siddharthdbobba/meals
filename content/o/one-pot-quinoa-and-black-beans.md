This started as a stovetop dinner with canned beans and a fresh pepper. Neither survives a pack, so the beans become instant flakes and the vegetables get dried. The spice load stays the same, which matters more than it sounds: quinoa and beans are both flat on their own, and the chili and cumin are the entire flavor budget.

Order is the only real technique here. Quinoa needs a genuine simmer and about twelve minutes; bean flakes need almost nothing and will glue the pot shut if they go in early. Cook the grain first, thicken with the beans second, and the whole thing stays one pot and one wash.
