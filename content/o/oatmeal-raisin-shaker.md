The original is a blender drink built on a branded shake mix and liquid maple syrup, neither of which belongs in a pack. Generic vanilla protein powder plus dry whole milk gets the same body, and maple or brown sugar carries the flavor without a sticky bottle. Grinding the oats at home is the part that matters: whole rolled oats will not soften in cold water inside a shaker, so they end up as wet flecks. Ground fine, they thicken the drink and add the chew the name promises.

Raisins go in at the end rather than in the mix. Packed with the powder they draw moisture and clump the whole bag into a brick over a few days. This is a recovery drink more than a meal: about 26 g of protein and enough sugar to start refilling what a long climb took out, at roughly two ounces of carried weight.
