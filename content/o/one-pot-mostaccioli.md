The oven version bakes because the cheese needs to brown. On trail there is no oven, so the dish gets rebuilt around a single boil: the pasta cooks directly in the sauce water instead of being drained, which means no strainer, no wasted starch, and no second pot.

Tomato powder replaces the jar of marinara and does the heavy lifting on weight. It goes in after the heat is off, because tomato solids scorch on a thin pot bottom and thicken the water enough to stop the pasta cooking evenly. Block mozzarella does not travel without a cooler, so canister parmesan carries the salt and the fat instead.
