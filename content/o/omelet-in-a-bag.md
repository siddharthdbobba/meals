The original wants a fresh egg, a fridge, and a cutting board. None of that travels. Whole egg powder rehydrated in the bag does the same job at a fraction of the weight, and dried peppers and onion soften in the two minutes it takes the pot to come up.

Simmer, not boil. A rolling boil knocks the bag against the pot wall and thins the plastic where it touches metal. The bag is doing the cooking and the dishes at the same time, which is the whole reason for the method.
