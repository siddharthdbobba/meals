Khichdi is normally rice and lentils simmered soft. Oats get there in a fraction of the fuel, which is the whole reason to carry this version. The order matters more than the recipe does: vegetables and onion need the full soak, oats need about two minutes, and putting them in together leaves you with crunchy carrot in porridge.

Everything fresh in the original became a powder. Tomato powder and dried onion carry the base, chili flakes replace the green chilli, and lemon juice powder does the job of the squeeze at the end. Add the oil off the heat so it stays on top of the grain instead of cooking away.
