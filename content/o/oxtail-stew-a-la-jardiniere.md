Oxtail is a long braise at home, not on the trail, so the work is front-loaded. Simmer the oxtail with the tomatoes, brandy, and aromatics for three hours, strip the meat off the bone, shred it fine, and spread it on trays with the sauce and vegetables. Dry the meat hard — any fat left in it will go rancid in a week. Skim the braise cold before drying and the packed weight drops noticeably.

Collagen is why this rehydrates better than most dried beef. It sets to gel when the braise cools and dissolves back into body when hot water hits it, which is what makes a stew instead of wet shreds. The potato flakes cover the rest. The alcohol was in the original braise and burns off at home, so nothing is carried.
