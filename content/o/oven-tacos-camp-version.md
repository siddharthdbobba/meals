Baking tacos before you fill them is backwards on the trail. The point of heating them assembled is that the cheese welds the filling to the shell, so it holds together in one hand next to a fire instead of falling apart on your knee.

The fresh toppings are what got cut. Lettuce, tomato, and avocado all die in a pack, so dried slaw rehydrated in cold water stands in for the crunch. Instant bean flakes do double duty: protein, and a binder that keeps the shell from going soggy the way loose meat would.
