This adapts commercial orange chicken meals for trail cooking. Instant rice saves fuel and time, and freeze-dried chicken rehydrates into full pieces in boiling water. The result tastes like chicken, not powder.

Most commercial freeze-dried meals are mostly sugar masking mediocre ingredients. This one cuts the added sugar by half and gets its flavor from orange zest instead. Brown sugar is optional — the dish works without it if you want less sweetness.
