The advertised serving sizes on freeze-dried meals are lies told by people who eat a 300-calorie dinner. Oil solves this: one tablespoon adds 120 calories and makes the whole thing taste less like cardboard. Tortillas aren't a side; they're structure, calories, and a way to turn 'meal from a bag' into an actual dinner.

This setup costs almost nothing beyond what you'd carry anyway. Pack oil in small bottles so you control how much goes on a given trip. Spices are optional but worth the grams—a commercial meal seasoned for everyone tastes like it was seasoned for no one.
