Real French onion soup is thirty minutes of caramelizing onions, which is thirty minutes of fuel you do not have. Crispy fried onions are already browned and already fried, so the sweetness and the fat are done before you leave the house. The dried minced onion goes in alongside them to put back some of the sharp onion flavor that frying cooks off.

The flour cooked in oil first is the only fussy step and it is worth keeping. It gives the broth enough body to hold the bread up instead of drowning it. Cheese melts under a lid on residual heat, so there is no broiler and no second pot. Hard cheese travels for days unrefrigerated; soft cheese does not, which is why swiss or gouda and not the low-fat havarti this came from.
