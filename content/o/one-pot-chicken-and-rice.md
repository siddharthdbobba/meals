Evaporated milk rehydrates freeze-dried ingredients more effectively than broth alone, and it thickens the rice without needing fresh cream. The starches released by the cooking rice work with the milk solids to create a creamy base.

Add the cheese at the end off the heat—this prevents it from breaking. The True Lemon cuts through the richness and works like the acidity in a home kitchen where fresh vegetables would be doing the same job.
