Couscous is made for cold hydration: overnight in water it softens without cooking, while canned chicken provides complete protein without any fuel. The dried vegetables and powdered cheese contribute flavor and umami as they plump up in the cold water.

Mediterranean cooking without fresh ingredients relies on salt, acid, and fat—all portable in dry form. Lemon powder does the acid work. Pack the olive oil in a separate dropper bottle or it will grease your entire food bag, but it's worth the weight. It's what makes this taste intentional instead of just filling.
