Ropa vieja on the stove at home is a three-hour braise: a whole flank steak goes soft enough to pull into threads. You cannot do that on a canister. So the recipe starts where the braise ends, with freeze-dried beef that already comes apart in strands and rehydrates in minutes.

The rest is substitution. Tomato powder replaces fresh tomato and thickens the liquid at the same time. Dried peppers and onion go in at the start because they need the full simmer, while the cilantro goes in off the heat, where it still tastes like something. Wine is dead weight on trail, so vinegar carries the acid instead.
