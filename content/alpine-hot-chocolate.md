Packet hot chocolate is sugar and a rumor of cocoa. This is 320 calories of actual
fat and sugar going into a cold person right before bed, which is the single most
effective way to sleep warm.

The pinch of salt and the quarter teaspoon of espresso are what separate this from
the packet. Neither is detectable on its own. Both are obvious if you leave them out.
