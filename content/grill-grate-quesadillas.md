Quesadillas are the correct group camp lunch because they cook in three minutes,
everyone can customize, and the failure mode is a slightly burnt tortilla rather
than a ruined dinner.

Cheese on both the bottom and the top is what glues it together. Filling in the
middle with cheese only on one side comes apart on the flip.
