Protein is the thing backcountry food is usually worst at. Most dehydrated dinners
are a wall of carbohydrate, and after four days of that you feel it. A foil tuna
packet is 20 grams for 3 ounces and needs no refrigeration.

Works cold, works hot, works either way after a rainy afternoon when lighting the
stove feels like a project.
