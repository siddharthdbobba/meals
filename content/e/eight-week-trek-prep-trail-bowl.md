This is a training-block dinner. Eight weeks of walking with a weighted pack means eating like you walked, and the two things that get skipped are protein and salt. Red lentils and a foil chicken packet put roughly forty grams of protein in one pot without hauling anything that needs a cooler.

The lentils go in first because they need real boiling time; the rice only needs steam. Adding them together gets you crunchy lentils in mush. The oil is not optional — a lentil-and-rice bowl is thin without fat, and fat is where the calories are.
