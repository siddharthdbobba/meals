This started as a baked casserole: breaded eggplant, layered with sauce and two cheeses, forty minutes of oven. None of that survives a stove and one pot, so what carries over is the flavor structure. Eggplant, tomato, breadcrumb, salty cheese.

The breadcrumbs do the work the oven used to. Instead of a crust on each slice they thicken the whole pot, which is the only way to get a spoonable dinner without a colander. Mozzarella is out entirely; milk powder gives the fat and the softer dairy note, and the hard parmesan brings the salt. Rehydrate the eggplant first and fully. Dry eggplant stays squeaky and no amount of stirring later fixes it.
