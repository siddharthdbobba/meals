This is the cheesy macaroni out of a 72-hour emergency kit, rebuilt so it works on a stove instead of a pouch. The kit version is meant for a disaster shelf and a 25-year date. The trail version wants the same thing a stormbound tent wants: hot starch and fat with one pot and one boil.

The measured water matters more than the timing. Six hundred milliliters is roughly what the pasta absorbs, which means no draining and no pot of hot starch water to dump in the dark. Whey milk powder does the work cream would do at home, and it goes in off the heat: powdered dairy dropped into a rolling boil seizes into lumps you will not stir out.
