The bread is doing two jobs. It contains the egg so the white does not run across the pan, and it soaks up butter on both sides while the egg cooks, so you get toast and a fried egg from one flip instead of two operations.

This one needs a cooler, so it is a car camping or first morning breakfast. Keep the heat low. A hot pan sets the bottom of the white before the top has any idea what is happening, and the bread turns black while the yolk is still cold.
