This is the one camp breakfast that needs no water, no boil, and no cleanup beyond wiping a pan. The hole in the bread is not decoration. It gives the egg a wall to set against, so you get a fried egg and toast in the same flip instead of chasing a white around a hot skillet.

Heat control is the whole recipe. A campfire runs far hotter than a home burner, so the bread burns long before the yolk sets if you leave it over flame. Cook it at the edge of the coals, or lift the pan. Egg and bread both need a cooler, which makes this a car-camping and basecamp meal rather than something you carry.
