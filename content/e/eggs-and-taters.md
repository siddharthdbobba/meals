Freeze-dried eggs on their own rehydrate into something loose and thin. Potato flakes fix that. They soak up the extra water, give the eggs a body to sit in, and bring their own seasoning, so nothing else has to be carried.

The potato flakes rehydrate in a minute; the eggs take ten or more. That mismatch is the whole reason for the cozy and the long wait. Pull it early and the potatoes are fine but the egg is gritty. Match the water to the potato pouch's own instructions if it differs from what is listed here, since flake density varies by brand.
