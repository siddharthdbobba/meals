The original is an oven dish on two sheet pans. A skillet over coals does the same job because the point is dry heat: eggplant browned before it meets anything wet stays firm instead of going to mush. Fresh tomatoes bring too much water and too much weight, so dry-packed sun-dried tomatoes rehydrate in a mug instead.

The panko goes on last, toasted separately in the same pan. Bread it first and it steams under the cheese. Toast it and hold it back, and you get the crunch that a breaded-and-fried version never has by the time it reaches the plate. The cheese is the only cooler item here, and a quarter cup per stack is enough to bind it without turning it into a cheese bake.
