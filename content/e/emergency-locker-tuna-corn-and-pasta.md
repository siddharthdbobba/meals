This is the meal that lives in the bottom of the food crate for the whole trip and gets eaten on the day nothing else is left. Everything in it keeps for years at ambient temperature, which is the entire point. There is no fresh dairy and no fridge step; the parmesan is the hard grated kind that sits on a shelf.

The oil from the tuna is doing the work of a sauce. Drain it away and you get dry pasta with fish in it, so pour it in and let the starch left in the pot bind it. Corn adds sweetness and bulk against the salt, and the cheese thickens the whole thing as it melts. It is heavy food for a vehicle or a boat, not for a pack.
