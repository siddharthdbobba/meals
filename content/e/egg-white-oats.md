Oatmeal is almost entirely carbohydrate, which is why it stops working around mid-morning. Egg white powder fixes that without the chalk of a protein shake. It contributes nothing to the flavor and a great deal to the texture, thickening the pot into something closer to custard than porridge.

The original uses liquid egg whites and a fresh banana, neither of which survives a pack. Powdered whites and dried banana are the direct swaps. The one thing to get right is heat: dump the powder into a rolling boil and you get scrambled egg in your oatmeal. Off the heat, stirred in slowly, then briefly back on low.
