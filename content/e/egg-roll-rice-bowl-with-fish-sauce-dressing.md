The original is a keto skillet dish built on fresh cabbage and a fried egg, which does not travel. What survives the trip is the structure: a big pile of shredded vegetables cooked with seasoned ground meat, then cut through with nuoc cham at the end. Cabbage and carrot dehydrate well and rehydrate fast, so the vegetable half of the bowl costs almost nothing to carry.

The dressing is why this is worth the extra bottles. Sugar, lime, vinegar, and fish sauce make a sharp, salty liquid with no oil in it, and it hits a one-pot rice dinner in a way soy sauce alone will not. Dissolve the sugar in hot water first or it sits gritty in the bottom of the cup. The eggs and fresh garnishes from the source are gone; the sesame oil and sesame seeds do the finishing work instead.
