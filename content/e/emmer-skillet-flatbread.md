The original is a two-day sourdough loaf that needs an oven at 260°C, proofing baskets, and a mixer. None of that comes backpacking. What survives the translation is the emmer itself, the hazelnuts, and the salt ratio, so this version keeps those and drops everything that requires a bakery.

Instant yeast plus powdered starter does the lifting in one hour instead of overnight. The starter powder is there for flavor, not rise. Cooking flat in a pan is the real substitution: a thin round cooks through on a camp stove, while anything loaf-shaped burns on the bottom before the center sets. Keep the heat low and be patient.
