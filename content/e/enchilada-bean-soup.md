The original is a stovetop pot of canned sauce, canned beans, and shredded cheese, which is most of a pound of water per serving. Tomato powder and enchilada sauce mix carry the same flavor at a fraction of the weight, and instant refried beans do double duty: protein plus the thickener that keeps this from being red water with things floating in it.

The fat-free dairy in the source was there to cut calories. On trail that logic runs backwards, so the cheese powder and the oil stay. Dehydrated white beans need the full off-heat soak — pull them early and they stay chalky in the middle.
