The source made these as baked egg bites for a fridge. That does not travel, so this is the same ingredient list run through a pot instead of a muffin tin: powdered whole egg, freeze-dried spinach, cheddar, bacon bits. Cottage cheese was in the original for softness and is gone here, since nothing about it survives a pack.

Two things decide whether this comes out as breakfast or as rubber. Hydrate the egg powder fully before heat touches it, and keep the flame low with the spoon moving. Powdered eggs set faster than fresh ones and will seize on a hot spot in seconds. The cheese goes in after the burner is off so it melts through instead of oiling out.
