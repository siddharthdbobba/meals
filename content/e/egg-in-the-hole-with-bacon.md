This is the simplest hot breakfast a griddle can produce. Cutting a hole in the bread lets the egg cook flat inside the toast instead of sliding around loose, and the bread soaks up whatever fat is already on the surface. Cook the bacon first so the toast has something to fry in.

The original assumes an already-dirty griddle from the night before and a fair bit of beer. Neither is required. What matters is a hot, greased surface and eggs that made it to camp intact, so carry them in a rigid case and keep the whole thing in the cooler. This is a drive-in breakfast, not a backpacking one.
