Egg drop soup is mostly technique, and the technique survives the trip intact. The cornstarch slurry is what keeps the egg suspended: without it the egg sinks and you get broth with a lump at the bottom. Thicken first, then pour the egg off the heat and stir. Boiling egg powder directly in the pot scrambles it.

The home version wants fresh eggs, fresh ginger, and green onion. Egg powder, ground ginger, and dried chives do the same job dry and weigh a few grams. Turmeric is not there for flavor so much as color, which matters more than it should when you are eating grey food in the dark. Sesame oil goes in last, after the heat is off, or the toasted smell cooks away.
