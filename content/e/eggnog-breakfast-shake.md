The source version runs on kefir, a raw egg yolk, and a blender, none of which survive a trip. What actually makes eggnog taste like eggnog is fat, sugar, vanilla, and nutmeg, so this keeps those and gets the body from whole milk powder and protein powder instead of yolk. Nothing here needs a fridge or a blade.

Maple sugar over maple syrup matters. Syrup is water weight and it finds a way out of any bottle you carry it in. The two-minute rest is not optional either: milk powder shaken once is gritty, shaken twice with a pause between is not.
