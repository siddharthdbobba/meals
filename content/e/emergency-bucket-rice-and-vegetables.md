Freeze-dried bucket meals are mostly instant rice, dried vegetables, salt, and a protein filler, sold at a large markup for a shelf life you will never use. This is the same meal assembled from bulk parts, and it costs about a dollar.

The order matters more than the ingredients. Vegetables and soy protein rehydrate slowly, rice rehydrates fast, so the rice goes in last and the pot comes off the flame immediately. Cook them together from the start and you get mush around hard peas. The oil goes in at the end because a meal this lean has almost no fat, and fat is what makes it hold you until morning.
