Wild rice is the whole reason this takes an hour. It is a grass seed, not rice, and it holds its hull until it has had thirty-five minutes of real simmer. Start it with the meat so the dried elk rehydrates in the same liquid it will season.

The original is a home stew with fresh potatoes, carrots, and kale. Dried versions of all three go in later and drink up the broth, which is why the water starts high. Kale flakes last because they collapse in a minute and turn to sludge if they cook with the rice.
