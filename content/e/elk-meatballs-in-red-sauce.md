Elk is lean enough that browning meatballs first dries them out. Dropping them raw into simmering sauce fixes that: they cook in liquid the whole time, and the fat and gelatin they do release stays in the pot instead of sticking to a pan. The eggs and parmesan are the binder, since lean game has no fat to hold the shape on its own.

This one needs a cooler, so it is a first- or second-night car camping dinner rather than a pack-in. Everything else travels dry. The long low simmer is the point — a hot fire will boil the sauce down and toughen the meat before the centers are through.
