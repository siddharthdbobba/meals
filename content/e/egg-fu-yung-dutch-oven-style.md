This is an omelet that gave up on being an omelet. The scout version fries the vegetables first and then floods the pan with egg, which is the right order — the sprouts and celery need real heat to lose their water, and eggs at that temperature seize. Dropping the oven to around 300°F before the eggs go in is the whole technique.

Two changes from the original. Evaporated milk replaces fresh, so nothing dairy has to live in the cooler. Draining the canned sprouts and mushrooms properly matters more than it sounds — leftover can liquid turns the eggs into a wet scramble that never sets. If you want this shelf-stable, powdered egg and pouched chicken get you there without the cooler at all.
