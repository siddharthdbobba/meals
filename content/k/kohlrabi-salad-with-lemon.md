Kohlrabi is one of the few raw vegetables that packs well. It stays firmer and crisper than lettuce, and thin slices won't bruise the way fragile greens do. The work happens at home—mandoline the bulb paper-thin and pack it carefully in a rigid container. Once at lunch, salt and lemon juice soften the slices over a few minutes and draw out their subtle sweetness. Oil and maple syrup provide the calories.

This only works when you're eating it the same day, so it's limited to day-hikes and car-camping. If you're planning a backpacking trip, dehydrate kohlrabi at home and cold-soak it at camp—you lose some of the crispness but gain shelf-stable weight savings. Either way, the point is a fresh vegetable salad without carrying a mandoline or doing prep work in the field.
