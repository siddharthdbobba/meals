Kayaks give you weight you would never carry on your back, but the hatches are narrow and everything gets wet. That pushes you toward dense dry bags and away from coolers. This pot is built on that trade: nothing needs cold storage, and the whole meal packs flat enough to slide past a bulkhead.

The bean flakes are doing the structural work. Instant rice alone stays loose and watery, and after six hours of paddling you want something you can eat with a spoon without chasing it. The flakes thicken the pot and add protein at the same time. Cured sausage brings fat and salt, both of which you have been sweating out all day.
