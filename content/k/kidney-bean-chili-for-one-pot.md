The original is a can-opener dinner: canned beans, canned tomatoes, canned sauce, a pound of ground turkey. All of that is water and steel. This version carries the same chili as powder and flakes and puts the water back at camp.

The two swaps that matter are tomato powder for the two cans, and bean flakes for whole beans. Whole dried kidney beans need an hour of fuel and a soak; flakes are done in five minutes off the heat and they thicken the pot at the same time, which is why nothing here needs to reduce. The 15 minutes of simmering is for the freeze-dried meat, not the beans.
