Dried vegetables are the cheapest weight savings in camp cooking. A cup of dried mixed veg weighs about an ounce and rehydrates into most of a pot of soup, so you carry the flavor and leave the water at home. The trade is time: real dried carrot and celery need a genuine simmer, not a five-minute soak, which is why this one runs fifteen minutes instead of the usual boil-and-wait.

The potato flakes at the end are the difference between soup and dinner. Vegetables alone leave you with hot broth and no calories, so the flakes give it body and the oil gives it fuel. Add the flakes off the heat and slowly, or you get lumps that never break up.
