This is the standard big camp dinner built out of things that do not need ice. Root vegetables, onions, and cabbage last a whole trip in a hatch, and dry-cured sausage lasts nearly as long. That means you can put it on day four when the cooler is warm water and disappointment.

The chorizo is the cooking fat, not a garnish. Frying it first and cooking the potatoes in the rendered fat is where the paprika flavor comes from. The lid matters too: half a cup of water plus a lid steams the potatoes through in a fraction of the fuel it takes to boil them.
