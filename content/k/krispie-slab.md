This is the Easter dessert version stripped of the food coloring and the parsley garnish. Shapes do not survive a pack. What survives is a dense rectangle of sugar and fat, which is what you actually wanted from it on the trail.

It works because there is no water in it. Marshmallow and butter set into a solid at room temperature and stay there for a week, so nothing needs a cooler and nothing needs a stove. Press the slab firmly enough that the bars hold their edges, but stop short of compacting it, since over-pressed bars turn hard and chip apart in the bag. The salt is an addition. Without it the whole thing reads as one flat note of sweet.
