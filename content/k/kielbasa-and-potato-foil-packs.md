This is a car-camp dinner, not a trail dinner. Potatoes and onions are heavy and wet, and the sausage wants a cooler. That is fine at a site you drove to, and the payoff is a full meal with no pot to scrub.

The potato size is the whole recipe. Everything else in the pack is either already cooked or cooks fast, so half-inch cubes are what keeps the pack at thirty minutes instead of an hour. Doubling the foil matters too: a loaded pack is a couple of pounds, and a torn one dumps dinner into the coals.
