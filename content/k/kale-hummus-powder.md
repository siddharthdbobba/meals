The original is a blender recipe: canned chickpeas, raw kale leaves, roasted garlic cloves. None of that travels. What does travel is the same thing in powder form. Dehydrated chickpeas ground fine rehydrate into something close to hummus, dried kale crumbles to dust and disappears into it, and garlic powder stands in for the roasted cloves with less sweetness but the same backbone.

Tahini and oil are not optional. They are most of the calories and all of the texture, and without fat the mix reads as wet chickpea flour. Add the tahini after the water, not before, or it clumps and you spend five minutes chasing lumps around a bag.
