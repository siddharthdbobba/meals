The home version leans on fresh ginger, garlic, and a cutting board full of vegetables. None of that survives a pack, so this rebuilds the same broth from dried pieces. Mushrooms are the important swap: they give back glutamates as they rehydrate, which is most of why a water-based broth tastes like anything at all. Cabbage, carrot, and celery come back close enough to their fresh selves in ten minutes of simmering.

The soy sauce and sesame oil go in at the end, not the beginning. Sesame oil loses its character when boiled, and soy sauce reduces into something harsh. Adding both off heat keeps the seasoning sharp and lets you taste before committing to the second tablespoon. The sugar is not for sweetness — it takes the edge off the salt.
