Fruit leather is mostly a patience problem. Kiwi puree is thin and wet, so it has to go on the tray thin and even or the thick spots stay tacky long after the edges have gone brittle. Flipping it partway through is what gets the underside dry without overcooking the top.

The honey is not just for sweetness. Kiwi is sharply acidic and gets sharper as the water leaves. Honey never fully dries, so it also keeps the finished sheet flexible instead of shattering in your pack. Store it sealed and it will outlast the trip.
