The chemistry does the work. Acid from the lime curdles the proteins in sweetened condensed milk into a thick custard, no heat and no eggs. That is why the original bakes for so little time, and why it does not need to bake at all.

The changes here are about the fridge. Heavy cream will not survive a day in a pack, so instant pudding mix and powdered whole milk stand in for the whipped topping; the pudding starch sets it firm enough to spoon. Everything else is shelf-stable, but the condensed milk is dairy once the can is open. Eat it the same hour or keep it on ice.
