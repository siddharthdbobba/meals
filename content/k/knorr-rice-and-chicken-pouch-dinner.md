This is the assembled dinner most paddlers and backpackers land on eventually: one flavored starch packet doing the seasoning, one protein pouch doing the work. The rice side is cheap, sold everywhere, and already carries enough salt and fat that you do not need to pack spices.

The vegetables go in before the rice on purpose. Dehydrated peas and carrots want a full ten minutes of hot water and the rice only wants seven, so starting them early is the difference between vegetables and gravel. The chicken goes in off the heat because it is already cooked and boiling it just makes it stringy. Oil at the end pushes the calories up to something that counts as dinner.
