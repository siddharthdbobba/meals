This started as a home dinner off a grocery budget list. Two changes make it work in camp: everything cooks in the pot the pasta cooks in, and the pasta boils directly in the thinned tomato instead of in a separate pot of water you would have to drain and dump. That saves a pot, a strainer, and about a liter of water.

The starch coming off the elbows is what thickens the sauce, so undercook the liquid rather than overcook it and stir the bottom often or it will stick. Carrots go in first because they are the only thing here that needs real time. Beans go in last because they are already cooked and will turn to paste if you boil them with the pasta.
