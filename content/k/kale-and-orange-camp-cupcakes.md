This is a car camping bake, not a trail one. It needs a cooler, real eggs, and coals you can control, so it belongs at a site where you are staying two nights and have time to burn.

The kale is doing color work more than flavor work. Boiled, wrung out, and mashed to a paste, it turns the crumb green while the orange zest and juice carry everything you actually taste. Wring the leaves properly. Wet greens mean a heavy, dense cake, and a dutch oven is unforgiving about that. Coal count matters more than time here, so check early and rotate often.
