This is a keg-pot recipe: everything goes in at once and the fire does the rest. The one change worth making is order. Potatoes and carrots take twice as long as cabbage, so they go in first and the cabbage and corn ride on top as a steam layer. Dump it all in together and you get mush on top of raw potato.

The beer is not there for flavor so much as for salt and body. Sausage fat renders into the liquid and the starch off the potatoes thickens it, which is why the broth at the bottom is worth eating with a cup. This one does not travel — it is heavy, wet, and cooler-dependent, so it belongs at a car camp or a basecamp with a fire ring.
