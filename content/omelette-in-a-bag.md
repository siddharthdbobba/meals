The appeal is entirely the cleanup. A camp omelette normally means a greasy pan
and a scrubber; this means a pot of water you can reuse for coffee.

Cook several at once and label the bags. Everyone gets their own fillings, which
is worth more at a group breakfast than any single good pan omelette.
