Powdered egg gets a bad reputation from people who tried to scramble it. Stirred
into a hot starch it disappears completely and adds 6 grams of protein for almost
no weight, which is exactly what a backcountry breakfast is short on.

Buy plain grits and season them yourself. The flavored packets are mostly sugar
and they will not take the cheese.
