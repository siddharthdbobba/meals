Winter day hiking has a real problem: stopping to cook means standing still in the
cold with bare hands on a stove, and most people just skip lunch instead. A thermos
removes the decision entirely.

This is the one recipe here with a hard shelf life. It is a day-one meal by
definition, and it does not belong on an overnight trip.
