A dessert omelet is the one sweet thing you can make with a pan, a spoon, and no oven. The original calls for two fresh eggs and cream, which is a refrigerator problem. Whole egg powder and powdered cream solve it without changing what happens in the pan: the same fat-to-egg ratio, the same set.

Heat control is the whole recipe. Low flame, edges pulled inward, jam added at the last second. Push the heat and you get a browned, tough disc instead of something that folds. Ghee holds up better than butter in a warm pack, and it will not burn as fast.
