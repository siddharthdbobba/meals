Pinwheels are party food, but the concept works on trail. Swap out the mayo and fresh vegetables for shelf-stable jerky and roasted seeds. The tortilla sticks to itself through pressure—you don't need a creamy binder, which is lucky because mayo spoils in a pack. Jerky and cheese are your protein, seeds add calories, and the tortilla holds the whole thing.

Assembly takes a few minutes and you can do it at home or at lunch. Slicing into pinwheels is easier on flat ground with a sharp knife, but you can also just unwrap and eat it as a roll if you're moving. These don't need refrigeration and they don't spoil, which makes them one of the few legitimate finger foods on trail.
