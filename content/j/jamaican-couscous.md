Couscous does not cook, it swells. That is the whole reason this scales to eight people on one burner: you boil once, dump, cover, and walk away while it does the work off the heat. The residual heat in the pot is enough, so a stove that would never simmer eight servings evenly can still feed a group.

The original called for canned broth, canned mango nectar, fresh peppers and scallions, and a dutch oven over coals. Dried vegetables and mango drink powder get the same sweet-and-savory base without hauling three cans of liquid. Allspice, ginger, and cinnamon go in after the heat is off; they scorch fast and lose their edge if they boil.
