Jerk seasoning is usually a wet paste built on scallions, fresh chile, and lime. None of that keeps in a pack, so this is the dry version: allspice and thyme carry the flavor, brown sugar and cinnamon fill in the sweet backbone, and dried chile does the work the habanero used to. The rub goes together at home and lives in a jar.

Frog legs are lean and small, which means they overcook fast. Oil before the rub keeps the sugar from scorching and helps it stick. Ten minutes total over coals is enough — past that the meat tightens and pulls off the bone in dry threads.
