The source used fresh eggs, milk, and a fridge. On a camp table that is three cold items to babysit, so this version runs on powdered egg and powdered milk rehydrated with 90 ml of water. The batter behaves the same: enough protein to set into a crust, enough sugar in the milk to brown.

The jam goes inside before the dip, not on top after. Sealed between two slices it heats through and stays put, and the batter-coated crust keeps it from running out the sides. Bread that is a day or two old is the right bread here. Fresh soft bread absorbs too fast and falls apart in the pan.
