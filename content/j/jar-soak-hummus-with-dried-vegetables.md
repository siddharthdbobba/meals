The vegetables are the reason this needs lead time. Hummus powder rehydrates in minutes, but dried pepper and carrot need an hour or more in cold water before they stop being gravel. Starting them early and adding the powder at the stop keeps both parts right instead of splitting the difference.

Oil matters more than it looks. Hummus powder is mostly protein and starch, so without added fat this is a thin, chalky bowl for very few calories. A tablespoon of olive oil roughly doubles the payoff for a third of an ounce.
