This started as a plain white sandwich loaf and picked up popper fillings along the way. The cream cheese is the part doing structural work, not the jalapeños. Fat in the dough shortens the gluten and gives you a soft, tight crumb instead of the chewy open one bread flour usually makes. The diced cheddar melts into pockets rather than dispersing, which is why it goes in whole rather than shredded.

The fresh milk is replaced with whole milk powder mixed into the dry bag, so the only cooler items are cheese and peppers. Dutch oven baking is mostly a heat-management problem: more coals on the lid than under the pot, or the bottom burns before the middle sets. Two hours of that is a car-camping or basecamp project, not something to attempt on a stove.
