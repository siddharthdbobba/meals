This is a dinner for camps where every liter of water came in with you: a jug in the truck, a dromedary in the canoe, whatever you filled at the last tap. Instant rice and bean powder both rehydrate at a fixed ratio, so you can measure the water once and not guess.

The order matters. Rice first in plain boiling water, beans second off the heat. Bean powder dumped in early glues to the bottom of the pot and scorches, and scrubbing a burned pot costs more water than the meal did.
