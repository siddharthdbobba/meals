The original was a car-camp stir fry with fresh zucchini, capsicum, and a shallot. Dried vegetables replace all of it, which means the pot starts as a soak instead of a saute. Five minutes under the lid gets the zucchini most of the way back; the pepper never fully softens and that is fine.

The curry soup powder goes in last and off the heat. It is a thickener as much as a flavor, and boiling it just makes it gluey. Two packets of seasoning on one noodle cake is a lot of salt, so drink accordingly or hold back half the curry packet.
