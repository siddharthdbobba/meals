Home-canned meat is the point here. It is pressure-cooked in the jar, sealed, and safe on a shelf, which means real meat protein at camp with no cooler and no ice budget. Glass is the tradeoff. This is a car-camping or basecamp recipe, not something you carry far.

Do not throw out the liquid in the jar. That is concentrated stock, and using it as part of your cooking water is what keeps the rice from tasting like plain rice with meat on top. The oil at the end matters too: canned meat is lean and the fat has to come from somewhere.
