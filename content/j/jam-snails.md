This is a bakery roll rebuilt for a fire ring. Fresh milk and eggs are the two things that will not ride in a pack, so both come as powder and rehydrate in the same water that hydrates the dough. Instant yeast replaces fresh cake yeast for the same reason: it lives in the flour bag for months and does not care about temperature swings.

The jam has to be thick. A runny one soaks into the dough during the rise and the rolls bake into a wet layer instead of a spiral. Coal placement matters more than the count on the lid, so keep the heat weighted toward the top and turn the pot regularly or the bottom scorches before the middle sets.
