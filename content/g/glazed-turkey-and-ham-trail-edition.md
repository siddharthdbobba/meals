The original version of this is a whole ham and a whole turkey breast cooked on a boat grill, then eaten as leftovers for three days. None of that travels. What does travel is the flavor logic: honey and mustard on the ham, barbecue sauce on the turkey, both of them sweet enough to make plain cooked meat worth eating. Foil-pouch turkey and shelf-stable diced ham get you the same protein with no cooler and no two-hour oven.

Potato flakes do the work a roasting pan used to do. Sauce alone slides off pouch meat and pools at the bottom of the pot; a thickened base holds it against the protein and stops you from drinking your dinner. The powdered egg is optional but it is the cheapest way to push this past 45 grams of protein, and it nods at the breakfast omelet the leftovers used to become.
