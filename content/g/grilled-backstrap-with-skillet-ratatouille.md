Backstrap is lean enough to punish you for overcooking it, so the whole plan here is moderate heat and a thermometer. At 350F a two to three pound loin takes about 45 minutes to reach 135F, which happens to be exactly how long a skillet of sliced summer vegetables needs to go soft without turning to mush. One fire, two things cooking, no timing math.

The dressing does double duty: acid and oil to tenderize the meat overnight, then a second cup poured over the vegetables as the cooking fat and the seasoning at once. Throw out the marinade the meat sat in. This is a cooler recipe, not a backpacking one — fresh squash and a parmesan wedge are not shelf-stable, and there is no dried version of this dish worth eating.
