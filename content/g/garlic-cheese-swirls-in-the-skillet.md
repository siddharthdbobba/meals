This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. Yeast dough needs an hour of warmth, a cooler for the egg and butter, and a pan you would never carry on your back. The only real adaptation is the milk: boxed UHT or reconstituted powder behaves the same as fresh here, since the milk is only there to warm the yeast and soften the crumb.

Coals, not flames. A skillet set in a live fire scorches the bottoms before the middles set, and the middles of a swirl are dough on all sides. Bank the coals underneath and put more on the lid so heat comes from both directions. Rotating the pan matters more than the exact time, because no camp fire burns evenly.
