The source blends the vinaigrette in a food processor. There is no food processor at a campsite, so this version keeps the pine nuts whole and shakes everything in a jar. Mustard is what makes that work — it holds the oil and vinegar together without any mechanical help, and the nuts become texture rather than paste.

Leeks are one of the few vegetables that improve under direct fire. The outer layers char, the inner ones steam in their own water, and the whole thing goes sweet. Split them through the root or they fall apart on the grate, and wash them after splitting, not before. This is a side dish; it needs meat, beans, or a pot of rice next to it to be dinner.
