Two things happen in one pot here. The potato layer cooks off the heat before the pot ever goes on the coals, so all the fire is doing is baking the cornbread sitting on top of it. That layer also insulates the bottom of the batter, which is usually what burns in a dutch oven.

The mayonnaise is the reason this works without a cooler. It is oil and egg already emulsified and shelf-stable in packets, so it does the job the box wants an egg and a splash of milk to do. The chile liquid is deliberate: it is the only wet ingredient with any flavor in it, and draining the can throws that away. Fresh potatoes were the original here. Flakes cut the weight, the peeling, and twenty minutes of boiling.
