This started as an oven roast, and an oven is the one thing camp does not have. A dutch oven with coals above and below is the closest substitute: the lid traps steam, the bottom coals brown the potatoes, and the top coals keep the chicken from staying pale. Rotating the pot is not fussiness. Coals burn unevenly and a stationary pot scorches on whichever side is hottest.

The marinade is doing more than flavor. Oil and lemon juice carry the oregano and garlic into everything the chicken touches, and the fat is what keeps the potatoes from drying into chalk under an hour of dry heat. Splash in water only if the pot runs dry — the chicken and mushrooms release plenty on their own, and too much water leaves you with soup.
