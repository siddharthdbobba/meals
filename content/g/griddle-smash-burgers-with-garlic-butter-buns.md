Smashing is what makes this worth hauling a griddle for. Pressing a loose ball onto screaming metal forces the surface into full contact, and that contact is where the browning happens. A patty formed at home and grilled on grates never gets that crust.

Two things wreck it. Packing the meat into a puck before it hits the heat, which makes it dense, and flipping too early, which leaves the crust stuck to the griddle. Wait for the edges to darken and use a stiff metal spatula. The griddle needs to be genuinely hot, so give it the full ten minutes.

This one stays a cooler recipe. Beef, cheese, butter, and lettuce all need cold, so it is car camping or a basecamp with a real cooler, not something to carry.
