Fruit leather is the cheapest trail snack you can make, and grapes are the easiest fruit to make it from because the pulp is already the right consistency. The two passes through the food mill matter more than they look: the second pass is where most of your yield comes from, and skipping it means half the grapes go to the compost.

Drying at 105F rather than in an oven is not about enzymes. It is about flavor. Higher heat scorches the sugars at the thin edges and the whole sheet picks up a burnt note. Low and slow keeps the color dark and the taste like grapes. The 1/4 inch thickness is the other variable that decides whether this works; thicker sheets stay tacky in the middle and mold in the bag two weeks later.
