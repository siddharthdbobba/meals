This is car-camping food, not trail food. A bone-in steak and four sweet potatoes are heavy, need a cooler, and want a real fire. What makes it worth the weight is that everything cooks over the same bed of coals in sequence: potatoes first while the fire is settling, steak second when the grate is at its hottest, peppers last in the pan the butter came out of.

The salad is the part that carries the meal. Simmering the sweet potatoes in wine, stock, and vinegar seasons them all the way through instead of just coating them, and the acid keeps the dish from going flat next to a fatty cut of beef. The raw cabbage goes in off the heat so it stays crisp. Pull the steak at 53 C measured close to the bone, since that is the slowest part to come up and it keeps climbing while it rests.
