Elote depends on two things a campsite cannot supply: mayonnaise and crumbly cheese. Mashed pinto beans with oil and lime cover the first job. They are not an emulsion, but they are thick, acidic, and they cling to a hot ear of corn, which is all the sauce has to do. Ground almonds with nutritional yeast and salt handle the second. The yeast supplies the savory edge that would otherwise come from aged cheese.

The almond mix is worth grinding at home and carrying in a jar. Doing it in camp means either a food processor you did not bring or ten minutes of chopping. Everything else is knife work you can do on a tailgate while the coals settle. Corn keeps a day out of the cooler without complaint, so this is a first-night dinner.
