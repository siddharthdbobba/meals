The original is a blender aioli: raw egg yolk, fresh lovage, oil drizzled in until it emulsifies. None of that survives a trailhead. Raw yolk is a bad idea without refrigeration, and a blender is not coming. Shelf-stable mayonnaise is already the emulsion the recipe was building, so this version starts there and adds everything else.

The dry herbs need the five-minute soak. Dropped straight into mayo they stay gritty and the color never spreads. A tablespoon of water and a short wait gets you the green and the flavor both. Mustard is not optional here the way it is in the original — it holds the mix together once you thin it with water and citrus.
