Backstrap trim is the reason this exists. Whenever you break down a whole loin there are ends and wedges too small to grill, and they are the best meat on the animal. Cut into cubes and browned hard, they cook in about ninety seconds a side.

The camp changes are the mushrooms and the fat. Dried mushrooms weigh a fraction of fresh and their soaking water becomes the pan sauce, which is where the finishing butter went in the original. Ghee replaces butter outright: no milk solids means a higher smoke point and no cooler space. Venison is lean enough that without that fat the whole pan goes dry.
