Avocado over fire is mostly about heat management. Direct heat for a minute gives you char on the cut face; any longer and the flesh turns to warm paste. The rest of the cooking happens off to the side, where the fruit only has to warm through and the mango has to soften slightly.

The lime and chili are doing structural work, not decoration. Avocado is fat and almost nothing else, so it needs acid and heat to read as a dish rather than a spoonful of grease. This one is day-one food: whole avocados and a fresh mango are cooler cargo, and neither survives a hot pack for long. Everything except the lime and the avocados can be prepped at home.
