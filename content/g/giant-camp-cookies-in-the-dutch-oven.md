This started as a 410°F oven recipe, which a fire pit will not give you reliably. Coals run cooler and less even, so the bake here is longer and lower, four cookies at a time, with the lid carrying most of the heat. Everything else stays: cake flour and cornstarch keep the crumb tender, and creaming cold butter rather than softened keeps the dough thick enough that a hand-sized ball holds its shape instead of spreading into a sheet.

Mix the dry goods at home. Measuring seven things out of separate bags at a picnic table in the wind is how you end up with two teaspoons of baking soda. Butter and eggs need a cooler, so this is a car-camping recipe and nothing else. The chips pressed on top before baking are not decoration; they are the only part that melts visibly, and a cookie baked under a lid comes out pale otherwise.
