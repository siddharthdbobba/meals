This started as a question about gluten-free MREs, which mostly do not exist in a form worth carrying. The workaround is to build the meal yourself, because every component here is independently gluten-free and none of them need refrigeration.

The jerky does double duty: protein and salt. It rehydrates into something closer to stew meat if you cut it small at home, which is the one step you cannot do well in the dark. Oil at the end is where the calories live, and it goes in off the heat so it does not just coat the pot.

Watch the jerky label. Teriyaki and most soy-glazed styles carry wheat, and bouillon cubes are another common place gluten hides. Both are easy to buy around, but you have to actually check.
