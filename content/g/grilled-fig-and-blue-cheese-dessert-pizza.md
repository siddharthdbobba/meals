This is a car-camping recipe because it needs a cooler and a real fire, and there is no honest way around either. The two soft cheeses are the whole point, and neither travels warm. What did change is the yeast: instant dry yeast keeps in a pack for a season and does the same job as the fresh cake the original called for.

Grilling the dough naked first is what makes it work over a fire. Toppings on raw dough over coals gives you a scorched bottom and cold fruit. Cook the base, flip it, build on the done side, then finish it away from the flames so the blue cheese has time to melt without the crust going black. The honey at the end is there to push back against the salt in the blue.
