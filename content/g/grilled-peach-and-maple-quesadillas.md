This is a dessert built around a grate that is already hot. Nothing here needs its own fire, its own pot, or any water. The fold does the structural work: the filling stays put, and both flat faces get direct contact with the metal, which is what makes the outside crisp instead of leathery.

Drain the peaches hard. Syrup or packing liquid left on the fruit steams the inside of the tortilla and you end up with a soggy fold that tears when you turn it. The cream cheese is not just richness either, it acts as a barrier between wet fruit and dry flour. Cook these last, eat them within a few minutes, and do not seal the leftovers hot.
