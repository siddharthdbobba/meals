This is a car-camping recipe and it stays one. The steak needs a cooler and the cheese needs a second pot, which rules out anything you carry on your back. What makes it work over coals is that everything is cut at home: the onion and pepper are sliced and seasoned, the garlic is minced, the cheese is cubed. Grill heat only goes one direction, and you do not have time to prep while it does.

Velveeta is the right call here for once. It has emulsifiers in it, so it melts into a sauce with two tablespoons of milk instead of breaking into oil and curds the way real cheddar does over an uneven fire. Shelf-stable boxed milk or a spoon of powder works the same; nothing in the sauce needs to be cold. Keep the pot at the edge of the grate, not over the coals, or it scorches on the bottom before the top melts.
