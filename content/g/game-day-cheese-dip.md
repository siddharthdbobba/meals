This dip bridges car-camping (with coolers and fresh ingredients) and backpacking (with dehydrated staples). Without cream cheese, the cheese itself provides body—six ounces of cheddar and Monterey Jack carry enough fat to make this work. The canned tomatoes contribute liquid that keeps it from seizing up into a thick paste; you're after something you can actually scoop.

Dehydrated vegetables rehydrate in the heat of the oil and the tomatoes' brine. The recipe asks for minimal cleanup and one pot, which matters when you're eating around a fire and someone hands you their cookie sheet to dip with.
