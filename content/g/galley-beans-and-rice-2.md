The original is a boat-galley dish: fresh onion, fresh peppers, rice cooked in a second pot. On trail that becomes one pot and dried aromatics. Dried pepper and onion rehydrate in the same water the rice drinks, so nothing gets sauteed and nothing gets drained.

Beans go in last and off the boil. They are already cooked, and hammering them in a rolling pot turns them to paste. The oil at the end is not optional either. Rice and beans on their own are a lot of bulk for the calories, and a tablespoon of oil buys 120 of them for almost no weight.
