This started as an oven casserole with a roux, sour cream, and thirty minutes at 350. The trail version keeps the same three layers and drops the oven. The roux still matters: flour cooked into fat is what makes the sauce cling to the beans instead of pooling at the bottom of the pot, so butter powder and flour go in together and get stirred hard.

Sour cream powder is the piece that makes this work without a fridge. It rehydrates into something tangy enough to stand in for the real thing, and it goes in off the heat because it will break if you boil it. The cornflakes are stirred with oil in the bag, not in the pot, so they stay crisp for the first few bites.
