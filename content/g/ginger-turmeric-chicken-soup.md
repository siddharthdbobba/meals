This started as a pressure-cooker soup with fresh vegetables and raw chicken breast. Neither travels. The trail version swaps in freeze-dried chicken and a dried mirepoix blend, which rehydrate in the same water that becomes the broth, so nothing gets poured off.

The carrot is the slow part. Everything else is soft in five minutes, so the covered soak is timed for the hardest ingredient. Ginger, turmeric, and red pepper come along dried because they are dried in any kitchen anyway. The oil goes in last: a broth this thin has almost no fat, and a tablespoon is the difference between a warm drink and dinner.
