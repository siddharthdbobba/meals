Pancakes are a car-camping breakfast, not a backpacking one, but the only thing standing between them and a camp stove is refrigeration. Milk powder and powdered egg solve that. Both rehydrate in the same water the batter needed anyway, so the ratios do not change.

Dried blueberries replace fresh because fresh ones turn to pulp in a pack. They pull moisture out of the batter as it rests, which is why the five-minute wait matters. Heat control is the other half: coals that feel comfortable to hold a hand over are about right, and anything hotter cooks the crust before the baking powder finishes lifting.
