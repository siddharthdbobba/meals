Freeze-dried vegetables are pre-cooked before drying, so they rehydrate in 15 minutes of hot water with no actual cooking required. Blackberries might seem like an odd addition to oregano-seasoned green beans, but the tartness cuts the salt while the sweetness keeps it from tasting entirely savory. The fruit makes this taste intentional instead of like a vegetable side from a packet.

Olive oil is where this goes from obligation to something you'll actually finish. It carries flavor and adds calories without adding weight. Carry it in a small dropper bottle or as individual packets, and stir it in just before eating. One tablespoon is enough for two people.
