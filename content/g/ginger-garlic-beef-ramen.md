The home version browns fresh ground beef and dumps in a bag of frozen vegetables. Neither of those travels, so the beef gets cooked and dried at home and the vegetables come dried. That changes the cooking order more than anything else: the meat and vegetables need a five-minute simmer to drink water back up, and the noodles only need three. Put the ramen in early and you get mush around gravel.

Rinse the fat off the beef before drying it. Fat left in dried meat goes rancid in a pack, and it is the reason home-dried ground beef sometimes tastes off two days out. The sesame oil at the end puts the fat back, after the heat is off, where it will not oxidize sitting in a food bag.
