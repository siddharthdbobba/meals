This is a cooler-dependent recipe and it does not pretend otherwise. Tuna steaks are worth carrying anyway, because they are the rare camp protein that cooks in five minutes and gets worse the longer you leave it on the heat. Freeze them solid at home and they act as a second ice block for the drive in.

The honey is doing more than sweetening. A trace of sugar in the marinade browns fast on a hot grate, which is how you get a crust in two minutes instead of a slow grey overcook. Keep the marinade time short, though. Lemon juice will chew through the surface of the fish if you let it sit all afternoon.
