The original is a picnic salad built on fresh cucumber, grape tomatoes, and a cold fridge. None of that travels. Sun-dried tomatoes replace the grape tomatoes and rehydrate in the pasta water for free. Cucumber is mostly water and adds nothing once dried, so it is optional here. The olives and feta are the parts worth carrying: both are salty, both are fatty, and both survive a day in a pack without refrigeration.

Drain well and wait before dressing. Wet pasta dilutes the vinegar and the whole thing goes flat. The mustard is not a flavor note, it is an emulsifier, and it is the reason the oil and vinegar cling to the pasta instead of pooling at the bottom of the pot.
