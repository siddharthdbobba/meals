Smashing the rolls is not a gimmick. A cinnamon roll cooked round on a griddle burns on the two spots that touch metal and stays raw in the spiral. Pressed flat, the whole thing is about a half inch thick, so both faces caramelize and the center is done at the same moment. Ten minutes, one pan, no oven.

Press once. Every extra press tears the dough seams and pushes the cinnamon layer out, which is where the browning comes from. The parchment between spatula and dough is the difference between a flat roll and a roll stuck to your spatula. This is car camping only: the dough is refrigerated and does not survive a warm pack.
