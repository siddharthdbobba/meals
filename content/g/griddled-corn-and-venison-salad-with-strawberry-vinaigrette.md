This is a car-camping dinner, not a pack meal. It leans on a cooler, a grate, and a knife, and every one of those is fine when the food rides in a truck. The point of the sequence is that the corn, tomatoes, and onion go into the pan the meat just left, so they pick up the fond instead of tasting steamed.

Rest the backstrap. Venison is lean enough that slicing it hot dumps most of the juice onto the board. Five minutes off the heat is the difference between tender and dry. The vinaigrette gets shaken in a bottle rather than whisked in a bowl because a bottle is the only clean way to emulsify oil and vinegar on a picnic table, and the sugar in the strawberries and honey does the work of holding it together.
