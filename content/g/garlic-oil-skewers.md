This is picnic food built for a fire. The original is a party platter; the change here is that everything gets cut at home and the seasoning is reduced to garlic, oil, and salt in one bottle. That bottle does the work of a marinade you have no counter space or hours to make.

Coals matter more than technique. Cook over flames and the outside chars while the onion stays raw. Wait until the fire has burned down to even red coals, and the vegetables cook through in about the time it takes to turn them four times. Save the last of the oil for the end, because garlic over direct heat goes bitter fast.
