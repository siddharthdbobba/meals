This started as a joke on a canoe forum: a group down to Bisquick, milk powder, and garlic powder, and somebody made pancakes out of it. The joke is fine but the ratio actually works. A complete baking mix already carries its own fat and leavening, so powdered milk and water are all it needs to become batter. Garlic and pepper pull it away from breakfast and toward something you can eat next to a pot of beans.

Two things go wrong. Too much water gives you crepes that tear on the flip, so start dry and add slowly. Too much heat burns the outside while the center stays raw, which is the failure mode that gives camp pancakes their reputation. Keep the flame low and give them the full five minutes.
