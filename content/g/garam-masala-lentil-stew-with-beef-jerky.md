The lentil stew's long simmer at home breaks down the vegetables into a cohesive mass, then dehydration concentrates everything into a lightweight package. Unlike soups that thin out with too much liquid, this stew stays substantive because you're working with a specific ratio of solids to water.

Beef jerky adds protein without requiring fresh meat, and it's working double duty: it softens into the stew while lending savory depth. The garam masala blend ties it all together—the complexity from four or five different spices needs heat to bloom, which it gets when you pour boiling water over the bark.
