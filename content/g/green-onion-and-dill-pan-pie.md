The original is a coiled pastry with fresh cow's cheese, green onion tops, dill, and melted butter, baked in an oven. None of that survives a pack. Tortillas replace the pastry, freeze-dried cottage cheese replaces the fresh curd, and oil replaces the butter. The flavor holds up because dill and green onion dry well and lose almost nothing.

Water discipline is the whole recipe. Too much and the filling runs out the edges and steams instead of browning. Start at 80 ml, let it stand, and only then decide.
