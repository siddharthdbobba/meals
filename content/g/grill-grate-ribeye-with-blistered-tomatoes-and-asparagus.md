This is a cooler meal, not a pack meal. Ribeye and a pint of tomatoes weigh what they weigh, so it belongs at a site you drive to. The steaks travel frozen and thaw over a day or two, which also keeps the rest of the cooler cold.

The dressing does the heavy lifting. Mustard holds the oil and vinegar together so it clings to the vegetables instead of running to the bottom of the foil, and the honey balances the acid against the fat off the steak. Mixing it at home means one bottle in camp instead of five small containers. Everything cooks over the same coals, and the foil packet means the only thing to wash is a knife.
