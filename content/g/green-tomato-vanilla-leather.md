Green tomatoes are mostly pectin and acid, which is why they set into jam so willingly and why that jam dries into a leather that does not crack. The vanilla and lemon are doing structural work, not decoration: the bean rounds off the raw, grassy edge unripe tomatoes carry, and the acid keeps the sugar from tasting flat.

A jar of jam is dead weight on a trail and needs a spoon and a lid you will lose. Drying it removes about three quarters of the water and turns a preserve into a pocket food. The trade is time at home, not time at camp. Dry it until it stops being sticky in the middle or it will mold on a long carry.
