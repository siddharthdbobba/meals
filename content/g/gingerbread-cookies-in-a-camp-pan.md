Cookies over a fire fail in one predictable way: the bottom burns before the middle sets. Everything here is aimed at that. Low heat, a lid to trap air heat above the dough, parchment between dough and metal, and a pan you rotate instead of trusting. The dough is stiff and low-moisture, so it tolerates a long slow bake without drying out.

Browning the butter at home is not a flourish, it is the reason the dough tastes like anything after a day in a pack. Powdered egg replaces fresh so the container can ride at ambient temperature; use real eggs only if you are baking within a few hours of leaving the house. The pepper is the point of the spice mix, not a typo. It gives the sweetness something to push against.
