Molasses is hygroscopic, which is the whole reason this cookie travels. It holds onto moisture instead of drying into a cracker, so a batch stays chewy through about a week in a pack. The high spice load also masks the stale note that creeps into most baked goods around day four.

The source version tops these with cinnamon cream cheese frosting. That is out here: no fridge, no point. Underbaking slightly is the substitute. Pull them when the middles still look wet and they finish setting on the sheet, which leaves the interior soft enough that you do not miss the frosting. Everything else stays the same, and the sugar coating adds a crust that keeps them from sticking to each other in the container.
