This one stays in the cooler category on purpose. Cream cheese is the structural element here, not a garnish: it glues loose shredded vegetables to a tortilla so the whole thing survives being held in one hand. Swap it for something shelf-stable and you get a wrap that sheds carrot with every bite.

The enemy is water. Cucumber and pepper both weep, and a tortilla that sits in that liquid tears when you roll it. Seed the cucumber, keep the cut vegetables dry and cold, and spread the cheese all the way to the edge as a barrier. Assemble per person, right before eating, and there is nothing to wash but the knife.
