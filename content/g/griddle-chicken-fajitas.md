This one stays a cooler recipe on purpose. Chicken, cheese, and sour cream all need cold, so it belongs at a car camp or basecamp with a full cooler, not in a pack. What it buys you is one hot surface and almost no cleanup.

The cutting happens at home. Sliced chicken and pre-cut peppers pack flatter, chill faster, and mean you are not doing knife work on a picnic table in fading light. Heat matters more than anything else here: a griddle that is not fully hot will steam the chicken grey instead of browning it. The lime goes on at the end, off the heat, or it cooks down to nothing.
