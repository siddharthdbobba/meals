This started as a cast iron grill pan recipe, which is a stove problem, not a camp problem. Over a fire the method is the same: hot grate, four turns, no lid. The quarter-turn rotation is not decoration. It gives you crosshatch contact and, more usefully, it forces you to leave the meat alone long enough to build a crust.

The marinade is four things because lamb does not need more. Salt and oil do the work of getting seasoning onto fat that is about to render into the coals. Fresh rosemary is better if you have a plant at home to cut from, but dried is close enough and it survives a week in a pack. Do the whole thing in the bag the chops thawed in and you are down to a plate and a pair of tongs.
