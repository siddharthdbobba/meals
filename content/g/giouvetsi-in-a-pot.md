The original is a Greek oven dish that sits in tomato sauce for hours until the pasta drinks it all. That hour count is the whole point at home and completely unavailable on a canister stove, so the work moves elsewhere: tomato powder gives you concentrated sauce without a long reduction, and the five-minute cold soak before lighting the stove does the rehydrating that a slow oven would have done gently.

Orzo is the reason this works in one pot. It cooks in under ten minutes and releases enough starch to thicken the tomato into something that clings, so you never drain anything. Keep the simmer low and keep stirring. The cinnamon sounds wrong and is not; it is what makes this taste Greek instead of like generic red-sauce pasta.
