The original is grits cooked in milk and broth, set firm, cut into cakes, grilled, then topped with marinated shrimp and a mascarpone sauce. None of that survives contact with a stove and one pot. What survives is the pairing: a salty, fatty corn base under something with garlic and paprika on it.

Mascarpone becomes powdered milk plus cheese powder, which gets you the fat and the body without a cooler. The shrimp are freeze-dried and rehydrated separately because throwing them into the grits raw-dry leaves you chewing on erasers. Cook the grits low and stir them; the bottom of a titanium pot is thin enough to burn corn in under a minute.
