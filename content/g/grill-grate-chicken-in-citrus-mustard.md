This started as a home grilling marinade and needed almost nothing changed for a car camp, because everything in it is already shelf-stable except the chicken. The oil carries the garlic and pepper onto the meat, the sugar gives you browning over coals that a lean acid marinade never would, and the mustard seeds hang on through the fire instead of rinsing off.

The citrus is the part with a clock on it. Lemon and lime juice cook the surface of the meat chemically, and past a day you get chalky, grainy chicken. Bag it in the morning, grill it that evening. Draining the marinade before it hits the grate matters too: sugar and oil dripping into coals means flare-ups and a black crust over raw middle.
