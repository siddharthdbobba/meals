Watermelon is 92% water, which makes it a bad thing to carry and a good thing to eat where a car is parked. This one stays in the cooler-only column on purpose. The syrup is the reason to bother: it fixes an underripe melon, which is most melons bought at a gas station on the way to a trailhead.

Sugar alone would just make it sweet. The ginger simmer pulls out enough heat to cut through, and the salt makes the melon taste more like itself. Make the syrup at home and this stops being a recipe with a cook time at all.

One real constraint: cut melon is a two-day food even on ice. Plan it for the first night out, not the last.
