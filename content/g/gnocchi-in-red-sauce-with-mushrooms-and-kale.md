This started as an oven dish: sauce built in a skillet, then baked under fresh mozzarella. The oven is the first thing to go, and fresh mozzarella is the second. Shelf-stable vacuum-packed gnocchi cooks in five minutes of simmer, so the whole thing collapses into one pot on one burner.

Tomato powder does the work of a can of sauce at a tenth of the weight, and the tomato paste gives it the depth a thin rehydrated sauce otherwise lacks. Milk powder covers for the cream, and an aged hard cheese melts into the sauce instead of sitting on top of it. The nutmeg looks odd on the list and belongs there anyway.
