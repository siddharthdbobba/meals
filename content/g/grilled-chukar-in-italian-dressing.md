Chukar is lean to the point of being unforgiving. There is almost no fat inside the muscle to buy you time, so the window between done and dry is about ninety seconds wide. The dressing solves half of that: the oil coats the outside and slows moisture loss, and the vinegar loosens the surface proteins so the meat stays tender under high heat. You are not adding flavor so much as adding insurance.

The other half is your hands. Pull the birds early, rest them, and let carryover finish the job. This is a cooler recipe, not a shelf-stable one — game meat rides frozen and thaws in the marinade on the drive in. If you are packing in on foot, this is not your dinner.
