Ramen built around dehydrated vegetables for substance. The veggies rehydrate separately in a small pool of water, then everything goes to the pot together. Boiling and simmering at once means almost no liquid waste—the noodles absorb what you cook with, so there's nothing left to drain.

Ginger and garlic do the heavy work here. Without the ramen flavor packet, you need another layer of salt and umami to keep this from tasting hollow. That's why the soy sauce or coconut aminos matters, and why the lemon juice isn't optional.
