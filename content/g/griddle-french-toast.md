This is a car-camping breakfast because it needs a cooler and a flat hot surface, and there is no honest way around either. What makes it workable in camp is doing the messy part at home: eggs, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon go into a jar days ahead. In the morning you shake a jar instead of cracking eggs over a picnic table.

Thick bread is the whole game. Texas toast has enough structure to hold custard without collapsing, which is why the dip is short — two seconds a side, not a soak. Butter goes on between batches rather than all at once; it burns off, and browning stops when it's gone.
