This started as a picnic salad that assumes a refrigerator. The trail version swaps rotisserie chicken for canned, fresh parmesan for the shelf-stable grated kind, and jarred mayo for single-serve packets. Packets matter more than they look: an open jar of mayo in a warm pack is a bad decision, but sealed packets are stable and you use exactly what you open.

The grapes are the only ingredient with a clock on them. They travel fine for a day in a hard container, and after that dried fruit does the same job of cutting the fat. Everything gets mixed in the bag it came in, so there is no bowl and nothing to wash.
