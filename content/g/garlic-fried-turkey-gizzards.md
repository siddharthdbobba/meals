Gizzards are working muscle, so they cook in two stages or not at all. The simmer breaks the connective tissue down until a knife goes through without argument; the fry crisps the outside and gets the garlic into the fat. Skip the simmer and you get rubber.

The home prep matters more than the camp cooking. Salt soak pulls out blood, milk soak takes the edge off the organ flavor, and both are impossible to do well from a cooler. Do them in your kitchen the morning you drive out. Sesame oil goes in after the pan comes off the fire because it burns and turns bitter over coals.
