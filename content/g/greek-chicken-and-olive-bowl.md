The original is a baked yogurt-marinated chicken with a salad alongside, which is three refrigerated components and an oven. On trail it collapses into one pot: couscous carries the starch, a foil packet does the chicken, and the yogurt dressing gets rebuilt from powder at camp instead of being a marinade. Lemon and oregano are doing the identifying work, so keep them even if you drop something else.

Mix the dressing separately and thick. Yogurt powder stirred into a hot pot turns grainy and disappears into the couscous; a few tablespoons of cool water in a mug gives you something that stays a sauce. Olives and feta are the salt and fat here, which is why the seasoning list is otherwise short. Note the dietary tags: this has chicken and cheese, so only the nut-free claim holds.
