Venison has almost no intramuscular fat, so it dries out before it browns and it has nothing to carry seasoning past the surface. The half-oil, half-barbecue-sauce mix fixes both problems at once: the oil coats the meat and moves the garlic, celery salt, and Italian seasoning into it, and the sauce sugars give you crust over direct coals.

Baste after each flip rather than marinating overnight. Sugar sitting on hot lean meat burns, and the acid in a long soak turns the outer layer mealy. Rum extract and lemon are both there in trace amounts to cut the sweetness. Measure them. This needs a cooler and real coals, so it is a car-camping dinner, not a pack-in one.
