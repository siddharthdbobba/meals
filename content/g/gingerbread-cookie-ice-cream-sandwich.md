This is a car-camping dessert because it depends on a cooler, and no amount of adapting changes that. The cookies are the only part that survives the trip on their own: butter, two sugars, and a gingerbread spine of cinnamon, ginger, and cloves, baked short so the middles stay soft. Bake them at home. A camp stove will not do this.

The brown sugar and the underbaking are what keep the cookies pliable in the cold. A fully baked, crisp cookie shatters the moment you press it onto frozen ice cream. Cut the ice cream in slabs rather than scooping it, so the sandwich is flat and holds together while you eat it. Assemble one at a time, in the shade, and hand each one off as it is made.
