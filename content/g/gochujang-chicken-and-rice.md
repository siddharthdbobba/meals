The original is deep-fried wings in six cups of oil, which is not happening on a stove that holds a liter. What survives the trip is the sauce: gochujang, ketchup, sugar, soy, and sesame oil, which is a complete flavor built entirely from things that ship at room temperature. Pour it over rice and pouch chicken and you get the same sweet-hot, salty payoff without the fry.

Mix the sauce cold and separately. Gochujang is thick and will sit in a lump if you drop it straight into hot rice, and the sugar needs somewhere to dissolve. Honey from the source recipe is gone here — brown sugar does the same work, weighs less, and does not leak.
