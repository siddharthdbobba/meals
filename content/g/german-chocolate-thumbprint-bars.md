This started as a home cookie and got flattened into a bar because thumbprints do not survive a food box. The cake mix is doing the job of flour, sugar, cocoa, and leavening in one measured bag, which is the whole reason it belongs at a campsite. Cut the liquid down to a few tablespoons and the mix behaves like a press-in shortbread dough instead of a batter.

Evaporated milk is the substitution that matters. It is shelf-stable, it carries the sugar into a filling that sets, and nothing in the recipe needs a fridge until the can is open. Coal placement is the only real skill here: most of the heat goes on the lid, or the bottom burns while the top is still raw.
