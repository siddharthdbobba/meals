Home gardens produce more than a household eats, all at once, and then nothing for eight months. Drying the surplus is the fix. Thin slices of zucchini, green beans, tomato, and pepper hold their flavor for a year in a bag and weigh almost nothing on your back.

Couscous is the partner because it needs no boil of its own — just hot water and ten minutes under a lid. That leaves the fuel budget for rehydrating the vegetables, which is the part that actually takes heat. The oil goes in at the end so it coats the grain instead of sitting on top of the water.
