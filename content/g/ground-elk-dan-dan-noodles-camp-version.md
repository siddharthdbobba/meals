The original is a weeknight skillet dish with fresh broth, fresh spinach, and a pound of ground elk. Three things had to change for camp. The broth became bouillon and water, since you are boiling water anyway. The spinach became flakes, which go back to something leaf-shaped in about five minutes. The meat gets browned and dried at home, which is the only part that takes planning.

Cornstarch is what makes this a sauce instead of soup. Noodle water alone is too thin to hold peanut butter, and undiluted peanut butter seizes into lumps. Slurry the cornstarch in cold water first and stir the peanut butter in off the heat, and the whole pot pulls together in about a minute.

No dietary tags here: it has meat, wheat noodles, and peanuts.
