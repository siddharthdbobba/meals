Peameal bacon is cured pork loin rolled in cornmeal, not belly, so it browns fast and gives up almost no fat. That drives the sequence: cook the meat first for the fond, then add butter for the eggs, because the pan will not give you enough grease on its own.

This one stays a cooler recipe. There is no shelf-stable swap for a fried egg that is worth writing down, and peameal keeps only a few days on ice. Cook it in the first day or two of the trip and eat every portion the morning it comes out of the pan.
