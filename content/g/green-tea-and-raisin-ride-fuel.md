This came off a list of what a cyclist actually ate in a day, not out of a cookbook. Green tea, water, raisins, protein. On the trail that is a caffeine source, a fast sugar, and enough protein to keep a snack from being pure sugar crash.

The protein has to ride on the raisins rather than in the tea. Powder stirred into hot tea seizes into lumps and coats the mug. Sticky fruit takes a dry coat and carries it down cleanly. The pinch of salt matters more than it looks like it should: sweating all day, plain sugar and water without sodium sits badly.
