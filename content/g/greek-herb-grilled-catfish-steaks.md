Catfish steaks are thick and dense, which is why the long slow cook matters. High heat dries the outside before the middle is done. Held around 250F in a foil tray, the fish steams in its own moisture and the lemon slices, and it comes off the bone in whole flakes instead of shredding.

Every seasoning here is dried, so the rub is a jar you mix at home and carry all season. That leaves exactly one perishable item in the plan, and the foil tray means the grate never gets scrubbed. Trim the red line down the center of each steak before cooking. It is the strongest-tasting part of the fish and it does not improve with heat.
