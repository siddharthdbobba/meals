Gnocchi is the most useful thing in this pot. It comes shelf-stable in a vacuum pack, cooks in about three minutes, and never needs draining, so the starch it sheds thickens the broth instead of going down a hole in the ground. Browning it in oil first is worth the extra few minutes: raw gnocchi dumped into water turns gluey.

The bouillon and tomato paste are doing the work a long-simmered sauce would do at home. Concentrates are compact, they weigh almost nothing, and they save you from carrying a jar. The bean pouch is the protein and it goes in last, since it is already cooked and only needs warming.
