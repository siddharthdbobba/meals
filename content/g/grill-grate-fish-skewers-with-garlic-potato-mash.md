The original version leans on two things that do not travel: a food processor and a pot of boiled potatoes. Instant potato flakes replace both. Add water off the heat, stir in the vinegar and oil, and you get the same thick, sharp, garlic-heavy base without peeling anything. Garlic powder does the work of a dozen raw cloves; raw garlic that has ridden in a cooler for two days tastes like a different vegetable.

Everything else is timing. The mash sits happily for ten minutes while the skewers cook, so make it first and get the pot out of the way. Fish cooks fast and sticks to a cold grate, so let the coals settle and oil the metal. The feta is optional weight, but it browns in two minutes and carries the fat that the lean fish does not.
