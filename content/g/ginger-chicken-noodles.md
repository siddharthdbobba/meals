The original is a fry-pan dish: raw chicken, fresh stir-fry veg, a second pot for the noodles. None of that survives a warm pannier or a bear hang, so everything here is dried and the whole thing collapses into one pot. Freeze-dried chicken goes in first because it takes longer to come back than noodles take to cook.

Ditching the seasoning packet matters. It is mostly salt and MSG aimed at broth, and it muddies the ginger. Soy sauce and sesame oil added off the heat do the same job with more direction — sesame oil in particular loses its point if you boil it.
