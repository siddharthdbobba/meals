This is a car-camping dessert and it stays one. Cream cheese and puff pastry both need a cooler, so there is no honest way to shrink it into a pack. What it does buy you is a real dessert for six out of one foil pan and thirty minutes of coals you already have burning.

The parchment under the pastry is the part people skip and regret. Foil alone glues itself to the bottom crust and you spend the whole meal picking it out. Indirect heat matters too: the sugar on the fruit will scorch long before the pastry cooks through if the pan sits over live flame. Push the coals to one side, keep the pan on the other, and cover it so the top sets.
