The original is a stovetop soup built on a rotisserie chicken and hours of simmering. Neither survives a pack. Freeze-dried chicken carries the protein at a fraction of the weight, bouillon replaces the broth, and dried onion and carrot do the aromatic work that fresh vegetables did at home.

Turmeric goes in off the heat. Boiled hard it turns bitter and chalky; stirred in at the end it stays bright and colors the broth the way it is supposed to. The oil matters more than it looks — soup is thin food, and a tablespoon of fat is what makes this a dinner instead of a hot drink.
