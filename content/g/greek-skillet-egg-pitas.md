The original is a cooler breakfast: fresh eggs, fresh spinach, a tomato you cut on a board. This version drops all three so it can ride in a pack for a few days. Powdered whole egg rehydrates into a real scramble as long as you mix it with water first and never dump the powder straight into the pan. Dried spinach and sun-dried tomato give back most of what the fresh versions did, and they weigh almost nothing.

Feta is the one thing worth carrying cold-ish. It is a brined cheese, so it holds up better than most, but plan on eating it in the first two days out. The pita is structural, not decorative — it turns a loose scramble into something you can hold, which is the whole reason to make this instead of eggs in a bowl.
