Portobellos work on a grate because they are already mostly water and structure. The balsamic and Worcestershire soak into that open sponge on the drive, so the seasoning goes all the way through instead of sitting on the surface. Two caps per bun is not overkill; one cap shrinks to about half its raw size once the moisture cooks off.

Gill side up first matters. The cap holds the marinade like a bowl and it reduces down into the mushroom rather than dripping into the coals and flaring. The aioli is just mayonnaise with garlic and acid stirred in, mixed at home so camp work is spreading and stacking. Everything here needs a cooler, so this is a first or second night meal, not a resupply dinner.
