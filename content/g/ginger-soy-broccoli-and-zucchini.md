The original is a fresh stir-fry, which is a problem three days from a road. Dried broccoli and zucchini rehydrate in the same water that becomes the sauce, so nothing gets poured off and no water is wasted. Ground ginger and dried garlic stand in for the fresh versions; they lose some brightness but survive a pack.

The cornstarch is the part worth getting right. Stir it into cold soy sauce first, not into the boiling pot, or it seizes into lumps that never dissolve. One teaspoon is enough to turn thin broth into something that clings to the vegetables and the rice underneath.
