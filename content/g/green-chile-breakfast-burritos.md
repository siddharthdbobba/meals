The dehydrator does the cooking here, which is why this works at all. Scrambled egg dried into hard crumbs rehydrates back into something with texture, and cutting it into chunks while it is still hot keeps it yellow instead of grey. Dry it thoroughly or it will not keep.

Water is the variable that ruins this. Cover the dry mix and no more. Too much and you have soup that soaks through the tortilla. The freeze dried cheddar goes in off the heat because direct heat turns it into a skin on the bottom of the pot.
