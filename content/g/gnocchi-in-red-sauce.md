The original is a baked casserole: boil the dumplings, bury them in jarred sauce and fresh mozzarella, melt it under a broiler. None of that travels. Tomato powder is the swap that makes it work, because a jar of marinara is mostly water and glass. Rehydrated with the starchy gnocchi water, it comes back thicker than you would expect.

Cheese is the other change. Fresh mozzarella needs a fridge, so parmesan does the salt and milk powder does the body. Stir both in off the heat or the parmesan goes grainy and separates. The gnocchi themselves are the whole reason this is fast: three minutes in the pot against ten for pasta, which is real fuel saved.
