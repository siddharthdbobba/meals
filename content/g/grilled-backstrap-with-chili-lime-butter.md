steps:
  - "Coat the backstrap on all sides with the seasoning and let it sit out while the fire burns down. Cold meat straight from the cooler cooks unevenly."
  - "Build a hot bed of coals and set the grate close over it."
  - "Grill the backstrap 4 to 6 minutes a side, turning once, until the outside is dark and the center still gives when you press it. Medium-rare. Venison has no fat to save it past that."
  - "Move it to a cutting board or a plate and leave it alone for ten minutes."

The sauce is the point. Venison backstrap is lean to the point of austerity, and a hot butter dip puts back the fat it does not have. Lime and mustard keep that butter from tasting like plain grease; the horseradish and pepper flakes do the rest.

Everything but the meat and the butter is shelf-stable, so the cooler load is small. Cook it hot and fast and pull it early. Overcooked backstrap turns to liver, and no amount of sauce fixes that.
