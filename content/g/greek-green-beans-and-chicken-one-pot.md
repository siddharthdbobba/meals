The original cooks for hours in a slow cooker so the beans go soft and take on the fat from bone-in thighs. Neither of those is available on a stove with one canister. The workaround is a short boil and a long covered soak: dried green beans rehydrate on their own heat, and the pot lid does the work the crock did. Chicken from a foil pouch replaces the thighs, and the olive oil replaces the fat those thighs rendered.

Lemon and dill are what make this taste Greek rather than generic, and both survive drying. Add the lemon powder off the heat so it stays sharp. Sun-dried tomatoes stand in for fresh ones and bring their own salt and acid, which is why the bouillon is the only other seasoning that matters.
