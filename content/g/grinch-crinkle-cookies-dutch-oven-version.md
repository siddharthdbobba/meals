This started as a home kitchen cookie with butter, fresh eggs, and a four-hour chill in the fridge. All three are gone. Shortening replaces the butter because it keeps at ambient temperature and holds its shape better in a hot Dutch oven. Powdered egg replaces the eggs. The long chill drops to thirty minutes in the coldest spot in camp, which is enough, since shortening dough is already stiffer than a butter dough.

The powdered sugar coat is what makes the crinkle. It stays dry on the outside while the cookie rises and splits underneath, so the cracks show white. Roll the balls heavily, not lightly. Coals on the lid do most of the baking here; more heat underneath than that and the bottoms scorch before the centers set.
